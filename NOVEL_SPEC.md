# Novel Specification

Title: The Soul Network
Repository slug: novel-soul-network
Genre: modern cyber-fantasy / mystery
Male lead: A practical student and night-shift equipment repairer who exposes a dormant counterpoint capacity in an existing consent-based civic network — the detail is in `bible/characters.md` and the live state is in `state/current.md`, and both outrank this line where they disagree
Length target: 760 chapters
Relationship policy: One slow-burn relationship or no romance, decided in the bible phase.
Power rule: Growth must be earned through practice, cost, failure, relationships, and changed decisions. The lead must not become instantly overpowered unless the approved genre premise requires it.

## Premise
A modern student discovers that an existing city network can improve techniques by pooling observations from willing people.

## Ending
The network becomes a shared public institution rather than one person's private power.
