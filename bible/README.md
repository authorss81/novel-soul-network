# Series Bible

The bootstrap canon is defined in these files:

- `premise.md` — the story promise, opening engine, long-form engines, and author-only mystery answer
- `world.md` — Nacre, its districts, history, institutions, factions, and social consequences
- `characters.md` — major cast, wants, flaws, relationships, and planned arcs
- `power-system.md` — techniques, consent rules, share lifecycle, costs, hinges, and Marek’s power stages
- `themes.md` — thematic questions, motifs, character tests, and final thematic answer
- `terminology.md` — canonical names, terms, permissions, places, and interface language

The series destination is in `../outline/series.md`; the final volume and resolution are fixed in `../outline/ending.md`. No chapter prose has been generated yet. Workflow phase completion is tracked by the controller. The next planning phase is the detailed Volume 01 and Chapter 1–10 outline.
