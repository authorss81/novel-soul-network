## Logline

A man who has spent a term and a half refusing to be findable is now the only person in this city who can be asked, in a room with no name on it, about a rule that is printed, numbered, dated, on a wall, and has never been withdrawn — and the rule is not a conspiracy, it is an emergency procedure, and the people who invoke it are competent, and the thing it overrides is a person who has not come back yet.

## Central pressure

Four layers. The first three are load-bearing and the fourth is the volume.

- **Immediate pressure: a printed standard with a number on it.** A practice card must perform the boundary-release beat or it must not be issued. That standard exists, is in print, is on a wall in a public room in a district office, has a date and a reference and has never been withdrawn. **A clerk of forty-four can invoke it in about nine seconds and it is correct, and invoking it takes about nine hundred cards out of use in about four days, and it changes nothing, because the cards were never the thing anybody was using.** That is the movement's finding and it is the volume in miniature: *a correct action by a competent person, which is right, and which nobody was harmed by, and which does not reach the thing.*
- **Structural pressure: Talia Venn's campaign for a right to withdraw.** **A right to withdraw is a clause in something, and the first thing this volume does with her campaign is show her refusing to supply the credibility, and refusing it for a reason that is not noble.** She is asked, in a room, to say that a person can take a share back without a form, and she says yes, and the person asking her says that is not a thing anybody has ever had to do, and she gives the figures: a page of about forty-one people that cannot be closed from the outside, a box in pencil in Bower Street holding one name, about four hundred and one replies in a company's postbox of which about nine got the company answer, about nine hundred doors carrying a number off the wrong line of one company's list, and the phrase *patient identifiers, if available*. **The campaign's real content is that none of those is a system and all of them are correct, and a campaign for a right has to end up in a document, and the document is a thing that can be invoked.**
- **The private pressure: the word, and the first time it is said out loud to a person.** Nine, ten and eleven a minute, uncorrectable, and it has not been spoken in front of another person in six volumes. **In Movement IV he says the number out loud, to one person, once, and it is not an apology and it is not a request, and she does not report it, and the not reporting is her decision and not his, and he has to live inside a thing he did not hand over.**
- **The volume's own discovery, and it is the stone it lays for Volume 07 without spending it: *a procedure that is fast enough to be worth having and slow enough to be arguable will be invoked by somebody competent while the person it is about is still on their way back, and there is no version of the rule that fixes that, and the only thing that fixes it is a person arriving.* The Return Path is required and is required and is not repealed in this volume. The volume builds the smallest thing available against a printed rule — a person who can be found, a room where a fact can be said, a binder a page can be taken out of, and a number of keys that is short — and it discovers that all four of those are about findability and not about consent, and that findability is the half of the problem that has a price.**

## Midpoint reversal (Chapter 275, the Thursday of week ninety-six)

**A recording of eleven minutes, made fourteen years ago in a room in Crown Terrace, in which a woman of fifty-three is asked a question by a person doing her job, and she answers it, and she says the words *the Crown Clause* herself.** The recording exists because a clerk of forty-four was a typist in that room and has remembered one sentence of it for fourteen years and has never said it out loud, and because a machine in a basement in Nine Bridges has been stuck for about four years and a man of thirty-four who works nights opened the door. **Iona Sorn enters the prose of this manuscript for the first time and she enters as a voice, and she is not met, and no message arrives from her, and nobody goes looking for her, and she does not say one word that is not on the recording.**

**The reversal is not who wrote it.
