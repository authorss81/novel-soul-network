## Central pressure

**A district has given notice that it intends to withdraw from the public network arrangements, and the institution has to answer inside a procedure that has been printed since before the spring and has never once been run.**

**The instant a right exists, somebody uses it, and the city discovers that it built a door and never built a frame.** The contest of this volume is not over whether a district may leave. It may leave; that was the point of the last volume and this volume does not reopen it. **The contest is over what the institution owes the people who exercise the right, and the honest answer turns out to cost about four hundred people a practice that nobody has ever written down and that one person with a hand that will not steady is holding by himself.**

## The subject, which is taken, and the question, which is inherited and answered here

**TAKEN: *a right is not a kindness.*** The phrase is what a person says in a room when they mean that somebody should be made to feel bad about it. It is the second half of Volume 12's *a shared answer is not a shared institution*, the second half of Volume 13's *a return is a place and not a permission*, the second half of Volume 14's *an answer is a shape and a shape can be printed*, and the second half of Volume 15's *many hands is a figure and not a room*.

**INHERITED AS A QUESTION AND ANSWERED HERE:** *what does an institution owe the people who use the right it gave them?* It is answered in the last movement, in a room, in about nine seconds, by a person who is not entitled to answer it and who is right, and **the answer is that it owes them the truth about what they are leaving, told to them before they go, and it is not allowed to make that truth a favour.** That is a sentence and not a scene and it is not a speech and it is not on any sheet.
