## Logline

A man who spent a term proving that the company that sold the kit is not the author of the omission finds a wage, a rota and about eleven people a week who are going to be taught to wait for him — and finds out in the same fortnight that somebody is being paid to say out loud the one thing he has spent two volumes refusing to say.

## Central pressure

Four layers. The first three are load-bearing and the fourth is the volume.

- **Immediate pressure: the rota.** He is four days a week on grade two and two days on a Friday release callout at grade three, and **the only condition on the rota is a card in date or an exception recorded and renewed.** About eleven people a week are going to be taught that a release waits for a word, and about four of the eleven are not on the rota at all because there is no course on a Friday. **He is now the fourth thing in about eleven rooms a week, and a supervisor is above him, and the numbers get better.** The horror of this volume is not that the hinge is bad. It is that the hinge is good, and that he is inside it, and that it is being measured.
- **Structural pressure: the belief is worth money.** A man of about fifty-two said in a market in week twenty-four that there is a technique being handed round and nobody is putting their name to it, and eleven people went two seconds quieter. **The operations director forecast on the Friday of week twenty-one and again on the Thursday of week twenty-eight that in about three weeks a second person would say it out loud in a different room because he has been asked to, and would be paid, and it would not be him, and he knows who and will not say it.** Volume 03 pays that forecast. **The payment is not a bribe. It is a line in a specification, and the specification is bought with a levy that about eleven thousand people pay, and the money is honest, and the sentence in the specification asks for the coverage.** This is the volume's discovery in its first movement and it is not a discovery about corruption. It is a discovery about **how a true-sounding fear becomes a funded programme with nothing in it about the truth.**
- **The private pressure: the word.** *Confirmed.* In a room where a card holder is confirming somebody he cannot keep a sentence of his own, and there is no switch, and he said it out loud once in front of four hundred and six people and was not believed and has said he will not say it twice. **Movement I puts him in that room two days a week for a living.** The sensitivity stops being a strange thing that happened to him in week twenty and becomes a **working condition of a job he chose in a chair**, and he cannot tell anybody, and he has to invent a way of working that costs him something.
- **The volume's own discovery:** *a counterfeit hinge and an original hinge are the same shape, and the counterfeit is the one that gets noticed.* Shape cannot tell them apart. **Only one question can: who is the technique waiting for.** Every hinge in this novel is the same hinge, whoever cut it. The one that gets a warrant, a seminar, a levy-funded series and a leaflet is the one somebody made by hand this month.

## Starting state (the Monday of week 31, 07:40, a depot on the far side of the city from his own bench)

- **Marek Senn, 22.** No Solo Seal and never one. A provisional Working Share with seven headings, four named partners who may end it by word, and a **fourth condition that he may not demonstrate, teach, assess or assist at any practice in that department, on its premises, under its insurance, at any time, unlimited by hours**, plus the list of the four names the department may put in front of any of them. **Four of the trust's six conditions lapsed on the Wednesday of week sixteen and two stayed, and one of the two is the prohibition on demonstrating, teaching, assessing or assisting.** A one-term conditional extension on six conditions, of which the course exception is the fifth.
- **The job.** Monday to Thursday, district service, grade two, the same rota and the same depot. Fridays, release callout, grade three, the release rate, on a card in date or a course exception recorded and renewed. **The exception is the fifth clause, in a company's system, in his own handwriting, in a field marked *reason*, and it says he has not got a reason he is willing to give.** A district service manager of fifty-two has undertaken to renew it on a Friday in an office and to write that he has not got a reason and will not invent one.
- **The word.** Cannot be turned off. Origin asserted in a load book in week twenty and **not dramatised anywhere in Volume 02, and no Volume 03 batch may imply that it happened earlier.**
- **The case.** The licensor is unnamed and the field is blank on three documents in three buildings and on a company's own stock docket. A lot onward on a venue line. A number in biro on page nine of a printout in a tray that goes at the end of the year, read four times and never written down. **AWAITING RELEASE** in a visitor column behind a barrier, eleven times out of about four hundred lines, on a laminated sheet with nine words on it and no name, and the same four words in a different hand in the visitor column of a second barrier on the same contract in another company's building. The company has recalled its bundle on four facts, survives, and is not the author.
- **What he has instead of a power:** a word he cannot turn off, a rota with a supervisor in it, about eleven people a week, a review with no determination, a chair a booking clerk laid for him, a fifth clause in a company's system in his own handwriting, a lamp he invented a fault for, a drawer under a bench with five things in it that cannot be produced to anybody, four of them other people's sentences and the fifth an envelope with nothing written on it, and a book no department and no trust is entitled to read, which is at forty-two lines and is not going to forty-three until the first Wednesday of the new term.

## Midpoint reversal (Chapter 125, last chapter of Movement III)

**Some of the cells printing counterfeit hinge techniques are not printing a copy of a bad practice. They are printing a copy of a good one.** A card produced in the Quiet Quarter in about six weeks by a printer who has never seen a company's stock has four correct steps and a fifth line that says *withdraw and report*, and there is no key-holder in it anywhere. **It is safer than the company's, by the company's own argument, and it is being handed to people who are being told it is the way to be safe from a technique that is made of a card and a word.** The movement's own sabotage did it, and the result is that the only hinges anyone can point at are the ones the movement made, and the original is invisible again because there is a hand in front of it.

## Escalation sequence

### Movement I — The Word and the Line (Chapters 101–110, weeks 31–32)

- **Pressure type:** work, money, mechanism, character. The quietest movement of the volume and the one that has to be read as a threat, because everything in it is somebody being reasonable.
- **The rota begins on Friday of week 31 and he is the fourth thing in about eleven rooms a week.** The pause is the practice. The pause is now a man with a lanyard and a course exception in a company's system. The word *confirmed* is a working condition of his job and he cannot tell anybody and he invents a way of working that costs him sentences.
- **The belief is bought.** A levy-funded evening series with a specification, a man who is not lying, and one line in a document that asks for exactly the sentence he has spent two volumes refusing to say.
- **The unsigned side of paper comes through the letterbox** and it names a company and a job title, and it is the sentence, and it arrived free, and it cannot be produced to anybody.
- **The second barrier is visited second.** Somebody has already been there and told a man of four years' standing that it was a company practice under review, and the man has gone quiet, and the four words are not in the book for the last six weeks. **The cost of the belief being paid for is that asking anybody anything now is asking second, and the people who can answer have already been given an answer by a stranger.**
- **Leo Marr is on stage for the first time** and is not the author of the paper and has never used the name she is being called by.
- **What changes:** the case stops being about who wrote the fourth beat and becomes about who is going to be asked to leave a room. **And he finds out that the number of people who are going to be taught to wait for him and the number of people who are going to be told who to wait for have never been in the same sentence, and that he is the only person in the city who can put them there, and that putting them together is a document.**

### Movement II — Cut Off (Chapters 111–120, weeks 32–34)

- **Pressure type:** consequence, work, isolation, first public exposure.
- **Civic Spine 7 crews are told they will not attend a customer's site** on a subcontract change nobody signed off, and every mechanic in the city reads it as a decision about them.
- **The hospital exchange room is found open when it should have been closed, or with a plate that has been changed in a way nobody signed.** A sabotage, and it is not the first of its kind, and there is a leaflet about it before the room is even opened.
- **A care-link becomes a public scandal.** Not because anybody broke a rule. Because a record about a person was copied into a series that a man of fifty-one paid a levy towards, in a case study, in a handout, with his name on it under a heading about safety.
- **A panic**, in a district, over a card that is not a company's and is not a movement's and has no name on it.
- **Marek's own network becomes a target for accusation:** a lanyard, a course exception, and eleven people a week are exactly the shape a frightened room is looking for.
- **What changes:** he learns that a false line can persist because everybody in the room wants the same answer, and that he is the most findable person in the city.

### Movement III — Not Mine (Chapters 121–130, weeks 34–37)

- **Pressure type:** investigation, moral cost, the counterfeit becoming real.
- **The unsigned paper is traced to a printer in the Quiet Quarter** and the printer is not a liar and has been paid in small sums by a mailbox.
- **The midpoint at Chapter 125:** the counterfeit is not a bad copy. **It has a fifth line that says *withdraw*, and it is safer than the company's, and it is being used to cut people off from a link a ward needs.**
- **The consequences of the leak come back a third time** — the eleven workers, the six places, the woman of thirty-nine with a page of her own — and the counterfeit is what makes them news.
- **Oren Vey is right in public about the counterfeit being dangerous and wrong about the original being the same shape**, and he is owed nothing and gives one fact unasked.
- **What changes:** the city can point at the movement again, and the movement's real work is now indistinguishable from the counterfeit, and the case goes quiet.

### Movement IV — Three Doors (Chapters 131–140, weeks 37–42)

- **Pressure type:** work under pressure, the relay, a physical crisis in an exchange room.
- **The cascade.** The exchange room is out, or partly out, and about forty people are on a link that nobody can end safely, and there is no card holder in the building and no man in a lanyard whose word they are waiting for, **which is the first good thing about the crisis and the thing that makes it hard.**
- **The relay, demonstrated, and it is expensive.** He passes a bounded observation through a person who did not want it and who can stop it and who does stop it, and the observation arrives with the shape of the carrier on it and somebody downstream can tell, and **the person who carried it is the one who pays.** **This is the Stage 2 relay and it is staged here for the first time in the manuscript and it is not a triumph.**
- **The team opens a visible, voluntary route through three independent practices** — a nurse's, a repairer's and a courier's — and does not restore the hidden default and does not put his name on it.
- **Leo saves the data that proves her group was used**, at a cost to herself, and does it by publishing something she would rather burn.
- **What changes:** the volume's argument is answered by three people who are not him.

### Movement V — The Taker (Chapters 141–150, weeks 42–46)

- **Pressure type:** proof, choice, aftermath.
- **The choice.** The manufactured shape of a case wants his name on the front of it, because a name is the cheapest way to close a room. **He lets the thing be published with no name on it** and pays for it in the only currency he has, which is that about eleven people a week will go on waiting for a man nobody can find.
- **Climax and resolution:** the hospital adopts a visible withdrawal procedure with a date and a person on it, and a woman of about thirty-one who has no post in this city for it is told by a clerk that there is no box for it, and the clerk writes one, in pencil, on a form, and it is a form and not a system and it is the volume's whole resolution.
- **The counterfeit and the original are shown to share a common structure** and the only thing that separates them is who the technique waits for.
- **Leo Marr agrees to a limited emergency notice and refuses institutional membership**, in about nine words, and does not soften it.
- **Power state at the end: Stage 2, with a costly relay, a permanent sensitivity, and a new fact** — he can pass a bounded observation through a person, once, and it cost a person, and he will not do it again without asking the person what it will cost them first.

## Volume climax (Chapters 141–148)

The exchange room comes back the only way it can, which is three independent practices that can each be refused, and none of them is his, and the room's own ledger says the same thing in three different hands. The counterfeit is exposed not by him but by a printer in the Quiet Quarter and a levy officer who did not want it exposed. **And the price is that a thing that could have been a case is published as an unsigned set of documents, and four hundred people will be told that a practice belongs to nobody, and that is a sentence almost nobody in this city can make themselves say.**

## Concrete resolution (Chapters 148–150)

- **The hospital adopts a visible withdrawal procedure**: a date, a form, a person whose job it is to telephone, and **a box on a form instead of a post**, and a woman of about thirty-one who has a diary and is not thanked.
- **Threadline survives and is more trusted than it was, and is not the author.** Its assurance is cited in a leaflet as evidence. **That is the cost and it is not repaired in this volume.**
- **The licensor is still unnamed.** The lot is still a lot. The Continuity Office still declines, in writing, with a count and a reason.
- **Leo Marr refuses membership and agrees to a shared emergency notice.** Nine words, said once, not softened.
- **Rafi Pell's fifth line is still his and still has two names against it and neither of them is Marek Senn.**

## A note on the volume's method

Volume 02's stated failure mode was a batch of ten chapters that was nothing but documents, and the volume's own rule was that **every document has to cost a person something, be refused by a person, or be carried by somebody who walks.** That rule carries into Volume 03 unchanged, and Volume 03 raises the stakes in a specific way: **Volume 02's documents were mostly about companies. Volume 03's documents are mostly about money that ordinary people pay, and that makes them harder to refuse and easier to get right, and the volume's failure mode is a batch of ten chapters in which everybody is reasonable and nothing is at stake.**

**The volume's second rule, new here:** *a mechanism that is funded must be examined in the same room as a mechanism that is a lie, and the writer must not let the funding make the sentence true or the lack of funding make it false.* The volume's antagonist is a **specification**.
