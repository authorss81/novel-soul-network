## Central pressure

**The institution did the one thing it is able to do about nine people it cannot name, and then the thing it did arrives.**

Volume 18 ended by doing one thing well and correctly: it printed a date on nine rows of a sheet that carries no names, and it printed on the reverse, in four lines, that nobody was asked about this date, that this date is not an agreement, that no person on this form was asked anything, and that nine people cannot be given one day and be nine. **Every one of those four lines is true, and all four of them are the reason the date does not reach anybody.** A notice about nine people that names none of them is a notice that reaches nobody at all. The institution has not solved the problem. It has produced the exact document that makes the problem permanent and legible, and that is the engine of this volume.

**The contest is not over the date.** The contest is over whether an institution can find a person without writing the person down, and whether a record can exist without a holder.
