# Volume 01 Batch 0001 Outline — The Drill

Chapter range: 1–10
Volume: 1 — The Missing Beat
Batch goal: Establish the volume's central problem as a body, not a theory.

## Batch goal

Marek Senn goes from a failing student with a private hunch to a failed licensee holding physical evidence, one injured classmate, and a plan that requires evidence he cannot get inside his own institution.

This batch must deliver, in order: his ordinary work and family life; a concrete rescue; the first deliberate share; the cost of that share; the public refusal that costs him the seal; the relationship correction that names his flaw; the structural proof that the beat is missing; a second rescue in which he himself becomes the key-holder for two seconds; the institutional squeeze; a refusal of the Institute's deal; and a decision to take the evidence outside the Institute.

By Chapter 10 the reader must be able to state the genre promise in one sentence: a technique can be true enough to teach and still carry a coercive boundary, and the cost of noticing is borne by bodies.

## Starting condition

- Marek, 22, third-year at the Nacre Institute of Applied Practice, night repairer at Civic Spine 7.
- Two failed practicals on his record. Solo Seal run scheduled in three weeks. Scholarship review triggers without it.
- **Timeline anchor:** Chapter 1 opens on Tuesday of week 1. The original Solo Seal is scheduled for Thursday of week 4; after Marek fails that run in Chapter 5, the Institute offers a witnessed assessment provisionally for Thursday of week 7. Chapters 6–10 occur over the following two weeks, so Chapter 10 leaves the provisional assessment one week away; Movement II runs weeks 7–9, withdraws the date in Chapter 12 before it can arrive, and the final assessment is set five days after Chapter 40.
- Stage 0, Latent Counterpoint. He notices things about practice that other people do not notice, he has never told anyone, and he gets physically ill when a technique closes around an unstated assumption.
- He has told Talia Venn no twice.
- The Institute has just received Threadline brace kits, revision 4-19, four months newer than the manual.

## Starting relationships

- **Rafi Pell:** easy yes, would rather help than think. First person Marek will ask.
- **Davan Rusk:** the instructor whose approval is the exam. Friendly, competent, entirely confident.
- **Talia Venn:** professional friction. He hears her consent questions as accusations about his competence.
- **Sera Quill:** distrustful of shortcuts, protective of the Spine 7 crews, unwilling to let the public network be used as laboratory equipment.
- **Lena and Nell Senn:** the family is warm and the silences around the network are a wall.
- **Tam Arrick:** a classmate Marek knows as a reliable pair of hands on a ladder, not yet a person with a stake in the story.

## Batch midpoint (Chapters 6–7)

Talia stops Marek using Tam's practice without a durable share, and he learns that his ability has been running on borrowed material. He asks for a bounded comparison instead, watches her perform a brace that has a real boundary-release, and then goes to the Institute store room in the dark and counts the crates. The omission is in every plate, the card's boundary label has no performed check, and the bundle's upstream licensor field is blank. The problem is a product, not a failure of his hands or his student.

## Batch climax (Chapter 8)

A courier loading dock on the Saltmarket waterfront. Tam's release is cue-locked and he is stranded on a wet dock with a shifting load. Marek says Rusk's cadence himself to stop the fall, hears what he has become, refuses to finish that way, and pays for it: Tam's forearm is cut to two tendons. The worst shame in the volume is not the failed exam. It is the sound of his own voice inside someone else's dependency.

## Aftermath (Chapters 9–10)

The Institute edits the drill record, locks the plates instead of quarantining them, and offers Marek a technician's apprenticeship and a clean transcript in exchange for a hardware-fault explanation. He refuses, copies the paper record, and gives a copy to someone outside the Institute. Then he goes to the Saltmarket Exchange and finds that a courier's balance step carries the same fourth-beat gap, which means the Institute's kit is not the only place the omission lives.

## Required character changes

- Marek goes from isolated to obligated. He cannot go back to being a failing student with a private complaint, because he is now the reason a classmate cannot finish a technique alone.
- He learns that a share is not free and not private. He is made to tell Tam what he did, in person, and to accept a smaller permission than he wanted.
- He learns that his instinct to take responsibility before being asked is a hazard, not a virtue. Talia names it in Chapter 6; Chapter 8 proves it.
- He moves from exposure to method. By Chapter 10 he is thinking in lineages, scopes, and returns instead of in wins and losses.
- He resents Talia for the rest of the evening and then becomes one of the few people who tells her something true.
- Rafi's yes is established early and honestly, so that his later dependence is his own fault and not the reader's surprise.
- Davan Rusk is established as a decent man inside a bad procedure, so his protection by the department is a fact about institutions rather than a fact about cowards.

## Required power changes

- Stage 0 throughout, with three concrete demonstrations rather than an increase in capability.
- Chapter 3: first deliberate two-source line, roughly four seconds, unrepeatable. It yields the shape of the missing beat and a shared aftertaste Marek did not ask for.
- Chapter 4: attention debt introduced as a tracked physical condition. Marek begins writing his load down by hand, a habit that persists for the volume.
- Chapter 6: a real consent cost, not a power cost. He loses a source of comparison.
- Chapter 7: he can now name what is missing, which does not help him fix anything. Naming is not capability.
- Chapter 8: he produces a working non-hinged release exactly once, at a price, and only because the injured person's own body supplied the boundary check. He cannot repeat it on demand.
- Chapter 10: three independent lines are visible in the city. He has directly compared only one.

## Required world reveals

- The Institute's manual shows revision 3-08. The kit is 4-19. Nobody updated the manual and nobody asked.
- A hinge is not a missing boundary label. The card still names a boundary, but the performed check is gone. The first batch preserves the instructor script as an artifact and establishes the absence; Chapter 25 is the first scene to connect that script to design approval and revision history, making the substitution deliberate.
- Threadline assembled and sold the bundle. The upstream licensor field is blank, so the vendor is a conduit and not the author. This is planted in Chapter 7 and left unanswered for the whole volume.
- Silence, proximity, and a pre-signed school form are not agreement. This is stated in Chapter 6 and demonstrated in Chapter 9, when the Institute's own consent posture is exposed.
- The same omission exists in work techniques the Institute never supplied.
- Sera recognizes the signature in a Marrow Falls maintenance log as work from her past and refuses to discuss it. She does not identify the signature as Iona's authorship or say the name aloud anywhere in Volume 01.

## System usage in this batch

- Chapter 3: one short panel, during the first two-source line. Sparse, ambiguous, no solution.
- Chapter 7: one short panel, showing agreement and absence. It explicitly does not conclude.
- No other panel. Chapters 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, and 10 must run on bodies, tools, rooms, and paperwork.

## Open questions after the batch

1. Who licensed the 4-19 bundle, and why is the licensor field blank rather than wrong?
2. Why does a courier's balance step carry the same gap when no school supplied it?
3. Can Tam finish a technique without a voice, or has the dependence become permanent?
4. What does the Institute do when the kit is locked but not quarantined and two satellite sites plus Talia's clinic still use it?
5. Will Talia's bounded share hold, or will Marek push it into something larger?
6. What is in the Marrow Falls log that Sera will not read aloud?
7. The provisional witnessed assessment is one week away at the end of Chapter 10 and requires a scored release that begins and completes with no substituted cue used, followed, or accepted. Marek has one accidental non-hinged release from Chapter 8, but it follows a moment when he voices Rusk's cadence before refusing it, so it does not satisfy the no-cue requirement. The Institute later withdraws the provisional date.

### Chapter 0001 — "Night Shift Ledger"

POV: Marek

Location/time: Civic Spine 7 repair hub, Lattice Ward, Tuesday of week 1, 01:40

Immediate goal: Clear the night's queue before the 05:00 relay swap so he can get four hours and still make the 09:00 practical review.

Resistance: The third Threadline practice plate this month has a scorched return-path contact. Sera wants it logged as a vendor fault and signed out. The Institute buys from Threadline, and a student who files a fault report against Institute equipment pays for it in the transcript. His own body is also against him: he skipped dinner, and his shoulder is wrong from a courier job he took at six. The 09:00 review is the third item on a list he is failing.

Information revealed: His work life, his real competence, and his habit of counting exits. Lena's night shift at the residential clinic, Nell asking for money and getting silence, rent as a live problem. Evan's scratched transit sequence card lives in his tool roll and gets used as a shim to hold a plate square. The Solo Seal date: Thursday of week 4. The Institute's kit order, revision 4-19, against a manual still printed at 3-08.

Action or decision: He repairs the plate instead of signing it out, and writes the fault in his own hand on the back of the form, which is where warnings go when there is no room on the front. Then he goes home and sleeps four hours instead of working the extra hour, which is the first time in the volume he chooses a limit over a gain.

Emotional change: Small, private pride in the repair, immediately followed by the knowledge that being good with tools has never once converted into a license.

Power or resource change: Stage 0 established. He can see the plate's damage as a shape that does not close, and looking at it makes him mildly sick. Noticing is physical, not intellectual. No capability gained.

Continuity fact: Evan's card has four beats and a blank fifth. It is a tool in this volume, never a clue the reader can solve. Revision 4-19 shipped four months after 3-08 with no changelog.

Ending type: discovery

Next-chapter pull: The 4-19 revision is exactly the revision the practicum will drill with on Thursday morning.

### Chapter 0002 — "The Practicum"

POV: Marek

Location/time: Nacre Institute, practical room two, Thursday of week 1, 09:20

Immediate goal: Get through the team rescue drill without a mark on his record, and get it over with.

Resistance: Tam Arrick is on the ladder above him and would rather be anywhere else. The kit is 4-19. Rusk runs the drill from a script and rewards exact cue timing, because exact cue timing is what the record rewards. Marek has slept four hours, his shoulder is bad, and he is the only person in the room who is frightened.

Information revealed: The drill itself, the room, the brace as a physical object — a mineral-fiber plate, a copper return contact, a strap that has to be tightened in a specific order. Rusk's teaching voice, which is genuinely good. And the thing that changes the book: the fourth beat has a name in the script, spoken aloud in sequence, and nothing in the technique that answers it.

Action or decision: Marek runs the sequence correctly on the first pass, which nobody expected, including him. Then Tam's ladder slips, and he hears the count in his own head arrive at a place where nothing follows.

Emotional change: Competence curdles into dread. For the first time his ability tells him something his instructors do not want to hear, and he cannot tell whether it is real or the sleep deprivation talking.

Power or resource change: Stage 0 in use. The noticing produces a physical response — metallic taste, a two-second inability to name the color of the floor — and he has to hold the wall to stay upright.

Continuity fact: Rusk is not a villain. He is a certified instructor whose standing depends on the approved answer, and he has never had cause to doubt it.

Ending type: reversal

Next-chapter pull: The gap is about two seconds wide and a person is falling into it.

### Chapter 0003 — "Four Beats"

POV: Marek

Location/time: Same room, seconds later

Immediate goal: Stop Tam's shoulder from taking the fall.

Resistance: He holds no license that lets him do this. He offers Rafi, on the ground team, a share in under four seconds without explaining what it costs, and Rafi says yes because somebody is falling and Marek asked. Rusk's voice arrives on schedule with the fourth beat. And the only completion available to Marek in the next two seconds is Rusk's own.

Information revealed: What a deliberate share is, spoken badly and fast. That two sources can make a line that neither could make. That the interface says one short thing and then stops helping.

Action or decision: He asks Rafi for use only in a single sentence and gets a line for about four seconds. It shows him the shape of the missing beat. He finishes the release himself, on Rusk's timing, and catches Tam. Then his own anchor goes and the line collapses, leaving him with a shape he cannot reproduce, a headache, and a piece of Tam's terror that does not feel like his.

System panel (one, sparse): Two practices agree. One beat is not answered. Do you offer the next step? Nothing follows it.

Emotional change: The first time usefulness feels like relief instead of proof. He is twenty-two and for four seconds he was not a problem.

Power or resource change: First two-source line. Roughly four seconds, unrepeatable, and trying to reproduce it makes it worse. Use only; no remember, no derive. Borrowed adrenaline, shared fear, metallic taste.

Continuity fact: The line collapses when Marek loses his own anchor. A link he cannot hold alone is not a tool he can practice.

Ending type: cost

Next-chapter pull: Tam's shoulder will be fine. His release will not.

### Chapter 0004 — "Aftertaste"

POV: Marek

Location/time: Student housing, then Civic Spine 7, that night

Immediate goal: Get back to ordinary work and keep the shape he saw in Chapter 3.

Resistance: The shape will not hold still. He cannot name a screwdriver for ninety seconds. He writes down the fourth beat three times and gets three different beats. Rafi is cheerfully unhelpful and wants to do it again. Sera tells him plainly that a return path is a physical maintenance job and not an idea, and refuses to let him put the Spine 7 relay on a test line, which means he cannot reproduce his four seconds at work either.

Information revealed: The cost of a share, named in body terms. Sera's history of refusing to make the public system into a shortcut. The difference between noticing something and knowing it. And the fact that he has twice told Talia he cannot come to the clinic, which was a decision he made for reasons he has not examined. On the Spine 7 wall, among the handwritten maintenance notes, he finds a fourteen-year-old Marrow Falls log with the phrase "last approved civic sequence" and a signature he cannot place; Sera sees him reading it and refuses to discuss it.

Action or decision: He stops. He gives the shape up for the night instead of testing it at his bench, and he does not test it on the Spine's equipment even though that is the only equipment that would let him try. He copies the Marrow Falls line into his notebook without asking Sera what it means; she tells him not to ask. He begins writing his attention debt down by hand.

Emotional change: Fear of being a burden arrives well ahead of any evidence for it. The shame is not about the drill; it is that four people saw him hesitate.

Power or resource change: Attention debt becomes a tracked physical condition, logged by hand. No gain. A real limit, established on the page.

Continuity fact: Sera recognizes the signature in a Marrow Falls log and will not discuss it. Do not name it aloud anywhere in this volume.

Ending type: cost

Next-chapter pull: The next three weeks are ordinary shifts and failed attempts; the original seal run is on Thursday of week 4, and the fourth beat is the last thing on the card.

### Chapter 0005 — "The Seal Run"

POV: Marek

Location/time: Institute exam hall, Thursday of week 4 (the originally scheduled Solo Seal run)

Immediate goal: Pass the Solo Seal and stop the scholarship review from opening.

Resistance: The seal is scored on a solo run with an instructor present, which means Rusk's voice is part of the scoring conditions and not part of the technique. The exam office's rule treats a missing beat as a student's failure until proven otherwise. Declining the cue in front of three examiners is not a private act; it is a public statement by a student with two failed practicals and no allies with power.

Information revealed: What the Solo Seal does and does not confer — it proves solo competence and grants no permission to work with partners. The exam office's actual language. And the reason refusal costs him everything at once: the seal is the scholarship, the transcript, and the apprenticeship in one piece of paper.

Action or decision: He performs the first three beats correctly. He reaches the fourth, declines the substituted cue, and attempts the honest release alone. His hand reaches the load, but the boundary check never becomes an action and the run ends incomplete. The exam office records: incomplete fourth beat, dependent on external cue, even though he declined the cue. He does not receive the seal.

Emotional change: Shame first, then a lightness that is not pride — the first taste of having chosen a rule over a reward, and the immediate understanding that he has chosen it in public.

Power or resource change: No capability. A cost, and a new constraint: he now needs a second route to a license, and the institution has given him one, with conditions.

Continuity fact: He is offered a witnessed assessment, with Thursday of week 7 proposed as a provisional date and the scholarship suspended until it happens. This is the assessment the volume climaxes on; the Institute later withdraws the provisional date, adds a demonstration requirement, and schedules a new date only after the term review. The final assessment is set five days after Chapter 40. He will not hold a Working Share before Chapter 49.

Ending type: decision

Next-chapter pull: The assessment requires proof that the fourth beat is missing, not proof that he failed to produce one.

### Chapter 0006 — "Not a Keepsake"

POV: Marek

Location/time: Talia's mobile clinic, Saltmarket route, Tuesday of week 5, evening

Immediate goal: Get one second independent observation of a working boundary-release, so he can show a board that the kit is incomplete rather than that he is.

Resistance: He arrives carrying Tam's practiced release inside him as an echo he never asked to keep and did not know he had. Talia stops him in front of a patient, in plain words, in front of the patient: he has Tam's finish and Tam never gave anyone a keepsake. He argues that it saved a life. She does not soften it, and then she has to explain to the patient why the nurse is not using the man on the table as a reference.

Information revealed: Use, remember, and derive as three different permissions. What a written scope is and why the patient is owed one. Silence and proximity are not agreement, and a pre-signed school facilities form can authorize equipment handling without authorizing a share. Talia's actual work, and the fact that her clinic stocks bundles from the same revision family, which is why this matters to her and not only to him.

Action or decision: He apologizes badly, and does the thing that costs him: he goes to Tam, in person, tells him exactly what he did, and asks whether Tam is willing to have it remembered. Tam allows the completed rescue as use only, refuses any future use or retention, and says never again; the aftertaste Marek already carries cannot be erased. Then Talia lays the Institute's pre-signed facilities form beside the patient's chart and, standing close enough to share, asks the patient to name the purpose and limits. The patient grants only a two-performance comparison. Talia offers a bounded alternative — she will perform her brace twice in front of him, use and remember only, no derive, on a written scope with a date and a review, and she keeps a copy. At the clinic door, Marek tells her that Tam's fear is still in him, a truth he has offered no one; the disclosure is not warmth, and Talia keeps the written scope between them.

Emotional change: He resents her for the rest of the evening and then tells her something true about the aftertaste he is still carrying; he realizes she is the only person who has told him the truth about what he did, including the part he did not know.

Power or resource change: No capability and one lost source. He is now able to compare honestly, and only because someone made him pay for the comparison.

Continuity fact: Talia keeps a copy of every scope she writes. The correction is not softened and is not rewarded with warmth in this chapter.

Ending type: reversal

Next-chapter pull: Two performances of a brace with a real boundary-release, and a question of what the difference will look like.

Immediate goal: Compare Talia's brace against the kit and find the difference.

Resistance: The interface will show him a possible shape and will not conclude anything. He has two observations, one of them from a nurse trained in the same family of courses he was, so the agreement he is hoping for may be manufactured. His own wanting keeps pressing the two sources together until they look like evidence.

Information revealed: The structural difference. Talia's brace performs its boundary as an action, at an observable point, and the learner finishes without an outside cue. The available teaching material shows the gap, but not the approval trail or revision history that would make the substitution deliberate.

System panel (one, sparse): Two practices agree. One boundary is not performed. It gives no conclusion and no next step, and the silence has to be worked in.

Action or decision: He goes to the Institute store in the dark, opens the crate, counts every plate, photographs the bundle label and the empty licensor field, and leaves the crate locked exactly as he found it. He copies the count by hand because he does not trust a photograph to survive a week.

Emotional change: The dread becomes specific and cold. This is not his competence, his teacher, or his school. It is a product that people signed for.

Power or resource change: Stage 0 with evidence attached. He can now say what is missing, which does not let him fix anything, supply anything, or get a license.

Continuity fact: Threadline assembled and sold this bundle from an unnamed upstream technique bundle. Threadline is not the author, and the blank licensor field must stay unanswered for the whole volume.

Ending type: discovery

### Chapter 0008 — "The Voice He Hates"

POV: Marek

Location/time: A courier loading dock on the Saltmarket waterfront, Tuesday of week 6, after Tam's kitchen shift, 19:20

Immediate goal: Get Tam off the dock safely.

Resistance: Tam's practiced release is cue-locked. He cannot finish a routine brace without Rusk's cadence, and he is standing on a wet dock with a shifting crate and no way to call for help. Marek has the shape of the fourth beat and no way to install it. The real danger is internal: he can supply the cue himself, and part of him wants to, because it would work.

Information revealed: What cue dependence looks like from the outside — not madness, not addiction, just a person whose body waits for a voice that is four hundred kilometers away. And how fast a key-holder can be replaced by somebody who only means well.

Action or decision: He says Rusk's cadence to stop Tam's fall. He hears himself use it, in that rhythm, and he stops. He finishes the release the slow way instead, with Tam's own hand on the crate edge serving as the boundary check; Tam refuses a share and offers only that present physical stop. The crate goes over. Tam's forearm takes it, and two tendons go with it.

Emotional change: The worst shame of the volume. Not the exam, not the transcript, not the money — the sound of his own voice inside somebody else's dependency, which is the thing he has been arguing against since Chapter 2.

Power or resource change: One working non-hinged release, produced once, at a price, and only because the injured person's own body supplied the check. He cannot repeat it on demand and he knows it.

Continuity fact: Tam loses kitchen work for weeks. The lost wages are real and Marek's part in them is real, but not the whole of them. The recovery is long and is not reset later.

Ending type: cost

Next-chapter pull: Tam tells him to stop speaking for him, and the Institute has already rewritten the drill record.

### Chapter 0009 — "What the Record Says"

POV: Marek

Location/time: Institute practical department office and corridors, Wednesday of week 6

Immediate goal: Get the omission into an official record before the term's licensing board meets.

Resistance: The equipment is locked, not quarantined, and the same revision is in use at two satellite sites and at Talia's clinic. Rusk is protected as a certified instructor. The department's facilities form is pre-signed by the Institute, and staff treat a student's silence and physical proximity as permission to handle the observation. The department offers Marek a technician's apprenticeship, a corrected transcript, and help characterizing the failure as a hardware fault, in exchange for keeping it quiet. A Threadline service contractor arrives to service the kit and asks very precise questions about who has opened the crate.

Information revealed: Institutions can be complicit without a villain in them. A record is a physical object that people edit, and the person editing it is usually someone with a reason you can understand. The Institute's pre-signed form covers equipment handling, not a personal share; silence and proximity are not agreement. Threadline's service bulletin number, which is the first thread that can be followed upward.

Action or decision: He refuses the deal. When the contractor reaches for the plate, Marek asks everyone present to state the purpose, duration, and what may remain instead of accepting the pre-signed form; the access note is rewritten as equipment-only, with no personal share. He does not file a public complaint yet, because a complaint now hands the contractor a reason to reissue the plates and lose the crate. Instead he copies the ledger, the crate count, the bundle label, the drill script, and the Marrow Falls maintenance note, and he puts a copy somewhere outside the Institute.

Emotional change: The move from exposure to method. He is frightened, and for the first time he is thinking like someone who intends to last longer than a term.

Power or resource change: Resources, not power: a paper record, a service bulletin number, and one copy outside the building. He still has no license and no standing whatsoever.

Continuity fact: The Institute's pre-signed form covers equipment handling only; it does not authorize a personal share. The Continuity Office arrives as forms this chapter and as a named assessor later. Oren Vey does not appear in person in this movement; his name may be on the invoice.

Ending type: decision

Next-chapter pull: To prove the beat is missing in a form a board will accept, he needs observations from people the Institute never taught.

### Chapter 0010 — "A Different Shoulder"

POV: Marek

Location/time: The Saltmarket Exchange, then a courier's dock and a hospital ward, Thursday of week 6, evening

Immediate goal: Find one honest independent release to compare against, and help Tam start a recovery plan while he is in a hospital bed.

Rafi is the obvious source and says yes before he understands the boundary, which is exactly the habit that will hurt him later in the volume. And Tam, from a hospital bed, tells Marek that the recovery plan will be Tam's to write and asks for an observer who was never taught the kit, before Marek has decided what to say.

Information revealed: Independent practice already exists at the Exchange, at working scale, and it is better than the Institute's version. Rafi's courier balance step has the same missing fourth beat, and he has been compensating for two years by putting a shoulder against a wall, which he calls technique. Dessa's kitchen timing and a fish-market worker's release are visible as separate routines, though neither is offered for formal comparison. Tam's recovery will be a plan he writes himself, based on observations the Institute never taught him.

Action or decision: At the Exchange, Marek first watches Dessa's kitchen timing and a fish-market worker complete a release without an audience. He then follows Rafi to a courier's dock and asks him honestly for the first time — the purpose, the duration, what stays and what goes — and Rafi agrees to use only. Rafi demonstrates the balance step and the fourth beat is simply not there. Marek does not try to fix it. In the hospital ward, Tam begins his own recovery plan and names the observer he wants; Marek writes the observation down and then writes what he does not know at the bottom of the page.

Emotional change: The first time the work feels larger than his own failure, and the first time that is more frightening than the failure, because a large problem cannot be solved by a person being brave.

Power or resource change: Three independent lines are now visible in the city: Rafi's courier balance step, Dessa's kitchen timing, and a fish-market worker's release. He has observed all three, but has directly compared only Rafi's. No capability gain. The shape of the entire volume's work is suddenly on a single page.

Continuity fact: Dessa refuses to let her kitchen practices be turned into a standard. Rafi's balance step carries the same omission, and he is its first victim rather than its opponent.

Ending type: earned hook

Next-chapter pull: The provisional assessment is one week away, the kit is locked but not quarantined, and nobody inside the Institute can supply a release that works without accepting a voice.
