## Logline

Four days of weather nobody in this case can explain arrive in a city that has spent fourteen years arguing about a printed rule, and the only person in it who can hold a large thing together is findable at a door — so within nine days he is named on a document, which is the last thing he wanted and the first thing in six volumes anybody has been able to give him.

## Central pressure

Four layers. The first three are load-bearing and the fourth is the volume.

- **Immediate pressure: a practice that finishes early.** Across unrelated buildings, unrelated crafts and unrelated people, practices that have run the same way for years are ending before the person doing them is ready, and a lift in Nine Bridges stops between floors it has never stopped between. **Nobody dies in the first ten chapters and nothing is reported as a fault by anybody.** The finding is that there is no fault: the thing is happening in kitchens and stairwells and lift shafts and a clinic and a training room, and the correct action — a competent person investigating a competent person's work — changes nothing, because there is nothing broken.
- **Structural pressure: the emergency order, and the name on it.** A Continuity Office writes an instrument, in the shape this manuscript gives such things, with a count and a reason, and the instrument names one person as the point of coordination for a resonance event. **He did not ask, and the paper is not wrong, and the cost of the paper is that a person can now be asked about him, and about four hundred competent people in this city can invoke a printed rule about somebody who has not come back yet, and one of them is going to use his name in the next four days.**
- **The private pressure: the word, and the middle.** Nine, ten and eleven a minute, uncorrected, said out loud once to one person, not being reported. **The pressure in this volume is not the word. It is that the volume's engine is a man who is offered the middle and takes it, and a woman of twenty-four who will not let him, and the man who took the middle was right about the arithmetic.** Being right is the failure and it is a new failure for him: in six volumes his flaw has been taking responsibility before anybody asked. **Nobody asked this time. Somebody wrote it down and he said yes.**
- **The volume's own discovery, and it is the stone it lays for Volume 08 without spending it: *a route that works through one person is not a slower version of a route that works through a council, it is a different practice with the same results, and the results are better in the first hour and worse in the fourth year, and in the fourth year nobody can tell you who chose it, because the person who chose it was working at speed and the people who did not choose it were not asked, and they were not asked because they were slower.* The Return Path is required and is not repealed in this volume. The volume builds the smallest thing available against a single answer — a chain of people who can each say no at a door, a set of local practices, and a woman who will not be on a telephone — and it discovers that all three of those are about authority and not about power, and that authority is the thing this city has never had to give away.**
