# Chapter 694 — A Friday, A Bench Until Two And A Counter Until About Six, A Sixth Room Whose Sentence Is Six Words Long And Which Does Not Know That, A Seventh Room That Was Not Asked Anything And Asked About Itself, And A Man Of Twenty-Two Who Was Asked Which Of Them Was Right And Who Said That Nobody Had Asked

**A Friday was a bench until two, a counter until about six, and four rooms off that service road from about six. This one is two rooms, the shortest sentence anybody has said this week and a longer one that was not asked for, and a room that turned the question round and asked about itself instead of answering.**

---

Ten dated jobs went out on that Friday and ten people came over the counter for them, the last of them at half past four. At ten the shutter came down and shook the cage for the thirty-first time in eleven years.

**Those four rooms off that service road stand at one thousand two hundred and seventy-one days, a hundred and eighty-one weeks and four days; four of them and one behind the three, warm. The card on end in the rail by that door is one thousand two hundred and seventy-five days, a hundred and eighty-two weeks and one day, four days past them, and the rail takes two. The nineteenth line on that board of two nails is nine hundred and sixty-seven days, a hundred and thirty-eight weeks and one day.**

The hardboard's sixteenth is one thousand and sixty-one, a hundred and fifty-one weeks and four days, and the hold on nine crates in a yard in the second of the four towns is nine hundred and four, a hundred and twenty-nine weeks and one day.

Six hundred and fifty-one days is the separation on this Friday, and that is ninety-three weeks to the day. It was shut in a one-line box in a form about twelve weeks ago, it has been walked every day since, it is not shorter, and nothing has gone back into it.

---

**He was in the sixth of the nine rooms from about nine until about half past nine and it is a first-floor room in a first district above a firm that repairs office chairs, and there are two people in it, a man of about forty-five and a woman of about twenty-three, and the man of about forty-five did the talking.**

"**One sentence,**" Marek said.
"**In your words.**"

The woman of about twenty-three said, "**Go on then,**" before he had finished the sentence, and he started again.

"**What we get, we send back,**" the man of about forty-five said.

Nobody said anything.

"**That is it.**"

"**That is six words,**" Marek said.

"**It is six words,**" the man said, "**and I know how many they are because I have been saying them about eleven years, and it is not a short answer to your question, it is the only sentence this room has ever had, and I would write it on the door if there was somebody coming who needed reading.**"

"**That is fine,**" Marek said.

"**It is not fine,**" the man of about forty-five said.
"**It is six words and I am sixty in a fortnight and there is a woman of about twenty-three here who is on a Thursday and a Friday and I do not know what this room says on a Saturday.**"

"**Nobody has asked me what any of the nine rooms says on a Saturday,**" Marek said.

"**You have now.**"

"**And I am not going to answer it,**" Marek said, "**because I have got about six of the nine in my head and none of them has been written down and I am not going to be the man who starts comparing them on a Friday afternoon in a first district.**"

He wrote nothing down and the woman of about twenty-three looked at him the whole time to see whether he was going to and he was not.

---

**He was in the seventh of the nine rooms from about half past eleven until about ten past twelve and it is one room in a third district with a door onto a yard, and it has about nine people in it on an ordinary day and there were four.**

"**I am not asking you for a sentence,**" Marek said.
"**I have been in six rooms and I am not going to be in seven in a week. Go back to work.**"

"**No,**" said a woman of about forty-eight.
"**You have come into this room, so you are going to be asked something, and I have decided now that it is going to be me.**"

"**Ask.**"

"**Is there a thing we are supposed to be doing that we have not been doing.**"

---

**And he answered it in about nine seconds and the answer was true and it was not the answer to the question.**

"**No,**" he said.

"**Right,**" said the woman of about forty-eight, "**then we are doing the thing.**"

"**That is not a sequence,**" he said.
"**You cannot go from that to that.**"

"**We go from that to that every day,**" she said, "**and there are four of us in here today and there are nine on a Thursday and none of us have ever been told to do anything and the ones of us who have been told to do something since the spring have been told by somebody in a different room.**"

"**That is not the same as a sentence.**"

"**It is the sentence,**" she said, and then stopped, and about four people in that room did not look up.

"**Say it again slower.**"

"**No,**" she said.
"**You asked me for one thing and I have given you two and I am not going to give you the second one twice.**"

---

**And she came out into the yard after him about four minutes later, on her own, and stood about nine feet off and did not come closer than that.**

"**You are going to say it to the other eight.**"

"**I am not going to say it to any of the other eight,**" Marek said.
"**They have not been asked what any of the others said and they are not going to be. That is the only thing I have actually got to keep and I am not going to break it over a thing you said to a room with four people in it.**"

"**Then it will be the only one anybody has said twice.**"

"**It will be the only one anybody has said twice,**" Marek said, "**and nobody will know that, including you, in about four years.**"

She said that was the best of the seven answers he had given anybody that week and went in, and the door shut, and **she was not thanked and she did not ask to be and there is nobody in this city entitled to thank her.**

---

**And what happened next, four minutes after that, is on this page because it happened and because it was a refusal with a name on it.**

**A man of about thirty-three was in that yard.** He was not one of the four.
He had come back for a box of strapping that had been left there in the spring and had been there ever since and had been on a list of about nine items in about four different places in this city, and it is not the first item on any of those four lists and it is not the eleventh.

He came through the door and stopped when he saw the two of them standing in it.

"**Is this a private bit.**"

"**It's a yard,**" Marek said.

"**Right,**" said the man of about thirty-three.

**Nobody asked him what he was doing in the yard and nobody asked him what was in the box and he carried the box out to the gate and put it down on the pavement and went back in for his coat, and he did not look at either of them again and he did not ask, and four minutes later he came out with his coat on and shut the door.**

**Nobody in this city knows that a man stood in that yard on that Friday and that nobody asked him anything, and there is no line anywhere for it and there is not going to be one, and he has not been back to that yard and the box of strapping has not gone back in and it is still on the pavement.**

---

**So the seventh room has got a sentence and nobody has put a number on it and the woman of about forty-eight said the word sentence out loud before she had finished saying it, and did not know for nine seconds afterwards that she had given one, and nobody has attached a number to it and nobody is going to.**

He has got seven.
Two of them are in towns a day and a night of bus travel away, and one of the two he has not arranged for anybody to go to, and the other one is the one he sent a woman of about thirty-six to with a card in a car park.
**He has got six words from a room with a man of about forty-five and a woman of about twenty-three and a sentence of some other length from a room with four people in it on a Friday, and he has been in this stretch of days as long as they have, and no person in this city has compared them, and no person in this city has asked him to, and he has not compared them either.**

---

**At about twenty past twelve a man of about thirty-six came in and asked which of the sentences was the correct one.**

"**No,**" Marek said.

"**There's got to be a correct one.**"

"**There is not, and no room in this city has asked for one, and the moment one of them asks for one the other eight will find out that one of them has been picked out and they will stop,**" Marek said.
"**So no. Nobody is going to be told which one is right and I am not going to be the man who starts choosing.**"

"**Then what's it for.**"

"**It's for having said it,**" Marek said.
"**I know that is a bad answer at a counter. It is the answer and there is not a better one.**"

The man of about thirty-six said that was fair and bought four feet of chain and paid eleven pounds for it and has not asked anybody else in this city and did not ask Marek a second question.

---

**The rest of the counter.**

At about half past twelve a woman of about forty-five brought in a lamp with a switch on the cord and was told in four seconds that it is the switch.

**At about half past one a man of about forty-eight came in with a print he had done himself and asked whether it was worth doing them himself. Marek said that it was worth doing them if he was going to do nine and it was not worth doing them if he was going to do one, and asked how many, and the man said four.**

"**Then keep four in a drawer,**" Marek said, "**and take them out on a day you have not chosen yet and not on a day you have.**"

"**And if somebody rings.**"

"**Then you were out that day,**" Marek said, "**and that is not a reason not to, it is the reason, and the man who tells you otherwise wants you to believe there is a day that is safe.**"

The man of about forty-eight said that he had not been told that by anybody, and paid for nine feet of cord, and has not been back, and **there is no arrangement in this city for telling a man with four prints in a drawer which day to take them out on and nobody is going to build one.**

At about two a man of about twenty-two was charged four pounds for four minutes, which is more than four minutes is on the sheet, and he said so, and Marek said it was because the man had brought it on the bus and the bus was late, and the man said right and paid it.

At about five a woman of about thirty-one asked whether a room could say a sentence out loud to a person who was not collecting sentences, and was told in four seconds that it could and that this is what happened to four of them this week.

---

**He noticed three things on that Friday and not one of them was on a form.** Six words in a room above a firm that repairs office chairs, spoken by a man of about forty-five who has been saying them about eleven years and knows exactly how many there are.
**A smell of vinegar in a third-district room where nobody keeps vinegar and has never kept vinegar and where the four people in it on a Friday do not remark on it.** And a door onto a yard with the latch side worn shiny on the outside only, which means it is opened from the yard more than it is opened from the room.

---

**He wanted, for the whole of that Friday, to be told which of the nine was wrong.**

**Nobody told him and nobody was going to and he had told a man of about thirty-six at four o'clock that nobody would be, four seconds before he wanted it, which is the second time this week he has said the correct thing out loud and then wanted the other one.**

He said none of it.
Ten dated jobs, ten names, the shutter at ten, and the walk home at ten past ten with nothing in his hands and nothing in his head that he had not been given.

---

**A book on a table at the top of a stair in Saltmarket stands at sixty-four lines and did not change on this Friday, and the tin beside it is on seventy-three with its lid on, and a week is not long enough for anybody to have gone up that stair on purpose, and this is the fourth day this week with no sitting in it and none of the four announces anything.**

**From half past six until ten the fourth of those four rooms off that service road was not empty, and the one other person in it is about thirty. Nobody put a word to her for three and a half hours. Nobody has apologised to her in any form and nobody ever will. There is a page with her name at the head of it, the eighth of eight, on a back shelf in a ring binder, and it did not come out.**
