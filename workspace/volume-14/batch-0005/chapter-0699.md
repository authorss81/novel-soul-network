**The woman of about fifty-two who chairs nine chairs came into that shop at about half past ten and she was on the counter for about four minutes and she is not on any list and she was not thanked.**

"**There's more doors with that sheet on them than there is anybody in a box on it,**" she said.
"**That is the whole of it and I have said it out loud twice since the spring and I am saying it a third time to you because you are the only person who has ever asked me a question about a sheet and did not want it turned into something.**"

"**I'm not going to do anything with it.**"

"**Why not sent.**"

"**Because a person who is sent to find somebody is a person who has been given a route,**" she said.
"**And a street that has been handed a route about four houses is a street that has lost the only thing that was protecting it, and about four hundred people who have not been sent anywhere are the only reason anybody in those four houses is still in those four houses.**"

**And then she said the other half of it in nine seconds and then she stopped talking about it.**
