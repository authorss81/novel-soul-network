**At eleven a man of about forty-four who works for a district council came in, and he had a piece of paper with a copy of about nine words on it, and he put it on that counter face up and said, "**Is that yours,**" to Talia Venn, and she said it was.**

He waited, and about four seconds went.

"**Can I ask why.**"

**At ten past five a man of about forty-one came in with a folder and put it on that counter and left it there.**

He runs operations for a firm that keeps up most of the equipment between this city and the coast. **He is commercial pressure and he is not the villain of anything and he is not owed anything and nobody thanked him.**

"**Nine units in a row on a wide road,**" he said. "**Two shop units and seven that are not shops and have been empty about four years. They come due and somebody will want somebody to keep them standing, and that is a year's work for a man on his own and about eleven weeks for two.**"

"**No.**"

"**You have not heard the figure.**"

"**I do not need the figure.**"

"**The figure is why I am standing here,**" Oren Vey said, "**and I am not going to pretend to you that it is not. Here is what I actually came to say, and I have said it to about four people and I am saying it to you because you are the fourth one who has said no and I want the number of noes on this to be on the table somewhere.**"

"**Go on.**"

"**Four things in this city have failed in a fortnight, in four places, and there is a trade in that and it is not a dirty trade,**" he said. "**There are about nine people in this city who have noticed it already and four of them have put a van on it. I am one of the four. I am not asking you to come and work for me. "**Right.**"

"**That is not a refusal, that is a person who has heard something,**" he said, and picked the folder up and left with it.

**He did not take the coffee that is on that counter for anybody who asks for one, and nobody asked him, and he said on the way out that he never takes it, and Marek said that four people in this city know that and about forty more do not, and Oren Vey said he did, and went.**
