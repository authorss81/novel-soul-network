# Chapter 688 — A Friday, A Bench Until Two And A Counter Until About Six, Four Rooms Off A Service Road From About Half Past Six, A First Floor In A Second District From About Four Until About Half Past Six, Four People At A Table And A Fifth Standing, A Record That Was On That Same Table On The Friday Nine Weeks Ago In Front Of Those Same Four People, And A Man Of Institutional Records Who Is Not A Hero And Who Gave Nothing Away

**A Friday was a bench until two, a counter until about six, and a first floor in a second district from about four until about half past six. This one is four people at a table, a record, and the fact that it was never hidden.**

---

Ten jobs carried a date against each on the Friday and ten people came over the counter, the last of them let in at ten past five, and the shutter came down at ten and shook the cage for the twenty-fifth time in eleven years.

**Those four rooms off that service road, four of them and one behind the three, are warm, and they are one thousand two hundred and fifty-seven days, a hundred and seventy-nine weeks and four days. The card on end in the rail by that door is one thousand two hundred and sixty-one days, a hundred and eighty weeks and one day, four days past them, and the rail takes two.**

The nineteenth line on that board of two nails is nine hundred and fifty-three days, a hundred and thirty-six weeks and one day. The hardboard's sixteenth is one thousand and forty-seven, a hundred and forty-nine weeks and four days, and the hold on nine crates in a yard in the second of the four towns is eight hundred and ninety, a hundred and twenty-seven weeks and one day.

The separation is six hundred and thirty-seven days, ninety-one weeks to the day. It was shut in a one-line box in a form about twelve weeks ago and it has been walked every day since and it is not shorter and nothing has been put back into it.

---

**The day was a day and the nine dated jobs were done and two of them were not, and that is the whole of the counter.**

A woman of about thirty-nine brought a gate in and left it leaning against the shopfront for about half an hour and came back for it, and apologised, and was told not to.

**A man of about thirty-four wanted to talk about a bill from a firm that maintains equipment between this city and the coast and Marek said in four seconds that he did not do their work and did not know what they charged, and the man said that was fine and had come to talk anyway, and talked for about nine minutes about a valve, and left.**

At one o'clock a sheet came over that counter with a mark in a box at the bottom of it, and Marek looked at the mark and did not look at it long, and put it in the drawer under the bench. **The drawer is shut and there are four sheets in it and nobody has ever looked at the fourth, and the fourth has been in there since about the spring.**

**At about twenty to three a man of about thirty-nine came in and said he had heard there was a list going round and asked whether this shop had one, and was told in four seconds that this shop holds four sheets in a drawer under a bench and that nobody has looked at the fourth, and the man said that is not an answer, and Marek said it is the only one there is.**

**And at about twenty past one a woman of about forty-two brought in a length of a pipe that had come apart in a joint and asked whether the joint could be made again, and it could, and it was made again, and she asked Marek what had happened to the joint, and he said that the joint had been held together for about nine years by something that is not a joint, and she said right, and paid, and went, and has not been back.**

---

**There were five chairs at that table and four people at it and Marek did not sit down.**

A man of about thirty-eight who keeps a warehouse. A man of about fifty-two. A woman of about forty-five. A man of about thirty-one. And **Iven Sore, who keeps the records and is not a villain and is not on anything and is owed nothing and is not thanked.**

At four he put a folder on the table. **It is about two inches thick and the corner of a page is sticking out of it and the corner is soft rather than bent, which means it has been in and out of that folder about nine hundred times.**

"**That is the nine weeks,**" he said. "**Go to the back of it.**"

---

**They went to the back of it. It is a list, made by somebody, in nine hands, and it does not say anything is going to happen.**

It says what could stop working. **It has four headings and under the first of them it has a building on a road where the buses turn and a line about one size of a pad. Under the second it has a bridge on a wide road and a line about a parapet and about nine feet of a seam. Under the third it has a yard in a third district and a line about a Sunday and about a driver who has a lorry. Under the fourth it has a length of road and a line about a man with a handcart and a gate.**

**Nobody in that room said the words *we should have*, and nobody said the word *forecast*, and nobody said anything at all for about a minute and a half.**

Then the man of about fifty-two said: "**What is this for now.**"

"**Nothing,**" Iven Sore said. "**That is what is for now. That was for the Friday of nine weeks ago and the four of you were at that table on the Friday of nine weeks ago and it was the second sheet from the bottom then as well, and it was not locked and it was not marked and I did not take it off the table.**"

"**Who was on it then.**"

"**The four of you.**"

"**Did anybody else see it.**"

"**About nine people saw it,**" he said, "**and all nine of them had been asked to see it.**"

**And the man of about thirty-eight who keeps a warehouse asked the question that four people at that kind of table always ask, which was whether it had been minuted. Iven Sore said that it had, and that the minute of it is a page in the same folder, and that the page with the minute on it is the page in front of the one they had just read, and nobody turned back and looked at it and nobody asked him to.**

---

**And then he said the thing that is the whole of this Friday, and he said it in nine seconds and he said it once and he did not say it twice.**

"**A thing that was on a table in front of four people nine weeks ago cannot be exposed,**" he said. "**It was never hidden. There is nothing to uncover and nobody to uncover it with and no one in this room did anything wrong by keeping it where it was, and that is worse and it is the only thing I have come here to say, and I am not going to give anybody the folder.**"

**He put his hand flat on it and said that it stays in the room and goes back in a drawer on Monday, and it was on the table from ten past four until about half past six and in that time nobody copied anything out of it and nobody photographed it and nobody asked him to.**

Nobody thanked him. Nobody thanked anybody. **He gave nothing away, he is owed nothing, he is not a hero, he is not on a list, and about four people in this city will hear about that Friday and none of them from him, which is the thing he said he wanted and nobody checked whether he wanted it.**

---

**Marek did not ask a question. He had two and he kept both and one of them was the question and the other one was a name.**

The name was a woman of about thirty-one who supplies food and heat to people waiting between shifts, and he did not say it, because **saying it would have put a person on a table that has four people at it, and there is no chair and there is no hour and the sentence that goes with a name like that in that room is *so what did you want her to do*, and there is no answer to that which is not an instruction.**

---

**He noticed four things on that Friday and not one of them was on a form.** **A folder with a soft corner on the front sheet, which is nine hundred goes in and out of the same place.** Five chairs pushed in at five different angles, four of which are the angles of people who sit there and one of which is not. **A pen put down on that table with the cap off and left that way for two hours.** And a hand laid flat on a folder the way a hand is laid on a book, which does not open anything and which four people watched.

---

**He wanted, for the whole of that Friday, to be at a table where four people read a thing that was on that table nine weeks ago, and where nobody makes a speech about it.**

That is a table in a first floor in a second district and there are about nine of those in this city and he has never been at one and there is no way to be at one. **What he got instead was a standing place and a silence of about a minute and a half and a sentence in nine seconds from a man who has said nothing else in eleven years that anybody has written down, and it was the right sentence, and it changed nothing for anybody who was in the room.**

**And then, at about half past five, the man of about fifty-two said the thing that four people say about once every few years, which was: "**So what would you do now.**" And Iven Sore said: "**Nothing. That is not a question I have been asked and I have not prepared for.**"**

Nobody picked the conversation up again. **The man of about thirty-eight who keeps a warehouse asked about the first heading, which was a building on a road where the buses turn, and asked whether that was the same road, and Iven Sore said he would not say whether it was the same road, and gave a reason, and the reason was that if he said it was then four people at that table would go and stand in it, and that had happened before and it had cost about nine weeks.**

**And the man of about thirty-one said, in four seconds, the sentence that the other three had been in that room to hear and had not got: "**So you kept it.**"**

"**I kept it,**" Iven Sore said. "**I kept it and I brought it and I have put it in front of you and I am going to take it away again, and that is the same as not bringing it, and I did it anyway because a thing four people read twice is different from a thing four people read once, and that is the only argument I have got and it is about nine years old.**"

**Nobody said that was right. Nobody said it was wrong either, and the man who keeps the records put his hand back on the folder and left it there until about half past six.**

**And about half past five Iven Sore said one more thing, which was not in any of the nine weeks, and said it to the table and not to the four of them: "**The four headings on that list are the four headings I have been asked for about nine times since and I have said no to about seven of them and I have said yes to about two and I have never written down which two, and that is the same thing this page is about, and I have not come here to be told about it.**"**

---

**She is about thirty. From half past six until ten she was in the chair the door is behind in the fourth of those four rooms off that service road, and nobody in that building put a word to her for three and a half hours.**

**Nobody has said sorry to her in any form. Nobody is going to.**

**What is hers is the eighth of eight in a ring binder on the back shelf of that room and it did not come out.**
