**Asha Reed came in at eleven and asked for a printer, which is the whole of what she came for.**

She teaches people the words that go in a form before they sign it. She has been doing it since she was nineteen and she does it for about nine hours a week and she is paid by nobody for any of it except one afternoon a month.

"**I want four things on a sheet, all the same size, and I want them by Friday,**" she said.

"**Colin Nall. Road off Saltmarket.**"

"**I know the road. I want to know whether he will want paying before I put them in his hand.**"

**At about half past eleven a woman of about thirty-four came in with a page in her hand and asked Asha Reed whether the wording on it was correct.**

She had come to the counter and Asha Reed happened to be standing at it and the woman asked the woman, not the man.

"**Would you look at that and tell me if it's right.**"

Asha Reed read it. It took her about nine seconds.

"**No.**"

"**No it's wrong, or no I won't.**"

"**No I won't,**" she said, "**and the reason is that you are going to sign it and live with what it said, and if a stranger says the words are right then for the rest of your life it is not your page any more, it is a page somebody else checked.**"

**Nobody asked her a second question. The woman put the page back in her bag, said thank you to nobody in particular, and went, and she has not come back to that counter and she is not owed anything by anybody in it.**

Marek said nothing. **He was four feet away and he was on a bench with a job in his hands and there was about eleven seconds in which he could have said the same sentence and did not, and not saying it is not modesty, because he is not qualified to say it and has never said it.**

**At two a man of about fifty-five came in with a fridge on a hand truck and asked whether the shop would send somebody to help him get it up four steps and round a corner.**

"**It is four steps and it is a corner and it is about nine minutes there and back and I would be gone and there would be nobody on this counter, and I have been on this counter since nine.**"
