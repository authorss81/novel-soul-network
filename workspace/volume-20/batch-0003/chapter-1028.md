# Chapter 1028 — The Place Where She Is Sitting

**A Thursday was a bench until two and a counter until six and then four converted units off that service road, and about four people came up that stair on their own and were asked three things at that bench, and by about half past seven everyone in the room had noticed something about the third one.**

---

That Thursday took nine callers through that shop and nine names were written against that day's book as each of them came in, and the last of them was let away at about half past eight, and the shutter came down at ten.

**The four converted units off that service road stood at one thousand nine hundred days, two hundred and seventy-one weeks and three days. The sheet that had been on a wall in a fourth district was seventy-three days old and was in a carrier bag under the long bench at the back of that shop, the second printing was fifty-nine days old, and the four words were fifty days old.**

---

**The first two people answered all three, and about four people in that room have said since that before the third question was finished both of them had already glanced at the chair the woman of about fifty-four was sitting in, and that she saw it happen twice and said nothing.**

The woman of about thirty-four said: **you are all looking at her.**

Marek said: **I am not looking at her.**

The woman of about thirty-four said: **you were, and so was the man behind me and so was the woman by the door. She has said one thing about the third of three things in a week and now the third of three things has a face on it, and it is not her face, it is the place where she is sitting.**

Marek said: **then I should stop asking it.**

The woman of about thirty-four said: **you should not. But you should know what you have done and you have not said one word about it since Monday.**

---

**And then Marek said what he had done, in about nine words, and about four people in that room have said since that he said it standing up and not at the bench.**

Marek said: **I have put a silence in the middle of that room and I cannot get it out again.**

The woman of about thirty-four said: **can you stop asking the third one.**

Marek said: **no. And I do not know whether I should be doing any of this and I have not known since about Monday and there is nobody in this room whose advice I am owed.**

---

**And then she started to say what the room was for, and about four people in that room have said since that nine people went quiet and waited and that she stopped after three words.**

The woman of about thirty-four said: **this room is—**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **do not.**

The woman of about thirty-four said: **I was going to finish it.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **I know you were, and I would like it noticed that four of you have just waited about four seconds for you to do it, and that not one of you could tell me afterwards what you were waiting for.**

---

**And then the man of about thirty-seven came up at about eight and said he had six days left and had counted, and about four people in that room have said since that he said the number out loud and that nobody had asked him for it.**

The man of about thirty-seven said: **six days. I have asked eleven people that came up that stair since Tuesday and I have got about nine answers and two of those nine were changed on Friday and one of them was changed in here on Wednesday, so the two methods together have got me eleven answers and about two changes, and I do not know which of us is doing the work.**

Marek said: **you are stopping on the Thursday.**

The man of about thirty-seven said: **I am stopping on the Thursday and I will come up and tell you the number and I am not going to tell you the names and you are not going to ask me.**

Marek said: **I would not ask you.**

The man of about thirty-seven said: **you would have asked me once. On Sunday you would have asked me. And you have not, and I have noticed, and I am not going to make anything of it because you have a woman in here who has not given you one of the three either.**

---

**Nobody thanked anybody on that Thursday and nobody forgave anybody, and a woman got three words into a sentence in front of nine people and was stopped by another woman, and a copy of that sheet with an empty fifth column was on that table for its one hundred and twelfth night.**

---

**The four jobs that Thursday went into that shop, that yard, that kitchen and a stairwell, and they came to fifty-two pounds, exact.**

A distribution board in that shop had two circuits joined together with a bit of cable and a junction box out of reach above the ceiling.

He separated the circuits, put a board where it could be reached and proved the lot.

"**Fourteen pounds,**" he said. "**Two circuits joined together above a ceiling is a board that nobody can get to when it trips. Boards in that shop are like it, and one of them has been a ceiling about four of them have had a chair and a torch under.**"

A light in that yard had been put in with a lamp holder screwed into a length of conduit that was hanging on two clips.

He took the conduit down, made a proper fixing and proved the light.

"**Thirteen pounds,**" he said. "**A holder screwed into conduit hanging on two clips is a holder that moves every time somebody walks past. Lamps in that yard are like that, and one of them has been a gable about four of men have had a ladder up to for four years.**"

A cooker in that kitchen had been put in with a neutral bar that had come loose behind the appliance.

He tightened it, re-terminated the bar and proved the cooker.

"**Twelve pounds,**" he said. "**A cooker with a loose neutral is a cooker that works until it does not. Cookers in that kitchen are like that, and one of them has been a kitchen a woman has had to stop using the oven in.**"

A stairwell light had been put in with a lamp in the middle of the flight where it could not be reached.

He took it out, put two lamps in where they could be reached and proved both.

"**Thirteen pounds,**" he said. "**A lamp in the middle of a flight is a lamp that means a ladder in a stairwell. Stairwell lights are like it, and one of them has been a stair about four of them have gone up in the dark for four years.**"

**Fifty-two pounds is the sum of those four on that Thursday, exact.**

---

**At half past six on that Thursday the fourth of those four rooms shut, and a woman of about thirty was behind that door, and the binder at the back of that shelf did not come off it, and this file sets no figure against what the binder holds.**
