# Chapter 1025 — What She Said On The Pavement

**A Saturday was a counter from about ten and then four converted units off that service road, and Marek walked about twenty minutes with a woman who holds an office and they stopped on a pavement outside a clinic in a fourth district and stood there in the wind.**

---

That Saturday took five callers through that shop and five names were written against that day's book as each of them came in, and the last of them was let away at about half past four, and the shutter came down at ten.

**The four converted units off that service road stood at one thousand eight hundred and ninety-five days, two hundred and seventy weeks and five days. The sheet that had been on a wall in a fourth district was sixty-eight days old and was in a carrier bag under the long bench at the back of that shop, the second printing was fifty-four days old, and the four words were forty-five days old.**

---

**She had come out of that clinic about ten minutes before he did and had been standing on the same stretch of pavement since, and about four people who have passed that pavement have said since that the two of them were talking and that neither of them was looking at the other for most of it.**

Marek said: **I am going to ask you a thing and I want to say before I ask it that you can say no in about nine words and go.**

Talia said: **go on.**

Marek said: **if I were to be asked in that room one of the three things by you, would you do it.**

---

**And she said no in about nine words, and about four people who have passed that pavement have said since that she said it in one go without stopping and that he did not ask a second time.**

Talia said: **I am not a member of it. I cannot be asked inside it, and I cannot be asked about it either.**

Marek said: **that is two reasons.**

Talia said: **and the third is that you are asking me because there is nobody else and I have been the person you ask when there is nobody else, and that is how this has worked for a long time, and it stopped working somewhere in the last month and I have not told you that it stopped.**

---

**And then he said that he had not got a better answer than hers, and about four people who have passed that pavement have said since that he said it looking at a doorway across the road and not at her.**

Marek said: **I know. You have answered it and I have not got a better sentence than the one you have already got, and I am not going to make one on a pavement.**

Talia said: **you wanted somebody to stand on that stair on Monday and say nothing.**

Marek said: **yes.**

Talia said: **I could stand there. That is not why I am not going to. If you asked me for a person I would find you a person by Thursday and you would not know what I had done, and that is a different offer from standing there, and it is one I am also not making on this pavement.**

---

**And then she said the one about the office, and she said it in about nine words, and about four people who have passed that pavement have said since that she said it to the pavement rather than to him.**

Talia said: **I am not going to use what I can do at your name, and you are not going to ask me to.**

Marek said: **I have never asked you for anything.**

Talia said: **no. You have not, and that is why I said it, and if you had asked I would have said the same nine words and we would both have been embarrassed and I would have done it anyway.**

Marek said: **you would.**

Talia said: **I would, and I would have gone home and been about four days with myself about it.**

---

**And then she said the thing she had said once before and had not said anything else about since, and about four people who have passed that pavement have said since that he did not ask about it again and that she noticed him not asking.**

Talia said: **I still have it.**

Marek said: **I am not going to ask.**

Talia said: **I had it ready about a week ago the first time and I have decided twice since to say it and have not, and I am telling you it is ready and nothing else, because you have never once asked me and I am not going to make you.**

---

**Nobody thanked anybody on that Saturday and nobody forgave anybody, and a man asked a woman a question on a pavement and was refused in nine words he had no better answer to, and a copy of that sheet with an empty fifth column was on that table for its one hundred and seventh night.**

---

**The four jobs that Saturday went into that shop, that yard, that kitchen and a bathroom, and they came to twenty-eight pounds, exact.**

A socket in that shop had been put in behind a shelf a person had to move to reach.

He took the shelf off, cut in a box, refit it and prove the socket.

"**Six pounds,**" he said. "**A socket behind a shelf is a socket nobody will ever report. Sockets in that shop are like that, and one of them has been a counter a person has had to clear to find.**"

A lamp in that yard had been put in with the flex twisted round a nail and painted over twice.

He took it down, re-fixed it on a proper rose and proved the lamp.

"**Seven pounds,**" he said. "**Flex twisted round a nail and painted over is flex somebody wanted not to look at. Lamps in that yard are like that, and one of them has been a gable a man has had to repaint round.**"

A cooker in that kitchen had been put in with its cable pinched behind the kickplate.

He took the kickplate off, freed the cable and proved the cooker.

"**Eight pounds,**" he said. "**A cable pinched behind a kickplate is a cable that gets cut the day somebody sweeps out. Cookers in that kitchen are like that, and one of them has been a floor a woman has had to be careful with a mop about.**"

A light in that bathroom had been put in on a fitting with no earth at all.

He earthed it off the box and proved it with an instrument.

"**Seven pounds,**" he said. "**A fitting with no earth in a room like that is a room a person cannot put a plug in safely. Lights in that bathroom are like it, and one of them has been a wall somebody has kept a hairdryer away from.**"

**Twenty-eight pounds is the sum of those four on that Saturday, exact.**

---

**Half past six on that Saturday closed the fourth of those four rooms, and a woman of about thirty stood behind that door, and the ring binder did not come off the back of that shelf, and this page puts no figure at all against the page the binder holds.**
