# Chapter 1029 — The Hour He Did Not Ask Her For

**The woman who holds an office stood on that service road at about ten past six and walked about nine minutes with him and did not mention it once, and that Friday was a bench until two and a counter until six and then four converted units off that service road.**

---

That Friday took six callers through that shop and six names were written against that day's book as each of them came in, and the last of them was let away at about half past seven, and the shutter came down at ten.

**The four converted units off that service road stood at one thousand nine hundred and one days, two hundred and seventy-one weeks and four days. The sheet that had been on a wall in a fourth district was seventy-four days old and was in a carrier bag under the long bench at the back of that shop, the second printing was sixty days old, and the four words were fifty-one days old.**

---

**He had wanted to ask her something since about half past seven on the Wednesday and had not, and about four people who come to that shop have said since that she arrived at ten past six and stood about four feet off him and waited.**

Marek said: **two people came up that stair on Tuesday after seven and the door was on the latch and they stood outside it for about nine minutes and then went off down the road.**

Talia said: **and you want it left off the latch for another hour on the two evenings when you are in.**

Marek said: **yes.**

Talia said: **that is a landlord and a caretaker and a form.**

Marek said: **it is a bolt on a door.**

---

**And then she said the thing that was not about the bolt, and about four people who come to that shop have said since that she said it without stopping and that he kept walking for about nine seconds before he answered it.**

Talia said: **I know what you wanted to ask me on Wednesday and I know you did not ask me, and I have had three days to decide what to do about the fact that you did not, and I have decided to do nothing, and I am telling you I have decided to do nothing.**

Marek said: **then neither of us will.**

Talia said: **say why.**

Marek said: **no.**

Talia said: **no is fine. I did not say it to get it out of you and I am not going to pretend I did. I said it so that neither of us has to sit on this pavement for about four minutes working out how long we were going to not say it.**

---

**And then she said the other half, and about four people who come to that shop have said since that she said it at the end of that road where the two of them turned round.**

Talia said: **I could get that bolt. It would take me about four days and a form and one conversation and I am not going to have any of the three.**

Marek said: **no.**

Talia said: **I know I am not. I said it out loud so that you cannot come back in a fortnight and ask me as though it had not been on the table.**

Marek said: **it will not be asked in a fortnight.**

Talia said: **you do not know that, and I am not asking you to promise it, because a promise is a thing people say to get through a Friday and I have been in this for long enough to hear about nine of them.**

---

**And then they got to the corner and she stopped, and about four people who come to that shop have said since that she stopped for about nine seconds and then carried on, and that neither of them mentioned the stop afterwards.**

Talia said: **about four people came up that stair this week that had been told by somebody on a road that it was somewhere you could be told about a form.**

Marek said: **I know. Two of them asked me for a form on Wednesday and I had nothing to give them, same as ever.**

Talia said: **and you have said out loud in a room full of people that a stranger is asking them at the bottom of that stair, and you have not once said out loud what this stair is for, and there are about four people in this city who now think the stranger is the one who has understood it.**

Marek said: **yes.**

Talia said: **that is not me telling you it is a mess. That is me telling you I can hear it from here and I am not able to do anything about it from a distance of about four minutes.**

---

**Nobody thanked anybody on that Friday and nobody forgave anybody, and a man did not ask a woman for a thing she could have had in about four days, and a copy of that sheet with an empty fifth column was on that table for its one hundred and thirteenth night.**

---

**The four jobs that Friday went into that shop, that yard, that kitchen and a porch, and they came to thirty-one pounds, exact.**

A socket in that shop had been put in behind a chest of drawers that had to be moved.

He moved it, cut in a box, refit it and proved the socket.

"**Eight pounds,**" he said. "**A socket behind a chest of drawers is a socket that is fine for about four years. Sockets in that shop are like it, and one of them has been a corner a person has shoved furniture at for four years.**"

A lamp in that yard had been put in on a holder with a piece of tape round the base of it.

He took it off, made a proper holder and proved the lamp.

"**Seven pounds,**" he said. "**Tape round the base of a holder is a holder somebody has already given up on. Lamps in that yard are like that, and one of them has been a wall that about four of them have taped.**"

A cooker in that kitchen had been put in with the cable tied behind it with a bootlace.

He untied it, put it in a proper clip and proved the cooker.

"**Eight pounds,**" he said. "**A cable tied behind an appliance is a cable that has been cut once already. Cookers in that kitchen are like that, and one of them has been a cooker a woman has had to hold away from the wall.**"

A porch light had been put in with a switch that buzzed when it was pressed.

He took it out, found the fault and proved the light from the step.

"**Eight pounds,**" he said. "**A switch that buzzes is a switch with an arc in it about a week before it stops. Porch lights are like that, and one of them has been a step that about four of them have come home up in the dark.**"

**Thirty-one pounds is the sum of those four on that Friday, exact.**

---

**That Friday had the fourth of those four rooms shut from half past six, with a woman of about thirty behind that door, and the ring binder still on the back of that shelf, and no figure for the page it holds appears anywhere in this file.**
