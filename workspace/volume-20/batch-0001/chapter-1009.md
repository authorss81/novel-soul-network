# Chapter 1009 — She Answered It This Time

**A Thursday was a bench until two and a counter until six and then about nine minutes on foot to a first floor off a line in Saltmarket, where a sheet of paper came off a wall in a fourth district in the morning in a carrier bag, and a man asked a woman the same question he had asked her a fortnight before and this time she answered it in about nine words.**

---

That Thursday took nine callers through that shop and nine names were put against it as they went in, and the shutter came down at ten.

**The four converted units off that service road stood at one thousand eight hundred and sixty-five days, two hundred and sixty-six weeks and three days. The sheet on that passage wall was thirty-eight days old that morning and was in a bag by lunchtime, and the second printing was twenty-four days old and had never been on that wall, and the four words were fifteen days old.**

---

**He was not in that passage and about four people who use it have said since that the man with the ladder came at about nine in the morning and took two drawing pins out of a wall and put a piece of paper in a carrier bag and did not read it as he did it.**

A woman of about thirty-one said: **you are not the one who paid for it.**

A man of about forty-four said: **no. A man in a shop is.**

A woman of about thirty-one said: **and he is not here.**

A man of about forty-four said: **he said he would not be.**

---

**And about nine people stood in that passage while it happened and about four of them had come for on the strength of a woman at a desk, and none of them said anything, and about four people who use that passage have said since that it took about four minutes and that the two clean rectangles were still there at the end of it.**

A woman of about thirty-one said: **there are two patches.**

A man of about forty-four said: **there are two patches.**

A woman of about thirty-one said: **was there two sheets.**

A man of about forty-four said: **there was one sheet and one that went up before it. I am not going to tell you what was on the other one and you did not ask me twice.**

---

**He was told about it at about half past four at his own counter by a man who had walked there, and about four people who come to that shop have said that he said one sentence and then went and did four jobs.**

Marek said: **is it off.**

A man of about forty-four said: **it is off and it is in a bag and I have brought the bag because you are paying me for it and not for a wall.**

Marek said: **put it under the bench.**

A man of about forty-four said: **under the bench.**

Marek said: **yes.**

---

**He went up at about half past six and there were about eleven people in that room, and he did not go and look at the wall first, and about four people who sit in that room have said since that he came in as though the wall had not happened at all and that it had.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **you have not asked me anything for about a week.**

Marek said: **I have been asking you things for about four years.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **and I have answered about one in nine. I know the count and I have never said it out loud and I am not going to now.**

---

**And then he said that he was going to ask her the thing he had asked her the Wednesday before last, and that he was going to ask it because about nine people in that room had heard a version of it by mouth and that the version he had got was not it.**

Marek said: **you said a fortnight ago that I was standing there with it and that I was to stop.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **you have asked me four things since then and this is the fifth.**

Marek said: **then I will not ask it.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **no. You will ask it. Ask it now while there are about eleven people here, because it will not be worth asking me in a week.**

---

**And he asked it, and about four people who sit in that room have said since that nobody helped him and that nobody in the room knew it was coming.**

Marek said: **then whose was it.**

---

**And nobody in that room has said since that she took about four seconds, and she answered him in about nine words, and the page that did not carry it on the fortnight before carries it now.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **it is where three things get asked out loud.**

---

**And then he asked her the other one, because he had four days of not asking it in him, and about four people in that room have said since that it was the only time in about four years that he asked her two things without waiting.**

Marek said: **and what is it for.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **I do not know, and I have said so twice, and you have written nothing down either time.**

Marek said: **I am not going to write anything down.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **then it is the first thing anybody in this city has said out loud about it and you have said it to about eleven people and not one of them can do anything with it.**

---

**And then he told her what he had done with the paper, and he told her before he told anybody else, and about four people in that room have said since that she looked at him for a while before she said the next thing.**

Marek said: **the sheet is in a bag under the bench of my shop and I paid a man with a ladder to take it down and I did not print anything else.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **and the second one.**

Marek said: **the second one was printed and never went on that wall and I do not know where it is and I am not going to find out.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **so there are a hundred and fifty of them somewhere in this city.**

Marek said: **yes.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **and nothing on any of them and nobody who could put something on any of them.**

---

**And then a man of about thirty-four at the back of that room said the thing that about four people in there had come to say, and he said it to Marek and not to her, and nobody answered him.**

A man of about thirty-four said: **so is there going to be something else.**

Marek said: **no.**

A man of about thirty-four said: **there was going to be a door.**

Marek said: **there was a door for about four weeks and it said what it said and I am not printing another thing this month.**

The man of about thirty-four said: **you will.**

Marek said: **I am not going to argue with you about it in this room.**

---

**Nobody thanked anybody on that Thursday and nobody forgave anybody, and two clean rectangles were left on a wall in a fourth district and about four people who had come for on the strength of a woman at a desk were told there would not be another one, and a copy of that sheet with an empty fifth column was on a table in that shop for its seventy-fifth night.**

---

**That Thursday's remainder went into that shop, that yard and that kitchen, and there were four jobs in it and not a fifth.**

A socket in that yard had been put in on a lead that came off an old three-pin round-base plug rewired behind it.

He took the plug off, made a proper pin to the pattern and proved it.

"**Eleven pounds,**" he said. "**A three-pin plug on a modern socket is somebody's grandfather's lamp in a kitchen. Sockets in that yard are like it, and one of them has been a shed a man has had to rewire himself for about two years.**"

A light in that shop had been put in on a fitting that was not earthed and had a metal part accessible from the floor.

He earthed it, made the accessible part good and proved it with an instrument.

"**Twelve pounds,**" he said. "**A fitting with metal you can touch from the floor is a fitting nobody should have put in. Fittings in that row are like it, and one of them has been a counter somebody has been worried about for four years.**"

A bell in that kitchen had been wired to a relay that had been left in with its back plate off and its terminals exposed.

He fitted the back plate, made off the ways and proved the bell.

"**Twelve pounds,**" he said. "**A relay with its back off is a bell somebody has had open in a kitchen. Bells in that street are like it, and one of them has been a kitchen a woman has had a spider killed in for about four years.**"

A pull switch in that second room had been put on a wall beside a bed and could be knocked off by a shoulder in the dark.

He moved it to the frame by the door and proved it from the bed.

"**Ten pounds,**" he said. "**A switch beside a bed is a switch somebody knocks in the dark. Switches in that room are like it, and one of them has been a bedroom a man has got up and turned on for four years.**"

**Forty-five pounds is the sum of that Thursday's four, exact.**

---

**That Thursday shut the fourth of those four rooms from half past six, and a woman of about thirty stood behind its door, and the ring binder did not come off the back shelf of that room, and no figure for what that binder holds is entered anywhere on this page.**
