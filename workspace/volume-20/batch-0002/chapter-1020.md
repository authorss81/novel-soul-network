# Chapter 1020 — What He Chose Added To Nothing

**A Tuesday was a bench until two and a counter until six and then that shop again, and the last thing that happened in it was a woman of about thirty-four put a folder down on that counter and said that she had gone in on Friday after all, and had been told the whole of it by the woman who had said it.**

---

That Tuesday took nine callers through that shop and all nine of them went onto that day's book before the shutter came down at ten.

**The four converted units off that service road stood at one thousand eight hundred and eighty-four days, two hundred and sixty-nine weeks and one day. The sheet that had been on a wall in a fourth district was fifty-seven days old and was in a carrier bag under the long bench at the back of that shop, the second printing was forty-three days old, and the four words were thirty-four days old, and about four people in this city know where to walk to and not one of them knows what the room is about.**

---

**She had been in a queue twice that week and had not gone in twice, and about four people who come to that shop have said since that she said this at the counter before she had taken her coat off, and that she had a folder under her arm and that she did not put it down until about two minutes after she had started talking.**

The woman of about thirty-four said: **I went in on Friday.**

Marek said: **why.**

The woman of about thirty-four said: **because you said no, and I have been to a queue twice not going in and I got to about a quarter to twelve on Friday and I thought, well, if it is not going to be printed then at least it can still be said by the person who said it.**

---

**And then she told him what the woman of about forty-three had said, and about four people who come to that shop have said since that Marek stopped what he was doing and stood with his hands flat on that counter for the whole of it.**

The woman of about thirty-four said: **she did not pretend it had not happened. That is the first thing. She said, I said that to about four people and it has come back to me four ways and two of them are wrong, and I am telling you now because you came in front of me and I am not going to have you find it out from somebody who has made it up.**

Marek said: **what did she say after that.**

The woman of about thirty-four said: **she said, I do not know how I know that. And then she filled a form in for somebody who was standing there, and she did not say anything else about it for about ten minutes, and then she said, if you want me to say it differently from now on I will.**

Marek said: **and what did you say to her.**

The woman of about thirty-four said: **I said no. Because I do not know what the other thing is, and if she says it differently from now on then the four people will have one version and I will have another, and I am the one who will be standing in a passage on my own saying it.**

---

**And then Marek said the arithmetic of the week out loud at that counter to nobody in particular, and about four people who come to that shop have said since that he said it to the till and that all three of them looked up.**

Marek said: **about four people knew where to go on the Thursday before last and nothing at all about what it was for. On Monday I decided not to put a second thing up. So about four people still know where to go and know nothing about what it is for, and about four more people who heard a version of it from a person who is not in my care have not been told anything, and there are about nine people in this city who have been given a fact this month and every one of the nine has been given it by somebody who was not able to make it a bit more accurate than it was.**

The woman of about thirty-four said: **that is not true.**

Marek said: **which part.**

The woman of about thirty-four said: **she was. On Friday. She said she did not know how she knew, and that is a different thing from a person who cannot be bothered.**

Marek said: **yes. That is a decent thing to say and I have been in a shop for about four weeks.**

---

**And then the woman of about forty-three came in at about half past seven with her coat off, which she had not done on her first night, and about four people who come to that shop have said since that she left it on the stool and sat down on the customer side for about half an hour.**

The woman of about forty-three said: **she came in on Friday and I told her.**

Marek said: **she told me at about a quarter past seven this evening.**

The woman of about forty-three said: **she came in on Friday and I told her and I have come in here tonight to tell you that I told her, and not to tell you about anything else, and I want that understood.**

Marek said: **it is understood. Why are you telling me at all.**

The woman of about forty-three said: **because there is nobody else who would know what it was about.**

---

**And then she told him where the piece of paper was, and about four people who come to that shop have said that she said it flatly, as though she were reporting a fault on a job.**

The woman of about forty-three said: **it is in a drawer in my own flat. It has been there since Monday night and it is not coming out again this month.**

Marek said: **I did not ask.**

The woman of about forty-three said: **no. And a man in a second district said something to you on Friday about a drawer, and I have thought about it for about two days, and I would like to say it back to him and I am saying it to you instead because you are the one who will understand what it means.**

Marek said: **I am not sure I will.**

The woman of about forty-three said: **he said a drawer is not a promise. He was talking about his own. I am talking about mine, and mine is worse, because I put it there myself and I know exactly where it is and so does my landlord.**

---

**And then she said the last thing of that week, and it was nine words, and about four people who come to that shop have said since that she said it to Marek and not to the woman of about thirty-four, who was still in the shop.**

The woman of about forty-three said: **no. I am not going to say it to people any more.**

Marek said: **that is about four people.**

The woman of about forty-three said: **no. You keep saying that as though the four of them were the whole of it, and they are not the whole of it, and I have been at that desk for years and I know about nine people whose names I am not going to give you in a shop.**

Marek said: **I have not asked you for the names of anybody.**

The woman of about forty-three said: **no. And I have not given you one, and that is the only thing I have managed this month, and I would like it noticed that it is one thing.**

---

**Nobody thanked anybody on that Tuesday and nobody forgave anybody, and a woman of about thirty-four went in to a queue on Friday after saying no for about three weeks, and about four people who had been told the four words had been told the whole of it by the woman who said it, and a copy of that sheet with an empty fifth column was on that table for its ninety-sixth night.**

---

**A woman of about thirty-four was in that shop until about half past eight, and after she had gone there were four jobs in it, and the four went into that shop, that yard and that kitchen.**

A socket in that shop had been put in with the earth taken off the metal box and onto the plaster behind it.

He took the earth off the plaster, put it on the box and proved it with an instrument.

"**Eleven pounds,**" he said. "**An earth on plaster is an earth on dust. Sockets in that row are like it, and one of them has been a counter somebody has had to keep a cloth off for about two years.**"

A light in that yard had been put in on a fitting that had no earth at all and a metal ring anybody could touch from the ground.

He earthed it off the box and proved it from the ground.

"**Twelve pounds,**" he said. "**A fitting you can touch from the ground is a fitting nobody should have put in. Fittings in that yard are like it, and one of them has been a yard a man has had to keep a ladder in the wet for about four years.**"

A cooker in that kitchen had been put in with its lead joined to the fixed wiring with a couple of connectors and about four inches of flex.

He took the connectors out, made the joint properly and proved the cooker.

"**Ten pounds,**" he said. "**A cooker joined with connectors to about four inches of flex is a cooker that somebody has repaired in about nine minutes. Appliances in that kitchen are like that, and one of them has been a cooker a woman has had to watch for about four years.**"

A switch in that second room had been put in on a plate with two of the screws in a plasterboard edge and not in a box.

He took it out, cut the wall back, put a box in and proved the switch.

"**Twelve pounds,**" he said. "**Two screws in an edge and none in a box is a switch that comes off the wall when anybody leans on it. Switches in that block are like it, and one of them has been a wall somebody has had to put a plate back on for four years.**"

**Forty-five pounds is the sum of that Tuesday's four, exact.**
