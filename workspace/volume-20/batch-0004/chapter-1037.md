# Chapter 1037 — The Woman At The Desk Was Refused

**The word a woman of about forty-three had kept on a pad for about nine years went up that stair inside her coat on that Friday and came back down again, and behind it all was a bench until two, a counter until six and after six, four converted units off that service road.**

---

Nine callers came through that shop on that Friday and nine names were written against that Friday's book as each of them came in, the last of them let away at about half past eight, and the shutter was down by about ten.

**The four converted units off that service road stood at one thousand nine hundred and fifteen days, two hundred and seventy-three weeks and four days. The sheet off a wall in a fourth district is eighty-eight days old and lies in a carrier bag under the long bench at the back of that shop, seventy-four days old is the other printing, and the four words were sixty-five days old.**

---

**She had walked in off the street and up the stair without stopping, and about four people in that room have said since that she was still in her coat and that she put the paper down before she took it off.**

Marek said: **you have been here.**

The woman of about forty-three said: **I have not been here.**

Marek said: **you have not been here since you told me about your fifth column.**

The woman of about forty-three said: **I have not, and I have come because you went to my desk, and I want that on whatever you write down.**

---

**And then she turned the paper over, and there was a word on the top of it, and about four people in that room have said since that nobody in that room got close enough to read it and that about four of them tried.**

The woman of about forty-three said: **that is a word and I have had it on a pad at my desk for about nine years.**

Marek said: **whose.**

The woman of about forty-three said: **mine. I wrote it about four days after I started that job and I have never given it to anybody.**

Marek said: **what is it for.**

The woman of about forty-three said: **that is a question I am not going to answer and we both know what would happen to it.**

---

**And then the woman of about fifty-four said the second refusal of that week, and it came in about nine words, and about four people in that room have said since that it was a different nine words from Thursday's.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **no. It is her word and she has come up here to give it.**

The woman of about forty-three said: **I have come up here to put it on a table, which is not giving it to anybody.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **it is the same thing with the door open and that is worse.**

The woman of about forty-three said: **yes, and I had worked that out on the stair, and I have come anyway, and I would like somebody to say that part out loud.**

---

**And then about nine people in that room said nothing at all for about four seconds and then the man of about thirty-seven said the thing nobody had asked him to say, and about four people have said since that he had come up early and had been standing at the back.**

The man of about thirty-seven said: **put it on the card.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **no.**

The man of about thirty-seven said: **I am not asking you. I am asking her. Put it on the card and about four people a week who come off a description will know where they are.**

The woman of about forty-three said: **and about four people a week will put a sheet with a word on it on a wall, and it will be a different word in a month, and there is one of the four I am trying to stop.**

Marek said: **there are about four of those and you are one of them.**

The woman of about forty-three said: **I am, and that is why I do not want my own word doing it.**

---

**And then Marek asked her one thing, and about four people in that room have said since that he asked it in nine words and that she answered him in four.**

Marek said: **do you want it on the card.**

The woman of about forty-three said: **no.**

Marek said: **then take it down.**

The woman of about forty-three said: **I am going to leave it there for about nine minutes and then I am going to take it, and I want about nine of you to watch me not take it.**

---

**Nobody thanked anybody on that Friday and nobody forgave anybody, and a woman of about forty-three carried a word up a stair in her coat and put it face up on a bench and did not get to keep it there, and a copy of that sheet with an empty fifth column was on that table for its one hundred and twenty-seventh night.**

---

**The four jobs that Friday went into that shop, that yard, that kitchen and that alley, and they came to thirty-eight pounds, exact.**

A lamp in that shop had been put in on a ceiling rose that had been screwed to a length of timber.

He took the rose off the timber, cut a box in and proved the lamp.

"**Eleven pounds,**" he said. "**A rose screwed to a length of timber is a rose that moves when the timber moves. Roses in that shop are like that, and one of them has been a ceiling about four of them have had to hold a hand under.**"

A socket in that yard had been put in on an enclosure that had been filled with a foam filler.

He cleaned it out and proved the socket from the path with a lamp on it.

"**Eleven pounds,**" he said. "**Fill an enclosure with foam and you have packed the terminals in. Enclosures in that yard are like that, and one of them has been a socket about four of them have had to stop using in the wet.**"

A cooker in that kitchen had been put in with the old outlet box left in the wall behind it.

He took it out, made the hole good and proved the cooker.

"**Eight pounds,**" he said. "**Leave a box in a wall behind an appliance and the next one is into a dead one. Boxes in that kitchen are like it, and one of them has been a wall a woman has had to open twice.**"

An outside light in that alley had been put in on a gland that had been tightened on the sheath.

He undid it, made it up properly and proved the light from the step.

"**Eight pounds,**" he said. "**A gland tightened on the sheath is a gland holding the wrong end. Glands in that alley are like that, and one of them has been a step about four of them have had to walk round in the wet.**"

**Thirty-eight pounds is the sum of those four on that Friday, exact.**

---

**Half past six on that Friday shut the fourth of those four rooms, and a woman of about thirty stood behind that door, and the ring binder on the back of that shelf did not come off it, and this page carries no figure for the page it holds.**
