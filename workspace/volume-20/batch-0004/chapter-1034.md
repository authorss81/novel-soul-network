# Chapter 1034 — She Sat Down For Half An Hour

**A Sunday kept that counter open from about ten until about two and the shutter always comes down early on it, and about four of the people who came up that stair in the afternoon of that Sunday had not been in that room since the Wednesday.**

---

Five callers passed through that shop on that Sunday and five names were written against that Sunday's line, the last of them let away at about half past one, and the shutter came down at about two.

**The four converted units off that service road stood at one thousand nine hundred and ten days, two hundred and seventy-two weeks and six days. The sheet that hung on a wall in a fourth district is eighty-three days old and sits in a carrier bag under the long bench at the back of that shop, sixty-nine days old is the second printing, and the four words were sixty days old.**

---

**She came up at about half past twelve and did not stop in the doorway, which is a thing about four people in that room have said she has never once done, and she sat down without taking her coat off.**

The woman of about sixty-two said: **I have come for about half an hour.**

Marek said: **you have got it.**

The woman of about sixty-two said: **I am not going to be asked anything and I am not going to ask for anything.**

Marek said: **that is your business and not mine.**

---

**And then she did not stand up, and about four people in that room have said since that they know what standing up cost her and that nobody in the room has ever seen it and that they know anyway.**

The man of about thirty-three said: **you can go home whenever you want.**

The woman of about sixty-two said: **I know.**

The man of about thirty-three said: **you stood up the last time.**

The woman of about sixty-two said: **I did.**

The man of about thirty-three said: **and you have never said what that cost.**

The woman of about sixty-two said: **no, and it is not a thing anybody is going to ask me about tonight.**

---

**And then about four separate people in that room nearly asked her, and about four people who come to that shop have said since that every one of them got as far as opening its mouth and then looked at Marek.**

The woman of about thirty-four said: **I have wanted to ask you something since the day I first came up here.**

The woman of about sixty-two said: **then you are not going to.**

The woman of about thirty-four said: **why not.**

The woman of about sixty-two said: **because if I answer it you will have it and you will be different about it, and if I do not answer it you will have had nothing, and I would rather hand you nothing.**

---

**And then the man of about thirty-three said out loud that she had given one word once, and about four people in that room have said since that he said it flatly, as a fact about a room, with no weight on it at all.**

The man of about thirty-three said: **you said one word in here on a Friday about four months ago and three people have been carrying it about ever since and none of the three has said where they got it.**

The woman of about sixty-two said: **I have never said a word.**

The man of about thirty-three said: **you said it and somebody heard you and that is all it takes.**

The woman of about sixty-two said: **then one of those three is wrong, and I am not going to stand up in this room and find out which.**

---

**And then she said the one thing she came for, and about four people in that room have said since that she said it to the window and not to anybody in it.**

The woman of about sixty-two said: **I have heard about four people say out loud this month that nobody in this city has told them what this is.**

Marek said: **about four people is about what I would have said.**

The woman of about sixty-two said: **it was about four a month ago as well and I have not been able to find out whether it is the same four.**

Marek said: **I have not either and I have not counted.**

The woman of about sixty-two said: **do not start counting.**

Marek said: **I am not going to.**

The woman of about sixty-two said: **I am not asking you to. Counting is how a person finds out which of the four it is, and then those four know they are being counted, and the thing goes quiet for about a month and comes back louder than it was.**

---

**Nobody thanked anybody on that Sunday and nobody forgave anybody, and a woman of about sixty-two sat in that room for about half an hour and was not asked one thing, and a copy of that sheet with an empty fifth column was on that table for its one hundred and twenty-second night.**

---

**The four jobs that Sunday went into that hall, that yard, that kitchen and that lavatory, and they came to nineteen pounds, exact.**

A bell in that hall had been wired straight to a junction box under the stairs.

He took it out onto the wall, made off the ends and proved the bell from the door.

"**Five pounds,**" he said. "**A bell on a box under a stair is a bell that stops when the box gets damp. Bells in that hall are like that, and one of them has been a front door that about four of them have had to knock at instead of ringing.**"

A lamp in that yard had been put in on a rose that had been glued to the ceiling.

He got a proper box in, fixed the rose to it and proved the lamp from the step.

"**Five pounds,**" he said. "**A rose glued to a ceiling lets go on a cold night and takes the ceiling finish with it. Roses in that yard are like that, and one of them has been a ceiling about four of them have had to repaint round.**"

A socket in that kitchen had been put in with the earth lead clipped to a cold water pipe.

He took it off the pipe, put it on the box and proved the socket with a kettle.

"**Six pounds,**" he said. "**An earth clipped to a water pipe is an earth that stops being an earth the day a tap is closed. Sockets in that kitchen are like it, and one of them has been a kettle a woman has had to keep away from the wall.**"

A light in that lavatory had been put in on a fitting with a holder that did not reach the earth terminal.

He made it reach, tightened it and proved the light with the door shut.

"**Three pounds,**" he said. "**A holder that does not reach the earth is a fitting that has been earthed by somebody hoping. Fittings in rooms like that one are like that, and one of them has been a switch about four of them have touched with wet hands.**"

**Nineteen pounds is the sum of those four on that Sunday, exact.**

---

**About half past one on that Sunday closed the fourth of those four rooms, and a woman of about thirty stood behind that door, and the ring binder stood at the back of that shelf where it stands, and no figure for what it holds is written in this file.**
