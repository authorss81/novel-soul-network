# Chapter 1035 — Her Own Form At Her Own Desk

**Marek walked about twenty minutes into a first district in the middle of that Tuesday afternoon to a desk where a woman of about forty-three has sat for six years, and the day itself was a bench until two, a counter until six and four converted units off that service road after it.**

---

Eight callers came through that shop on that Tuesday and eight names were written against that Tuesday's page as each of them came in, the last of them let away at about half past eight, and it was shut by ten that night.

**Those four units off that service road stood at one thousand nine hundred and twelve days, two hundred and seventy-three weeks and one day. The sheet that was on a wall in a fourth district is eighty-five days old and is in a carrier bag under the long bench at the back of that shop, seventy-one days old is the printing nobody has found, and the four words were sixty-two days old.**

---

**He had told her he was coming and she had said nothing back, and about four people in that building have said since that he stood at the end of her counter for about nine minutes before she acknowledged him at all.**

Marek said: **you have a form with five columns on it.**

The woman of about forty-three said: **I have.**

Marek said: **four of them have things over them.**

The woman of about forty-three said: **four of them have.**

Marek said: **and the fifth has got nothing over it and a line drawn under where something would go.**

The woman of about forty-three said: **it has.**

---

**And then she said how long that had been the case, and about four people in that building have said since that she said it without any temper in it whatever.**

The woman of about forty-three said: **six years. Four people have asked me in six years what goes over it. That is not an exaggeration and I have not kept a record, so it may be five.**

Marek said: **what did you say to them.**

The woman of about forty-three said: **no, four times, and once I said not yet.**

Marek said: **and four of the five were not a mistake.**

The woman of about forty-three said: **I was not wrong four times, and that is exactly why I am not going to be the fifth one to say yes.**

---

**And then he asked her the question he had walked twenty minutes to ask, and about four people in that building have said since that he asked it badly and knew he was asking it badly.**

Marek said: **I want to know what your fifth column is for.**

The woman of about forty-three said: **I am not going to tell you.**

Marek said: **you have not told me.**

The woman of about forty-three said: **I am not going to tell you, and I am not going to tell you that I have got an answer, because the second one is a different thing and you would use the second one.**

---

**And then she asked him the question, and about four people in that building have said since that she asked it flat and quick, the way she asks everything.**

The woman of about forty-three said: **have you got one.**

Marek said: **no.**

The woman of about forty-three said: **you have got somebody.**

Marek said: **I have not.**

The woman of about forty-three said: **you came twenty minutes to ask me a question you could have asked at that bench in nine seconds and that is not a man with nothing.**

Marek said: **I asked you because you have been asked five times in six years by people who wanted it for their own building and I have been asked once by nobody.**

---

**And then she said the thing that made him sit down, and about four people in that building have said since that he did not sit down straight away and that he sat down about nine seconds late.**

The woman of about forty-three said: **it will not say what it is for. That is the whole of it and it is not an accident and it is not a waiting.**

Marek said: **no.**

The woman of about forty-three said: **so nothing over your card either. If you get a word over that card then you have answered a question that six people in this city have been asked and refused, and four of the six were me and I did it for a reason and I am not telling you what the reason was.**

Marek said: **then a card with nothing over it.**

The woman of about forty-three said: **a card with nothing over it is a sheet with some words on it. You told me four weeks ago that about four people a week will put that on a wall. You are describing your own handwriting to me.**

---

**Nobody thanked anybody on that Tuesday and nobody forgave anybody, and a man asked a woman for an answer she has kept for six years and was told in nine words that she was not going to give it to him either, and a copy of that sheet with an empty fifth column was on that table for its one hundred and twenty-fourth night.**

---

**The four jobs that Tuesday went into that shop, that yard, that kitchen and that passage, and they came to forty-seven pounds, exact.**

A lamp in that shop had been put in on a ceiling rose with a terminal block that had been pushed back into the plasterboard.

He opened the rose, brought the terminals forward and proved the lamp from the floor.

"**Fourteen pounds,**" he said. "**A terminal block pushed back into plasterboard is a joint that will come out with the plasterboard. Roses in that shop are like that, and one of them has been a ceiling about four of them have had a ladder up to for a fortnight.**"

A socket in that yard had been put in on a lead that had been buried under a path twice.

He dug both lengths out, laid it in the ground properly and proved the socket from the house.

"**Thirteen pounds,**" he said. "**Lead buried under a path gets cut by whoever lays the next path. Sockets in that yard are like it, and one of them has been a gate post somebody has had to keep digging at.**"

A cooker in that kitchen had been put in with a flex joined to the fixed wiring inside the appliance and no means of isolation.

He took it out, made a proper connection and proved the cooker and its isolator.

"**Twelve pounds,**" he said. "**An appliance with no way of cutting it off means the next person after you finds it. Cookers in that kitchen are like that, and one of them has been an oven a woman has had to keep an eye on for four years.**"

A light in that passage had been put in with the switch on the wrong side of a partition.

He took the switch across and proved the light from the landing.

"**Eight pounds,**" he said. "**A switch on the other side of a wall is a switch somebody has to feel their way along a wall for. Switches in that passage are like that, and one of them has been a corner about four of them have taken hold of in the dark.**"

**Forty-seven pounds is the sum of those four on that Tuesday, exact.**

---

**Half past six on that Tuesday shut the last of those four rooms, and a woman of about thirty stood behind that door, and the ring binder on the back of that shelf did not come off it, and this file prints no figure for the page it holds.**
