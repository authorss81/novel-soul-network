### 2.1 THE THREE MEASURES OF RECORD USED TWO DIFFERENT QUOTE-SKIPPING RULES, ONE FOR EACH PARAGRAPH RULE, AND NEITHER RULE REPRODUCES ALL FOUR PUBLISHED CELLS

**`batch-0002/SUMMARY.md` §14.0 defines quote-skipping in words as *a sentence that lies wholly inside a double-quoted run*, which is a quotation-state rule: a segment is discarded when the quotation mark count inside it leaves the reader inside a run. **This instrument therefore uses the quotation-state rule, because it is the rule the two earlier files define in words rather than the rule that happens to fit one paragraph mode, and it publishes both rows side by side rather than choosing.** A pass that reproduces Movement III's DROPPED quote-skipping row of 385 with this instrument should not be surprised to read 370 in the KEPT cell where the published figure is 384. **The equalities and the mechanism at `batch-0002/SUMMARY.md` §14.5 and `batch-0003/SUMMARY.md` §2 are not disturbed by any of this and neither is any conclusion those two files draw.**

### 2.2 THE APPARATUS'S *BREAKS KEPT* ROW CANNOT BE PRODUCED BY ANY RULE THAT PRODUCES ITS *BREAKS DROPPED* ROW, AND THE REASON IS STRUCTURAL

**In all three measures of record the apparatus's KEPT and DROPPED rows print the same number — 168, 163, 160 — and that equality is not a coincidence and is not a property of the prose.
