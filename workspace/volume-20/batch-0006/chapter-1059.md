# Chapter 1059 — The Card Came Off That Rail

**A Monday was a counter from about ten and that shop after it, and about four people went up that stair, and a card about the size of a hand came out of the rail above that bench and went onto a table and did not go up on a wall.**

---

Six callers came into that shop on that Monday and six names were written against that Monday's book as each of them came in, the last of them was let away at about half past nine, and the shutter went down at ten.

**Those four units off that service road stood at one thousand nine hundred and sixty days, two hundred and eighty weeks to the day. The two hundred and fifty sheets under that long bench are a hundred and thirty-three days old, the printing nobody has looked for is one hundred and nineteen days old, and the four words were one hundred and ten days old.**

---

**He took it out of that rail at about half past six with about four people in the room and put it flat on the table in the middle, and about four people who come up that stair have said since that he put it down without saying anything and that about nine seconds passed before anybody said anything either.**

Nobody said anything.

Marek said: **it is on a table.**

The woman of about thirty-four said: **I can see that.**

Marek said: **I am not putting it on a wall and I am going to say why in one go and then nobody is to argue with me about it, because I have had this argument with about four people already this month and I am tired of it.**

---

**And then he said why, out loud, and about four people in that room have said since that he said it about a wall and about a bag and not about the room at all.**

Marek said: **The last thing anybody hung up in this city for anybody to walk to was a hundred and fifty sheets with a door on them and no list, and about four people a week went and looked at it for about four weeks, and about four of them are the reason that room is hard to describe now.**

The woman of about thirty-four said: **That is not about the room and you have not said that it is.**

Marek said: **It is about the sheets and it is the only reason I have got, and I have said it out loud and that is all I am going to say about it.**

---

**And then a woman who holds an office came into that shop and said one thing she had not said before, and about four people in that shop have said since that she said it to the counter and not to him.**

Talia said: **I have stopped saying yes.**

Marek said: **To what.**

Talia said: **About four people in a first district have asked me this month whether that room is still open, and I said yes every single time without knowing what I was saying yes to, and this week I have said I do not know four times and I am not going back to yes.**

Marek said: **that is a change and I am not going to ask you about it.**

Talia said: **I know you are not. I have a name ready and I have said so four times now and I am still not going to say it, and what is behind that door is not what I have been keeping.**

---

**And then a man of about thirty came in and said out loud that he was not coming up that stair again for about four weeks, and about four people in that shop have said since that he said it standing up and had not taken his coat off.**

The man of about thirty said: **I am not coming up for about four weeks.**

Marek said: **you do not have to say why.**

The man of about thirty said: **I know I do not have to. I am saying it out loud because you have been planning a Tuesday at that bench for about three weeks and I am not going to be the thing you planned it around.**

Marek said: **I was not going to plan anything around it.**

The man of about thirty said: **You said it three times in a room. That is planning it around me with your mouth, and I have been in that room and I have watched you do it.**

---

**And then nobody said anything for about nine minutes, and about four people in that shop have said since that it was the second-longest silence of that week and that nobody said one kind thing in it.**

Nobody said anything.

Nobody moved a chair.

The woman of about fifty-four said: **You can come up. That has not changed and it is not going to change because of about four weeks.**

The man of about thirty said: **I know it has not. I am telling him, not you.**

---

**Nobody thanked anybody on that Monday and nobody forgave anybody, and a card about the size of a hand came off that rail and went onto a table and was not hung on a wall, and a woman who holds an office said she had stopped saying yes to about four people in a first district, and a man of about thirty said out loud that he was not coming up that stair for about four weeks, and a copy of that sheet with an empty fifth column was on that table for its one hundred and seventy-second night.**

---

**The four jobs that Monday went into that shop, that yard, that kitchen and that gate, and they came to sixty-five pounds, exact.**

A lamp in that shop had been put in on a switch plate that had been painted the same colour as the wall behind it.

He cleaned it back and proved the lamp from the floor.

"**Sixteen pounds,**" he said. "**A switch painted the colour of the wall is a switch nobody can find in the dark. Plates in that shop are like that, and one of them has been a wall about four of them have had to feel along it with a hand.**"

A socket in that yard had been put in on a plate with a terminal block that had one of its screws missing entirely.

He put a proper screw in, re-torqued the block and proved the socket from the gate.

"**Nineteen pounds,**" he said. "**A block with a screw missing is a block holding a cable by friction. Blocks in that yard are like that, and one of them has been a socket about four of them have had to wiggle the plug to make the lamp come on.**"

A cooker in that kitchen had been put in with a cooker cable that had been tied in a knot four inches from the appliance.

He cut it out, made it to length and proved the cooker with all four rings.

"**Fifteen pounds,**" he said. "**A cable with a knot in it is a cable somebody has shortened without owning a pair of cutters. Cables in that kitchen are like that, and one of them has been a cooker about four of them have had to pull it out to get it out of the way of the sink.**"

A light at that gate had been put in on a fitting whose earth had been run to the gate frame and the frame had been painted over two or three times since.

He brought the earth back to the fitting and proved the light from the path.

"**Fifteen pounds,**" he said. "**An earth to a gate frame is an earth that disappears under about four coats of paint. Frames at gates are like that, and one of them has been a gate about four of them have had to test the lamp by putting a hand on it.**"

**Sixty-five pounds is the sum of those four on that Monday, exact.**

---

**Half past six on that Monday shut the fourth of those four rooms, and a woman of about thirty stood behind that door, and nothing was taken off the back of that shelf and nothing whatever is printed here for the page that binder holds.**
