# Chapter 1050 — What He Said Coming Down That Stair

**A Friday was a bench until noon and a counter until four and then that shop until ten, and a man of about thirty stood on the step outside it at about ten past six and said out loud that he was coming back and did not say whether he would answer.**

---

Six callers came into that shop on that Friday and six names were written against that Friday's book as each of them came in, the last of them let away at about a quarter to nine, and the shutter came down at ten.

**Those four converted units off that service road stood at one thousand nine hundred and thirty-six days, two hundred and seventy-six weeks and four days. The two hundred and fifty sheets in the carrier bag under that long bench are a hundred and nine days old, the printing that was never on that wall is ninety-five days old and its whereabouts are not known, and the four words were eighty-six days old.**

---

**He came in about nine minutes late and said the thing he had come to say before he had taken his coat off, and about four people in that shop have said since that he said it standing up inside the door so that nobody would have to move.**

The man of about thirty said: **I am coming back.**

Marek said: **I know you are. I was not going to say anything about it either way.**

The man of about thirty said: **I have not said whether I am going to answer it and I am not going to, and if I come up and get asked and cannot do it then I am not going to be the one who says that it does not matter, because it does.**

The man of about thirty said: **that is two things and you only needed one of them.**

Marek said: **I have not got a place to put the other one and it can wait.**

---

**And then Talia came into that shop at about half past seven and asked him one question about the front door, and he answered it, and about four people in that shop have said since that the whole of it was about a door and neither of them said anything else.**

Talia said: **that door does not shut properly and it has not shut properly since before the room was there.**

The man of about thirty said: **it has not shut for me either.**

Talia said: **I have been asked by about four people in a first district this month whether that room is still open, and I have said yes every single time without knowing what I was saying yes to.**

The man of about thirty said: **do you want me to tell you.**

Talia said: **no. I said I had a name and I have said so three times now and I am still not going to say it, and telling me what is behind that door is not what I have been keeping.**

---

**And then Marek said the half of a thing out loud that he has never said out loud to anybody, and about four people in that shop have said since that he said it to the woman of about thirty-four with the man still in the room and did not say it to the man.**

Marek said: **I have been thinking since Thursday about whether a person who cannot answer that last one ought to be sitting at that bench at all.**

The woman of about thirty-four said: **say the rest of it.**

Marek said: **I have not got the rest of it.**

The woman of about thirty-four said: **Then you have not got the first half of it either, and I am not going to sit in a room on a Tuesday listening to half of it.**

Marek said: **You are not going to be in that room on a Tuesday.**

The woman of about thirty-four said: **I have not decided that. I have decided that I am not going to be kind to you in front of anybody while you have not got the rest of it, and you have not got the rest of it, and that is the whole of what I have to say about it.**

---

**And then nobody said anything else kind for the rest of that Friday, and about four people in that shop have said since that the man of about thirty stayed until the shutter was half down and that he did not go up that stair again.**

The man of about thirty said: **what happens to the four people I would be talking about, if I said it in there.**

Marek said: **I do not know.**

The man of about thirty said: **and what happens to you.**

Marek said: **nothing. I will stand at that bench on a Tuesday and I will ask it and either you will answer it or you will not, and I will go home after it either way.**

The man of about thirty said: **that is the first thing anybody has said to me in about four weeks that I have believed.**

Marek said: **Do not believe it. I have not got to the end of it and I have said so out loud to a woman who works in a first district while you were standing in the room.**

---

**Nobody thanked anybody on that Friday and nobody forgave anybody, and a man said out loud that he was coming back and did not say whether he would answer, and a man behind a counter said out loud the first half of a thing he does not want and was told by a woman that he has not got the first half of it either, and a copy of that sheet with an empty fifth column was on that table for its one hundred and forty-eighth night.**

---

**The four jobs that Friday went into that shop, that yard, that kitchen and that cupboard, and they came to twenty-three pounds, exact.**

A socket in that shop had been put in on a plate with a screw turned into a plastic plug in a hollow wall.

He put a proper box in behind it and proved the socket from the wall.

"**Seven pounds,**" he said. "**A plug in a hollow wall is the one fixing everybody tries first. Plugs in that shop are like that, and one of them has been a wall about four of them have had to open up to get a back box.**"

A lamp in that yard had been put in on a bracket with a bushing pushed the wrong way round in it.

He turned it round, seated it and proved the lamp from the step.

"**Four pounds,**" he said. "**A bushing pushed the wrong way is a lamp somebody has had to hold by the glass. Bushings in that yard are like that, and one of them has been a wall about four of them have had to change a bulb two-handed.**"

A cooker in that kitchen had been put in with a flex caught in its own door hinge.

He took it out of the hinge, made a proper joint and proved the cooker with the door open and then shut.

"**Six pounds,**" he said. "**Flex in a hinge is flex that gets cut and does not know it. Cookers in that kitchen are like that, and one of them has been a kitchen about four of them have had to open the door slowly.**"

A light in that cupboard had been put in on a flex somebody had shortened because they thought it was too long.

He dropped a proper one and proved the light from outside the door.

"**Six pounds,**" he said. "**A flex somebody has cut for being too long is a flex somebody took a blade to. Flexes in cupboards are like that, and one of them has been a cupboard about four of them have had to reach round the door for.**"

**Twenty-three pounds is the sum of those four on that Friday, exact.**

---

**Half past six on that Friday shut the fourth of those four rooms, and a woman of about thirty stood behind that door, and nothing on the back of that shelf was disturbed, and this file records nothing whatever about the page that binder holds.**
