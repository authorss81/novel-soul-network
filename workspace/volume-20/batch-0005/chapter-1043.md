# Chapter 1043 — Something He Would Not Say Out Loud

**That Tuesday was a bench until two and a counter until six and then four converted units off that service road, and the man of about thirty stood at the back of that room for about an hour while about nine people in it were asked three things out loud and answered all three.**

---

Eleven callers came into that shop on that Tuesday and eleven names were written against that Tuesday's book as each of them came in, the last of them let away at about half past nine, and the shutter was down at ten.

**Those four units off that service road stood at one thousand nine hundred and twenty-six days, two hundred and seventy-five weeks and one day. The printing nobody has found is eighty-five days old, the two hundred and fifty sheets in the carrier bag under that long bench are ninety-nine days old, and the four words were seventy-six days old.**

---

**Four people were asked three things that evening between half past six and half past eight and every one of them answered all three, and about four people in that room have said since that he was at the back for the whole of it and that nobody once turned round to look at him.**

Marek said: **that is the first of three.**

A woman said something short.

Marek said: **that is the second of three, and the man at the back can tell you what it is if you want it.**

The man of about thirty said: **I do not want it. I have got as far as the second one on a bus.**

The woman said: **then you are the second person this week who has told me that.**

---

**And then he asked Marek at that bench, out loud, in front of everybody, whether Marek could stop it, and about four people in that room have said since that he asked it as though he had been carrying the question for about a week.**

The man of about thirty said: **can you stop it.**

Marek said: **I can stop asking.**

The man of about thirty said: **that is not the same thing.**

Marek said: **no. And it is the only half of it I have got, and I have never pretended to anybody that it is all of it.**

The man of about thirty said: **I have been told the other half exists.**

Marek said: **then somebody has been told something I cannot give you.**

---

**And then he asked for a day and an empty room and was refused in about nine words by a man who said out loud afterwards that the refusal was his own and not a rule, and about nine people in that room have said since that he asked for it in an ordinary voice.**

The man of about thirty said: **give me a Thursday and tell the others not to come.**

Marek said: **no. I will not choose your day and I will not empty that room for you.**

The man of about thirty said: **you could. You stand at that bench.**

Marek said: **I could, and it would be the first thing I have ever done in that room that I could not undo, and I am not doing it on a Tuesday.**

The man of about thirty said: **nobody asked you to make a rule. I asked you for a quiet Thursday.**

Marek said: **you did, and the answer is the same, and the reason is not a rule and I am not going to stand here and pretend it is.**

---

**And then the man of about thirty-three answered a question nobody put to him, and about four people in that room have said since that he said it flatly and did not look at anybody while he said it.**

The man of about thirty said: **you have been in here about four times. Have you ever managed that last one in front of people.**

The man of about thirty-three said: **twice. I have managed it twice and it stopped nothing at all on either occasion and I would go back and do it again tomorrow.**

The man of about thirty said: **what did you say.**

The man of about thirty-three said: **I am not going to tell you, because I have been asked for it out loud by about four people in this city this month and I have said no to all of them and I am not saying it to a man who has not answered one himself.**

The man of about thirty said: **that is the same answer I would have got from the woman who carries the folder.**

The man of about thirty-three said: **It is. I have given that answer to about four people this month and you are the first one who told me in advance that he was going to get it.** 

---

**Nobody thanked anybody on that Tuesday and nobody forgave anybody, and a man asked for a quiet Thursday with an empty room and was refused by a man who said the refusal was his own, and a copy of that sheet with an empty fifth column was on that table for its one hundred and thirty-eighth night.**

---

**The four jobs that Tuesday went into that shop, that gate, that kitchen and that passage, and they came to fifty-three pounds, exact.**

A socket in that shop had been put in on a back box that had been cut down to fit a wall it was never meant for.

He cut a new one in, made the plasterwork good and proved the socket from the wall.

"**Eleven pounds,**" he said. "**A box cut down to fit a wall is a box that was bought for another wall. Boxes in that shop are like that, and one of them has been a wall about four of them have had to bring a plasterer to for.**"

A lamp in that gate had been put in on a gland with the sheath not clamped inside it.

He re-made the gland and proved the lamp from the path.

"**Twelve pounds,**" he said. "**A gland with the sheath loose pulls the whole of it out of the fitting. Lamps at gates are like that, and one of them has been a gate about four of them have had to prop open with a boot.**"

A cooker in that kitchen had been put in with a hob element that had been put on the lighting board.

He took it off that board, terminated it properly and proved the cooker with that ring on.

"**Fourteen pounds,**" he said. "**A hob element on the lights is a hob somebody has had to live round. Hob elements in that kitchen are like that, and one of them has been a kitchen a woman has cooked one ring at a time in for about four years.**"

A light in that passage had been put in on a switch that had been fitted upside down.

He turned it over so that the blades faced down and proved the light from the door.

"**Sixteen pounds,**" he said. "**A switch the wrong way up is a switch about four of them have hit with the back of a hand. Switches on passages are like that, and one of them has been a passage a person has had to learn to be careful in.**"

**Fifty-three pounds is the sum of those four on that Tuesday, exact.**

---

**Half past six on that Tuesday shut the fourth of those four rooms, and a woman of about thirty stood behind that door, and the binder never came off the back of that shelf, and no figure for the page that binder holds is entered in this file.**
