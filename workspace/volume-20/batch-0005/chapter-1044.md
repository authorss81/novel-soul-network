# Chapter 1044 — He Began It In The Middle Of That Room

**A Wednesday was an ordinary day at that counter and about nine people went up that stair after it, and Marek got three words into a sentence in front of all of them and stopped, and the man of about thirty was standing at the bench while he did it.**

---

Six callers came into that shop on that Wednesday and six names were written against that Wednesday's book as each of them came in, the last of them let away at about a quarter to nine, and the shutter was down at ten.

**The four units off that service road had been standing there one thousand nine hundred and twenty-seven days, which is two hundred and seventy-five weeks and two days. What is in the carrier bag under the long bench at the back of that shop is a hundred days old, the second printing is eighty-six days old and has never been looked for, and the four words were seventy-seven days old.**

---

**He asked Marek what Marek would say if Marek were ever asked that last one in that room, and about four people in that room have said since that he asked it as a joke and it was not taken as one by anybody in the room.**

The man of about thirty said: **if you were ever asked it here, what would you say.**

Marek said: **that is not the question you want answered.**

The man of about thirty said: **it is the only question I have got.**

Marek said: **I know it is, and I am not going to stand here and hand a stranger the two ends of it.**

The man of about thirty said: **I am not a stranger. I have been in this room three times.**

Marek said: **You have been in this room three times and I have not asked you one thing, and that is the whole of what I am able to do for you and it is not nothing.**

---

**And then Marek began one in front of about nine people and stopped after three words, and about four people in that room have said since that two of the nine looked at the card on the table and one of them looked at the floor.**

Marek said: **what that room is, it is—**

He stopped there.

The woman of about fifty-four said: **that is four.**

Marek said: **It is not four. It is three and I have said so out loud and I am not going to finish it in this room.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **I know you are not, and that is not the same as finishing it somewhere else.**

---

**And then the man of about thirty said nothing about it at all for about nine minutes, and about four people in that room have said since that he did not use the opening, and that they were waiting for him to use it all evening.**

Nobody said anything about what Marek had not finished.

The man of about thirty said, after about nine minutes: **you did not have to stop that in front of everybody.**

Marek said: **I know. I would rather you heard it from me than from somebody who has been saying it about me at a door.**

The man of about thirty said: **I am not going to be one of those somebody.**

Marek said: **I have not written you down as one.**

---

**And then the woman of about fifty-four told him what she has got and where she says it, and about nine people who come up that stair have said since that she said it to him and not to the room and that the room did not hear a word of it.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **you want to know whether it can be done. It can be done. I have got an answer to that last one and I have had it for years and I have given it in about nine doorways in this city in about four weeks to people who wrote it nowhere.**

The man of about thirty said: **then why have you not said it in here.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **because in here about nine people write things down.**

The man of about thirty said: **nobody in this room writes anything down.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **Somebody does. Not me and not tonight.**

---

**Nobody thanked anybody on that Wednesday and nobody forgave anybody, and a man got three words into a sentence in front of about nine people and stopped, and nobody in that room said one kind thing about it or to anybody else, and a copy of that sheet with an empty fifth column was on that table for its one hundred and thirty-ninth night.**

---

**The four jobs that Wednesday went into that shop, that yard, that kitchen and that landing, and they came to twenty-nine pounds, exact.**

A lamp in that shop had been put in on a flex that ran behind a hot pipe and had gone stiff and browned where it lay.

He took the flex out, dropped a proper one and proved the lamp from the floor.

"**Seven pounds,**" he said. "**Flex behind a pipe is flex that has been cooked. Flexes in that shop are like that, and one of them has been a bench about four of them have had to stand a jug under.**"

A socket in that yard had been put in on a plate carried on one screw that had been driven into a hollow block.

He found solid, put a second screw in and proved the socket from the gate.

"**Eight pounds,**" he said. "**One screw into a hollow block is a plate waiting for a dropped spade. Plates in that yard are like that, and one of them has been a wall about four of them have had to keep a ladder leaning off.**"

A cooker in that kitchen had been put in with a fan packed solid with grease behind a grille that had never once been cleaned.

He cleaned it out, proved the cooker and left it running.

"**Nine pounds,**" he said. "**A fan packed with grease is a fan that has not moved air in about four years. Cookers in that kitchen are like that, and one of them has been a kitchen about four of them have had to open a window to boil a kettle.**"

A light on that landing had been put in on a fitting whose earth had been taken off by holding the brass screw that the holder itself was fixed with.

He moved the earth to the terminal and proved the light with the door shut.

"**Five pounds,**" he said. "**An earth taken off a screw somebody else is holding is an earth that only works until the next lamp is changed. Fittings on landings are like that, and one of them has been a landing about four of them have changed a bulb on their knees.**"

**Twenty-nine pounds is the sum of those four on that Wednesday, exact.**

---

**Half past six on that Wednesday shut the fourth of those four rooms, and a woman of about thirty stood behind that door, and the binder stayed on the back of that shelf where it has been, and no figure for what it holds is written anywhere on this page.**
