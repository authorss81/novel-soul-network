# Chapter 1045 — He Started Answering

**That Friday ran a counter from about ten and the day's work finished before it, and the man of about thirty was at that bench at about half past seven and was asked three things in front of about nine people and gave two of them away in about nine words each.**

---

Eight callers came into that shop on that Friday and eight names were written against that Friday's book as each of them came in, the last of them let away at about half past eight, and the shutter was down at ten.

**Those four converted units off that service road stood at one thousand nine hundred and twenty-nine days, two hundred and seventy-five weeks and four days. In the carrier bag under the long bench at the back of that shop are two hundred and fifty sheets that are a hundred and two days old, the printing that was never on that wall is eighty-eight days old and has not been looked for, and the four words were seventy-nine days old.**

---

**He asked to be asked there and then, in front of everybody, and about four people in that room have said since that he asked it at the top of his voice while somebody was still sitting down.**

The man of about thirty said: **ask me now.**

Marek said: **I was going to.**

The man of about thirty said: **you did not say that back on a Saturday.**

Marek said: **I was going to ask you at about half past six and you were not here until about half past seven and I am going to ask you now, and I am not going to ask you anything twice, and I am not going to ask you a fourth thing that is not on that card.**

The man of about thirty said: **I know there are three.**

---

**And then the first of three was asked out loud and answered in about nine words, and about four people in that room have said since that two of the nine looked at each other before he had finished it.**

Marek said: **that is the first of three.**

The man of about thirty said: **I will say it and I will say it out loud and I would like everybody here to hear it, and I am not saying it because I have been asked and I am saying it because I have carried it about four days.**

Nobody said anything.

The man of about thirty said: **that is all of it and I am not going to say it again for anybody.**

---

**And then the second of three was asked and answered, and about nine people who come up that stair have said since that it took longer than the first one and that the room got quieter in the middle of it rather than louder.**

Marek said: **that is the second of three.**

The man of about thirty said: **I have been carrying that one since a Wednesday and I have said it to nobody including the woman who carries a folder, and I am saying it now and it is not about anything in this room.**

Marek said: **I did not ask you what it was about.**

The man of about thirty said: **I know you did not. That is why I have said it.**

---

**And then the third of three came and he put a time on it in front of about nine people, and about four people in that room have said since that he said the day out loud before Marek had finished asking and that nobody wrote the day down.**

Marek said: **that is the third of three.**

The man of about thirty said: **not tonight.**

Marek said: **That is not an answer and I am not going to let anybody in this room treat it as one, and I am not going to write it down as one, because nothing is written down tonight.**

The man of about thirty said: **I know it is not an answer. I have told about four people in this room that it will be on a Thursday, and I have said the day out loud, and I said it before you had finished the question.**

Marek said: **Then you will come on a Thursday and I will ask it again in front of whoever is here.**

The man of about thirty said: **in front of whoever is here.**

Marek said: **You named the day yourself. I am not going to move one thing in here to suit you, and you were told on a Saturday what happens, and there is nothing else in it for me to do.**

---

**Nobody thanked anybody on that Friday and nobody forgave anybody, and a man gave two of three things away in about nine words each in front of about nine people and put a day on the third one out loud, and a copy of that sheet with an empty fifth column was on that table for its one hundred and forty-first night.**

---

**The four jobs that Friday went into that shop, that store, that kitchen and that yard, and they came to forty-nine pounds, exact.**

A lamp in that shop had been put in on a rose with two lamps joined together at the back of it.

He took the join out, put a two-way in and proved the lamp from the floor.

"**Thirteen pounds,**" he said. "**Two lamps joined at the rose is two lamps somebody has made work once. Roses in that shop are like that, and one of them has been a ceiling about four of them have had to switch at the door.**"

A socket in that store had been put in outdoors on a plate too wide for the lid that shuts over it.

He made a lid that fitted and proved the socket from the doorway.

"**Ten pounds,**" he said. "**A plate too wide for its lid is a socket that has been wet more than once. Sockets in stores are like that, and one of them has been a store about four of them have had to move stock off a wall to work on it.**"

A cooker in that kitchen had been put in with an oven lamp wired live off the element.

He made the lamp a switched one and proved it with the door open.

"**Twelve pounds,**" he said. "**An oven lamp live off the element is a lamp that lights up a hot oven. Cookers in that kitchen are like that, and one of them has been an oven about four of them have had to clean in the dark.**"

A light in that yard had been put in on a flex buried under a path with a board laid over the top of it.

He dug the flex up, took it out and put the lamp on the wall.

"**Fourteen pounds,**" he said. "**Flex under a path is flex that gets walked on twice a day. Lamps in that yard are like that, and one of them has been a path about four of them have had to keep a board over and a corner turned up.**"

**Forty-nine pounds is the sum of those four on that Friday, exact.**

---

**Half past six on that Friday shut the fourth of those four rooms, and a woman of about thirty stood behind that door, and the ring binder did not come off the back of that shelf, and no figure and no range of a figure for what it holds is written in this file.**
