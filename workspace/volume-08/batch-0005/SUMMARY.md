## 1. What Movement III delivered, in one paragraph

A flood came into three of the four districts in the night of the Monday and was in them for four days, and about nine ordinary businesses and about four rooms and about nine people are what stands between that and a night of it, and nobody dies, and the four days is longer than a man in the middle would have taken and nobody in those districts said so and nobody has to. About four of the nine practices do not get there in time and there is no procedure in this city for saying in advance that a shop will be shut. On the Wednesday the water comes back from a direction it had not come from, and a weighbridge office answers a question with the fact that it does not know which of the four rooms is doing what, because the night rota of four sentences failed on the Thursday of week one hundred and thirty-two, which is twenty days earlier, and there is nothing written down anywhere about any of it. A man of about twenty-nine who works on lifts gets nine of eleven people down nine floors over a day and says the third true thing of his in this volume, and is not the man of about twenty-nine who comes on a Friday four miles away, and the two have never met. On the Thursday a woman of about fifty-three asked a man of twenty-two for one name, in about nine seconds, and he said no in about nine seconds, and the reason was that if he is on it then everything they do from that day goes through him and in a week it will be him and not them. On the Friday a floor in a company training room was run for the fifth time, the post was bare for its third week, and a woman of about twenty-three asked a man of about forty-four who had not spoken in that room for four years a question and he answered it, and nobody improved on it and nobody thanked him. On the Monday of the next week about nine lorries stood at a company's own gate and did not go through it, and a man of about fifty-one with a tool bag was asked to open that gate and said no in nine words and was not thanked, and nobody said why and nobody has since. On the Wednesday of that week the Exchange sat for the twenty-third time in nineteen years and the book did not open, which is the second sitting in a row, and the reason was the same shape as last time and is not the same sentence and is not a rule, and no number was given into the tin, so the tin stands where it stood. On the Friday the laminated sheet came back to that training room in a jiffy bag and went into a drawer and not onto the post, and a man of about thirty-six was asked for the first time in four years whether he was on the list and said no. On the Monday a printed card listing four rooms, three of which are right, went round four buildings, and a woman of about fifty who types in an office had about nine words of standing and used all of them and was not thanked, and the card went up on a canteen board anyway. On the Wednesday about eleven people went up an unlit stair to a top floor that had been empty for two years, with the light on and nobody there, and two of them named what it cost in about nine seconds each, and they are not the four who lost a day's pay and they are not the woman in the flat above the dark room. And on the Thursday of week one hundred and thirty-seven four rooms and about nine people kept a district and nobody died and about eleven people lost a working week and nine of them said so out loud in about four minutes, and then a woman of about thirty-eight lifted a page of paper off a wet table in order to move it and turned it over, and there is a printed paragraph at the foot of the back of it, and it grants the office that still has emergency authority the power to merge four rooms into one during a declared emergency, and the man of fifty-eight with a bad hip said out loud that his office did not write it and cannot say who printed it, and a woman of forty-nine with a stamp said the word naming the public body once and only once, and a woman of twenty-four said one thing about the paragraph and not about any man in the room and then wrote for four minutes and telephoned none of it and told nobody. **Nothing was merged. Nothing was refused. Nothing was agreed.**
