# Chapter 373 — Third Day, And A Man Asked For A Thing On A Thursday

The water stopped rising in two of the three districts on the Thursday and went on rising in the third, and **about eleven people in the three districts were out of their work that week and did not know it yet, and at ten past eight in the morning a woman of about fifty-three asked a man of twenty-two for something in about nine seconds and he said no in nine seconds, and the woman whose Thursday it is was four miles away in a workroom and was in none of it.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last of them at about six in the evening. He was at the counter of a repair shop in Lattice Ward from about half past eight, and about nine people were in the shop, and the last of the day's jobs was done at about six.

Nine and ten. That room, off a service road, is four hundred and seventy-two days old and warm, and about four people came in on the Thursday for the temperature and nobody asked any of them a question.

---

She came in out of the working Ashfields with a plastic bag and four inches of river in it, and she asked at the counter, and it took her about nine seconds, and nobody in that shop improved on it afterwards and nobody thanked him.

"**I have nine people who have nowhere to be told to go and I need one name that four of them can say out loud in four rooms, and you are the one name I have got, and I am asking you for the day.**"

He said no, and it took him nine seconds, and she did not repeat it and nobody else in that shop said anything.

"**I am not going to be a thing four rooms can point at. If I am on it then everything you do from today goes through me, and then in a week you will be doing what I say without noticing, and that will be me, and not you.**"

Nobody said anything for about nine seconds. She said all right and put the bag down and took it up again and said the second half of it once, which was that she had not wanted to come in at all, and left.

**Nobody in that shop thanked him and nobody was angry with him and about four of the about nine people in there have since said that he was right and about four have said nothing, and the two groups have not met. She has not been in that shop since and has not been asked to and is not going to be, and nobody has rung her about it, and the four of her nine people are somewhere and the man with the minibus has them.**

---

**It was a Thursday. She was four miles away in a workroom doing a job that gets paid for and she was in none of the three districts and she was not asked about any of it and she has not been told any of it. He had not spoken to her since a Thursday in an earlier week, and the four months between those two Thursdays is not shorter this morning than it was on Monday, and nothing in three days of water has made it longer.**

Nothing about the Thursday was spent. Nobody in that shop said the word. Nobody telephoned a workroom four miles away and nobody has done that in a fortnight, and there is nothing in either of the two workrooms that belongs to the other one, and the sheet she told him to stop bringing in an earlier week is in a drawer with clips in it and it is not a page and it is not the piece of paper with about eleven lines on it.

---

A man of about twenty-eight who works nights said one thing at about ten in the morning, in four seconds, to a customer, and it was not improved on.

"**I work nights and I went home through it at two this morning and about four of the streets were already under and nobody was on any of them, and I did not stop and nobody has asked me why I did not stop.**"

Nobody asked him why he did not stop. Nobody said he should have, and nobody has asked him since, and he was not thanked and is owed nothing.

The water stopped rising in Saltmarket at about eleven and stopped in the Lower Wards at about two in the afternoon, and it went on rising in the third district until about six, and nobody who has said that has said why those two and not the third.

A woman of about thirty-two who runs a food shop in a building in the first of the four districts was open on the Thursday and shut at about four, which is a half-hour earlier than Monday, and she said one thing at half past two to a man of about forty-two who types in an office of nine people in a building in Saltmarket.

"**I am shutting at four and I have told nobody that I am shutting at four, and about four people have already walked past me and gone on, and I would like it known that I did not shut early for fun.**"

He said all right. Nothing was added to it and nobody asked her why she was shutting early and nobody has since.

---

A man of about fifty-three who drives a minibus brought about nine people into the room above a line in Saltmarket at about four in the afternoon, which is when that room usually empties, and he said one thing at the table with his hand on the back of a chair, and nobody said it back better.

"**I have brought the same nine people here three days running and I have never been asked to and I have never been asked to stop, and about four of them have stopped coming and I have noticed and I have not asked them, and I am saying it out loud because nobody else is going to.**"

Nobody said anything. Nobody asked him which four. Nobody thanked him and about four people in that room have since said that somebody ought to have asked, and four have said that asking would have made him stop, and the two groups have not met.

A man of about thirty-two who works in a canteen in a building in the first of the four districts said one thing at about one in the afternoon, in four seconds, to nobody.

"**I have got a floor above me with about nine people in it who are all from the same building as me and not one of them is in my canteen, and I have been on that floor eleven times and I have never once been asked, and I have stopped minding, and I am telling you that I have stopped minding.**"

Nobody said it back better. Nobody said he had better not mind, and nobody has asked him about it since.

A woman of about thirty-four who checks about nine hundred doors in four buildings was in that room on the Thursday for about twenty minutes and did not say what she had come for and has not said since.

---

The woman of about fifty-three got back to the working Ashfields at half past nine and did not open the shop, and she has not opened it since the Monday, and the rent on it does not care what happened to a plastic bag on a counter in Lattice Ward.

She said one thing to a man of about forty-two who types in an office of nine people in a building in Saltmarket, on the Thursday afternoon, in nine seconds, and he has not repeated it.

"**I have been shut since Monday and I have nine people and no list, and there is a man in a repair shop who will not be on a list of mine, and I am telling you so that somebody in this city knows it, and it is not going to change my Friday.**"

He said he would write it down and he did not write it down, and about four people in that office have since said that somebody should have, and four have said that a piece of paper would not have opened her shop, and the two groups have not met.

---

Nine of the eleven people in the three districts who are out of their work this week are out because their work is in a building with a ground floor, and two of the eleven are out because they cannot get to a building and not because of their work at all. Nobody has asked the two. Nobody has asked the nine. The week is not over, and the pay for it is not yet a thing anybody could be paid and not being paid, and the whole of it is a figure that four of them could add up and none of them will, because four of them are the sort of person who does not add things up and the fifth of them has a book and does not want it.

Nobody has named the losing. It is named on a later day, out loud, by the people it happened to, and there is no list and there is no minute and the eleven did not agree to say anything and nobody asked them to.

---

The woman of about thirty-four who checks about nine hundred doors in four buildings said one thing at about twenty past four, in nine seconds, in a room above a line in Saltmarket, and nobody improved on it and nobody thanked her.

"**I have opened about nine hundred doors in four buildings in about four years and I could not open any of them this week if anybody asked me, and about four people in four buildings have now found that out the slow way, and nobody has told me about any of it and I have not asked.**"

Nobody asked her. Nobody said she should have telephoned. She has not telephoned and is not going to, and about four doors out of about nine hundred were shut for two days and nobody in this case has a figure for that.

A first-year of nineteen was at the counter of a repair shop in Lattice Ward and said one small unasked thing at four in the afternoon, in about four seconds, and nobody went further with it and nobody thanked her.

"**A woman came in at about ten and asked for one name and he said no, and about four of us heard it, and about four of the nine people in that room upstairs have got somebody's name and it is not his.**"

Nobody said anything. Nobody asked her how she knew about the room upstairs, and she has not said, and she is not going to be asked.

A woman of about thirty-nine who works in a pharmacy in Saltmarket was in that warm room for about half an hour on the Thursday and said one thing to nobody at about seven, in four seconds.

"**I have taken a sign off a door and four inches of water through the back of a shop and I have not been asked for anything by anybody in four days, and that is the correct arrangement and I would like it recorded that I think it is correct.**"

Nothing was added to it. Nobody said they thought it was correct. Nobody has rung her to say either.

A woman of about twenty-four who works as a resonance nurse at a mobile clinic was in that warm room for half an hour on the Thursday and said nothing at all, and she has not said anything in a room off a service road since a Thursday in an earlier week, and nobody asked her about that and nobody is going to.

A man of about fifty-one with a tool bag was in a warm room off a service road at about half past six and said nothing, and has not been asked about anything for about eleven weeks and one day, and the asking has not been renewed and nobody has stopped it.

At a sink was a cook of twenty-seven who said nothing at all. In the chair nearest the door was a woman of about thirty whom nobody asked anything, nobody thanked and nobody apologised to, and the reason she is in that room is the temperature, and the temperature costs more than it did, and both of those are correct.

A woman of about thirty-one who does building maintenance said one thing at about half past seven, in four seconds, to nobody.

"**I have been out since Monday and I have watched four floors of water go up and down a street that I have swept every morning for nine years, and nobody has asked me whether the water was clean, and I have not asked either.**"

Nobody asked. It was not improved on and nobody thanked her, and about four people in that room have since said that they would not have asked either and about four have said nothing.

Nineteen lines on a hardboard on two nails, and the newest of them is a lift mechanic's, eleven words, signed, about the middle, and it is the only promise on that board, and it is one hundred and sixty-eight days old, and a Thursday is a Thursday.

The heating is on and costs more, four names on the lease cost more than they did, and a fortnight a cook paid for is unpaid by anybody and is not resolved and is not going to be.

A woman of about thirty-one who does building maintenance said one thing at about six in the evening to about nine people, in nine seconds, and nobody went further with it.

"**I have been out since Monday and nobody has rung me and nobody has blamed me and that is the correct arrangement, and I have worked out that I would rather be telephoned by somebody and told to stay at home, and there is nobody to telephone.**"

Nobody said they would telephone her. Nobody said the arrangement was correct, and she has not been telephoned.

**About eleven people in the three districts were out of their work in the week that began on the Monday, and about nine of them did not know that on the Thursday, and two of them did and told nobody, and the week is not over and the pay for it is not a thing anybody in this case can ask anybody about.**

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at ten.

*The ask at a counter, as a record of about nine seconds each way.* **Asked by: a woman of about fifty-three who runs a hardware shop in the working Ashfields, at about ten past eight on the Thursday, at a counter in Lattice Ward, in about nine seconds, in front of about nine people, with a plastic bag with four inches of river in it. *What the day did not touch.* **A man of twenty-two said no to a thing that would have been useful and nobody has asked him to say yes, and there is no list anywhere of the asks that have been made of him, because a list of those would be a thing he could be found from. About eleven people lost a week of work in the three districts and none of them was asked to describe it, and the describing is not done here and is done on a later day by the people it happened to.
