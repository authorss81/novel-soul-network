# Chapter 371 — The Water Came In On The Monday

The water came into the Lower Wards and Saltmarket and the working Ashfields in the night — three of the four districts, and the fourth of them, the Quiet Quarter, was not touched — and was standing on the floor of about nine ordinary businesses by five in the morning, and **about four rooms and about nine people are what stands between that and a night of it, and it takes four days, and nobody dies, and the four days is longer than a man in the middle would have taken, and nobody in those three districts said that out loud and nobody has to.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last of them at about six in the evening. He was at a bench in Lattice Ward from half past eight until six and knows none of this.

The word was at nine and ten. The room off a service road is four hundred and sixty-nine days old and is warm, and about four people came in on the Monday for the temperature and nobody asked any of them anything.

---

The water came in at the back. That is what a woman of about fifty-three who runs a hardware shop in the working Ashfields said at about seven, to nobody, to a floor with about four inches on it, and she has said it since to about four people and to nobody's face and she was not asked and will not be asked.

A man of about forty-three who runs a laundrette in Saltmarket had machines running until ten. He said one thing at about ten, with his hands still wet, and nobody improved on it.

"**I have got about four machines running on a floor that is under a foot of it and I am going to keep them running until they stop themselves, and I am not going to tell anybody how to do it, because I do not know.**"

Nobody asked him how. Nobody said that was the wrong thing to say.

A woman of about thirty-nine who works in a pharmacy in Saltmarket walked in at about a quarter to eight and the first thing she did was take the sign down off the door, which took her about four seconds, and put it in a plastic bag, which was the correct thing and which nobody has since thanked her for.

A woman of about thirty-two who runs a food shop in a building in the first of the four districts opened at about seven and was open for two hours. A man of about fifty who owns a garage in the Lower Wards moved four cars out of a basement by hand and could not get a fifth and did not go back for it. He said one thing about the fifth, to himself, in about nine seconds.

"**It is a nine and it is not worth nine hundred pounds and I am not going back down there for it, and if somebody wants to say I left a car, then I left a car.**"

Nobody heard him say it. A man of about forty-two who types in an office of about nine people in a building in Saltmarket stood in a doorway and let about eleven people past him and then stood there again. He said one thing to the last of them, in about four seconds.

"**I am letting you through because the door is open and not because anybody told me to, and if it shuts I am going to shut it, and you are all going to have to think of that yourself.**"

Nobody improved on it and nobody thanked him.

That is six of about nine. **Nobody told anybody else which of the nine would be open, because nobody knew, because there is no procedure in this city for saying in advance that a shop will be shut, and about three of the nine were shut by eight o'clock and one of them had no telephone in it at all and a fourth was open with nobody behind the counter.**

The woman of about thirty-nine in the pharmacy said one thing at eight, to a customer, in four seconds, and nobody improved on it and nobody thanked her.

"**Take what you need off the bottom shelf and go up the stairs, and I am not saying that to get rid of you, and I am also not saying it because I have a plan.**"

Nobody improved on it. Nobody asked her what the plan was, and she has not got one, and she said so afterwards to about four people and was not thanked.

The woman of about thirty-two said one thing at about half past eight to nine people and it took her four seconds.

"**I am open until about ten and after that the door is shut and the reason is the door, and if anybody asks me why I am going to say the door, and I am telling you now so that you know I am going to say the door.**"

Nobody improved on it. Nobody said she was being clever.

A woman of about thirty-five who works in a laundrette two doors from the hardware shop telephoned the room above a line in Saltmarket at ten past seven to say that about three of the nine were shut and did not say which, because she did not know which, and the person who took the call wrote nothing down and could not have told anybody the same thing twice.

---

The room above a line in Saltmarket opened at about seven in the morning, earlier than it has ever opened, and there were nine chairs in it and a table and a sheet with nine words on it face up in the middle of the table where it has been since it was put there.

About nine people came in before nine o'clock. A man of about fifty-three who drives a minibus for a firm that has nothing to do with any of this brought six of them in one load and came back for three more and did not say why he was doing it and was not asked.

**Nobody in that room was in charge of it. Four people in it said something about where people should go and four said something different, and the two groups have not spoken to each other about it, and about four of the nine noticed that they had disagreed and about four did not.**

The man with the minibus said one thing, at ten past eight, standing at the table with his list in his hand, and it took him four seconds, and nobody improved on it.

"**I have brought nine people here off a list I keep in my own head and I am telling you that the list is mine and not yours, and if any of them says they are going somewhere else I am the one who will have been wrong and not you.**"

Nobody said anything. Nobody asked him for the list. Nobody suggested writing it down, and nobody has since, and the list is in his head on the Tuesday and on the Wednesday and is in his head now.

A woman of about thirty-one who does the books for a hall in Saltmarket said one thing at half past eight, and it was the second thing anybody had said in that room that morning that contradicted the first, and she did not know that and was not told.

"**The hall on Ridley Street has a roof and about thirty people in it already and nobody has told them there is a room in Saltmarket, and I am not saying that somebody should tell them, I am saying that I have been standing here for an hour.**"

Nobody answered her. Nobody said she was right and nobody said she was wrong, and about four people in that room heard it and four did not, and four of the nine have since said that somebody should have told the hall and four have not.

The woman of about thirty-five who runs the laundry in the working Ashfields said nothing about the morning to anybody and has not since, and she had four inches across her floor and nine people on it at about nine o'clock and she shut at half past eight and lost the morning, and she did not say what it cost her and nobody asked and she has not been thanked and is owed nothing.

---

The third of the four was the only one of them the water did not reach.

Four feet of shop in the Quiet Quarter, a counter, a kettle, one window, a pull switch by the door and a flat above it with two windows. It stayed lit. It was dry at about nine in the morning and it was dry at six in the evening and it was full, and about nine people came into it in the course of the day who had come out of the other three districts.

**A woman of thirty-five who chose that room's dark for about eleven hours on purpose a week ago, and has not left, and has not been forgiven, and has not been told she has, was standing at the counter in it at ten o'clock on this Monday handing out tea. Nobody in that room said one word to her about the water and she said nothing about it, and four people in that room noticed her being there and four did not.**

She said one thing at half past one, in nine seconds, to nobody, and nobody improved on it and nobody thanked her.

"**About nine people came in here today and none of them asked me anything and that is how this room works, and I am not going to start a different thing today because there is water outside.**"

Nobody said anything. Nobody asked her what the different thing would have been.

Nobody has put anybody in the flat above it. Nobody has offered it. It was empty on Monday morning and it was empty on Monday evening, and the woman and the child in it are not on any list anybody in this case has, because there is no list.

A man of about fifty-four who came in out of the Ashfields at ten said one thing to her about the kettle, in four seconds, and it was the only thing anybody said to her in that room all day.

"**I do not know anything about you and I have got a wet coat on and I would like some tea, and that is the whole of why I am talking to you.**"

She said all right. Nobody improved on either. Nobody asked her who she was and nobody has since and nobody in this case is going to.

---

A man of about twenty-nine who works on lifts was in a building in the first of the four districts at about eleven on the Monday, and the lift in that building was between the ground floor and the first from about ten, and he was not sent for and he was there.

**Nobody in this case was asked for him. Nobody in that building knew he was in it until he was in it, and about four hundred competent people in this city can each write down what to do about a building with no lift, and not one of them has anywhere to write it that a person can be found at.**

---

A woman of about thirty-nine who works in a pharmacy in Saltmarket said one thing at about nine in the morning, in four seconds, to nobody, in that building.

"**I have taken a sign off a door and put it in a bag and about four people have since told me that was the right thing, and about four have told me it was the wrong thing, and both of those groups have never met and neither of them has asked me.**"

Nobody asked her. Nobody improved on it and nobody thanked her, and the sign is in a plastic bag and the bag is in a drawer.

A first-year of nineteen was at the counter of a repair shop in Lattice Ward from about half past eight and said one small unasked thing at about ten to four in the afternoon, in four seconds, and nobody improved on it and nobody thanked her.

"**There are three people in that building who cannot get down and about four people downstairs who have been saying for an hour that somebody will come, and nobody has come, and the man who came came on his own, and that is a different thing.**"

Nobody said anything. Nobody asked her which building. Nobody said that somebody always comes.

A man of about fifty-one with a tool bag was in a warm room off a service road at half past six and said nothing, and has not been asked about anything for about ten weeks and five days, and the asking has not been renewed and nobody has stopped it.

A man of fifty-four who came in out of the Ashfields said one thing at seven, in four seconds, and nobody improved on it and nobody thanked him.

"**There is a room in the third of the four districts with about nine people in it and nine chairs and a table, and about four of those people have been in this room, and I am not saying they should have come here, I am saying that they are somewhere.**"

Nobody said anything. Nobody asked him which four. Nobody told him that this room is in a different district, which it is.

At a sink was a cook of twenty-seven who said nothing at all. In the chair nearest the door was a woman of about thirty whom nobody asked anything, nobody thanked and nobody apologised to, and the reason she is in that room is the temperature, and the temperature costs more than it did, and both of those are correct.

A woman of about thirty-two who runs a food shop in a building in the first of the four districts said one thing at about seven in the evening in that warm room, in four seconds, to nobody.

"**I lost a morning and a half of trade in two days and nobody has said anything about it to me, and I would like somebody to say the days out loud, and about four people have said the days out loud to me and about four have not, and the two have not met.**"

Nobody said the days out loud. Nobody improved on it and nobody thanked her, and about nine businesses in three districts lost days that week and only two of them have been said out loud.

Nineteen lines on a hardboard on two nails, and the newest of the nineteen is a lift mechanic's, eleven words, signed, about the middle, and it is the only promise on that board and it is one hundred and sixty-five days old, and nothing about a lift that stopped between two floors in a building in the first of the four districts has made it more or less true.

The heating is on and costs more, about four names on the lease cost more than they did, and a fortnight a cook paid for is unpaid by anybody and is not resolved and is not going to be.

**The water is in three districts and four rooms and about nine people are what is between it and a night of it, and it will be about four days before it is down, and nobody died on the first of the four days, and nobody in this case has anything to say about that either.**

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at ten.

*374. Monday of week 135, at about ten on a service road, and this entry is water in three districts, about nine ordinary businesses, about four rooms, about nine people at a counter in one of them, a lift between two floors in a building in the first of the four districts, a man who was not sent for, and nobody's name on any of it.*

*Conditions and docket.* **Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last at about six in the evening, all of them at a bench in Lattice Ward, and he was told none of this and did not ask. Water standing on the floor of ordinary businesses at five in the morning: yes, in three of the four districts, in six of the nine that anybody can name, and three of the nine that nobody in this case can name. Businesses shut by eight o'clock that nobody had been told would be shut: three. Businesses open with nobody behind the counter: one. Procedures in this city for saying in advance that a shop will be shut: none, and there has never been one, and nobody invented one this morning. One room above a line in Saltmarket opened at about seven, earlier than it has ever opened, nine chairs, a table, and a sheet with nine words on it face up in the middle where it has been. People in that room before nine o'clock: nine. Of those, people who said something about where other people should go: four, in four directions, and the four have not compared notes, and four of the nine noticed and four did not. One list made of who was in that room: one, kept by a man of about fifty-three in his own head, not asked for and not written down. One room of the four the water did not reach: the four feet of shop in the Quiet Quarter, lit at about seven, nine people through it in the day, and the flat above it empty, nobody offered it, and nobody in this case has a list. Cars moved out of a basement by hand: four, and a fifth that stayed. One lift between the ground floor and the first from about ten in a building in the first of the four districts. One person sent for: nobody. One room off a service road from half past six, nine people. The rate held at nine and ten. The heating is on, dearer, four names on the lease dearer still, a fortnight a cook laid out that nobody has repaid. Room off a service road: four hundred and sixty-nine days. Card in a rail in Lattice Ward: four hundred and seventy-three days, a third object, in a third building, and the two have never been in one room. Hardboard, on two nails: twelve lines three hundred and eighty-nine days, the thirteen three hundred and forty, the fourteen three hundred and five, the fifteen two hundred and eighty-four, the sixteen two hundred and fifty-nine, the seventeen two hundred and forty-one, the eighteen one hundred and eighty-seven, the nineteen one hundred and sixty-five. Wall unchanged: nineteen lines, thirteen hands, fourteen names, none added, none removed, none rubbed off. Line one is a cook's and has never moved. Line nineteen is a lift mechanic's, the newest, and the only promise on that board. Eight things in a ring binder, unopened, unread, shut. The woman's page is unread and is shut with them. The master's fifth line empty, its drawer unopened. The Exchange did not sit on this day. Its last sitting was a Wednesday nineteen days ago and its next one is a Wednesday nine days from today. The book stands at fifty-one lines, the tin at seventy-three, the count announced twenty-seven times, and the business nineteen years old. The ask stands at twenty-two weeks and five days and was made at the seventeenth sitting. All four printed, none converted.**

*The nine, as a record of ordinary businesses on an ordinary morning.* **Six of the nine can be named from what has been said in a room, and the six are a hardware shop in the working Ashfields with four inches on its floor by seven, a laundrette in Saltmarket with its machines running until ten, a pharmacy in Saltmarket whose sign came off the door in four seconds, a food shop in a building in the first of the four districts open for two hours, a garage in the Lower Wards with four cars moved out of a basement by hand and a fifth left, and an office of nine people in a building in Saltmarket whose doorway let eleven people past it. The other three are not described here and are not going to be. Whether any of the nine was told that the others were in it: no, and there was no way to tell any of them, and nobody made one this morning. Times a business was rung on the Monday morning and answered: four. Times a business answered and said it was open: four. Times a business answered and said it was shut: four, and three of them are the three nobody had been told would. Owner: the nine, and the Monday, and nobody.**

*The four days, as a record of a figure nobody has said out loud.* **First day in the three districts: the Monday of week one hundred and thirty-five. Whether the water will be down by the Friday: nobody in those three districts knows on the Monday, and the chapter that says it will be is the chapter that is on the Monday, and nothing written here reports any day but this one. Whether anybody died: no, on this day. Whether anybody thought at about eight in the morning that it would be four days: about four did and about four did not, and the two groups have not met, and the two groups were not the same groups on the Friday. Whether the work would have been done faster by a person in the middle of it: yes, and there was no person in the middle of it on Monday morning and there is no way of getting one, and that is the whole of the reason anybody expects it to be four days, and nobody in those three districts has said it and nobody is going to. Whether anybody asked the man of about thirty-three who has held four buildings and about nine hundred doors for a hundred and two days with no stamp, no reference, no date and nobody's authority behind him whether he was needed: no, and nobody has, and he was not told that any of this was happening. Improved on: by nobody. Thanked: by anybody, no. Owner: the Monday, and the four rooms, and nobody.**

*The lift, as a record of a machine and a man who came on his own.* **Building: one in the first of the four districts. Fault: the car between the ground floor and the first, from about ten in the morning. People above the ground floor at eleven: about eleven, of whom about four could not use a stair. How many he got down that day: nobody wrote it down and nobody has since said. Person sent for by anybody: nobody, and no procedure exists in this city for sending. Person who came: a man of about twenty-nine who works on lifts, who was in that building and was not called, and about four of the people in the building have since said that he was quick and about four have said nothing, and the two groups have not spoken. Times anybody said his name: none. Whether he was asked to be there: no. Whether he was thanked: no, and he has not been thanked and is not going to be, and he is owed nothing. Whether anything was written down about any of it: no, and there is nowhere in this city to write it that a person can be found at. Owner: him, and the car, and nobody.**
*What the day did not touch.* **Water went into nine ordinary places and it went into no document, and nobody wrote down which eight it had been in, and the nine businesses that were open have not been compared with each other by anybody. The page of paper with about eleven lines on it in a woman's hand with four other hands in it was not copied that day and is not finished in eight of its nine copies, and the first disagreement in its fourth line has still not been found and was not looked for. Four rooms in four districts were not merged and no merger was proposed, because none was asked for, and the night rota of four sentences was not looked at by anybody in any of the three districts, and the three empty nights in it are still three. A hardboard on two nails in a different building was not written on, a card in a rail did not move, a drawer stayed shut, and the folded page at the bottom of a ring binder is where it was on Monday. Nobody died. Nobody thanked anybody. Entry 374.**
