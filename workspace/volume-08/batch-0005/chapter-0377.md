# Chapter 377 — The Sixth Friday, And A Sheet In A Jiffy Bag

A company training room in Crown Terrace ran its floor on the Friday evening of week one hundred and thirty-six for the sixth time in four months, and **a laminated sheet arrived at that building in a jiffy bag, and it was put in a drawer and not on the post, and a man of about thirty-six was asked in that room whether he was on the list and said no in about four seconds, and nobody referred to any other Friday and nobody is going to.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last of them at about six in the evening, and there are no jobs of his after about six on a Friday. He was at a repair shop in Lattice Ward until about half past four.

His word was at nine and ten. The room off a service road is four hundred and eighty days old, and it is warm, and four people came in on the Friday for the temperature and nobody asked them anything.

---

Nine folding chairs and about eleven people on the second floor. Seven of the eleven are on the company's list and four are on none. The post by the corridor end is bare and has been bare for about four weeks.

The run went at half past seven. Nine people, one floor, about nine minutes, called from the front by a woman of thirty-six with a lanyard on who works there. Nobody read anything. Nothing was handed to anybody. Nobody counted for anybody and nobody prompted anybody.

**Nobody in that room referred to any other Friday. Not one of the about eleven, and not the woman of thirty-six, and and no one in that building could tell you which Friday of them it was.**

---

The jiffy bag came at about ten past seven on the Friday, before the run, and it was left at the desk by the fire door and nobody in that room saw who left it, and the woman of thirty-six opened it at about a quarter to eight in front of about nine people.

It was one laminated sheet. It is about eleven lines long and it says what a run is and who calls it, and it does not say who is expected, and the copy that came out of that bag is the one that was taken off that post four weeks ago, and nobody said so and everybody in that room could tell.

She said one thing about it, in four seconds, and nobody improved on it and nobody thanked her.

"**It came back. I am not putting it on that post and I am not throwing it away, and it is going in the second drawer of that desk, and that is where it will stay, and I am telling you that so that when you find it in about four months you do not think somebody has hidden it.**"

Nobody said anything. Nobody asked to see it. Nobody asked her to put it back and about four people in that room looked at the drawer and about four looked at the post, and the two groups have not spoken and are not going to.

The drawer is the second one on the left of a desk by the fire door. It does not lock. It is not a form store and there is nothing in that building that keeps a register of what is in a drawer.

---

Afterwards, at ten to nine, the woman of thirty-six asked a man of about thirty-six who works there a question, and he answered in four seconds, and nobody improved on it and nobody thanked him.

"**Are you on the list?**"

"**No. I have never been on it.**"

Nobody said anything. Nobody said that was a problem and nobody said that it was not, and four people in that room noticed that the answer had been given and about four did not, and about four have since said that the list is a list and about four have said that a list is a list, and the two groups have not met.

**Nobody in that room had ever asked him before, in about four years, and the four who are on no list have never been asked by anybody, and the other three of those four have not been asked since and are not going to be, and that is not a favour and it is not an arrangement and it is on nothing.**

A man of about twenty-nine who comes on a Friday, who has not said a word in that room in four years and a bit, said nothing on this Friday. A woman of about thirty-eight, who said nothing in that room on six Fridays and then broke that silence on the seventh, said one thing this time, in four seconds, and it was about a bus.

"**I am not in until about seven on a Thursday now and I get the same bus both ways, and I have got that bus for about a year and nobody in this room knows that and I am saying it because the post is bare and there is nothing else to say.**"

Nobody improved on it. Nobody asked her what time the bus is. About four people heard it and four did not, and she has not said anything else in that room and nobody has asked.

A woman of about twenty-three who stacks shelves in a place that opens at eight in the second of the four districts was in that room and said nothing at all, and nobody has asked her anything since the evening she asked a man of about forty-four a question, and she has not asked him anything, and the two of them put their chairs back on the stack and did not speak.

---

A man of about twenty-five who is in that room because a man of twenty-seven asked him once in a car park said one thing at the bottom of the ramp, in four seconds, to nobody.

"**About nine of us went up a floor tonight and about four of us do that every Friday and about four of us have never done it twice, and nobody has worked out which four, and I have worked out which four, and I am not saying.**"

Nobody asked him which four. Nobody improved on it and nobody thanked him, and he is owed nothing.

A man of about thirty-six who works in that building and who had come up to the floor for the run said one thing to the woman of thirty-six in the lobby, in four seconds, and she did not improve on it and nobody thanked him.

"**There is a jiffy bag in that room and it has got my building's name on it, and I did not send it and I did not tell her to throw it away, and that is all I am going to say about it in this building.**"

She said all right. Nobody said anything else and nobody has asked him since, and the bag is not in that room and the sheet is in a drawer.

He said a second thing at about a quarter to nine, in four seconds, to a chair, and nobody improved on it and nobody thanked him.

"**I have been in this room about nine times and I have never known what it is for, and I asked one person about it in a car park once and he said come and see, and that is the only instruction I have ever had about it.**"

Nobody improved on it. Nobody told him that was the wrong instruction, and the man of about twenty-seven who gave it heard him and said nothing.

A woman of about thirty-four who was in that room on this Friday said one thing at ten to nine, in four seconds, about the run and not about the drawer.

"**I have done the whole of it properly for about two years and nobody has ever said so, and I am not asking anybody to say so now, and I would like it written down that I have noticed.**"

Nobody wrote it down. Nobody said that noticing was a thing to write down, and four people in that room heard her and four did not, and she has not said anything else in that room.

A woman of about forty-five who was in that room on this Friday said one thing to the man in the booth on the forecourt, in four seconds, and he has never been inside that building and would not be told.

"**You have never been up there and you have never been asked what goes on up there, and I have just come out of it, and I am not asking you for anything, I am saying that you and I are the same this evening and neither of us is.**"

He said nothing. Nobody improved on it. Nobody thanked her, and he has not been asked about it, and four of the eleven have since said that it was about four seconds well spent and four have said nothing.

They went out through a lobby and up a ramp and onto a forecourt with about nine cars on it and a man in a booth who has never once been inside that building and does not know any of the eleven and has never been asked to and would not be told.

A man of about fifty-eight who has been in that room for four years and who came for the sake of being in it said one thing at the bottom of the ramp, in four seconds, to nobody, and nobody improved on it and nobody thanked him.

"**I am not going to be in this room in the autumn and I have not told anybody that and I am telling nine strangers on a forecourt because there is nobody else in this city I could tell, and that is a reason and it is not a good one.**"

Nobody said anything. Nobody said the autumn back to him and nobody said they were sorry, and he got into a car and four of the people in that room have since said that he looked tired and four have said nothing, and the two have not met.

---

A man of about forty-two who types in an office of nine people in a building in Saltmarket was in that warm room for about twenty minutes and said one thing at about seven, in about nine seconds, and nobody improved on it and nobody thanked him.

"**There is a piece of paper on a board in a canteen in the first of the four districts and it has been there nine days and about nine people have read it and not one of them has taken it down, and I have not taken it down either, and I do not know what a person is supposed to do with that.**"

Nobody said what a person is supposed to do with that. Nobody said it was somebody else's board, and nobody has rung the man who put it up, and he has not taken it down.

About nine people came in at half past six into a warm room off a service road, and a cook of twenty-seven was at a sink and said nothing at all. In the chair nearest the door was a woman of about thirty whom nobody asked anything, nobody thanked and nobody apologised to. The reason she comes is the heat. The heat costs more than it did. Both of those are correct.

A woman of about thirty-five who works in a laundrette in the working Ashfields was in it and said one thing in four seconds about a shop opposite nine lorries, and nobody improved on it and nobody thanked her, and four people in that room had heard and about four had not.

A woman of about thirty-two who runs a food shop in a building in the first of the four districts was in that warm room for about twenty minutes and said one thing in four seconds, to nobody.

"**There is a number at the foot of a card on a wall where I work and I have written it down, and I have written it down in a book, and I have shown it to about four people, and I am telling you that and not telling you why.**"

Nobody asked her why. Nobody improved on it and nobody thanked her, and the book is a book and is on nobody's system.

A woman of about twenty-three who stacks shelves in a place that opens at eight in the second of the four districts was in that warm room for half an hour and said one thing in four seconds, to nobody.

"**I asked a man a question in that building last week and he answered it and about four people heard him and about four did not, and I have been thinking about that for seven days, and I still do not know whether he wanted to be asked.**"

Nobody improved on it. Nobody told her whether he wanted to be asked, and she has not been in that building since, and nobody asked her about it.

A man of about fifty-one with a tool bag was in it and said nothing, and has not been asked about anything for about twelve weeks and two days, and the asking has not been renewed and nobody has stopped it.

Nineteen lines and two nails, and the newest of the nineteen is eleven words a man of twenty-nine wrote about the middle, and it is the only promise on that board and is one hundred and seventy-six days old. The heating is on and costs more, the lease costs more for four names than it did, and the fortnight a cook paid for is paid for by nobody and is not resolved.

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.

*The sheet that came back, as a record of an object and a drawer.* **Object: one laminated sheet, about eleven lines, saying what a run is and who calls it and not saying who is expected. How it came: in a jiffy bag, left at a desk by the fire door at about ten past seven, by a person nobody in that room saw. Opened: at about a quarter to eight, in front of about nine people, by the woman of thirty-six with a lanyard on. Statement about it: about four seconds, that it came back, that it is not going on the post, that it is not being thrown away, that it is going in the second drawer on the left of the desk, and that she is saying so that when somebody finds it in about four months they do not think it has been hidden. Whether it is the sheet that came off that post about four weeks earlier: yes, and nobody said so and about nine people in that room could tell. Where it is now: a drawer that does not lock. *The list, as a record of a question asked for the first time in about four years.* **Asked by: a woman of thirty-six with a lanyard on who works there, at about ten to nine, in about four seconds, to a man of about thirty-six who works there. Asked: whether he was on the list. Answered, in about four seconds: no, and that he has never been on it. Times he had been asked that before: none, in about four years. People in that room on no list: four. People in that room who have been asked whether they are on it: one, as of this evening. Whether the other three have been asked since: no, and nobody is going to, and that is not an arrangement and it is not a kindness and it is on nothing. Times anybody said the answer was a problem: none. Times anybody said the answer was not a problem: none. People who noticed the answer being given: about four, and the other four did not. Whether the two groups have spoken: no. Owner: the man of about thirty-six, and the list, and nobody.**

*What the day did not touch.* **A floor was run and nothing about it was measured, named, or put on a surface, and none of the six Fridays that room has had has been compared with any other by anybody in it, and there is no way in that building of knowing which of the eleven knows that this is the sixth. A laminated sheet went into a drawer and is not on a form and is not on a list and nobody has asked what a drawer in that building is for. A page of paper with about eleven lines on it in a woman's hand with four other hands in it was not in that room and was not discussed in it, and eight of its nine copies are unfinished, and the first disagreement in its fourth line has not been found. A hardboard on two nails in a room off a service road was not written on, a card in a rail did not move, a drawer there stayed shut, and the folded page at the bottom of a ring binder is where it was on Friday. Nobody died. Nobody thanked anybody. Entry 380.**
