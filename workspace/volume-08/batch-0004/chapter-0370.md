# Chapter 370 — Four Names And A Day

The Thursday evening of week one hundred and thirty-four was the evening the four people who lost a day of pay said so out loud, in their own words, in a warm room off a service road, **and it is eight days since the Wednesday they lost and it is a fortnight since that cooperative was last paid, and one of the eleven is a day short in it, and nobody improved on any of the four of them and nobody thanked any of them and the four rooms in four districts are four rooms and not one.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last of them at about six in the evening. He was at the repair shop in Lattice Ward from about half past eight until about six and was not in that room and knows none of this.

The word was at nine and ten. The room off a service road is four hundred and sixty-five days old and is warm, and four people came in on the Friday for the temperature and nobody asked any of them anything.

---

The cooperative pays on a Thursday every two weeks and it was paid a fortnight ago and this Thursday is the next one, which is a fortnight. Eleven people are on that list and about nine of them got what they expected and four of them did not, and the difference is one day each, and nobody in that cooperative has said anything about the difference.

**There is no officer. The money goes out on a Thursday because one of the eleven has an account and has always had an account and it is not anybody's job and it is not an office, and on this Thursday about nine transfers went out at about ten in the morning and four did not, and nobody has said on any surface anywhere that four did not.**

**Seven of the eleven are not in that room. The four are, and the four came because it is the only warm room any of them knows of, and nobody arranged it and nobody asked them to come and nobody has asked them why they came.**

Seven of the eleven went to work. A courier of twenty-one did a collection on the far side of the city and got back after eleven. Two people are at places in the second of the four districts. A man of about twenty-six who works in a shop in the working Ashfields was in that shop until about six and has not said whether the day cost him anything, because the day did not cost him anything. **Seven of the eleven are somewhere else on the Thursday of a pay day and about four of the seven have said since that they did not think about it and about four have said nothing, and neither group has been asked which they are.**

A man of about twenty-one who works on a counter in a shop in the working Ashfields is not in that room. Nobody asked him to be and nobody has said anything to him since the Friday morning thirteen days ago when a woman of about thirty-four telephoned him and told him that he had been on a rota of four sentences, and he is at the counter on a Thursday until about nine and he has not been asked about any of this and is not going to be.

---

They did not do it in turn. It happened the way things happen in a warm room where about eleven people are standing about at half past six, and it took about nine minutes, and each of the four said about nine seconds and no more.

A woman of about thirty-one who does building maintenance said hers first, and she said it to nobody.

"**I have telephoned that company four times about the Wednesday and four times I have been told it was noted, and the four times were correct, and not one of the four has changed anything, and I am not going to say I minded and I am not going to say I did not.**"

Nobody said anything. Nobody agreed with her and nobody said she was owed it and nobody said somebody should pay her.

A man of about thirty-eight who drives a van said his, in about nine seconds, to the floor.

"**My hire is on a Friday. That is the whole of it. It is not a complaint and I am not asking anybody for anything and I have already told my wife and she said it was a day.**"

Nobody improved on it. Nobody asked his wife anything. He has not been thanked and is not going to be.

A woman of about twenty-six who works shifts in the second of the four districts said hers last, in about four seconds, and she said it to the child of about six, who was on the floor again and who is not hers to leave and who was in the flat on the Monday when the light was off and who was brought here on the Wednesday evening and who was brought again.

"**I am one day short. That is all. I am not going to say it to anybody who can do anything about it because there is nobody.**"

Nobody asked her a question. Nobody said she should put it in writing and she said afterwards that she had put it in writing and that that is where it had ended up.

**And the man of forty-seven who drives a refuse lorry did not say his at all until about seven, and he said it to the woman of about thirty-one who does building maintenance, who had said hers an hour earlier and who had not looked at him when she said it, and who did not look at him now.**

"**I have been a refuse driver for twenty-two years and I have never once not turned up, and I turned up on the Monday, and on the Wednesday I was not there, and that is the first time in twenty-two years and nobody in this room had anything to do with it and I would like it to stay that way.**"

Nobody said anything for about nine seconds. Nobody improved on it. Nobody told him that twenty-two years was a lot and nobody told him it was not.

---

Nobody thanked any of the four. Nobody said that it had been the right thing to do. Nobody in that room said the word *refusal* and nobody said the word *solidarity* and nobody said the word *together*, and about four people in that room wanted to say one of those three and about four did not, and the two groups have not met.

A woman of about thirty-four who checks about nine hundred doors in four buildings was in that room and had not heard any of the four statements before she came in at about half past six, and she did not ask what they were about, and nobody told her, and she has not asked since.

A courier of twenty-one was in that room and is one of the seven who was not on the Wednesday and who lost nothing. He said nothing at all about any of the four, and he was not asked, and nobody has said since that he should have said something, and he has not said it and is not going to.

**Nobody in that room has promised them the money, and nobody has offered to split it, and about four hundred competent people in this city could each write down what is owed to those four and not one of them has a line that a person can be paid on, and the four have not asked.**

---

A man of about thirty-three has held four buildings and about nine hundred doors for ninety-eight days with no stamp, no reference, no date and nobody's authority behind him, and in that time he has needed nothing from anybody and been given nothing, and he was not in that room on that Thursday and has not been in any of the four rooms for about three weeks.

A woman of about thirty-eight, who is in nine rooms in four districts copying out a page of about eleven lines by hand, was not in that room on that Thursday either, and about eight of the nine copies of that page are still not finished, and nobody in that room asked anybody how the copies were getting on and nobody in this case has asked.

A man of fifty-one with a tool bag was in it and said nothing and has not been asked about anything for about ten weeks and a day, and the asking has not been renewed and nobody has stopped it. A first-year of nineteen was at the counter and said one small unasked thing in about four seconds about a corridor in Lattice Ward being busier than it has been for about a month, and that nobody had said why, and nobody improved on it and nobody thanked her.

The woman of about thirty-one who does building maintenance said one last thing to nobody at about half past eight, in about four seconds, and nobody improved on it and nobody was thanked for it.

"**Nobody has to do anything about this. I want that said once and then I am going to go home. Nobody in this room has to do one thing about it, and if somebody does something, they are doing it for themselves, and I will find out on Friday, and that is how it will go.**"

Nobody said anything. Nobody went home early. Nobody has done anything since, and the Friday came, and she found out what she found out.

Nineteen lines on a hardboard on two nails, and the only promise among them is the newest, and it was written by a man of twenty-nine who works on lifts, and it is one hundred and sixty-one days old, and nothing this week has made it any more or less true. The heating is on and costs more, four names on a lease cost more, and a fortnight a cook paid for is still owed to her by nobody and is not resolved and is not going to be.

**Four rooms in four districts were four rooms on that Thursday: one off a service road in the Lower Wards with about eleven people in it, one on the first floor above a row of shops in Saltmarket, four feet of shopfront in the Quiet Quarter that was dark for eleven hours on the Monday and was lit again and has been since, and a weighbridge office in the working Ashfields whose Thursday night had nobody on it a fortnight ago. Nobody has merged them. Nobody is going to. Nobody in this case has any idea whether four of anything is enough, and nobody asked.**

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.

*373. Thursday of week 134, at about ten on a service road, and this entry is four people a day of pay, four statements of about nine seconds, one of about four seconds, one said to a child, one said at about seven, nobody's name on anything, and four rooms that are four rooms.*

*Conditions and docket.* **Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last at about six in the evening, and he was at a bench in Lattice Ward and is in none of this. One cooperative of about eleven people who own their own hours, paid on a Thursday every two weeks; last paid a fortnight ago, this Thursday is the next, which is a fortnight; eleven on the list, about nine paid what they expected, four not. Days between the day that was lost, which was the Wednesday of week one hundred and thirty-three, and the pay day it comes off: eight. Persons present from that cooperative in the room off a service road on this evening: four. Persons not present: seven. Whether the four were asked to come: no, and nobody has asked them why they came. Statements made: four, of about nine seconds, about four seconds and about nine seconds, one at about seven, none in turn and none improved on. Nobody said the right thing to do, nobody thanked any of the four, nobody promised them the money or offered to split it, and nobody asked a question of a person who had just named what they had lost. One courier of twenty-one in that room who was one of the seven and said nothing and was not asked. One child of about six on the floor, brought a second time. One room off a service road from about half past six, about eleven people. The rate held at nine and ten. The heating is on, dearer, four names on the lease dearer still, a fortnight a cook laid out that nobody has repaid. Room off a service road: four hundred and sixty-five days. Card in a rail in Lattice Ward: four hundred and sixty-nine days, a third object in a third building, and the two have never been in one room. Hardboard, on two nails: twelve lines three hundred and eighty-five days, the thirteenth three hundred and thirty-six, the fourteenth three hundred and one, the fifteenth two hundred and eighty, the sixteenth two hundred and fifty-five, the seventeenth two hundred and thirty-seven, the eighteenth one hundred and eighty-three, the nineteenth one hundred and sixty-one. Wall unchanged: nineteen lines, thirteen hands, fourteen names, nothing added, nothing removed, nothing rubbed off. Line one is a cook's and has never moved. Line nineteen is a lift mechanic's and is the only promise on that board. Eight things in a ring binder, unopened, unread, shut. The woman's page is unread and is shut with them. The master's fifth line empty, its drawer unopened. The Exchange did not sit on this day. Its last sitting was a Wednesday fifteen days ago and its next one is a Wednesday thirteen days from today, and the book is at fifty-one lines, the tin at seventy-three, the count announced twenty-seven times, and the business nineteen years old. All four printed, none converted.**

*The four statements, as a record of about nine minutes.* **In a warm room off a service road, at about half past six on the Thursday, four people who had each lost one day of pay named it in their own words, not in turn, in about nine minutes, and the words are these. A woman of about thirty-one who does building maintenance, first and about nine seconds: that she has telephoned the company four times about that Wednesday and been told four times that it was noted; that the four times were correct; that not one of the four changed anything; and that she is not going to say she minded and is not going to say she did not. A man of about thirty-eight who drives a van, about nine seconds, to the floor: that his hire is on a Friday; that that is the whole of it; that it is not a complaint; that he is not asking anybody for anything; and that he has told his wife and she said it was a day. A woman of about twenty-six who works shifts in the second of the four districts, about four seconds, to a child of about six: that she is one day short; that that is all; and that she is not going to say it to anybody who can do anything about it because there is nobody. A man of forty-seven who drives a refuse lorry, at about seven, to the woman of about thirty-one who does building maintenance: that he has driven a refuse lorry for twenty-two years and has never once not turned up; that he turned up on the Monday; that he was not there on the Wednesday; that this is the first time in twenty-two years; that nobody in that room had anything to do with it; and that he would like it to stay that way. Additions by anybody: none. Replies: none. Times any of the four was asked a question: none. Times anybody said they had been right to lose it: none. Times anybody thanked them: none. Improved on: by nobody. Owner: the four, and the day, and nobody.**

*The not-being-asked, as a record of ninety minutes.* **What was not said in that room between about half past six and about eight: the words refusal, solidarity, and together, and none of the three was said by anybody in about eleven people, and about four of them wanted to say one of the three and about four did not, and the two groups have not met. What was not offered: money, a split, a promise, a note, a letter, an apology, or anybody's time. What was not asked: what any of the four had been thinking, whether any of them minded, whether anybody intended to pay it back, and what anybody in that room intended to do about it. Words about how four hundred competent people in this city could each write down what is owed and none of them has a line a person can be paid on: in a narrator's sentence and not said by anybody. Whether the four have asked anybody for anything: no, and they are not going to, and nobody in that room has offered and nobody is going to. Owner: the room, and nobody.**

*The four rooms, as a record of four objects and not one.* **One: off a service road in the Lower Wards, warm, about eleven people in it on this evening, a hardboard on two nails in it, no name on the door, no key. Two: the first floor above a row of shops in Saltmarket, nine chairs and a table, a sheet with nine words on it face up in the middle. Three: four feet of shopfront in the Quiet Quarter, a counter, a kettle, one window, a pull switch, a flat above it with two windows; dark for eleven hours on the Monday of the previous week by somebody's hand, lit again at about six that evening, and lit since. Four: a weighbridge office in the working Ashfields, a weighbridge outside it, a telephone on a wall with a pencil tied to it by a piece of string, and a Thursday night a fortnight ago with nobody in it. Agreements between them: none in writing and none in a mouth, and there has never been a meeting of the four and no minute of one exists. Mergers proposed: none. Mergers refused: none, because none was asked for. Times anybody has said whether four of anything is enough: none, and nobody in this case has an opinion and nobody has been asked for one. Owner: the four, and nobody.**

*What the day did not touch.* **Four people named what they lost in about nine seconds each and nobody improved on it and nobody was thanked and nobody was repaid. Seven of eleven in that cooperative were not in that room and one of them said nothing about it and was not asked. A page of paper with about eleven lines on it was not copied again and about eight of the nine copies are not finished, and nobody in that room said whether the copying is a good thing. The four people who lost a day were not repaid by anybody, the four rooms in four districts did not agree on anything at any point on that Thursday, a card in a rail did not move, a hardboard on two nails was not written on, a drawer stayed shut, and the folded page at the bottom of a ring binder was not read. Nobody died. Nobody thanked anybody. Entry 373.**
