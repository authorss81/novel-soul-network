# Chapter 365 — One Working Day

The Monday morning of week one hundred and thirty-three was the morning a cooperative of about eleven people who own their own hours stopped moving a company's building for one working day, and **the refusal was made in about nine minutes in a rented room over a kitchen and was finished inside the day, and it cost four of them a day of pay, and the day of pay is real and is nobody's fault and has not been refunded by anybody and is not going to be.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last of them at about six in the evening. He was at the repair shop in Lattice Ward for the whole of the day and was in no room in this entry and was not asked about any of it and does not know what happened in a yard in the working Ashfields on the Friday before this.

The word was at nine and ten. The room off a service road is four hundred and fifty-five days old and is warm, and four people came in on the Friday for the temperature and nobody asked any of them anything.

---

The cooperative is eleven people and a rented room with a kitchen in it and a table that four of them bought in about two years. It has no name over the door and no office and no register of members and no person whose job it is to know where anybody is.

They are not a company and not a guild and not a body. **They own their own hours, which means that nobody tells them when to start and nobody tells them when to stop, and it also means that when there is no work on a day they do not get paid for that day, and about nine people in that room have known that for the whole time they have been in it and about four learned it this week.**

The job was two days. A company in the first of the four districts was moving the contents of a building from one street to another and the move was booked for the Wednesday and the Thursday of this week and eleven people had been booked for both of them.

---

On the Friday before, in a yard in the working Ashfields at about half past three in the afternoon, the man who had the building said one thing to about four of the eleven.

"**If Wednesday is not done there is not a Thursday.**"

He said it to four people and about four of the eleven were within about nine feet of it and about four were not, and he said it once, and he was not being cruel and he was not threatening anybody and he was telling the truth about a supplier's diary in a building where about nine hundred doors do not open for a person with a card.

**Nobody in that yard repeated it to the other seven and nobody has since and nobody is going to, and the four who heard it have not said to the other seven that they heard it, and the seven have not asked.**

On the Monday morning at about ten, in the rented room over the kitchen, the eleven were told the whole of it by a man of about thirty-four who works for the company that has the contract and who has a job and is not a villain and is owed nothing.

They voted. It took about nine minutes and it was the only vote anybody in that room has ever taken and there is no minute of it and no one is going to write one. **About nine of the eleven were for it and about two were not and the two did not stop it, and nobody has asked either of them since why and neither of them has said.**

The refusal is one working day. The Wednesday is not done. **The Thursday has not been touched and is not going to be cancelled, and all eleven are still on it, and about four people in that room do not yet know what Thursday is going to be like.** The company found out at about eleven and asked for a reason in four minutes, and about four of the eleven gave about four different reasons and not one of them was what the man in the yard had said, and nobody in that room said the yard sentence out loud then or since, and about four of the eleven have wondered since whether the company will ever work that out and about four have not.

**It is a Wednesday-to-Thursday kind of refusal and it was done in a morning and finished in a day, and that is the whole of it, and there was no second day and nobody sat in a room waiting for anybody to give in.**

---

Four of the eleven were on the Wednesday list and did not work on the Wednesday and were not paid for it.

A man of forty-seven who drives a refuse lorry for a company in the first of the four districts, and who has been in that cooperative for about two years, and who has a life outside it that nobody in that room has asked about. A woman of about thirty-one who does building maintenance. A man of about thirty-eight who drives a van. And a woman of about twenty-six who works shifts at a place in the second of the four districts.

Each of them lost a day. The day is money and it is not an abstraction and it has not been given back by the company, which did not cancel the Thursday, and it has not been given back by the cooperative, which pays on a Thursday every two weeks and the next one is the Thursday of next week, and it has not been given back by anybody.

The man of forty-seven said one thing about it, in about four seconds, when somebody in that room asked him whether he was all right, and nobody improved on it.

"**I am not going to say I did not mind and I am not going to say I minded, and I am going to be there on the Thursday, and that is the whole of what I have got to say about the Wednesday.**"

Nobody said anything. Nobody asked him a second question and about four people in that room wanted to and did not, and he has not brought it up since and has not been asked to.

**The four are not owed anything by this case. They are not owed anything by each other. They have not been thanked and they are not going to be thanked and they did not do it to be thanked.**

---

The other seven were not on the Wednesday list. About four of them wanted to say, in that room, on that morning, that the refusal was not their choice, and about four of the eleven did not, and the four who wanted to say it did not say it, and one of them has said since that he wanted to and did not and that he is not going to now either.

A courier of twenty-one was in that room and is one of the seven and owns his own hours and had been in that cooperative for about four years. He said one thing in about four seconds and nobody improved on it.

"**I was not on the Wednesday. I want that said before anybody starts thanking me for a Monday morning I was not in the middle of. I have been the one people ring for about four years and I was not in the middle of this, and the two of those are both true and I am not going to be asked about the difference.**"

Nobody said anything. Nobody thanked him. Nobody said he should have been on the Wednesday list and nobody said he was lucky, and about four people in that room wanted to say one of those two things and did not, and nobody has since.

A woman of about thirty-one who does building maintenance, who is one of the four, said one more thing while the room was emptying, in about four seconds, to the man of forty-seven.

"**If anybody in here says on the Thursday that it was worth it, I am going to go home. I have thought about that for ten minutes and that is what I have come to and I am telling you now so that you do not do it.**"

He said: "**Nobody was going to.**"

And about four people in that room believed him and about four did not.

---

He was at the repair shop in Lattice Ward from about half past eight until about six, and he did eleven jobs and one of them twice, and at about half past three a woman of about thirty-four who checks about nine hundred doors in four buildings came in and asked him a practical question about a catch on a door, and he answered it practically and at some length, and she did not mention the cooperative and he did not ask.

About half past six the room off a service road was warm and about nine people were in it. A cook of twenty-seven was at a sink and said nothing at all. A woman of about thirty was in the chair nearest the door, and she was not asked anything and was not thanked and was not apologised to, and the reason she comes is the temperature, and the temperature costs more than it did, and both of those are correct.

A man of fifty-one with a tool bag was in it and said nothing and has not been asked about anything for about eight weeks and six days. A first-year of nineteen was at the counter and said one small unasked thing in about four seconds, which was that a company in the first of the four districts had put a second van outside a building on the Wednesday and had taken it away by the Thursday, and that nobody in that room knew whether that had anything to do with anything. Nobody improved on it and nobody thanked her.

The hardboard is a hardboard on two nails, it has nineteen lines on it, and the nineteenth of them belongs to a man of twenty-nine who works on lifts and is the only promise on that surface and is one hundred and fifty-one days old. The heating is on, it is dearer, the four names on the lease are dearer, and a fortnight a cook of twenty-seven covered is covered for by nobody. **Four people lost a day on the Monday of this week and the room came in on the Monday evening, and nobody in that room said a word about it and nobody has since.**

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.

*368. Monday of week 133, at about ten on a service road, and this entry is about eleven people, one refused Wednesday, one vote of about nine minutes with no minute of it, four people a day of pay, one yard, and one Thursday that was done anyway.*

*Conditions and docket.* **Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last at about six in the evening, and he was at a bench in Lattice Ward and is in none of this. One cooperative of about eleven people who own their own hours, in a rented room over a kitchen, no name over the door, no office, no register, and no person whose job it is to know where anybody is. One job booked for two days, the Wednesday and the Thursday of this week, eleven people booked for both. One thing said in a yard in the working Ashfields at about half past three on the Friday before, to about four of the eleven, once, by a man with a building. One meeting of about nine minutes at about ten on the Monday morning. The only vote anybody in that room has ever taken; no minute of it and none is going to be written. For the Wednesday refusal: about nine. Against: about two, and neither of the two stopped it and neither has been asked since. One working day refused, on a Monday, inside one day. The Thursday: not touched, not cancelled, all eleven still on it. Times the company asked for a reason: one, in four minutes. Reasons given: about four, all different, and none of them the sentence a man said in a yard. Times the yard sentence was said out loud in that room: none. People who were on the Wednesday list and did not work it: four. Days of pay lost: four, and refunded by nobody. Pay day in that cooperative: a Thursday every two weeks, and the next one is the Thursday of next week, which is ten days from today. One room off a service road from about half past six, about nine people. The rate held at nine and ten. The heating is on, dearer, four names on the lease dearer still, a fortnight a cook laid out that nobody has repaid. Room off a service road: four hundred and fifty-five days. Card in a rail in Lattice Ward: four hundred and fifty-nine days, a third object in a third building, and the two have never shared a room. Hardboard, on two nails: twelve lines three hundred and seventy-five days, the thirteenth three hundred and twenty-six, the fourteenth two hundred and ninety-one, the fifteenth two hundred and seventy, the sixteenth two hundred and forty-five, the seventeenth two hundred and twenty-seven, the eighteenth one hundred and seventy-three, the nineteenth one hundred and fifty-one. Wall unchanged: nineteen lines, thirteen hands, fourteen names, nothing added, nothing removed, nothing rubbed off. Line one is a cook's and has never moved. Line nineteen is a lift mechanic's and is the only promise on that board. Eight things in a ring binder, unopened, unread, shut. The woman's page is unread and is shut with them. The master's fifth line empty, its drawer unopened. The Exchange did not sit on this day. Its last sitting was a Wednesday five days ago and its next one is a Wednesday twenty-three days from today, and the book is at fifty-one lines, the tin at seventy-three, the count announced twenty-seven times, and the business nineteen years old. All four printed, none converted.**

*The cooperative, as a record of about eleven people.* **What it is: eleven people who own their own hours, a rented room over a kitchen, a table four of them bought, and no name over the door. What it is not: a company, a guild, a body, a firm, a union, a society, or an institution, and it has not become any of them. Register of members: none. Officer: none. Person whose job it is to know where anybody is: nobody, and there is no list of who is in the room on any day and there never has been. What owning their own hours means in practice, as the room puts it: that nobody sets a start and nobody sets a finish, and that a day with no work on it is a day with no pay on it, and about nine people in that room had known that for the whole time they have been in it, and four of them learned the second half of that sentence on this morning. Votes ever taken in that room: one, of about nine minutes, with no minute of it. Times anybody has asked the two who were against why: none. Owner: the eleven, and the table, and nobody.**

*The yard, as a record of one sentence on a Friday.* **Said by: a man who had the building. Where: a yard in the working Ashfields, at about half past three on the Friday before. To: about four of the eleven, once, and about four more were within about nine feet and about four were not. What he said: that if the Wednesday is not done there is not a Thursday. Whether it was a threat: no, and he was not cruel and he was not trying to frighten anybody. Whether it was true: yes, and it was true about a supplier's diary in a building where about nine hundred doors do not open for a person with a card, and nobody has ever said to him that it was not true. Whether the seven who had not heard it were told: no, and nobody has told them and nobody is going to. Whether the four who heard it have said so to the other seven: no. Owner: the yard, and the man, and nobody.**

*The four, as a record of a day of pay.* **Number: four. Who: a man of forty-seven who drives a refuse lorry for a company in the first of the four districts and has been in the cooperative about two years; a woman of about thirty-one who does building maintenance; a man of about thirty-eight who drives a van; and a woman of about twenty-six who works shifts in the second of the four districts. What each of them lost: one working day, once, in money, and the money is not an abstraction. Refunded by the company: no, and the company did not cancel the Thursday. Refunded by the cooperative: no, and the cooperative pays on a Thursday and the day comes off that Thursday. Refunded by anybody: no, and nobody in this case is going to make it whole and it is not going to be made whole. Whether anybody said it was their choice: nobody has asked them and nobody is going to. Whether anybody thanked them: no, and they did not do it to be thanked. Whether they are owed anything: nothing. Owner: the four, and the day, and nobody.**

*The seven, as a record of the people who were not on the list.* **Number: seven. Among them: a courier of twenty-one who has been in that cooperative about four years, who owns his own hours, who was not on the Wednesday list, and who said one thing in about four seconds to the effect that he was not in the middle of it and was not going to be thanked for a morning he was not in. Whether anybody thanked him: no. Whether anybody said he was lucky: no, and about four people in that room wanted to and did not. Whether anybody said he should have been on the Wednesday list: no, and about four wanted to and did not. How many of the seven wanted to say the refusal was not their choice: about four. How many of them said it: none. Whether that has changed since: no, and one of them has said since that he wanted to and did not and is not going to now either. Owner: the seven, and the room, and nobody.**

*What the day did not touch.* **One day was refused and it was correct and it cost four people money, and the cost has been named by nobody in their own words yet and there is a Thursday coming when the money is not there. Nobody was blamed, nobody was thanked, and about four people wanted to say a thing and did not. A hardboard on two nails was not written on, a card in a rail did not move, a drawer stayed shut, and the folded page at the bottom of a ring binder was not read. Nobody died. Nobody thanked anybody. Entry 368.**
