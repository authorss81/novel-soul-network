# Chapter 366 — All Right

The Wednesday morning of week one hundred and thirty-three was a working Wednesday in a repair shop in Lattice Ward, and **at about eleven a woman of about thirty-four told a man of twenty-two, to his face, in about nine seconds, that she was not going to be the person who brought him the doors any more, and he said all right, and he did not ask her why, and he did not turn it into something he could do, and it has cost him eleven days of work he had already agreed to, and nobody has been told and he has not said.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last of them at about six in the evening. He was at the bench in Lattice Ward from about half past eight until about six and did no other work and took no calls and went nowhere.

The word was at nine and ten. The room off a service road is four hundred and fifty-seven days old and is warm, and four people came in on the Friday for the temperature and nobody asked any of them anything.

---

At about half past nine that morning a man of about forty with an ordinary job stood at that counter and offered him eleven days of cover work on the second and third of a company's rota, and the eleven days were eleven and not ten because that company's term runs the way it runs, and he said yes, and the man wrote nothing down and gave him nothing and the offer was not confirmed by anybody.

**Nobody else in this case knows he said yes. He did not tell the room off a service road and he did not tell anybody in it and there is no sheet and there is no note and there is no box anywhere in this city that has a record of it, and that is the reason the rest of the morning goes the way it goes.**

He went back to the bench. He had a lamp in his hand and a catch off a door and about four of the eleven jobs on the docket, and he did the first one twice because the first one had come out wrong.

The catch came off a door in the first of the four districts, and it took him four minutes, and it is the same catch he fitted to that door in about two years ago, and it has come off again, and nobody has said to him that it comes off, and he has not said. There is a man of about forty-four in that building who has never seen him and a woman of about thirty who works there who has, and neither of them has asked him anything this year.

A man of about forty-four who has been in that room for four years and who does not speak in it, who sat in a shut shop in the Quiet Quarter for eight hours on the Wednesday night of the rota without a telephone in it, said one thing in about four seconds at about half past four in the afternoon about a catch that has been coming off the same door for about two years.

"**It came off on the Tuesday and I put it on the Wednesday and it came off again on the Wednesday night. I have been doing that for two years and I have never once told him and I am not going to start, and I would like it noted that I have noticed.**"

Nobody improved on it. Nobody asked him to go and look at it and nobody in that shop went to look at it, and about four people in that room have said since that he was right to say it and about four have said nothing.

He went back to the bench. He had eleven jobs on the docket and four of them were done and seven were not, and he finished three of the seven before about six and did not finish the other four, and that is an ordinary Wednesday and nothing in it is unusual.

---

She came in at about eleven. She is about thirty-four and her job is to check about nine hundred doors in four buildings in four districts, and she has a list and a pen and about nine minutes before whatever she has to be somewhere for, and about nine hundred is a figure nobody in this case has ever put beside any other, and it is hers and it is not on a form and she is not a rate and nobody has ever asked her for it.

She has been bringing him the doors she could not open for about four years. **About four people in four buildings have been told his name by her and about four have not, and about four doors a month come to that counter out of about nine hundred and none of them is his and there is no column on her list for a door that is not hers.**

She said it in about nine seconds and she did not build up to it and she did not apologise for it and she did not explain it afterwards.

"**I am not going to be the one who brings you the doors any more.**"

"**All right.**"

That is about nine seconds.

Nobody said anything else in that shop for about nine seconds, and the shop is a shop and there is a bell on the door and the bell went twice.

He did not ask her why. He did not say he was sorry. He did not say he understood, and she had not given a reason to understand. He did not ask her what she was going to do with them instead. And he did not say that he would see what he could do, and he did not say that somebody would have to be found, and he did not offer to find somebody.

**About four people in that shop were within about nine feet of it, and about four of them noticed that he did not do any of those five things, and about four did not, and the two groups have not spoken to each other and are not going to.**

Then she said one more thing, in about four seconds, and nobody improved on it.

"**I am not saying you did anything. I have not come here to say you did anything. I have come here to say a thing about me, and you are the second person I have said it to and the first one was my own account of it on a Tuesday.**"

Nobody asked her what the first one was. Nobody asked her whether the doors would still come. She put the list under her arm and went out and the bell went twice more.

A man of about twenty-six who works at the counter in that shop, who has a job and is not in this case and is not going to be, said one thing to a colleague in about four seconds after the door had shut.

"**He did not ask her one question. Four years I have been on this counter and I have never once seen anybody not ask.**"

His colleague said nothing. **About four people in that shop have said since that they noticed, and about four have not, and the man of about twenty-six has not been asked about it and is not going to be, and he is not owed anything and nobody thanked him for saying it.**

---

Nobody in that shop has mentioned it since. Not one of the about four who noticed, and not one of the about four who did not, and about four people in four buildings who have been told his name by her have telephoned that counter in the last fortnight and about four have not, and nobody in this case has said to him that the doors have stopped coming and he has not said it either.

**And nobody has said to him that he said yes to eleven days, and nobody is going to, and no person in this city has a job to ask him whether he is coming.**

The eleven days begin on the Monday of the week after next. He has not told anybody. There is no list he is on and no register and no minute and no reference, and nobody's business is to know, and **the cover was never held for him in a way anybody could enforce, and a cover that nobody is holding is not a thing that anybody chases a person about, and the eleven days are going to pass with him at his own bench on all eleven of them and nobody will be any the wiser except him.**

He is not owed anything for that and it is not a punishment and it is not a loss anybody else will ever be able to see. **Nobody thanked him and nobody thanked her and nobody said that either of them had been fair to the other, and the two of them have not spoken since and neither of them is going to.**

---

At about half past six the room off a service road was warm and about nine people were in it. A cook of twenty-seven was at a sink and said nothing at all. A woman of about thirty was in the chair nearest the door, and she was not asked anything and was not thanked and was not apologised to, and the reason she comes is the temperature, and the temperature costs more than it did, and both of those are correct.

A man of fifty-one with a tool bag was in it and said nothing and has not been asked about anything for about nine weeks. A first-year of nineteen was at the counter and said one small unasked thing in about four seconds about a bench in Lattice Ward having had a job out of its ordinary order on the Wednesday, and that nobody had said why, and nobody improved on it and nobody thanked her.

The record between two workrooms four miles apart did not run on this day and was not mentioned by anybody, and it does not run on Wednesdays, and nothing in this entry belongs to it.

There are nineteen lines on a hardboard on two nails in that room and the last of them is a lift mechanic's and is the only thing on it that is a promise, and it is one hundred and fifty-three days old and nobody has added to it. The heating is on and costs more than it did, four names on a lease cost more, and a fortnight a cook paid for is unpaid and is not going to be paid.

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.

Whether he is findable: in four places, and this is the one that has just closed and the other three are open. Owner: nobody, and four buildings, and four years.**

Eleven days of work are not going to happen and there is no document in this city that says he had them.
