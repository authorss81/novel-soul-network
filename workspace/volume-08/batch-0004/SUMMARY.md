## 1. What Movement II delivered, in one paragraph

Four rooms in four districts each said one sentence about a night, and **there is no agreement anywhere in this case, because an agreement is a document and there is none**, and by the Monday evening there is a rota with four names in it, three empty nights, and no way of telling which room is on tonight. One of the four said his sentence because he was asked, at about half past three in a weighbridge office, and nobody asked him what his Thursdays are. A man of twenty-two was asked to be a fifth night in a corridor at about ten past four on the Monday and said no in about nine seconds, **and that is the second refusal of his in this case, and it is not the first one, and the difference is that the first was of a role and this is of a night.** At about ten past ten the following Thursday, with about four people in a warm room saying whose night it was, a first-year of nineteen said **asking somebody is not the same as having them**, in nine words, and nobody improved on it and she was thanked by nobody and is owed nothing. **The twenty-second sitting of an Exchange that has run on four-week spacing for nineteen years did not open, for the first of two sittings in a row in the record that have not, and the reason was given in a mouth at about eleven and it is not a rule and it was not decided by her that morning.** A woman of thirty-six with a lanyard took a laminated sheet off a post it had been on for four years and said the cost out loud in about four seconds. A cooperative of about eleven people who own their own hours refused one working day in about nine minutes with one vote and no minute of it, and **four of them lost a day of pay, and the losing is real, and about four people wanted to say the refusal was not their choice and none of them did.** On the Wednesday of that week a woman of about thirty-four told a man of twenty-two to his face that she was not going to be the one who brought him the doors any more, and he said all right, and **he did not ask her why, did not say he was sorry, did not say he understood, did not ask what she would do instead and did not say he would see what he could do, and it cost him eleven days of work he had already agreed to, and nobody was told and he did not say.** A room in the Quiet Quarter was dark for eleven hours on purpose by one pull switch, and **the person who chose the dark is not a villain, is owed nothing, is right about the boundary and wrong about the room, and said both of those in about nine seconds each, and a woman and a child in a flat above it paid for it and were repaid nothing.** And the four who lost a day named it out loud, in their own words, in a warm room, and nobody improved on any of it. **The four rooms in four districts are four rooms and not one, and nobody in this case has any idea whether four of anything is enough, and nobody asked.**
