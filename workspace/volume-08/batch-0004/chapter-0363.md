# Chapter 363 — Asking Is Not Having

The Thursday of week one hundred and thirty-two was the day a night rota of four sentences failed, and **it failed correctly, and nothing was anybody's fault, and the sentence that failed had been spoken in good faith on the Monday afternoon in a weighbridge office by a person who had been asked a question first, and the reason it failed was put into the room in nine words at about half past ten that night by a first-year of nineteen, and nobody improved on the nine words and nobody thanked her and she is owed nothing.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last of them at about six in the evening. He was at the repair shop in Lattice Ward from about half past eight until about six and was in none of the four rooms and was not told about the night and did not ask.

The word was at nine and ten. The room off a service road is four hundred and fifty-one days old and is warm, and four people came in on the Friday for the temperature and nobody asked any of them anything.

---

Three nights of it had been worked. Monday the room off a service road answered a call at about eleven at night about a stair light that had gone between two landings and went and looked at it. Tuesday the first floor above the row of shops answered a call at about half past ten about a company that had shut a floor and locked the stair to it, and the person on the telephone had to be let in by a person with a key who is not in this case.

Wednesday the four feet of shop did not get a call at all and the person in it sat from about ten until about six in the morning and nobody has ever found that out. He has a kettle and a counter and a chair and four feet of shopfront and a window, and he sat in it for about eight hours doing nothing, and he has not told anybody that he did and has not been asked, and he was not on the telephone because there is no telephone in that room.

Nobody checked on any of the three. There was nobody whose job it would have been to check, and nobody rang anybody to ask whether they had got a call, and about four people in four rooms have thought since about the Wednesday night and about four have not. **A rota nobody checks is not a rota that failed on Thursday. It is a rota that has never been checked, and those are the same thing and nobody has separated them.**

**On the Thursday morning nobody could find out who was on. That is the first thing that happened and it took about nine minutes, and the reason it took nine minutes is that there is a rota and there is nothing to look at.**

---

**The rota is four sentences and no two people had the same one, and no two people were in the same room when any of the four was said, and that is the reason nobody has been able to say since which of the four had been asked.**

The man who runs that office is a man of about forty-three with an ordinary job and he was in it at about ten on the Thursday morning and he said one thing to about four people in about four seconds, and nobody improved on it.

"**I asked him. I did not ask him whether he could. I have been doing this for about nine years and I asked a man a question and did not finish it, and I am not going to blame him for the half of it that was mine.**"

Nobody in that building said anything to that. About four people in it have said since that he should not have said it in front of the yard, and about four have said nothing, and he has not been asked to say it again and has not offered to.

The man of about twenty-one who works on a counter in a shop in the working Ashfields was at the counter. He was at the counter on the Monday and the Tuesday and the Wednesday and the Thursday, and the counter shuts at about nine, and nobody rang him, and nobody had his number, and nobody asked him.

**Nobody in this case blames him, and he does not think anybody does, and he is not a villain and he is not going to be made the answer to anything, and he is owed nothing and he was thanked by nobody and he is not going to be thanked.**

**Nobody wrote it down. On the Friday morning four people in three rooms were asked separately who had said their sentence and who had been asked, and about four answers said offered and about four said asked, and the two sets did not agree about anybody, and no piece of paper in this city records which of the four was which.**

---

The call came in at about eleven at night on the Thursday to a telephone in a room in the second of the four districts, and it was a man of about forty-four, who has been in that room for four years and who does not speak in it, ringing the one person he knew to be in a room.

Somebody had locked a stair door on the fourth floor of one of four buildings and there was a person on that floor and the stair door was shut and the person on that floor had a key and had not been in the building since about six. That is the whole of the call and it took about four seconds and it is about four buildings that a man of fifty-one can get into and that a person cannot get out of, and it is one of about four hundred, and nobody in that room put the two figures next to each other and nobody has done since.

He rang the weighbridge office because the man who runs the weighbridge office had said, in a yard, at about half past three on the Monday afternoon, in front of about four people, that the weighbridge office was the Thursday.

It rang out. He rang about four other numbers, and two of them were numbers that belonged to a person who had moved in about two years, and the third was the room off a service road.

---

At about half past ten, before the call came, about four people were standing in the room off a service road saying whose night it was, and nobody had the answer and about four of them had said the name of a room and about four had said the name of a person, and it went on for about nine minutes and it was not hostile and it was not an argument and everybody in that room was telling the truth.

Then a first-year of nineteen said a thing, and it took her about four seconds, and it is nine words.

"**Asking somebody is not the same as having them.**"

Nobody said anything for about nine seconds.

Nobody improved on it. Nobody asked her where it had come from and nobody said it was clever and nobody disagreed with it and about four people in that room have given those nine words exactly as she said them since, and about four have not given them at all.

A man of about twenty-seven, who has read and replaced the numbers on the doors in a building in the first of the four districts for four years and who moved a chair off a wall in about nine seconds on a Monday a fortnight ago, said one thing in about four seconds, and it was the only thing anybody said in about nine minutes and it was not an improvement.

"**Then the rota is four rooms and not five, and it was four rooms on the Monday, and the thing that happened on Thursday night is that four rooms is a smaller number than people think it is.**"

Nobody answered him. Nobody agreed with him and nobody disagreed with him and about four people in that room had thought the same sentence and about four had not, and the two groups have not spoken to each other and are not going to. **He did not improve on her and he said so himself afterwards to about four people and to nobody else, and he has not been thanked for it and is not owed anything.**

**She is nineteen. She is a first-year. She is owed nothing, she was thanked by nobody, and she is not going to be thanked, and she is not the answer to anything else that is wrong in this city, and nobody in this case has told her that and nobody is going to.**

**The call came in eleven minutes later.** The woman of about thirty-four took it, and said four sentences into it, and put a coat on over the one she was in, and went, and came back at about ten past twelve.

Nobody thanked her for going. Nobody asked her afterwards whether it had been a good idea, and about four people in that room understood that she had gone because a telephone rang and about four understood that she had gone because a room had said a thing in a yard, and the two groups have not met.

She said one thing when she came back, in about nine seconds, and nobody improved on it.

"**The door was not locked. It had been pulled shut and the closer had caught. He has got a bag in his hand and he is going to be all right and he is not going to be all right, and both of those are true and I am not going to say which one I think it is.**"

Nobody improved on that either. Nobody has asked her since which one she thinks it is, and she has not said, and she is not going to be asked.

---

On the Friday morning the four rooms did not do anything about it. Nobody made a list of who was on and nobody made a list of who had been asked, and nobody rang the man of about twenty-one to tell him, and he found out on the Friday from a woman of about thirty-four who telephoned him at about eleven in the morning and told him in about nine seconds and apologised once and did not apologise twice.

He said one thing back, in about four seconds, and it was not an apology and it was not an excuse and nobody improved on it.

"**I did not say no. I want that written down, and I know that there is nothing to write it down on.**"

Nobody wrote it down. About four people in this case have told about four other people that he said it and about four have not, and he has not asked for it to be put anywhere and has not been asked to put it anywhere.

A courier of twenty-one, who owns his own hours and who said the Tuesday sentence himself and rang two of the other rooms to say it had been said, put one view in about nine seconds at about half past four on the Friday, and nobody improved on it and nobody acted on it.

"**It was not the rota. It was that we had four rooms and seven nights and we all knew that, and we said four sentences anyway, and the four sentences did what four sentences do. If it had been nine sentences in nine rooms it would have been nine rooms and one of them would have been shut.**"

Nobody in that room said whether that was true. Nobody has said since whether it was true, and he has not been asked, and about four people in this case have thought about it and about four have not.

About half past six the room off a service road was warm and about nine people were in it. A cook of twenty-seven was at a sink and said nothing at all. A woman of about thirty was in the chair nearest the door, and she was not asked anything and was not thanked and was not apologised to, and the reason she is in that room is the temperature, and the temperature costs more than it did, and both of those are correct.

A man of fifty-one with a tool bag was in it and said nothing and has not been asked about anything for about eight weeks and a day, and the asking has not been renewed and nobody has stopped it. **Four nights on a rota of four sentences have now been worked, and one of them had nobody on it, and both of those facts are in this paragraph and neither of them is on a wall.** That wall is a hardboard on two nails with nineteen lines on it, the nineteenth being the newest and a lift mechanic's and the only promise on it, and it is one hundred and forty-seven days old. The heating is on and dearer, the four names on that lease are dearer, and a fortnight a cook put in is still in, unpaid, and nothing in this case is going to settle it.

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.

*366. Thursday of week 132, at about ten on a service road, and this entry is a rota of four sentences, a stair door with a closer on it, nine words said at about half past ten, one woman who went and came back, and nobody's name on anything.*

*Conditions and docket.* **Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last at about six in the evening, and he was at a bench in Lattice Ward from about half past eight and was told none of this. Three nights worked before this one: Monday answered, Tuesday answered, Wednesday no call and one person in a room from about ten at night until about six in the morning, and nobody has found that out and nobody has asked. Nights not covered by the rota at all: Friday, Saturday, Sunday. Whether anybody could find out who was on on the Thursday morning: no, and about four telephoned a weighbridge office in the working Ashfields at about ten and it rang out in an empty building. One telephone call in at about eleven at night, four seconds long, about a stair door on the fourth floor of one of four buildings and a person on that floor with a bag in his hand. Numbers rung after the weighbridge office: about four, of which two belonged to a person who moved two years ago. One woman left a warm room in a coat and came back at about ten past twelve. Nine words at about half past ten, in about four seconds, by a first-year of nineteen, and no reply from anybody. One room off a service road from about half past six, about nine people. The rate held at nine and ten. The heating is on, dearer, four names on the lease dearer still, a fortnight a cook laid out that nobody has repaid. Room off a service road: four hundred and fifty-one days. Card in a rail in Lattice Ward: four hundred and fifty-five days, a third object in a third building, and the two have never shared a room. Hardboard, on two nails: twelve lines three hundred and seventy-one days, the thirteenth three hundred and twenty-two, the fourteenth two hundred and eighty-seven, the fifteenth two hundred and sixty-six, the sixteenth two hundred and forty-one, the seventeenth two hundred and twenty-three, the eighteenth one hundred and sixty-nine, the nineteenth one hundred and forty-seven. Wall unchanged: nineteen lines, thirteen hands, fourteen names, nothing added, nothing removed, nothing rubbed off. Line one is a cook's and has never moved. Line nineteen is a lift mechanic's, the newest, and the only promise on that board. Eight things in a ring binder, unopened, unread, shut. The woman's page is unread and is shut with them. The master's fifth line empty, its drawer unopened. The Exchange did not sit on this day. Its last sitting was a Wednesday the day before yesterday and its next one is a Wednesday twenty-seven days from today. The book stands at fifty-one lines, the tin at seventy-three, the count announced twenty-seven times, and the business nineteen years old. All four printed, none converted.**

*The nine words, as a record of four seconds.* **Said by: a first-year of nineteen, standing, in a warm room off a service road, at about half past ten on the Thursday night, in the middle of about nine minutes of people trying to say whose night it was. The words, in the order she said them: asking somebody is not the same as having them. Length: nine words. Time taken: about four seconds. What was said immediately before it: four people saying the name of a room and four people saying the name of a person, and none of them hostile and all of them telling the truth. Replies: none. Improved on: by nobody, that night or since, and about four people have given the nine words exactly as she said them and about four have never repeated them. Asked where it came from: by nobody. Thanked: by anybody, no. Owed: nothing. Whether she has been made the answer to anything else: no, and she is not going to be, and nobody in this case has said that to her. Owner: her, and the nine words, and the four rooms, and nobody.**

*The failure, as a record of a night with nobody on it.* **Night: the Thursday of week one hundred and thirty-two. Room the rota gave it to: a weighbridge office in the working Ashfields. Person who had said the sentence for that room: a man of about twenty-one who works on a counter in a shop in the working Ashfields, at about half past three on the Monday afternoon, in answer to a question. Whether he said no at any point: no, and he said on the Friday morning that he did not say no and wanted that written down. Whether anybody wrote it down: no, and he knows there is nothing to write it on. Whether he was told before the Friday: no, and nobody had his number, and nobody rang him, and he found out from a telephone call at about eleven on the Friday morning. Whether anybody blamed him: no, and nobody in this case has, and he is not a villain. Whether anybody in the four rooms was hostile to any other: no, and nothing that happened on that night was anybody's fault. Owner: the four sentences, and the four rooms, and nobody.**

*The door, as a record of a thing that was not locked.* **Reported: by a man of about forty-four at about eleven at night, in about four seconds, on a telephone. Building: one of four, and the number of buildings a man of fifty-one can get into that a person cannot get out of is about four hundred, and that figure is one of at least three that carry the same number and nobody has put them in an order. Floor: the fourth. Door: a stair door, shut, with a person on that floor who had a key and had not been in the building since about six. What the person on that floor had: a bag. What was wrong with the door: nothing was locked; the closer had caught on a pull-shut. Time away: about fifty minutes, in a coat, over another coat. What she said on coming back, in about nine seconds: that the door was not locked, that the person was going to be all right and was not going to be all right, and that both of those were true and that she was not going to say which she thought it was. Improved on: by nobody. Asked which she thought it was: by nobody, then or since. Thanked: by anybody, no. Owner: the four rooms, and the woman, and nobody.**

*What the day did not touch.* **A sentence was failed and nobody was blamed and nobody was thanked, and the finding was nine words from a person of nineteen and it is not on anything. Four people in three rooms were asked on the Friday who had offered and who had been asked and the answers did not agree about anybody. No list was made, no sheet was written, no minute was kept, and the three nights the rota does not cover are still not covered. A page of paper with about eleven lines on it was not copied again and about eight of the nine copies are not finished. A hardboard on two nails was not written on, a card in a rail did not move, and the master's fifth line is still blank. Nobody died. Nobody thanked anybody. Entry 366.**
