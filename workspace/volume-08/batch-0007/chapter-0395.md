# Chapter 395 — Four Feet Of Shopfront, And The Person Who Pulled The Switch

On the Monday morning of week one hundred and forty-three a woman of thirty-five said out loud in a room of four feet of shopfront in the Quiet Quarter that the room is not going to be joined to another room, and **it took about four seconds, no reason was given, a man of about forty-one who has been in that room for four years was in it and did not speak in it and has not spoken in it, and the room in the Lower Wards that said the same thing on a Monday ten days earlier was not told and does not know.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last of them at about six in the evening. He was at a bench in Lattice Ward all day and was thirty-one miles from that shopfront and knows nothing about it and was not told.

His word was at nine and ten. The room off a service road is five hundred and twenty-five days old and warm, and about four people came in on the Monday evening for the temperature, and nobody asked them anything.

---

The shopfront is four feet wide and there is a counter across about three-quarters of it, a kettle on the counter, one window about nine foot by about four, and a light on a pull switch by the door. There is a flat above it with two windows and one landing, and the switch by the shop door is on the same board as the light in that flat, and about four people in that building have keys and one of the four is a woman of thirty-five.

**There is no name over the door and nothing on it and no key on the door.**

**There were about four people in there on the Monday morning. One of them is a man of about forty-one who has been in that room for four years and who does not speak in it.**

---

She said it at about twenty past ten, standing behind the counter, with the kettle in her hand and the cold water still in it.

"**We are not going to be part of another room. That is all of it. I am not going to tell you why and I am not going to be asked why.**"

**Nobody asked her why. The man of about forty-one was looking at the window and did not turn round, and about four people in that shopfront have said since that he did not turn round and about four have said that he would not have turned round either way, and the two groups have not met and he is in neither of them.**

**Nobody thanked her. Nobody said it was the right thing to have said and nobody said it was the wrong thing, and she has not been asked since and has not offered, and she went back to her round and was in four other buildings before the end of that day.**

**She is not a villain and she is owed nothing. Eleven weeks ago she put her hand on a pull switch by that door at about seven in the morning and pushed it up again at about six in the evening, and the light in a flat above the shop went off on the same board, and a woman of about thirty-three with an ordinary job in the second of the four districts lost four hours of work she was not paid for and has never been repaid, and nobody has asked her what the work was. She was right about the boundary and she was wrong about the room, and both of those were said out loud on the day she did it and neither has been improved on since.**

A man of about fifty-five who repairs boilers said, in that shopfront at about half past ten, and nobody took it up.

"**I have been in this room for about nine years and I have never once said anything in it, and I have worked out this month that the reason I do not say anything in it is that it is the only room in this city where saying nothing has never once cost me anything, and I would like that written down before I say the next thing.**"

Nobody wrote it down. Nobody has asked him to write it down and nobody has said it was true, and he has not said anything else in that room since, and about four people in that building have said that a man who has worked out why he is quiet is not a man who has stopped being quiet, and about four have said that he is.

A woman of about twenty-six who works in a hospital laundry was in that shopfront at about eleven and said one thing, and it was not improved on.

"**Two rooms have now said no to the same thing and neither of them has been told about the other, and I have been in both, and I have decided that I am not going to be the one who tells them, because the first time I do it somebody will ask me why I have been in two rooms I did not tell them I was going into.**"

**Nobody told them. Nobody has asked her why, and she has been in both rooms and has told neither about the other, and about four people in this case have said that keeping two rooms apart is a kind of work and about four have said that it is a kind of keeping, and she is not interested in which.**

---

**Nobody in that building has connected the Monday in the Lower Wards to the Monday in the Quiet Quarter. Nobody has connected either of them to the sheet of about nine lines in a room in the second of the four districts, and about four hundred competent people in this city can each put the printed paragraph on the back of that sheet in front of anybody and not one of them has anywhere to put an objection.**

A man of about thirty-three, holding four buildings and about nine hundred doors for one hundred and fifty-eight days with no stamp and no reference and no date and nobody behind him, has not been in that shopfront in about four years and has not been told about it.

**The nine people copying the front of a page of paper with about eleven lines in it are thirteen weeks into it with about eight of the nine still unfinished, and the first disagreement, a figure in the fourth line, right in six copies and wrong in three, has still not been found, and nobody has looked for it in a fortnight.**

---

At half past six on the Monday there were about nine people in a warm room off a service road. A cook of twenty-seven stood at a sink and said nothing. A woman of about thirty sat in the chair nearest the door, and nobody asked her anything, nobody thanked her, and nobody apologised to her. She is there for the temperature, which costs more than it did, and that is correct.

A man of about fifty-one with a tool bag was in that warm room and said nothing, and has not been asked about anything for one hundred and thirty-one days, which is eighteen weeks and five days, and nobody has renewed the asking and nobody has stopped it.

A first-year of nineteen was at a counter in Lattice Ward at about three in the afternoon and said one small unasked thing in about four seconds, and nobody improved on it and nobody thanked her.

"**There are two rooms in two districts that have said no and about nine people in this city have heard one of them say it, and I have been in both, and I am not going to be the third thing that joins them up.**"

Nobody joined them up. Nobody has asked her to and nobody has suggested it, and about four people in that shop have said that she is being careful and about four have said that she is being careful in a way that has a cost, and she has not worked out which and has not said.

A hardboard on two nails carries nineteen lines, of which the newest is a lift mechanic's — eleven words, signed, about the middle — and it is the only promise on that board, and it is two hundred and twenty-one days old, and two rooms saying no has not made it more or less true.

The heating is on and costs more, and four names on a lease cost more than they did, and a fortnight a cook paid for is paid for by nobody and is not going to be resolved.

**A woman of thirty-five said four seconds of no in a room four feet wide and went back to her round, and a man of about forty-one who has been in that room for four years did not speak in it, and there are two rooms in two districts that have said no and not one of them has been told about the other and not one of the two has been written on anything.**

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.

*The second refusal, as a record of four seconds behind a counter.* **Said by: a woman of thirty-five, one of about four people in that building who has a key, standing behind a counter in four feet of shopfront in the Quiet Quarter, at about twenty past ten, to about four people, in about four seconds. What she said: that the room is not going to be part of another room; and that she is not going to say why; and that she is not going to be asked why. Whether a reason was given: no. Whether one was asked for: no, and she made the not being asked the second half of the sentence herself. Present: about four. A man of about forty-one who has been in that room for four years was present and did not speak and did not turn round from the window. Whether anybody answered: no. Whether she repeated it: no. Whether anybody said it was right: no. Whether anybody said it was wrong: no. Whether she was thanked: no. Where it now exists: in about four people, and on nothing. Whether the room in the Lower Wards knows: no, and nobody has told it, and no plan of record requires that it be told. Owner: her, and the four feet, and nobody.**

*The eleven hours, as a record of a thing a person did with one hand.* **What was done: a pull switch by a shop door in the Quiet Quarter, pushed down at about seven in the morning and pushed up at about six in the evening on a Monday eleven weeks ago, by one person, in about a second. What it cost to do it: nothing that was issued, and no card, no key, no reference, no number and nobody standing behind it. What it affected: the light in that shopfront and the light in a flat above it, which are on one board, and about four people in that building knew that before the Monday. What it cost: four unpaid hours to a woman of about thirty-three with an ordinary job in the second of the four districts, and a child of about six on the floor of that flat for about an hour and a half. What was repaid: nothing, and nobody has offered, and nobody has asked her what the work was, and she has not said. Whether the person who did it was right: right about the boundary, and wrong about the room, and both of those were said out loud on the day and neither has been improved on since. Whether she is owed something: no, and she is not a villain. Whether she has been thanked: no, and she has not been in that room in a fortnight. Owner: her, and the board, and the two people above, and nobody.**

*The two rooms, as a record of two refusals that have not touched.* **First: a converted shop unit off a service road in the Lower Wards, Monday of the previous week, about nine seconds, by word, no reason, about five of about nine present, and about four of that room not yet in it. Second: four feet of shopfront in the Quiet Quarter, this Monday, about four seconds, no reason, about four present, and one man present who did not speak in it. Days between them: ten. Districts between them: two. Whether the first room has been told about the second: no. Whether the second has been told about the first: no. Whether either has been told about the sheet of about nine lines in the second of the four districts: no, and one of the nine lines on that sheet is the first refusal in writing and was written by somebody who was in the first room. People who know about both: about nine, and about four of them have not, and the nine have not met. Whether the printed paragraph on the back of that page of paper bears on either: no, and one of its printed sentences says that an arrangement formed by the members of a room is not a bar to that room being merged with another one, and both of these are arrangements formed by the members of a room. Owner: two rooms, and nobody.**

*What the day did not touch.* **Four hours of unpaid work were not made good to anybody, and the person who turned the light off was not asked about it, not thanked and not apologised to, and the second of those is not a thing anybody in this case would think to do. Two rooms in two districts said no to the same thing on two Mondays and neither of them has been told about the other, and nobody proposed that they be. A hardboard on two nails carried the same nineteen lines and was not written on, the card in a rail did not move, a drawer stayed shut, and the folded page at the bottom of a ring binder is where it was on Monday. Nobody died. Nobody thanked anybody. Entry 398.**
