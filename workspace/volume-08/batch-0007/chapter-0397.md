# Chapter 397 — A Floor For Nine Minutes, And A Shift He Agreed To In An Earlier Term

On the Friday evening of week one hundred and forty-three, the worst afternoon of a week that has gone wrong since the water, about eleven people stood on one floor in Crown Terrace for about nine minutes at half past seven, and **they were not involved in anything and they were not saved from anything, and the room in a district that had nine doors off their hinges two days before is in a different building four miles away and is not joined to that floor in any room by anybody, and the man of twenty-two finished a shift he had agreed to in an earlier term, and nobody improved on that.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last of them about two in the afternoon, which is earlier than any other day this month. He was at a bench in Lattice Ward until about half past ten, then at a counter until about one, and then four miles away for about five hours on a Saturday's shift that he agreed to in an earlier term, and he finished it.

His word was at nine and ten. The room off a service road is five hundred and twenty-nine days old and warm, and about four people came in on the Friday evening for the temperature, and nobody asked them anything.

---

**There were nine folding chairs on that floor and a corridor with a fire door at each end of it and about nine doors along it that are not opened during the run. There was a post at the corridor end with nothing on it. There was a desk by the fire door with four drawers, and the second drawer on the left of it has a laminated sheet in it that has been in there for eleven weeks.**

**There were about eleven people on the floor, seven on the company's list and four on none. The woman of about thirty-six with a lanyard called the run and was on the floor.**

---

The run went at half past seven. Nine people, one floor, about nine minutes, called from the front.

**Nobody said how long it took. Nobody said anything for about nine seconds afterwards, the way nobody has said anything for about four years, and about nine people stood still and about four of them thought about a post that has been bare for eleven weeks and four did not.**

**Nobody in that corridor knew that nine rooms in four districts had gone dark on the Wednesday. Nobody in that corridor knew that nine doors had come off their hinges. Nobody there has been told and nobody asked, and about four people in that building have said since that a district is a district, and about four have said nothing, and the two have not met.**

**A woman of about thirty-three who does building maintenance was in that room and said one thing, and it was about the Saturday rather than the Friday, and nobody took it up.**

"**There is a man who comes to this floor for about nine minutes every fortnight and who spends the whole of the Saturday in a building four miles away doing something he agreed to in a term that has ended, and I have never once asked him what the Saturday is and I am not going to, and I would like it on the record that I have wanted to ask.**"

**Nobody asked him. Nobody in that corridor has asked him and nobody is going to, and she has not said his name in that room and has not said it since.**

A man of about thirty-nine who works in that building said, at the bottom of the ramp at about ten to nine, and nobody answered it.

"**I have worked out today that this is the tenth of these, and I have worked out that in four years nobody has ever said the number out loud in this corridor, and I have worked out that I am the one who has just said it, and I would like somebody to tell me whether that makes it a thing or makes it mine.**"

Nobody told him. About four people on that forecourt have said that a number said out loud is a fact and about four have said that a fact only becomes a thing when somebody acts on it, and he has not acted on it and has not said he is going to.

A woman of about forty-two who is on the company's list said, at the desk, and it was about the drawer.

"**There is a laminated sheet in a drawer in this building that has been there for eleven weeks and that nobody in this building has asked to see, and I have worked out that the reason is that the last person who asked to see it was told no by a person in a different building, and I would like somebody to confirm that that is why and I am not going to ask anybody.**"

**Nobody confirmed it. Nobody said it was the reason and nobody said it was not, and the drawer does not lock and the sheet is still in it and nobody has asked for it in eleven weeks and she has not asked and has not said she is going to.**

---

The warm room off the service road had about nine people in it at half past six and a cook of twenty-seven at a sink saying nothing, and the chair nearest the door had a woman of about thirty in it who was not asked anything by anybody, was not thanked, and was not apologised to, and the temperature is why she is there and the temperature costs more than it did, and both of those are correct.

A man of about fifty-one with a tool bag was in that warm room and said nothing, and has not been asked about anything for one hundred and thirty-five days, which is nineteen weeks and two days, and nobody has renewed the asking and nobody has stopped it.

**A man of about thirty-three, holding four buildings and about nine hundred doors for one hundred and sixty-two days, which is twenty-three weeks and a day, with no stamp and no reference and no date and nobody behind him, was in that warm room for about ten minutes on the Friday and said nothing at all, and nobody has asked him about the two days since the Wednesday, and he has not raised it.**

**A chair against a wall in a room four miles from that warm room has its back to everything and has had since the last time it was moved, and nobody moved it on the Friday and nobody looked at it twice.**

A hardboard on two nails, nineteen lines, and the newest of the nineteen a lift mechanic's — eleven words, signed, about the middle — and it is the only promise on that board, and it is two hundred and twenty-five days old, and a Friday in a fourth building has not made it more or less true.

The heating is on, and it costs more, and four names on a lease cost more than they did, and a fortnight a cook paid for is paid for by nobody and is not resolved.

**Nine people went over one floor for about nine minutes at half past seven on the Friday, and in a district four miles away there are still nine doors lying on a pavement with the hinges off them and nobody has put them back and nobody has been paid to, and a man finished a Saturday he agreed to in a term that has ended, and the city has not noticed one of those three things and has not been asked to.**

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.

*400. Friday of week 143, at about ten on a service road, and this entry is the tenth of ten runs, nine minutes, about eleven people, a post bare for eleven weeks, nine doors still on a pavement, one Saturday finished on an agreement from an earlier term, and nothing joined to anything.*

*Conditions and docket.* **Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last of them about two in the afternoon, earlier than on any other day this month. Rate: nine and ten, and the word came out earlier than usual. Heating: on, dearer, four names dearer still, a fortnight a cook laid out that nobody has repaid and that is not resolved.  That Friday: one building in Crown Terrace, second floor, one floor, about nine folding chairs, one corridor, about nine doors not opened during the run, one post at the corridor end that has carried nothing for eleven weeks, that is seventy-seven days, and one desk with four drawers, in the second drawer on the left of which one laminated sheet has been for eleven weeks. People on the floor: about eleven, seven on the company's list and four on none, and the woman of about thirty-six with a lanyard was on it and called the run. Runs in five months: ten, this the tenth, and the number has been said out loud in that corridor once, tonight, by one man of about thirty-nine, and nobody acted on it. Times anybody compared this run with any other: none. Times the run was timed: none. Room off that service road: five hundred and twenty-nine days. Card in a rail in Lattice Ward: five hundred and thirty-three days, a third object in a third building, and it did not move on the Friday or on any of the ten Fridays. The Exchange: not today, and no Friday is a sitting in nineteen years; the last one was the last Wednesday and the next one is the Wednesday of next week; book fifty-two lines, telephone seventy-three, count announced last Wednesday twenty-nine of which twenty-four, nineteen years. The ask: two hundred and nineteen days, that is thirty-one weeks and two days. Nobody died. Nobody thanked anybody. Entry 400.**

*The two floors, as a record of four miles and no connection.* **First: one floor on the second floor of a building in Crown Terrace, from about half past six until about nine, about eleven people, seven of them on a list held in an office four miles away and four on none, a run of about nine minutes at half past seven, and a number said out loud once in the corridor. Second: about nine rooms in four districts, two of which are rooms of flats and one of which is a workshop, in which nine people each decided in about four seconds on the Wednesday not to answer a knock and turned a light off. Between them: four miles and two days. People on the first floor who know about the second: none. People in the second who know about the first: none. People in this case who have put the two in the same room: none, and the reason is that a room will treat a question in it as an item. Whether the run was helped: no, and nothing that happened on the Wednesday reached that floor, and nobody in that building was saved by anything. Whether the run was harmed: no, and nothing on the Wednesday had anything to do with a floor in Crown Terrace. Whether anybody noticed either: nobody. Owner: the two, and nobody.**

*The post, as a record of the tenth Friday and an object still empty.* **Runs in five months: ten. Fridays that building has had: ten. Whether those two numbers are the same: yes, and the day map says so, and nobody in that building knows it. On the post: nothing, for eleven weeks, that is seventy-seven days. In the second drawer on the left of the desk by the fire door: one laminated sheet, about eleven lines, for the same eleven weeks, and the drawer does not lock. Times anybody has asked to see it: none. Times anybody has asked for it to go back on the post: none. Times a woman of about thirty-six with a lanyard has been on that floor in these ten Fridays: eight. Why she was not on two: no reason given and none asked for. Whether the number ten was said out loud in that corridor before tonight: no, and it was said once tonight by a man of about thirty-nine and nobody acted on it and nobody thanked him. Owner: the post, and the drawer, and the company, and nobody.**

*What the day did not touch.* **About nine doors in a district were not put back on their hinges and nobody was paid to put them back and nobody was hurt and nobody was named. A Saturday that had been agreed to in a term that has ended was finished and nothing was said about it by anybody including the man who finished it, and nobody has since asked him what the Saturday was. A run was not compared with any other run and a floor was not told about a district and a district was not told about a floor. A chair against a wall was not moved. A hardboard on two nails carried the same nineteen lines and was not written on, the card in a rail did not move, a drawer stayed shut, and the folded page at the bottom of a ring binder is where it was on Friday. Nobody died. Nobody thanked anybody. Entry 400.**
