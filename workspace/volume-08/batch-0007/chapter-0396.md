# Chapter 396 — Nine Knocks In Four Districts, And Nine Rooms That Did Not Answer

On the Wednesday afternoon of week one hundred and forty-three a run of doors came off their hinges in a district that has no merge power in it, and **about nine rooms in four districts, none of which has ever met the others, each refused a knock and went dark on purpose, with a switch, a kettle and a bell-push that does not ring by itself, and the run stopped without anybody knowing that it had stopped, and no name is attached to any part of it and the city has looked for a person and has not found one, for the eighth volume running.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last of them at about six in the evening. He was at a bench in a building four miles from all of it from about half past eight until about five, and then on a maintenance rota in the same building until about six, and he is in none of the nine rooms and was in none of them on any of the four days this took.

His word was at nine and ten. The room off a service road is five hundred and twenty-seven days old and warm, and about four people came in on the Wednesday evening for the temperature, and nobody asked them anything.

---

A cascade is a trade word. It means a run of the same fault in one street in one afternoon, and the fault in this case was a hinge, and a hinge is the part of a door that is designed to give, and about four people in that district have said that a door on a good hinge is supposed to come off it, and about four have said that a run of them is not supposed to.

**About nine doors came off their hinges between about half past two and about four o'clock. They did not come off in order. They came off one, then another two streets away, then three more, and about four people in that district have counted them and have got four and five in two different orders.**

**Nobody knocked on any of them before they came off. Nobody has said who knocked or why, and about four people in that district have said that somebody knocked at about nine doors that afternoon, at about half past two, at about the same time as the first of them came off, and about four have said that nobody knocked at all.**

**Nobody was hurt. Nobody was in a doorway at the time and about four people in that district have said that whoever picked those doors up picked them up gently, and about four have said they picked them up at all.**

---

The nine rooms are not the four rooms. Nobody has ever connected them and nobody in this case has ever been asked to connect them, and about nine rooms in four districts, in a bus depot, a canteen, a yard office, a back room over a shop, four buildings of flats, a chapel hall that is not open, a workshop and one room above a line, each had somebody standing in it at about half past two on the Wednesday with a door that somebody was going to knock on.

**Nobody arranged those nine knocks. Nobody in this case knows who arranged them and nobody has said a name, and a knock is not a form and nobody has printed one.**

**About nine people each decided, separately, in about four seconds, not to answer.**

A woman of about twenty-nine who answers a telephone four hours a day in a building in the second of the four districts was in one of those rooms and said, and it took her about nine seconds:

"**I have decided that whoever is at that door is going to knock and I am not going to answer it, and I have decided that when the knocking stops I am going to turn the light off, and I have not told anybody either of those two things and I am not going to.**"

**Nobody asked her about either. Nobody improved on it and nobody thanked her, and about four people in that room have said that a person who decides two things in four seconds has usually had them in her head for a week, and about four have said nothing, and she is in neither.**

A man of about fifty-four who works nights at a depot in that district was in another of the nine rooms and was wrong about both halves of it, and he is right to refuse, and he has not been told that he is right.

"**I have worked out that a dark room is a room that has not answered, and I have worked out that the nine knocks are a count, and I have worked out that about four people in this city can now write down nine rooms that did not answer, and I am going to be in that, and I would like it on the record that I would rather be on a list than be at a door.**"

**He was wrong. A dark room is not a room that has not answered; the door still opens, the door has never been locked, and about four people knocked at his door that afternoon after the light was off and he let one of them in and did not let the other three, and the other three knocked again at about a quarter past three and are still knocking somewhere.**

Nobody corrected him. Nobody has said the word wrong to him and nobody has said the word right, and he has not asked, and about four people in that depot have said that a man who is on a list has lost a night of sleep that he would have lost anyway, and about four have said that being on a list in this city is a thing that happens to people and not a thing anybody does to them.

A woman of about twenty-seven who bakes in a bakery in that district was in a third of the nine rooms, and she is the second of the two who were wrong, and she has worked out what she did and has said so out loud and nobody has improved on it.

"**I went dark at about half past two and I answered a knock at about ten past three because it was raining on the bread, and I have worked out since that answering that one is how four more got in, and I am not going to say that going dark was the mistake, because going dark was not the mistake, and I would like somebody to write that down and nobody is going to.**"

Nobody wrote it down. Nobody said that going dark was the right thing and nobody said it was the wrong thing, and about four people in that room have said that she is the only one of the nine who has been honest about it, and about four have said that being honest about it is a thing a person can only afford who was not the one who stopped anything, and neither of those is true and about four people in that room know it.

**Nobody in any of the nine rooms knows it worked. That is the finding of the Wednesday and it is not in any of the nine rooms, and the man on the maintenance rota four miles away was not told anything about any of it until about seven in the evening, and the last thing he did that Wednesday was nothing.**

---

Half past six, a warm room off a service road, about nine people in it, a cook of twenty-seven at a sink with nothing said, and a woman of about thirty in the chair nearest the door that nobody in that room spoke to, thanked, or said sorry to. She comes for the temperature, the temperature costs more than it did, and both of those are correct.

A man of about fifty-one with a tool bag was in that warm room and said nothing, and has not been asked about anything for one hundred and thirty-three days, which is nineteen weeks, and nobody has renewed the asking and nobody has stopped it.

A woman of about twenty-four who works as a resonance nurse at a mobile clinic was not in that room and was not in any of the nine, and nobody in this case has asked her about the Wednesday and nobody is going to.

On two nails in a room off a service road hangs a hardboard with nineteen lines on it. The newest of the nineteen is a lift mechanic's, eleven words, signed, about the middle. It is the only promise on that board and it is two hundred and twenty-three days old, and about nine rooms going dark in four districts has not made it more or less true.

The heating is on and costs more. Four names on a lease cost more than they did. A fortnight a cook paid for is paid for by nobody and is not resolved and is not going to be resolved. **Eleven hours of dark in a Quiet Quarter building eleven weeks ago is not any part of what happened this afternoon and has not been joined to it, and a Friday afternoon in Crown Terrace is a third thing, and the three have not been put together by anybody.**

**About nine rooms in four districts each refused a knock and went dark on purpose with a switch and a kettle and a bell-push that does not ring, and two of the nine were wrong about what dark does, and one of those two is a man who works nights at a depot and is owed nothing and is not thanked, and no name is attached to any of it and the city has not found a person, for the eighth volume running.**

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.
