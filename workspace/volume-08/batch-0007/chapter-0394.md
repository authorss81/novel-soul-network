# Chapter 394 — The Ninth Of Ten, And A Post With Nothing On It

A floor on the second floor of a building in Crown Terrace was run for the ninth time in five months on the Friday evening, and **the post by the corridor end has been bare for ten weeks, which is seventy days, and the laminated sheet has been in an unlocked drawer for the same ten weeks, and about eleven people were on the floor, seven of them on the company's list and four on none, and the run went and nobody said how long it took and nobody asked.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last at about six in the evening. He was at a repair shop in Lattice Ward until about half past four and was four miles away from all of it and knows nothing about a post and was not told.

His word was at nine and ten. The room off a service road is five hundred and twenty-two days old and warm, and about four people came in on the Friday for the temperature, and nobody asked them anything.

---

**There were nine folding chairs on that floor and a corridor with a fire door at each end of it and about nine doors along it that are not opened during the run. There was a desk by the fire door with four drawers, and the second drawer on the left of it has a laminated sheet in it that has been in there for ten weeks. There was a post at the corridor end with nothing on it.**

There were about eleven people on the floor. Seven of them are on the company's list. Four of them are on no list at all, and about four of the seven on the list have been on it since the first of the ten Fridays and about three came on later.

**The man of about thirty-six with the lanyard who calls the run was on the floor and has not been on it for two Fridays and has not sent anybody in her place, and nobody in that building has asked her why.**

---

The run went at half past seven. Nine people, one floor, about nine minutes, called from the front.

**Nobody counted it. Nobody has ever counted one of these ten runs, and the building has no way of knowing which of the eleven was on which Friday, and about four people in that corridor could tell you it is the ninth and about four could not, and nobody has asked either group.**

A man of about thirty-one who stacks pallets at a yard said, at the top of the stairs at about ten to eight, to nobody in particular, and nobody took it up.

"**I have been on this floor nine times and I have never once worked out whether I am learning anything or whether I am standing in a corridor being counted by somebody I cannot see, and I have decided to go on coming and to go on not working it out, and I have wanted to say that out loud to about nine people before.**"

Nobody told him which of the two it is. Nobody said the counting is a fiction and nobody said it is not a fiction, and he has not been back to the subject, and about four people on that stairwell heard him and about four did not.

A woman of about twenty-two who is on the company's list and who is in that room because a man of about twenty-seven asked her once said, at the desk, and it was not about the run.

"**The man who took a shift two Fridays ago is not on this list any more. I have seen the list. I am not saying he was removed from it by anybody, because I do not know that, and I would like it noted that I have looked and that I am not saying a thing I cannot see.**"

**Nobody said whether he was on the list. The woman of about thirty-six with the lanyard who calls the run was not in that building on the Friday and has not been asked, and the list is a document in an office four miles away and nobody in Crown Terrace has a copy of it.**

**Nobody said anything about the drawer. Nobody has asked for the laminated sheet to go back on the post since the night it came off, and it has been in an unlocked drawer for seventy days, and about four people in that corridor have walked past the post on each of the ten Fridays and about four have not looked at it, and the two have never spoken about it.**

A woman of about thirty-seven who has been in that room about twice a month for four years, in the lobby at about a quarter to nine, and it was the fifth time she has said something in that room and nobody in it knows how many times.

"**I have worked out that the post being empty is the only thing in this building that has stayed the same for ten weeks, and I would like somebody to tell me what I am supposed to do with a thing that does that.**"

Nobody told her. Nobody said the post is on a rota and nobody said it is not on a rota, and she has not said it again in that room, and about four people in that lobby have said that a thing that does not change is a thing you can rely on and about four have said it is a thing you can be frightened of, and she is in neither of those.

**A man of about fifty-eight who has been in that room for four years was not on the floor on this Friday and has not been since the one before it, and nobody has asked him why and nobody has said that it would be a shame if he stopped.**

---

**They went out through a lobby, up a ramp, and onto a forecourt with about nine cars on it. The man in the booth has never once been inside that building and does not know any of the eleven.**

A woman of about thirty-eight who works in a pharmacy in Saltmarket was in that room and said one thing, and it was not about the post and not about the run.

"**There are four rooms in this city that have each said no to something in the last fortnight, and I have been in two of them, and I have not been in the room where it was said and I am not going to be, and I have decided that not going is a thing I am allowed to decide and I would like somebody to tell me if I am not.**"

**Nobody told her. Nobody has said she is wrong and nobody has said she is right, and she has not mentioned the four rooms again, and about four people in that forecourt have said that staying out of a thing is the same as being in it and about four have said nothing, and she heard both and said nothing to either.**

The same warm room off the same service road had about nine people in it at half past six, a cook of twenty-seven at the sink saying nothing, and a woman of about thirty in the chair nearest the door, unasked, unthanked, unapologised to, and the reason she was there was the temperature and the temperature costs more than it did, and both of those are correct.

A man of about fifty-one with a tool bag was in that warm room and said nothing, and has not been asked about anything for one hundred and twenty-eight days, which is eighteen weeks and two days, and nobody has renewed the asking and nobody has stopped it.

Two nails and a hardboard and nineteen lines, and the newest of those lines is a lift mechanic's — eleven words, signed, about the middle — and it is the only promise on that board, and it is two hundred and eighteen days old. The card in a rail in Lattice Ward is five hundred and twenty-six days old and is a third object in a third building, and the fourteen lines on that hardboard are three hundred and fifty-eight days old, and neither of those two figures is a date and neither of them is anything but an age.

Heating: on, dearer, and dearer again for four names on a lease, and the fortnight a cook paid for is paid for by nobody and is not going to be.

**Nine people went over one floor for about nine minutes at half past seven, four miles from a service road where about nine people are sitting in a warm room, and neither of those two facts has ever been in the same room and nobody has joined them and the man on the bench in Lattice Ward was four miles away from both.**

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.

*The post, as a record of an object that has been empty for ten weeks.* **Object: one post at the corridor end of a company training room in Crown Terrace. On it for about four years: one laminated sheet, written on never, read from twice, taken off at about ten to nine on a Friday in an earlier week by the woman who calls the run. On it now: nothing, for ten weeks, that is seventy days. Put back by anybody: no. Asked for by anybody: no. People in that corridor who have looked at it on this Friday: about four, and about four have not. Whether the two groups have ever spoken about it: no. What is in the second drawer on the left of the desk by the fire door: one laminated sheet, about eleven lines, put there on the same night, still there, not locked. How many of the eleven know both of those facts: about four, and the four are not the same four who look at the post, and nobody has put the two fours together. Whether the sheet is a form or a notice or a record: it is a laminated sheet with about eleven lines on it and it is in a drawer. Owner: the post, and the drawer, and the company, and the about eleven.**

*The seven and the four, as a record of a list nobody in that building administers.* **On the company's list: seven, and the list itself is in an office four miles away and no copy of it is in Crown Terrace. On no list: four, none of whom has ever been asked why they come. Ask times, the four on no list: none, in ten Fridays. Ask times, the man who works in that building: one, in about four years, answered in about four seconds, in the negative. Departures from the list in these ten Fridays: one man of about twenty-five who took a shift, and one woman of about thirty-six with a lanyard who has not been on the floor for two of the ten and who gave no reason. Whether either departure was put to anybody as a question: no, and the first was announced by a woman of about thirty-six who said in about four seconds that she was not going to try to get him back in the spring, and nobody improved on that and nobody thanked her. Which of the eleven could tell you this is the ninth: about four, and about four could not. Owner: the eleven, and the list, and nobody.**

*What the day did not touch.* **One floor was run for the ninth time and nothing about it was measured, named, compared or put on a surface, and the ten Fridays that room has had have never been compared with each other by anybody standing on that floor. A post was not filled and a drawer was not opened and a list was not produced. Four rooms in four districts said no to something in the fortnight around this Friday and none of the eleven in Crown Terrace was in any of them, and no person from that floor is in any of them and none of them is on any of them. A hardboard on two nails carried the same nineteen lines and was not written on, the card in a rail did not move, a drawer stayed shut, and the folded page at the bottom of a ring binder is where it was on Friday. Nobody died. Nobody thanked anybody. Entry 397.**
