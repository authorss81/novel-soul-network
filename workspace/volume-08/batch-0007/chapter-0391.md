# Chapter 391 — Nine Seconds, By Word, And No Reason At All

On the Monday morning of week one hundred and forty-two a room of about nine people in a converted shop unit off a service road in the Lower Wards said out loud that it is not going to be joined to another room. **It took about nine seconds. It was by word. No reason was given and none was asked for, nobody answered it, nobody thanked the person who said it, and about four of the nine were not in the room when it was said.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last of them at about six in the evening. He was on a bench in Lattice Ward from about half past eight and stood in that room for about nine minutes at about ten and was asked for nothing and offered nothing and heard the whole of it.

His word was at nine and ten. The room off a service road is five hundred and eighteen days old and warm, and about four people came in on the Monday evening for the temperature, and nobody asked them anything.

---

**The room has nine chairs, a table, a counter with nothing on it, a kettle, and a bell-push by the door that does not ring by itself. A hardboard hangs on two nails. A board over the door carries four words in chalk in the hand of a woman of about sixty-eight who is not in this case and is not named.**

About nine people were in it at about ten on the Monday. Four had come from a building in the second of the four districts and five had walked. About four of the nine had been in a room like this one before and about four had not.

**Nobody had a list. Nobody had a minute. There was nothing with a name on it on any wall in that room, and the page of paper with about eleven lines in it was not in there and was not discussed in there, and the nine people copying the front of that page are twelve weeks into it with about eight of the nine still unfinished.**

---

**The man of about forty-nine who works on a dairy round came in at about ten, earlier than he comes, and stood at the counter with his cap in his hand.**

He said it at about ten past ten. Nine seconds, and then he stopped, and the stopping was part of it.

"**This room is not going to be joined to another room. I am not going to tell you why and you are not to ask me, and if you want to be somewhere else that is a thing you can do on your own and I would not stop you doing it.**"

**Nobody answered him. About four people in that room looked at him and about four were doing something else, and nobody asked him why he was early and nobody asked him where else a person could go.**

**Nobody said it was the right thing to have said. Nobody said it was the wrong thing. He put his cap back on and went out to his round at about a quarter past ten and has not been asked since and has not offered.**

---

About a week before this Monday somebody in that room turned a sheet of paper over and read out about fifty words of print at the foot of the back of it, and nobody has said a word about those fifty words in that room since, and this morning was the first thing anybody has said in that room since the reading.

**One of the things the fifty words say is that an arrangement formed by the members of a room is not a bar to that room being merged with another one. What he said at ten past ten is an arrangement formed by the members of a room. So it has no standing in those fifty words at all, and nobody in that room has the sheet in front of them, and nobody in that room worked that out, and nobody has said so since.**

A woman of about twenty-six who works in a hospital laundry was in that room and said, about the fifty words, and nobody took it up.

"**I have worked out since last week that the thing on the back of that page says we cannot stop it by agreeing not to, and I have not worked out what we can do instead, and I would rather one other person in this city knew that than nobody.**"

**Nobody said what they could do instead. She has not said it again and nobody has asked, and about four people in that room have said since that she is right about the first half of it and about four have said nothing at all.**

A man of about fifty-two who drives for a firm was in that room at about eleven and said, to the counter, and it was not improved on.

"**There are four people in this city who can put those fifty words in front of a judge and not one of them has a desk to be angry at, and I have been trying since last week to work out who those four are, and I have not, and I have stopped trying, and that is a thing I did and I am saying it out loud.**"

Nobody found the four. Nobody said whether the four is a number of people or a number of offices and he has not said which he thinks it is, and about four people in that room have said that stopping is not the same as winning and about four have said nothing.

---

**About four of the nine people in that room had not come in when he said it. They came in between about half past ten and about eleven, and nobody told them, and none of them has asked since, and about four of them have told other people that they were in that room on the Monday morning.**

Nobody has put what he said on anything. It is not on the counter and it is not on the hardboard and it is not in the ring binder, and the hardboard carries nineteen lines in thirteen hands and has carried them since before any of this.

A first-year of nineteen was at a counter in Lattice Ward at about two in the afternoon and said one thing, and nobody at that counter improved on it and nobody thanked her.

"**Four people have now said no to a merge in four rooms in four districts and I have been in all four of those rooms since the summer and I have not been asked about any of it, and I have decided that not being asked is the correct arrangement and I am not going to do anything about it.**"

**Nobody asked her about it. Nobody has said since that she should have been asked, and about four people in that shop have said that a first-year of nineteen keeping her mouth shut about four rooms is a decision, and about four have said that it is not a decision, and she is interested in neither.**

---

About nine people came in at half past six into a warm room off a service road. A cook of twenty-seven was at a sink and said nothing at all. In the chair nearest the door was a woman of about thirty whom nobody asked anything, nobody thanked and nobody apologised to. She is in that room for the temperature and the temperature costs more than it did, and both of those are correct.

A woman of about thirty-four who checks about nine hundred doors in four buildings was in that warm room for about twenty minutes and said nothing at all, and nobody has asked her about anything since a Wednesday in another week and nobody is going to.

A man of about fifty-one with a tool bag was in it and said nothing, and has not been asked about anything for one hundred and twenty-four days, which is seventeen weeks and five days, and nobody has renewed the asking and nobody has stopped it.

Nineteen lines on a hardboard on two nails, and the newest of them is eleven words a man of twenty-nine wrote about the middle, and it is the only promise on that board, and it is two hundred and fourteen days old, and a room saying no in about nine seconds has not made it more or less true.

The heating is on and costs more. Four names on a lease cost more than they did. A fortnight a cook paid for is paid for by nobody and is not going to be and is not going to be resolved.

**One room said no by word, in about nine seconds, without a reason, and about four of its own nine were not in the room when it was said, and nobody answered it and nobody thanked the man, and the fifty words on the back of a page of paper are not a bar to it and nobody in that room has worked that out.**

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.

*The refusal, as a record of nine seconds said by word.* **Said by: a man of about forty-nine who works on a dairy round, standing at a counter in a converted shop unit off a service road in the Lower Wards, at about ten past ten on the Monday, to about five people, in about nine seconds. What he said: that this room is not going to be joined to another room; that he is not going to say why; that they are not to ask him why; and that anybody who wants to be somewhere else can do that on their own and he would not stop them. Whether a reason was asked for: no, and none was given. Whether the word merge was used: no, and about four people in that room have noticed that the sentence he used did not contain it. Whether anybody answered: no, and about four were looking at him and about four were doing something else. Whether he repeated it: no, and he has not been asked to and has not offered. Whether anybody said it was right: no. Whether anybody said it was wrong: no. Whether anybody thanked him: no. Where it now exists: in about five people's heads, and on nothing. Times it has been said again in that room: none. Owner: him, and the five, and nobody.**

*Fifty words, as a record of a printed thing nobody has argued with.* **Where they are: at the foot of the back of a sheet of paper with about eleven lines in it, in a woman's hand with four other hands in it. How many: about fifty. How they came to be there: not by the office that retains emergency authority, which did not write them, did not ask for them and cannot say who printed them, and that sentence is still the only one anybody in this case can say about it. Whether withdrawn: no, and a printed paragraph cannot be unprinted by a room that agreed to the page. Read out loud in that room: once, about a week before this Monday, by one person. Times anybody has said anything about them in that room since: once, and it was this morning, and it was about the room and not about them. What one of them says: that an arrangement formed by the members of a room is not a bar to that room being merged with another one. What that does to a refusal made by word in a room: nothing, and nothing at all, and the refusal this morning was such an arrangement. Whether anybody in that room has worked that out: no. People in this city competent to put those fifty words in front of anybody: about four hundred, and not one of them has anywhere to put an objection. Owner: the sheet, and whoever printed it, and nobody.**

*The four who were not in the room, as a record of an absence.* **In that room at about ten past ten: about five. In that room by about eleven: about nine. Told afterwards: none of them was told. Asked since: none of them has asked, and about four of them have said to other people that they were in that room on the Monday morning, and about four have not said anything. Times anybody has asked whether the whole room heard the nine seconds: none, and nobody has asked, and the question has not occurred to anybody in this case. Whether the refusal stands in the absence of those four: it is not written down, so there is nothing for them to have missed and nothing for them to agree to. Owner: the four, and the room, and nobody.**

*What the day did not touch.* **Nothing was merged and nothing was refused by anybody who had been asked to refuse something, and a page of paper with about eleven lines in it was not in that room and the nine people copying its front are twelve weeks into it with about eight of the nine unfinished and the first disagreement, a figure in its fourth line, right in six copies and wrong in three, has still not been found. The printed paragraph was not withdrawn and was not read out again. A hardboard on two nails carried the same nineteen lines and was not written on, the card in a rail did not move, a drawer stayed shut, and the folded page at the bottom of a ring binder is where it was on Monday. Nobody died. Nobody thanked anybody. Entry 394.**
