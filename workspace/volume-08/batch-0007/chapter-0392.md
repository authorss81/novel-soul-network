# Chapter 392 — Nine Lines On A Sheet, And The One Person Who Would Not Take One

On the Wednesday morning of week one hundred and forty-two about nine people in a room in a building in the second of the four districts wrote down about nine lines on a sheet of paper, and **one of the nine is a refusal, and the person who would have held it said no out loud in a room, and nobody improved on it and nobody thanked him, and the refusal is on the table with the other eight and is one of the nine, and nothing was adopted and nothing was signed and there is no document.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last of them at about six in the evening. He was at a bench in Lattice Ward from about half past eight until about four and then at a counter until about six, and neither building is in the second of the four districts, and he was not asked to come to that room and was not told that it was happening.

His word was at nine and ten. The room off a service road is five hundred and twenty days old and warm, and about four people came in on the Wednesday evening for the temperature, and nobody asked them anything.

---

**There were nine chairs in that room and eight of them were used and there was a table with nothing on it and a window that faces a wall about nine feet away. There were about nine people. Four of them had walked and about four had come on a bus and about four of the nine had been in a room like this one before.**

**Nobody had a minute. Nobody had anything printed, and nothing on that table had a heading on it, and the sheet the nine lines went on was a sheet of paper and not a form and not a return and not a notice.**

---

The nine lines took about four minutes and they were written by one person, at the request of four others, and the order they were written in was the order they were said in and nobody has said since that the order means anything.

**The first six are ordinary and none of them is a promise about anybody outside the room. The seventh is that the room will not be merged with another room, and that is the line a man of about forty-nine wrote down in about four seconds on Monday in a different district, and nobody in that room on the Wednesday knew he had said it, and nobody has told them, and one of the nine lines on that sheet is the first one anybody anywhere has written down.**

The eighth is that any room may refuse by word without giving a reason. The ninth is a thing about a light.

**Nobody adopted it. Nobody voted on it. Nobody put a name to a line.**

---

**The ninth line was the one they could not fill, and it took about nine minutes to work out that it could not be filled, and the finding was a person and not a sentence.**

**The ninth line was to be held by whoever let a thing be left in that room, and the person they had in mind for it was a man of about forty-two who fits kitchens and who has a unit in a yard in the second of the four districts and who had come in at about ten because somebody he works beside had told him there was a meeting.**

A woman of about thirty-nine who works at a counter in that building asked him at about ten past ten whether he would take the ninth line, and it took her about nine seconds and him about four.

"**No. I have had things left with me before and I am not doing it again, and I am not going to tell you what I am worried about because I have worked out that if I do you will all decide I am right and start again.**"

**Nobody improved on it. Nobody asked him what he was worried about, and nobody said he should take it anyway, and nobody said he had done the right thing and nobody said he had done the wrong thing.**

**Nobody thanked him. He stayed in the room for about half an hour after he said it and left at about half past ten, and the ninth line has nobody's name against it, and the eight other lines have nobody's name against them either, and about four people in that room have said since that this is the correct arrangement and about four have not said anything.**

A woman of about twenty-six who works in a hospital laundry said, at the table, about the eight, and nobody took it up.

"**There are eight lines with nobody's name on them and one line with nobody's name on it because of a man in a yard, and I have been trying since about half past ten to work out which of those two is the document, and I have worked out that they are the same document, and I would like somebody to be angry about that and nobody is going to be.**"

Nobody was angry. Nobody agreed with her, and she has not said it again, and about four people in that room have said that the refusal being counted is the only thing on the sheet that anybody can check, and about four have said nothing.

---

**The woman whose Wednesday it is was four miles away in a workroom on a shift and was not in that room and was not told that it was happening and has not said anything about it, and nobody in this case has asked her.**

A man of about thirty-three, holding four buildings and about nine hundred doors for one hundred and fifty-three days with no stamp and no reference and no date and nobody behind him, was not in that room and has not been in it. He has not been asked for anything and nothing has been given to him in that time.

A man of about fifty-one with a tool bag was not in that room. He has not been asked about anything for one hundred and twenty-six days, which is eighteen weeks, and nobody has renewed the asking and nobody has stopped it.

**The page of paper with about eleven lines in it was not in that room and was not discussed in it, and the nine people copying its front are twelve weeks and two days into it with about eight of the nine still unfinished, and the first disagreement in its fourth line has still not been found and nobody in that room looked.**

---

By half past six there were about nine people in that room and a cook of twenty-seven at a sink, saying nothing, and in the chair by the door a woman of about thirty who was asked nothing by anybody in the room, thanked by nobody and apologised to by nobody. She is there because of the heat. The heat costs more. Both of those are correct.

A woman of about thirty-eight who walks about nine miles to a building in a third of the four districts was in that warm room for about half an hour and said nothing at all, and she comes for the temperature, and the temperature costs more, and both of those are correct.

That hardboard on two nails still carries nineteen lines, and the last of them is a lift mechanic's — eleven words, signed, about the middle — and it is the only promise on it, and it is two hundred and sixteen days old, and eight lines and a ninth nobody took is not on it and is not going to be.

On, and dearer than it was, and dearer again for four names on a lease, and a fortnight that a cook laid out is owed to her by nobody and is not going to be repaid.

**About nine lines went onto a sheet on the Wednesday, one of them is a man's refusal and it is one of the nine, one of the nine is a room that will not be joined to another room and nobody in the room where the sheet was written knows that, and nothing was adopted, nothing was signed, and the woman whose Wednesday it is was four miles away on a shift and was not told.**

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.

*The ninth line, as a record of a thing held by nobody.* **What the ninth line was to be: that the person who lets a thing be left in that room will be the one who takes it on. Who it was to be: a man of about forty-two who fits kitchens, with a unit in a yard in the second of the four districts, present that morning because somebody he works beside told him there was a meeting. Asked by: a woman of about thirty-nine who works at a counter in that building, at about ten past ten, in about nine seconds. Answer: no, in about four seconds, and the answer was that he had had things left with him before, is not doing it again, and will not say what he was worried about because if he did they would decide he was right and start again. Improved on: no, by anybody. Asked again: no. Reason extracted: no, and he made the not-saying the condition of the no. Times anybody said he was right: none. Times anybody said he was wrong: none. Times he was thanked: none. How long he stayed: about half an hour, and he left at about half past ten. Name against the line afterwards: none, and there is none against the other eight either. Whether the sheet is a failure on account of this: no, and the refusal is one of the nine and is counted, and that is the whole of what it is. Owner: him, and the ninth line, and nobody.**

*The line about a room, as a record of one sentence written down four hundred yards from the room it happened in.* **The sentence: that this room is not going to be joined to another room, said in about nine seconds on Monday morning in a converted shop unit off a service road in the Lower Wards, by word, with no reason, to about five people. Who wrote it down: one person, on the Wednesday, in about four seconds, at the request of four others, in a room in a building in the second of the four districts. Where the person who wrote it got it: from a woman of about twenty-six who works in a hospital laundry, who had heard it from a man of about thirty-eight who drives for a firm, who was in that room on the Monday. Whether the man of about forty-nine knows it has been written down: nobody has told him and nobody is going to. Whether the room in the Lower Wards knows it has been written down: no. Whether the paragraph on the back of the page makes either room say anything: no, and one of the printed sentences says that an arrangement formed by the members of a room is not a bar to that room being merged, and a sheet of paper in a third building is not a room and is not an arrangement. Times this refusal has been said in a room to anybody who had not heard it: twice, in two buildings, on one afternoon. Owner: the two rooms, and the sheet, and nobody.**

*What the day did not touch.* **A refusal to hold one of nine lines was not turned into a failure and was not improved on and was not repaid, and the eight lines that were written carry no names and were not adopted by anybody. A printed paragraph on the back of a page of paper was not read out in that room and not discussed in it, and nine people in four districts are still copying the front of that page with about eight of the nine unfinished and the first disagreement in its fourth line still not found. A woman of twenty-four in a workroom four miles away was not asked a question and was not told anything and was not thanked. A hardboard on two nails carried nineteen lines and was not written on, the card in a rail did not move, the drawer stayed shut, and the folded page at the bottom of a ring binder is where it was on Wednesday. Nobody died. Nobody thanked anybody. Entry 395.**
