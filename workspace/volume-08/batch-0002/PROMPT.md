## 3. THE HOUSE RULES YOUR PLAN MUST ENCODE, NOT MERELY RESPECT

**These are the rules a Volume 08 writer will break without noticing, and a plan of record is where each of them is either given an exception in terms or confirmed as absolute. The previous phase listed them and this phase has to act on every one.**

1. **A name in a narrator's sentence and a name on a surface are two different objects.** A volume about a city that cannot answer is not a volume in which the person who answers first is a number. `Asha Reed` was not named once in Volume 07's body prose.
2. **A rule in this manuscript is a line somebody wrote by hand unless it is printed by somebody who is not in this case.** A hardboard on two nails is not a form, a minute, a notice, a report, a committee paper, a leaflet, a registration or a constitution.
3. **No name is attached to anything that happens, and no cause is named for any part of it.** The storm is a storm, the sequences are activated, and the city looks for a person and does not find one. A night with the lights off is not a broadcast's doing, and a chain of nine people in the dark is nine people in the dark and not an act of anybody.
4. **A message is not a broadcast**, and no character in this case received one privately and no character repeats what she said.
5. **No new final enemy, no new faction, no new office and no new post.** A person whose job it is to look at a stranger's wrist still does not exist.
6. **A document in this volume costs somebody something, is refused by somebody, or is carried by somebody who walks.** A correct action that changes nothing is on the page at least four times per volume, performed by a person who is not a villain and is owed nothing.
7. **No month-name, no month-date, no day-date, no year and no day number in the prose.** The only calendar nouns are the season words, the week and the day inside the week. **All interval figures are spelled out in words in the prose.**
8. **The second prohibited word is not used anywhere**, in narration, in a load book, or in anybody's mouth. It is a closed decision across two volumes and it is not to be reopened because a writer wants the phrase. **It is named at section 2.6 item 8 of `workspace/volume-08/batch-0001/PROMPT.md` and is not named here, and the not naming is the rule and not an evasion of it: a document that reports the absence of a prohibited word has used it, and the reporting is the use.**
9. **The third prohibited word is the one that belongs to a bag and to a person carrying a torch, and it does not occur in a Volume 07 file, in a load book, in a document or in a mouth.** **It is named at section 2.6 item 9 of `workspace/volume-08/batch-0001/PROMPT.md` and is not named here, for the reason in item 8.** A document that sorts by it has used it, and the sorting is the use. The matcher has to be the word and not the shape of the sentence around it.
10. **No load book in this manuscript reports the absence of any prohibited thing.** The recording of a guardrail's holding belongs in a summary and in a state file and not in a chapter. **This was broken six times in this repository and the last break was in Movement IV of Volume 07.** Note the open question at the Volume 07 close's review section item 2: whether the standing closing paragraph is itself a compliance report. **Its precondition is now walked to the file and the walk is three files and not two, so read it before you answer it: the standard heading “What the day did not touch” is in forty-eight of Volume 07's fifty files; Chapters 339 and 349 are the only two that lack it; the alternate heading “What was not in that room and was not raised” is in Chapters 326, 339 and 349; Chapter 326 carries BOTH headings; and all three of those sittings are Exchange sittings, the Wednesdays of weeks 116, 120 and 124. The full walk is at `workspace/volume-08/batch-0001/SUMMARY.md` section 4.5, and **the two-file version of it that an earlier hand-off published is superseded.**
11. **He may not demonstrate, teach, assess or assist any practice in that department, on its premises, under its insurance, at any time, unlimited by hours**, and no room is exempt because he is in it. He may tell anybody how a thing like a chain would be kept going and he may not keep one going. He may not be asked to coordinate again by anybody.
12. **The Exchange's four figures are not convertible into one another or into a number of sittings**, the holder does not give the no-conversion rule again unless your plan says so in terms, the book is not opened on a Friday, and the ninth chair is not moved and its mover is not named.
