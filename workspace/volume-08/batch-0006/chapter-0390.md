# Chapter 390 — Four Rooms, And A Copy Nobody Turned Over

On the Thursday of week one hundred and forty four rooms in four buildings in four districts know that there is a printed paragraph on the back of a page of paper and none of the four has said a word about it in a room, and **about nine people are still copying that page out by hand, and about eight of the nine are still unfinished, and the first disagreement in its fourth line has still not been found, and nobody went looking for it on Thursday, and nothing in this case can say what held a line, because there is nothing to point at.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last of them at about six in the evening. He was at a bench in Lattice Ward until about four and then at a counter until six, and neither building is one of the four.

His word was at nine and ten. The room off a service road is five hundred and seven days old and it is warm, and about four people came in on the Thursday for the temperature, and nobody asked any of them what they had been doing.

The day went: a shop in the working Ashfields opened its door for the first time in five weeks and nobody in that street said anything about it, and four of the nine people copying a page of paper by hand were in a weighbridge office at about eleven, and one of them brought a folder, and the folder went on a table, and nobody turned anything in it over.

Then: the folder stayed on the table, and the office emptied at about one, and it was still there at about four, and about four people have been in that building since and the rest have not.

---

A woman of about fifty-three who runs a hardware shop in the working Ashfields opened that shop on the Thursday morning at about half past eight, and it had been shut since the Monday of the flood, which is about five weeks, and she had shut it on the Monday and opened it on the Thursday and lost the three days in between, and she has said that out loud once, in a different room, in a different week.

Nobody in that street congratulated her. Nobody rang her, and about four people who live near her have said that they were pleased and the others said nothing, and the two groups have not met, and her rent on that shop is the same as it was in the week before the water.

A woman of about thirty-nine who works in a pharmacy in Saltmarket said, at about eleven in that street, and it was about the shop being open, and nobody said it back to her.

**I watched her close it on the Monday and I have watched her open it today, and in between I have said nothing to her once, and I have worked out that not saying anything is a thing I did on purpose, and I would like somebody to tell me whether a person can do that.**

Nobody told her. Nobody has asked her since and nobody is going to, and about four people in that street have said that a person who says nothing on purpose has made a decision and four have said that not saying is not deciding, and the two have not met and she has heard neither.

A man of about fifty who owns a garage in the Lower Wards was two doors away at about nine and said, across two doorways, in about nine seconds, and nobody took it up.

"**Five weeks. I have been counting them since the Monday and I have not worked out who I am counting them for, and I have worked out today that I am counting them for me, and I would like that written down somewhere so that somebody can look at it and say that is a strange thing to have counted.**"

Nobody wrote it down. Nobody said it was strange and nobody said it was not, and he has not mentioned it since, and about four people in that street have said that a man who counts his own bad weeks is a man who has time and about four have said nothing.

Four of the nine people copying that page were in a weighbridge office in the working Ashfields at about eleven on the Thursday. There is a weighbridge outside it and four yards and a hut with a window, and about nine people work in and around it, and about four of them are among the nine who are copying.

One of the four is a man of about thirty-four who runs that office. He had a folder with a copy of the front of that page in it, and he had been copying it for about four weeks, and he brought it to a table at about eleven and put it down and did not say what it was.

A woman of about thirty-nine who works in a pharmacy in Saltmarket was in that office and said, about it, in about nine seconds, and nobody improved on it and nobody thanked her.

"**He has put it face up on that table and there are four of us in this hut and not one of us has looked at it, and I have been looking at the corner of it for about four minutes, and I am saying so out loud because I would like somebody else to be doing the same thing.**"

Nobody said they were. A man of about fifty-two who drives for a firm said, at about a quarter past eleven, and it was the only sentence anybody said in that hut for an hour afterwards.

"**I am not going to be the one. I have a bad leg and I have got a yard to sit in and I am going to go and sit in it, and if that makes me the one who does not care then I will be the one who does not care, and I have decided that today.**"

He went and sat in it. Nobody stopped him and nobody said anything about where he was going, and about four people in that yard have said that a man who has decided not to care is a man who has decided something and four have not said anything have said nothing.

**Nobody turned the copy over. The back of it is where a printed paragraph is, and nobody in that hut knew that until about four weeks later, and nobody in that hut has said anything about it, and the copy is still on that table face up and about four people have been in that building since and four did not notice have not.**

A first-year of nineteen was at a counter in Lattice Ward at about two in the afternoon and said one thing, and it was longer than about four seconds and she did not stop in the middle of it, and nobody at that counter improved on it and nobody thanked her.

"**Four rooms have known about that paragraph for about a week and not one of the four has said the word in a room, and I have been trying for about a week to work out what that is called, and I have got it, and it is not called anything, which is the problem. A thing nobody will say in a room is not a secret. It is just a thing that everybody is holding and nobody has a place to put down.**"

Nobody said whether she was right. Nobody has said since, and about four people in this case have said that a first-year of nineteen is not a person whose judgement about rooms anybody has a duty to publish, and about four have said that she is the only person in this case who has been in nine rooms in four months, and the two have not met and she is not interested in either.

A man of about thirty-three was in that counter's building for about ten minutes at about three and said nothing at all. He has held four buildings and about nine hundred doors for one hundred and forty days, which is twenty weeks, and in all that time he has needed nothing from anybody and been given nothing by anybody, and nobody in that building knew he had come and nobody has asked him why he came.

A woman of about thirty-four who checks about nine hundred doors in four buildings was in that building and said, to him, which is the first time in this case that anybody has said anything to him directly about the doors, and it took about nine seconds.

"**I have checked those doors for about four years and I have never once seen you, and I have worked out this week why, and I am not going to say why out loud, and I have said that I am not going to, and I would like it recorded that I said the first half before the second.**"

Nobody asked her the second half. He did not ask either and said nothing at all, and she has not said it, and about four people in that building have since said that she knew something about that man and about four have said that she was talking about a door, and the two have not met and neither is true.

**Four rooms know. Nobody has said. Nine people are copying and about eight of the nine are unfinished, and the first disagreement in the fourth line is a number that is right in six copies and wrong in three, and it is still not found, and nobody has been able to find it, and there is no meeting at which it could be found, and nobody has proposed one.**
