# Chapter 351 — A Shop With A Door In It

The Monday morning of week one hundred and twenty-nine was an ordinary working morning in a repair shop in Lattice Ward, five days after a room in Crown Terrace took the only thing he was doing off him, and at about ten past eight a person came to the counter and asked for him by name. **He said no in about nine seconds, there was no rule that stopped him and there is still no rule that stops him, and the person went away in about four minutes, and the only thing the morning changed is that he now knows they will come back.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date. Eleven attended, and the last of them finished at about six in the evening. There is a bench along one wall, a parts cage behind it, a locked relay cabinet, a shutter, a lamp, a tool roll with a fourth pocket, and a rail of card slots along the front of the counter. The word was at nine and ten. The room off a service road is four hundred and twenty-seven days old and is warm, and about four people came in on the Friday for the temperature and nobody asked any of them anything.

---

There are four places in this city where a man of twenty-two can be found, and **he has never once had a conversation about them, and not one of the four is a procedure.**

The first is the shop. It has a door on it and a counter, and about nine people a week go past the front of it, and anybody who wants him can stand at the counter and wait, and about four of them have.

The second is a plate on the outside of a converted shop unit off a service road. It carries a telephone number and no name. It was put up by somebody else, it is not his, and it has been on that wall since before he had a share of anything.

The third is a company's own system, in a box, which holds a card in date or an exception recorded and renewed for the term, and which carries a field at the foot of it that reads *No reason given by holder*. A person with a lawful reason can read that box and a person with an ordinary curiosity cannot, and the whole of the difference between those two people is a piece of metal and a field.

The fourth is a name on a tenancy, in a managing agent's file, in a building in the first of the four districts. **That is the one that put him on a form four years ago, because a line on that form said to use the tenant of the premises below, and nobody thought to ask whether the tenant was the person who did the work.**

He stood behind the counter and went through all four of them in about nine seconds, and then he said the thing out loud, and what he said out loud was not the no.

---

She was nineteen and she was a first-year, and she had a piece of paper in her hand the size of both hands together, and it was not the page anybody has been copying.

"**I am not asking you to do anything.**"

"**You are holding a piece of paper.**"

"**It is nine lines and four of them are mine. It is on the back of a receipt. I am going to throw it away in four minutes and I would like you to have read it first, and that is the whole of why I am here.**"

He read it. It was nine lines and it was about doors, and about half of it was a question and the other half was a number, and he has not seen it since and was not asked about it since and is not going to be.

---

Then she said the ask. It took her about nine seconds. She did not build up to it and she did not apologise for the hour and she put the receipt face down on the counter while she said it, which is a thing a person does when they have decided in advance not to leave anything behind.

"**There is a table. Nine people, nine rooms, four districts, and a list that nobody in this case knows who keeps. And one of the nine will not come to a room and will not say why, and I have asked him twice, and I am not going to ask him a third time because I am not going to be the person who asks a man three times. Somebody has to be the one the other eight can ring. And I would like it to be you. And you would not have to do anything. You would only have to answer.**"

Nobody in that shop said anything for about nine seconds. A man came in the door behind her, saw a girl at the counter talking to the owner, and turned round and went out again, and neither of them looked at him and he was not looked at.

He said: "**If I am the one they ring, then in about four months there are eight rooms and a telephone. The telephone is in my name. And the eight rooms are mine because the other one is mine. And I will be a room.**"

She said: "**That is not what I asked you.**"

He said: "**No. It is what you will get.**"

---

She was quiet for about nine seconds and she did not argue, and about four people in that shop at about a quarter to nine heard all of it and about four heard none of it.

"**All right. Then I will tell them there is nobody to ring. And that will be true on Wednesday, and it will be true because you said it, and you will not be in that room when I say it.**"

"**That is correct. And you do not have to come to that room.**"

"**No.**"

"**I am not going to ask you again.**"

"**You should. Not today.**"

Nobody improved on any of it. She did not improve on it and he did not improve on it, and about four people inside a week were told the second half of it and not the first, and the two groups have not met and are not going to.

---

Here is the cost, and it was not the no.

A no he can enforce on himself is a thing with a shape, and he knows where he put it, and on a bad morning in about two years he can find it again. This one was said in a shop with a door on it, to a person of nineteen, at about ten past eight in the morning, at a counter that anybody in this city can stand at, and none of the four places has a procedure for a no, and nobody wrote one, and nobody is going to.

He is not unfindable. He has never been unfindable and there has never been an afternoon on which a person could not walk into a shop in Lattice Ward and wait. He is unfindable for a purpose. That was decided in a room with a stamp by about nine people who were not him, and what it changed was what a person might ask him, and a thing that changes what a person might ask is not a thing a counter can hold.

He had known that for five days. What he did not know until about ten past eight this morning is that somebody else had worked it out, had come to the front of the shop to say so out loud, and could not be told.

He got on with the morning. He did eleven jobs and he got one of them wrong on the first attempt and did it again, and the second one is the one on the rail.

---

At about half past six the room off a service road was warm and about nine people were in it. A cook of twenty-seven was at the sink and said nothing at all. A woman of about thirty was in the chair nearest the door and was not asked anything and was not thanked and was not apologised to, and the reason she is in that room is the temperature, and the temperature costs more than it did, and both of those are correct.

A man of fifty-one with a tool bag was at the counter and said nothing and has not been asked about nothing, and that is the standing arrangement and it is not going to be changed this month either.

A first-year of nineteen was at the counter and said one small unasked thing in about four seconds about a bus that had started from the market at ten instead of nine, and it was not about any of it and nobody improved on it and nobody thanked her.

She did not ask him again. She was in that room for about two hours and she did not ask him again, and about four people noticed her not asking and about four did not, and he was one of the four who noticed and has said nothing to anybody about noticing and is not going to.

The hardboard on two nails carries nineteen lines. The first of them is a cook's and is still the first line, and the newest is a man of twenty-nine's, and it is the only promise on that wall, and it is one hundred and twenty-three days old, and nobody wrote on it today.

The heating is on and it costs more, and four names on a lease cost more than they did, and the fortnight a cook paid for is unpaid by anybody and is not resolved.

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.
