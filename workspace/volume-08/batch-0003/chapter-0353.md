# Chapter 353 — A Limit About Something Small

The Thursday of week one hundred and twenty-nine was a Thursday, and a man of twenty-two and a woman of twenty-four who has been his Thursday for four years were four miles apart in two workrooms doing two jobs that get paid for, and **at about eleven in the morning she told him not to bring her the sheet any more, and it was a small thing, and he took it, and it was the first limit said to him in a room in four years that he did not immediately try to turn into something he could do.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date. Eleven attended, and the last of them finished at about six in the evening. He did no work of his own before half past eight and none at all after about two, and neither of those is unusual and neither of them is on any sheet.

The word was at nine and ten. The room off a service road is four hundred and thirty days old and is warm, and four people came in on the Friday for the temperature and nobody asked any of them anything.

---

Her workroom is one floor above a corridor with a plate on the outside of the building and about four desks in it, and one of the desks is hers, and there is a consent clipboard on the wall above that desk that no institution has ever asked her for and that has been there for longer than either of them has worked.

There are nine people in the building on a Thursday. There is a woman of about thirty-four on the second desk who has been waiting since about ten for a thing that is not medical and that she has twice been told is not urgent. There is a first-year of nineteen on the third desk doing a rotation, filing consent sheets into a folder nobody has asked the contents of.

He came up at about ten past eleven with a sheet in his hand. He has brought that sheet on about nine Thursdays in about four months. It is a page of his own with about seven lines on it in his own hand, and it is what he did that week and where he was and how long it took, and it has no heading and no date and no reference and no signature, and it is not a report and is not a return and is not a minute and is not a form and nobody has ever asked for it.

She read it standing up. It takes her about four minutes. She has read about nine of them and has answered about four.

---

Then she said the limit, and it took her four seconds, and she did not build up to it and she did not apologise for it and she did not explain it afterwards.

"**I am not going to keep reading that.**"

"**All right.**"

"**And I want to say the rest of it now and not keep saying no, because I have said no four times and you have not once asked me why, and you not asking me why is the reason I am saying the rest of it.**"

"**All right.**"

"**You are not to bring it. And if you bring it, it goes in the drawer with the clips and you will find it there in about a month, and that is not a punishment and I am not going to tell you when I have decided that, and I am not going to be the person you come to about a sheet of paper.**"

---

Here is what he did with it, and it is the whole of what is in this chapter.

He said: "**That is fair.**"

He did not ask her what she would prefer instead. He did not say he would send less. He did not say he would think about it. He did not say he understood why, because she had not given a why and he did not ask for one. He did not offer to do anything else instead of it, and he did not ask whether there was a version of the sheet she would take, and he did not say the word *Thursday* again for the rest of the morning.

**About four people in that building were within about nine feet of it and four of them noticed that he did not do any of those five things, and four did not, and the two groups have not spoken to each other and are not going to.**

He sat on the second desk for about two minutes with a woman of about thirty-four and did not tell her anything about a sheet of paper. Then he went downstairs at about twenty past eleven and did four jobs and got one of them wrong.

It is the first time in four years that a limit said to him in a room has not become a job he offered to do in the same breath, and nine people in this case have been waiting four years to find out what he would do with one, and he did nothing, and nobody has said anything to him about having done nothing.

---

There is a written record between them. There have been two for four years and there were three for about four months of that, and neither of them has ever called it anything, and it is not shared and it is not on a system and there is no copy of it in any building but the two they work in.

It goes four miles, twice a week, in a bag, on a bus or on foot depending on the weather, and it is carried by the person who made it, and the person who made it is the person who carries it, and it is handed over at a door or put in a rack or left on a counter, and it is about work.

**In the last four months that record has held, on about nine Thursdays and on about eleven other days, four kinds of thing: a count of something done; a thing that went wrong and what it cost; a thing somebody said that he has not decided what to do with; and one line at the foot, every time, in his own hand, which is a single word and means that he is not asking her to do anything about any of it.**

The word is *noted*. It has been on about nine of the sheets and he has never explained it and she has never asked. It is the only part of that record that is not work, and it is the part that exists so that the rest of it can be work, and neither of them has ever said so.

What is not in it, in four months, in about twenty sheets: the separation, a date, the word *Thursday*, the word *middle*, the fact that a room in Crown Terrace took something off him eight days before this Thursday, and a sentence beginning *I can carry it*, which he has said to her six times in four years and did not write down on any of them, and which is not written down now.

The first-year of nineteen on the third desk has read none of it. Nobody has given it to her and nobody has stopped her and it has never been on a desk she was at.

---

The first-year of nineteen was at the third desk and she said the true thing in nine words, and it was at about half past four in the afternoon, and he is not going to be asked about it and is not going to be asked why he did not answer it properly.

"**Nobody here asks you because you always say yes.**"

He said: "**That is not why. I say no.**"

Seven words. It was wrong. **He knew it was wrong while he was saying it, and he did not argue, and he did not correct himself afterwards, and he did not say anything else for nine seconds, and four people in that room were not in that room.**

**Nobody improved on it. She did not improve on it and he did not improve on it and four people have heard one half of it and about four have heard the other half and the two halves are not joined anywhere and are not going to be.**

She was right. She was right for a reason that has nothing to do with this room and that she did not give and was not asked for, and the reason is that a man of twenty-two has spent about four years making himself the easiest person in the city to use and has been proud of the trick, and being proud of the trick is what makes it a trick.

**He has not said this to anybody and is not going to. It is the only sentence in this chapter that is in this entry and not in the mouth of anybody, and it is here because a person of nineteen said nine words in a workroom and he was wrong in seven and has been carrying the other two since about half past four on a Thursday.**

---

At about half past six the room off a service road was warm and nine people were in it. A cook of twenty-seven was at the sink and said nothing at all. A woman of about thirty was in the chair nearest the door and was not asked anything and was not thanked and was not apologised to, and the reason she is in that room is the temperature, and the temperature costs more than it did, and both of those are correct.

A man of about fifty-one with a tool bag was in it and said nothing, and has been asked about nothing for about five weeks and a half, and the asking has not been renewed and nobody has stopped it.

The hardboard on two nails carries nineteen lines. The first of them is a cook's and is still the first line. The newest is a man of twenty-nine's and is the only promise on that wall and it is one hundred and twenty-six days old, and nobody wrote on it today.

The heating is on and costs more and four names on a lease cost more and the fortnight a cook paid for is unpaid by anybody and is not resolved.

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.

*356. Thursday of week 129, at about ten on a service road, and this entry is two workrooms four miles apart, one sheet of paper with about seven lines in one hand, one limit about a small thing, one refusal of it, and nine words from a person of nineteen at about half past four.*

*Conditions and docket.* **Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last at about six in the evening. One visit, at about ten past eleven, about twenty minutes. The rate held at nine and ten. The heating is on and costs more and no figure of days off is carried. The room off a service road is four hundred and thirty days old. The four figures, printed and not converted: fifty-one, seventy-two, twenty-six, nineteen years.**

*The limit, as a record.* **Said by: a woman of twenty-four, a resonance nurse, at about eleven in the morning on a Thursday, in about four seconds, in her own workroom, to a man of twenty-two who had come up with a sheet in his hand. Words used: that she is not going to keep reading it; and that she wants to say the rest of it now instead of saying no four more times, because he has not once asked her why and his not asking is the reason. Went downstairs at about twenty past eleven and did four jobs and got one of them wrong. Jobs offered to anybody: none. Jobs taken that he had not been asked to take: none. Times he said he would think about it: none. People within about nine feet who noticed he did not do any of those five things: about four. People within about nine feet who did not notice: about four. Improved on: by nobody. Thanked: by anybody: no. Owner: him, and the case, and the first time in four years that this has happened and about nine people have been waiting four years to find out.**

*The nine words, as a record.* **Speaker: a first-year of nineteen, at the third desk, at about half past four in the afternoon, in a workroom four miles from a repair shop in Lattice Ward. Words used: nine. Subject: him, and the reason nobody in that building asks him for anything. His answer, seven words, given immediately: that it is not why, and that he says no. Whether the first-year of nineteen on the third desk has read any of it: no, and nobody has given it to her and nobody has stopped her. Owner: the two of them, and the sheets, and neither of them is a room.**

*What the day did not touch.* **A sheet of paper with about seven lines in one hand was not improved on, not replaced, not summarised, not shortened and not sent anywhere, and its being stopped is not a rule and is not a form and is not enforceable against anybody. A separation four months old was not discussed and neither of them said a thing about it, and what passed between them that morning was about where a sheet of paper goes and who is allowed to open the drawer it is in. Nine people in about nine rooms in about four districts did not meet and no list was produced and no minute of any kind was taken. A man of about thirty-three who has held four buildings since the Thursday of week one hundred and twenty was not in either room and was not mentioned by anybody in either room. A hardboard on two nails was not written on, a card in a rail did not move, and the master's fifth line is still blank in a drawer nobody opened. Nobody died. Nobody thanked anybody. Entry 356.**
