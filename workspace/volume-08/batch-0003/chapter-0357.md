# Chapter 357 — The Second Friday

The Friday evening of week one hundred and thirty was the second Friday in a fortnight on which a company training room in Crown Terrace ran a floor, and eleven people were in it again, and **nothing about that room was resolved and nothing about it was rehearsed, and the only new thing that happened in it was eight words said at the back by a man of fifty-eight, and nobody asked him anything about them, and nobody wrote them down, and the room went on exactly as it was.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date. Eleven attended, and the last of them finished at about six in the evening, and there are no jobs of his after about six on a Friday.

The word was at nine and ten. The room off a service road is four hundred and thirty-eight days old and is warm, and about four people came in on the Friday for the temperature and nobody asked any of them anything.

---

The room has nine folding chairs in it and eleven people, and the laminated sheet is still on the post and has not been written on and has not been taken down and has been on that post for four years.

**The company buys the training. The company does not have anybody else on its own staff list in that room except the woman who calls it, and nobody in that room has ever been asked what the training is for, and nobody in that company has ever come to that room to look at it, and the two of those facts have been true for four years and nine people in that room know both of them.**

**Nobody in that room is on a rota. There is no rota in that building for that room, and there has not been one for four years, and the sheet on the post says what a run is and who calls it and does not say who is expected, and nobody has ever asked the company to say.**

Here is who is in it, and the description is nine lines long because there is nothing else to say about any of them and because about four of them have never given a reason for coming and four have not been asked for one.

A man of fifty-eight who works in a depot at the far end of the city and who comes for the sake of coming. A man of about thirty-one who also works in that depot and who has been in the room for about two years. A woman of about thirty-four who has been in it for two years and who has not spoken in it for a long time. A man of about twenty-nine who comes on a Friday and who has not spoken in that room in four years. A woman of about thirty-eight who has been in it for two years and who has now not spoken in it for four Fridays running. A woman of about thirty-six with a lanyard on who works there and who calls the run. A man of about forty-four who has been in it for four years and who does not speak in it. A woman of about thirty who is in it about twice a month. And a man of about twenty-seven who comes when he can get the hours and about whom nobody in that room knows anything except that he comes.

**That is nine of about eleven and the other two are not missing on any particular Friday and are not chased, and nobody in that room has ever made a list of who was there, and about four people in that room could produce a rough count of the Friday before from memory and about four could not and neither group has said so.**

**Two of the eleven did not come. Nobody chased them. Nobody in that room has chased anybody on any Friday and four people in that room have noticed that nobody chases anybody and four have not.**

**The floor is the second floor of a building in Crown Terrace and has a corridor, a fire door at each end, a stair, and about nine doors that are not opened during the run.**

---

The run went at about half past seven. Nine people, one floor, about nine minutes, called from the front by the woman of thirty-six, and everybody did the thing they did in the last one, and the sheet on the post was not read from and nothing was handed to anybody and nobody counted for anybody and nobody prompted anybody.

**Nobody in that room referred to the Friday before. Not one of the eleven, and not the woman of thirty-six, and not the man of fifty-eight, and four of them noticed that nobody had and four did not, and there is no way of knowing whether she thought about it and she has not said and is not going to be asked.**

**It finished. It finished the way a floor finishes.** Nobody said anything how long it took, and nobody said anything how long it did not take, and about nine people stood where they had stopped for about four seconds and then sat down, and the four seconds are not the four seconds of the Friday before and are not about the four seconds of the Friday before and are not being counted and have not been counted by anybody.

**The woman of thirty-four who has been in that room for two years did the whole of it properly, without being told. The man of about twenty-nine who comes on a Friday sat down at the right moment without being told.** About four people noticed that and four did not, and it is the fourth Friday in a row and four people have noticed every time and have not said one word about it to each other.

---

Then the man of fifty-eight said it, at the back, in about four seconds, and it is eight words and he has said that he was not going to say it and then he said it.

"**About four of us are not on anything.**"

Nobody said anything.

"**Not on the list and not on a rota and not on anything, and I have been in this room four years and nobody has ever asked me why I come, and I am not asking anybody now, and I have said this once and I would like it to be once.**"

Nobody improved on it. Nobody asked him a question about it. The woman of thirty-six did not respond to it and did not put it on the sheet and did not make it an item, and four people in that room looked at the floor and about four looked at the door.

**That is the only time anybody has ever said it who is in this case, and it is in a mouth and not in a record and not on a surface, and nobody who was not in that room has said it, and it has been said before, once, a long time ago, and it is not going to be said again.**

Here is what did not happen in the ninety minutes after it, and it is worth having in one place because the thing that usually happens is that a room makes a thing out of a sentence.

Nobody stood up. Nobody wrote anything down, and the woman of thirty-six has the only pen in that room on a Friday and it was in her pocket. Nobody asked the man of fifty-eight what he meant, and nobody asked him whether he meant about four, and nobody asked him to say it again for the two people who had not heard it, and the two people who had not heard it are about four and one of them was at the front and one of them was on the stair.

**Nobody suggested that the company be asked for a rota, and nobody suggested that the company be asked anything, and four people in that room have since told about four other people that a rota would be the obvious thing to do and none of the four has said it to anybody who could act on it.**

About four minutes afterwards a person asked the woman of thirty-six about a fire door on the second landing and she answered that question practically and at some length, and the room went back to being a room with nine chairs in it and about eleven people in it, **and the eight words stayed where they had been, which is in the air of a room in Crown Terrace for about ninety seconds.**

---

The man of fifty-eight left at about nine with two of the four who come on no list, and he did not say anything to either of them on the way out, and neither of them has said anything to anybody about walking out with him.

They were in the room for about ninety minutes afterwards and four of the eleven stayed and the rest went at about nine. Half of the eleven have been in that room on every Friday for four years and the other half for about two, and neither half knows it is a half.

They go out through a lobby and up a ramp and onto a forecourt with about nine cars on it and a man in a booth who has never once been inside that building, and he does not know any of the eleven and has never been asked to and would not be told, and four of the eleven have spoken to him and four have not and he keeps no list and has not been asked to keep one. **He is the only person in that building with a post, a stamp and a piece of paper, and none of the three has ever touched anything that happens on the second floor, and nobody has ever put those two facts next to each other and this entry does not put them next to each other either.**

A woman of about thirty-eight who has been in that room for two years and who has not spoken in it for a long time did not speak in it on this Friday either, and she has now not spoken in it for the fourth Friday running, and nobody has asked her and nobody has said anything about it.

---

From about half past six the room off a service road was warm and nine people were in it, a cook of twenty-seven at a sink and a woman of about thirty in the chair nearest the door, and she was not asked anything and was not thanked and was not apologised to, and the reason she comes is the temperature, and the temperature costs more than it did, and both of those are correct.

A first-year of nineteen was at the counter and said one small unasked thing in about four seconds about a landlord and a letter, and nobody improved on it and nobody thanked her.

A man of fifty-one with a tool bag was in it and said nothing and has been asked about nothing for about six weeks and two days, and the asking has not been renewed and nobody has stopped it.

**A card in a rail in Lattice Ward is four hundred and forty-two days in the rail and a hardboard on two nails carries twelve lines that are three hundred and fifty-eight days old, and the two figures are each other's age and neither of them is a day anybody has named, and a person reading this entry will not find a date in either of them.**

The newest line on that hardboard is a man of twenty-nine's and it is the only promise on it and it is one hundred and thirty-four days old. The heating is on and costs more and four names on a lease cost more and the fortnight a cook paid for is unpaid by anybody and is not resolved.

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.

*360. Friday of week 130, at about ten on a service road, and this entry is a company training room in Crown Terrace for the second Friday running, nine folding chairs, a laminated sheet on a post, about eleven people, one floor, nobody referring to the Friday before, and eight words at the back.*

*Conditions and docket.* **Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last at about six in the evening, and no jobs of his after that on a Friday. One company training room in Crown Terrace from about half past six until about nine, nine folding chairs, a laminated sheet on a post that has been there four years and has never been written on and has never been taken down, about eleven people, seven on the company's list and four on none. Two of the eleven did not come, were not chased, and nobody in that room chases anybody on any Friday. One run of nine people over one floor, about nine minutes, called from the front at about half past seven by a woman of thirty-six with a lanyard on who works there, and finished the way a floor finishes, with nobody saying anything how long it took or how long it did not take. One statement of eight words at the back, at about half past eight, by a man of fifty-eight who has been in that room for four years and who said he was not going to say it, and then said it once. One room off a service road from about half past six, about nine people. The rate held at nine and ten. The heating is on and costs more and no figure of days off is carried. The room off a service road is four hundred and thirty-eight days old. A card in a rail in Lattice Ward is four hundred and forty-two days in the rail, a third object, and is not in that room and is not in a company training room. The hardboard: twelve lines three hundred and fifty-eight, thirteen three hundred and nine, fourteen two hundred and seventy-four, fifteen two hundred and fifty-three, sixteen two hundred and twenty-eight, seventeen two hundred and ten, eighteen one hundred and fifty-six, nineteen one hundred and thirty-four, and the card's figure in that sentence and the first of those two figures are each other's anchor, and neither of them is a date. Wall unchanged: nineteen lines in thirteen hands and fourteen names, none added, none removed, none rubbed off. The first line is a cook's and is still the first line and the newest is eleven words and is the only promise on it. Eight items in a ring binder, unopened, unread. The woman's page is unread. The master's fifth line is blank and the drawer was not opened. The Exchange did not sit on this day and does not sit on any Friday. Its last sitting was a Wednesday sixteen days ago and its next sitting is a Wednesday twelve days from today, and the book is at fifty-one lines, the telephone at seventy-two, the count has been announced twenty-six times, and nineteen years is nineteen years. The four figures, printed and not converted: fifty-one, seventy-two, twenty-six, nineteen years.**

*The run, as a record of hands and seconds.* **Room: a company training room in Crown Terrace. Chairs: about nine. People: about eleven, seven on the company's list and four on none. Absent: two, unchased, and nobody in that room chases anybody. Sheet: laminated, about eleven lines, on a post, in place for four years, never written on, never taken down, and read from by nobody on this evening as on any evening. Interventions before the run began: none; no number was read from the post, no hand touched anybody's back, nobody counted, nobody prompted. Floor completed by: nine people. Time the nine stood where they had stopped: about four seconds, unconnected to any other four seconds and uncounted. Referrals to the Friday before by anybody in that room: none. Improved on: by nobody. Thanked: by anybody: no. Owner: the case, and eleven people.**

*The eight words, as a record.* **Said by: a man of fifty-eight who has been in that room for four years and who comes for the sake of coming. Where: at the back, at about half past eight, after the run. Length: eight words. Words: about four of us are not on anything. What he added, in about nine seconds: not on the list, not on a rota, not on anything; that he has been in that room four years and nobody has ever asked him why he comes; that he is not asking anybody now; and that he would like it to be once. Questions asked of him: none. Replies given: none. Put on the sheet: no. Made into an item by anybody: no. Response from the woman of thirty-six: none. People who looked at the floor: about four. People who looked at the door: about four. Said to anybody outside that room: by nobody. The two people closest to it have not said one word to each other about it. Times this has been said in this case: once here, and once before it, a long time ago, and it is not going to be said again. Improved on: by nobody. Thanked: by anybody: no. Owner: the case, and eleven people, and four of them.**

*The room, as a record of what is in it.* **Chairs: about nine folding. People: about eleven, of whom seven are on a company list with seven names on it and four are on nothing at all. Rota for that room in that building: none, and there has not been one for about four years, and the sheet on the post does not name who is expected. Sheet: about eleven lines, laminated, on a post, in place about four years, never written on, never taken down, read from by nobody. Floor: a corridor, two fire doors, a stair, about nine doors that stay shut. Called from the front by: one woman of thirty-six. Attendance kept: no, by anybody. Absent and unchased: two. Car park: about nine cars, a ramp, a lobby, and one man in a booth who has never been inside the building, does not know any of the eleven, keeps no list and has never been asked to. Post in that building: one, with a stamp and a piece of paper, and it has never touched the second floor. Owner: the company, and the booth, and the two have never met.**

*What followed the eight words, as a record of nothing.* **People who stood up: none. Things written down: none, and the woman of thirty-six had the only pen in that room on a Friday and it was in her pocket. Questions asked of the man who said them: none. Questions asked of him about whether he meant about four: none. Requests that he say it again for the two who had not heard it: none, and about four had not heard it and one of them was at the front and one was on the stair. Suggestions that the company be asked for a rota: none. Suggestions that the company be asked anything: none. Rota made: none. List made: none. Column added to anything: none. Item created: none. Minutes of the ninety minutes: none. Person who took the eight words outside that room: none, and about four people in that room have told about four other people and the chain of telling is four long and nobody has said what was said. Owner: nobody, and the eight words, and the room.**

*What the day did not touch.* **A rota was not made, a list was not written, a column was not added to anything, and the woman of thirty-six did not turn eight words into an item. A run happened on a Friday and it was not a rehearsal of the Friday before and it did not settle anything about the Friday before. A page of paper with about eleven lines on it was not in that room and was not discussed in it. A man of about thirty-three who has held four buildings for seventy-one days was not in that room and nobody in it mentioned him. A hardboard on two nails was not written on, a card in its rail did not move, and the master's fifth line is still blank. Nobody died. Nobody thanked anybody. Entry 360.**
