# Chapter 355 — Nine Copies Of One Page

The Monday morning of week one hundred and thirty was the morning the page of paper with about eleven lines on it stopped being the only one, and **about nine people in nine rooms in about four districts copied it out by hand while looking at it, and the first copy was finished at about half past four in the afternoon by a woman of about thirty-eight who said in about nine seconds that she had not made a copy, and nobody improved on that and nobody thanked her, and it is the first of the nine.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date. Eleven attended, and the last of them finished at about six in the evening. He was at the repair shop in Lattice Ward for the whole of the day and the counter of the room off a service road for the evening, and he did not see the page and was not asked about it.

The word was at nine and ten. The room off a service road is four hundred and thirty-four days old and is warm, and about four people came in on the Friday for the temperature and nobody asked any of them anything.

---

The page is about the size of both hands together. It is in the hand of a woman of twenty-four and it has eleven lines on it and each line is about a week.

It is not a form. It is not a minute. It is not a return, a schedule, a claim, a notification, a list of losses, a schedule of hardship, or a register. It has no reference, no date, no heading, no print and no box, and it is not signed, and there is no second copy of it anywhere in this city, and it is on nobody's system.

Four of the eleven wrote their own line on it themselves, in that room, with her pen, and about seven of the eleven said theirs to her over the course of a week and she wrote down what they said and did not improve any of it.

**It is on a table in a room in a building in the third of the four districts, and it has been on that table since a Monday in an earlier volume, and about four people look at it while they are emptying that room and about four do not, and one person has picked it up and read the whole of it standing up and put it back in the same place, and that is the difference between it and a form.**

---

Here is the whole of what has been asked for it, and it is in this entry because the nine people who are about to copy it are entitled to know it.

A Continuity Office man of fifty-eight with a bad hip came to that room at about half past ten on the Monday in an earlier volume and was not asked to. He said why he wanted it and what he wanted it for and how long he would hold it, and he did not say the word *scan* and he did not say the word *record* and he did not say that it would help.

They said no. About four of them said no, one of them at length and one of them in about four words. He did not ask a second time and did not ask a third time and did not say that it would be a pity and did not suggest a compromise.

**He wrote nothing down. He has a job in which he writes things down and he did not write anything down that morning, and nobody asked him why not, and he has not been in that room since, and there is no note of that request in any office in this city, which means that the only record of it is eleven people in a room and a woman of twenty-four's memory.**

Nobody thanked him and nobody has thanked the room for the refusal and the refusal is not a decision anybody can enforce against anybody who did not write it down.

---

The copying began at about ten in the morning and nobody organised it, and nobody asked the woman of twenty-four whether she minded, and nobody asked the eleven people whether they minded either.

**There is no list of who is copying it. There is no instruction and no form and no request and no deadline, and the person who asked the first person to copy it asked them on a telephone on a Friday, and the person who asked the second person asked them in a corridor, and about four of the nine have not been asked by anybody at all and are copying it because they were in a room and saw it on a table. Nobody has given anybody a piece of paper for it and nobody has been given one for it.**

Nine people, nine rooms, and about nine weeks, and the first of the nine is finished.

---

The first copy was made in the second of the four districts by a woman of about thirty-eight who has been in a company training room for two years and has not spoken in it for a long time, and she was in that room in the third of the four districts on a Monday in an earlier volume with about nine other people in it, and she brought nothing to that room that day and said nothing and was not asked.

She copied it out by hand, at a table, on the Monday, with the page in front of her, from about ten until about half past four, and she used her own pen and her own paper and she did not have a machine in that building.

She was slow. She was slower than it takes to copy eleven lines. She got the fourth line wrong the first time and did it again, and she did not change the wording of anything, and she did not put a heading on it and she did not date it and she did not sign it, and she has not been asked why and is not going to be.

Then she said the thing, and it took her about nine seconds, and nobody improved on it.

"**I have not made a copy. I have made a page with my hand on it, and her hand is not on it, and there are four other hands in the original that are not in mine, and mine is the only one in this that is mine.**"

Nobody said anything for about nine seconds.

A man of about twenty-seven who has read and replaced the numbers on the doors in a building in the first of the four districts for four years said: "**Then what have you made.**"

"**I have made nine words of mine and two words of hers, and I have not got the other four hands, and I have got a week in the wrong place on the fourth line, and I have got it right now, and about four people in that room are going to do this and get it wrong.**"

**Nobody improved on that either. She is not thanked and she is owed nothing and she holds no post and about four people inside a week were told the second half of what she said and not the first, and the two groups have not met.**

---

Here is the change and it is not a small one.

For about four months that page was the only record in this city of what about eleven people lost on a Friday night in an earlier volume, and it had exactly the property that made it a page and not a form: **anybody could pick it up, and anybody could put it back, and nobody could find it again, and if it was lost it was lost, and every person in that room knew that and none of them had ever suggested a drawer.**

At about half past four on the Monday of week one hundred and thirty there were two of them, and by about nine that evening there were two, and by the end of about nine weeks there are going to be ten, and the tenth is a sheet of paper in a woman's own hand in the second of the four districts with a woman's own hand nowhere on it.

**Nobody has decided that. It happened because a page can be looked at from across a table, and because about nine people were in nine rooms on the same Monday, and because the person who asked the first person to copy it did it on a telephone and did not tell her what she was supposed to be making.**

**That is the whole of the change, and nobody in this case has a plan for it and nobody has said out loud that it is a change, and the woman of twenty-four, who made the page, does not know that it has been copied and has not been told and is not to be told.**

---

At about half past six the room off a service road was warm and nine people were in it. A cook of twenty-seven was at the sink and said nothing at all. A woman of about thirty was in the chair nearest the door and was not asked anything and was not thanked and was not apologised to, and the reason she is in that room is the temperature, and the temperature costs more than it did, and both of those are correct.

A first-year of nineteen was at the counter and said one small unasked thing in about four seconds a clinic that had changed a booking system, and it was not about any of it and nobody improved on it and nobody thanked her.

A man of fifty-one with a tool bag was in it and said nothing and has been asked about nothing for about six weeks, and the asking has not been renewed and nobody has stopped it.

The hardboard on two nails carries nineteen lines and the newest of them is the only promise on it and it is one hundred and thirty days old. The heating is on and costs more and four names on a lease cost more and the fortnight a cook paid for is unpaid by anybody and is not resolved.

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.

*358. Monday of week 130, at about ten on a service road, and this entry is a page of paper with about eleven lines in it, a refusal that is on no surface, nine people in nine rooms, one copy finished at about half past four, and a page that is no longer the only one.*

*Conditions and docket.* **Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last at about six in the evening. He was at the repair shop in Lattice Ward for the whole day and at the counter of the room off a service road in the evening, and he did not see the page and was not asked about it. One room in a building in the third of the four districts, the page on a table in it, about nine chairs, no name on the door, nothing on the wall, not a meeting, no minute of it. Nine people copying the page out by hand in nine rooms in about four districts, in about nine weeks, of which one copy was finished on this day; no list of who is copying it, no instruction, no form, no request and no deadline; about four of the nine were asked by somebody and about four were not asked by anybody and are copying it because they were in a room. One copy finished at about half past four in a room in the second of the four districts, by a woman of about thirty-eight, from about ten until about half past four, in her own hand, with the page in front of her. One room off a service road from about half past six, about nine people. The rate held at nine and ten. The heating is on and costs more and no figure of days off is carried. The room off a service road is four hundred and thirty-four days old. A card in a rail in Lattice Ward is four hundred and thirty-eight days in the rail, a third object, and is not in that room and is not in a room in a building in the third of the four districts. The hardboard: twelve lines three hundred and fifty-four, thirteen three hundred and five, fourteen two hundred and seventy, fifteen two hundred and forty-nine, sixteen two hundred and twenty-four, seventeen two hundred and six, eighteen one hundred and fifty-two, nineteen one hundred and thirty. Wall unchanged: nineteen lines in thirteen hands and fourteen names, none added, none removed, none rubbed off. The first line is a cook's and is still the first line and the newest is eleven words and is the only promise on it. Eight items in a ring binder, unopened, unread. The woman's page is unread. The Exchange did not sit on this day. Its last sitting was a Wednesday twelve days ago and its next sitting is a Wednesday sixteen days from today, and the book is at fifty-one lines, the telephone at seventy-two, the count has been announced twenty-six times, and nineteen years is nineteen years. The four figures, printed and not converted: fifty-one, seventy-two, twenty-six, nineteen years.**

*The page, as a record of a surface.* **Size: about the size of both hands together. Hand of the writing: a woman of twenty-four. Lines: eleven, and each line is about a week. Lines in other hands: four of the eleven, written by the people themselves, in that room, with her pen. Lines said to her and written down by her: about seven, in about a week, and not improved by her. Heading: none. Date: none. Reference: none. Print: none. Box: none. Signature: none. Second copy: none, anywhere in this city. System: none, and there is no system in this city that a woman's handwriting would go into. Where it is: a table in a room in a building in the third of the four districts, where it has been since a Monday in an earlier volume. Picked up and put back by: one person, whole, standing. People who look at it while emptying the room: about four. People who do not: about four. Form: it is not a form, and the test of that is that anybody may pick it up and put it back and nobody can find it again, and if it is lost it is lost, and everybody in that room knew that and none of them had ever suggested a drawer. Owner: the woman of twenty-four, and eleven people, and nobody else.**

*The refusal, as a record of what is on the page.* **Asked by: a Continuity Office man of fifty-eight with a bad hip, at about half past ten on a Monday in an earlier volume, unasked, in about a room with nine people in it. Asked correctly: he said why he wanted it and what he wanted it for and how long he would hold it, and he did not say the word scan and did not say the word record and did not say that it would help. Answer: no, said by four of the nine, one at length and one in about four words. Second request: none. Third request: none. A pity: not said. A compromise: not suggested. Written down: nothing, by a man whose job is to written things down. In that office afterwards: no note, and no record, and the only places the request exists are nine people in a room and one woman's memory. Enforceable against anybody who did not write it down: nobody. Thanked: by anybody: no. Asked since: by nobody. Owner: nobody, and the page, which was not given.**

*The first copy, as a record of an object.* **Made by: a woman of about thirty-eight, in a room in the second of the four districts, on the Monday morning, from about ten until about half past four, at a table, with the page in front of her. Paper: her own. Pen: her own. Machine available in that building: none. Lines copied: eleven. Wording changed: none. Fourth line: done once wrongly, then done again, and it is now right, and she has said that about four people are going to get it wrong. Heading: none. Date: none. Signature: none. Hand or hands on the object: hers, and hers only. Hands on the original: five, in five people. What she said, in nine seconds, and nobody improved on it: that she has not made a copy, and that she has made a page with her hand on it, and that the hand of the woman of twenty-four is not on it, and that there are four other hands in the original that are not in hers, and that hers is the only one in it that is hers. What she was asked in reply, in six words: then what have you made. What she said, in about nine seconds: that she has made nine words of hers and two words of the other, and has not got the other four hands, and had a week in the wrong place on the fourth line and has it right now. Thanked: by anybody: no. Owed: nothing. Post: none. Owner: her, and the object, and the eight copies that do not exist yet.**

*The nine, as a record of hands and rooms.* **Number: nine. Rooms: nine, in about four districts, of which about four are a room in the Lower Wards, a room above a line in Saltmarket, four feet of shop in the Quiet Quarter, and a weighbridge office in the working Ashfields, and the other five are in buildings nobody in this case has been inside. Weeks: about nine. Copies finished at the close of this day: one. Copies unfinished: about eight. Instructions given: none. Paper supplied: none, and nobody has been supplied with any. Machines available in the buildings where the copies are being made: none that anybody has reported, and one woman has said that her building has none. How the first person was asked: on a telephone, on a Friday. How the second was asked: in a corridor. How about four of the nine came to be copying it: they were in a room and it was on a table. Whether the woman of twenty-four who made the page knows: no, and she has not been told and is not to be told. Whether anybody has asked her permission: no. Owner: nine people, and the page, and nobody has the other eight.**

*What the day did not touch.* **Nobody in that room asked the woman of twenty-four for permission to copy the page and she was not asked and does not know and is not to be told. A Continuity Office made no second request and wrote nothing down then or since. A table of about nine people with a list did not sit, the sheet with nine words on it from an earlier Wednesday was not raised, and no item was created. A man of about thirty-three who has held four buildings since the Thursday of week one hundred and twenty was not mentioned by anybody in any of the three rooms. A hardboard on two nails was not written on, a card in a rail did not move, and the master's fifth line is still blank. Nobody died. Nobody thanked anybody. Entry 358.**
