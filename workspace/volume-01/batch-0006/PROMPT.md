## The hard rules for this batch

- **Write no prose and no fiction.** This is a state document about a finished book.
- **Do not re-plan Volume 02.** No chapter cards, no scene lists, no new movements, no new characters with names. Volume 02's shape lives in `outline/series.md` and is not this batch's business.
- **Do not introduce a new enemy, a new canon rule, a new institution, a new post, or a new office.** If the close document notices that something is missing from the world — and it will — the correct sentence is *there is no such thing in this city*, which is a fact of Volume 01, not an invitation to design one.
- **Do not name the Crown Clause and do not let the close document identify the Marrow Falls signature's authorship.** It may record that the name is a name in a document and that nobody in the cast has said it aloud.
- **Do not repair the paperwork.** The manual is 3-08 and stays 3-08.
- **Do not reset any cost.** Tam's arm, the two tendons, the three months, the middle finger, the lost kitchen wages, the two voices, the transcript line, the strap envelope, the unwatched wrist, the count on the rail, and the unfiled request all stand.
- **Do not let the close document claim that Volume 01 solved anything.** It satisfied a requirement. It is a different verb and the distinction is the volume's last joke on itself.
- **Flag, do not fix, the two things a reviewer should look at**: Chapter 50's length (~11,400 words, 1.6× the manuscript's previous longest chapter) and the one date expression in the batch that uses a month, in Chapter 48, *it was the last week of September in the autumn*, which is consistent with the derived calendar but is the only such expression in the volume and may be normalised to a season word in a later pass.
