# Chapter 48 — Wednesday

"Because there are four boxes on that form and the second one is a person's name and I am not writing a first-year's name into a district form in a man's handwriting at seven in the morning without her being in the room," said Marek Senn. "I'll come to you."

"I can do that."

"You can do that in about twenty minutes," said Roisin Keogh, "and I will tell you why I am being like this. In September a man in a department handed us a form about a fit in a car park and it had a name on it that he had got from a counter, and he was being kind, and eleven weeks later that name was the first line of a review that took two working days and closed a building's open day for a season. I have never seen that man again and I have thought about him about once a fortnight for a year. So I am going to be like this about your pencil."
