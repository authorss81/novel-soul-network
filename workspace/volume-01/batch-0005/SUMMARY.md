## Relationship milestone 5, Chapter 50 — no romantic resolution

**Chosen proximity and separate workrooms.** What was actually built:

- **Separate workrooms**, argued as a professional in ninety seconds in a lay-by: the clinic and the shop are four miles apart and are going to stay four miles apart, and if they ever stop being four miles apart then every person with a plate on, and every person short of breath at four in the afternoon, and every wrist done for three years, ends up in one room with a nurse and a repairer in it and a door, **and the door will be the reason they come, and I have been the door for eight years and you have been the door since the Sunday of week thirteen, and if I stood in that room with you I would not be a nurse in it, I would be the reason the door was open, and every person who came through it would be finding us.**
- **Chosen proximity**: Thursdays, not this one, the one after; it moves without anybody writing down why; no form; nothing in either of their records; **and if anybody in this city ever asks either of us what that is, we say the same sentence, and the same sentence is: we had an hour.**
- **And the two refusals that keep it from being a resolution**: she will not be kissed in a lay-by at four in the afternoon on a Saturday in a term in which a boy of twenty-two lost six weeks of his wages on Wednesday, **because I would be doing it to end a day, and I have watched somebody do that to somebody else this term and I have watched what it cost, and I am not going to be the second one of us** — and she has thought about it, twice, at six in the morning, in a van, with a plate in a bag in a coat, and says so in one sentence so that he does not spend a fortnight on it. And she has two hundred and six pages in a folder in a drawer and has never given one away, and he is the second person in eight years who has been near that, **and neither of us is going to say what that is either.**
