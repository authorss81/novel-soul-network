## What the batch is actually about

**It turns a documented case into a place and then hands the work away, and the handing away does not work, and it does not work for the reason the case has been building since the dock.**

The place is the Lower Wards: a flood-prone ward with a lock-keeper's hut a hundred and forty years old, a hand-painted sign nobody was told to paint, an informal mesh of four to nine people, a school exercise book with a rubber band round it and **no names in it, kept for nine years**, and a district form that wants four headings in writing before the season — which is the same shape as a design approval and which would, if filled in, do to the ward what a September form did to a hallway in Nine Bridges. **The crews' poor records are used against them, and the mechanism is entirely ordinary:** nobody is hiding anything, there is simply nothing written down, and the only instrument that could find the fifty households is a list of ninety names in a department that has already locked a cage and told a journalist nothing. **Marek writes the records request out in ninety seconds and does not post it.** That is the batch's Rule Five test and it costs him the only instrument the case has ever had, a woman at a laundrette, and the fifty.

The place is also the answer to Vey's problem, and it is not an answer anybody wanted: a man who has been doing the job for twenty-nine years, with no card and no teacher, has a fourth beat of her own on a rail, counting to three on the way down off the last two. **The yard off the Meridian has a card, a horn, forty men and a training pack; the ward has a rail, a book and nineteen men. Both have the same gap. Neither is a conspiracy. The batch says this once, in Marek's own hand, and does not build a network out of it.**

**The second dependence is the batch's spine.** Nkechi Osei came off three about a fortnight before Marek first came up the hill. On the Monday of week 13, on his twelfth watch from a grass bank, she came off four. He made no sound and gave no cue; Corvin Ashby, thirty feet away with a tea flask, saw it. The chapter's whole argument is in one exchange:

> "She came off the last two about a fortnight before you got here. She was on three. She's on four."
> "I didn't do anything."
> "No. You did nothing. That's how it works."

**Nobody is cured, nobody is forgiven, and the dependence stays.** The handover goes to three people he does not manage and it does not work, and the batch proves *why* it does not work, which is the volume's real discovery: **the dependence was never the work.** A woman telephones a repair shop at six in the morning and gets nineteen minutes. A laundrette tells him he will have to keep coming and it is not a question. And the fourth person, the woman at the laundrette, cannot be handed to anybody, because the correct answer is a person whose job it is, and there is no such job in this city. **Rafi Pell names the half Marek cannot solve: you cannot hand it back, because there is nothing to resign from.** A key-holder by reputation, arrived at without a certificate, with no register on which the fact could be struck off.

## Relationship milestone 3, Chapters 33–35 — the narrow share

It is a rescue, and it is ninety seconds long, and Talia Venn writes the page herself, and it is smaller than anything Marek has ever proposed. **Seven headings: purpose, duration, what stays, who may stop it, who it is for, when it ends, how it ends.** The two that were missing in the first pass are the ones that matter most: the page ends on the day a person whose job that is looks at that arm and at the latest on the Friday of week 14, whichever comes first, and when it ends it is torn in half in front of him and neither half is kept. **Use only. Nothing stays. Three stop-words, by word, no reason asked and none recorded. He reads it twice and says *leave it*.** And she makes Marek come down the lane out of the eleven feet she told him to stand in and put his name on the back of it in pencil, because a line that gives a man the power to stop something has his name on it and not hers.

The rescue is a man of fifty-eight off a chain with a wrist unit from the September open day, where the contact has set in the shape of his own weight, so the cuff cannot be opened until the weight comes off the hand, and the weight comes off the hand when he lets go, and the chain holds a hundred and forty kilos of cast iron over nine feet of water. **The fourth beat is performed by the patient, to a witness, for the third time in this volume and the first time in a lane with no door.** It is his number and it is his choice.

**Intimacy advanced through professional competence and inconvenience, and not through a confession.** Marek is ninety-seven minutes late. Talia tells him, in front of nine people, to stand at the top of the lane and tells him what he is for. **He reads the outside of a cuff — the one thing he can do, because he is a repairer — says four words to a man (*it will not hold you*), and then stands on a bank for eleven minutes while somebody else does the difficult part.** He writes it up afterwards as *use only, nothing stays, it closed*, in a notebook he did not tear out. **She also puts the worst of it on the page herself, out loud, in front of nine people, and signs it: the ambulance is not rung for seventy minutes, the man was awake and asked her to sit with him, she decided at half past ten not to ring and did not ring until twenty to twelve, a crew rung then would have been on the lane by a quarter to eleven and made it two of them asking a man to let go of a chain, and — asked whether she would do it again — at half past ten, yes, but she would not sit on it for an hour and a quarter again.** That sentence is now the cost, in her own hand, and it is not in a book.

**It costs them both something specific.** Hers: a technique performed outside her route, on a patient who was not hers, on a day she was not working, with no care-link card in front of him, and a second page of a record that has to say so, and a district office that will read it in about three weeks; and the plate is in the second drawer of a trolley in her vehicle and is a piece of a wrist unit with no number on it and is not going back on anybody. His: her competence arrives in him and does not go away, and the best afternoon of his term so far is one he was sent away from. **She refuses three things** — to be thanked, to know whether he will file the request, and to let him near the second page — and she makes him say out loud that he did not arrange it, because if he had telephoned her she would have said no, and she came up the hill for a September list. **Nothing is softened. The Chapter 7 page stays closed. She is not a reward, a deputy, or a rescue from loneliness.**
