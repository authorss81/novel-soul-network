## Beat sheet (follow the shape; the prose is yours)

- **11.** The week begins with the date on the noticeboard and Marek trying to prepare something that can be prepared. A paid observation, or a second site visit, or a repetition attempt that costs him. Gus gets his promised visit and the truth, and Gus asks the question that has no good answer: who checked the plates before they shipped.
- **12.** The Institute withdraws the Thursday-of-week-7 assessment date. Not maliciously — a room booking, a board availability, a curriculum calendar. The withdrawal is competent, courteous, and final, and it costs Marek the only route he was given. He learns what the department's plan for him actually is.
- **13.** Talia's route. **Mal Farrow**, 61, bad hip, fish market. A care-link with a visible reason, a limited duration and a later review. His brace is a 4-19 variant and it catches during a fall. Marek asks Talia for a bounded, use-only share under Mal's visible emergency scope, with a written scope and a review date. The comparison lets them stop the pressure and return him to ordinary breathing. The share closes after the rescue. It leaves Marek with shared aftertaste, and Mal's own autonomy has to be respected at the moment it is least convenient.
- **14.** The four-site limited local quarantine: the Institute, both satellite sites, and the clinic. Each site acts on its own authority. Each may refuse the Continuity Office's recommendation later. The point of the chapter is that four people each saying a small no is a stronger act than one person saying a large one.
- **15–16.** Tam's non-hinged recovery plan starts to take shape from independent observations. It is slower, uglier, and much less impressive than the kit's version. A failed attempt. A bad night. A partial success that only works with Tam's own hand in it, after he gives fresh, explicit consent **for that named purpose** — and that fresh consent is not a replay of Chapter 6's revocation, which is closed.
- **17.** The three-source counterpoint fails. Three observers, all trained by one person. The interface, if it speaks at all, reports agreement. Rafi withdraws mid-practice, the line collapses, and Marek is left with a shared aftertaste and a hole where his evidence was. He learns that withdrawal is not betrayal and that false consensus is a failure of his own wanting, not of the network.
- **18.** The repair after the failure. Marek writes down what he actually knows and what he does not. He goes back to the people he has already harmed and asks them something smaller. Talia's scope holds, and holding it costs him something he wanted.
- **19.** Mini-climax. The Continuity Office's preliminary review declines to trace the licensor: the bundle is a commercial matter, the upstream field is blank, and an unnamed licensor is not a person a city can act against. The Institute offers the deal again, harder, with a deadline attached.
- **20.** Relationship milestone 2, and it is not affection. Rafi says he will slow his yes down. Marek realizes he has been doing the work of a parent he never asked to be. Movement II closes with the week-7 route closed, no replacement date, and the demonstration requirement hanging over him.
