## The spine of the hinge, deepened rather than repeated

Movement I established that the action under the printed boundary is missing. **Movement II added its physical signature.** A copper return contact that takes a load with no release performed folds over and sets hard in the shape of the load path, stays that way, and does it again next time. It is not a metaphor, it is a thing Talia holds in her own hand, it is what took two and a half minutes out of a man of sixty-one's breathing, and it is the one fact in the case that cannot be argued with. Rusk has seen it twice, once out of a fish crate in 2019, and there is a department stores file about it he has never read.

The chapter-level ritual is also unchanged and is stated three times in three registers: the **card** prints a boundary, the **booklet** calls the word a support beat and not a step, and the **load book** records what a person's hands actually did. The load book is now the batch's spine: it holds the Gate Farrow attribution, the honest ledger, the wax-paper memory, Tam's two voices, and the recognition, and the recognition is the only paragraph in the batch written in the middle of an ordinary repair job rather than in a room where something was being proved.
