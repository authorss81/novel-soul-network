# Chapter 989 — She Stood In Front Of It

**A Wednesday was a bench until two and a counter until six and then about nine minutes on foot to a room with nine chairs in it, where a woman stood up in front of about nine people and said what she would answer for, and about four people who were in that room will tell you that she said it in two goes and read nothing out.**

---

That Wednesday took nine callers through that shop and the ninth of them was let away near twenty-five past nine, and the shutter came down at ten.

**The four converted units off that service road stood at one thousand eight hundred and twenty-two days, two hundred and sixty weeks and two days. The request in the drawer in a second district was seventy-one days old on that Wednesday.**

---

**The book came down off the shelf at about half past six in that room and about nine people were in it, and about four of them had come through the door off that line without anything on them and had not been asked what they were for.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **that is the book. It is not open for you and it is not about you and nobody in this room is going to tell you what is in it.**

A man of about forty said: **then why is it here.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **because about four times a year it comes down off that shelf and I have never once known why and I am not going to start guessing in front of you.**

---

**And Talia was at that door and did not come in, and about four people who were in that room will tell you that she was outside it for the whole of the hour and that nobody asked her to be anywhere else.**

Talia said: **do you want me in here.**

Marek said: **no.**

Talia said: **that is allowed and you have about nine seconds to say why and you do not have to use them.**

Marek said: **because I am the only name on a piece of paper in this and if an office is standing in that room then the room is about the office.**

Talia said: **I am not standing in it.**

Marek said: **I know. I am saying what it would look like and I am saying it in about nine seconds and then I am going in and you are going to stay there.**

---

**And the woman of about sixty-two was at the back with her hands in her coat pockets, and the man of about thirty-three was not in that room at all, and both of those were known to about nine of the people there inside a minute.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **he is not coming.**

Marek said: he has told me why.

The woman of about fifty-four said: **and you let a room know that before it started.**

Marek said: **I did not. Four people in that shop heard it and one of them was me.**

---

**And the woman of about forty-three came in with three sheets of paper in a folder and put them face down on the table by the door, and nobody opened the folder.**

A woman of about fifty-one said: **is that the thing.**

The woman of about forty-three said: **three sheets and there is a mark on one of them and I am not turning them over.**

---

**And then Sera Quill stood up where she had been sitting at the side with her coat still on, and it took her about four seconds to get up, and nobody in the room helped her and nobody tried.**

Sera Quill said: **I am going to say two things and then I am going to sit down.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **sit or stand, it is the same room.**

Sera Quill said: **I am going to stand, because I have not stood up in a room in front of about nine people in four years and I would like there to be one.**

---

**And then she said the first of the two things, and she said it to the room and not to anybody in it.**

Sera Quill said: **I read a page on a Tuesday on my own kitchen table. I am the person who found it.**

---

**And about nine seconds passed in that room and about four people in it have said since that nobody said anything at all for the length of it and that a man at the back put his hand over his mouth.**

Marek said: **she is not going to say where.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **she has said she is the person who found it. That is four words and I am not going to ask her for a fifth.**

Marek said: **about four people in this room have been waiting four years for a second half.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **then they have waited four years for nothing and they can go on waiting, because that is not her sentence to give and it is not mine.**

---

**And then she said the second of the two things in one go, and she said it more slowly than the first and nobody in it breathed.**

Sera Quill said: **I will answer for having found it, and I will not read it out.**

---

**And nobody thanked her and nobody asked her to stay and about four people in that room will tell you that the first thing that happened after that sentence was nothing at all for about four minutes.**

The woman of about sixty-two said: **I heard it.**

Sera Quill said: **I am not asking you about anything.**

The woman of about sixty-two said: **I know that, and I am telling you that I heard you, and that is the whole of what I have come here for and I am going.**

---

**And the woman of about fifty-four said the thing that closed that Wednesday, and she said it to the folder by the door and not to anybody.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **that is it. That is what comes down off the shelf four times a year and about nine of you have been in this room waiting for it and I have been running it for six years without knowing.**

Marek said: **and the book.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **shut, and I will not say what it is beside, and there is a woman at that counter the next day who has been waiting about four years to be asked a question and this was not it.**

---

**One shop, one yard and two rooms took what was left of that Wednesday, and four jobs went into them.**

A socket in that yard had been put in on a plate with the earth taken off the conduit it sat on and the plate itself was the only earth there was.

He earthed it off the box, fitted a plate with an earth on it and proved it dead.

"**Ten pounds,**" she said. "**A plate that is its own earth is a plate that goes live the day the conduit is disturbed. That is four times over in that row in that row are, and one of them has been a wall somebody has been working on for four years.**"

A lamp in that shop had been put in on a rose that had been painted round with the ceiling, so that it could not be told from the ceiling.

He cut the paint back, made the edge clean and proved the fitting.

"**Eight pounds,**" he said. "**A rose painted into a ceiling is a lamp that is only findable by feel. The whole of that row is like it in that shop are, and one of them has been a ceiling somebody has had to feel for in four years.**"

A cooker in that second room had been put in with its flex through the plaster with no sleeve at all.

He cut back, sleeved the flex properly and proved it.

"**Nine pounds,**" she said. "**A flex through plaster with nothing in the hole is a flex somebody will cut one day with a screwdriver. Four of those in that street are, and one of them has been a kitchen somebody has been careful in since the year the roof was done.**"

A switch in that yard had been put in over a cold pipe with the plaster cut away around it.

He made the pipe safe, filled the hole properly and refitted the plate square.

"**Seven pounds,**" he said. "**A switch over a pipe is a switch somebody will cut into one day. Four of those plates in that block are, and one of them has been an outside wall somebody has been working at for the whole of the time he has been in trade.**"

**Those four came to thirty-four pounds on that Wednesday, exact.**

---

**That Wednesday the fourth of those four rooms was shut from half past six with a woman of about thirty behind that door, and the binder stayed where it has been all winter on the shelf at the back, and this page will not put a figure on the page that ring holds.**
