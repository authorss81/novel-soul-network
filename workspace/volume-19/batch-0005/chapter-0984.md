# Chapter 984 — She Named The Cost Before The Yes

**A Tuesday was a bench until two and a counter until six and then four converted units off that service road from about half past six, and a man asked a woman at that counter to be the one a thing stands in front of, and about four people in that shop have said since that she told him the price of it before she said yes.**

---

That Tuesday took eight callers through that shop and the eighth of them was let away about ten past nine, and the shutter came down at ten.

**The four converted units off that service road stood at one thousand eight hundred and fourteen days, two hundred and fifty-nine weeks and one day. The request in the drawer in a second district was sixty-three days old on that Tuesday.**

---

**She came in at about ten past six with her coat already open and sat down on the bench by the door, which she has never done, and about four people in that shop will tell you that she had been outside that door for about a quarter of an hour before she came in.**

Marek said: it is a Tuesday.

Sera Quill said: **it is a Tuesday and I know what day it is, and I have not been in here on the Monday and I did not say on the Monday that I would.**

Marek said: you said in this shop on a Monday that you would say it on the Monday after that one.

Sera Quill said: **I said I would say it on the Monday after that one. I have not been in this building on a Monday since and that is the first half of what I have to say about it.**

---

Marek said: a Monday went by.

Sera Quill said: **a Monday went by and there was nobody in here but you and about four people buying lamps, and that is what a Monday is.**

Marek said: I did not ask you on it and nobody in this building did either.

Sera Quill said: **no. You kept that, and you kept it in a shop where it would have been easy and about four hundred times cheaper for you not to.**

---

**And then he asked her, and he had it ready, and about four people in that shop will say that he got it out in about four seconds and that his hand went flat on that counter while he said it and that his hand was not doing what he asked of it.**

Marek said: will you be the person it stands in front of.

---

**And about nine seconds went past in that shop with the shutter half up and nobody in it saying anything at all, and about four people who have been in there have it that she did not answer it and that she looked straight at him the whole time and did not once look away.**

Sera Quill said: **four months of a life that is not mine, asked about daily.**

Marek said: that is the cost.

Sera Quill said: **that is one of the costs. You asked me a question and I am going to answer it in the order I have got them in, and the cost comes first because you will hear the yes and forget the rest of it.**

---

**And then she said the rest of it, and about four people in that shop have said since that she said it to the counter and not to him and that she took about nine seconds over the whole of it.**

Sera Quill said: **I will be asked how I knew, and I will not answer that, and if I do not answer it then nobody in this city can hold anything against me for knowing.**

Marek said: you do not know what you will be asked.

Sera Quill said: **I have had about four months to work out what I will be asked and it is one question and it is how I knew.**

Marek said: and if somebody asks it in front of the nine.

Sera Quill said: **then I will not answer it in front of them either, and I will have said a great deal for nothing.**

---

Marek said: say the yes.

Sera Quill said: **yes, on the Wednesday, and not reading it out.**

**And nothing at all happened in that shop after that sentence, and about four people who have been in it agree that the till went on and that two callers came through the door in about four minutes and were served and that neither of the two people at the counter moved.**

---

**And then Marek asked the question that was underneath the other one, and about four people in that shop have said since that he asked it in a different voice and that he knew the answer before he had finished it.**

Marek said: it would have to be a room you can walk into.

Sera Quill said: **it would have to be a room with about nine people in it, and there is exactly one of those in this city that nobody has to be invited to.**

Marek said: the woman who keeps that room will not put a notice up and has never put one up.

Sera Quill said: **then nobody is invited and everybody comes, and I will be in it if I am let in and I will not be let in by a list.**

---

Marek said: one thing before I move.

Sera Quill said: **you have been standing like that for about nine seconds. Say it or do not.**

Marek said: **the woman of about forty-five has already refused once this week to be a second voice, and if you stand in that room the only true answer to how you knew puts her in it with you, and you have not asked her, and she said no on a Saturday about three days ago.**

Sera Quill said: **no. And she can stand up in front of about nine people on the day and refuse again, and I will stand there anyway and I will not say a word about her.**

Marek said: and if she refuses it on the day, about nine of us will be in that room anyway.

Sera Quill said: **then I will have been in a room on my own account and that is a thing I can live with, and it is not a thing I can arrange in advance, and that is the second cost and you may have it now.**

---

**Nobody thanked anybody on that Tuesday and nobody forgave anybody, and she sat on that bench until about seven and then went out into a street that had been dark for about two hours, and Marek has said since that he did not walk to the door with her and that she had not once looked at the door on the way out.**

---

**Two rooms and one yard took what was left of that Tuesday, and four jobs went into them.**

A cooker in that first room, about nine feet from the door, had its earth taken off the appliance and put on the gas pipe, so that the pipe was the appliance's only earth.

He took the earth off the pipe and put it on the appliance and proved it dead with the isolator off.

"**Twelve pounds,**" she said. "**A gas pipe is not an earth, whatever anybody in that trade says to you. That is four times over in that row cookers in that street are like it, and one of them has been a kitchen somebody has wiped round for four years.**"

A light in that second room, about nine feet above the floor, had been put in on a rose with two holes in the back of it and a third hole made by the man who fitted it.

He turned the rose out, made the third hole the same as the other two and proved the fitting.

"**Six pounds,**" he said. "**A rose with a hole in it that is not a fixing is somebody's idea of a fixing. The whole of that row is like it roses in that row have one, and one of them has been a ceiling somebody has looked up at for four years.**"

A socket in that yard had been put in on an old round box that had no back on it and had been painted shut.

He took the paint off, put a proper blank on and proved the plate.

"**Five pounds,**" she said. "**A box painted shut is a box somebody wanted to stop looking at. Four of those backs in that row are painted, and one of them is an outside wall somebody has had a ladder against since the year the roof was done.**"

A consumer unit in that second room, about nine feet up a wall, had been mounted with its lid off and two of its ways left spare.

He fitted the lid, marked the spare ways and proved the whole board dead with the mains off.

"**Six pounds,**" he said. "**An open board with spare ways is a board with a plan for somebody else's fault. Four of those units in that block are like it, and one of them has been a hallway somebody has walked past for the whole of the time he has been in trade.**"

**The four of them came to twenty-nine pounds on that Tuesday, exact.**

---

**That Tuesday the fourth of those four rooms was shut from half past six with a woman of about thirty behind that door, and the binder stayed at the back of it on the shelf it has been on all winter, and this page is not going to say what is in the ring on that shelf.**
