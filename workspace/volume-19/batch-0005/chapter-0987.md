# Chapter 987 — The Tray Came Open On Its Own

**A Monday was a bench until two and a counter until six and then four converted units off that service road, and a woman who made a form with five columns in it opened a drawer she had shut herself thirty days earlier, on the last day of the month she had named.**

---

That Monday took seven callers through that shop and the last of them was let away about ten to nine, and the shutter came down at ten.

**The four converted units off that service road stood at one thousand eight hundred and twenty days, two hundred and sixty weeks to the day. The printed form up on a wall in a passage in a fourth district was ninety days old that Monday and had not been straightened once.**

---

**She came in at about eight with a folder under her arm and put it on that counter, and about four people in that building will tell you that she had carried it from her own desk about nine floors away and not out of the building's own tray.**

Marek said: you opened it.

The woman of about forty-three said: **I opened it on the thirtieth day of the month I said, and I have said that in front of about six people this morning and none of them asked me why.**

Marek said: **two days.**

The woman of about forty-three said: **two days before a Wednesday that nobody has told me about. I told a queue of six people on a Monday that I would open it when somebody stood in front of it, and a month is thirty days and today is the thirtieth, and I said a month so that a person could write the word down instead of counting.**

---

Marek said: nobody has stood in front of it as far as you know and as far as I know.

The woman of about forty-three said: **somebody said yes in about nine words in a shop on the Tuesday, I have not been told and I have not asked, and I have opened a drawer on the thirtieth day because I am sixty-one and a drawer is a thing you can do at sixty-one.**

Marek said: you are saying somebody is going to and about four of us have been waiting for it.

The woman of about forty-three said: **I am saying I have stopped being able to hold a thing shut against a date that has stopped meaning anything, and that is not the same and it is not better.**

---

**And then she opened the folder on that counter and about four people in that building will say that she did it slowly and that she did not take the sheets out and that she turned the third one round and put it on top.**

Marek said: there are three of them.

The woman of about forty-three said: **there are three of them and one of them was filled in and I put it in there myself, and I have told a queue that I did.**

Marek said: why.

The woman of about forty-three said: **because a tray with three sheets in it has a person in it, and a tray with one sheet in it has a person in it, and I had a month to sit with three and I could not.**

---

**And then she said the thing about the sixth column, and about four people in that building have it that she said it to the folder and not to him and that her hands stayed exactly where they were for about a minute and a half.**

The woman of about forty-three said: **there is something written in the sixth column on that sheet.**

Marek said: rows.

The woman of about forty-three said: **every row on it. And it is not a name.**

Marek said: what is it, and about nine people in that building will want to know by Friday.

The woman of about forty-three said: **it is a mark. Somebody has put the same mark against every row of a form I made with five columns in it, and there is no sixth column in this city that anybody in this building has been asked to head.**

---

**Neither of them said anything for long enough that the queue behind them went on the whole time, and about four people in that building have said since that about nine of them did not notice.**

Marek said: do you know who made that mark.

The woman of about forty-three said: **no.**

Marek said: you have read it twice.

The woman of about forty-three said: **twice. Once in a drawer and once on a counter with six people standing behind me, and about four of them heard the word rows and about nine of them will remember it.**

Marek said: that was not careful and I did not ask you to be careful.

The woman of about forty-three said: **no. It was thirty days of a drawer that I shut myself and told a queue of six people about, and care was the thing I ran out of, and I am telling you which one I ran out of.**

---

Marek said: will you say who filled it in.

The woman of about forty-three said: **no, and if you ask me again I will say it was nobody, and I will say it in front of the same people.**

Marek said: you will not do that.

The woman of about forty-three said: **I have said worse in front of them this month and I have not been asked to leave this room.**

---

**And then she said what she was going to do with the three sheets, and about four people in that building agree that she said it standing up and that she had put the folder down before she said a word of it.**

The woman of about forty-three said: **these three go into that room on the Wednesday. Not into a drawer in this building and not into anybody's coat.**

Marek said: a room off a line.

The woman of about forty-three said: **a room off a line that anybody may walk into, and I would rather three sheets of paper went somewhere nobody can lock than stayed in a drawer that only I can.**

Marek said: you have never asked that room for anything.

The woman of about forty-three said: **I have never asked that room for anything because I have never had anything that was not mine to keep.**

---

**Nobody thanked that woman on that Monday and nobody forgave anybody, and she carried the folder back out under her arm herself, and it went with her and it did not stay on that counter, and about four people in that building have said since that nobody saw the inside of it again, and that about four of them have wanted to.**

---

**Two shops and one passage took what was left of that Monday, and four jobs went into them.**

A meter in that first shop, about nine feet in from the door, had a tail that had been made up with a joint outside the enclosure.

He cut it back, terminated it properly and sealed the enclosure where it had been opened.

"**Seven pounds,**" she said. "**A meter with a joint outside its own box is a meter anybody can reach. All four of those are the same job. Tails in that row are like it, and one of them has been a cupboard somebody has been careful about for four years.**"

A light in that passage had been put in on a junction box mounted upside down, so that the lid was on the floor side.

He turned it upright, moved the cables out of the way and proved it.

"**Six pounds,**" he said. "**A junction box upside down is a lid that cannot be fixed. Not one of those is unusual. Boxes in that block are upside down, and one of them has been a passage somebody has put a ladder up in the dark for four years.**"

A socket in that second shop had been put in on a plate held by two screws, one of which went into a plasterboard joint and turned in the hand.

He moved the fixings into solid and proved the plate.

"**Six pounds,**" she said. "**A screw into a plasterboard joint is a screw that comes out with the plate. Four of those plates in that row are fixed like that, and one of them has been a wall somebody has been hanging things off and it has never once been looked at.**"

A bell in that passage had been left disconnected at the far end when somebody rewired the landing.

He found the break, joined it back and proved the bell from the button.

"**Six pounds,**" he said. "**A bell that was disconnected by accident is a bell that everybody reports as broken. Four of those bells in that row are cut off at the back, and one of them has been a landing somebody has stopped answering for four winters and one summer.**"

**Those four came to twenty-five pounds on that Monday, exact.**

---

**That Monday the fourth of those four rooms was shut from half past six with a woman of about thirty behind that door, and the binder stayed on the shelf at the back, and this page makes no offer of a figure for what is in it.**
