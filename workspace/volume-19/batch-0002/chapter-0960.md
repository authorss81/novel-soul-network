# Chapter 960 — The Holding Arranged

**A Tuesday was a bench until two and a counter until six and then a warm room with three people in it, and the holding was arranged without a page being opened, and Marek was told he will never know and said he knows.**

---

Nine callers came and went through that shop on that Tuesday, the ninth near twenty-five past nine, and the shutter came to at ten.

**The four converted units off that service road stood at one thousand seven hundred and seventy-two days, two hundred and fifty-three weeks and one day. The request in the drawer in a second district was twenty-one days old on that Tuesday.**

---

**Sera Quill came at half past seven with a dated slip in her hand, and about four people in that room have said since that the slip was the first new paper in this matter since the request, and that everybody looked at it and nobody touched it.**

She said: he has given me a day. Nine days from the Monday.

Marek said: which day.

She said: I am not telling you the day.

Marek said: then what are you telling me.

She said: that there is a day, that I go alone, that she stands outside, that the book comes home the same day by my hand.

The woman of about forty-five said: and after.

Sera Quill said: after, the two of us hold it, and nobody else is told until we decide what holding means.

**Nobody in that room said anything for about nine seconds, and about four people in that room have said since that Marek looked at the slip without reaching for it, and that this not reaching was the thing he had practised.**

Marek said: and if you decide it means nothing can be done.

Sera Quill said: then nothing is done, and you carry a signature for nothing.

Marek said: I signed for that too.

She said: you did. That is why the room trusts the signature.

---

**And then the woman of about forty-five said the thing she had come to say, and she said it to Marek and not to Sera Quill, and about four people in that room have said since that this was the first time she had spoken to him directly.**

She said: I am holding it with her and I am frightened.

Marek said: of what.

She said: of knowing nine things I cannot write, and being asked for the rest of my time.

Marek said: then do not hold it.

She said: I have said I will. Frightened is not unwilling. I wanted you to hear it from me.

Marek said: I have heard it.

**He has said since that this was the hardest minute of the ten days, harder than the corridor and harder than the sitting, because there was nothing in it he could fix and nothing he was allowed to carry.**

---

**And a man of about fifty-two put his head round the door at eight with a pencil behind his ear, and about four people in that room have said since that he had been waiting on the stair to catch the end, and that what he said next was not the end but the beginning of the next trouble.**

He said: somebody wants a sixth column.

Sera Quill said: headed what.

He said: told. Somebody to fill it in when the nine are told.

Marek said: who fills it.

He said: that is the part nobody has answered. That is why I am telling you and not writing it.

Sera Quill said: rub it out if it is in pencil.

He said: it is in pencil in a margin, and I have not rubbed it because it is not my margin.

**Nobody in that room laughed, and about four people in that room have said since that the pencil in the margin stayed where it was that night, and that about four people know whose hand it was and none of them will say.**

---

**The rest of that Tuesday went into a shop and a flat above it for about two hours, and four jobs went into them.**

A sign supply in that shop had been wired with no switch at all, so that it burned whenever the board was on.

He put a switch on the supply and proved the sign off with the shop lit.

"**Nine pounds,**" he said. "**A sign with no switch is a tube nobody can put out. Four of those signs in that row burn day and night, and one of them has been a window lit till morning for four years.**"

A smoke head in that flat had been painted over at the last decorating, so that it could sense nothing.

She changed the head and proved it with the test button.

"**Twelve pounds,**" she said. "**A head painted over is a ceiling that cannot smell. Four of those heads in that block are painted shut, and one of them has been a landing with no warning for four years.**"

A socket in that shop had been wired with its screws barely caught, so that the face rocked.

He re-terminated the wires and proved the face solid.

"**Seven pounds,**" he said. "**A face barely caught is a counter with a loose point. Four of those sockets in that row rock, and one of them has been a till somebody has held steady-handed for four years.**"

A bath in that flat had its bonding missing, so that the pipes stood unconnected.

She bonded the pipes and proved the bond continuous.

"**Ten pounds,**" she said. "**A bath with no bond is a room nobody should stand wet in. Four of those baths in that block stand unbonded, and one of them has been a bathroom somebody has washed in quick for four years.**"

**Thirty-eight pounds is what those four came to on that Tuesday, exact.**

---

**That Tuesday the fourth of those four rooms sat shut at half past six with a woman of about thirty behind that door, and the binder stayed put on the back shelf, and this page offers no figure for the page it keeps.**

---

*Conditions of the close.* **Upstairs the green-covered book stands where the sitting left it, shut, and the tin beside it stands where Volume 18 left it, and this page prints neither of them and would not know what to do with the difference.
That ninth chair has its back to the whole of that room and it did not move on that Tuesday, and no page of this stretch of days says who last moved it. The empty place behind that chair was not named on that Tuesday and a figure for it appears on no page of this movement.
There is a room under a building in a first district and at about eleven on that Tuesday there was nothing burning in it, and nothing is burning in it at this hour either, and it is unlit and has been since it was made.
A figure of four stands against the register of correct acts that changed nothing at both the start of that Tuesday and the end of it, and nobody in this city writes that figure anywhere. A frightened holder who is not unwilling is not on it.**

*What the day did not settle, and the rest of it.* **The passage under this one names ten objects and no two are ever named in one breath. The rail with two places in it. A doorway held open by a card creased once. The board on two nails. Nine hand copies of the front of one page. A tin whose lid is down. The shutter. A book in a green binding. The binder standing on a shelf at the back. A dated slip nobody touched. A pencil in a margin nobody rubbed out.
The cage down one side of a counter stands outside all ten, and so does a request in a drawer in a second district with one name crossed off it and one name under it. A page of nine names is none of the ten and is not counted here and this page does not print it.
Frightened is not unwilling, and a day unnamed is not a day unknown, and a column in pencil is not a column.
A dated rule stands over a question that is not this one, and the records behind it are public and disputed, and any person who comes and asks may read them.
Nobody thanked anybody and nobody forgave anybody on that Tuesday. A holding was arranged without a page being opened, and a frightened woman spoke to the man it excludes, and a pencil waits in a margin for somebody to answer who fills it.**

**AND THE SIX THINGS A LATER PASS MUST CARRY FORWARD FROM THESE TEN.** **A records room in a second district is a landlord and a filing system, and its holder has shown one line where a copy went out and did not come back.** Sera Quill has a room with one chair and a shelf with nothing worth taking, and a day nine days from the Monday that she will not name. **A woman of about forty-five has said yes to holding without reading and has said to Marek's face that she is frightened and not unwilling.** Talia opened a file on Marek in a corridor about the request he signed and closed it, and he answered and was permitted to answer. **The sixty-ninth sitting shut the book without reading and promised the room on a day of her choosing with the second person outside the door.** Somebody wants a sixth column headed told in pencil in a margin, and about four people know whose hand it was and none will say. **The register stands at four with no fifth printed, the binder has not come down, the ninth chair did not move, and the room under the building in a first district was dark at about eleven on all ten days.**
