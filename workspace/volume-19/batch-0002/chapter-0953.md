# Chapter 953 — What The Signature Cost Him

**A Wednesday was a bench until two and a counter until six and then a corridor in a second district, and Talia opened a file on Marek about the request he had signed, and he was permitted to answer and he answered.**

---

On that Wednesday nine callers came into that shop, the ninth at twenty-five past nine, and the shutter closed at ten.

**The four converted units off that service road stood at one thousand seven hundred and fifty-nine days, two hundred and fifty-one weeks and two days. The request in the drawer in a second district was eight days old on that Wednesday.**

---

**She did it the way her office does it, standing and not sitting, with her back to her own door, and about four people in that corridor have said since that they had never seen her do it and that they knew what it was by the stillness.**

She said: I am opening this because you signed something.

Marek said: I know.

She said: it is about the request and not about the nine. Say if you understand that.

Marek said: I understand it.

**Nobody in that corridor said anything for about nine seconds, and Talia has said since that she let the quiet stand because the office requires it and not because she wanted it.**

She said: why did you sign it in your own name.

Marek said: because nobody else could sign it without making it theirs.

She said: whose did you make it then.

Marek said: mine. That is the cost and I knew it on the day.

She said: and what did you buy with it.

**He has said since that this was the question he had been afraid of for eight days, and that he had hoped she would ask it in a room and that she asked it in a corridor instead, and that the corridor made it harder.**

Marek said: time. About nine days for somebody else to decide.

She said: and if she does not decide.

Marek said: then I carry a name on paper for nothing, and that is also mine.

---

**And then she read it back to him, in her flat office voice, and he stood with his hands at his sides and did not interrupt.**

She said: you asked for a page about nine people you cannot name, and you were told you will never be told who they are, and you signed that condition in your own name.

Marek said: yes.

She said: you have not asked me to do it, and you have not asked Sera Quill about it since the Thursday, and you have not been back to that room.

Marek said: no.

She said: that is all I have. I am closing this file and you may go.

**About four people in that corridor have said since that he did not go, and that he stood a moment after she had closed the file, and that she waited with him and did not tell him to go twice.**

Marek said: did I do the thing your office would have told me not to do.

She said: my office does not tell people what to do. It tells them what they did.

Marek said: then what did I do.

She said: you put yourself on paper to keep a stranger off it. That is all this file says.

---

**The rest of that Wednesday went into a shop and a yard for about two hours, and four jobs went into them.**

A shutter motor in that shop had been wired with no limit stops set, so that it drove hard into both ends.

He set both stops and proved the shutter up and down three times.

"**Thirteen pounds,**" he said. "**A motor with no stops is a shutter driven into its ends. Four of those motors in that row are unset, and one of them has been a shop shut by hand for four years.**"

A floodlight in that yard had been aimed straight into a window across the way, so that it lit a bedroom all night.

She re-aimed it down at the yard and proved the window dark.

"**Nine pounds,**" she said. "**A light in a window is a night with no dark in it. Four of those lights in that yard are aimed wrong, and one of them has been a bedroom lit all night for four years.**"

A socket in that shop had been left with the back box proud of the plaster, so that the face stood off the wall.

He re-seated the box and proved the face flat.

"**Six pounds,**" he said. "**A box proud of the wall is a face held by air. Four of those boxes in that row stand proud, and one of them has been a counter somebody has knocked for four years.**"

A cable in that yard had been clipped too tight round a corner, so that the sheath showed white at the bend.

She re-clipped the run with slack at the corner and proved the sheath whole.

"**Seven pounds,**" she said. "**A clip too tight is a sheath cut by its own fixing. Four of those runs in that yard are clipped tight, and one of them has been a run somebody has watched whiten for four years.**"

**Thirty-five pounds is what those four came to on that Wednesday, exact.**

---

**That Wednesday the fourth of those four rooms stood shut at half past six with a woman of about thirty behind that door, and the binder remained where it stands at the back, and no number for the page in it appears on this page.**

*956.
Wednesday of week 319, at ten. That is the one hundred and ninety-eighth day of this stretch of days. That Wednesday brought nine callers into that shop and put nine names onto that day's book, the last of them at about twenty-five past nine.

Talia opened a file on Marek in a corridor about the request he signed and not about the nine, asked him why he signed it in his own name, read his answers back to him, and closed the file. *Conditions of the close.* **Upstairs the green-covered book stands where Volume 18 left it and the tin beside it stands where Volume 18 left it, and this page prints neither of them and would not know what to do with the difference.
That ninth chair has its back to the whole of that room and it did not move on that Wednesday, and no page of this stretch of days says who last moved it. The empty place behind that chair was not named on that Wednesday and a figure for it appears on no page of this movement.
There is a room under a building in a first district and at about eleven on that Wednesday there was nothing burning in it, and nothing is burning in it at this hour either, and it is unlit and has been since it was made.
A figure of four stands against the register of correct acts that changed nothing at both the start of that Wednesday and the end of it, and nobody in this city writes that figure anywhere. A file opened and closed in a corridor is not on it.**

*What the day did not settle, and the rest of it.* **The passage under this one names ten objects and no two are ever named in one breath. The rail with two places in it. A doorway held open by a card creased once. The board on two nails. Nine hand copies of the front of one page. A tin whose lid is down. The shutter. A book in a green binding. The binder standing on a shelf at the back. A corridor with a door at each end. A clock that ticks loud enough to count by.
The cage down one side of a counter stands outside all ten, and so does a request in a drawer in a second district with one name crossed off it and one name under it. A page of nine names is none of the ten and is not counted here and this page does not print it.
A file about a signature is not a file about nine people, and a corridor is not a room, and neither of those was said out loud on the day.
A dated rule stands over a question that is not this one, and the records behind it are public and disputed, and any person who comes and asks may read them.
Nobody thanked anybody and nobody forgave anybody on that Wednesday. A woman asked a man why he signed in his own name, and he said to buy time for somebody else, and she closed the file and he stood a moment after it.**
