# Chapter 999 — She Stood At The Side Of A Full Room

**A Friday was a bench until noon and a counter until four and then about nine minutes on foot to a first floor off a line in Saltmarket, where about sixteen people were in a room that holds nine chairs, and a woman stood at the side of it with her coat on and told all of them where it was, and read nothing out.**

---

That Friday took eight callers through that shop and eight names were written against the day before they went, and the shutter came down at ten.

**The four converted units off that service road stood at one thousand eight hundred and forty-five days, two hundred and sixty-three weeks and four days. The request in that second-district drawer was ninety-four days old that Friday and there was a second hundred and fifty sheets of a door somewhere in that city, printed by a building in a second district and paid for out of a shop off a service road, and neither of those two facts is on the sheet.**

---

**Nobody had been told about that Friday except the woman who keeps the room, and about four people who sit in that room have said since that she put a bench along one wall on the Thursday evening and that nobody asked her where it had come from.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **there are about sixteen of you.**

A man of about thirty said: **there are about sixteen of us.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **and there are nine chairs and one of those chairs has been under something since the winter before last.**

Marek said: **you have taken it out.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **it is in the passage. It is not coming back in and I am not putting a box on it and I am not asking you to help.**

---

**And then Sera Quill said that she was going to do it, and she said it from the side with her coat on, and about four people in that room have said since that she did not step into the middle and was not asked to.**

Sera Quill said: **I am going to stand where I am and say where this room is.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **anybody may walk in. There is nothing to be said.**

Sera Quill said: **that is what I am going to say and I am going to say it out loud in a room with about sixteen people in it.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **go on, then.**

Sera Quill said: **I am not going to read anything out and I have said that in this room before and I am saying it again so that nobody in here spends the evening hoping I will.**

---

**And then she said it, and it took her about half a minute, and about four people in that room have said since that nobody moved while she said it and that a man of about thirty put his hand over his mouth at the end of it.**

Sera Quill said: **there is a room off a line in Saltmarket and anybody may walk into it, and I found the thing that room is about, and nobody in here is going to be told what it is, and nobody in here is going to be told whether it is theirs, and I will not be told either.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **that is the whole of it.**

Sera Quill said: **that is the whole of it and I have stood at the side of this room for about six weeks wanting somebody to say one word more, and nobody has, and I have stopped wanting it.**

---

**And then she named what it would cost her, and she named it in about ten words and about four people in that room have said since that nobody said she did not have to.**

Sera Quill said: **they will look at me and I cannot stop it.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **yes.**

Sera Quill said: **and I will not know what to do when they do.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **no. You will stand at the side and you will not read anything out and that will be the whole of what you do, and about nine of them will not come back and about four will.**

Sera Quill said: **I know the arithmetic. I have done it about nine times this week.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **then you have done it and not me, which is worse, because it is yours and not this room's.**

---

**And the woman of about sixty-two was at the back with her hands in her coat pockets and she spoke once, and about four people in that room have said that she had not spoken in that room in about four weeks and that the whole of it turned on what she said.**

The woman of about sixty-two said: **I will come.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **you will do no such thing.**

The woman of about sixty-two said: **I have not got in the way of anything in six years and I have not been in the way of this.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **you have not been in the way of this because you have not come.**

The woman of about sixty-two said: **I will come and I will not stand up this time. I stood up about four weeks ago and it cost me something I did not tell anybody about and I am not doing it again.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **sit or stand. It is the same room.**

The woman of about sixty-two said: **it was not the same room the last time.**

---

**And a man of about thirty-four asked the question that about four people in that room had come to ask, and he asked it to Sera Quill and not to the woman of that room, and she answered him in one short sentence.**

A man of about thirty-four said: **what do I do when I get there.**

Sera Quill said: **you walk in and you stand there.**

A man of about thirty-four said: **and then what.**

Sera Quill said: **and then she will tell you it is not hers, or she will say she does not know, and either of those is an answer and I am not going to give you a better one.**

A man of about thirty-four said: **can I ask whether it is about me.**

Sera Quill said: **you can ask. You will not get it. I have asked twice.**

---

**And Marek noticed the space behind the woman of about fifty-four's chair while that was going on, and it had nothing standing in it and there was a mark on the wall where something had stood on it, and he asked about it once and was told about it once.**

Marek said: **there is a mark on your wall behind you.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **there is.**

Marek said: **something was on that space.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **there has been nothing on that space for the whole six years I have had this room and before that it was not mine and I have never asked.**

Marek said: **you have never asked.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **I am not going to ask now either, and you are not going to ask me a second time about it in front of about sixteen people.**

---

**Nobody thanked anybody on that Friday and nobody forgave anybody, and about sixteen people came out of that room at about half past seven and two of them went at the same time as a woman who had not stood up, and the sheet with an empty fifth column was on that table for its fifty-seventh night.**

---

**Four jobs went into that room, that yard and that shop on that Friday, about two hours of the day.**

A light fitting in that passage had its chain earthed to a water pipe running behind the plaster.

He took the earth off the pipe, ran it back to the fitting and proved it dead.

"**Eleven pounds,**" he said. "**A chain earthed to a pipe is a fitting that is on a water main. Fittings in that passage are like it, and one of them has been a passage somebody has put a hand on for four years.**"

A pendant in that room had been hung over a bench where people worked with their heads down.

He took it up, shortened the flex and hung it clear of the working height.

"**Ten pounds,**" she said. "**A lamp over a bench is a lamp in somebody's eyes all day. Lamps in that room hang low, and one of them has been a bench somebody has had a cap on for about two years.**"

A socket in that kitchen had been put in on an old round-pin pattern and then rewired behind an adaptor with tape.

He took the adaptor out, made a proper socket to the pattern and proved it.

"**Nine pounds,**" she said. "**Tape behind an adaptor is somebody's hands at about eleven at night. Sockets in that street are like that, and one of them has been a kitchen a woman has been proud of for four years.**"

A boiler in that room had been left with its case open over the floor of a cupboard.

He closed the case, put the clips back and proved it with the boiler hot.

"**Ten pounds,**" she said. "**A boiler case open on a cupboard floor is a cupboard nobody can put anything in. Cases in that block are like it, and one of them has been a cupboard somebody has been standing in front of for about a winter.**"

**Forty pounds is the sum of that Friday's four, exact.**

---

**At half past six on that Friday the fourth of those four rooms was shut, and a woman of about thirty was behind that door, and the ring binder was still on the shelf at the back of it, and this page puts no figure on the page it keeps.**
