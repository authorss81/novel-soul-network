# Chapter 944 — The Notice He Would Not Take Down

**A Thursday was a bench until two and a counter until about six and then a warm room with about nine people in it, and a woman wanted something taken off a wall in a fourth district, and a man who put it there said no and gave her a reason out of nine years that nobody in that room was able to argue with, and that was the worst part of the evening.**

---

Nine people came into that shop on that Thursday and the ninth of them was in before half past nine, and that shutter came down at about ten.

**The four converted units off that service road stood at one thousand seven hundred and forty-six days, two hundred and forty-nine weeks and three days. The sixteenth of those lines on that board, which has never once been written on, stood at one thousand five hundred and thirty-six days, two hundred and nineteen weeks and three days.**

---

**She brought it up at ten past six and she brought it up to Marek and not to the man of about thirty-three, and she had it in her hand as a piece of work to be got through, and she had been holding it since about four.**

She said: I want that thing off that wall.

She said: it is a date. Nine people have never been asked about it. It is going by two drawing pins in a passage in a fourth district and about nine people walk past it every day and every one of them reads it and not one of them can find out whether it is about them.

**She said that in a run of about nine sentences without stopping, and she has said since that she had it ready in four sentences and she could not get back to four, and that this is what happened to her four sentences.**

He said: who put it up.

She said: the man from the clinic.

---

**The man of about thirty-eight came up those stairs on that Thursday because he is in that room on a Thursday and always has been, and he heard the last of it standing at the bottom of the stair, and he said his name and the floor of the clinic where he keeps it and neither of those was asked for.**

He said: I put it up. I carried it out of that room on the Tuesday before last and I put it up myself and I did it in about twenty minutes and I did not ask anybody.

He said: and I am not taking it down.

**Nobody in that room said anything. About four people in that room have said since that they were surprised by how calm he was and that they have thought about that since.**

---

**Then he told them why, and it took him about nine sentences and he told it flat and without any heat in it at all, and it is the reason this volume has a shape.**

He said: I have kept that clinic for nine years and in that clinic people are told what day they are coming in and they are not asked whether that suits them, and they are told it a week before and sometimes the day before, and they come.

He said: there are about four people who have not come and I know which four and I have never asked any of them, and I have worked out over nine years that the ones who do not come are not the ones who did not mind.

**He said the next part to Marek and not to the woman of about forty-three, and about four people in that room have said since that he did that on purpose.**

He said: when you take a notice down, the only people who see it are the ones who were already looking. I have watched that happen on a wall about nine times.

---

**And Marek said the thing that made the evening go the way it went, and he has gone back over it a great deal since.**

He said: that is correct.

**Nobody in that room said anything to that either. He said it twice, the second time slower, and about four people in that room have said since that he did not seem to have understood that he was making it worse each time he said it, and that he had, and that he went on saying it.**

He said: that is correct and I am not going to argue with it and it is the reason I cannot give you what you came in here for.

**She said: I did not come in here for that.**

He said: you did.

---

**And then she asked him what the notice is for, and he opened his mouth, and nothing came out of it for about four seconds, and then he said the true answer and the true answer was a sentence about himself.**

He said: I do not know. I have looked at it every day for the two weeks it has been up and I have not been able to say what it is asking anybody to do.

**Nobody in that room said one word back to him about that, and the man of about thirty-eight said one word, which was no, and it was not said at him.**

The woman of about forty-three said: it is not asking anybody to do anything. That is what is wrong with it.

He said: I know.

**And that is the last thing anybody said in that room on that Thursday about the notice, and about four people in that room have said since that it was the only sentence anybody said that night that made the thing smaller instead of larger.**

---

**The rest of that Thursday went into two stairwells and a yard for about two hours, and four jobs went into them.**

A stair light in one block had been put in on the lighting at the top and the lighting at the bottom, so that one of them came on when the other went off.

He split it and proved both.

"**Ten pounds,**" he said. "**A top light on a bottom way is a stair with half a stair. About four of those fittings in that block are wired that way, and one of them has been a landing that somebody has had to cross in the dark for about four years.**"

A light over a bin store had a lamp holder with the neutral and the live both landing on it, so that a fault in the lamp lit the case.

He rewired it and proved the case with the holder out.

"**Nine pounds,**" he said. "**Both legs on one holder is a holder that can put the case live. About four of those holders in that store are done that way, and one of them has been a switch that has tripped a breaker in a different flat for about four years.**"

An outside light in the yard had been wired with a four amp fuse in a fitting rated for thirteen, so that nothing on it had ever been protected properly.

She put the right fuse in and proved it.

"**Eleven pounds,**" she said. "**Four amps in a thirteen is a fitting that has never been tested. About four of those outside lights in that yard have a fuse like that in them, and one of them has been a light that nobody has been able to trust for about four years.**"

A spur box in a passage had its cover missing and its terminals exposed behind a loose plate.

He fitted a cover, sealed it and proved it with the plate off.

"**Five pounds,**" he said. "**A box with no cover is a box anybody can touch. About four of those boxes in that passage have no cover on them, and one of them has been a live metal edge that two children have put hands on for about four years.**"

**Thirty-five pounds is what those four came to on that Thursday, exact.**

---

**That Thursday the fourth of those four rooms was shut at half past six with a woman of about thirty behind that door, and the binder on the shelf at the back of that room did not come off that shelf, and this page prints no figure for the page inside it.**
