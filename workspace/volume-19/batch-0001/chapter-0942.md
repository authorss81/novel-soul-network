# Chapter 942 — The Man Who Shows Files

**A Tuesday was a bench until two and a counter until about six and then two hours in a yard. A man who has been in the same room as one of the nine said four sentences in a warm room and three of them were new, and the fourth of them was the reason nothing could be done about it until Wednesday.**

---

Nine callers came through that shop on that Tuesday, the first of them a little after seven and the ninth at twenty past nine, and that shutter came down at about ten.

**The four converted units off that service road stood at one thousand seven hundred and forty-four days, two hundred and forty-nine weeks and one day. The man of about fifty-one had not moved off that north wall for one thousand three hundred and fifty days, one hundred and ninety-two weeks and six days.**

---

**He said it at ten past six and he said it to the rail and not to anybody, and the man of about thirty-three turned round off the rail and said the four sentences and he has said since that he had been holding them since about four years ago and had never had a reason to open his mouth.**

He said: they were written down once.

He said: somebody put a pen to it and nine people went in as nine.

He said: that was in a room in a second district and the room is a landlord and a filing system and it is still there.

**Nobody in that room said anything for about nine seconds and then the woman of about forty-three asked him one question and it was the right one and she asked it flat and without any weight on it at all.**

She said: by whom.

He said: I do not know. I have been in that room twice and I have never once seen the hand they were written in.

---

**And then she asked the second question, which is the one that has been standing at the back of this for nine days, and Marek has said since that he heard it happen and understood it about nine seconds after it had finished.**

She said: have you asked that room to show you.

He said: I have asked that room for two things and been given both, and that was about a year ago and about four months ago, and I have not been back since.

**That is a different thing from being refused and everybody in that room heard the difference, and about four people in that room have said since that the two of them have been to that room and that he had not mentioned it.**

He said: they will not show me again.

**Nobody in that room said what the reason was. He said it afterwards, in the yard, on his own, without being asked, and he said it to Marek and not to the woman of about forty-three, and about four people have said since that he did that on purpose and not by accident.**

---

**What he said in the yard was this. There was a request about four years ago for a copy of a page, from a room in a fourth district, signed at the top of a sheet by a person who has not been in this city since. He copied the page and it went out in an envelope and it did not come back, and he asked for it once, in writing, and got an answer that was about the envelope and not about him, and that was four years ago and he has not let anybody past his desk since who has not given him a reason he could say out loud in a room with other people in it.**

**He is right about all of that. About four people in that city who have dealt with him have said that he is right about all of that, and one of them has said that it is the single most correct position anybody in this matter has ever taken and that it cost him two of his own.**

Marek said: is there a way to ask him.

He said: yes.

Marek said: say it.

He said: it has to be in writing and it has to have a name on it.

**And that is where that Tuesday stopped, and Marek went back to work at half past eight, and about four people in that room have said since that the two of them arrived at the same place within about nine seconds of each other and neither of them said anything about it.**

---

**A woman of about sixty said one thing at half past seven on that Tuesday and it was not about the room in a second district.**

She said: I have copied that sheet out twice for myself and the two copies disagree, and the place they disagree in is the fifth column.

**Nobody in that room asked her whose copies they are. Nobody in that room asked her where the two copies are. She has said since that she was not expecting to be asked and that she would have answered, and that nobody asked, and that she noticed it about four seconds after she had stopped talking.**

She said: in one of them the fifth column is ruled and in the other it is not, and in both of them there is nothing in it.

**Nobody in that room said a word about that. It is the only thing said out loud in that room on that Tuesday that nothing came back on, and about four people in that room have said since that they had each been in the room at that point and that they remember waiting for somebody to say something else.**

---

**The rest of that Tuesday went into a yard behind a row of shops for about two hours, and four jobs went into it.**

A shed had a light in it wired off a spur that came out of a joint box under the floor, so that the whole spur was unfused and unfound.

He put the spur back onto the board and proved it with the shed door open.

"**Seven pounds,**" he said. "**A spur off a joint box is a circuit nobody can find to switch off. About four of those spurs in that yard came out of a joint box, and one of them has been a light that somebody has had to keep off for about four years because nobody could cut it anywhere else.**"

An outside tap in the yard had been fitted with an isolator on the pipe instead of on the supply, so that turning it did nothing except let somebody believe it had.

He took the isolator off the pipe and put it on the incoming and proved it.

"**Sixteen pounds,**" he said. "**An isolator on a pipe is a handle on a thing that is not the thing. About four of those taps in that row have one on the pipe, and one of them has been a yard that has been washed down every morning for about four years because the stop would not hold.**"

A socket in a workshop had been put in on a lighting way at the far end of the bench, so that a power tool on it dimmed the whole shop.

She gave it a way of its own and ran a saw on it with the lights off.

"**Eight pounds,**" she said. "**A tool socket on the lighting is a saw that costs you the light. About four of those sockets in that workshop are on a lighting way, and one of them has been a bench that nobody can see to work on for about four years.**"

A hand lamp in a store had its flex trapped where it went through a bracket, worn through to the copper and blackened at the point of the trap.

He cut it back to sound flex, fitted a proper gland and proved the lamp.

"**Eight pounds,**" he said. "**A flex trapped at a bracket is a flex that will fail in the bracket. About four of those flexes in that store are trapped where they go through, and one of them has been a lamp that has gone out in a man's hand for about four years.**"

**Thirty-nine pounds is what those four came to on that Tuesday, exact.**

---

**That Tuesday the fourth of those four rooms was shut at half past six with a woman of about thirty behind that door, and the binder on the shelf at the back of that room did not come off that shelf, and this page prints no figure for the page inside it.**

*Conditions of the close.* **Upstairs the green-covered book stands where Volume 18 left it and the tin beside it stands where Volume 18 left it, and neither of those two and this page does not set one of those two against the other and prints neither.
That ninth chair has its back to the whole of that room and it did not move on that Tuesday, and no page of this stretch of days says who moves it. The empty place behind that chair was not named on that Tuesday and and no page of this movement puts a figure on it.
There is a room under a building in a first district and at about eleven on that Tuesday there was nothing burning in it, and nothing is burning in it at this hour either, and not one person has ever switched a light on in there.
A figure of four stands against the register of correct acts that changed nothing at both the start of that Tuesday and the end of it, and nobody in this city keeps a count of them. A room that will not show the same file twice is not on it.**

*What the day did not settle, and the rest of it.* **The passage under this one names ten objects and every line below this one names one of them and no pair. The rail with two places in it. A dated rule stands over a question that is not this one, and the records behind it are public and disputed, and they are open to anybody who asks for them at that counter.
Nobody thanked anybody and nobody forgave anybody on that Tuesday. A man said a room will not show him a file again and nobody asked him to say it twice, and a woman said her two copies disagree and nobody asked her a question about either of them.**
