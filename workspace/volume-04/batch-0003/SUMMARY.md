# Batch 0003 Summary — Volume 04, Chapters 171–180 ("A List And A Lock")

**This is Movement III of Volume 04, weeks fifty-five to fifty-eight, and it is the movement that carries the volume's midpoint and the first time the volume's own argument is proved against the volume's own hero. It follows `outline/volume-04.md`, which is the plan and was not re-argued. It delivers the movement's turn exactly as the outline states it: *the case stops being about a plate on a wall and becomes about a rule that is still in force, and the rule is the thing nobody can repeal, because repealing it needs an authority, and the only authority that exists in that building is a column heading.***

**And it delivers the thing the batch prompt names as this movement's whole finding: *they went looking for a person with authority over a building's own records, and there is not one, and the reason there is not one is that the authority is a company and a landlord and a woman with a trolley, and none of the three has ever been asked to decide anything.** All ten of the ten chapters are carried by somebody who is not Marek Senn, and the master does not leave the pocket and nobody identifies an authorship.*

## The canon decisions of this batch, recorded so that a later writer quotes them rather than re-derives them

1. **THERE IS NO PERSON IN THIS CITY WITH AUTHORITY OVER A BUILDING'S OWN RECORDS, AND THE THREE WHO COULD BE ARE A COMPANY, A LANDLORD AND A WOMAN WITH A TROLLEY, AND NONE OF THE THREE HAS EVER BEEN ASKED TO DECIDE ANYTHING.** A service plate, a manufacturer's line, a service number off a schedule of doors that no building has ever seen, twenty-two years in the trade, and an engineer's sentence that is both true and useless. 2. **A DISTRICT INSTRUMENT CARRIES A PRINTED LINE AT ITS FOOT THAT SAYS *NO LOCAL ADDITIONS*, AND A CLERK OF FIFTY-THREE HAS READ IT EVERY DAY FOR NINETEEN YEARS.** **She broke it once, in the week of week forty-five, ruling a box in pencil for a woman of thirty-one, and told nobody until a man of twenty-two asked her a different question.** She has now told exactly one person. **She did not say whether she was right and is not going to, and the page goes back in the stack, and there is no way in this city for a thing that was never in a system to be put right, and she is not going to invent one.** **The second name was NOT put in the pencil box and the box was not touched. 3. **THE PLANS ARE OPENED. A DRAWER ON A HOOK IN A BASEMENT IN THE FOURTH BUILDING, NO LOCK AND NEVER ANYTHING ON IT TO LOCK, ONE ROLL, A DATE IN INK ON THE OUTSIDE, NINETEEN YEARS OLD, THE INK OLDER THAN ANY MANUFACTURER'S LINE ON ANY PLATE IN THE BUILDING, AND THE SERVICE CONTRACT FOUR YEARS OLD.
