# Chapter 177 — What He Cannot Hear

The ninth of the eleven was on the Friday of week fifty-six, four weeks after the eighth, in the same room in Crown Terrace, and the stack by the door had been made again.

He was four rows from the back with a docket flat on his knee and a lanyard round his neck he had not asked for, and the microphone on its stand at the front of the room had nobody behind it, and the leaflet on the front of the stack had the fee on it and the session title and five jobs, and two of the five jobs are him, and a system chose those two out of two job sheets in the week of the thirty-fourth and nobody in the room chose anything.

---

Corin Vasska read the specification out in the voice he reads everything in, and the third of the three lines came round, and a line is not a section, and nine people in that room did not hear the difference for the third sitting running.

*And a session addressing the coverage of a supervised confirmation where the holder is not a named party to the share.*

"Which means," he said, "**that every sheet on that stack is telling nine hundred rooms the same sentence, and the sentence is nine words, and it is the funded one, and I have now said this three times and I am not going to say it a fourth time in a better voice.**"

The woman who types things was at her table by the wall and she did not stand up, and nine people did not ask her anything, and there are eleven letters in the folder behind her and they have been there since the spring.

---

He stood up in the fourth row at half past nine. It was the second time in four weeks that he had put the condition into the first person with his own mouth, and the seventh time it had been said in public in this city, and the microphone on its stand had not been asked to go off, and nobody in this city is able to ask.

"**There is a condition on my share. I may not demonstrate, teach, assess or assist at any practice in this department, on its premises, under its insurance, at any time, unlimited by hours, and the department may put four names in front of any of them.**"

He stopped there and nobody in that room put a hand up.

"**It stops me doing the thing. It does not stop anybody asking me about the thing. There is no rule in this company or in this city about who may be asked, and I have said that in this room twice now and I am not going to improve it by saying it a third time in a better sentence, and there is not one, and the reason there is not one is that a rule about who may be asked is a rule about who is findable.**"

Then he said the other half, and about four people in that room did not have a shape for it.

"**And there is a second condition and it is not on any sheet, and I have never said it in a room, and I am saying it now because I have found out that the first one is the smaller half.**"

Nobody wrote anything down.

"I am the only person in this city who can say what he heard in a corridor, and I cannot hear a corridor. That is not a mystery and there is nothing wrong with me that a doctor has found, and it is not going to be fixed this term. And in a fortnight I talked over three people in three rooms and I failed to hear one of them, and the one I failed to hear is the reason a thing in this case was not noticed, and it was not noticed for a fortnight, and nobody in this room has been told that and I am telling it to you because if I do not say it in a room then it stays in a book in an inside pocket and there is no version of me that anybody can rely on as a witness to his own ears."

---

A woman of thirty-four stood up ten minutes later, and she had a file with her own name at the top of it and she had put it there herself on a Friday in the spring with her own hand in the box that says *completed by*, and nobody has disciplined her for it.

She said her name and where she worked, and she said it into a room with a microphone on a stand on it.

"**Then I am going to say the thing that is the reason you can say that in here without anybody stopping you, and nobody is going to say it for you, and I have been sitting on it for four weeks.** There are four people in this city who have got a file with their own name at the top of it that they put there themselves, and I have met three of them and I have not met the fourth. The condition on your share is the only reason the four of us are not a group. That is what it is for."

"You have said your name into a room with a microphone on it."

"I have," said the nurse of thirty-four, "**and it cost me a file that was already mine, and there is a man in a district jacket somewhere who heard me on a Friday in the spring and told a corridor in Nine Bridges about it, and I am not going to say his name and he did not do it on purpose, and I would like somebody in this room to notice that I have just said the price out loud and that nobody has written it down.**"

Nobody had written it down. The woman of twenty-six at the table by the wall looked at the folder behind her and then at the woman standing in the fourth row and put her pen down.

"**Then say the other half of yours out loud in this room,**" the nurse of thirty-four said, "**because you have said the half that is on a sheet and nobody in here has ever heard the other one, and I have come to four of these sessions and I have never once heard anybody say that the thing they cannot do is the smaller half.**"

He said it again and it was shorter the second time and it did not get better.

And a man of thirty-four who works nights, who had come on his own time and had told nobody he was coming, said from the sixth row that he has got a version of it in nine words in biro on the back of a card in his own wallet, and that his nine words and this one's are the same shape, and that he was not going to say his out loud in a room with a microphone on a stand.

That is the first time in four terms that anybody has put the two things next to each other in a room, and it was not a question and nobody answered it and nobody wrote it down.

---

The woman of twenty-six said it from her seat, which she has not done in four years, and it took her nine seconds.

"There are eleven letters in this folder and they are all addressed to a man of forty-four and all of them ask the same question in a different first line, and I have not answered one of them and nobody has asked me to." She put her hand flat on the folder. "**And I have written the answers to four of them. I wrote them over three evenings in about four hours and they are good, and they are in the bottom drawer of that table, and I have not sent them, and I am saying that in a room of about thirty people because if I do not say it in a room then in about four years there are four answers in a drawer and no record anywhere of why.**"

"Will you send them."

"**No. And that is not a decision. I have decided there is nothing on the paper to answer them with, and that was true in the spring and it is true now, and the four of them are written and they are not going out, and both of those are true at once and I have not got a rule that says which one I should have been.**"

The officer of the fund read the fourth section and the third line again at a quarter to eleven and the microphone was still on its stand and nine chairs were still empty.

---

Nothing was settled and nothing was minuted, because there is nobody in the room whose business it is to minute. The fund still has a specification of four sections and three lines; the newest of the three is nine words long and is paid for; the levy behind it is paid by premises that number in five figures; the committee of about nine that met once in the week of week thirty-seven has not met since and is not going to.

**The standing problem came up for the seventh time in public, and it came up with the other half attached to it for the first time, and it was not resolved, and nobody in that room invented a rule about who may be asked.**

There is also now no mechanism anywhere in this city for telling the difference between a man hearing a room and a man hearing a room and getting it wrong, and the man in question said so himself before anybody asked him, and a district nurse of thirty-four said her own name into the same microphone eight minutes later, and neither of those two sentences exists anywhere in print.

At a quarter to eleven the microphone was still on its stand at the front of that room, nobody had asked for it to be switched off, and nobody in this city is able to ask.
