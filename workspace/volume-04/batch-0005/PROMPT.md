Hold the batch average at or under 10.75 per thousand and every chapter at or under 12.4.** A bold span marks the beat inside a person's speech; **the elaboration after a speech tag loses the emphasis and the lead-in keeps it, and no line may carry a bold span on both sides of one speech tag**; in a load-book line the first and the last span always survive. Do not use a mechanical longest-span rule across a paragraph. **Nothing in the narration is bolded at all.** **Movement IV finished at 159 and 6.45 with a worst chapter of 7.86, and the order worth keeping is to enforce the rule as the chapters are drafted rather than sweeping afterwards.**

**Every line must carry an even number of `**` and an even number of `"`, and diff the finished files against a pre-edit copy.** Then sweep: **no duplicated paragraph at an eighty-character threshold, no `* * *` marker, no month-name, month-date, day-date, year or day number, and no eighty-character overlap with an earlier chapter's wording for a sentence you are remembering on purpose. Movement IV's eighty-character sweep, rebuilt and run against all one hundred and eighty earlier chapters, returned 38 maximal shared runs, 32 distinct strings, a largest of 144, a mean of 108 — the lowest of the four movements of this volume. Its method, verbatim: whitespace-collapse both sides, slide an eighty-character window over the new chapter, call it a hit for an earlier chapter when that exact string occurs in it, and define a maximal run as a maximal span over which every consecutive window is a hit for the *same* earlier chapter, its length being that span plus the seventy-nine characters of window overlap the span does not cover. The same string in *K* earlier chapters yields *K* runs. Three things a Chapter 191 writer must take from it: the corpus is EVERY earlier chapter and not a remembered subset, the match is made on whitespace-collapsed text so that a paragraph break cannot disguise or manufacture a run, and reuse is reworded rather than classified. Movement IV reworded six blockers and all six were its own framing prose and not a refrain, including a 558-character run in its close against Chapter 180 and four near-duplicate bench passages, and that is the procedure.**

## What the review of batch 0004 found, and what has been corrected, in six lines
