## 1. What Volume 04 was about

Fifty chapters about a record. A man spent a term and a half making himself unfindable, and a term and a half of other people carrying things he would not write down, and a rule that everybody in Nacre has been quietly not obeying for fourteen years because it was never withdrawn and the only copy anybody opens is the copy in a box.
