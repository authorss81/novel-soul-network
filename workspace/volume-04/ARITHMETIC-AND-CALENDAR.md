# Arithmetic and calendar — Volume 04

**This file exists because the day-number interval map and the arithmetic-discrepancy bookkeeping were removed from `state/current.md` in the review repair pass of Volume 04 batch 0001, on the finding that they had grown to about a thousand five hundred words of a file every prompt has to read in full, and that they belong to the volume close rather than to every batch prompt. Nothing was deleted: the full text is in the git history of `state/current.md`, and everything load-bearing is restated below. `state/current.md` carries a one-line pointer here, and the live batch prompt's day map is still the only place a chapter may be assigned a day.**

## The operative rules, and they are four

1. **No day number may be printed in a chapter.** No month-name, no month-date, no day-date and no year. The only calendar nouns permitted are the season words and the inherited canon. The two inherited years from `chapter-0053.md` and `chapter-0056.md` stand and are not extended.
2. **A day is assigned only by the live batch prompt's day map, which is fixed and used and is not to be re-derived.** Where this file and a batch prompt disagree on a day, the batch prompt is right for its ten days and this file is right for the run.
3. **Weeks run Monday to Sunday.** Week 46 = Mon 208 – Sun 214, and Volume 03 ended on the Monday night of week forty-six. Week 47 = Mon 215 – Sun 221. Week 48 = Mon 222 – Sun 228. Week 49 = Mon 229 – Sun 235. Week 50 = Mon 236 – Sun 242. Week 51 = Mon 243 – Sun 249. Week 52 = Mon 250 – Sun 256. Week 53 = Mon 257 – Sun 263. Week 54 = Mon 264 – Sun 270.
4. **The Exchange and Asha Reed's sentence run on four-week spacing from the Wednesday of week sixteen: 16, 20, 24, 28, 32, 36, 40, 44, 48, and therefore 52, 56, 60, 64, 68.** The Wednesday of week forty-four is the meeting that happened in Chapter 147. **The Wednesday of week forty-eight is the meeting that happened in Chapter 156, and the book was not opened.** The next one is **the Wednesday of week fifty-two, which is Chapter 165 of Volume 04 Movement II.** Bea Nunn will not open the book on a Friday, and the book does not go to forty-five in this volume.

## The three recorded discrepancies, none of which is re-based, and all three of which are closed to writers

1. **The map is two days low from the Monday of week thirty-one onward, measured against its own anchor.** Monday of week 31 should be 105 and is printed as 103. The break is at the week 30/31 seam, where the last sitting on the Friday of week thirty is day 102 and the following Monday prints as 103. This was inherited from Volume 02 batch 0001, **has now been recorded six times rather than re-based, and no chapter in the manuscript has ever printed a day number.** It is recorded instead of re-based because a re-base would require re-dating every day figure in five batch summaries per volume and in every volume outline, for no gain on the page. **Use the map as printed. Do not re-derive it from the anchor. Do not print a day number.**
2. **The Thursday of week 36 is day 141, not 139.** Corrected at the top of Volume 03 batch 0003 and standing. Week 36 is Mon 138 – Sun 144, and 139 is the Tuesday. No chapter printed a day number for that session, so no prose was wrong.
3. **A stray sentence once derived the next Exchange date and printed *and the Wednesday of week 40 is day 170*, contradicting the map's own 168.** Removed in Volume 03 batch 0003 and it must not be reintroduced. **A fourth bad line also once stood in `state/current.md`, saying the next Exchange was the Wednesday of week forty-five in Chapter 149's week. It was wrong on two counts and it is gone.**

## The load-book run, which is the other piece of arithmetic every prompt needs

**Continuous, no duplicate and no gap: entry 50 is Chapter 1, entry 153 is Chapter 150, and Volume 04 begins at Chapter 151 with entry 154.** Movement I of Volume 04 is entries 154 to 163, one per chapter, on ten days across weeks 47 to 50. **Movement II is entries 164 to 173, one per chapter, Chapters 161 to 170.** The run ends at entry 203 in Chapter 200. **Eight of the ten entries in Movement I are about somebody who is not Marek Senn, and the two that are about him are the choice, entry 160 in Chapter 157, and the close, entry 163 in Chapter 160. The ten entries have ten different opening labels and ten different closing headings, and no two entries in a batch may end on the same heading.**

## What a later writer must not do with any of this

Do not print a day number. Do not re-derive the map from the anchor. Do not re-base it. Do not move the Exchange off its four-week spacing or put it on a Friday. Do not grow the Exchange's book past forty-four lines. Do not say Asha Reed's sentence again. Do not stage the relay again. Do not let a load-book entry re-argue its own chapter's dialogue, and do not let an entry attribute to a scene words that are not on the page in that scene.
