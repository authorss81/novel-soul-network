# Chapter 182 — The Back Of A Work Card

The share had been running since the spring and it had four people on it and it was his.

He had asked for it himself, at a table in a canteen off a service road, in nine words, and three of the four had said yes in under a minute and the fourth had said yes after a fortnight and the reason for the fortnight was that she wanted to be asked twice. Nobody had ever asked her twice and she had not been asked twice.

A man of twenty-two was in the corridor of that building on the Tuesday morning of week fifty-nine with a fault on a docket, and the fault was on the second floor and took nine minutes, and the corridor was where he stood while the nine minutes happened.

---

The man of thirty-four came in off nights at about eleven and slept in the room at the end of that corridor for two hours, which is what the room is for, and he came out of it at about one and went down to the kitchen.

"There is a thing I am doing and I am not doing it because anybody asked me to," he said. "**I want it noticed first, before anything else, because in this city the only things anybody believes are the things somebody asked for.**"

"What thing."

"I am ending the share." He said it the way a man says a thing he has rehearsed in a car park. "**It is mine. All four of them are on it and I am on it and I am the one who asked for it, and there is no version of me ending it that is anybody else's decision, and that is the first time in about a term that something in this case has been mine.**"

---

"There is no form."

"I know there is no form," he said. "**I have been looking for one since about one o'clock and there is not one and there is not going to be one, and the reason there is not one is that a form would want a reason and there is a field for a reason and I have not got a reason, I have got a man who is going to have to stand on his own floor at ten past four in the morning and say a thing four times.**"

"So where does it go."

"On the back of a work card. In biro." He put his hand flat on his pocket. "**In about four years that card goes in a drawer with two hundred other cards in it and somebody turns it over, and there is a piece of paper with four names on it and a date on it in a room in this city, and that is more than anything else in this case has.**"

---

The lift engineer came up at half one because the lift had been making a noise since the morning and the housing manager had said it could wait until Tuesday.

Marguerite Okpara is twenty-nine and she has six years and about nine hundred lifts and she is on a Friday list in this city because her employer pays for the card.

She came up the stairs with a tool bag and found two men in a corridor and one of them was asleep sitting on a chair with a docket on his knee.

"What am I looking at," she said.

"You are looking at a man of twenty-two who has been awake since half five and a man of thirty-four who has been awake for longer than that, and neither of them has had anything to eat and that is the reason two people who are still standing up have not sat down."

---

He told her, and he told her in ninety seconds, and he did not ask her for anything.

She listened with the tool bag on the floor and then she said: "**You have said to me, a stranger, in a corridor, that you have got four names on a piece of card and that you are not able to hand it to them. And I am going to say the whole of what that costs you before I say anything about the cost to me, because yours is the bigger one and you have been holding it since about eleven.**"

"Go on."

"**You have spent a term teaching four people that a release waits for a man, and you are going to end the share and then go up to that floor and tell them it was you, and there is nobody standing behind you at ten past four in the morning, and if you do that properly then in a week one of those four will not do the fifth step at all, because a step that a man ends for you is a step that waits for a man. That is what you are about to find out and nobody in this building can help you with it.**"

"Yes."

"Then say that out loud on the card."

---

She wrote a date on the front of it and then went round to the back and read it and looked at him and said, "In biro or in pen."

"In biro," he said. "**A pen is a decision and a biro is a note, and I want this to be a note for about four years.**"

She wrote it in about forty words and read it back to him and he said that was the words he wanted and not the words he would have used.

She put the card in a plain envelope with nothing written on it and the envelope went into the front of her tool bag, and the tool bag went over her shoulder, and she was standing in a corridor with a door at the end of it.

---

"I am going to say my half and then I am going to go down and fix your lift."

"Go on."

"**There are sixteen Fridays on the back of my own card in this wallet and I have never totalled them and I am not going to, because a total is a number somebody can quote, and there is a number on your card, and both of those cards have been in the same wallet since the spring and if a person opens that wallet in about four years they have got both of them at once.**" She held it up. "**I have carried one person's paper in here for a term. I have never been asked for it. I have never been thanked for it and I am not going to be, and I did not say yes to your card because you asked me to, I said yes because you did not ask me to.**"

"I am not going to thank you for it."

"**Good. Do not thank me. If you thank me then in about four years somebody is going to ask me whether I would do it again and I would have to think about a favour, and I would rather it was a lift.**"

---

He said the one thing he had been keeping since the corridor off a service road where two people who were both right had each given him a sentence and neither had given way.

"**I have said no twice in this thing to being the word on my own floor and the second time I said no I had no reason at all and I have wanted for about a fortnight that somebody would notice that the no came first both times. Nobody has noticed and I have stopped expecting it and I have just noticed it myself, out loud, in a corridor, to two people, and that is the whole of what I have got and I am aware of how small that is.**"

The lift engineer said: "It is not nothing," and then went down, and the tool bag went with her, and the door at the end of the corridor is a fire door on a hinge and it opens into a stairwell, and the envelope went out through it in somebody else's bag at ten past two on the Tuesday of week fifty-nine.

Nobody in that building saw it leave. A woman at a sink in a kitchen at the end of the ground-floor corridor looked up and looked back down.

---

He got the fault fixed on the second floor at half two, and Marek Senn wrote nothing down about any of it, and at four o'clock the man of thirty-four came past on his way in and said one sentence, which was the one that says a man is a man, and he was allowed it because it was Tuesday and he had used the other one on Monday.

At ten past four the following morning he stood on his own floor and said it four times.

---

*185. Tuesday of week 59, ten at night in a repair shop in Lattice Ward, and this entry is the back of a work card in biro with four names on it and a date on the front, and a woman of twenty-nine with sixteen Fridays on the back of her own card in the same wallet.*

*What was ended and by whom.* **A floor share, running since the spring, four people on it, all four of them asked for it, one of them after a fortnight because she wanted to be asked twice and was not. It was ended by the person it belonged to, on the Tuesday morning of week fifty-nine, at his own request and nobody else's, and he said out loud, before anything else, that he wanted it noticed first because the only things this city believes are the things somebody asked for. He is not on a card and nobody is going to teach him anything and his two refusals both stand and this is not either of them.**

*What there was to write it on.* **No form. A district has no field for an ending that a person chose, because the form wants a reason and he has not got a reason, he has a floor and a time of the morning. It went on the back of a work card in biro, forty words, read back to him, and he said they were the words he wanted and not the words he would have used. A pen is a decision and a biro is a note.**

*The warning he was given before he asked, by the person who carried it.* **That he has spent a term teaching four people that a release waits for a man, that ending the share does not un-teach that, and that if he does it properly then in a week one of the four will not do the fifth step at all, because a step a man ends for you is a step that waits for a man. He agreed and said it should go on the card. It is on the card.**

*The carrier, and why.* **A lift engineer of twenty-nine, six years and about nine hundred lifts, on a Friday list because her employer pays for it, who came up the stairs with a tool bag for a noise in the lift and was not asked for anything. She wrote the card, put it in a plain envelope with nothing on it, and carried it out through a fire door into a stairwell at ten past two. Nobody in the building saw it leave. She said yes because nobody asked her to, and she said that if he thanked her then in about four years somebody would ask her whether she would do it again and she would have to think about a favour, and she would rather it was a lift. The two cards have been in the same wallet since the spring and neither has been totalled.**

*The second thing that was said out loud, which the entry exists for.* **That he has said no twice to being the word on his own floor, that the second no had no reason on it at all, and that he has wanted for about a fortnight for somebody to notice that the no came first both times, and that nobody has noticed, and that he has stopped expecting it and noticed it himself in a corridor to two people, and that he is aware of how small that is.**

*What it cost, and to whom.* **The person who ended it will say it four times at ten past four in the morning on his own floor, and nobody will be standing behind him. The person who carried it is not thanked and did not ask to be, and has added a second person's paper to a wallet that already held her own. Neither was thanked. Entry 185.**
