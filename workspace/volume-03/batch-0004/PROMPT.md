**Bold emphasis has a ceiling and batch 0003 breached it in three chapters and under-reported it.** Volume 02 Movement V ran **7.8 to 12.4 spans per thousand words**. **Hold the batch average at or under 10.75 per thousand and hold every single chapter at or under 12.4** — batch 0003 finished at 418 spans, 10.75, in a range of 7.17 to 14.90, with Chapters 121, 122 and 123 over the ceiling and Chapter 125 at 12.35 just under it. **If a chapter is mostly two-handers, un-bold the second speech after a speech tag rather than stripping the emphasis off the line that carries the argument, and do not use a mechanical longest-span rule across a paragraph — batch 0003 did and it cost four load books a bolded thesis clause.** **Every line must carry an even number of `**` and an even number of `"`, and diff the finished files against a pre-edit copy, because a hand-rolled regex will eventually produce a line like `a man of-six` and the only thing that catches it is the diff.** Then sweep: no duplicated paragraph at an eighty-character threshold, no `* * *` markers beyond the permitted panel, and no month-name, month-date, year or **day number** anywhere.
