# Chapter 140 — The Third Door

Rafi Pell is twenty-one and he has a practice of his own on a grey card with two names against it, and neither of the two names is Marek Senn, and one name was taken off the whole row with a knife in a fish market in the middle of last term, and nobody took a vote about that and nobody has taken a vote about anything since, and he has been a member of a wagon share for about four months and he is a courier and he is a man who is not a node in anybody's network and has not been for a term and a half.

He opened the third one on the Tuesday of week forty-second at about ten in the morning, in a yard off a service road, at the back of a van, on the back of an invoice, because that is the only paper he has ever had in his hand that was already his.

---

"**Read it out,**" he said. "And before you say anything else, I have had this in my pocket for four days and I have not shown it to about nine people and I am showing it to you because you are the fourth thing and you are the only one of the four who has not asked me a question about it."

The invoice was a courier's invoice and on the back of it, in his own hand, in biro, in a list:

*Any ending may be brought to this yard. I will take it to the door in person. I will wait. If nobody answers I will leave it in a hand on the step and I will telephone once from the cab and if it is not answered I will leave a second one the next working day, and I will not leave a third. Any person may refuse to receive it. I will not ask why.*

*No card. No form. No signature. I am one person with a van and a name and you can find me from the name.*

"**And the third line is the only one that matters and it is the one that took me four days,**" said Rafi Pell. "If nobody answers I leave it on the step. I do not leave it in a postbox. I do not leave it with a neighbour. I do not leave it on a table in a building where a person I do not know is sitting with a woman who is looking for a door."

---

"**Where does that come from.**"

"**A man of forty-one drove a woman two hours in the middle of last month and she was not told, and she found out at eleven at night from a man at her work, because her mobile had been off since the summer. That is in about four thousand leaflets as a safety case and it is not in any of them.**" He did not look up. "And I have been a courier for about four years and I have left things on about nine thousand steps and I have never once left a third, and I decided about four days ago that that is not luck."

"**You have not been asked a question about this.**"

"**You have not asked me one, and there is a man of forty-four who stood in a room and said that a rule about who may be asked is a rule about who is findable, and a woman of thirty-one in a market who has written nine refusals on the back of a notice, and a woman of fifty-eight who gave me a piece of card off her counter and told me not to bring it back, and none of the three of them asked me anything and one of them gave me something.**" He folded the invoice once. "That is the reason I am showing it to you. Not because of the case. Because you have been asked four questions this fortnight and you have not asked anybody a question back, and I noticed about four days ago, and I have a van."

---

"**Two things and then I have got nine stops,**" he said.

"**Go on.**"

"**One. Do not put your name on it. I do not mean on the paper. I mean anywhere, including a corridor, including a form in a district, including a minute, and if somebody asks you in about four years who opened the third door you say a courier opened a door and you do not say my name, and I will not be angry and I will not be grateful, and I will know.**"

"**Agreed.**"

"**And you should not have agreed that fast,**" said Rafi Pell. "You have agreed to four things in nine days that other people had to pay for and this is the first one that costs you nothing, and I would like you to notice the difference and I am not going to explain it."

"**Go on.**"

"**Two. There is a word that you have used in this case since the spring and I have never said it and I am not going to say it now, and I want it written down that I know what it is and I have not said it and I am not going to start, and if anybody asks me in about four years whether I had a practice that was a version of somebody else's I am going to say that I had a practice with two names on it and a van and a list of about nine stops, and that is the whole of it.**"

---

He got in the van and then got out again for nine seconds.

"**The word is not *variant*.**" He said it flatly and did not soften it and did not use the other one either. "I know what it says about me on a page in a building in Bower Street in the middle of last term. It is not on my card. It has never been on my card. I have had a grey card with two names on it since I was nineteen and one of the two names came off a row in a fish market, and nobody asked me to stay and nobody asked me to go, and I would like the third door to work exactly the way that worked."

He shut the door.

---

Leo Marr has been in this book three times and she has not cut the wire, twice, and the second not cutting was a judgement nine people copying cards, and she has never once used the word that the four hundred leaflets and the circular and the drop-down all use, and the word came out of a form into a district office about four years ago and after that it was a name.

She telephoned the shop at about four in the afternoon on the Thursday of week forty-second and she said her name in the first four seconds, which she has done every time.

"**I am going to send you something today and it is going to be in about nine places by Friday and one of them is a trade paper, because I have paid for the trade paper and I am not going to pretend I did not.**"

---

"**What is it.**"

"**Four years. Every document in this city in which that word entered a piece of paper, in order, with the date on it and the building on it and the field it went into.**" She let four seconds go by. "A form into a district office in the middle of last term that came out of a free field. A circular in about six thousand copies with a group in a box at the bottom. Four hundred leaflets on a levy note. A nine-field incident report at ten past five on the Wednesday of week thirty-five. And a booklet that a district office printed in the spring of the year before last, which is the one that made it a word instead of a sentence, and that one has about nine hundred copies and I have never seen a copy and I have been looking for one for about four years."

"**That proves it was used.**"

"**That proves nobody in that list is the author. That is all it proves and it is worth about four years of anybody's life, and I have got it because I have had people in eleven rooms for nine years who have kept copies of things they were given.**"

---

"**And the part I would rather burn,**" said Leo Marr. "It is eleven names. It is eleven people in this city who have copied a card, or been handed money, or used that word in a room, and it is nine of them who did it because somebody told them the movement did it, and four of them who did it because they were frightened, and I have got all eleven by the names I know them by and four of those names are names of people who are in this city this week."

Nobody said anything for four seconds.

"**If I send that, then the movement is not a rumour and it is not a name on a leaflet, it is eleven people, and four of them can be found, and one of them is thirty-one and has a district fleece and a daughter of seventeen, and I have been the person who decides who gets hurt in that argument for nine years, and this is me handing it to a stranger in an envelope.**"

"**Yes.**"

"**Then here is the nine words and then I am going to ring you back in four days with a copy and I am not going to say anything else on this telephone.**"

---

"**I am publishing a list of my own people to prove we were used.**"

And then: "**And I am not joining anything, and I am not going to be asked twice, and if anybody wants to put me on a group in a district system I would like them to read line two of that document first.**"

---

Marek Senn said, after about four seconds: "**You are aware of what you have just done.**"

"**I have been aware of it since the Thursday of week thirty-six,**" said Leo Marr, "when a man of forty-one stood up in a room in Crown Terrace and said that a company cannot recall a document nobody admits to issuing. About nine people heard him and I was one of them and I have had four days. In four days you can get very brave and I have been very brave for nine years and it has not once been the same as being right."

---

By the Friday it was in nine places.

It is the first document in this case with about nine names of the movement's own people in it and no name of the person who wrote the four correct steps on four hundred and thirty-two cards, and four of the eleven can be found, and one of them is thirty-one and has a district fleece.

**It does not say who cut the card in half and wrote a line on the back of a copy. It says the word entered a form and then a district and then four hundred leaflets and then a drop-down, and it gives the date on every one of those, and a printer in the Quiet Quarter is not in it and is not on it and has not been asked and would not say.** Nobody in this city has withdrawn a document in this case, and four people have now put their own names in one on purpose.
