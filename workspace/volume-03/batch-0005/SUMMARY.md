## The hedge, measured

- **Raw instances of the word *about*: 306 across 29,868 words, 10.25 per thousand.** Batch 0004 was 370 / 34,343 / 10.77; batch 0003 was 449 / 38,873 / 11.55. **The batch beats the figure it inherited.** **This file previously printed 296 at 9.92. That figure came from a lowercase-literal matcher, and the batch's own hard rule requires the stricter matcher, because the loose one cannot see a capitalised *About* opening a load-book label. There are eleven of those in this batch, one each in Chapters 141, 142, 144, 146, 149 and 150 and four in Chapter 148, and 295 + 11 is 306.** The direction of the error is the one the rule exists to prevent: the loose matcher under-reports, and under-reporting is the direction that hides a ceiling.
- **Counting only *about* that is immediately followed, optionally through *a*, *an* or *the*, by a number word or a counted quantity, gives 203, 6.80 per thousand.** Batch 0004 was 194 at 5.65. **The batch does not beat the figure it inherited on this measure and the honest figure is the one above.** **This file previously printed 168 at 5.63, and that figure is withdrawn: it was not reproducible from the rule printed here, four of its ten per-chapter figures were not reproducible either, and a hedge count that cannot be re-derived is worse than no hedge count, because a later writer copies it.** The reason it was low is the narrowest reading of the rule — a number word had to be followed by a counted noun before it counted, so *about nine* and *about four of the eleven* fell out. Every one of those is a hedge in this manuscript's own idiom and every one of them is now counted.
- **The counting rule, restated so that a later writer can reproduce it mechanically, and the word lists are part of the rule rather than an illustration of it.** Count one hedge for each occurrence of *about*, matched as a whole word and case-insensitively, immediately followed by an optional article (*a*, *an*, *the*) and then by **either** (A) **a number word, alone or in a chain of number words joined by a hyphen or a single space** — *one two three four five six seven eight nine ten eleven twelve thirteen fourteen fifteen sixteen seventeen eighteen nineteen twenty thirty forty fifty sixty seventy eighty ninety hundred thousand million* — **or** (B) **one counted quantity** — *half dozen couple fortnight fortnights second seconds minute minutes hour hours day days week weeks month months year years room rooms form forms card cards word words line lines page pages field fields step steps part parts premise premises fold folds pound pounds penny pence job jobs hand hands name names face faces sheet sheets chair chairs seat seats item items entry entries copy copies place places building buildings door doors column columns packet packets envelope envelopes invoice invoices time times people premises men women woman document documents share shares case cases printer printers quarter size thing things*. **A possessive form (*about a minute's drive*) is excluded**, because a possessive duration is a clock reading.
