## 1. What Volume 03 was about

A man spent a term being handed the thing he had spent two volumes refusing — a wage, a rota, a card in a lanyard, and about eleven people a week who were going to be taught to wait for him — and found out in the same fortnight that somebody was being paid out of a levy to say out loud the one sentence he would not say, and that the sentence was not a lie. **The belief was bought with honest money and it turned into a programme anyway, because a specification is where a true-sounding sentence goes to have a person attached to it.** And then the counterfeit that the movement made by accident turned out to be a copy of a good practice, printed four hundred and thirty-two times out of a postage-stamp photograph on page two of a company's own handout, **which is safer than the company's card by the company's own argument and is the one that gets a leaflet.** And the city turned up a room that could be rebuilt by three people who were not him, and a corridor where nobody needed him for nine minutes, and a woman who has said no to a group in nine words and put her own name on a piece of paper on purpose, and a printer of fifty-eight who stopped printing and did not undo it. And at the end of it he let a thing be published with no name on the front of it, in a room above a print works, out loud, and nine people did not agree with him, and about eleven people a week went on waiting for a man nobody can find, and the hospital got a box in pencil on a form with no reference number on it, and nobody thanked the woman of thirty-one who made it happen.

**And the thing the volume actually proved, which is the worst version of the lesson and is not about anybody but him: a false line can persist because everybody in the room wants the same answer, and the room includes him.**

## 10. The first question of Volume 04

**Not who signed the upstream field, which is still open and will be open for a while, but a question this volume's ending puts on the table for the first time:**

> **A document in this city can now say *who ended it*, in pencil, in a clerk's hand, on one page on a counter, and it is empty. And the same city has about four hundred people who have been handed a page with their own date on it and no person at the top of it. What is the difference between a form that has one box in it and a form that has none — and who is supposed to be the difference, given that the person who drew the box was told twice that there was no box for it?**

**The series sketch's Volume 04 is *Civic Weave*: the team enters the old network archives while Iven Sore's office seals records connected to Marrow Falls; the Civic Compact originally required every technique to show its return path; an unauthorised working-copy amendment overrode that visible exit without announcing the change; the climax retrieves a partial maintenance ledger from the Crown Vault; the resolution puts that ledger in public custody and makes Marek a recognised researcher rather than a public hero; and the volume's next-volume question is whether the network can be useful without Threadline, the Office, or a single privileged builder.**

## 11. What Volume 03 was for, to the writer of Volume 04

**Three things a Volume 04 writer should treat as load-bearing and not as furniture.**

**A man of fifty-eight with a carpenter's pencil who has answered a telephone on a wall for thirty-one years and now holds a stranger's box number and has nobody to hand it to.** He is the best argument in this manuscript that a fact in a man's head and a fact in a hut are not the same object, and he is not in any file, and a Volume 04 writer who puts him in one has undone the volume.

**A woman of thirty-one with a diary who has been told twice this term that she has no standing, who put a box in pencil on a form because a nurse of twenty-four spent her one clean call a week saying the name of a district and nothing else, and who has not been thanked and is not going to be.** **The next chapter that touches this counter has to work out what standing is, and it must not arrive as a post.**

**And a man of twenty-two, in a term and a half, who said a sentence out loud to four hundred and six people, was not believed, said he would not say it twice, and then let a document go out to about eleven thousand premises with no name on the front of it so that about eleven people a week would go on waiting for a man nobody can find — and who knows exactly what that cost, has written it down, and did it on purpose in a room with nine witnesses.** He is not a boy and Volume 03 has finished making him one. **Volume 04 should give him the thing he has never had: an argument that is not about being findable at all, made by somebody else, in a room he is not in.**

**And one thing a Volume 04 writer must not do.** **Do not make him the person who is needed.** The crisis that would have made him necessary lasted nine minutes on a Tuesday in the autumn, there is no card holder in that building, there is no man in a lanyard in that corridor whose word anybody is waiting for, and the three doors belong to a nurse, a repairer and a courier. **About eleven people a week are waiting for him because the belief is being taught, not because the work needs him, and the difference between those two is the whole of this volume and it is not closed.**
