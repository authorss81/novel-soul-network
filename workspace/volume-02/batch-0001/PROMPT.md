## What this batch must do, in order

1. **Ch 51 — the trust.** Marek asks Anders Kolt what a failed condition does, after thirteen weeks of writing down that he has not asked. **The answer is not a punishment and it is not a lesson.** Condition three lapses on the Wednesday of week 16; all five other conditions lapse with it; the ledger stops being open to a department and a trust; the list comes off the wall. **And the trust will lift the other five the same day the request is filed**, because condition three is the only piece of work in the list and the other five are conditional on the trust still being in the room, and the trust stays in the room while there is work. **He is handed, by a man who likes him and who says on purpose that he has made it easy, a perfect reason to do the thing he has refused nine times.** He does not take it. **Also in this chapter: Ruslan Petric and the box at the post office on Weaver Street.** He is the second candidate from the Tuesday of week 14, he followed Rusk's word, he asked for nothing and was given nothing, and he has written to a postbox with no form on it asking for the fact of that to be taken out. **What comes back is three words on a card.** The cost is that a record of nothing is still a record, and he has now made it permanent by asking.
2. **Ch 52 — the second shelf, going upward.** Petar Vance at the shop, or on the telephone and then in the room. **The visitor column has said the same four words twice, six weeks apart, in two depots in the district, and the four words are not on any Threadline document he has ever seen.** A stock docket number, a recycled manifest, a signature in a van book that is not a driver's and not a depot hand. **The mechanism is two phone calls and a column, not a conspiracy.** Also: Rafi's new yard, and a foreman who has heard the word *college*, and Marek's own load-book rule about breaking rule three in a hurry.
3. **Ch 53 — forty-one.** The Institute's notice of the new term's intake. Anneke Roos. Davan Rusk and a manual that is still 3-08. **The intake is on the Monday of week 19 and forty-one people will be taught revision 4-19 out of it.** Marek is asked whether he will be one of the people who can teach it, **and he cannot: the fourth condition of his own Working Share forbids holding a practice alone on Institute equipment, on Institute premises, or under Institute insurance, at any time, unlimited by hours.** The condition that was written to keep him safe is the condition that keeps him out of the room where it matters most. He does not ask for it to be read differently. He asks to sit at the back of a briefing and is allowed to, and Rusk teaches the card, and Marek says nothing.
4. **Ch 54 — withheld pending notice.** Talia Venn's answer in the first-floor room in Bower Street, **on the Friday of week 15.** She produces **a count and not the pages** — two hundred and six, of which some number are hers — and the word she will use in writing is *withheld pending notice* instead of the word they want, and she is going to be asked again. **Marek is not in the room and is told afterwards in one telephone call.** **And the friction with receipts:** she asks him for a line out of the maintenance ledger and he says no, and he asks her for a page and she says no, **and neither apologises and neither goes and stands somewhere else.** The series' requirement for this volume is that they work together and do not trust each other's judgment. Deliver it in this chapter.
5. **Ch 55 — a place with a door.** **The Saturday of week 15.** A free drop-in in the back room of the Saltmarket Exchange: a two-hour refresher, coffee, a booklet, and a certificate at the end. Given away, not sold. **Yvette Kohl, 46, Threadline district safety and training supervisor, first appearance.** Warm, cited, and good with people, and she is liked in that room. **Nkechi Osei's prediction from the repair shop cashes exactly, and it happens by being generous.** Rafi is in the room, because he lost six weeks of Saturdays and there is a certificate at the end of this one. **Marek sees the shape of it and cannot name it by the end of the chapter, and Dessa Kwan is the one who lets it happen and is angry about it and says so to him afterwards.** The Exchange has no vote and the back room was lent by a member who did not ask.
6. **Ch 56 — the yard off the Meridian.** **The Sunday of week 15, and the day the fortnight on Tam's column two expires.** Reuben Daw takes Marek to a goods yard off the Meridian: a blue card bent at the corner, a horn in a control post, a training pack from the district dated on the front, and about forty men standing on wagons waiting for a voice they did not choose. **A shoulder out of 2019 that went in front of nobody because there is no form.** This is Volume 01's thread 28 and it gets its scene here. **Marek does not speak, and the chapter is about why not, and the honest reason is that anything he says would make him the fourth thing in a yard.** Reuben writes a line in the Exchange's four-column book in his own words and signs the end of it, **because a book is not a rule and it is a place where a thing happened.**
7. **Ch 57 — the second reading.** **The Monday of week 16 at nine.** The first reading had nobody in it. **One person comes, and she is Dessa Kwan, and she asks for her row to come out of the ledger, and it does — the whole row, not the name — and the name of whoever took the call goes in the fourth column.** The taker is Marek Senn and his name goes in. **And Bea Nunn's name goes in beside his, because the rule is that the taker's name stays, and Bea Nunn took the Exchange's first call in week 12, and this is a book in a different building.** He rings her to tell her. She says one sentence. **The cost is real: the ledger's only page with three names on it loses one, and the one it loses is the one that was the hardest to get.**
8. **Ch 58 — the reason on the card.** **Oren Vey, in person, for the first time in this volume.** He offers a job in writing: a technician at the district service depot, a wage, a rota, four days, **starting on the Monday of week 19**, and a condition that Marek take the company's own 4-19 course within his first month, which issues a certificate. He says plainly that he is not asking Marek to stop anything and is asking him to be paid, and that the only way a city learns what a hinge does at scale is if somebody with Marek's ear and somebody with Threadline's stock are in the same building. **He is owed a reply about the blank upstream field at intake and he gets one, in four sentences, spoken by a third-year he is about to pay.** He carries the reason up into his own company. **He also gives away a consolidation-point address that is not his to give**, from a customer's van manifest: *Meridian consolidation point, lot onward.* **The lot leads to a building and not to a man, and nobody inside Threadline has ever been inside it.** The price of the job is a certificate, and the certificate is a door for somebody else, and Marek hears it and does not say so.
9. **Ch 59 — the first Wednesday.** Morning: **Asha Reed's sentence, in a first-year common room, the first Wednesday of the month, one voice in it, and it is hers.** She has said out loud that she does not know how to fix it and does not know whether it is fixable. **Marek is in the corridor and does not go in, and Asha comes out and tells him she saw him, and does not soften it.** Afternoon: **the Exchange monthly meeting**, four minutes an item, no vote for anybody, Bea Nunn's chair, a tin, and about twelve people. Items include the list on a wall, a request about a room, **the card in the goods yard off the Meridian, and a card from a man who has lost six Saturdays.** Evening: **the trust's condition three expires on this day and no letter comes, and the chapter is about a deadline that passes in silence.** Load-book page.
10. **Ch 60 — column two.** **The Thursday of week 16.** Tam Arrick says what he wrote at the top of column two on the Saturday of week 13, and the fortnight ran out on the Saturday of week 15 and he said nothing, and **what he says is that there is a two-day course with a certificate at the start of next term and he wants it.** He has a middle finger that has not moved, a kitchen that has had him off every board since the week he went into the ward, and a transcript line that will be standing in nine years. **Marek does not talk him out of it and does not talk him into it.** He does the hardest available thing: he points him at **the one person whose certificate is not in question and who is four miles away**, and says so out loud, in plain words, as a professional matter, and does not make it a thing — **and Tam says no**, because going to Talia Venn makes it a thing between three people, and he has spent a term being a case. Marek accepts that. **And the hinge is identified.** In about nine seconds, in a shop, on the Saturday drop-in's certificate, Marek sees what it is: a practice with a person in it and a practice with a word in it, and a certificate that makes the word portable and permanent and free of the room it was learned in. **He cannot say it in a way anybody will hear**, because he has no licence, four people who can end his share, and a conditional extension. **One System panel in the whole batch, in this chapter, plain, and it does not conclude.** Then Tam asks him for the one thing only he can give — what the fourth beat is — and Marek has to answer, **and his answer is that he does not know, and that the four sentences in the fifth line are the boy's own, and that he is not allowed to write them down.** And the last beat: **Marek writes it in the load book, which is the first time in thirteen weeks he has written a page that is not his own debt.**

## Hard rules for this batch

- **Do not name the Crown Clause. Do not let anyone identify the Marrow Falls signature's authorship. Sera Quill has said the name nowhere and must not in this volume.** If she appears she withholds, goes cold, or leaves the room. She may not explain. **She is not in the demonstration room in Chapter 97 and that is correct.**
- **TRC-419-88 is spent and stays spent.** No revision history, no approval page, no re-argument that the omission was chosen. **The blank licensor field stays unanswered through Chapter 100.** The Continuity Office still declines to trace it, in writing.
- **Threadline is the assembler and the seller, never the author, and this volume ends without Threadline being the author.** **Oren Vey is not defeated, is not the final enemy, is right about something, and is owed a reply — he gets one in this batch.**
- **No new final enemy, no new faction, no new office and no new post.** Yvette Kohl is a company supervisor and not a villain. The drop-in is a Saturday kindness and not an organisation. The Quiet House does not appear in this batch; if a Quiet House document is referred to, it is the unsigned sheet from Chapter 50 and the box at the post office on Weaver Street, which is a postbox and not a membership.
- **No Quiet House member is on stage and Leo Marr does not appear.**
- **Marek must not become good at something he has not practised.** He has one working non-hinged release, performed once, in front of a panel, badly and slowly, on the Tuesday of week 14, and he cannot do it again on demand. **The hinge he identifies in Chapter 60 is a detection and not a technique, and it has no name of his own and it releases nobody.**
- **No share without a named purpose, a named scope, and a person who could stop it.** Any share in this batch runs on the seven headings. **No share is performed in this batch at all**, with one possible exception: if you want a share in Chapter 60, it is Rafi granting one, once, in writing, and it is the movement's cost, not its reward.
- **The Working Share's fourth condition is load-bearing in Chapter 53 and is not renegotiated for convenience.**
- **Talia is not a reward, a deputy, a romantic payoff, or a route to the boy.** Milestone 5 stands: Thursdays, separate workrooms, *we had an hour*, no form, nothing in either record. **Milestone 3 (trust under exposure) belongs to Volumes 03–04 and must not be spent here.** The Chapter 7 page stays closed and the Chapter 33 page is not reopened.
- **Rafi is not forgiven the word *variant* and is not a yes.** He counts to two out loud, visibly, in a room, where anyone can watch. **He has no fourth beat at all and says so first.** Nobody took a vote about him and he says so.
- **Nell Senn is unwritten.** She does not appear and is not referred to. The silence that began on the Wednesday of week 10 is not resolved, not forgiven cheaply, and not turned into a device.
- **Tam's injury, the lost kitchen wages, the middle finger and the three months before anything heavy are permanent costs.** His own figure of six weeks is his and is not anchored to the operation and is not recomputed. **Nothing in this batch resets any of it, and nobody tells him he will be ready.**
- **Enid Sarkis is not resolved by a telephone number.** A crisis route is not a job. Her page and the records request are in the same drawer under a battery and a roll of solder, and **neither of them goes in a postbox in this batch.**
- **Do not let the Institute's manual get updated.** Still 3-08. Rusk requisitioned the reprint himself in March.
- **There is still no such thing in this city as a person whose job it is to look at a stranger's wrist, and this batch must not appoint one.** The fifth room stays shut and **nobody is given the job of taking the name out for the people who will sit in rooms that cost them one.**
- **The Choir of Practice's shoebox record does not come out again.** It came out once, in Chapter 25, by a vote of eight to three.
- **The load book is the spine and it must be varied.** Four headings, first person, a *Do not know* at the foot, exact dates. In this batch: **Ch 51 three lines and no headings; Ch 54 four headings and a long page; Ch 58 short, about an offer; Ch 60 a page that is not his own debt and is the first such page in thirteen weeks.** Consider whether he writes one at all in Chapters 56 and 59.
- **Every refusal in this batch should cost the refuser something.** That is the method Talia Venn named in the ninth week and it has held for fifty chapters.
- **A scene break is `---`. A panel is `* * *`.** **One panel in the whole batch**, in Chapter 60, plain, and it does not conclude and does not tell him where to look. The interface has spoken three times in fifty chapters — Chapters 3, 7 and 17 — and has never once helped.
- **The failure mode for this batch is making the paperwork the plot.** Every document in it has to cost a person something, be refused by a person, or be carried by somebody who walks. **A document that only moves the investigation is a chapter that should not have been written.**
