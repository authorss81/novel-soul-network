## What this batch must do, in order

1. **Ch 81 — the ask, and the first call on a number.** **The Monday of week 22.** He asks Petar Vance for a service callout to Meridian consolidation point, lot onward. **Vance agrees in about four seconds and will not write the box number down, and the reason he gives is a mechanism and not caution.** The out-of-hours line is his from this morning and it rings at about twenty past eight, and it is a Saturday's worth of work done in six minutes by a man reading a manufacturer's plate down a telephone, and the number is on about two thousand cards and fridges in this district. **And at ten past six he is not at the Weir for the first time in three weeks, and a man of twenty-six is standing where he stood, and nobody has told him what he is standing in, and Marek Senn is four miles away in Lattice Ward with a lamp on over a bench and he does not go and does not ring.**
2. **Ch 82 — two things in a drawer, and a blank line in a return.** **The Tuesday of week 22.** The letter from the Continuity Office arrives with a reference number on it and goes into the drawer under the bench **with page four of a company's induction booklet, and neither of the two can be produced to anybody.** Talia Venn sends her eleven-line answer to Roisin Keogh and writes her own return: **not by a company**, and no reason. **And the first of what a company now does instead of a field goes live, and nobody rings a supplier in this chapter.** Friction with receipts, one of the batch's four.
3. **Ch 83 — a man of twenty-three's day.** **The Wednesday of week 22.** **Oren Vey rings Ruslan Petric as the operations director of a company that has spent four days being discussed on a counter.** A form, no target, no company in the room. Petric's own record: a hand-written sheet through a box at a post office on Weaver Street in week sixteen, three words in ballpoint on a playing card, and a line in a book in a market at forty. **He says yes. He asks for one thing and it is not the thing anybody would ask for. Marek Senn is not in this chapter and does not know any of it has happened.**
4. **Ch 84 — the building.** **The Thursday of week 22.** The service callout goes. **A booking is a gate and a gate does not care who is on the invoice, and the lanyard is what gets him through the second door.** Four days of rigging, a site office, nine men who do not work for the company, and an assembly docket in a drawer with **lot onward** typed in a stores clerk's hand and a box number written in a different pen by a different person. **He gets an address, a smell, a name over a door and about eleven words that are already in a book in Lattice Ward. He asks nobody anything and he does not try, and the chapter must show the cost of not trying as a physical thing.** Nothing is resolved. The licensor is not named. Threadline is not the author.
5. **Ch 85 — he says yes and he gets it.** **The Friday of week 22.** **Rafi Pell rings the number back.** Two modules, a card at the end of the second day, a start date, on the clock. **And the fourth beat on that course is a supervisor's confirmation, and the boy who has never had a fourth beat at all is the easiest person in this city to convert, because he has nothing to lose and something to be given.** He counts to two. **Nobody took a vote. Nobody has forgiven him the word *variant*.** He tells Marek Senn the same evening, in the first person, with his own name at the end, because that is what he does, and **Marek Senn has no sentence.**
6. **Ch 86 — the third floor.** **The Saturday of week 22.** Thirty plastic chairs, about twenty women on somebody's books, a district woman with a list with a tick on it and not a name. **He is in a building in Bower Street for the first time in his life and nobody asked him to come and nobody is going to thank him for coming.** He does not do anything about the chairs. He carries some. **And the nine hundred words are in that room in somebody's bag, unfinished, and Dessa Kwan's rule from week sixteen is the thing that keeps the room a room.**
7. **Ch 87 — the stairs, for the fourth time in nine weeks.** **The Sunday of week 22.** Enid Sarkis, sixty-eight, three years, a plate every day, under his own bedroom. **He goes in.** And Delia Frost is two streets away with a hip unit fourteen weeks old and nobody whose job it is to be told. **There is still no such thing in this city as a person whose job it is to look at a stranger's wrist, and this batch does not appoint one, and the chapter has to be worth reading without one.**
8. **Ch 88 — inside the course, and the sentence he is not allowed to say.** **The Tuesday of week 24, and week twenty-three has no chapter and is written into the opening.** Rafi Pell does not ask him not to come; **Marek Senn does not offer to drive him and Rafi Pell notices in about a second and says good, because he had a whole thing ready and did not have to use it.** **A supervisor with a lanyard gives a confirmation, and it is the fourth beat, and it is about eleven parts, and it is the best engineering in this city, and Rafi is good at it and finds that he likes it.** Rafi does not know what it is. **Marek Senn does, and is not in the room, and does not tell him, and the chapter must be able to be read twice.** No share is performed. No System panel.
9. **Ch 89 — forty-one lines.** **The first Wednesday of week 24.** Asha Reed's sentence. **The Exchange's monthly meeting, four minutes per item, a tin, and a four-column book: Ena Truslove's card is read out in full and the taker's name goes in the fourth column and the book goes from forty lines to forty-one and does not move again this term.** And Bea Nunn asks the only question anybody has asked about a room in four years — *not who is responsible for it but who would take the name out for them* — and gets four answers and one of them is worse. **And a man in the market says *the technique*, and it is going to be the word by the end of the month, and it is wrong, and it is in a book by nine o'clock.**
10. **Ch 90 — the word review, and a first-year's own words.** **The Friday of week 24.** **Somebody says *review* to him who has no standing to call one, and he does the thing a woman gave him for nothing on a Sunday: he says the true sentence, he does not explain it, he does not fill it in, and he asks for the reference in writing. And a reference comes, and the sitting is real, and it is on the last Friday of week thirty, six weeks away, and nobody can say who called it, **and the whole of the mechanism is a form with the middle one of three boxes ticked and a woman in a booking office typing a room number into a system because her job has a form and a room in it.**** **And Rhoda Ilesanmi, nineteen, gives him her own account of her own forearm in her own words and tells him she is going to say it herself on the Friday and is not going to have him say it for her.** End on the account, not on a cliffhanger.

## Hard rules for this batch

- **No System panel.** The volume's one panel was Chapter 60 and it is spent. The interface has spoken four times in one hundred and ten chapters and has never once helped. **Do not add one.**
- **No share is performed in this batch.** The Working Share's four named partners and its fourth condition — *may not demonstrate, teach, assess or assist at any practice in that department, on any premises of it, at any time, and it is not limited by hours* — stand, and neither is renegotiated. **Both surviving conditions of the trust are live.**
- **Marek must not become good at something he has not practised.** He can do maintenance. He cannot teach, may not demonstrate, is not asked to, and does not. The hinge remains a detection, has no name of his own, releases nobody, and is not a technique.
- **Do not name the Crown Clause. Nobody identifies an authorship. Iona Sorn is a name in a document and nobody says it aloud. Sera Quill has said the name nowhere and must not.** No Quiet House member is on stage; **the leaker is not named and is not speculated about by anybody, including the man who loses most by it.**
- **No new final enemy, no new faction, no new office and no new post.** People are allowed. Posts are not. **There is still no such thing in this city as a person whose job it is to look at a stranger's wrist.** Vey does not get an ethics committee.
- **Nell Senn is unwritten.** She does not appear and is not referred to.
- **Every document in this batch costs a person something, is refused by a person, or is carried by somebody who walks.** A document that only moves the investigation is a chapter that should not have been written.
- **Threadline is the assembler and the seller, never the author, and the volume does not end with it being the author.**
- **The Institute's manual is still 3-08 and nothing in this batch repairs the paperwork.**
- **The Exchange's four-column book is at forty lines until Chapter 89 and goes to forty-one in Chapter 89 and does not move again in this term.**
