# Chapter 764 — A Thursday, A Bench Until Two And A Counter Until About Six, A Room Over A Shop In A Second District At About Half Past Five With About Nine People In It And A Woman Of About Twenty-Four Who Was Asked For By A Stranger And Was Not Asked For By Him, And A Column That Is Not On The Form

**A Thursday was a bench until two, a counter until about six, and a room over a shop in a second district from about half past five. About nine people were in it. A woman of about twenty-four was sent for by somebody who had met her twice, and wrote a reason on the back of a sheet because the form has no column for one.**

---

Eleven names went on that Thursday's sheet and eleven dates went with them, the last at about twenty to five. The shutter came down at about ten.

**The rooms off that service road are at one thousand four hundred and ten days, two hundred and one weeks and three days, and that card in the rail by the door is one thousand four hundred and fourteen, two hundred and two weeks to the day. The rail takes two.**

**Line twelve is one thousand three hundred and thirty, a hundred and ninety weeks to the day, and line fifteen is one thousand two hundred and twenty-five, a hundred and seventy-five weeks to the day. The hold on nine crates is one thousand and forty-three, a hundred and forty-nine weeks to the day.**

Seven hundred and ninety days is the separation, a hundred and twelve weeks and six days.

---

**The woman of about thirty-four asked, in a corridor at about half past four on the Wednesday, whether a woman of about twenty-four would come and sit in a room with about nine people in it and say a thing about a form.**

He was told about it at about half past five by a man of about fifty-two who had been in the room and who put it as a fact and not as a favour.

**He was not asked to go. He went. There is a difference and he has never once been able to explain the difference to anybody including himself, and about four people in this city have tried.**

---

**She said it in about nine seconds and she said it to the room and not to him, and he was four feet from her and looking at a coat on a hook.**

"**I can put it in a register,**" she said. "**A register is not a reply.**"

The form on the table in that room has three columns. The first is what was asked. The second is what was done. The third is a date.

"**There is no column for a reason,**" said a woman of about forty-four who keeps a room of lists and who has kept it for about nine years. "**There is no column for a reason because in nine years nobody has ever needed one.**"

That was correct and it was said in about nine seconds and nobody contradicted it.

---

**And then the woman of about twenty-four turned the sheet over and wrote on the back of it in her own hand, and she wrote it fast, and it took her about four minutes, and about nine people watched a form fill up on the wrong side.**

She did not ask whether she was allowed. She was entitled — her terms say so, and somebody entitled said them, and not him.

**The sheet went back into the register with the reason on the back of it and nobody turned it over, and it is in a drawer in a building in that district, and it has been in a drawer since that Thursday.**

---

**What was on the back of it was about a shift and about a gate that opens at five and about four hundred yards of yard that a person can get across in eleven minutes at a walk and cannot get across at all in the dark.**

There was no sentence on the back of that sheet about anything else, and nobody in that room added one, and about four people read it upside down from where they were standing.

---

---

**About nine people were in that room and about four of them had come from the district and about five had come from outside it and nobody could say afterwards which was which.**

There is no way to tell. They are the same people in the same coats in the same room and about four of them work shifts and about four of them do not and nobody asked anybody.

---

**A man of about fifty-two, who had been to a service group meeting the week before and had said the sensible thing on a landing and been refused, spent the whole of that Thursday saying nothing.**

He sat at the back with his hands on the table in front of him. He did not interrupt. When the woman of about twenty-four finished writing on the back of the sheet, he said one sentence, and the sentence was that somebody ought to tell the district before Friday that a reason had been recorded, and then he said nothing else for about an hour.

**Nobody told the district before Friday. About four people in that district found out on the Monday from somebody who had been in the room and about nine people in that district found out in the summer or have not found out at all.**

---

**About half past six the woman of about thirty-four stood up and put her hand flat on the table for the length of a breath and lifted it again and said that she had to go and open a gate at five tomorrow and that she was opening it early because of the notice.**

Somebody asked whether opening it early made any difference to anybody, and she said no, and said that she was going to do it anyway, and that she had been doing it early on and off for about eleven years and had never told anybody and was not telling anybody now.

**Nobody thanked her for that and about four people in that room noticed it and about two of them have since said something to somebody about a woman who opens a gate early and does not say why.**

---

---

**Afterwards he walked about four minutes from that room to the shop and the shutter was down and he did not open it and stood at the window for about nine minutes.**

A woman of about twenty-four's car had gone by then. There was nothing to look at.

He worked out, in about nine minutes, that he had been in that room for an hour on a Thursday because a man of about fifty-two had said a thing in a corridor at half past four and that he would have gone if the man had not said it.

**He would have gone anyway. He knows that and it took him about four minutes to establish it and it did not help.**

---

**The register is in a drawer and the reason is in it on the back and about nine people know the reason and about four of them have read the front.**

That is not anybody's fault. It is what a register is. It has about nine hundred lines in it and about four of the lines have anything on the back and about none of the four are about anything anybody asked a question about.

**Nobody in this city has ever been told that there is a place where a thing they said is written down where nobody reads the other side of it, and about four hundred people have signed a form whose box says what the last approved answer was, and about nine of them have been in that room.**

---

---

**What she wrote on the back of that sheet was four sentences and about thirty words and about half of them were about distances.**

Four hundred yards of yard. Eleven minutes at a walk. A gate that opens at five. A dark that comes at about four in that week.

**Nobody in that room asked her for the rest of it and she did not offer the rest of it, and about four people have since asked her for the rest of it and about four people have been told there is no rest of it.**

---

**He wanted to say to her that she had made it into an answer, and he did not, because it was not an answer, and because a person who has written down the first true sentence anybody has said in that room for four weeks does not need a man of twenty-two to tell her what she has done.**

She left at about half past six and got into a car outside and he saw her through the window of the shop from about nine feet away and neither of them raised a hand.

---

**What it cost was her office four minutes and a drawer with something in it that nobody asked for, and what it cost him was about four minutes of standing in a room being the wrong person in it, and neither of those is a thing either of them would have called a cost.**

**There are four rooms off that service road and one behind the other three, and from about half past six a woman of about thirty sat in the fourth of them, at her chair, with her door shut, and her page is the eighth of eight and it has been on that back shelf for a hundred and ninety-nine days and nobody has asked her about it and nobody is going to.**

---
