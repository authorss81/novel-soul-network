# Chapter 767 — A Tuesday, A Bench Until Two And A Counter Until About Six, A Room Off A Road At About Half Past Seven With About Nine People In It And A Man Who Was Given A Key Six Years Ago And Put It Down Himself Putting Money On A Table And Being Refused In About Nine Seconds For A Reason About Work

**A Tuesday was a bench until two, a counter until about six, and a room off a road from about half past seven. About nine people were in it. A man offered money for two years and was refused in about nine seconds, and then he said one true thing, and nobody could prove it either way.**

---

Ten callers were dated on that Tuesday and ten names went against them, and the last of the ten was taken at about twenty to five. The shutter came down at about ten.

**The rooms off that service road are at one thousand four hundred and fifteen days, two hundred and two weeks and one day, and the card on end in the rail by the door is one thousand four hundred and nineteen, two hundred and two weeks and five days.**

**The nineteenth line is one thousand one hundred and eleven, a hundred and fifty-eight weeks and five days. The hold on nine crates is one thousand and forty-eight, a hundred and forty-nine weeks and five days. The post at that corridor end is nine hundred and sixty-three, a hundred and thirty-seven weeks and four days.**

Seven hundred and ninety-five days is the separation, a hundred and thirteen weeks and four days.

---

**Nine of them are service groups now, each with its own name and its own crews and its own person who can refuse a release inside it, and about four of the nine no longer take an instruction from anybody, and the man who lost that put the key down himself and said that was the only part he decided.**

He offered to pay for the district's transition out of one group's budget for two years, and the condition was that the group keeps the local route.

**He put it in a folder and put the folder on the table, and about nine people looked at it, and it was not a large amount and it was enough.**

---

**She refused him in about nine seconds with a reason that was about the work and not about him.**

"**A route kept by a company for money is a route the company can stop,**" she said. "**I would be spending two years building the thing I am trying to leave.**"

Somebody said that two years was a long time to be certain about anything.

"**Then do not be certain about it,**" she said. "**Plan for it ending.**"

---

**And then he said the true thing, which he has been known for saying for six years and which nobody in that room believed and nobody in that room could disprove.**

"**I would rather pay for a district to leave than for it to stay and cost me more,**" he said. "**That is the whole of my reason and you can write it down and it is the same reason I would have given you if you had stayed.**"

He did not say it as a confession. He said it as a price list.

---

**About four people in that room looked at each other when he said it and about two of them have since said that they believed him and about two have since said that they did not, and both of those pairs have not said it to him and neither of them has said it to anybody who would repeat it.**

Nobody thanked anybody and nobody said thank you and nobody said he was wrong.

---

**There are nine service groups and about four of them are older than the man who lost control of them and about two of the nine were started by people who are dead.**

He can name the four. He cannot name the two without about nine seconds of thinking and about four people in that room have watched him do it and about two of them have noticed that he gets it correct.

**Each of the nine has its own name, its own crews and its own person who can refuse a release inside it, and that is what he put on a table six years ago and it is the only part he decided.**

---

**A man of about thirty-eight asked what happens to a group when the money stops, and nobody answered him, and he asked it again at about eight o'clock in a slightly different way, and a woman of about thirty-four said that it stops.**

He said that was a bleak answer and she said it was an answer, and he said that was the same thing said differently, and about four people in that room laughed for about four seconds and it was not a laugh anybody enjoyed.

---

**The folder went back in a case at about ten to eight and the case went under a chair and about four people looked at the chair.**

That is the whole of what happened to the money. It went under a chair in a room off a road for about an hour and a half and then it went out of the room in a case with somebody's initials on the strap and about nobody has asked whether the initials were his.

---

---

**The room off that road has about nine chairs and a table and a kettle that belongs to the building and a window that does not open.**

It is used on about four evenings a week by about nine people and on the other three it is a room with a table in it. About two of the nine have keys. About four of the nine have not been in that room since before the spring and about one of the four has been in it every week since before the spring and says nothing about that.

**That is the membership of an institution nobody in that room will describe as one: a kettle, a window that does not open, about nine chairs and one person who is always there.**

---

**The talk before the offer was about a gate.**

That is the whole of it. About nine people spent about nine minutes on a gate in a second district because a lorry had taken the hinge off it and because the man of about fifty-two had been told about it by a driver and had passed it on, and the passing-on took about four days and lost about nine details and arrived as a gate that does not close.

**A woman of about thirty-one said the gate needed a plate and not a hinge and that a plate costs about nine pounds and that it had been open for about four days.**

Nobody wrote it down. A woman of about twenty-nine who was in that room wrote it on her hand in about four seconds and rubbed it out at about nine o'clock because it was not hers to write and because about four people have watched her do that for about eleven years.

---

**---

**There is one more thing about that room and about him and about nine people in this city know it and none of them has ever said it in a room.**

He asks, on about four occasions in about eleven years, whether anybody has eaten. He does it in about nine seconds, standing up, to a room of about nine people, and about four of the nine have never heard him do it and about nine of them have heard somebody else do it about a hundred times.

Nobody thanks him for it. He has never asked for thanks and about four people have said afterwards that they thought he was being grand about it, and about two of those four have said that to him, and he has agreed with them both times.

---

He picked the folder up and put it under his arm and said that if the answer was no then the answer was no and that he would not come back with a second version of it. He has not come back with a second version of it.

---

**He was in that room and he was not asked for anything and he did not offer anything and about nine minutes of that evening were spent on him standing by a door, and he has said since that if he had been asked to say something he would have said the wrong thing quickly.**

Afterwards, in a corridor, a woman of about thirty-eight said the thing nobody had said.

"**Nobody has costed it,**" she said. "**That is the thing. You have all been talking about whether to pay for it.**"

**Nobody in that room had a figure, and the district has never had one, and it never had one, and it is not going to get one out of a company that would rather it stayed.**

---

**There are four rooms off that service road and one behind the other three, and from about half past six a woman of about thirty sat in the fourth of them, at her chair, with her door shut, and her page is the eighth of eight and it has been on that back shelf for a hundred and ninety-eight days and nobody has asked her about it and nobody is going to.**
