# Chapter 765 — A Friday, A Bench Until Two And A Counter Until About Six, A Water Yard In A Second District At About Half Past Four Where About Nine People Work And One Of Them Cannot Hold A Line Steady, And A Man Who Is Not Going To Be Investigated About It

**A Friday was a bench until two, a counter until about six, and a water yard in a second district at about half past four. About nine people work in that yard. He was asked to look at one of them and he said in about four seconds that there was nothing to look at.**

---

Twelve names went on that Friday's sheet and twelve dates went with them, the last at about twenty to five. The shutter came down at about ten.

**Those four rooms off that service road are at one thousand four hundred and eleven days, two hundred days and one weeks and four days, and the nine hand copies of the front of one page are at nine hundred and seventy-seven, a hundred and thirty-nine weeks and four days.**

**The nineteenth line is one thousand one hundred and seven, a hundred and fifty-eight weeks and one day. Line seventeen is one thousand one hundred and eighty-three, a hundred and sixty-nine weeks to the day, and the post at that corridor end is nine hundred and fifty-nine, a hundred and thirty-seven weeks to the day.**

Seven hundred and ninety-one days is the separation, a hundred and thirteen weeks to the day.

---

**The yard is not big and there is a wall on one side of it with a lean-to against the wall, and about nine people work there on a Friday, and about four of them had been in that yard for over nine years.**

He had a job there in the spring — a gate post and a latch — and the woman of about thirty-four had found him in the shop because of it, and she asked him to come and look at a hand.

Not to fix it. She said look at it, and she said it in the way a person says a thing they have already decided is not going to be fixed.

---

**He is about forty-seven. He has worked in that yard for about eleven years. His right hand has not been steady since a night about a year ago that about four hundred people in this city still call the nine minutes, and it is not new, and it has not got worse in a way anybody has noticed, and nobody has ever investigated it, and there is no file on it.**

He was holding a release line at about half past four when they came in and he put the line down before anybody said anything, which told the woman more than she had expected to be told.

---

**She said it in about nine seconds and it was about the yard and not about anything else.**

"**He holds it steady with the other one and takes about four seconds longer than he did,**" she said. "**I have timed it. Nine years I have watched that man hold that line and I timed it last week and he takes about four seconds.**"

The man of about forty-seven said that was about correct and would not look up.

---

**He looked at the hand for about eleven seconds. He did not ask the man to squeeze anything and he did not ask him to hold anything and he did not put a thumb on the wrist, and about four people watching from the lean-to have each described that to somebody as though it had been a thing a doctor did.**

"**There is nothing to look at,**" he said. "**That is not a part. It has not got a fault in it that a person could find with money. A hand that will not do what it did is not something I repair, it is something that happened, and it happened about a year ago and there is nothing in it that came off a shelf in this city.**"

Somebody at the lean-to said *a year ago* in the way people say a number they have checked.

---

**And then she asked him, in about nine seconds, why he had said no to her and not to the man.**

"**I did not say no,**" he said. "**I said there is nothing there. Those are different and one of them is about the work.**"

"**I know they are different,**" she said. "**I asked because I did not want to be the only person in this yard who had said it out loud to somebody who fixes things.**"

He said that was a thing to say to a person who fixes things and not to him, and she said he was the person who fixes things, and neither of them said one word about that.

---

---

**The work went on for about forty minutes after he had looked at the hand and nobody asked him to do anything and he did nothing.**

That is the whole of what he did in that yard. He stood at the back under the lean-to with his hands in his pockets for about forty minutes while about nine people worked about forty yards of yard in front of him, and about two of the nine spoke to him, and one of those two told him he could go.

**He has worked in about nine yards and about four water works and two of them for about eleven years and standing in one of them not being asked to do anything has happened to him about nine times and he has never once known what to do with his hands.**

---

**A man of about forty-seven did it nine times in about forty minutes and the ninth one was the worst of the nine and about four people saw the ninth one and about two of the four said something to each other about it afterwards, on the wall, quietly.**

---

---

**There is a clinic a day and a night of bus travel away and about four people in that yard have been to it and about nine have not.**

It is on a wall in that city that a hand that will not steady is not treated and is not measured and is not recorded, because recording it would mean a person had been asked about it, and a person who has not been asked about it cannot be recorded.

He has been to that clinic about nine times in about eleven years and about four of the nine were for somebody else.

---

**He did not charge her for the eleven seconds and she noticed and neither of them said a word about that either, and it is the second time in four days that he has not charged somebody and it is going to be noticed by somebody who does not have anything else to do.**

---

**A yard is not a room. About nine people work in it and about four of the nine have worked in it for over nine years and the other five have worked in it for under two, and the two groups do not stand in the same places.**

The four who have been there nine years stand at the front and know where the pipes are before they see them. The five who have not stand at the back and are told where the pipes are.

**He has been at the front for eleven years. He is about forty-seven and he is the only person in that yard who does not need to be told anything and about nine people there have watched him be told nothing for about eleven years.**

---

**They work on a release because a release on that line comes off a shared supply, and when it comes off, the yard stops, and it stops whether anybody is holding it or not.**

That is the whole of the thing. About four hundred people across three districts and half a bus route put a hand on a line that the man of about forty-seven is holding, and about nine of them are children, and the line does not stop for any of them, and if his hand goes, everything on that line goes at once.

**He has told about nine people this in about nine years and about four of them have written it down and none of the four has ever sent it anywhere.**

---

**While they were there Marek put his hands in his pockets and took them out again about four times.**

He did not offer. He did not ask what he could do. He watched the man of about forty-seven do it nine times in about forty minutes and counted, because he is a person who counts things when he cannot do anything else, and it was nine, and the best of the nine was about nine seconds and the worst of the nine was about four.

**Nobody knew he was counting and about two of the nine people in that yard noticed him standing still and one of those two said, in about nine seconds, that he could go if he wanted, in a way that was not unkind.**

He said he would stay, and he stayed, and he counted nine.

---

**On the way out the woman of about thirty-four said that she had asked him because her own brother works on that line and had not said anything to her about his hand, and that she had found out about it from the yard.**

He said that was the wrong way round and she said she knew it was.

---

About nine people worked in that yard on that Friday and about two of the four had said nothing to anybody about it in about eleven months.

---

**What it cost him was about eleven seconds and the knowledge that being the person a stranger says a true thing to in a yard is not the same as being useful, and he has known that for eleven years and it had never before been said to him in a place where about nine people could hear it.**

**There are four rooms off that service road and one behind the other three, and from about half past six a woman of about thirty sat in the fourth of them, at her chair, with her door shut, and her page is the eighth of eight and it has been on that back shelf for two hundred days and nobody has asked her about it and nobody is going to.**
