## 2A. A CARD FOR EVERY ONE OF THE TEN DAYS, AND THIS SECTION IS NOT OPTIONAL

**761 — Monday, day 1769, entry 764. *The sheet goes on a board, nine feet from a man who is on the floor.***

Immediate goal: get a door closer on the top of a stair in a second district to stop slamming, and charge about nine pounds for it. Resistance: the corridor is outside a room that is being cleared, and the room's cleaner — **a woman of about thirty-four who keeps the books for a haulier on that road and who has signed a notice on behalf of about nine people she has met twice each** — is working with two drawing pins and a sheet of card and does not acknowledge him. Major turn: at about eleven she pins it up and he reads it upside down from the floor and it has about nine words and a date and a district's name on it and **neither of them says anything to the other.** Consequence: **he is not asked what he thinks and he is not asked to do anything, and he goes back to the shop at about half past one with the closer fitted and does not mention it at the counter.** Ending hook: **he charges a woman of about thirty-eight about nine pounds for a part he could have got at cost, because he does not want to be the man who gave something away on the day a sheet went up, and she pays, and he does not tell her why.** Required continuity: the till is still slow; the rail by that door takes two and there is a card on end in it; the new sheet is pinned by **two drawing pins** and is **not** the board on two nails that carries nineteen lines.

**762 — Tuesday, day 1770, entry 765. *The question nobody in the room can answer.***

Immediate goal: find out whether the district has to keep a local route open for the people inside it who have not themselves given notice. Resistance: the answer is not written down anywhere she has been able to look, and a man of about fifty-two wants to supply it. Major turn: **the woman of about thirty-four says, in about nine seconds and in front of about nine people, that she has looked in four places and is not going to guess**, and a man of about fifty-two is refused by her in about nine seconds with a reason about the work — a thing kept because somebody promised it in a room is not a thing anybody can plan around. Consequence: **Marek offers, in about four seconds, to look at the route himself, and is refused by her in about four seconds, with the reason that he does not hold it and she is not putting it on him.** Ending hook: the question is written on the back of a docket by somebody's hand and put in a pocket, and nobody has been told who.

**763 — Wednesday, day 1771, entry 766. *The room is open and no number is said.***

Immediate goal: nothing. He goes because it is a Wednesday and he has been going on Wednesdays for years. Resistance: **about four people have stopped coming and she has about nine other Wednesdays and nobody has asked her why.** Major turn: **a man of about forty-four asks the woman of about sixty one question, as he does, and she says in about nine seconds that she has been in that room nineteen years and has never once seen anybody leave it, and that the only thing that has ever come through that door about leaving is a bill.** Consequence: **the book does not open, no number is said, and he does not ask her why there was no number, and the reason there was no number is printed here once: a number is said at a sitting and this was not a sitting.** Ending hook: **the place behind the woman's chair is two hundred and eighty-seven days old on this page and forty-one weeks to the day, and it is printed here and on no other file of this movement, and he wanted to say the figure and did not say it.**

**764 — Thursday, day 1772, entry 767. *A reason on the back of a sheet, because the register has no column for one.***

Immediate goal: get a district's stated reason into the record in a form that is worth something. Resistance: **the register has a column for what was asked and a column for what was done and no column for a reason, because nobody ever needed one, and the woman of about forty-four who keeps the room of lists says so and is right.** Major turn: **Talia Venn says, in about nine seconds and to the room and not to him, that she can put it in a register and that a register is not a reply** — and she writes the reason on the back of the sheet in her own hand, and the sheet goes into the register with the reason on the back, and nobody turns it over. Consequence: **the institution now holds a statement it did not ask for and has no process for, and Talia's own office has to decide whether that is hers.** Ending hook: the reason is about a work shift and about a gate that opens at five and about four hundred yards of yard, and **it is not about a principle and no one in the room uses a word about one.**

**765 — Friday, day 1773, entry 768. *A hand that will not steady, and nothing to repair.***

Immediate goal: be useful at a water yard in a second district where about nine people work and one of them cannot hold a release line. Resistance: **the man of about forty-seven's right hand has not been steady since the Hush, it is not new, nobody has investigated it, and it is not a mystery.** Major turn: **she asks Marek to look at it; he looks; he says in about four seconds that there is nothing to look at, because a hand that shakes is not a part and there is no repair for it and he is a person who fixes things.** Consequence: **she says, in about nine seconds, that she knows that, and that she asked him because she did not want to be the only person in her district who had said it out loud to somebody.** Ending hook: **he is the person she said it to, and it does not help, and it is not a scene about him, and he does not charge her and she notices and neither of them says one word about that.**

**766 — Monday, day 1776, entry 769. *The procedure, and the word that has no number in it.***

Immediate goal: find the written procedure and read it out in a room. Resistance: **it has been printed since before the spring and it names nobody and it has never been run.** Major turn: **step two says a district shall state a reason; step three says the institution shall respond within a period the institution considers appropriate; and about nine people in a room cannot say who it is appropriate to, and a woman of about forty-four uses the word four times in about nine minutes and it means nothing in four mouths.** Consequence: **the only question anybody asks about the work on this page is asked by Marek and it is three words long and it goes on the back of a docket with no answer.** Ending hook: **a printed sheet with four steps on it and one of the four has no number in it, and this movement exists because of that, and nothing is decided on this Monday.**

**767 — Tuesday, day 1777, entry 770. *Money, offered in about nine seconds and refused in about nine seconds.***

Immediate goal: find out what the transition costs, because the district is leaving and has no money. Resistance: **Oren Vey offers to fund it out of one of his nine service groups for two years, on the condition that the group keeps the local route.** Major turn: **she refuses in about nine seconds with a reason about the work: a route kept by a company for money is a route the company can stop, and she would be spending two years building the thing she is trying to leave.** Consequence: **Oren says one true thing — that he would rather pay for a district to leave than for it to stay and cost him more — and nobody in the room believes him and nobody in the room can prove it, and both of those are true.** Ending hook: **the district has no money for the transition and never had, and this is the first time anybody has said so out loud, and he did not argue and has not been thanked and is not owed anything.**

**768 — Thursday, day 1779, entry 771. *A caller asks him whether he can stop a district leaving.***

Immediate goal: an ordinary Monday at a counter. Resistance: **a woman of about twenty-nine who does not give her job asks him a straight question.** Major turn: **he says no in about four seconds, and then says the harder half, which is that he could have stopped it once, in a different way, and did not.** Consequence: **she asks whether that is going to happen again and he says he does not know and does not say it will not.** Ending hook: **he wanted to be able to say yes and he cannot, and the sheet has been up six days and about four people have read it and he has not been asked about any of it.**

**769 — Friday, day 1780, entry 772. *The register of correct acts with no consequence moves from three to four.***

Immediate goal: finish the week and get the books straight. Resistance: **the notice was correct in every particular — the form, the date, the service given, the copy to the institution — and it changed nothing on that Friday, because the district is not leaving for four weeks and nobody has answered.** Major turn: **a man of about fifty-two says, in about nine seconds, that a correct act that changes nothing is the fault of whoever could have done something; she says no, in about four seconds, that it is the fault of a rule with no number in it.** Consequence: **what the fourth instance cost is a woman of about thirty-four who has been signing other people's names for eleven years and has signed one thing she chose, and about nine people in her district who were not asked and about four who have not agreed and one who has said nothing.** Ending hook: **nobody thanked anybody, and the register stands at four, and nobody in this city has counted it and nobody is going to.**

**770 — Monday, day 1783, entry 773. *The second half of the notice, and the one thing.***

Immediate goal: read what the district has served. Resistance: **the district names what it will keep doing until it goes, and it names one thing it will not keep doing, and that one thing is a practice about four hundred people in that district use to hold a release steady, and it is held by one person, and he is the man whose hand is not steady.** Major turn: **nobody in the room can find the practice on a list, and a woman of about thirty-eight says in about nine seconds that it is not on a list because nobody has ever had to write it down, and that this is the first time anybody has needed to know who holds what.** Consequence: **he writes the number of people and the name of the one person in his own book at the counter, in his own hand, and gives it to nobody, and it is the first thing he has done in this volume that nobody asked him to do.** Ending hook: **the second half of a notice is on a table and about nine people are looking at it and nobody knows what the institution is supposed to say.**
