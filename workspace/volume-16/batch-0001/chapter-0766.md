# Chapter 766 — A Monday, A Bench Until Two And A Counter Until About Six, A Room Above The Building That Receives The Returns At About Six Where A Printed Sheet With Four Steps On It Comes Off A Board, And A Word That Has Been Used Four Times In Nine Minutes By Four Different People And Does Not Mean One Thing In Any Of Them

**A Monday was a bench until two and a counter until about six, and a room above the building that receives the returns from about six. About nine people were in it. A printed sheet came off a board and it has four steps on it and about nine people read all four of them out loud.**

---

Eleven names went on that Monday's sheet and eleven dates went with them, the last at about twenty to five. The shutter came down at about ten.

**The rooms off that service road are at one thousand four hundred and fourteen days, two hundred and two weeks to the day, and the card on end in the rail by the door is one thousand four hundred and eighteen, two hundred and two weeks and four days. The rail takes two.**

**Line twelve is one thousand three hundred and thirty-four, a hundred and ninety weeks and four days. Line thirteen is one thousand two hundred and eighty-five, a hundred and eighty-three weeks and four days. The nineteenth line is one thousand one hundred and ten, a hundred and fifty-eight weeks and four days.**

Seven hundred and ninety-four days is the separation, a hundred and thirteen weeks and three days.

---

**The building that receives the returns is a landlord and a filing arrangement and it has a first floor with about nine chairs in it, and about four of the chairs work, and there is a board on the wall with about nine sheets on it and one of them is the withdrawal procedure.**

It has been printed since before the spring. It names nobody. It has never been run.

The woman of about forty-four took it off the board at about six, squared it up, and put it flat on the table in front of about nine people, and nobody touched it.

---

**It has four steps and the first is that a district may give notice in writing and the second is that a district shall state a reason.**

"**There is no column for a reason,**" said a woman of about thirty-four, "**and there has never been a column for a reason.**"

"**It says state,**" said a woman of about forty-four. "**It does not say where.**"

---

**The third step is the one the whole of this is about and it is nine words long and not one of the nine is a number.**

*the institution shall respond within a period the institution considers appropriate.*

**Nobody in that room could say how long appropriate is. About nine people read that step out and about four of them read it differently and about two of those four read it the way they would read it if they were the one who had to decide.**

The word *appropriate* was said four times in about nine minutes by four people and it did not mean one thing in any of the four.

---

**A man of about thirty-one asked whether appropriate was a word for a person or for an office, and about nine seconds afterwards somebody else asked the same question in different words, and then nobody answered either.**

A woman of about forty-four said that a period the institution considers appropriate is a period the institution chooses, and a man of about fifty-two said it was the same thing said more politely, and a woman of about thirty-eight said it was not the same thing at all, because an office choosing and a person choosing are two different things when the person goes home at the end of it.

**That was the only thirty seconds of that evening in which anybody said anything that changed what anybody else thought, and nobody thanked her for it and she was not thanked.**

---

---

**There are about nine sheets on that board and about four of them are about things that have not happened yet.**

One is about a gate. One is about a bin. One is about the closing of a room off a service road for one day a week, which has not been decided and has been on that board since before the spring.

One is about a practice held by one person, and it has been on that board since before the spring, and it has got a heading on it and no body, and about nine people in that room have read that sheet and about four of them have asked for the body and been told that there is not one.

**Nobody in that room on that Monday connected the heading on that sheet to the woman of about thirty-four who had signed something on a Monday eight days earlier, and about two of them had the same building in their head and did not say so.**

---

**A woman of about thirty-eight said that a period the institution considers appropriate was a period the institution chooses, and a man of about fifty-two said it was the same thing said more politely, and about four people agreed with the man and about four agreed with the woman and about one did not care.**

The one who does not care is a man of about sixty-one who has been coming to that room for about nine years and who said, in about nine seconds, that a period you have to name is a period you can be held to, and that nobody had ever held anybody to a period they had not named.

**He said it to the floor and not to anybody, and about nine people heard it and about four of them wrote it down and none of them wrote his name against it.**

---

**At about half past seven the woman of about forty-four took the procedure off the table, squared it up, and put it back on the board, and put a pin through the top left corner of it, and the pin went in crooked and stayed crooked.**

She did not fix it. About four people noticed and nobody said anything and it is still crooked, and it is the only thing on that board that anybody has put a crooked pin through.

---

---

**The building that receives the returns has about nine thousand returns in it a year and about four people work there and about two of the four have worked there for about nine years.**

It is a landlord. It has a counter, a stair, a first floor, nine chairs and a board on a wall.

**Nobody who works there is paid to be in that room on a Wednesday evening and about nine of them have been in it anyway on about nine occasions a year for about eleven years and that is the whole of what that institution is made of.**

---

**A man of about thirty-one asked, later, in a car park and to nobody, whether the word on that sheet was about an office or about a person, and he asked it because he had not got an answer in the room and had been thinking about it for about eleven minutes.**

A woman of about thirty-four who was walking to her car said it was about both and that was the difficulty, and then she said sorry and got in the car.

**He has not asked anybody else and he has not been told and about nine people in this city have an opinion about which one it is and about four of them have never put it in a room.**

---

---

**Nobody in that room knew what the answer to that question would be and about four of them had thought about it before they came and about nine had not.**

He had. He had thought about it for about four days, on a bench, with a bracket in his hands, and the answer he had come to was a woman who keeps a list and has kept it for about nine years, and he did not say it, and about four people in this city would have said it out loud in about nine seconds.

**That is the fifth time in eleven years that he has had an answer in about nine seconds and not said it, and about two of the other four were about people who are dead.**

---

**He asked one question in that room and it was three words long and it was the only question anybody asked on that Monday that was about the work.**

"**Who is it appropriate to.**"

It went on the back of a docket in about four seconds and the docket went into a pocket and nobody has said whose pocket and there is no answer on it anywhere and there is not going to be one in this movement.

---

**The fourth step is that a withdrawal takes effect on a date the district has given, and it is the only one of the four that anybody in that room agreed about without arguing, and about nine people agreed about it in about four seconds and then about four of them looked at each other because agreeing that fast is usually a sign that nobody has read the rest.**

He did not sign anything and no form in that room carried his name.

**There are four rooms off that service road and one behind the other three, and from about half past six a woman of about thirty sat in the fourth of them, at her chair, with her door shut, and her page is the eighth of eight and it has been on that back shelf for a hundred and ninety-seven days and nobody has asked her about it and nobody is going to.**
