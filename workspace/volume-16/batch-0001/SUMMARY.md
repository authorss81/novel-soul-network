## 3. THE FIGURES, MEASURED BY THE INSTRUMENTS BELOW, EVERY ONE WITH ITS BOUNDARY

**The instruments are printed in full at §3.1 and were run on the saved files, and the files were written and saved before any instrument was pointed at them.**

### 3.1 The instruments and their declared boundaries

1. **Two-sided interval walk** — boundary printed beside every figure: a figure is a maximal run of letters, hyphens and spaces immediately before the token `day`/`days`/`weeks`, so any punctuation stops it; a sentence is the text between two of `. ! ?` followed by whitespace, or between two newlines; inside a sentence every `weeks` token with a non-empty run to its left is a weeks figure, its tail is the next up-to-four tokens and must be `to the day`, `and one day` or `and <n> days`, and any day-token inside that tail belongs to it; each weeks figure pairs with the number-word run immediately before it or, failing that, the nearest unconsumed day-token within ninety characters; `weeks × 7 + days` must equal the figure.
2. **Arabic-digit walk** — boundary: every character of the file except the H1 line.
3. **Prohibited-term walk** — boundary: whole-word, exact term, case-insensitive, whole file including the H1. Twenty-eight terms.
4. **`right` walk** — boundary: whole-word, case-insensitive, read by hand with its context printed.
5. **Opening-paragraph count** — boundary: the first `**…**` block after the H1, counted in whitespace-separated tokens, target forty to seventy-five.
6. **Duplicated-sentence walk** — boundary: the body only, H1 excluded and the italic load-book block excluded, split on `.`/`!`/`?`, whole sentences identical across two different files at twelve tokens or more.
7. **Shared-run walk** — boundary: contiguous lowercased alphabetic tokens, target thirty-one, whole file and body separately.
8. **Words, apparatus share and hedge** — boundary: apparatus is everything from the italic load-book entry onward; body is everything above it; hedge is the eleven printed terms, whole-word, case-insensitive, over the body.
9. **Speech-line walk** — boundary: every `**"…"**` delimiter pair; a line ending inside a run is a defect.
10. **Whole-number-of-weeks vector** — computed as a per-cell modulo from the anchor table and not read off any chapter, and then compared with the files.

**Two instrument repairs, and the instrument was wrong before the text was on both occasions.**

1. **The interval walk was written four times before it was trusted, and it was wrong every time.** The number parser failed eleven of twenty-five assertions: a tens table indexed from zero, so *forty* returned 4; hyphenated tens not split; and *and* inside a thousand double-counted. **The regex then grouped an alternation without a non-capturing bracket, so `\s+and one|two|three` matched the bare word *two* with a zero-width match and a `None` group, and the walk silently stopped pairing.** The third version expanded a figure leftwards over prose and swallowed the label *Line twelve* into the figure, returning thirteen thousand three hundred and twenty-seven for one thousand three hundred and twenty-seven. **The fourth failed on the house's own elided form *a hundred and fifty-six*, which the parser rejected as unparseable and which Volume 15 uses on every one of its files.**
2. **The speech-line walk reported seven defects on the first run and all seven were its own.** The pattern `\*\*"(.+?)"\*\*` with a dotall flag paired a quote in one paragraph with a quote three paragraphs later and then reported the closing delimiter as a line ending inside a run. **A delimiter-pair check that pairs across paragraphs is not a delimiter-pair check.**

**That is the eleventh time in this repository that an instrument has been wrong before the text is, and the third time the assertion caught it rather than a second opinion, and the first time the same walk was wrong four separate ways before it was pointed at a chapter.**
