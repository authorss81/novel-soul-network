# Chapter 779 — A Friday, A Bench Until Two And A Counter Until About Six, One Of The Four Rooms Off That Service Road From About Half Past Six Where Four People Do A Sum In About Nine Minutes And Three Answers Come Back, And A Counter In Lattice Ward At About Half Past Six Where A Figure That Is Not His Goes Into A Docket Book Between Two Books About Hinges

**A Friday was a bench until two and a counter until about six, and then an evening in a room on that service road. Four people did a sum in about nine minutes and three answers came back, and one of the three came from somebody who has costed a thing before, and it is the only one that got written down.**

---

The sheet for that Friday carried ten names with ten dates beside them, and the tenth name was taken at about twenty to five. The shutter came down at about ten on that Friday as it does on every day of this stretch of days.

**Line seventeen is one thousand two hundred and four, one hundred and seventy-two weeks to the day. The post at that corridor end is nine hundred and eighty, one hundred and forty weeks to the day.**

Eight hundred and twelve days is the separation, one hundred and sixteen weeks to the day.

---

**What nobody in that district had ever done is the ordinary thing, and it is the thing a shop does every Friday without noticing, which is to say out loud what a thing costs before anybody agrees to do it.**

Nobody had asked. That is the first half of it. On that Friday a man of about thirty-eight asked, in about nine seconds and standing up, what it would take to hold that line for the people who were going to lose it, and about four people in that room had a figure and about two of them had half of one.

**About four people worked it out in about nine minutes. Three numbers came back. About nine people in that room heard all three and about four of the nine wrote one of the three down and about two of the four wrote the same one and they did not copy each other.**

---

**The first number came from the man of about thirty-eight and it was about four weeks, and it was made out of the eleven seconds and about four times a day and the fact that about four hundred people would all do it themselves if they could.**

A woman of about thirty-one said that was four hundred people who had all, at some point, put a hand on a line for about eleven seconds and not known whose hand it was, and that four hundred people is four hundred people with a habit and not with an opinion.

**Nobody argued with her and about four people in that room had thought the same and about two of them had written it down and the two of them did not write her name next to it.**

The second number came from a man of about thirty-one and it was about nine weeks, and it was made out of a training period and a rota and about four people who already know the line.

A man of about thirty-eight said that a rota in that district is made seven days ahead, so nine weeks is a rota that has been made and not a person who is going to turn up, and about four people in that room said nothing and about two of them nodded.

---

**The third number came from a woman of about thirty-six and it is the one that is written down, and she is the only person in that room who has ever costed a thing before.**

She has costed the heat in a workshop in a third district for three winters running and she keeps her figures in a hard book of her own and about four people in that city know that she does and about two of them have seen the book.

**She said the number in about nine seconds and then she said where it came from, and the second half of that is the half that matters.**

"**Eleven weeks,**" she said. "**And that is four people doing it, and none of the four has been asked, and if you go and ask them then the eleven is a different number and you will not know for about a month what it is.**"

---

**And then she said the other thing, and she said it to the table, and about nine people in that room heard it and about four of them have written it down since in about four different places.**

"**You have got a number now and it is the wrong number. It is not wrong because it is wrong. It is wrong because four people are in it who have not been told they are in it, and the only way to make it the other number is to go and ask them, and that cannot be done on a Friday.**"

**Nobody in that room asked her to cost anything else. About two of them asked her whether she would look at the sheet with the four names on it, and she said that four names is not a cost and that it is a decision, and that a decision and a cost are two different objects and this page does not put them together.**

---

**Nobody has costed this before. That is not a complaint and it is a fact about the district, and about four people in that room have said since that the district has never had a figure for anything and that this is the first one and that the first one is never the one you keep.**

A man of about fifty-two said, in about nine seconds, that a figure nobody has asked for is a figure that arrives too late to be useful and in time to be frightening, and about four people in that room agreed and about two of them said that was the wrong way round.

**Nobody wrote down which of those two was which, and about two people in that room have written down one of them and about none of the two has written down the other's name.**

---

**And the whole of it went into a docket book on a shelf behind a counter in Lattice Ward at about half past six, and the book is between two books about hinges and nobody opens it for hinges.**

The man of twenty-two wrote it in his own hand and gave it to nobody. It is a different figure from the one that is already in that book and it is on a different line and the two lines are about nine days apart.

**He said, in about four seconds, to a woman of about thirty-four who works four days a week, that it was not his figure and that there was nowhere else in that shop to put a piece of paper, and she said that was fine and did not look at it.**

The woman of about thirty-six who costed it said, when she was told on the Monday where it had gone, that a number in a docket book behind a counter is worth about as much as a number on a wall, and then she did not say anything else about it.

---

**He has decided in advance that he will not take that book out. He decided that about nine days ago about a different figure and he has not changed his mind since and about four people in this city would say that is the correct order.**

What he said on that Friday, to nobody, was that there are about nine figures in that book now that are about other people and about none of them are his, and that a book that is mostly other people's figures is not a private book and that he has never been sure what it is.

**About four people in this city would say he has just described the only arrangement in it that works and about two of them would say he has described a reason to be careful and those two have not said so out loud.**

**And the eleven weeks is not a number about money and it is not a number about days and it is about four people and about eleven weeks, and about four people in that room heard that and about two of them wrote down the four and not the eleven.**

A woman of about thirty-four said, in about nine seconds, that a figure about four people is a figure about four people and that about four hundred people do not appear in it at all, and that the four hundred are the ones who will find out on a Tuesday.

**Nobody said that the four hundred were owed anything and nobody said that they were not owed anything, and about two people in that room have said since that the four hundred are not owed a number because nobody has ever offered them one.**

---

**A man of about thirty-eight asked, at about ten past seven, whether the four people in the eleven weeks are the four who said no on the Tuesday, and about nine people in that room understood at the same moment that nobody knows whether they are.**

The four who said no are four names on a sheet. The four in the eleven weeks are four people a woman of about thirty-six did not name and has not named and would not name in a room of about nine people.

**She said, in about nine seconds, that they are nobody he has met, and that the two sets of four are not the same four and that he must not put them together, and that this is the second thing in two days that people have tried to put together that does not go together.**

He did not put them together again. About four people in that room have since put them together anyway, in about four kitchens and in about one room, and about none of the four has said so to anybody.

---

**And the last part of that Friday in that room is that about four people asked who would be told the answer, and that there is no answer to that yet, and that the answer is not in that room.**

A man of about thirty-one said that about four hundred people use the line and that about nine of them are children and that the people who would have to be told are not in that room and are not on any list that anybody in that room has.

A woman of about thirty-four said, in about nine seconds, that they are the four hundred and the nine and the four who said no, and that the four who said no already know, and that the four hundred will find out on a Tuesday, and that nobody in this city has arranged a Tuesday for them.

**About four people in that room agreed with her and about two of them did not and about none of the two said anything, and the meeting ended at about half past eight without a vote and without a minute and without anybody writing down the eleven in that room.**

---

**And a man of about thirty-one was in that room on that Friday and was about the third person from the door and he had the eleven weeks in his head before anybody said it, and he has said since that he did, and about four people have believed him and about two have not.**

He was asked where his came from and he said it came from the same three facts, and the three facts are a rota made seven days ahead and eleven seconds and a gate that opens at five.

**Nobody checked him and nobody checked her and about four people in that room now hold two numbers of about eleven weeks and about two of those four have noticed and neither of the two has said.**

---

**The rest of that Friday was a bench and a counter and about an hour and a half of a kettle that has been going for about eleven years.**

The kettle belonged to a man of about sixty-one who brought it in on that Friday with a lead that had come away and a flex that had gone soft, and he had had it in a carrier and had carried it on a bus, and he was about sixty-one and he apologised for the state of the carrier twice in about four minutes.

**He was told it would take about an hour and a half and he said that was fine and went and came back at about ten, and it cost sixteen pounds, and he said that was a decent price for the job and carried it out in the same carrier.**

A man of about fifty-two came in at about six for a switch and was not asked anything. A woman of about twenty-nine came in at about half past five and bought nine feet of cord and was not asked anything. **Neither of them has been in that shop on any other day of this stretch of days and about four people in that shop have said that the two of them together are the reason the Friday was quiet.**

**The fourth of the four rooms off that service road has a woman of about thirty sitting in it from about half past six with her door shut. What has been on the back shelf behind her for two hundred and twenty-one days is the eighth of eight, and it has not been read, and nobody is going to read it, and nobody has said one word to her about any of it.**
