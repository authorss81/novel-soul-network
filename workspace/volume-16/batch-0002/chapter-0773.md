# Chapter 773 — A Thursday, A Bench Until Two And A Counter Until About Six, A Room Off A Road In A Second District From About Five With A Council Of About Nine People At It, And A Woman Of About Twenty-Four Who Is Asked For By The Council And Not By Anybody Else And Says No In About Nine Seconds

**A Thursday was a bench until two and a counter until about six, and then a room off a road in a second district. A council of about nine people sat in it. A woman of about twenty-four was asked for by them and said no to what they wanted in about nine seconds, and they accepted it in about four.**

---

Twelve names went on that Thursday's sheet and twelve dates went with them, the last at about twenty to five. The shutter came down at about ten.

**Those four rooms off that service road stand at one thousand four hundred and twenty-four days, two hundred and three weeks and three days. The card on end in the rail by the door is one thousand four hundred and twenty-eight, two hundred and four weeks to the day.**

**Line twelve is one thousand three hundred and forty-four, one hundred and ninety-two weeks to the day. Line thirteen is one thousand two hundred and ninety-five, one hundred and eighty-five weeks to the day. Line fourteen is one thousand two hundred and sixty, one hundred and eighty weeks to the day. Line fifteen is one thousand two hundred and thirty-nine, one hundred and seventy-seven weeks to the day. The nineteenth line is one thousand one hundred and twenty, one hundred and sixty weeks to the day. The hold on nine crates is one thousand and fifty-seven, one hundred and fifty-one weeks to the day.**

Eight hundred and four days is the separation, a hundred and fourteen weeks and six days.

---

**He was in that room because there was a light on the stairs that had been going since the spring, and he was asked for by nobody, and he has been in a great many rooms this month on that basis.**

The council of that district is about nine people. It has no office. It uses a room off a road because the room is warm and the woman who opens it does not charge for it and nobody has ever asked her why she does not charge for it.

**About four of the nine had come in work clothes and about five had not, and the chair he was given was a chair that does not have a leg on it and he put his bag under it.**

---

**What they wanted was about four sentences long and it was in a form and the form had been printed in the autumn and nobody had ever read it out loud in that room before that Thursday.**

The form is what the institution sends when a district has given notice. It has three boxes. The first asks what the district will keep doing until it goes. The second asks what the district will stop doing. The third is a line with a word printed at the top of it and nothing printed under the word.

The word at the top of the third box is *limit*.

**The council wanted that word taken out and something else put in its place, and the something else was a sentence about what would be held back and for how long, and about four people in that room said the sentence out loud and about two of them said it differently.**

---

**And then a woman of about twenty-four was asked for, and she came, and nobody asked her to come twice.**

A man of about fifty-two had been in that room the week before and had put it to her in a corridor and had put it as a fact and not as a favour, and he had told the man of twenty-two about it at about half past five the same evening in about eleven words.

**She was not asked to stay. She stayed about half an hour and then she went, at about half past six, and got into a car outside, and neither she nor the man said one word to the other on the way out of the room or in the corridor or afterwards.**

---

She read the form. It took her about eleven seconds and she read the third box twice and she put a finger on the word at the top of it and left it there for about nine seconds.

Then she said no, in about nine seconds, and the reason she gave was about the work.

"**A limit that is soft is not a limit,**" she said. "**It is a sentence, and a person cannot put a shift on a sentence. You can put a shift on the third box on that form and you cannot put a shift on a sentence anybody is holding back for you.**"

---

**Nobody argued with her for about nine seconds and then the council accepted it in about four, and the accepting is worth saying properly because it is not the usual thing.**

A woman of about thirty-eight said, in about four seconds, that the council had wanted the sentence because the sentence sounded better and because about four of the nine had read the third box and thought it was harsher than the work was.

**A man of about thirty-one said that a district that has given notice does not get to decide how the form reads, and that the form is the form, and that the council was about nine people arguing with a printed sheet.**

The woman of about twenty-four said that was correct, in about four seconds, and then said that the council was still entitled to have wanted it, and that she was not going to spend an evening on whether they were.

**Nobody thanked her. Nobody thanked anybody. She was not thanked when she arrived and she was not thanked when she left and about four people in that room have described her refusal in about four different ways since.**

---

**What nobody in that room said, and what about four people in it knew, is that the third box is not a description of a feeling.**

The word at the top of it is a number with nothing in it, the same way the third step of the withdrawal procedure is a period with nothing in it, and the two are on two different walls in two different buildings and nobody in this city has ever put them in the same sentence.

**Softening what a withdrawal severs does not change what a person feels about it. It changes what a person has when they go.**

About four hundred people put a hand on a line at a water yard in a second district and about nine of them are children and about four of them are in a register kept by a woman of about forty-eight who has kept it for about nine years, and none of the four in that room on that Thursday had ever stood at that yard and all four of them knew what the third box was.

---

**The council wrote on the back of its own minute that the third box is to be left as printed, and it wrote it in about nine words, and the minute went into a folder and the folder is in a room off that road.**

Nobody read the back of it.

**That is the second sheet in about eleven days in this city with something on the back of it, and the first one is in a drawer in that same district with a reason on it, and the two have never been in the same room and nobody has asked whether they are the same sort of thing.**

---

**The council of that district has been meeting in that room for about four years and it has nine people in it and about four of the nine come every week and about five of them come when there is something on the paper.**

There is a pot on a table at the back that belongs to the building and not to the council, and about four people in that room have made tea from it about nine times a year, and nobody has ever asked the woman who opens the room whether they may.

**That is the nearest thing in that district to an institution and it is a pot and a warm room and a woman who has never charged anybody for either, and about four of the nine in that council would put all three in a paragraph if anybody asked them to and none of them has been asked.**

A man of about thirty-one said, in about nine seconds and to the middle of the table, that the council had spent about eleven minutes of a Thursday evening on a word at the top of a box, and that the district was going to lose a thing on a Thursday four weeks from now, and that about four people in that room had given up about nine hours of their own time to be sitting in it.

**Nobody said that was not so. A woman of about thirty-four who keeps the books for a haulier on that road was not in that room and has not been in it, and she is not on the council, and nobody has told her what the council wanted on that Thursday.**

---

**And the whole of what was argued in that room is now one line of a minute, and the line is the third box, and the third box is a word with a number under it that nobody has filled in.**

The number under the word *limit* on that form is not a count of people and it is not a count of days. It is what the district will not be holding on the day it goes, and it is put in by the district and not by anybody who receives the form, and about four people in that room had seen the number go in on a different form and had not thought about it once.

**About four hundred people use the thing that third box is about. About nine of them are children. The man who holds it is about forty-seven and has held it for about eleven years and has never been asked by anybody whether he will stop.**

---

**He came away at about half past six and was at the counter by about ten to seven and the counter took him until about ten, and about four people came in and about two of them wanted him to say what had been said in that room and he told them what had been said in that room and nothing else.**

One of them asked whether the council had been told what it cost. He said he did not know and that it had not been costed, and that nobody had asked for it to be costed, and that he had not offered.

---

**The bench part of that Thursday was a light fitting over a door in a first district that had been going on and off since the autumn, and it wanted a new switch and not a new fitting, and it was nine pounds, and about four minutes, and the man who brought it said an electrician had told him the same thing in the spring and had not come back.**

**A man of about twenty-eight brought a bag of parts on that Thursday as well, about nine days after he had brought one and been told no, and he put the bag on the counter and asked whether anybody in that shop could look at it, and he was told yes by a woman of about thirty-four who works there four days a week, and she looked at it for about nine minutes and gave it back and did not say why.**

---

**Behind the other three of those four rooms there is a fifth, and from about half past six a woman of about thirty sits in it at her chair with the door shut. What is on the back shelf is the eighth of eight and it has been there two hundred and eight days, and nobody in this city has put a question to her about it and nobody is going to.**
