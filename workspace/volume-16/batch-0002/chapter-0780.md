**What the about four people in that room wanted to say was that the four who said no have been let down, and not one of the four could get to it in a sentence.**

**And then the woman of about thirty-four said it in about nine seconds, and she said it to the table, and what she said was that the refusal is the procedure working.**

"**The four of them did the only thing this procedure has ever been able to do,**" she said. "**Nobody had to sign anything. They did not sign. The sheet says so and the sheet will go with nine names on it and that is what a procedure is for.**"
