# Chapter 313 — The Division

The Thursday morning of week one hundred and twelve was the morning about nine crews of eleven read a piece of paper in a bay behind a workshop and went out, and about four did not, and the four that did not are the same four that did not go out a week ago, and they are the four whose people have been with them more than about four years, and nobody took a vote, and the division was not about the weather, and a lead repairer of forty-five said in about nine words that a page in another building is not one of the people who decides.

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date. Four jobs attended and one shift change at that workshop which is not a job and has not been on this docket in about nine years.

The word was at nine and ten and reached eleven for about an hour in the middle of the morning. Four names on a lease cost more than they did. The room off the service road is three hundred and eleven days old and is warm.

---

The bay has four benches and a yard behind it and about eleven crews, and the crews go out in the morning and come back in the afternoon, and a lead repairer of forty-five wears a metal braid of tools under her shirt and writes warnings on the backs of forms because she does not trust neat records that fit on one page.

A copy of the instrument was on the middle bench at about half past six and it had been there since about nine the night before and nobody in that yard had put it there, and there is no form in this case that says who.

She read it out. She read all of it, both sides, in about four minutes, including the reference, which she could not pronounce and read as about nine characters with a pause in the middle of it.

---

Then about nine crews went out.

**Nobody took a vote and there was no meeting and no list and no petition and nobody instructed anybody, and about four of the eleven crews did not send anybody out, and they are about the same four as last week, and that is the finding.**

Nobody in that bay said the sentence. A crew lead of about thirty-four from the second crew said it in about nine seconds instead, to the bay and not to the lead repairer:

"**That page does not put anybody in the room at the end of it. Last Thursday the woman standing at this bench told us the person who owns the ending is the person who does the ending, and that has been the shape of it for nine years, and it is not on a page, and the page has not got a person on it.**"

Nobody improved on it. She said it was the correct shape of the problem and it was not the problem, which is what she said last Thursday and word for word, and then she said the other half, which was new.

"**Nine crews read that page and went out, and four read it and did not, and the four are the four I would have said in advance would not, and I have taught those four for nine years and I have never once been able to say why the ones who stay are the ones who stay.**"

---

Her cost, named out loud, in her own words, at about ten past seven, to about nine people in a bay, unasked for, and it is the only cost anybody named that morning:

"**On Thursday I said I would rather be wrong about a person than right about a pump, and about four people in this yard heard me say it, and two of the four crews that are not going out this morning have been carrying that sentence around since Thursday and using it as a permission, and I have not given it to them, and I am not going to, because it is not a permission, it is a thing I said about myself on a Thursday morning. And they have taken the worst four years of my life and put my name on it, and if one of them gets hurt this week then my name is on that as well, and that is the first time in nine years that anything anybody has done in this bay has had my name on it that I did not choose to put there.**"

Nobody said anything. A man at the back said that was not fair, and she said that it was not, and that it was also not the point, and went to the middle bench.

---

She was right about one thing and wrong about another and both of them are on the page.

**Right:** she said on Thursday morning, in that bay, that in about four days this room would want a piece of paper about what was happening, and the piece of paper arrived, and she was right, and nobody improved on it and she was not thanked for being right in advance.

**Wrong:** she said the paper would be about ninety, and that the nine would go into it, and the paper is about nine sites in four districts, and the nine on a bench in a stairwell in the Lower Wards on a Thursday of last week are not in it and are not in anything, and about four people in that bay have worked that out and about four have not.

She said both of those things out loud, in that order, at about half past seven, and said she had been wrong about the number and right about the paper and that she would take that.

Nobody thanked her. Nobody said she was honest. A man of fifty-one with a tool bag was in the second bay and was not a crew and was not there for that and had a stair door in a building in that district, and he said nothing, as he does.

---

**The two crews that went out to the nine sites did the correct thing, both of them, and it changed nothing, and neither of them is owed anything.**

They attended nine sites in four districts between about seven and about half past nine. Every one of the nine was attended. Every one was attended by a crew that had a job number and a form and the second half of the report, which is the thing a lead repairer of forty-five requires and which she told the bay on Thursday she would ask for and would not be able to check.

They found: about nine releases that had not arrived, at nine sites, in four districts, which is the count on the page, and the count on the page is right. **The count on the page is right and the page names a person for coordinating it and the two facts came from two entirely different directions and the crews could not tell anybody which of the nine sites the person named has ever been inside, and neither can the person named.**

The report went back on the forms. The forms went onto the middle bench. The middle bench is not a record and nobody keeps it, and in about four days it will be a piece of paper in a drawer in a building this case has never been inside, and about four hundred competent people in this city can invoke a printed rule about the doing of this and not one of them is on a list.

A crew lead of about thirty-four said, on the yard at about half past ten, in about nine seconds, that they had done everything they were supposed to do and that doing it had told them nothing they did not already know, and that the thing they had learned was that a crew can be right about nine sites and have nothing to say to the man in the second bay.

Nobody improved on it. Nobody thanked her.

---

About four of the eleven crews did not go out, and they will go out tomorrow, and they went out on the Friday before this, and they will be the four that the bay is, in about four weeks, calling the sensible ones.

Nobody took a vote about them. Nobody in that yard is to ask them to stop.

---

A room off a service road was warm at half past six. A first-year of nineteen read the copy that got to that room, which is not the copy in the bay and is not the sheet in that office, and said one sentence about it in about nine seconds, to the room and to nobody in it.

"**Four crews did not go out and about nine people are not going to be told that they did not, and in about four days a person is going to be asked whether the order was right, and the answer is going to be that four crews decided it was wrong, and about four people in that bay have just done a correct thing for the second time in a fortnight.**"

Nobody improved on it. Nobody thanked her. She sat down.

A woman of about thirty was in the chair nearest the door and was not asked anything and was not thanked and was not apologised to, and the reason she is in that room is the temperature, and the temperature costs more than it did, and both of those are correct.

*316. Thursday of week 112, at about half past six at a counter in a room with two pieces of paper on it, and this entry is nine crews, four that did not, one correct morning, and a name on a page.*

*Conditions and docket.* **Four jobs attended. A shift change at a workshop behind four bays is not on this docket and has not been on one in about nine years. About eleven crews; about nine went out; about four did not; no vote, no meeting, no list, no petition, nobody instructed anybody. Nine sites attended in four districts between about seven and about half past nine, every one of them, with job numbers and with the second half of each report, which is the half that records what was not found. The rate held at nine and ten and reached eleven for about an hour in the middle of the morning. The heating is on and costs more and no figure of days off is carried. The room off the service road is three hundred and eleven days old. A card in a rail is three hundred and fifteen. The hardboard: twelve lines two hundred and thirty-one, thirteen one hundred and eighty-two, fourteen one hundred and forty-seven, fifteen one hundred and twenty-six, sixteen one hundred and one, seventeen eighty-three, eighteen twenty-nine, nineteen seven. Wall unchanged: nineteen lines in thirteen hands and fourteen names. No line added, none removed, none rubbed off. The first line is a cook's and is still the first line. Eight items in a ring binder, unopened, unread. The woman's page is unread and this entry is not the reason. The master's fifth line is blank and the drawer was not opened.**

*The division, and what it is not, as a record.* **Site: four bays and a yard, about eleven crews. Document: a copy of a Continuity Office instrument, on the middle bench from about nine the previous evening, put there by nobody anybody in that yard can identify; no form in this case says who and none was created. Read out in full, both sides, about four minutes, by the lead repairer of forty-five, including a reference she could not pronounce. Result: about nine crews out, about four in. **The four that did not are the four that did not go out on the previous Thursday, and they are the four whose people have been with them more than about four years, and no document in this city records that and none was created and there was no vote on either Thursday.** Her statement, in about nine words: nine years of teaching those four and no account of why the ones who stay are the ones who stay. Nobody improved on it. **The division is not about the weather, it was not about the weather last week, and the same four did the same thing under a spoken instruction and under a printed one, which is the finding of this entry. The sentence about a release and the person who owns it was said out loud once in this volume, in this bay, a week ago, and is not said again by anybody here, and this record does not carry its wording, because a record that carries it is a second saying: the argument is carried instead by a crew lead of about thirty-four, in about nine seconds, to the bay and not to her — that a page puts nobody in the room at the end of a practice — and by the standing instruction of the previous Thursday, which is not on a page, and which holds that the person who owns the ending does the ending. A page has not got a person on it.**

*Her cost, and two claims, one right and one wrong.* **Named aloud, unasked, at about ten past seven, to about nine people, in about nine words and then more. Items: a sentence said on the previous Thursday in this bay about preferring to be wrong about a person than right about a pump; about four people heard it; two of the four crews now sitting it out have been carrying it since Thursday as a permission; she has not given it and is not going to; it is not a permission but a thing she said about herself; they have put her name on the worst four years of her life; if one of them is hurt this week her name is on that too; and this is the first time in nine years that anything done in this bay has carried her name that she did not choose. Not thanked. A man at the back said it was not fair; she said it was not and that it was not the point. **Right about one thing: the previous Thursday she said this room would want a piece of paper in about four days, and one arrived. Wrong about another: she said the paper would be about ninety and that the nine would go into it; the paper is about nine sites in four districts, the nine on a stairwell bench are in nothing, and about four people in that bay have worked that out and about four have not. She gave both out loud in that order and said she would take that.**

*Nine sites, and a correct morning that reached nothing.* **Attended: nine, in four districts, about seven to about half past nine, by two of the nine crews that went out, with job numbers issued, forms completed, and the second half of every report present, which is the half that records what was not found and which she required on the previous Thursday on the ground that a crew asked afterwards for it will invent it and she will not be able to tell. Found: about nine releases not arrived, at nine sites, which is the count on the page, and the count is right. Not found: any person, any mechanism, any cause, any connection between the nine sites and any individual anywhere. **The count on the page is right and the page names a person to coordinate it, and the two arrived by unrelated routes, and neither crew could say which of the nine sites the person named has been inside, and he has been inside none of them and does not know he is on the page.** A crew lead of about thirty-four, on the yard at about half past ten, about nine seconds: everything they were supposed to do, nothing they did not know, and one thing learned — a crew can be right about nine sites and have nothing to say to the man in the second bay. Not improved on. Not thanked. The forms are on a bench that is not a record and in about four days will be paper in a drawer in a building this case has never entered, where about four hundred competent people can invoke a printed rule about the doing of this and not one of them is on a list. Owed nothing. Not villains. **Second correct action in a fortnight by people who are not villains, after the plant earlier this month and the premises confirmation two chapters ago.**

*What a first-year of nineteen said, and the four crews.* **Standing, in about nine seconds, to a room and to nobody in it: four crews did not go out and about nine people are not going to be told they did not; in about four days a person will be asked whether the order was right; the answer will be that four crews decided it was wrong; and about four people in that bay have just done a correct thing for the second time in a fortnight. Not improved on. Not thanked. **Her name is in the narrator's sentence and in no load book, no document, no surface and no mouth in this chapter, and she gave it to nobody in that room.** The wall is seven days old and was not written on. About four of the eleven crews did not go out, will go out tomorrow, went out on the Friday before this, and will be the four this yard calls the sensible ones in about four weeks. Nobody took a vote about them and nobody is to ask them to stop.**

*What the day did not touch.* **He taught, demonstrated, assessed and assisted nothing, and a bay behind a workshop is not a practice in that department and the reason is not that he is the exception. No panel, no marker, no relay, nothing run, nothing forwarded. The printed rule was not mentioned by anybody in that bay and about four hundred competent people in this city can still invoke it. The Exchange did not sit and its next sitting is in a fortnight. The secondment is unchanged and the chair of forty-nine has still not been told. The heating is on, costs more, and the fortnight is unpaid and unresolved, and four crews went out into a bad week again without any of that being mentioned to any of them. The hardboard was not written on. The woman's page is unread. The standing question is not stated here. The question written once on a service road in week one hundred and eight is not answered here and is not in anybody's mouth. No message arrived from anybody. Nobody thanked anybody. Entry 316.**
