# Chapter 318 — Not Going

The Monday morning of week one hundred and fourteen was the morning a man of fifty-eight with a bad hip was asked by a competent person with a good reason to go to a district he has never worked in and be the person who can decide, and he said one line and did not go, and the reason for the asking was better than the not going, and nobody thanked him and nobody asked him why and he is not to be asked; and on the same evening a person of about twenty-six came to a room off a service road and asked that room what it thought, which is the first time in about four years that anybody has come back to it and asked a second time about anything.

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date. Five jobs attended and one address not attended and the address is the one that has been not attended for about a fortnight.

The word was at nine and ten. Four names on a lease cost more than they did. The room off the service road is three hundred and twenty-two days old and is warm and about four people came in on the Sunday evening for the temperature and one of them is a woman of about thirty who was not asked anything about that either.

---

The telephone call came to him at about ten past nine in the morning, at home, and the caller is a person of about thirty-four with an ordinary job in a district office in the second of the four districts, and she had a piece of paper in front of her and she read off it, which is the correct way to do it.

She said that about four buildings in that district had a release that was not coming, that nobody in that district knew what to do about it, and that the district had nobody local who could be in a stairwell at eleven at night and decide whether a stranger was allowed to finish what they had started.

**Then she said why she was ringing him, and it was a good reason, and it is better than his not going.**

She said that a man in a room in another part of the city had said, out loud, in front of about nine people, that there were about four buildings in a district he had never worked in where a release was not coming and nobody local knew it, and that she had been told about that by a person of sixty who has never once lied to him, and that she was ringing him because in this city there is no list of anybody and he was the only name anybody had.

---

He was in the corner of a room off a service road at half past six that evening and had been since about half past five and had not had a cup of tea, as he does, and he said one line and did not go.

"**That is my sentence from Thursday and I gave it to a room and not to a district, and I am not going to be one.**"

Nobody said anything. Nobody asked him why. Nobody improved on it and nobody thanked him and he had said two things in about four years and the second one was a question that a man of twenty-two said yes to, and the third of the five things he has been asked about is one he refused in an earlier term and nobody has put it to him again and nobody is going to.

**The reason he gave is in his own words and is about four years of being a man people can find, and it is not recorded in this entry as anything except what he said, and it is not to be asked about a second time.**

---

A person of about twenty-six came to that room at about half past six on the same evening.

She had come about forty minutes on a bus. She is not a mechanism and holds no post and is owed nothing. She works in a district office in the first of the four districts, she has a clipboard, and she has one question, and it is the correct question, and she asked it standing up in about nine seconds.

"**What does this room think about the instrument.**"

Nobody improved on the question. There is a sheet with a district office's mark on the counter that has been there a fortnight, and a document with a reference in a form nobody in that room can read, and about nine people were in the room, and about four of them had already read the second one.

---

**The answer was given in about four minutes and it was given by about four people and it was not one answer, and the woman of twenty-six wrote all of it down in her own words and did not summarise it and read it back before she left, and both of those are correct.**

A man of fifty-one with a tool bag said what the room could do, which was about nine people who turn up, and about four folding chairs, and a kettle, and that he had been in that room on about nine Fridays in four years and could not remember what the room had ever been asked and would not have said.

A cook of twenty-seven said what the room could not do, which was that it could not find anybody, because it has never had a list and has refused every offer of one, and because a room that can find a person is a room that can be asked to produce a person.

A first-year of nineteen said the thing that is the finding of this entry, in about nine seconds, to the room and to the woman with the clipboard and to nobody in particular:

"**There is a piece of paper on this counter that has his name on it and about four of us read it and nobody chose us, and you have come to find out what we think, and we are going to tell you, and the reason we are going to tell you is not that we trust you. It is that a room that has answered a question once is a room that can be asked again, and if we do not answer this one on a Monday then somebody in your office is going to write down on a form that we were asked and did not reply, and that is a worse thing to have on a piece of paper than an answer.**"

She said the last part at about half past six, and a woman of about thirty-one with a clipboard wrote it down in her own words, and nobody thanked the first-year of nineteen and she did not ask to be thanked and has never asked.

---

**The cost, named out loud, in her own words, at about four minutes to seven, by a man of fifty-eight with a bad hip who had said one line at about ten past six and had not been asked for another and was asked for nothing all evening:**

"**About four years ago I could not be found at all, and then I could be found by about four people who knew me, and then I could be found by anybody with a piece of paper, and every one of those has been a good thing for somebody and the last one has cost a man of twenty-nine his afternoon and a woman of twenty-four her morning and me a telephone call at ten past nine this morning, and I am not saying which of them I would give back.**"

Nobody said anything. Nobody improved on it. Nobody thanked him, and he had said two things in about four years, and this was not one of the two and nobody counted it and nobody is to count it.

---

The woman of twenty-six read back what she had written, at about seven, and it was about nine lines, and it was in her own words and it was about a room and about a kettle and about a man who could not remember what the room had been asked, and about a woman who could not find anybody and did not want to, and about a first-year of nineteen who had said out loud that a room that has answered a question once is a room that can be asked again.

**There is no form in this case for that. It is on a clipboard in a building this case has never been inside, and it is in her own words, and about four people in that room are not in it and are not to be added to it and were not asked whether they wished to be in it.**

The room now has three pieces of paper in it. The one with a district office's mark and a ruled line with nothing on it. The one with a reference in a form nobody in that room can read. And the fact that somebody has come and been told, which is not a piece of paper and is the first of its kind.

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.

*321. Monday of week 114, at about ten on a service road, and this entry is one telephone call, one not-going, one question asked of a room, and the first answer this room has ever given anybody.*

*Conditions and docket.* **Five jobs attended, one address not attended, and that address has been not attended for about a fortnight. About nine people in the room off a service road from half past six. A telephone call at about ten past nine in the morning to a man of fifty-eight at home, from a person of about thirty-four in a district office in the second of the four districts, reading off a piece of paper. A visitor from a district office in the first of the four districts at about half past six, about forty minutes on a bus, one question, a clipboard, about nine lines written in her own words and read back before she left. The rate held at nine and ten. The heating is on and costs more and no figure of days off is carried. The room off the service road is three hundred and twenty-two days old. A card in a rail in Lattice Ward is three hundred and twenty-six days in the rail, a third object, and is not in that room and is not in a room above a line. The hardboard: twelve lines two hundred and forty-two, thirteen one hundred and ninety-three, fourteen one hundred and fifty-eight, fifteen one hundred and thirty-seven, sixteen one hundred and twelve, seventeen ninety-four, eighteen forty, nineteen eighteen. Wall unchanged: nineteen lines in thirteen hands and fourteen names, none added, none removed, none rubbed off. The first line is a cook's and is still the first line. Eight items in a ring binder, unopened, unread. The woman's page is unread and the room was asked a question on Monday evening and she was not asked anything and is not in the answer. The master's fifth line is blank and the drawer was not opened. The Exchange sat on Wednesday and its next sitting is in a fortnight.**

*The asking, and the reason for it, and the not going, as a record.* **One call, about ten past nine in the morning, to a man of fifty-eight with a bad hip, at home, from a person of about thirty-four with an ordinary job in a district office in the second of the four districts, reading from a piece of paper, which is correct. Four items, in her order: about four buildings in that district with a release not coming; nobody local knowing what to do; no local person who can be in a stairwell at eleven at night and decide whether a stranger may finish what they started; and the reason for the call — a statement made out loud in a room in another part of the city, in front of about nine people, about four buildings in a district he has never worked in; a report of that statement by a person of sixty who has never once lied to him; and the fact that there is no list of anybody in this city and he was the only name available. **The reason is competent, the reason is good, and the reason is better than the not going; all three are true and none is improved on here.** Answer: no, one line, about half past six, in the corner of a room off a service road, by a man who had been there since about half past five and had not had a cup of tea. Subject of the line: that it is his own sentence from the Thursday, that he gave it to a room and not to a district, and that he is not going to be one. He did not go. No question was asked. No improvement. No thanks. **He has said two things in about four years. The second was the question on the Thursday of week one hundred and eleven that a man of twenty-two said yes to. The third of the five things he has been asked about is one he refused in an earlier term, has not been put to him since, and is not going to be. His Monday reason concerns about four years of being findable, is his own, and is not to be asked about twice. Nobody thanked him and nobody is to thank him; he has said he will not be.**

*The question asked of a room, and the answer, as a record and not as an account.* **Question: one, standing up, about nine seconds, asked by a visitor of about twenty-six who travelled forty minutes on a bus, from a district office in the first of the four districts, and it was correct and was not improved on by anybody. **First occasion on which anybody has returned to that room to ask a second time about anything, in about four years; the sheet on that counter had been there a fortnight; the cost of not refusing that sheet was named in that room a fortnight earlier and was not wrong.** Speakers: about four, in about four minutes, and they did not agree and were not asked to. One, a man of fifty-one with a tool bag: what the room can do — nine people who turn up, about four folding chairs, a kettle, and a recollection of about nine Fridays in four years in which the room was asked nothing. Two, a cook of twenty-seven: what the room cannot do — it holds no list, has declined every offer of one, and a room able to produce a person is a room that can be asked to produce a person. Three, a first-year of nineteen, to the room and the visitor and no one in particular, in about nine seconds: a document naming him is on the counter, about four people read it, nobody chose them; the room will answer; the reason is not trust; an unanswered question gets written on a form, and a form is a worse thing to hold than an answer. Her reason for answering was a document, and she named it, and it was the cost she named a fortnight ago. Handled: her own words, no summary, read back before she left, about nine lines. **No form in this case exists for the answer. It sits on a clipboard in a building this case has never entered. About four people in that room are not in it, were not asked, and are not to be added.***

*The cost, and the second thing said in that room on a Monday evening, which is not to be counted.* **Named aloud, unasked, at about four minutes to seven, by a man of fifty-eight with a bad hip, who had said one line at about ten past six and had been asked for nothing since: that about four years ago he could not be found at all; that then he could be found by about four people who knew him; that then he could be found by anybody with a piece of paper; that every one of those has been a good thing for somebody; that the last one has cost a man of twenty-nine his afternoon, a woman of twenty-four her morning, and him a telephone call at ten past nine; and that he is not saying which of them he would give back. Nobody said anything. Nobody improved on it. Nobody thanked him. **This is not one of the two things he has said in about four years and nobody counted it and nobody is to count it, and it is the third statement in this entry and it is not a third sentence and it is not a thing he owes anybody.**

*The three things in that room after Monday, and what they are.* **One: a large sheet with a district office's mark in the corner and a ruled line under the mark carrying no characters, present on that counter since a Friday a fortnight ago, in a plain envelope, delivered by hand, asking the reader to note a condition and naming no cause and no person. Two: a document with a mark at the top and a reference in a form nobody in that room can read aloud correctly, a count, a reason, a date on the letter, and one name in a body at the foot of the second side, put on that counter on the Monday of week one hundred and twelve. Three: **the fact that somebody has come to that room and asked it a question and been answered, which is not a piece of paper and is the first of its kind and has no mark on it and no reference and is owed by nobody.** The first two are not the same kind of object. The third is not an object anybody can require. Nobody has refused any of the three and nobody has accepted any of the three and about four people in that room have a different view of each one and none of the three has been discussed at length by anybody.**

*What the day did not touch.* **He taught, demonstrated, assessed and assisted nothing. No panel, no marker, no relay, nothing run, nothing forwarded. The printed rule was not mentioned and about four hundred competent people in this city can still invoke it and that number is not on a list. The Exchange did not sit. The secondment is unchanged and the chair of forty-nine has still not been told. The heating is on, costs more, and the fortnight is unpaid and unresolved. The hardboard was not written on. The woman's page is unread and the room was asked a question and she was not asked anything and is not a category and is not counted. The standing question is not stated here. The question written once on a service road in week one hundred and eight is not answered here and is not in anybody's mouth. No message arrived from anybody and a visitor with a clipboard is not a message. Nobody thanked anybody. Entry 321.**
