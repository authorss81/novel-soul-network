# Chapter 314 — The Word Itself

The Friday evening of week one hundred and twelve was the third Friday rota in this term, in a company training room at the end of a passage in Crown Terrace with nine folding chairs in it and about eleven people in it, and for about four seconds at the end of the last run nobody in that room said the word, because the woman of thirty-six who says it had gone to a counter at the back for about four seconds, and in that gap a woman of about forty-one who has been coming for about four years and is on no list at all said it herself, out loud, to nobody, and then about four other people said it.

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date. A shift in a training room is not a job and has never been on this docket and about four hundred of these Fridays are on no surface in this city.

The word was at nine and ten. The room off the service road is three hundred and twelve days old and is warm and costs more.

---

The room has nine folding chairs, a laminated sheet on a post, a counter at the back, and a bell-push of its own that nobody presses. There is a company name on the door of the building and the company is not the company that assembles anything and is not the author of anything and is a customer.

About eleven people were in it at half past five. Seven of them are on the company's list, which is a card in a box on a table and has been for about four years, and about four of them are on no list at all, which is the standing figure and the number is on the back of one woman's own card in a drawer in a flat in Nine Bridges and is on no form.

A woman of thirty-six with a lanyard ran it, and has run it for about four years, and is not a mechanism and holds no post and is owed nothing.

He was in the fourth row against the wall and he has been in the fourth row against the wall on about four hundred of these Fridays.

---

The last run of the evening is the one everybody does at the end. It is a hold with a count in it and a release that is supposed to arrive when the instructor says the word. She says the word at the same point every Friday. The word is not on the laminated sheet. The word has not been on the laminated sheet in about four years and about four people have asked her for it and she has said no four times.

At the end she went to the counter at the back for the tally sheet, and the bell-push is by the counter, and somebody in the last row put a hand near it and did not press it, and she was about four seconds away.

Nobody said the word for about four seconds.

**And the release arrived in that gap, from the room, and not from her.**

A woman of about forty-one, on no list, who has been coming for about four years and has not spoken in that room in the whole of that time, said it out loud in the flat voice of a person saying a thing they have said four hundred times, and about four other people said it with her, about half a second apart, and nobody in that room looked at anybody.

Then she came back from the counter and said the word herself, about four seconds later, and it was over, and about nine people got up.

---

Nobody said anything about it for about nine seconds.

Then the woman of about forty-one said, to the chairs and not to anybody, in about four words, that she had not known she was doing that.

Nobody asked her why. Nobody improved on it. The instructor of thirty-six said nothing at all about it, then or afterwards, and she has been asked for a reason by about four people in this movement and she gave one the last time and nobody asked her a second time and she did not give it again.

**She did not write it down. She was not asked to write it down. She has not been asked to write it down and there is no sheet in that room with two columns on it and there is not going to be one, and the reason she gave the last time she was asked is in a room about four weeks ago and it has not been given again and is not improved on here.**

---

A man of thirty-four who buys training for the company that runs about four of the rooms in an audit said one thing at the door, at about half past seven, and it was not to anybody.

"**There are seven of you on that list and there are seven of you on that list and about four more of you in this room on no list at all, and I have been in this building for about two terms and I have never once seen the word written down anywhere and I have now seen a person say it who has never been asked to, and I am not going to tell you what that means, and I am not going to write it down, and both of those are mine to decide and I have decided them, and I am not going to be the man in the lanyard and I am not going to stop buying the training either.**"

He put a card in the box on the table. He was in it about nine seconds. Nobody stopped him and nobody thanked him and he is owed nothing.

---

**The protagonist noticed all of it and did not write any of it down, and this is the third occasion in about a fortnight on which he has had a true thing in his hands and not written it, and he has not given the three of them a name and has not said a sentence about them to anybody, and nobody has noticed him not writing.**

What he did do, at about half past seven, in the corridor, was ask one question of a man of twenty-nine who works on lifts, who was in the room and has been in that room for about four years and is not on the company's list and has never been on it.

"**Were you in the room on the Friday of week one hundred and two.**"

"**Yes.**"

"**I am not asking about that. I am asking whether the word was said by anybody else in that room on that Friday.**"

"**No.**"

"**Right.**"

Nobody improved on it. Nobody thanked him. The man of twenty-nine said that he had been about to say something else and had decided not to, and he did not say it then and has not said it since.

---

The four seconds are settled for one Friday in an earlier term and are not to be re-opened and are not called solved. **This is a third Friday and is not that Friday, and a second Friday in the last fortnight was a different thing entirely and is not joined to this one, and the two are not to be joined to each other or to this.** The first time a run did something nobody expected, it finished about four seconds early and nobody in the room said anything. The second time, a man did the run backwards with the count started at the other end and it worked. The third time, the room said the word.

Three Fridays, three different shapes, and no two of them have been put together by anybody, and a woman of thirty-six has declined to put them together twice, and the not-putting-together is the only procedure in this entry.

Nobody thanked anybody. About four people in that room went out into a street in the dark at about half past seven and one of them said the word on the way, quietly, to nobody, and nobody heard it but a man of twenty-nine who works on lifts, who has not said anything about it and is not going to.

*317. Friday of week 112, at about ten on a service road, and this entry is four seconds of an empty room, one word said by the wrong person, and a third Friday.*

*What happened at the end of the last run, in the order it happened.* **Run: the last of the evening, a hold with a count, a release supplied by an instructor who says a word at the same point every Friday. The word is not on the laminated sheet and has not been for about four years and has been asked for four times and refused four times. The instructor went to a counter at the back for a tally sheet at the moment the run ended. The bell-push is by that counter. A hand went near it and did not press it. Distance: about four seconds, and the absence was an ordinary human absence and not a fault and not a fault of any kind and is not connected to the weather and no character in this entry has connected it to anything. In the gap: a woman of about forty-one, on no list, four years' attendance, no speech in that room in the whole of that time, said the word out loud, once, flat, in the voice of a person saying a thing four hundred times; and about four other people said it with her, roughly half a second apart; and nobody in that room looked at anybody. That is the whole of the finding and it is the finding of a room and not of a city, and it does not name a cause, and no name in this case is attached to it, and there is no mechanism in it and there is no device in it.***

*Three Fridays, and no two of them joined.* **First, an earlier term: a run finished about four seconds before the expected point and nobody in the room said the word. **Those four seconds are settled for one Friday and are not to be re-opened by anybody and are not called solved.**** Second, a fortnight ago: a man of twenty-nine who works on lifts did the run with the count started at the other end, it worked, and he said the true thing out loud in about nine seconds and nobody improved on it. Third, this evening: the room said the word. **The second Friday is not the first Friday and this one is not the second Friday, and the three are not to be joined to one another by any writer or any character, and the only procedure in this entry is that they have not been joined.** A woman of thirty-six with a lanyard has declined to put anything together twice. **No count of days is taken between the three and none is printed, and no interval phrase of any kind is used in this movement.***

*What was not written down, and by whom, and the reason that was not given again.* **The instructor of thirty-six did not write it down, was not asked to write it down, and has not been asked. **The reason she gave the last time she was asked for one, in a room about four weeks ago, was given in her own words and concerned the moment a sheet exists with two columns on it, and about four hundred people in this city being handed that sheet by somebody who has never done a run in his life. That reason is not repeated in this chapter, is not improved on, and is not asked for a second time by anybody in this entry.** There is no sheet in that room and there is not going to be one. About four hundred is a figure that is not a rate and is on no form, and it is not the same four hundred as anything else in this case, and the two are not to be converted into one another. A card in a box is a third object and is not the wall and is not the rail and is not the master, and no sentence in this entry merges the three.**

*What two people said at a door and in a corridor, and the third occasion.* **At the door, at about half past seven, a man of thirty-four who buys training for the company, to nobody: that there are seven on the list and there were seven on the list and about four more in the room on no list; that he has been in that building for about two terms and has never once seen the word written down anywhere; that he has now seen a person say it who has never been asked to; that he is not going to say what that means; that he is not going to write it down; that both of those are his to decide and he has decided them; that he is not going to be the man in the lanyard; and that he is not going to stop buying the training. Card returned to the box: one, about nine seconds, unobserved. Not thanked. In a corridor, at about half past seven, a man of twenty-nine who works on lifts was asked two questions by a man of twenty-two: whether he was in the room on the Friday of week one hundred and two, and whether the word had been said by anybody else in that room on that Friday. Answers: yes, and no. The asker then said one word, which was *right*, and stopped, and did not explain it. Not improved on. Not thanked. The man of twenty-nine said he had been about to say something else and had decided not to; he did not say it then and has not said it since. **The man of twenty-two did not write any of this evening down anywhere: not on a docket, not in his own book, not on a service road. This is the third such occasion in about a fortnight. He has not given the three of them a name, has said a sentence about them to nobody, and nobody has noticed him not writing.***

*What the day did not touch.* **He taught, demonstrated, assessed and assisted nothing; he was in the fourth row and he asked two questions. No panel, no marker, no relay, nothing run by him, nothing forwarded. No practice was taught, demonstrated, assessed or assisted by anybody in that room, and a company training room is not a practice in that department and the reason is not that he is the exception. The printed rule was not mentioned and nobody in that room knows the number of people in this city who can invoke it. The Exchange did not sit and its next sitting is a Wednesday in a fortnight. The secondment is unchanged. The heating is on, costs more, and the fortnight is unpaid and unresolved. The hardboard was not written on. The woman's page is unread.
