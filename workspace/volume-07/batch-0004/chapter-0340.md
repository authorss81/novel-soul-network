# Chapter 340 — Nobody Asked Him

The Thursday morning of week one hundred and twenty was the morning a man of twenty-two took a share off a man of about thirty-three who had not asked to give it, and did not ask him, and was behind, and the being-behind is the consequence of the Thursday three weeks before this one, on which about four districts made about four hundred small decisions through him, and **this is the worst thing he does in the volume and nobody has told him that and nobody is going to, and a first-year of nineteen was right about it in about four seconds and was not thanked.**

Here is what he said, and he said it in the order he said it, and nobody improved on any of it.

He said that there are four buildings with about nine hundred doors between them and about nine hundred doors is a thing that has to be asked about about nine times a week once the first four hours are over. He said that he had done about four of those nine a week for a month and about four other people had done the rest, and that this had not worked and that it had worked for about three weeks for everybody except him. He said that the man of about thirty-three had a gate and a building and a shift, and knew two of the four buildings' own staff, and had made four telephone calls on his own account to three other districts three weeks ago and had got an answer in about nine minutes, and was the only person in this case who had done that without him. He said that he was not able to do four buildings and a gate and a shift and eleven jobs and that he was behind.

And he said: "**So the four buildings are yours, and I am telling four people in a room instead of asking you, because if I ask you in front of them you will say yes because they are in the room, and you would be right to say yes, and I would rather you said it to my face than to theirs.**"

**He did not ask him. He has not been asked about that sentence and he is not going to be asked about it and nobody in that room is going to raise it.**

---

The man of about thirty-three said yes in about four seconds.

He said it correctly. He said that a gate and a building and a shift and four buildings is a Thursday afternoon and a Tuesday afternoon and about four evenings, and that he had the gate at night, and that he would want it in writing, and that a man of twenty-two was the only person he knew who could put a thing in writing that anybody would keep.

Nobody thanked either of them. About nine people were in that room and about four of them understood what had just happened and about four did not, and the four who did have not said so since and are not going to.

**It is the correct thing to do. That is what makes it the worst thing in the volume. The share is real, the man can carry it, he said yes in four seconds, nobody said no, and a person in this case who has spent four years refusing to be a burden handed a burden to a person who did not ask for it, in a room, in about four minutes, and it took about four minutes and it is done.**

---

**And the reason is not a reason anybody could have argued with, and that is the whole finding.**

He is behind because four districts made about four hundred small decisions through him for about eleven hours on a Thursday three weeks ago and the thing that came out of it was not that the junction held. It is that about nine people in this city worked out that they did not have to work things out, and the working out has not come back.

**What he did on that Thursday morning is the thing four districts did to him. It took about four minutes and it was correct and it is the same shape, and he has not said that out loud and nobody in this case is going to say it to him, and this entry says it once and says it about him and not about anybody else.**

He is not a villain and nobody in that room thinks he is one. He is the person a room asks, and he has found out this month that being the person a room asks is a thing you can run out of, and what he did about it was the only available thing and it was the worst available thing.

A first-year of nineteen was at the counter.

She said three words, in about four seconds, to nobody, and they were the right three words, and about four people in that room heard them.

"**Nobody asked him.**"

Nobody improved on it. Nobody thanked her. Nobody repeated it. She went back to the counter and did not say anything else for about an hour and she has not said anything about it since and is not going to and it is not to be asked her about.

**She has spent one document-shaped finding in this case and it was about six weeks ago and it is not this. This is three words about one man in a room, it is not a finding about anything, it is not a mechanism, it is not to be written down by anybody, and it is the truest thing anybody said in that room and nobody said so.**
