# Chapter 329 — Nobody Asked You Whether You Wanted It

The Wednesday morning of week one hundred and seventeen was the morning a Continuity Office telephone operator gave four people from four buildings in four districts the same day and the same address, which is the correct thing to do and which nobody designed and for which there is no form, and a man of twenty-two said yes four times in about nine minutes standing on a landing, and a woman of twenty-four who was doing work that gets paid for in the ground floor of that building saw all four of it and said one sentence about him, and it was not about the weather, and he did not answer it, and there was no scene and there was no declaration and there was no kiss, and nothing was resolved.

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date. Six jobs attended, one of them in a building in the third of the four districts, on a landing, at about ten in the morning, for about nine minutes, and the other five were not.

The word was at nine and ten. The room off a service road is three hundred and forty-five days old and is warm and about four people came in on the Tuesday for the temperature and nobody asked any of them anything.

---

The four-word question went to a telephone on a Wednesday morning and was answered on a Wednesday morning, and the answer was not an answer and it was correct.

A man of about thirty-three, a duty supervisor in the second of the four districts, put it himself on Monday afternoon to a person with an ordinary job. She is about thirty-one and she answers a telephone in a building in Crown Terrace for a body that is not the Office and is not a company and has no name anybody uses, and she has about four hundred calls a week and no procedure for any of this.

**She did not pretend to have an answer. She said, in about nine seconds, that she could not say who decides, that nobody had ever told her office, and that she was not going to guess at nine in the morning on somebody's behalf.**

Then she said the other thing, in about nine seconds, and it is what she is for:

"**The document names a person. I can give you the reference and I can give you the day. I cannot give you an address because he does not have one in this building and he has never been in this building. What I have got is a district, and a building in it, and a floor, and the day he is on the docket, and that is more than I have ever given anybody for that document and I have not had to think about it and I have thought about it since about ten past nine.**"

Nobody improved on it. Nobody thanked her. She did not put it in writing and there is no form in that building for giving out a person and she did not create one.

---

Four people got the same day and the same address, and it is the fourth of the four districts' own accident, and no two of the four of them knew about the other three.

A man of about forty-five whose job is buildings, from the first of the four districts.
A woman of about twenty-nine, from the fourth.
A man of about thirty-eight who keeps a gate, from the third.
And a woman of about thirty-four, from the second, who had been in that second district's building on Friday and who is not the woman who has refused to be a list and is a different person with a different job and the same building.

**They came because four buildings in four districts had the same site on a list that a Continuity Office count had put there, and the four buildings had put four requests in writing, and the four requests were the same request, and the answer to a request is a person and the person is him.**

---

He was on a landing at about ten in the morning with a tool roll and a docket.

He did not know any of them. Not one of the four had met him and not one of the four had heard of him except through a piece of paper, and about two of them had read that piece of paper with his name at the bottom of it and understood about a third of it.

He listened to about nine minutes of it in total. He listened to all four properly, standing up, one at a time, in about nine minutes, and he asked none of them one question.

**Then he said yes four times.**

To the first, in about four seconds, to a release in a building in the first of the four districts.
To the second, in about four seconds, to the same, in the fourth.
To the third, in about nine seconds, having checked the docket first, to a gate.
To the fourth, in about four seconds, to a building.

And the fourth of them asked him, before he said it, whether he was the one named at the foot of the second side of that document, and he said yes, and that was the fourth yes and it is the only one of the four that was a question and it is the only one that anybody has ever asked him in a building he was working in.

---

Nobody thanked him. Nobody said anything after the fourth one except the man of about thirty-eight, who said, in about four seconds:**I have got a gate and about nine people a day past it and I have been told to hold it and nobody has told me by whom.**"

He did not answer that one either. He has not answered it, nobody has asked him again, and he is not going to.

---

**And here is the thing that happened and nobody has said it out loud and it is not in any document and it is not going to be in any document: in about nine minutes, four people in four buildings were told that a route exists, and none of the four of them asked whether it was the only route, because none of the four of them knew that it was a route and not a favour.**

Nobody in that building noticed that. About four people who were not involved in any of it and have never met any of the four were on that landing for other reasons.

---

And Talia Venn was on the ground floor of that building and she saw all of it, and she was not part of it, and she has not been asked about it and is not going to be.

A mobile clinic was in the ground floor of that building for the day because the district's car park was full and the room that is normally used was not free. She is twenty-four, she is a resonance nurse, she holds no post, she is owed nothing, and she was doing work that gets paid for with about nine people through the morning and eleven appointments.

The landing is inside the same stairwell and the stairwell is open and the four conversations happened in it and she was coming and going with people all morning.

She heard about three of the four in full, standing at the bottom of the stairs with a tray, and the fourth one she heard from a man of about thirty-eight who said the word *yes* loudly and then said nothing else at all, and she looked up at the top of the flight and saw a man of twenty-two with a tool roll in his other hand.

---

She said it once, at about eleven o'clock, from the bottom of the stairs, in about four seconds, to him and not to the man of about thirty-eight, and nobody else in that stairwell was close enough to hear it, and about nine people went past.

"**Nobody asked you whether you wanted it and you said yes four times.**"

He did not answer it.

She did not say anything else. She went back into the ground floor and finished her morning and she has not raised it since, and she is not going to, and it has not been improved on by him or by anybody and it is not to be improved on by anybody.

Nobody heard it. Nobody thanked her, because there was nobody to thank. Nobody asked either of them anything else for the rest of the day.

**There was no scene. There was no declaration. There was no kiss. Nothing was resolved, and the resolution of what she has been saying to him since a Monday in a workroom four miles away is held to a later volume and is not held here and is not to be settled inside a fortnight by anybody.**

---

And what the four people did afterwards is a record and not a story, and it is the only reason any of the rest of this happens.

All four of them left that building with a yes, and none of them told anybody that they had got it from a man on a landing, because none of them thought of it as having come from a man on a landing. Two of them telephoned their own buildings. One of them wrote it down. One of them did nothing until the following week.

**A route that works through one person is not a slower version of a route that works through a council. It is a different practice with the same results, and the next day, in a corridor, somebody is going to say so in nine words, and nobody is going to improve on it.**

---

A room off a service road was warm at half past six that evening. A cook of twenty-seven was at the sink and said nothing at all. A man of fifty-one with a tool bag read the board standing up and said nothing. A first-year of nineteen was at the counter and said nothing, and has said very little this month, and the reason is not recorded in her mouth and is not to be asked for.

A woman of about thirty was in the chair nearest the door from about two and was not asked anything and was not thanked and was not apologised to, and the reason she is in that room is the temperature, and the temperature costs more than it did, and both of those are correct.

Nobody thanked anybody. Nobody in that room knew what had happened on a landing four miles away except two people, and neither of them said it, and one of them was in another part of the city by then.

The shutter came down at about ten.
