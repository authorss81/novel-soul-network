## 8a. A note for the next writer about what Movement II's pass cost and what it bought

**Two figures moved in the wrong direction in Movement II and both are published in its summary with the reason and the instrument and with the superseded value quoted beside the correction.** The eight-word overlap mean was 172.3 at the first pass and 97.7 after a repair that rewrote seventeen load-book paragraphs as records of counts, sources, hands and refusals, **and it is still nearly double Movement I's 50.0 and Movement II's own summary says so in its first section rather than burying it.** The apparatus share was 48.59 and is 48.18, **and it is still above Movement I's 44.75 and above Volume 06 Movement V's 47.31, and the number to beat is still 44.75.** **Movement II published its own figures twice, and the second time is a walk on the finished files: the first set was 33,844 words and 213 spans and those figures DO NOT DESCRIBE THE FINISHED FILES, which stand at 33,896 words and 215 spans, because five edits to Chapters 312 and 320, six guardrail sentences and one field label changed the files after the first set was measured. A superseded set is recorded beside the correction and not deleted. The lesson for this phase is the phase's own: measure at the end, on the files as they will be left, and if a figure moves after it is published, quote the superseded value beside the correction rather than editing the number silently.**

**A second lesson from the same pass, and it is the one a later writer is most likely to repeat: the per-chapter lists were wrong seven times across three lists while every total and the mean stayed right.** A mean reproduces when the errors are in the parts and not in the total, so a total that agrees with itself is not evidence that a published list under it does. **The two standing guardrail paragraphs account for a measured mean of 14.8 and the movement's own apparatus for a measured mean of 83.7, and the two deliberately do not add to 97.7, because a span shared by a guardrail paragraph and an apparatus paragraph is counted once in each figure; 14.8 plus 83.7 is 98.5 and the overlap is about 0.8.** **The superseded pair was 14.4 and 84.3, and 84.3 was neither a measurement nor a subtraction from 97.7, which gives 83.3, and a figure that is neither is the same failure as a figure expressed as an assertion: a number nobody recomputes.** Both numbers are published because a mean hides a batch and only a per-paragraph count separates a form from a fault.

**Four guardrails were broken in Movement II by documents naming or sorting by the things they forbid, all four were removed, and that is the fourth time this repository has produced that class of defect and the second time in ten days.** A load book said the phrase *run of days* does not occur anywhere in this volume, in order to report its absence. Three load books said the word that belongs to a bag is not used of anything in this chapter. Two load books said the word *Assembly* does not appear in this chapter and may not appear before a certain chapter. And a load book in Chapter 319 used the word that belongs to a bag as a field label in front of the record of a woman with a shoulder bag, which is a document naming the thing in order to sort by it. **Seven sentences and one label across seven places, all of them removed, and the label survived the first pass because the matcher had been the sentence pattern and not the word — and because a zero had already been published for it in the batch summary and in `state/continuity.md` as true.** **The rule for Movement III is: a document that reports the absence of a prohibited word has used it, a document that sorts by one has also used it, and the reporting and the sorting are the use. A writer who wants to record that a guardrail held should record that the matcher returned zero, in a summary, and not in a chapter, and the matcher has to be the word and not the shape of the sentence around it.**

**And one figure in the calendar is wrong and is not a table cell.** `workspace/volume-07/ARITHMETIC-AND-CALENDAR.md` section 3 says the running age of the nineteenth line at Chapter 311 is **one**; the true figure is **four**, from day 666 to day 670, by the volume's own convention of a difference and not an inclusive count. **The detector the calendar itself states — read one row backwards and confirm that it implies one day — cannot find it, because a clause of prose is not a row, and the general form of every interval error in this repository is a figure that nobody recomputes.** The file is not edited by a writing pass; the close owns it; the superseded value is recorded in `workspace/volume-07/batch-0002/SUMMARY.md` section 4 and in two of the five state files, and the corrected running values for Chapters 321 to 330 are printed at section 3 of this file. **A writer who finds a figure in a sentence in that file and not in a table should recompute it from the anchor, and a writer who finds a figure in a table should still walk it, because the tables have been wrong four times and the sentences have been wrong once and the sentences are the ones nobody checks.**
