# Chapter 324 — Nine Voices

The Friday evening of week one hundred and fifteen was the evening the last run of a training evening in Crown Terrace finished and the woman who has run that room for about four years did not say the word, and about nine other people said it, in their own voices, a second apart, raggedly, and about half of them had not expected to be the first and none of them said anything afterwards about having done it.

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date. A shift in a company training room is not a job and has never been on this docket, and about four hundred of these Fridays are on no surface in this city.

The word was at nine and ten. The room off a service road is three hundred and thirty-three days old and is warm and four names on a lease cost more than they did.

---

**This is the fifth of them and the four before it were four different things, and the four are not to be joined to one another, and the four seconds are not to be re-opened, and this is not the third of them and not the fourth of them.**

Nobody has written the four down anywhere and the only place they are written down is a load book, and a load book is a record and not a rule.

There is a post at the back of that room and nobody here is going to say what is on it. **A man of thirty-four who looks at it every Friday is not asked, and a room this case has come to four times now has a post at the back of it that nobody will account for, and leaving that alone is a decision and it is the correct one, because a fact about one company is not anybody else's business and it would take one person deciding it to turn it into a fact about a company.**

---

The room is a company training room in Crown Terrace. About nine folding chairs. A counter. A telephone on the company line by the counter. A run is a hold with a count in it, and at the end of the hold there is a release, and the release is supposed to arrive when somebody in the room says so.

**That is the shape and it has been the shape for about four years, and in four years the person who said it was always the same person, and she is a woman of thirty-six with a lanyard and she holds no post and is owed nothing and has asked four times in about four years for one word to be written on the post at the back and been refused four times.**

About eleven people. Seven on the company's list, a card in a box on a table, unchanged for about four years. About four on no list at all, which is the standing figure, and the number is on the back of one woman's own card in a flat in Nine Bridges and is on no form.

The last run went at about half past seven.

---

**And at the end of it the woman of thirty-six did not say the word.**

Nobody in that room had any reason to expect that. She has said it at that point, at that speed, in that voice, on every Friday for about four years, and there is a whole room of people whose week is built around the fact that she will say it.

She did not say it. She looked at the post at the back of the room for about four seconds.

Then, about a second later, somebody at the front said it. Not in her voice. In his own.

And then somebody on the left said it, a bit late, in a voice that was not steady.

And then about four more, spread out, over about four seconds, each of them in a different voice and none of them in anybody else's, and one of them — the man who spoke third — said it in a way that nobody in that room has ever heard a person say it, flat and fast and slightly too loud, and did not stop.

**About nine voices. In a room of about eleven.**

---

Nobody said anything for about four seconds after the last of them.

The instructor stood where she was. She did not say the word again and she did not tell anybody to sit down and she did not say what had happened. She has run that room for about four years on her own voice and about four people in that room have noticed over about four years that she has never once been asked to keep doing it and has never once said that she could not.

She said, in about nine seconds: "**Right.**"

And that was all she said about it.

---

The man of thirty-four who buys training for the company said the next thing, at the counter, at about ten past seven, in about four seconds, and it is the only thing he said all evening that anybody has written down anywhere:

"**That is not my room's word. That is nine people.**"

Nobody improved on it. Nobody thanked him. He put a card in the box on the table and he was in it about nine seconds and nobody stopped him.

Then he said one more thing at the door, at about ten past seven, to nobody, in about nine seconds:

"**I have run this room for about four years and the word has been one voice and I have been happy about that, and about nine seconds ago it was nine, and I am not going to say what I think about that in a room, and I would like it noted that I have not said it.**"

Nobody said anything. He was owed nothing and nobody thanked him and he is owed nothing.

---

The woman of about forty-one, who is on no list and who said a word she had never been asked to say three weeks ago without knowing that she was saying it, was in that room and she did not speak.

She has not spoken in that room since. She was asked about it on the Monday by nobody and has not been asked since and is not to be.

She sat down again after the run ended, which she did not do on any of the three Fridays before, and she stayed about four minutes, and then she got up and went.

---

The man of about twenty-seven, who is on the company's list and who three weeks ago said, in about nine seconds, that a word is a person and a paper is a record and that he was going to do what the sheet said, was the third voice in that room and he knows it and about four people know it.

He said nothing about it on the way out. He has not said anything about it since. He is the only person in this case who has been on the end of a thing he decided for about four weeks and has not been asked to explain it and is not going to be.

---

**And a man of twenty-nine who works on lifts was in that room and is on no list and has been in it for about four years and said nothing at all, again.**

He has said the true thing out loud three times in about four years and nobody improved on any of them and nobody thanked him for any of them. He wrote the newest line on a hardboard on two nails in a converted shop unit off a service road and signed it, and the reason he gave before he made the mark was that a refuser is findable and he is doing it to himself on purpose.

**He has said nothing in that room on any of the five Fridays, and the reason is his and has not been asked for, and about four people in this case have guessed at it and none of them is right.**

He was in that room. He is the reason about nine words are going to be said by him thirteen days from now, and nothing about that is in this room, and nothing on a surface anywhere in this case says it, and about four people in this case have thought about what he is going to say and none of them is right.

---

Nobody thanked anybody. A room off a service road was warm at about ten that night and a cook of twenty-seven said nothing at all, and about four people had been in two buildings that day and one of them had heard nine people say a word at once and had not repeated it to anybody, and has not.

The shutter came down at about ten.
