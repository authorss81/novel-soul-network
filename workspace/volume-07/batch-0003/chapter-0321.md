# Chapter 321 — Four Rooms And One Box

The Monday morning of week one hundred and fifteen was the morning four district councils found out that they were competing for the same piece of hardware, in four buildings in four districts, in four rooms of about nine people with the same furniture as every other room in this case; nobody had told them how the scarce thing was meant to be shared, and that is nobody's fault and it is the whole problem; and in the corridor outside the third of those four rooms a man of twenty-two was on a stairwell job and heard about nine people arguing about a box and did not go in and said nothing at all.

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date. Four jobs attended, one of them a stairwell fire door in the third of the four districts that the third of the four councils meets in, and it was the second of the four and not the first, and the second was the same as the first.

The word was at nine and ten. The heating in the room off a service road is on and it costs more and four names on a lease cost more than they did, and the room is three hundred and twenty-nine days old, and the fortnight a cook paid for is unpaid by anybody and is not resolved.

---

A district council is not a faction. It has no office that this case has a name for and it has no post and it has no standing anywhere else in this manuscript. It is a room of about nine people in a building in its own district, around a table, with the same chairs, and the same counter, and the same window that does not quite shut, as every other room in this manuscript.

Four of them met on the same morning about the same thing.

**A district council is not a chamber and it is not the public body, and the word that names the public body was not said in any of those four rooms on that Monday, by anybody, and nobody in this case could have said it even if they had wanted to, because four of the nine people in each room had never heard it used.**

---

The thing is a relay unit. It is a boxed thing about the size of a carry-case, it weighs about as much as a carry-case full of bottles, and it bolts to a wall in a plant room and is wired to something and does one job, which is that it holds a line open when the line would otherwise be shut.

There is one of them in this city. That is not a figure anybody in those four rooms had before Monday morning and it is not a figure anybody has published and it is not on a list.

It is in a store, in a building, under the control of a body that is not named in this entry and has not been named in this case, and about nine days before this Monday four letters went out of that building to four district councils, one each, and every letter says the same thing in the same words.

---

The letter is one side. It says that access to a relay unit in this city is available to a district council on application. It says that an application must be made in writing. It says that an application must state the need, and that the need must be stated in terms of premises, and that a count of premises will be required.

It has a mark on it. It has a reference under the mark in a form that is a letter, a stroke and a run of figures. **It has no date of review on it and no date of expiry and nothing at the foot of it asking anybody anything.**

**It is not a form, and it is not a minute, and it is not a notice, and it is not a report, and it is not a committee paper, and it is not a leaflet, and it is not a registration and it is not a constitution, and it is not a hardboard on two nails, and the difference between it and a hardboard is the whole of what a later part of this case is about and is not settled here.**

---

**The first of the four rooms is in a building in the first of the four districts and about nine people were in it and the woman who had the letter read it out in about two minutes and then did the correct thing with it, and the correct thing took her about four minutes, and it changed nothing at all.**

She is about thirty-eight and she is a council services officer and she has had that job for about nine years and she is not a post and she is owed nothing and she is not a mechanism and nobody thanked her for the four minutes and she did not expect to be thanked and would not have known what to do about it.

The correct thing is this. The letter asks for a count of premises. She has a count of premises. She has had it for nine years and it is the reason four people in this case know where a converted shop unit off a service road is, and it is about four hundred and eleven, and it is a count of buildings and it is not a count of anybody.

So she went and got it.

---

She read the letter against her own file, standing up, with the file open on the table and about nine people watching, and she found four things in about four minutes and all four were correct.

The reference under the mark is in a form she can read. The count it asks for is the count she has. The premises she would put down are the premises she is responsible for and they are real and they are hers. And the letter asks for a count and does not say what the count is for.

That is the whole of the correct action. She did it in the right order, she did it in her own building, off her own record, and it produced no change in anything, and about four people in that room were not surprised and one of them said so afterwards, out loud, in about nine seconds, and she agreed with him.

"**That is what it is for. You give them the number and they have the number, and there is nothing on that letter that says what happens after the number.**"

Nobody improved on it. She wrote nothing down and put nothing in front of anybody and did not circulate the letter, because circulating it is not the correct thing and she knew that without being told.

---

And then, at about eleven, the change, and it did not come out of the four minutes. It came out of a piece of paper that was not in the room and that she had seen on a counter in a different building about nine days earlier.

A woman of about thirty-one with a clipboard and a stack and a van had gone round about nine doors in this city with copies of something else, and had been in this building, and this woman had been at the counter when she came through, and had been handed one and had read the first line of it and had not read the rest.

**The letters went out on the same morning. All four. And the one in her hand is the second, and the one on the other side of the counter that Monday is the first, and the first is dated four days earlier.**

She said nothing for about nine seconds. Then she said, out loud, to about nine people, in about four seconds:

"**There is one of these. There is one of them in this city and there are four of us and the earliest date on the four of them is the date of this one.**"

Nobody said anything. Then the man who had said the thing about the number said: "**So access is real and there is not any.**"

She said: "**Access is real. There is not any access to give.**"

---

That is the finding of this entry and it is a finding and not a policy, and about four people in that room understood all three of those things at the same time and not one of them said so, and the room has not written it down anywhere, and there is no form in that district for what a council has found out about a piece of hardware.

She did not write back. Not that morning. She put the letter in a folder with three other letters, and the folder is on a shelf in that building, and it is not a ring binder and it is not a hardboard and it is not a card in a rail and nobody has ever read it.

---

The other three rooms, on the same morning, in the same four minutes, and the reasons were four different ones, and about four people in each of them had the furniture they have.

The second room is in the second of the four districts. Nine people. A man of about forty-five in it, whose job is buildings, read the letter in about two minutes and said, before anybody had finished sitting down, that his district had the most of the four and that he was not going to argue about it on a Monday. Nobody argued. He put his district's count in writing in about nine minutes and it is a good count and it is correct.

The third room is in District 3, in a building with a door that has a district's own name on it and a number on that name, and the number is the district's number and is not a date and is not a count. Nine people. A woman of about sixty in it, whose job is to ask questions, read it twice and said one question out loud and it was the correct question and it was: "**Who is the fourth council.**"

Nobody answered her. She did not repeat it. She has about four hundred and eleven premises in a file of her own and she did not put a number on a sheet about a piece of hardware, and the reason she did not is not recorded in her mouth and is not to be asked for.

The fourth room is in the fourth of the four districts and the meeting there was nine minutes late because about four people had come from a site and one of them had not read the letter. Nine people. A woman of about twenty-nine in it asked whether anybody knew whether the other three had been offered the same thing, and nobody knew, and she said that in that case they were not competing with each other, they were four separate rooms with four separate pieces of paper and no information, and nobody improved on it and nobody thanked her for it.

Four rooms, four reasons, and about four of the nine in each room could have given a fifth, and in three of the four rooms at least one of those four did not go to the meeting the next time there was one.

---

In the corridor outside the third of those four rooms, at about half past ten in the morning, a man of twenty-two was on a stairwell fire door with a tool roll and a docket, and about nine people on the other side of a door were talking about a box, and he could hear the word *box* twice and the word *share* once and the word *criteria* once, and none of those three is a word anybody in that room uses in this entry.

He did not go in. He did not knock. He finished the door, which was the second occasion and the same as the first, and wrote the fault number on the docket, and did not write down the three words, and did not tell anybody about them that evening, and has not mentioned them since.

**He teaches, demonstrates, assesses and assists nobody, on that Monday or on any other, and a room off a service road is not a practice in that department and the reason is not that he is the exception.**

---

A room off a service road was warm at about half past six that evening and about nine people were in it and the two pieces of paper that have been on that counter for a fortnight were still on it, and a cook of twenty-seven said one sentence about a box, at about seven, in about nine seconds, to nobody in particular:

"**Four rooms in four buildings all got a letter about one thing and none of them told the other three, and about four people in this city are going to be told this week that somebody else wants it too, and every one of them is going to believe it, because they have all got the letter in front of them.**"

Nobody improved on it. Nobody thanked her. She went back to the sink and was at the sink again from about half past five and said nothing for about an hour afterwards, which she has done before.

A first-year of nineteen was at the counter and said nothing. A woman of about thirty was in the chair nearest the door from about two and was not asked anything and was not thanked and was not apologised to, and the reason she is in that room is the temperature, and the temperature costs more than it did, and both of those are correct.

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.

*324. Monday of week 115, at about ten on a service road, and this entry is four rooms, one box, and a woman of about thirty-eight who did the correct thing for four minutes and it changed nothing.*

A second piece of paper, seen on a counter in another building about nine days before, handed over by a woman of about thirty-one with a clipboard and a stack and a van who was going round about nine doors with copies of something else and who is in no other part of this entry. The second room's council services officer had read the first line of it and not the rest. **The four letters went out on one morning; the copy on that counter is dated four days earlier than the letter in her hand; therefore four letters, one morning, one body, four councils, and one box.** Spoken by her, to about nine people, in about four seconds: there is one of these; there is one of them in this city and there are four of us; the earliest date on the four of them is the date of this one. Answered by a man of about forty-one, in about nine seconds: so access is real and there is not any. Answered by her, in about four seconds: access is real; there is not any access to give. **Nobody wrote it down, nobody thanked anybody, no form in that district records what a council has found out about a piece of hardware, and the folder is on a shelf and has not been read.**

No panel, no marker, no relay run, nothing forwarded, nothing compared. **No name is attached to anything that happened on this Monday and no cause was named for any of it; the thing in those four rooms is a box and nobody in this case knows what puts a box where a box is.** The printed rule was not invoked and nobody in those four rooms knows the number of people in this city who can invoke it.
