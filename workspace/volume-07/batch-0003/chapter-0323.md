# Chapter 323 — Quoted Back

The Thursday morning of week one hundred and fifteen was the morning a woman of about sixty whose job is to ask questions quoted a Continuity Office instrument back at a Continuity Office man of about forty-one, correctly, using its own count and its own reason, and got a small thing, and the small thing was a stairwell fire door in a four-storey building in District 3 that went back into service at about half past two that afternoon, and it was the first time in this case that a document this case has produced had been used as an instrument against the office that wrote it, and nobody thanked her and she did not expect to be thanked and is not going to be.

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date. Four jobs attended, two of them in the same building and one of those in the second of the four districts, and the day was in the middle of the week and nothing about it was unusual in it.

The word was at nine and ten. The room off a service road is three hundred and thirty-two days old and is warm and costs more, and about four people came in on the Wednesday for the temperature and one of them is a woman of about thirty who was not asked anything.

---

A Continuity Office man of about forty-one came to District 3 practice standards at about ten in the morning, and he came in a car and he came on his own and he did not have a driver, and nobody in that building had asked him to come and about four people have wondered since whether anybody did.

The reason he came is the reason a man named in this case was found in an earlier week, and it is not a dramatic reason. **A district is the only body in this city that keeps a count of buildings, and a document that names a person and a count of sites cannot be checked anywhere except in a building that has a register of addresses.**

He had a folder. He had about four minutes' worth of questions prepared. He had a badge and a coat and a job.

**He is a person with an ordinary job. He is not a post. He is owed nothing. He is right about the thing he is right about, and that thing is the count, and he is not a villain and is not going to become one in about four minutes.**

---

And then somebody asked him a question and the question was not one of his four.

She is about sixty and her job is to ask questions and she has about four hundred and eleven premises in a file of her own, and about nine rooms' worth of paper around her, and a wall binder of printed standards with numbers and dates on them, and a counter.

She has been right every time she has opened her mouth in this case. It has cost her the fact that there is now one more person in this city who has read a name of his and said it back to somebody in a building with a stamp, and she has not named that cost out loud and nobody is to ask her for it.

She asked him about a stairwell fire door in a four-storey building in her own district.

**Nobody had asked her to. The question was hers, it was about a building, and it was in about nine seconds, and it was the correct question, and it cost her the whole of the next four minutes.**

---

The building. It is four storeys and it has about four flats on each floor and about nine people live in it and about four of them are over the age of sixty. There is one stairwell fire door on the ground floor and it has been held shut and locked for four days.

It was held by a caretaker on his own initiative on the fourth day, after a report from a person who does not live there, and the district's own practice standards office endorsed that in one line on the fifth day, and no instrument caused it and no instrument mentioned it and nobody in Crown Terrace has ever heard of the building.

**That is all true and none of it is anybody's fault, and the woman of sixty did not say it was anybody's fault, and it was not.**

She put it to him like this. The instrument says that a condition described in nine lines is present across about nine sites in four districts. It says the count. It says why, and the why is a count. It says the point of coordination for it is one person, and the person is named at the foot of the second side.

And she said, in about nine seconds, and it is the finding of this entry:

"**Then the condition is in my building and the point of coordination is a person, and I am not allowed to ask the caretaker to open a door because the condition is the reason he closed it, and I am not allowed to ring the person at the foot of your second side, because he is not on any list in this city and you are the only body in the room that has his name on a piece of paper.**"

Nobody improved on it. She was right.

---

He said, in about nine seconds, that he would want to see the door before he said anything about the person.

So they went and saw the door. It took about eleven minutes on the ground floor of a building in District 3 and it was done by two competent people who did not need to be there and neither of whom thanked the other.

He did not touch it. He looked at the closer, the frame, the signage, and a line of paint on the frame at about the height of his hand, and he asked one question about the paint, and was told the answer, and wrote nothing down.

Then they went back upstairs.

---

And then she did the thing this entry is named for, and she did it with his own sentence.

She said that the count on the second side is about nine sites, and that a site is a building, and that her own file has about four hundred and eleven premises, and that the number of buildings in her district with a release in some state this week is not nine and has never been nine, and that therefore the count on the instrument is a count of buildings and not a count of the number of decisions somebody is going to have to make.

She said it in about eleven seconds, standing up, with the folder still closed on the table, and she did not raise her voice and she did not repeat herself and she did not say it again after the first time.

"**Your count is a count of sites. It is not a count of doors. If somebody reads that number in four weeks as a count of how many things need somebody to decide, then this city is short by about nine hundred, and about nine hundred is not my figure, it is a figure a woman in another part of this city has, and she and I have never met.**"

---

He said, in about four seconds, that the count is a count of sites and that it has always been a count of sites.

And she said: "**Then say that out loud in a room, and I will write it down, and I will write down that you said it.**"

**He said it.** He said it in about nine seconds to about four people in that room and he said it twice, once to her and once to the man of about thirty-eight who takes minutes there, and the second time he said it with the word *sites* in it and the word *doors* in it and he said it in the past tense about his own document.

**Nobody improved on it. Nobody thanked him. He was right, and being right is the whole of what he did in that room, and what it cost him was that a sentence is now in a building in District 3 that says the count on an instrument is a count of buildings.**

---

And then she used it, and she used it correctly, and this is what a document is for.

"**So the count is a count of sites and the point of coordination is a person. I am not asking you for the person. I am asking you to be the way I reach him about one door.**"

He said yes, in about four seconds, and did it from the chair he was sitting in, on his own telephone, in about nine seconds, to the caretaker of a building in District 3, and he said the office's name and his own job and one sentence about a person, and he did not explain the person and did not know anything about the person and had not met the person.

---

**The stairwell fire door on the ground floor of a four-storey building in District 3 went back into service at about half past two that afternoon, and about nine people live in that building, and the line that came back was one line and it is not a form and it is not a minute and it is not a notice and it is not a report and it is not a committee paper and it is not a leaflet and it is not a registration and it is not a constitution and it is not a hardboard on two nails.**

The caretaker did it himself and did not want anything for it and nobody offered him anything.

**Nobody thanked anybody. The woman of sixty was not thanked by the man of forty-one and did not ask. He was not thanked by her and did not ask. The man of about thirty-eight who took the minute wrote it in about four minutes in a book that is not a ring binder and is not a hardboard, and he has never in his life taken a minute like it and nobody has told him that he has.**

---

And the cost, in her own words, at about half past three, in about nine seconds, to nobody, at her own counter, and it is the only cost named out loud in this entry:

"**I have had that file for about nine years and I have never once used a document I was sent in order to get something. I did it this morning because it worked. I would like somebody to write down that I did it because it worked, and not because I have found anything out about anybody.**"

Nobody improved on it. Nobody wrote it down. She has not said it again and is not going to, and the reason is hers and is not to be asked for.

---

A room off a service road was warm at half past six that evening. A man of fifty-one with a tool bag was at the far side with his back to the wall, reading the hardboard the way he does, and about four people had read about a door in District 3 by then, from about four different people, and he said one sentence, at about seven, in about nine seconds, to nobody in particular:

"**That is the first time in about four years I have heard of a piece of paper being used on a person rather than on a door, and I do not know yet whether that is better.**"

Nobody improved on it. Nobody thanked him. He has not said it again and has not been asked.

**A man of twenty-two was in that room from about half past five and did not have a cup of tea, as he does, and said nothing about any of it, and about four people noticed that he said nothing and not one of them has said so. The door in District 3 is not in his file and he was not asked about it and is not going to be. He taught, demonstrated, assessed and assisted nothing, and a room off a service road is not a practice in that department and the reason is not that he is the exception.**

A cook of twenty-seven was at the sink and said nothing at all. A first-year of nineteen was at the counter and said nothing. A woman of about thirty was in the chair nearest the door from about two and was not asked anything and was not thanked and was not apologised to, and the reason she is in that room is the temperature, and the temperature costs more than it did, and both of those are correct.

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.
