# Chapter 325 — The Broadcast

The Monday morning of week one hundred and sixteen was the morning a voice was heard live, in four rooms in four districts and in a great many other places, by about four million people, and it said one thing about this city and it said it to the city and not to anybody, and about nine people in this city ended that day with less of something than they had, and the person who noticed was a woman of about forty-four who takes calls at a counter and who heard what was said at about seven in the morning and went to work at eight.

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date. Six jobs attended, one address not attended, and the address is the one that has been not attended for about a fortnight and is not chased.

The word was at nine and ten. The room off a service road is three hundred and thirty-six days old and is warm and about four people came in on the Sunday evening for the temperature and one of them is a woman of about thirty who was not asked anything.

---

**It is a thing that was said. It is not a message, and it was not delivered, and there is no envelope and no courier and no clipboard and nobody in this case received it privately, and it is addressed to none of the people in this manuscript.**

About four million people heard it. Most of them heard it in a room they were already in. It is not a recording, it was live, and the only reason a person in this case can put a time on it is that about four of them noticed the time and no two of the four agree.

**It named nobody. Not one person in this city was named in it, and no character in this case is in it, and no name of the person who said it is attached to a sequence or to the weather or to a door or to a crew, and the strongest temptation this case has had in seven volumes is to put her name next to what happened on that Monday and it is refused here and it is to be refused everywhere after it.**

---

**Iona Sorn.**

That is the whole of what this entry does with her name. She is a person in this manuscript who has been in it once before, in another volume, as a voice on a recording, and she has never been met and she is not met now, and she is not in a room, and she is not a post and not an office and not a chair of anything, and **she has nothing to do with the weather, with any of the releases, with any door, or with any of the nine accounts in a locked drawer, and no character in this case has established any of those links and this entry does not supply one.**

---

What she said, once, and it is not said again anywhere in this volume, and nobody in this case repeats it, not to a person, not in a meeting, not in a room off a service road, not in a workroom four miles away, and not in a load book:

**That only one central anchor can end what is happening in this city.**

That is the whole of it. She said it in about eleven seconds. She did not say who the anchor is and she did not say where it would be and she did not say how anybody would be allowed to reach it, and she did not say the word that names the public body, which is not in it and was not said by anybody in this case on that Monday.

**She was right about the arithmetic. A reader is able to check that arithmetic later and this entry does not do the check, because doing the check here would be the same as attaching her name to it, and the reason this volume has refused to attach her name to anything for seven volumes is that the moment a name is next to a thing that works, the answer to everything she says becomes that somebody said so, and it is that nobody can.**

---

**And the only thing this movement says about it, apart from what is in this entry, is that a person with an ordinary job heard it and went to work.**

She is about forty-four. She takes calls at the counter of a district office in the second of the four districts, on a Monday morning, on a telephone on a desk. She heard it at about seven at home, on a small thing in a kitchen, and she was not at work, and she was not on a telephone, and nobody knew she had heard it and nobody asked and nobody is going to.

**She went to work at eight. That is the whole of what she did about it. She has not been asked for an opinion and does not have one that she has given, and about four people in this case would like one and are not going to get it, and one of them is a man of twenty-two who was told about it at about ten and who has not said a word about it and is not going to.**

---

The nine people, and the less they have.

Three of the four councils took applications on the Friday and the Monday. The letter says an application must be made in writing and that the need must be stated in terms of premises. **The need, stated in terms of premises, is a number, and a number of premises in this city is in four buildings and is a thing a district office can do in about nine minutes, and three of the four rooms did it correctly.**

To say that a building needs the thing, a person has to say that something in it is not right. The way the three rooms did it, in writing, without anybody objecting, was to take the names of the people who had come to a counter in their own district over the last fortnight and asked for a release to be looked at in their own building.

That is the correct procedure. It is in a binder in each of those three buildings and it is about eleven years old and it has never been used for this.

**About nine people in this city went to a counter in the last fortnight, said a thing about their own building, said their own name because that is what a counter asks for, and went home. On the Friday or the Monday their names were read out in a room of about nine people as evidence that the building needed the thing.**

They have not been told. Nobody rang any of them. **What they have less of, and it is the only thing they have less of, is the thing they had before the Friday, which was that a person could come to a counter and say a thing and the counter was the end of it.**

---

The woman who noticed, and what she noticed, and what she did with it.

She filed four sheets on the Monday morning. That is her job and she has had it for about six years and about four hundred of a week go through her hands.

One of the four sheets had nine names on it. All nine of them were people who had come to her counter and spoken to her, and she remembers about four of them by the sound of their voice and none of them by name.

She said one thing about it, at about half past ten on the Monday morning, to about four people, in about eleven seconds, and it is the only cost named out loud in this entry:

"**Nine people said a thing to me at this counter and went home, and now their names are on a sheet in this building because I asked them for their names when they told me. I did that. It is what the counter does. I have done it about four hundred times a week for about six years and I have never once thought about where the piece of paper goes after, and I have thought about it since about ten o'clock this morning and I am not going to be able to stop.**"

Nobody improved on it. Nobody thanked her. She has said it once and has not said it again and nobody is going to ask her for it a second time.

---

The four rooms, and why four of them would rather have the box.

The four had been asked, in those four rooms, whether they wanted a word on a recording or a thing in a plant room they could walk to and put a hand on.

**In two of the four rooms the reason was the same and it was given plainly and it is not a stupid reason: the box is a thing you can see and the anchor is a word on a recording, and a person in a room can be shown the box in about nine seconds and cannot be shown the anchor in any length of time at all.**

In the first of those two rooms a man of about forty-five said, in about nine seconds, that he had spent about nine years being responsible for things he could put his hand on, and that he is not going to be told that a piece of furniture in another part of the city is more real than a door he has opened. Nobody improved on it. He is right about the door.

In the fourth of the four districts a woman of about twenty-nine said the other version of the same reason, in about four seconds, and she said it more sharply: that a person who asks her district to give up a thing it can see, in favour of a thing that arrives as a voice, is asking her to explain to about nine people on Thursday why it is better, and that she has not got an answer that fits in a Thursday.

Nobody improved on that either.

**The other two rooms gave two other reasons. The second of the four districts said that its own count was the highest of the four and it had put that count in writing and was not going to unsay it. The third of the four, in District 3, did not give a reason at all, and the woman of about sixty who has never once been wrong in this case said nothing in that room for the first time, and the reason is hers and is not to be asked for.**

---

And the change, and it happened in about nine minutes in a corridor, and it is the whole of what this movement is for.

The four rooms had been arguing, on and off, for four days, about a box. At about eleven on the Monday morning a man of about thirty-three, who is a duty supervisor in the second of the four districts and who was handed a document on Wednesday and did the correct thing with it and got a query back three times, said the following in about nine seconds to about nine people, and he was not in his own building and he was not on a list and he was nobody:

"**We have spent four days on a box. Not one of the four of us can say who decides. I asked for a number per person on Wednesday and got the same question back three times and there is no column for it anywhere, and that is not a box, that is a question about who is allowed to ask what.**"

Nobody improved on it. Nobody thanked him.

**And then, in the same nine minutes, four people in four buildings stopped saying *we want the box* and started saying the other thing, which is a question, and the question has not got an answer in any of those four buildings and none of the four rooms has a procedure for it and there is no form anywhere in this case for a question about who decides.**

**The argument stopped being about a piece of hardware at about ten past eleven on the Monday morning of week one hundred and sixteen and it has not been about a piece of hardware since. It is the movement's subject and it is tested in about eleven hours' cost three days later, and nobody in this case has said that yet, and about four people have noticed what has happened and not one of them has said so.**

---

A room off a service road was warm at about half past six that evening and about nine people were in it. A cook of twenty-seven said nothing at all. A first-year of nineteen was at the counter and said nothing. A man of fifty-one with a tool bag read the whole board standing up, which he does, and said nothing.

A woman of about thirty was in the chair nearest the door from about two and was not asked anything and was not thanked and was not apologised to, and the reason she is in that room is the temperature, and the temperature costs more than it did, and both of those are correct.

Nobody thanked anybody. Nobody repeated what was said at about seven that morning. The shutter came down at about ten.

To state a need, three of the four rooms read out the names of people who had come to a counter in their own district in the last fortnight and had asked for a release to be looked at in their own building. **About nine people in this city came to a counter in a fortnight, said a thing about their own building, gave their own names because that is what a counter asks, and went home; on the Friday or the Monday their names were read aloud in a room of about nine people as evidence.** Nobody rang any of them. Nobody has told any of them. Notifier of the practice, and the only cost named aloud in this entry: a woman of about forty-four who takes calls at that counter, about six years, about four hundred of a week, in about eleven seconds at about half past ten to about four people — nine people said a thing to her at that counter and went home, and their names are now on a sheet in that building because she asked them for their names when they told her, which she has done about four hundred times a week for about six years without once thinking about where the paper goes afterwards, and she has thought about it since about ten o'clock and is not going to be able to stop. Not improved on. Not thanked. Said once and not again and not to be asked for twice.**

Not thanked. **Four people in four buildings then stopped saying they wanted the box and started asking a question. No answer exists in any of the four buildings.
