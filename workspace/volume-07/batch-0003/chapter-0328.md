# Chapter 328 — There Is A Word And None Of Them Have It

The Monday morning of week one hundred and seventeen was the morning about four people in four rooms in four districts found out that there is a word for the thing the four of them are in, and that the word is one word, and that none of the four of them has it and none of the four of them can get it, and not one of the four said it out loud, and it is not printed anywhere, and it is not in anybody's mouth on that day, and the woman who holds a chair of forty-nine in a building in Crown Terrace was not told and did not say it and is not going to say it for a long time yet, and the secondment is unchanged and is not explained here.

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date. Five jobs attended, one address not attended, and the address is the one that has been not attended for about a fortnight.

The word was at nine and ten. The room off a service road is three hundred and forty-three days old and is warm and about four people came in on the Sunday for the temperature and nobody asked any of them anything.

---

**Four rooms have been arguing about one piece of hardware for nine days. On this Monday morning four people in those four rooms discovered that the argument is not about the hardware, and that the reason it is not is that the thing they are all in has a name, and that the name is one word, and that they do not have it.**

Nobody told them. Nobody refused to tell them. Nobody was asked and nobody said no.

---

Where it was available.

One of the four letters about a relay unit is two-sided. **On the second side, below the mark, under the reference, there is a line that is the name of a body, printed, in the form a name takes when a name is printed under a mark.**

It is not a signature, because nobody signed it. It is not a person. It is not a question at the foot and it is not a form and it is not a minute and it is not a notice and it is not a report and it is not a committee paper and it is not a leaflet and it is not a registration and it is not a constitution.

**It is the only place anybody knows of, up to this moment, where the word exists, and it exists in print, on a document, under a mark, and it has been under a mark for about eleven days and four people have had that letter in their hands and three of the four have not read to the bottom of the second side.**

---

One. The first of the four districts. A woman of about thirty-eight read to the bottom of the second side on this Monday morning, at about half past nine, standing up, with about four people in the room.

She read the line. She said, in about four seconds, and she said it to the room and not to anybody in it:

"**There is a name on this. It is not the body's name that sent it, it is the name of the thing that the body is part of, and I have read that line about four times this morning and I could not tell you the word.**"

She did not say the word. Nobody asked her for it that morning, and there is nobody in that room who has it either.

What she said instead, in about nine seconds, and nobody improved on it, is this:

"**Four of us have spent nine days on a box and the box is not the thing, and the thing has got a name, and the name is on this sheet under a mark in a form I have not seen before, and if the four of us cannot say the word then we are four rooms arguing about a box in a language that nobody in any of the four buildings is speaking.**"

---

Two. The second of the four districts. The man of about forty-five, whose job is buildings, had the same letter and had had it for eleven days and had read the first side only, because the first side is the side with the number on it and the number is what he wanted.

He read the second side on the Monday at about eleven, at a table, alone, having been asked to by a person of about thirty-three from his own building who had been told something on Friday.

He did not say the word. He said:**That is a word for the whole city. I have got four hundred and something buildings in my own file and I have never once been asked what I am part of.**"

Nobody improved on it. Nobody thanked him. He did not know the word and did not learn it that day and has not learned it since and has not asked anybody for it and is not going to.

---

Three. The third of the four districts, District 3. The woman of about sixty, whose job is to ask questions, was asked for the word on the Monday afternoon, by a person with an ordinary job who had come about forty minutes on a bus and had asked correctly.

She had the letter. She had read both sides on the Friday. She looked at the line under the mark for about four seconds and then said, in about nine seconds:

"**I know that there is a word. I have known there was a word since the Friday. I have not had the word since the Friday and I am not going to be able to tell you it, and I am going to tell you what I can tell you, which is that it is on that sheet and it has been on that sheet for about eleven days and four of us have had that sheet.**"

The visitor wrote that down in her own words and read it back before she left, and did not summarise it, and that is correct.

**The woman of sixty did not say the word. Nobody in that building has said the word in about nine years and about four people in it have served the whole of that time and one of them is that woman.**

---

Four. The fourth of the four districts. The woman of about twenty-nine was the only one of the four who had already worked it out, and she did it on the Friday, from about nine buildings, and she did not tell anybody until the Monday, and she told about four people in about nine seconds on the Monday and not one of them wrote it down.

She did not say the word either. She said, in about nine seconds:**There is a body that is not us, that is not the Office, that is not a company, and it sits above all four of us and none of us has ever been in a room with it, and I have been in this job for about four years and I could not have told you that this morning and I found it out on a Friday by looking at a letter.**"

Nobody improved on it. Nobody thanked her. Nobody in the fourth district has asked her how she worked it out and she has not said.

---

**And the woman who holds a chair of forty-nine in a room in Crown Terrace was not told, and did not say it, and was not asked, and does not know that about four people in four rooms in four districts have spent the morning discovering that a word exists.**

**The secondment is unchanged. It is the only thing still running that was set up before this began. It is not explained here and is not to be explained for a long time yet, and nobody in this city is in a position to explain it and about four people have tried.**

**The printed rule is still named and restricted and not withdrawn, and about four hundred competent people in this city can invoke it, and the word is not in it, and nobody who can invoke it has ever had to use that word to do so, and about four people have wondered this week whether that is a coincidence and none of them has said so to anybody.**

---

And the change, which is what the Monday was for, and it was made in about four minutes that afternoon by a person with an ordinary job who was not in any of the four rooms and is not a protagonist of anything.

The four had spent nine days asking four different questions in four different buildings about a box. Nobody in any of the four buildings could say who decides, and there was no procedure for finding out and no form for it and no person whose job it is.

At about half past three on the Monday afternoon a man of about thirty-three, a duty supervisor in the second of the four districts, who was handed a document on a Wednesday and did the correct thing with it and got a query back three times with no column for the answer anywhere in this city, telephoned the other three rooms himself, and he did it on his own telephone, and he did not go through anybody.

He did not ask about the box. He asked whether the other three would put the same question in the same words.

**Three of the four said yes. The third of the four — the room in District 3 — did not say yes and did not say no and said, in about nine seconds, that she would answer a question properly when she was asked one, and about four people in that building noticed that and none of them has said so.**

The question is four words long. It is a question about who decides, and it is going to be put to somebody, and **it is not written down anywhere, and the reason is that whoever it is put to is going to be asked it cold and about four of the four people who wrote it would like to see that happen.**

---

A room off a service road was warm at half past six that evening and about nine people were in it and there are three pieces of paper on the counter, and the third one is not a piece of paper and has no mark on it.

A cook of twenty-seven said nothing at all. A man of fifty-one with a tool bag read the board standing up and said nothing. **A first-year of nineteen was at the counter and read a sheet of paper of her own with about nine lines on it and put it back in a drawer that locks, and did not tell anybody what was in the drawer and has not told him and is not going to this week.**

A woman of about thirty was in the chair nearest the door and was not asked anything and was not thanked and was not apologised to, and the reason she is in that room is the temperature, and the temperature costs more than it did, and both of those are correct.

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.

*331. Monday of week 117, at about ten on a service road, and this entry is one word on a piece of paper that nobody said, four people who do not have it, and four words of a question that is not printed here.*

It is not a person, not a question at the foot, not a form, not a minute, not a notice, not a report, not a committee paper, not a leaflet, not a registration, not a constitution, and not a hardboard on two nails. It is printed, on a document, under a mark, and it is the only place in this case up to this moment where the word exists.** **Four things that are true about it: **it was not said by anybody; it is not printed in this entry; it was not said in a room on this Monday; and it was not said by the woman who holds a chair of forty-nine in a room in Crown Terrace**, who was not told, was not asked, does not know that four people have spent the morning discovering that it exists, and is not going to say it before a later day in this volume. **The word naming the public body is available in this movement and is not used in it.** The secondment is unchanged, is the only mechanism still running from an earlier volume, is not explained here, is not to be explained before the volume's close, and no character in this case can explain it and about four have tried. The printed rule is named, restricted, not withdrawn, about four hundred able to invoke it, does not contain the word, and has never required it to be used.**
