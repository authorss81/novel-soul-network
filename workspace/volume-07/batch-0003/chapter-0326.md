# Chapter 326 — The Eighteenth Sitting

The Wednesday morning of week one hundred and sixteen was the eighteenth sitting of an Exchange that has run on four-week spacing for nineteen years, and the book did not open, and a person who telephoned on the Tuesday got about nine words out and then could not finish, and the holder of the book did not write it, and the telephone moved from sixty to sixty-three, and the book stands at forty-nine lines, and the count was announced for the twenty-third time, and nobody in that room asked her for anything and she gave nothing in place of it and nobody thanked her.

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date. Three jobs attended, one of them in a building in the first of the four districts, and the day was in the middle of the week and nothing about the day was unusual in it.

The word was at nine and ten. The room off a service road is three hundred and thirty-eight days old and is warm and about four people came in on the Tuesday for the temperature and nobody asked any of them anything.

---

The shutters were up in a room above a line in a building in Saltmarket and the holder of the room said so at the start, as she says at the start of every sitting.

Nine chairs in a curve with the two ends turned in. The ninth was against the wall with its back to the room and it stayed where it was and nobody looked at it and nobody had to. There is a table, a tin that has never had a lock on it, and a book with four columns and forty-nine lines in it.

Nine people. A man of about forty-four, eighth attendance, who asked for something four weeks ago and was given nine words that were not it. A young man of twenty-one in attendance since he was seventeen, who keeps a count he has never given her. A young woman of twenty-one in attendance since she was fifteen, who has never been asked anything and was not asked anything this morning. And about five others undescribed.

**Nobody wrote down that she was there. Nobody writes it down.**

---

She did not open the book, and she said so herself, and it took her about four seconds, and she gave a reason, and the reason is hers.

A person telephoned on the Tuesday. They got about nine words out and then they could not finish the sentence. They stayed on the line for about four minutes afterwards and neither of them hung up and neither of them said anything else, and at the end of it the person said they would ring again, and has not rung.

She did not write it. There is no rule that says she has to and she has never once in nineteen years needed one and she has said so in about nine seconds.

---

**And the reason is a person and not a room, and nobody in that room improved on it, and about four people in that room understood what it was and not one of them said so, and the word about it is not used by anybody, in that room or in this entry.**

She was not asked why. She was not asked whether the person had rung back. A man of about forty-four, eighth attendance, who asked for a rule four weeks ago and was told he could do without her, sat down at about a quarter to ten and did not ask, and about four people in that room noticed that he had not asked, and he was not asked about that either, and about four people in that room noticed the noticing.

---

Nobody said anything for about nine seconds.

Then the holder said one more thing about it, unasked, in about nine seconds, and it is the only thing she said about anybody all morning:

"**I have not written down a thing somebody could not finish in nineteen years and I have not had somebody stop in the middle of one in nineteen years, and I am not going to tell anybody in this room that those are the same thing.**"

Nobody improved on it. Nobody thanked her. The young man of twenty-one said nothing and did not give her the count he keeps, and she did not ask him, and she has not asked him in nineteen years and is not going to.

---

And nobody asked her for the rule, and she gave nothing in place of it, and that is the whole of it.

She gave it for about the ninth time in nineteen years, to people who did not need it, and in the last week of a volume she said she had said it for the last time and that anybody in that room who needed it after that morning could do without her. Nine people were in that room. **Four weeks ago, in this room, one of those nine asked her for it again, having said out loud that he wanted to be able to tell somebody he had asked, and she gave him nine words that were not it, and the promise was kept, and about four people noticed the keeping and not one of them said so.**

This morning nobody asked. She did not say the rule, did not refuse it, did not paraphrase it, did not allude to it, did not say anything at all in place of it, and if a reader has been waiting nineteen years to be told what it is, this is not the volume.

---

The count came at about ten past ten, unasked, as it comes.

"**Forty-nine to sixty-three. Three in a fortnight. The book is up nothing and the telephone is up three, and the telephone was ahead of the book, and it was ahead four weeks ago, and it was ahead the time before that, and I am telling you the direction and not the difference, and I am not going to look into it in front of nine people.**"

"**You have announced that count how many times.**"

"**Twenty-three. Eighteen of those are the eighteen Wednesdays this book holds and five predate it, and there is a sheet with the five on it that I have not shown anybody in nineteen years and am not going to on a Wednesday.**"

Nobody said anything. **Nothing was converted. A call did not become a line and a line did not become a sitting and none of the three became either of the other two, and the number three was not explained and was not asked about, and the direction was given and the difference was not, and she has said the direction in five sittings running and has not looked into it once.**

---

The last line in that book went in four weeks ago, at the seventeenth sitting, and it is forty-nine days old. Nobody in that room gave an interval and nobody asked for one.

The telephone is fourteen ahead of the book, and the direction has not changed sign in this case since before a word was written down, and the difference is not a number of sittings and is not converted by anybody in that room, and she has not offered it and nobody has asked her for it.

In a converted shop unit in another part of the city there is a hardboard on two nails carrying nineteen lines in thirteen hands and fourteen names. It is a different object from that book, it is not in that room, and it has never been read in that room in nineteen years. Its newest line is thirty-four days old and is eleven words long and is a promise.

**The two objects are not to be added to one another by anybody. One of them holds what callers said to a woman's face and the other holds things people wrote down in a room and signed, and the difference between those two is the whole of what this case is about, and it is not settled in this entry and is not to be settled by anybody who was not in this room.**

The ninth chair was against the wall at the close and was not moved back, its mover is not named, and the answer about furniture was given for the ninth time and was not confirmed, because confirming it is a minute and she does not take minutes.

---

Nobody said anything in that room about the four days that had gone before it, and about nine people in four buildings had changed the subject in about nine minutes on the Monday, and a person of about twenty-nine in one of those rooms has said, in a corridor, that there is a word for the thing the four of them are in, and that none of the four of them has it, and **none of that was in this room and none of it was asked about and no one in this room knows about any of it.**

The printed rule is still named and restricted rather than repealed and about four hundred competent people in this city can still invoke it, and that number is not a conspiracy and is not on a list and is not reducible, and nobody in this case can produce it.

A Continuity Office still holds an emergency authority. A man of twenty-two was shown a page in a building in Crown Terrace ten days ago and found that his own appointment carries a reference, a count, a reason and no date, and the page is still in a file on the second side of that building with no date on it, and nobody in this room knows any of that and it was not raised.

---

At about half past eleven she said one more thing, and it was unasked, and nobody had asked her for anything since about ten past ten.

"**Somebody will ring or somebody will not. I am sixty-one and I have run this in a room above a line for nineteen years and I have never once not written it, and I am not going to start now, and I would like about four of the nine of you to notice that I have not explained myself and I am not going to.**"

Nobody said anything. A young man of twenty-one said one word, which was that he had noticed, and she said that was not the same thing and he said he knew.

He has not said another and has not been asked.

---

A room off a service road was warm at half past six that evening and there were about nine people in it, and there are two pieces of paper on the counter, and one of them is a sheet with a district office's mark on it and a ruled line under the mark carrying no characters, and the other one is a document with a reference in a form nobody in that room can read, and the two of them are not the same kind of object.

A cook of twenty-seven was at the sink and said nothing at all for about an hour, and a first-year of nineteen looked at the door when he came in and said nothing, and a woman of about thirty was in the chair nearest the door and was not asked anything and was not thanked and was not apologised to, and the reason she is in that room is the temperature, and the temperature costs more than it did, and both of those are correct.

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.

*329. Wednesday of week 116, at about ten on a service road, and this entry is one book that did not open, one person who could not finish, three calls, and a rule nobody asked for.*

*Conditions and docket.* **Three jobs attended, one in a building in the first of the four districts. The rate held at nine and ten. The heating is on and costs more and no figure of days off is carried. The room off a service road is three hundred and thirty-eight days old. A card in a rail in Lattice Ward is three hundred and forty-two days in the rail, a third object, and is not in that room and is not in the room above a line. The hardboard: twelve lines two hundred and fifty-eight, thirteen two hundred and nine, fourteen one hundred and seventy-four, fifteen one hundred and fifty-three, sixteen one hundred and twenty-eight, seventeen one hundred and ten, eighteen fifty-six, nineteen thirty-four. Wall unchanged: nineteen lines in thirteen hands and fourteen names, none added, none removed, none rubbed off. The first line is a cook's and is still the first line and the newest is eleven words and is the only promise on it. Eight items in a ring binder, unopened, unread. The woman's page is unread and is not this entry's business. The master's fifth line is blank and the drawer was not opened. Four letters about one piece of hardware were sent to four district councils in four districts and three application sheets were filed on the Friday or the Monday, one of them carrying nine names.**

*The sitting, and the two ordinals, and the open and shut pattern of the volume.* **Number: eighteenth. **Second of the five sittings of this volume, and one of the two of them at which the book does not open.** Gap since the last: four weeks, to the day. Day: a Wednesday, and she has not opened that book on a Friday in nineteen years and nobody asked her about that this morning. Room: above a line in a building in Saltmarket, shutters up, the holder announcing it at the start. Furniture: nine chairs in a curve, both ends turned in, the ninth against the wall with its back to the room and not moved back at the close; mover not named; the answer about furniture given for the ninth time and unconfirmed, because confirming it is a minute. Tin: never locked. Present: the holder, sixty-one; a man of about forty-four, eighth attendance; a young man of twenty-one, in attendance since seventeen, keeping a count he has never given her; a young woman of twenty-one, in attendance since fifteen, unnamed, unasked, and not the first-year of nineteen of another building; about five others undescribed. **The book did not open.**

*The reason, as a record, and the two words that are not used about it.* **Given by the holder, unasked, in about four seconds: a person telephoned on the Tuesday, got about nine words out and could not finish, stayed on the line about four minutes, said at the end that they would ring again, and has not rung. She did not write it. **There is no rule requiring her to and she has never needed one in nineteen years and said so in about nine seconds.** The reason concerns a person and not a room, and no character improved on it, and about four people in that room understood it and none of them said so, and no word of priority is used about it by anybody in that room or in this entry, and the reason is offered and not claimed. She was not asked why. She was not asked whether the person had rung back. A man of about forty-four, eighth attendance, who had asked for a rule four weeks ago, did not ask, was not asked about not asking, and about four people noticed the noticing. Then, unasked, in about nine seconds, and it is the only thing she said about anybody all morning: **that she has not written down a thing somebody could not finish in nineteen years and has not had somebody stop in the middle of one in nineteen years, and that she is not going to tell anybody that those are the same thing.** Not improved on. Not thanked. The young man of twenty-one said nothing and was not asked.**

*The rule that was not asked for, and what was given instead.* **Given, in nineteen years, about nine times, to people who did not need it. Promised at the end of the last week of the last volume to be said for the last time, with nine people present and told they could do without her. Four of those nine are in this room. **Four weeks ago, this room: one of the nine asked again, having first said aloud that he wanted to be able to tell somebody he had asked. She gave nine words that were not it. The promise was kept in front of the same nine. About four noticed the keeping. None of them said so.** This morning: no ask. Not said, not refused, not paraphrased, not alluded to, nothing given in its place. **The beat available and not taken: two standing claims about a room being the reason a book was shut are on the page sixteen weeks apart in the same hand and neither is repaired. This movement supplied a third reason and claimed nothing. Owner of the repair of the other two: a later pass, not this entry.**

*The four figures at the close, and the direction.* **Number: eighteenth. Book: forty-nine to forty-nine, up nothing. Telephone: sixty to sixty-three, up three in a fortnight. Announced count: twenty-two to twenty-three, of which eighteen correspond to the eighteen Wednesdays this book holds and five predate it on a separate sheet not shown to anybody in nineteen years. Spoken at about ten past ten, unasked, as it comes. **Four figures, printed and not converted: forty-nine, sixty-three, twenty-three, and nineteen years. Direction given, difference not, and she said she was not going to look into it in front of nine people; the telephone is fourteen ahead of the book, the direction has not changed sign in this case, and the difference is not a number of sittings.** The last line went in at the seventeenth sitting, forty-nine days ago; nobody gave an interval and nobody asked for one. The hardboard in a converted shop unit in another part of the city is a different object, is not in that room, has never been read in that room, and its newest line is thirty-four days old; the two are not to be added to one another by anybody.**

*What was not in that room and was not raised.* The four days of four rooms changing the subject in nine minutes. The nine names on an application sheet, and the woman of about forty-four who has not stopped thinking about where a piece of paper goes. The person of about twenty-nine in one of the four rooms who has said there is a word for the thing the four of them are in and that none of them has it. The printed rule: named, restricted, not withdrawn, about four hundred able to invoke it. The Continuity Office, and a man of twenty-two's appointment, which carries a reference, a count, a reason and no date, and one true sentence about a standard adopted as temporary, said once in a room in Crown Terrace and not improved on since. **None of it was raised. None of it was asked about. Nobody in that room knows about any of it, and this entry places them side by side and joins them in no way.**

*What the day did not touch.* **He taught, demonstrated, assessed and assisted nothing and is the subject of nothing in this entry. No panel, no marker, no relay, nothing run, nothing forwarded. **No name is attached to anything that happened on this Wednesday.** The Exchange sat and did not open. The secondment is unchanged and the chair of forty-nine has still not been told. The heating is on, costs more, four names on a lease cost more, and the fortnight is unpaid and unresolved, and about four people came into a room off a service road for the temperature. The hardboard was not written on. The woman's page is unread. The standing question is not stated in this entry and carries no number. The question written once on a service road in week one hundred and eight is not answered here, is not in anybody's mouth, and its sentence is not printed in this entry. No message arrived from anybody. Nobody thanked anybody. Entry 329.**
