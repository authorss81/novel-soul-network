# Chapter 330 — The Junction Holds

The Thursday morning of week one hundred and seventeen was about eleven hours long, and in it a man of twenty-two answered a telephone in a building four miles from a room off a service road, and about four districts in this city made about four hundred small decisions through him and made almost none of them themselves, and a hospital junction held, and nobody died, and the man of twenty-two was right about the arithmetic, and the people he routed through him began, in about the fourth hour, to stop asking him the things they had been about to ask, and the tenth one was the one that mattered, and a man of twenty-nine who works on lifts said the true thing about it in nine words at about five o'clock, and the first time he said it nobody improved on him, and this time about four minutes later somebody did.

**He taught, demonstrated, assessed and assisted nothing. Nobody asked him to and he would have said no. Nobody in that exchange said *why* and nobody has said it since and nobody is going to.

About nine. About four hundred decisions in about eleven hours, and this is what the day was made of.

Not one of them was technical and not one of them was about the practice of anything. They were about four minutes: whether a crew goes now or in twenty minutes, whether a door on a ground floor stays open while four people a day pass it, whether a gate is held or not held, whether a car park is used for about two hours by people who work there, whether a telephone at the end of a shift rings or does not ring, whether a person is sent to a building to find out whether another person has been told.

He was right about nearly all of them, and the reason he was right is that he is the person other people tell things to, and the reason that works is about four years old and is not a technique and belongs to him and cannot be taught because he has never tried to teach it.

Nobody has ever described him as indispensable, and about four people in four buildings described him as reachable during that day and none of them used that word to his face.
