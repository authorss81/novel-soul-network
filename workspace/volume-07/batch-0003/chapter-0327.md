# Chapter 327 — Four People In One Building

The Friday evening of week one hundred and sixteen was the evening the thing that had been travelling through this case for five weeks arrived in one building in Crown Terrace and became visible in about four people, by name, as a thing that is happening to them and not as a thing that is happening to a network, and nobody in that room said what it was, and no sheet was written, and the man who owns the building and pays for the room was asked four times in one week whether he was going to write it down and said no four times.

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date. Two jobs attended and neither of them was in the building this entry is about. A shift in a company training room is not a job and has never been on this docket.

The word was at nine and ten. The room off a service road is three hundred and forty days old and is warm and about four people came in on the Thursday for the temperature and one of them is a woman of about thirty who was not asked anything.

---

This is the sixth Friday of this volume and it is a sixth thing, and the five before it were five different things, and the six are not joined to one another by anybody and are not to be joined.

The room is the same room. About nine folding chairs, a counter, a telephone on the company line by the counter, a run that is a hold with a count in it, and a release that is supposed to arrive when somebody in the room says so.

The post at the back is not described in this entry either. That is the second week in a row and it is not a habit and it is a decision and the reason is the same as last week's and it is not anybody's business what is on one company's post.

Three weeks ago about nine people said the word together in this room. Nobody has written down what happened that night in any building in this city, and the only place it is written down is a load book, and a load book is not a record anybody can produce.

---

One. The woman who runs the room is thirty-six and has a lanyard and holds no post and is owed nothing. She has run this room for about four years on her own voice.

**She cannot start any more.**

She began three of the last four runs by looking at a man of about twenty-seven and waiting for him to move. He has not known that. He is not a person who has ever once asked her to do it and he was not asked and he does not know, and on the third of those three she said, before anybody had moved, "**Are we going.**"

That is not a thing she has said in four years. In four years she has begun that run by saying a number.

Nobody in that room said anything about it and about four of them noticed and about four of them have been noticing for about a fortnight and not one of them has said a word to anybody about it, including to each other.

---

Two. The woman of about forty-one is on no list and has been in this room for about four years and said a word she was never asked to say, three weeks before the first of those nine voices, without knowing that she was saying it. She has not spoken in this room since.

**She cannot leave.**

She stays about four minutes past the end of every run now, sitting down again afterwards, which she did not do for four years. On the fourth Friday she did not go at about nine o'clock at all, when about four people had got up, and a man of about thirty-four said her name, and she got up.

She has not been asked about it. She has not been asked about it twice. She is not to be asked about it, and the reason is hers and is not recorded in her mouth and is not to be asked for.

---

Three. The man of about twenty-seven has been on the company's list for about four years and decided, three weeks ago, in about nine seconds, that a word is a person and a paper is a record and that he was going to do what the paper said. He has said nothing since about that decision and is not to be asked.

**He cannot answer a question without something in front of him to read from.**

On this Friday, a woman of about thirty-one from a different company in the same building came in to watch for about an hour and asked the room a question at the end of it, which was an ordinary question, and he did not answer it. He said he would need to check.

There is nothing to check. It was a question about how the room worked.

Then he asked the woman of thirty-six to read him the count off the company's own sheet before he would answer it, and she read it, and he answered it, and it was correct.

**Nobody improved on it. Nobody thanked him. The woman of thirty-one from the other company has not been in that room since and does not know about any of it and was not shown any of it and nobody told her anything.**

---

Four. The man of twenty-nine who works on lifts is on no list and has been in that room for about four years and has said nothing on any Friday of this volume.

**He cannot begin a thing without knowing who is going to finish it.**

On this Friday he came in at about half past five, which he has done about four Fridays out of four, and he stood by the door for about nine minutes before he sat down.

On the Friday before, he came in and asked the man of thirty-four, in about nine seconds and in a corridor, whether anybody would be in the building on Saturday. He was told that about four people would be. He said, "**Right,**" and did not say anything else about it.

Nobody in that building has asked him why. He has said the true thing out loud three times in about four years and nobody improved on any of them, and he wrote the newest line on a hardboard on two nails in a converted shop unit off a service road, and the cost of that line was that a man who has said he will not take the middle is now findable as a man who has said he will not take the middle.

He is one of the four, and he is the only one of the four who has already paid for a thing, and nobody in that room knows the price and he has not said it.

---

**And nobody wrote it down, and there is no sheet, and nobody is going to write one.**

The man of thirty-four buys the training for the company and pays for the room and the room is his to keep open, and he has said twice in about four weeks that he is not going to be the man in the lanyard, and both of those statements are his own and are not to be joined to each other.

He has been asked, by about four people in one week, whether he is going to put it in a report.

He said no four times, and on the fourth occasion he added about nine seconds, and this is what he said:

"**I have run this room for about four years and I have never once written down anything about a person in it, and that is not because I am careful. It is because there has never been anything to write down, and there is now, and I have been asked four times this week, and I am going to keep saying no, and I would like somebody to notice that I have not given a reason and that I am not going to.**"

Nobody improved on it. Nobody thanked him. He gave no reason and he is not going to and the record of this entry says he gave none and does not supply one.

---

About four people in that room knew, on that Friday night, that four people in that building had been altered in the last three weeks by the removal of a cue they had been taught to need.

Nobody said it. Nobody wrote it. **There is no form in that company, no report, no register, no minute, no note and no column, and the four of them were not asked, and about four other people in that building are going to be in that room on Friday and are not going to be told.**

The word has not been said. The injury has not been named. The mechanism is not a mechanism and is nobody's doing and has never been anybody's fault, and the finding about it, wherever it lands, is not in this room and is not going to be in this room.

---

A room off a service road was warm at about ten that night. A cook of twenty-seven said nothing at all. A man of fifty-one with a tool bag read the whole board standing up and said nothing. A first-year of nineteen was at the counter and looked at the door when he came in and said nothing, and the reason is not recorded in her mouth and is not to be asked for.

A woman of about thirty was in the chair nearest the door and was not asked anything and was not thanked and was not apologised to, and the reason she is in that room is the temperature, and the temperature costs more than it did, and both of those are correct.

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.

*330. Friday of week 116, at about ten on a service road, and this entry is four people in one building, and no sheet.*

It is not a mechanism, it is nobody's doing, it has never been anybody's fault, and the finding about it — that waiting is not a fault and that a person can be damaged by the absence of something they were never supposed to learn to do without — is not in that room and is not going to be in that room. **The four were not asked, nobody improved on anything, and about four other people in that building are going to be in that room on Friday and are not going to be told.** Owner of the thread: the case. It is to be tested and not to be closed, and the two things this volume may not spend are that finding and the sentence about a standard adopted as temporary with no date on it.**
