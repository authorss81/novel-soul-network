# Chapter 345 — The First Yes

The Monday evening of week one hundred and twenty-three was the evening a man of about thirty-three asked a woman of thirty-one with a key in her pocket to be at a door on the Friday night for about four hours, and she said yes in about four seconds, and **she is the only person in this volume who has ever been asked to carry anything, and nobody in this case had known that until this happened, and about four people in that room found it out at the same time and did not say so.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date. Eleven attended, and the last of the eleven was in a building in the first of the four districts and finished at about half past five.

The word was at nine and ten. The room off a service road is three hundred and eighty-five days old and is warm and about four people came in on the Friday for the temperature and nobody asked any of them anything.

---

She is thirty-one and she has a key in her pocket and has had it for about two years, and the key is one of two that exist for a converted shop unit off a service road, and the other one cannot be asked back by anybody. She keeps a diary. Nobody in this case has asked her for anything in about three movements and she has not offered.

She came in at about half past six on the Monday evening with the key already out of her pocket, which about four people noticed, and she had it in her hand for the whole of the four minutes and put it back afterwards.

Nobody asked her why she had it out. **She has been asked why she keeps it by exactly one person in about two years and she answered that person in four words and has not offered it to anybody since, and the four words are not in this entry and are not to be asked her about.**

The man of about thirty-three asked her at about half past six on the Monday evening in about four minutes, at the counter, in front of about four people, and he did not build up to it and he did not explain why and he did not apologise for the timing.

He said: "**On Friday night, from about nine until about one, you would be at your own door. You would open it. If anybody came who you did not know, you would say no, and if you said no then nothing would happen, and nobody would ring you afterwards to ask what happened, and nobody would come to your door a second time.**"

She said yes in about four seconds.

Then she said the thing that is the whole of it, and it took about nine seconds, and nobody in that room improved on it.

"**And I want one thing said, and it is not a condition, it is the whole of it. If I say no at that door then nobody is to come and find out why. Not you. Not anybody in this room. And I am not going to be asked about it afterwards in a room, and if somebody asks me I am going to say nothing, and that is not me being difficult. That is the only thing in this that is worth doing.**"

He said: "**Agreed. And you will not be asked by me and there is no version of Friday in which anybody comes to that door twice.**"

Nobody thanked her. Nobody thanked him. She wrote it in her own diary that night, in her own hand, with her own pen, and that is the only record of it that exists and it is not a form and it is not a register and it is on no list and nobody in this case can see it.

---

**There are about nine of them.** Nine people, nine buildings or nine doorways, four hours, and each of them asked separately and each of them said yes separately, and none of them knows what the other eight have agreed to and none of them has been told and none of them has asked. About four of the nine have carried something in this case already, in a stairwell or a car park or a corridor, and about five of the nine have not, and the two groups do not know that they are two groups.

Each of the nine can say no at a door. That is the only thing about the nine that was arranged in advance, and it was arranged by being said, once, by the man of about thirty-three, to each of them, in four minutes each, over two days, in nine different places, and by nobody else.

There is no list. Nobody coordinating it. Nobody who checks on any of the nine and nobody who will know whether any of the nine is still at a door at about one o'clock.

**And it is not a mechanism and it is not a device and it is not a thing anybody in this case has done before, and nothing about it got easier and nobody taught any part of it to anybody.**

---

At about seven he said the thing he says.

He said it out loud, at the counter, in about four seconds, and it was the oldest thing he does and about four people in that room had heard him say it in five other weeks in about four years.

"**Then I'll take the middle.**"

The man of about thirty-three said no.

"**No. That is the whole of what we have been not doing since the Thursday of week one hundred and seventeen, which is about six weeks, and you did it once in a room in about four minutes eighteen days ago and one of the four of us has been living in it ever since, and I am not being rude, and you are not the middle, and if you were the middle then nine people who said yes this week would have said yes to you and they did not.**"

Nobody said anything for about nine seconds.

**He did not argue. He has not argued about it since and is not going to, and about four people in that room watched a man of twenty-two hear the word no in a room and not do the thing he usually does about it, and not one of them has said so, and it is not to be asked him about.**

He said: "All right. Then what do you need."

The man of about thirty-three said: "**Nothing. That is the first thing I have been told no to about in three weeks and it is the first no I have had off anybody in about six weeks, and I would like it recorded that I needed nothing, and I am not going to record it anywhere, so it will have to stay in the room.**"

Nobody improved on it. Nobody thanked anybody.

---

A first-year of nineteen was at the counter and said one small unasked thing in about four seconds, and it was about a door number and not about any of it, and nobody improved on it and nobody thanked her, and her one document-shaped finding in this case was spent sixty-one days ago, which is about nine weeks, and this is not it and there is a half hour of silence from an earlier month that is still not in her mouth and is not to be asked her for.

A cook of twenty-seven was at the sink and said nothing at all. A woman of about thirty was in the chair nearest the door and was not asked anything and was not thanked and was not apologised to, and the reason she is in that room is the temperature, and the temperature costs more than it did, and both of those are correct.

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.
