# Chapter 349 — The Twentieth Sitting

The Wednesday morning of week one hundred and twenty-four was the twentieth sitting of an Exchange that has run on four-week spacing for nineteen years, and **the book did not open, the telephone moved from sixty-six to sixty-nine, the count was announced for the twenty-fifth time, the book stands at fifty lines, and the reason she gave was about a room, and about four people in that room noticed that nobody said anything about the reason, and not one of them said anything, and this entry does not say what they were going to say either.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date. Seven jobs attended, three of them in buildings in the second and third of the four districts, and the day was in the middle of the week and nothing about the day was unusual in it.

The word was at nine and ten. The room off a service road is three hundred and ninety-four days old and is warm and about four people came in on the Tuesday for the temperature and nobody asked any of them anything.

---

The shutters were up in a room above a line in a building in Saltmarket and the holder of the room said so at the start, as she says at the start of every sitting.

Nine chairs in a curve with the two ends turned in. The ninth was against the wall with its back to the room and it stayed where it was and nobody looked at it and nobody had to. There is a table, a tin that has never had a lock on it, and a book with four columns and fifty lines in it when the morning started, and the book was on the table all morning and was not opened.

Nine people. A man of about forty-four, tenth attendance, who asked for something twelve weeks ago and was given nine words that were not it and has not asked since. A young man of twenty-one in attendance since he was seventeen, who keeps a count he has never given her and did not give her this morning. A young woman of twenty-one in attendance since she was fifteen, who has never been asked anything and was not asked anything this morning. And about five others undescribed.

**Nobody wrote down that she was there. Nobody writes it down.**

---

The light in that room comes in over about nine feet of a landing and it is on at about a quarter to ten and off again by about half past eleven, and about four of the nine have sat with their backs to it and about four have not, and nobody in that room has ever suggested moving a chair.

A young woman of twenty-one has been in that room since she was fifteen and has never been asked anything in six years, and she was not asked anything this morning, and she was not asked anything at the eighteenth sitting or the nineteenth either, and she is not in this entry twice.

---

She did not open the book, and she said so herself, and it took her about four seconds, and she gave a reason, and the reason is hers.

A person telephoned on the Tuesday. They said one thing to her and the thing was about a room — a room in a building in the third of the four districts, in which about nine people had arranged to be at about nine o'clock on the Friday night, and they had not been asked to, and they had each been asked, and the person on the telephone could not get from the room to anything else in about nine seconds and then stopped.

She did not write it. There is no rule that says she has to and there has not been one in nineteen years.

---

Then she said one more thing about it, unasked, in about nine seconds, and nobody improved on it and nobody thanked her.

"**I have written down about nine people in nineteen years and I have written down a room, and I am not going to say anything else about that this morning.**"

Nobody said anything for about nine seconds.

One of the nine started to say something and did not, and about four people in that room noticed that he had started and did not say what it was, and he was not asked about it, and about four people in that room noticed the noticing, and **what any of them was going to say is not in this entry and is not on any surface and is not to be said now by anybody, and there are two claims in this case about a firstness of that kind and both in an earlier volume and both on the page, and neither of them is touched by this morning and neither of them is confirmed by it.**

She was not asked why. She was not asked whether the person had telephoned back. A man of about forty-four, tenth attendance, who had asked for a rule twelve weeks ago and was told he could do without her, sat down at about a quarter to ten and looked at the ninth chair, and about four people in that room noticed him looking at it, and he did not ask about it, and he was not asked about that either.

---

The one small thing happened at about half past ten and it took about nine seconds and it was not about the book.

"**What happens to the sheet with the five on it.**"

"**Nothing. It stays where it is.**"

He did not ask a second question. She did not offer anything else and nobody in that room asked her for anything else and about four people in that room heard the exchange and about four did not.

---

The count came at about ten past ten, unasked, as it comes.

"**Sixty-six to sixty-nine. Three in four weeks, to the day. The book is where it was and the telephone is up three, and the telephone is ahead of the book, and it was ahead four weeks ago, and it was ahead the time before that, and I am telling you the direction and not the difference, and I am not going to look into it in front of nine people.**"

"**You have announced that count how many times.**"

"**Twenty-five. Twenty of those are the twenty Wednesdays this book holds and five predate it, and there is a sheet with the five on it that I have not shown anybody in nineteen years and am not going to on a Wednesday, and the sheet is not in this room.**"

Nobody said anything. **Nothing was converted. A call did not become a line and a line did not become a sitting and a sitting did not become a count, and the three was not explained and was not asked about, and the direction was given and the difference was not, and she has said the direction in seven sittings running and has not looked into it once.**

---

Nobody asked her for the rule, and she gave nothing in place of it, and that is the whole of it.

She gave it for about the ninth time in nineteen years, to people who did not need it, and in an earlier volume she said in front of nine people that she had said it for the last time. Four of those nine are in this room. **Twelve weeks ago one of the nine asked again and was given nine words that were not it; eight weeks ago nobody asked, and four weeks ago nobody asked; this morning nobody asked.** She is not asked, and she does not say it, and she does not refuse it, and she does not paraphrase it and does not allude to it and does not give anything in its place, and anybody who has been waiting nineteen years to be told what it is will not be told it this morning, and this entry does not say what it is either.

---

Nobody in that room said anything about the four weeks that had gone before it.

There were nine doors in six buildings and two of them had been shut for about nine minutes each on purpose on a Friday night, and a piece of paper with eleven lines on it in a woman's handwriting that a man of fifty-eight had asked for and had been refused, and about eleven people who had lost a week, and a man of twenty-two who had cut something of his own. **None of it was raised. None of it was asked about. Nobody in that room knows about any of it, and this entry places the facts side by side and joins them in no way.**

The printed rule is still named and restricted rather than repealed and about four hundred competent people in this city can still invoke it, and that number is not a conspiracy and is not on a list and is not reducible, and nobody can produce it.

A Continuity Office still holds an emergency authority. A man of twenty-two was shown a page in a building in Crown Terrace about two months ago and found that his own appointment carries a reference, a count, a reason and no date, and the page is still in a file on the second side of that building with no date on it, and nobody in this room knows any of that and it was not raised.

The ninth chair was against the wall at the close and was not moved back, its mover is not named, and the answer about furniture was given for the tenth time and was not confirmed, because confirming it is a minute and she does not take minutes.

---

A room off a service road was warm at about half past six that evening and about nine people were in it, and a cook of twenty-seven was at the sink and said nothing at all, and a man of fifty-one with a tool bag said nothing, and a first-year of nineteen was at the counter and said one small unasked thing in about four seconds about a bus and it was not about any of this and nobody improved on it and nobody thanked her.

A woman of about thirty was in the chair nearest the door and was not asked anything and was not thanked and was not apologised to, and the reason she is in that room is the temperature, and the temperature costs more than it did, and both of those are correct.

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.

*352. Wednesday of week 124, at about ten on a service road, and this entry is a book that stayed shut, a telephone that moved by three, a reason about a room that about four people did not comment on, a question about a sheet, and a rule nobody asked for.*

*Conditions and docket.* **Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; seven attended, three of them in the second and third of the four districts. One room above a line in a building in Saltmarket, shutters up, about nine chairs in a curve, the ninth against the wall with its back to the room and not moved back at the close, mover not named, a tin that has never been locked, a table. Nine people: the holder, sixty-one; a man of about forty-four, tenth attendance, who has not asked since the eighth; a young man of twenty-one, in attendance since seventeen, keeping a count he has never given her and did not give her this morning; a young woman of twenty-one, in attendance since fifteen, unnamed, unasked, and not the first-year of nineteen of another building; about five others undescribed. The book was on the table all morning and was not opened. One telephone call taken on the Tuesday, about nine seconds of a thing about a room, and the caller stopped. Nothing written. One question from a man of about forty-four at about half past ten and one answer of four words. The rate held at nine and ten. The heating is on and costs more and no figure of days off is carried. The room off a service road is three hundred and ninety-four days old. A card in a rail in Lattice Ward is three hundred and ninety-eight days in the rail, a third object, and is not in that room and is not in a room above a line. The hardboard: twelve lines three hundred and fourteen, thirteen two hundred and sixty-five, fourteen two hundred and thirty, fifteen two hundred and nine, sixteen one hundred and eighty-four, seventeen one hundred and sixty-six, eighteen one hundred and twelve, nineteen ninety. Wall unchanged: nineteen lines in thirteen hands and fourteen names, none added, none removed, none rubbed off. The first line is a cook's and is still the first line and the newest is eleven words and is the only promise on it. Eight items in a ring binder, unopened, unread. The woman's page is unread. The master's fifth line is blank and the drawer was not opened. The Exchange sat today. Its last sitting was a Wednesday twenty-eight days ago, to the day. The four figures at the close of this entry, printed and not converted: fifty lines in the book, sixty-nine on the telephone, the count announced twenty-five times, and nineteen years.**

*The sitting, the ordinal, and the open-and-shut pattern of the volume.* **Number: twentieth. Fourth of the five sittings of this volume, and the second of the two at which the book is shut. Gap since the last: four weeks, to the day. Day: a Wednesday, and she has not opened that book on a Friday in nineteen years and nobody asked her about that this morning. Present: as recorded above. The book was on the table and was not opened. Owner: the case.**

*The call, and what was not written.* **Taken on the Tuesday, on that telephone, by the holder. Content as given: one thing, about a room in a building in the third of the four districts, in which about nine people had arranged to be at about nine o'clock on the Friday night, and had not been asked to, and had each been asked. Length of the thing said: about nine seconds, and then the caller stopped. Whether the caller telephoned again: not this morning and not on this record. Written down: nothing. Rule requiring it: none, and there has not been one in nineteen years. The holder's statement, unasked, about nine seconds: that in nineteen years she has written down about nine people and has written down a room, and that she is not going to say anything else about that this morning. Improved on: by nobody. Thanked: by nobody. Owner: her, and the case.**

*The unsaid thing, as a record of what was not done.* **Silence after her statement: about nine seconds. One of the nine began a sentence and did not finish it. What any of the nine was going to say: not recorded, not on any surface, not in this entry. Number of people who noticed that he had begun: about four. Times he was asked about it: none. Times he has been asked since: none. Two claims about a firstness of that kind exist in this case, both in an earlier volume, both on the page, both in the same hand, and neither is confirmed, contradicted, quoted or touched by this morning. Owner: nobody, and the case, and a later pass over the two earlier pages is the owner of those two and not this one.**

*The four figures, and the direction.* **Book: fifty to fifty, up none, and not opened. Telephone: sixty-six to sixty-nine, up three in four weeks, to the day. Announced count: twenty-four to twenty-five, of which twenty correspond to the twenty Wednesdays this book holds and five predate it on a separate sheet not shown to anybody in nineteen years and not in the room. Spoken at about ten past ten, unasked, as it comes. Four figures, printed and not converted: fifty, sixty-nine, twenty-five, and nineteen years. Direction given, difference not, and she said she was not going to look into it in front of nine people. The telephone is ahead of the book, and was ahead four weeks ago, and was ahead the time before that; the direction has not changed sign; the difference is not a number of sittings. Nobody gave an interval for the line that was not written and nobody asked for one. Owner: the case, and nineteen years.**

*The rule that was not asked for.* **Given in nineteen years about nine times, to people who did not need it, and promised at the end of an earlier volume to be said for the last time, with nine people present and told they could do without her. Four of those nine are in this room. Twelve weeks ago one of the nine asked again and was given nine words that were not it. Eight weeks ago nobody asked. Four weeks ago nobody asked. This morning: no ask, from anybody, including the man of about forty-four, tenth attendance, who looked at the ninth chair at about a quarter to ten instead. Not said, not refused, not paraphrased, not alluded to, nothing given in its place, and this entry does not say what it is either. The single question put to her this morning was about a sheet with five things on it, and the answer was four words, and there was no second question. Owner: the case, and the not taking is the whole of the beat and there was no beat.**

*What was not in that room and was not raised.* **Nine doors in six buildings. Two doors shut for about nine minutes each on purpose. One piece of paper with eleven lines on it in a woman's handwriting, requested by an office and refused. About eleven people who lost a week. A man of twenty-two who cut an arrangement of about four years standing with a man of twenty-one. A man of about thirty-three holding four buildings and about nine hundred doors for about twenty-seven days. The printed rule: named, restricted, not withdrawn, about four hundred able to invoke it. The Continuity Office, and a man of twenty-two's appointment, which carries a reference, a count, a reason and no date. None of it was raised, none of it was asked about, nobody in that room knows about any of it, and this entry places the facts beside each other and joins them in no way. No name is attached to anything that happened in that room. The secondment is unchanged and the chair of forty-nine has still not been told. The heating is on, costs more, four names on a lease cost more, and the fortnight is unpaid and unresolved. The hardboard was not written on. The woman's page in the ring binder is unread. The standing question is not stated in this entry and carries no number. The question written once on a service road in an earlier week is not answered here, is not in anybody's mouth, and its sentence is not printed in this entry. No message arrived from anybody. Nobody died. Nobody thanked anybody. Entry 352.**
