# Chapter 343 — Four Words

The Thursday morning of week one hundred and twenty-two was the morning a man of twenty-two went four miles to a workroom that was not his and was asked a question four words long and answered it with something that was true and was not yes, and **he said a sentence he has said to her six times in about four years and meant it as a promise on every one of the six, and she did not react to the number, and nobody in that building was told anything and about four people noticed that he had gone out into the corridor and come back in.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date. Six jobs attended, one of them on a stair in a building in the first of the four districts, at about eight in the morning, in the dark, because the light on that stair comes on at about eight.

The word was at nine and ten. The room off a service road is three hundred and eighty-one days old and is warm and about four people came in on the Wednesday for the temperature and nobody asked any of them anything.

---

He walked it. Four miles, and he could have taken a bus and did not, and the reason he did not is not one anybody asked him for and is not in this entry.

The workroom is a workroom with about four people in it and a table and a door that opens onto a corridor, and it is her Thursday and it has been her Thursday for about four years, and he is not the person who speaks for her and she is not the person who speaks for him.

About four of those four people were in the room. He came in at about eleven, said good morning to the room, was given a chair, and then went back out into the corridor about four minutes later on the pretence of a telephone, which is a pretence and which about four people in that room noticed.

---

The corridor. About four minutes. A window at one end with the light coming through it and a door at the other end that was shut and a radiator that had been on since about seven and had not been on before that week because of what it costs.

The asking was four words long and it was not an accusation and it was not a favour and it was not softened.

"**Do you still want this?**"

---

He took about two minutes to answer it and he did not look at her while he did it and he has not been able to account for the two minutes since.

"**I don't know. And I have thought about it every day since the Thursday of week one hundred and seventeen, which is about five weeks, which is longer than I have thought about anything, and what I keep arriving at is that you are the only person I have ever said a true thing to in a room. If there isn't a Thursday then there is nobody I would say a true thing to, and I would be worse at it and not better.**
**And that is not yes. I know it is not yes. I am giving it to you because you asked me a question and I have never not answered one, and I would rather you told me now that I have answered a different question than that we found out in about four weeks.**
**And I am going to say the other thing, because if I do not say it now I will carry it, and carrying it is what I do. I can carry it. I have said that sentence to you six times and I meant it as a promise every single time and you have never once believed me and I have never once asked you to.**"

She did not react to the number. She has not referred to the number since. He has not raised it and is not going to.

---

Then she said the limit, and it took about four seconds, and she said it once and she did not ask him about it afterwards.

"**Then here is the limit and I am not asking you about it. If you decide something and you tell me after, that is a Tuesday. If you decide something and you tell me before, that is a decision you made twice and I will not be in the second one.**"

He accepted it in about two seconds. He said one word, and the word was *all right*, and it was acceptance and nothing else, and about two seconds is a very short time to accept a thing like that and he has not been asked about the two seconds and is not going to be.

---

Then about nine seconds went by.

Nine seconds in a corridor with a radiator in it, and he looked at the floor, and then he asked her whether the ground floor of a building in the third of the four districts was free on the Friday morning, and he said the reason, which was that the table runs about eleven in a morning and if the room is not free they move it to the Tuesday and by the Tuesday it is a different week and the six that did not happen last time do not happen at all.

She said: "**That is a different question.**"

He said: "**I know.**"

---

The four minutes were over at about eleven and about four minutes. He went back into the workroom, said nothing to the four people in it, was given a chair, and left at about half past eleven.

He said goodbye to the room rather than to her, which is what he has done on about nine Thursdays in about four years, and one of the four people in that room has noticed that and has not mentioned it, and there is no arrangement about it and there is not going to be one.

She went back into her own workroom and shut the door of the corridor and did not shut the door of the room, and about four people looked up and about four did not, and she picked up the thing she had been holding and carried on.

The rest of that Thursday was four jobs, two of them in one building, and he finished them. He has not been in that workroom since and she has not invited him and he has not asked.

**That is the whole of the four minutes and the whole of the failure in it, and the failure is not that he is wrong about the room or about the Tuesday or about the table, and he is not wrong about any of the three and has not been. The failure is that it took him nine seconds to turn a limit that was not about a job into a job, and she watched him do it, and neither of them said a word about that, and he has not said a word about it since and is not going to.**

The second half of that is the part that is going to matter in about four weeks and neither of them can see it from where they are standing, and it is not that he turned it into a job. He would have been able to live with turning it into a job. **It is that she said the word job and he did not hear her say it, and that she has watched him not hear a thing like that six times, and that this is the seventh, and that she did not tell him it was the seventh because she was not going to be the person who keeps the count of him.**

Nobody in that workroom was told anything. About four of them noticed that he had gone out and come back in and not one of them has said so and none of them is going to. Nobody thanked anybody.

---

A room off a service road was warm at about half past six that evening and about nine people were in it, and a first-year of nineteen was at the counter and said one small unasked thing in about four seconds about a bus timetable and it was not about any of it and nobody improved on it and nobody thanked her. A cook of twenty-seven was at the sink and said nothing at all, which she does on about four evenings in five.

A man of about thirty-three came in at about seven and said nothing about the four miles or the corridor or the workroom and about four people in that room knew that something had happened that morning and about four did not, and he has not asked and nobody has told him.

A first-year of nineteen said a second small thing at about half past seven, in about four seconds, about a shop that had shut on that road and not reopened, and it was not about any of it and nobody improved on it and nobody thanked her.

A woman of about thirty was in the chair nearest the door and was not asked anything and was not thanked and was not apologised to, and the reason she is in that room is the temperature, and the temperature costs more than it did, and both of those are correct.

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.

What the nine seconds were: the interval in which a limit that was not about a job was turned into a job, in the presence of the person who had set it. Whether that was said: not said, by either of them, then or since. Owner: him, and he is not going to be told about it and has not asked.**
