# Chapter 348 — What Was Lost, On A Counter

The Monday morning of week one hundred and twenty-four was the morning after a night in which about eleven people in this city lost a week, and the week is not coming back, and the cost of it is on a piece of paper made by a woman of twenty-four in her own hand out of the words of the people it happened to, and **a Continuity Office with the power to put a reference, a count and a reason on it sat in a room with that paper for about an hour and could not make the week back and did not write anything down.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date. Eleven attended, two of them in the afternoon rather than the morning. Those two were in a building in the second of the four districts and neither of them was attended in the morning and both were chased and both were attended by about four in the afternoon.

The word was at nine and ten. The room off a service road is three hundred and ninety-two days old and is warm and about four people came in on the Friday for the temperature and nobody asked any of them anything.

---

**There is a piece of paper.** It is about the size of both hands together and it is in the hand of a woman of twenty-four and it has eleven lines on it and each line is about a week.

It is not a form. It is not a minute. It is not a return, a schedule, a claim, a notification, a return of a count, a list of losses, a schedule of hardship, or a register. **It has no reference, no date, no heading, no print, and no box, and it is not signed, and there is no second copy of it anywhere, and it is on nobody's system because there is no system in this city that a woman of twenty-four's handwriting would go into.**

Four of the eleven wrote their own line on it themselves, in that room, with her pen, and about seven of the eleven said theirs to her over the course of a week and she wrote down what they said and did not improve any of it.

---

About nine people were in a room in a building in the third of the four districts on the Monday morning and it is the same room as every other room in this case: about nine chairs, the same furniture, no name on the door, nothing on the wall, not a meeting and no minute of it.

About four of the eleven were in that room. The rest were not and were not asked to come and about four of them have said what they lost to somebody who is not in this case and nobody has collected it and it is not going to be.

What the eleven said, in their own words, and nobody improved on any of it:

A woman of about thirty-eight: a week of shifts she had already had, and about two days of notice she had not been able to spend, and a fortnight's rent on the other side of it.

A man of about thirty-four: a course with about four weeks in it, and the fourth week is on a Tuesday, and the college will not put him back into it in the autumn because the autumn one is full and there is not another one until the year after that.

A woman of about sixty-one: a week of a contract to clean four offices, and a letter on Friday morning saying that the four offices had found somebody else, and the letter is correct and she does not think it is anybody's fault and has not said so out loud to anybody and said so in that room to about four people.

A man of about twenty-seven: a week of about nine appointments he had booked, and a message on his telephone on the Saturday saying the list had been made new, and the new list is on a board in a clinic and his name is on it and his times are not.

---

One of the eleven telephoned that room at about eleven and spoke to the woman of twenty-four for about four seconds and the call was about a bus fare and a shift and a Tuesday and the woman of twenty-four wrote one line onto the paper in front of her while she was on the telephone, in her own hand, and gave the line back to the caller to check before she put it down, and the caller checked it and did not change it.

**She is the only person in this city who has that list and there is a second copy of nothing, and about seven of the eleven are somewhere else in the city this morning with a week gone and no piece of paper at all, and about four of them have said so to somebody and about three have not said it to anybody.**

---

A Continuity Office man of fifty-eight with a bad hip came at about half past ten and was not asked to and did not have to and about four people in that room understood that he had come anyway.

He read the paper. He read it twice and he put it down flat on the table in front of him and squared the edge of it with one finger, which is a thing he does, and which about four people noticed.

Then he said the only sentence he came to say, and it took about nine seconds, and it is the sentence about a count and it is not new and it is not improved on by anybody and it is not quoted here.

"**I can put a reference on it and a count on it and a reason on it, and all three of those are things this office can do, and not one of the three is a week.**"

The woman of twenty-four said: "**You can put a count on it. I have got a count. What I have not got is anybody who can put it back.**"

He said: "**No. And I have looked for one since the Saturday and I have not found one, and I am telling you that in a room with about nine people in it because you are entitled to know that I looked.**"

---

He asked for the paper at about eleven.

He asked correctly. He asked whether it could be given to his office, and he said why he wanted it, and what he wanted it for, and how long he would hold it, and he did not say the word *scan* and he did not say the word *record* and he did not say that it would help.

They said no.

About four of the nine said no, one of them at length, and one of them in about four words, and the man of fifty-eight did not ask a second time, did not ask a third time, did not say that it would be a pity, and did not suggest a compromise, and about four people in that room noticed that he did not do any of those four things.

**He wrote nothing down. He has a job in which he writes things down and he did not write anything down that morning, and nobody asked him why not, and it is not to be asked him why not.**

Nobody thanked him. He was asked nothing else. He left at about ten past twelve and has not been in that room since.

---

The paper went back on the table at about a quarter to one and it was still there at about two when the room emptied, and about four people looked at it while they were emptying, and about four of them did not, and one of them picked it up at about one, read the whole of it standing up, put it back in the same place, and left.

**That is the difference between it and a form. A person can pick it up and put it back and nobody can find it again, and there is no drawer it lives in and no copy and no register and no address, and if it is lost it is lost, and everybody in that room knows that and nobody has suggested a drawer.**

---

At about seven the man of about thirty-three asked one of the nine to do it again on the next dark night, and a woman of about forty-one said no.

She said it in about four seconds and she gave no reason and he did not ask for one, and about four people in that room heard it and about four did not, and nobody said anything at all, and nobody stopped her, and nobody has said anything to her about it since, and he has not asked her twice.

He did not ask the other eight anything else. There are about eight of the nine now and nobody has told them.

He has had four buildings and about nine hundred doors for twenty-five days and he has not asked anybody for anything in that time and has not been given anything.

---

A cook of twenty-seven was at the sink and said nothing at all. A first-year of nineteen was at the counter and said one small unasked thing in about four seconds about a queue and it was not about any of it and nobody improved on it and nobody thanked her.

A woman of about thirty was in the chair nearest the door and was not asked anything and was not thanked and was not apologised to, and the reason she is in that room is the temperature, and the temperature costs more than it did, and both of those are correct.

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.

*351. Monday of week 124, at about ten on a service road, and this entry is one piece of paper with eleven lines on it, a man of fifty-eight who can put a count on it and not a week, one refusal, and no drawer.*

*Conditions and docket.* **Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, two of them not attended in the morning and both attended by about four in the afternoon, both in the second of the four districts. One room in a building in the third of the four districts from about ten to about two, about nine chairs, no name on the door, nothing on the wall, not a meeting and no minute of it. One Continuity Office man of fifty-eight with a bad hip, present from about half past ten to about ten past twelve, not asked, not obliged, and asked for one thing and refused. One piece of paper, about the size of both hands together, eleven lines, each line about a week. The rate held at nine and ten. The heating is on and costs more and no figure of days off is carried. The room off a service road is three hundred and ninety-two days old. A card in a rail in Lattice Ward is three hundred and ninety-six days in the rail, a third object, and is not in that room and is not in a room in a building in the third of the four districts. The hardboard: twelve lines three hundred and twelve, thirteen two hundred and sixty-three, fourteen two hundred and twenty-eight, fifteen two hundred and seven, sixteen one hundred and eighty-two, seventeen one hundred and sixty-four, eighteen one hundred and ten, nineteen eighty-eight. Wall unchanged: nineteen lines in thirteen hands and fourteen names, none added, none removed, none rubbed off. The first line is a cook's and is still the first line and the newest is eleven words and is the only promise on it. Eight items in a ring binder, unopened, unread. The woman's page is unread. The master's fifth line is blank and the drawer was not opened. The Exchange did not sit on this day. Its last sitting was a Wednesday twenty-six days ago and its next sitting is a Wednesday two days from today, and the book is at fifty lines, the telephone at sixty-six, the count has been announced twenty-four times, and nineteen years is nineteen years. The four figures, printed and not converted: fifty, sixty-six, twenty-four, nineteen years.**

*The paper, as a record of a surface.* **Size: about both hands together. Hand: a woman of twenty-four's, throughout, except for four lines written by the persons to whom those lines belong, in that room, with her pen. Lines: eleven. Subject of each line: about a week. Reference: none. Date: none. Heading: none. Print: none. Box: none. Signature: none. Second copy: none. System: none, in this city or any other. Picked up and put back in the course of the morning: once, by a person who read the whole of it standing up. Drawer, file, folder or envelope it lives in: none. Whether it is a form: no. Whether it is a minute: no. Whether it is a return, a claim, a schedule, a notification, a list of losses, a schedule of hardship or a register: no. Requested by an institution: once, correctly, with the reason and the intended holding time given, and refused by about four of the nine and not asked again. Written down by that institution on the day: nothing. Owner: the eleven, and the woman of twenty-four, and nobody in this case, and if it is lost it is lost.**

*The eleven, as a record of what was said and not of what was lost.* **Present on the Monday morning: about four of the eleven. Absent: about seven, not asked to attend, and about four of those have said what they lost to somebody outside this case and it has not been collected. Said, in the room, in four hands, in these words and not improved: a woman of about thirty-eight, a week of shifts already held, about two days of notice not able to be spent, a fortnight's rent on the other side; a man of about thirty-four, a course of about four weeks whose fourth week falls on a Tuesday, with no place available in the autumn and none in the season after that; a woman of about sixty-one, a week of a contract for four offices and a letter on the Friday morning that the four offices had found somebody else, the letter being correct and it being her view that nobody is at fault and that view not said before that room; a man of about twenty-seven, a week of about nine appointments and a message on the Saturday that the list had been made new, his name on the new list and his times not on it. Improved on: by nobody. Thanked: by nobody. Owner: the eleven.**

*The man of fifty-eight, as a record.* **Present from about half past ten to about ten past twelve. Came unasked and was not obliged. Read the paper twice; squared its edge with one finger. One statement of about nine seconds: that he can put a reference, a count and a reason on it, and that none of the three is a week. Answer given by the woman of twenty-four, about nine seconds: that she has a count and has not got anybody who can put it back. Reply from him, about nine seconds: that he has looked since the Saturday and has not found one, and that the room is entitled to know that he looked. Request made at about eleven, correctly, with the reason, the purpose and the intended holding time given: the paper. Refused. Second request: none. Third request: none. Remarks made: none about it being a pity, none about a compromise, none about a system. Written down by him on the day: nothing, in a job in which he writes things down. Asked a question about any of it: by nobody. Thanked: by nobody. Owner: him, and he is owed nothing and has said he will not be thanked and nobody is to.**

*The refusal, and what was not done about it.* **Time: about seven in the evening. Asked: one of the nine, by a man of about thirty-three, for the next dark night. Answer: no, in about four seconds, with no reason given. Reason asked for: no. Second asking: none, then or since. Comments made by anybody: none. Persons stopped, pressed, or asked again: none. Number of the nine after this: about eight. Whether the other eight were told anything: no. Days the man of about thirty-three has held four buildings and about nine hundred doors: twenty-five. Requests made by him of anybody in this case in that time: none. Owner: her, and the nine, and the case, and the standing arrangement that a person asked may say no at a door and is not to be asked about it has now been kept in front of about four people who were not asked to keep it.**

*What the day did not touch.* **Nothing was taught, demonstrated, assessed or assisted, and a room off a service road is not a practice in that department and the reason is not that he is the exception. Nothing was run by him, forwarded, or compared. No name was attached to anything that happened that day. The printed rule was not invoked and was named in no mouth. The Exchange did not sit on this day. The secondment stands and the chair of forty-nine has still not been told. Heating on, costs more, four names on a lease cost more, the fortnight unpaid and unresolved. The hardboard was not written on: nineteen lines, thirteen hands, fourteen names, none added, none removed, none rubbed off; the newest is still the only promise on it, it is a man of twenty-nine's, and it is eighty-eight days old. The woman's page is unread. His appointment carries a reference, a count, a reason and no date, and no date was put on it, and the sentence about a standard adopted as temporary with no date on it was not said and was not needed. The standing question is not stated. The question written once on a service road in an earlier week is not answered, is in nobody's mouth, and its sentence is not printed here. No message arrived from anybody. Nobody died. Nobody thanked anybody. Entry 351.**
