# Chapter 346 — Four Minutes, And Four Hours

The Wednesday evening of week one hundred and twenty-three was the evening a room off a service road had two ways in front of it and chose the slower one in about four minutes, and **the cost of the slower one was that about eleven people in this city lose a week, and the cost of the faster one was that about eleven people in this city lose a week, and about four of those eleven were in that room, and the four minutes were not about that and everybody in that room knew it and nobody said it first.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date. Ten attended, one not attended, in the second of the four districts, and it is chased.

The word was at nine and ten. The room off a service road is three hundred and eighty-seven days old and is warm and about four people came in on the Friday for the temperature and nobody asked any of them anything.

---

The load came back up on the Tuesday night and nobody can say why and no name is attached to it.

Six buildings and two junctions, and the four buildings on a company's maintenance schedule are two of the six, and the other four are not on any list anybody in this case has seen. It is not a storm and it is not a fault and there is nothing broken in any of the six, and a man of fifty-eight with a bad hip is in a building in Crown Terrace and has been told and has not written anything down.

About nine people were in a room off a service road at about half past six on the Wednesday evening and about two of the nine chairs are empty because two of the nine are out of this city and about four of the nine had not met each other before the Monday.

---

There were two ways and both of them were said out loud in about four minutes and the man of about thirty-three said both of them first because he had done the arithmetic on both of them and nobody else in that room had.

The first way is about an hour. The whole of the load goes through the one named point of coordination, which is a man of twenty-two whose appointment carries a reference, a count, a reason and no date and which four buildings found on a sheet nine days after it was written. That is what a count of hours is for and it works and everybody in that room knows it works. It requires about eleven people to be out of six buildings for about a week, because you cannot hold a junction down with people standing in it, and those eleven lose the week.

The second way is about four hours. Nine people, nine doors, four hours, a building at a time, and every one of the nine can stop. It is slower and it might not work and there is no version of it in which anybody can be told to do it.

Nobody improved on either. Both were said out loud and both were left standing.

---

A woman of twenty-four was the first of the four minutes.

"**I want the hour said out loud again and I want it said first, because the hour is the good one and nobody in this room is going to say so. About eleven appointments in a morning go through that table and there is not one of them that can be moved to the week after. The table did not run at all on the Friday four weeks ago and six of them did not happen on the Wednesday, and if you take the hour I will write eleven of them on a board in about a week and I have already done that twice and I am the only person in this city who has to.**"

A man of twenty-one said the second half of it, and he counts things for a living and has said that about himself before.

"**Forty-one days ago about four districts put about four hundred small decisions through one man for about eleven hours and every one of them was right and a junction held and nobody died. And about nine people in four buildings have not made a decision since and are not going to this week. I am not making a point of it. I have counted that twice and I am telling you what I counted, and the hour is that again, and it will work again, and it will be the fourth time it has worked, and I would like somebody else in this room to say the word fourth out loud so that it is not just me.**"

Nobody said the word.

---

A man of fifty-one with a tool bag said what he could and what he could not and both of them were short.

"**I can be at one door. I said that last Wednesday in this room and about four of you were here and nobody asked me a second time and I would like it noted that nobody asked me a second time. If you take the hour you have not chosen me. You have chosen that I am somewhere else when it goes, and I will be, because I have a day job and it is not this and it pays for the bag.**"

A cook of twenty-seven was standing at the back and she said the thing that was the reason the four minutes took four minutes and not two.

"**I am in the hour. I lose the week. That is mine and I am not asking anybody for it and I am saying it in this room so that the four minutes have got my week in them and not only my hands. And there is a kitchen on a ground floor in the second of the six and about nine people in it at nine o'clock on Friday and I am not moving them, and if the four-hour one wants anybody at a door then I am already at a door, and you do not have to ask me, and you are not going to ask me, and I would like both of those said in that order.**"

A man of about thirty-eight who does a shift in one of the six said the other half of the week, in about four seconds, and nobody in that room improved on it and nobody has improved on it since.

"**I lose the week as well. I am not going to be dramatic about it. What I am losing is not the money. The money I can get back in about nine weeks and I have had notice of this for about two days and I have not been able to spend any of it. The rest of it I am not going to get back at all and I do not know what to call it, and neither do you, and I would rather it went in this room than in a column in about a month.**"

Nobody said anything. Nobody said it was a shame. Nobody said it would be looked at.

---

The man of fifty-one said the thing that ended it, and it took about nine seconds and it was not an instruction and nobody asked him for it.

"**Then I am at one door at nine o'clock and nowhere else, and I am going to be at that door whether this room decides it or not, and nobody has asked me, and I am choosing it, and I would like it said that I chose it.**"

And the room had a way, and it was not voted on and there was no minute of it and nobody counted hands and there is no form anywhere in this city that says what happened at about seven o'clock on the Wednesday of week one hundred and twenty-three.

---

The room had the same furniture as every other room in this case and there is no name on the door and nothing on the wall, and it was not a meeting because nobody called it one, and there is no minute of it anywhere in this city and there never will be.

About half way through the four minutes the two ways stopped being two ways. That is not a decision and nobody took one. It happened because the man of fifty-one said he was going to be at one door whether the room decided it or not, and after that about four other people in that room started saying what they were doing on Friday night rather than what they thought about the two ways, and the woman of twenty-four's table came up in the middle of it and the cook's kitchen came up after that, and nobody went back to the hour.

---

A man of twenty-two was in that room and was not asked to vote and did not vote, and he said one thing, and it was the correct thing, and it was not a decision.

"**Both of those ways cost about eleven people their week and about four of them are standing in this room and you have all said so, out loud, in the right order, and I am not going to improve on any of it. The only difference between the two is that in about four hours nobody in this city will be able to say afterwards that a person did it, and in about one hour everybody will. That is the whole of what is different and I am not choosing it, and I have been asked nothing, and I am going to sit down now.**"

He sat down and said nothing else for about four hours.

---

A first-year of nineteen was at the counter and said one small unasked thing in about four seconds about the price of a haircut and it was not about any of it and nobody improved on it and nobody thanked her.

A woman of about thirty was in the chair nearest the door and was not asked anything and was not thanked and was not apologised to, and the reason she is in that room is the temperature, and the temperature costs more than it did, and both of those are correct.

Nobody thanked anybody. The nine doors were open at about nine.

*The four minutes, in the order of the speakers.* **One, a woman of twenty-four, about nine seconds: the first way is the good one and nobody in the room is to say so; about eleven appointments in a morning; the table did not run on the earlier Friday and six did not happen on the Wednesday, and none can move to a later week, and she is the only person in this city who writes those on a board. Duration of his silence after: about four hours. He was not the decider and did not become the decider and was not asked to vote. Owner: the case, and the room, and the two of them are recorded because both are facts and neither is a complaint.**
