# Chapter 344 — The Ninth Friday

The Friday evening of week one hundred and twenty-two was the ninth Friday this volume has put in a company training room in Crown Terrace, and about eleven people were in it, and the man of thirty-four who buys the training for the company that runs it began the run, and nobody in that room has said a word about that since and nobody is going to. **About four buildings in this city were also without light from about five until about nine this evening, and the two of those facts have not been put next to each other by anybody and this entry does not put them next to each other either.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date. Eight jobs attended and three not attended, and the three are in the second of the four districts and he has chased two of the three.

The word was at nine and ten. The room off a service road is three hundred and eighty-two days old and is warm and about four people came in on the Thursday for the temperature and nobody asked any of them anything.

---

About four buildings in this city were without light from about five until about nine this evening. No stairwell in any of them was lit. Nobody rang anybody. The man of about thirty-three was in one of them and has said nothing about it.

---

The room has about nine folding chairs and about eleven people in it, seven on the company's list and about four on no list at all, and there is a man of thirty-four in it who buys the training for the company that runs it and who has a lanyard on and who does not work there, and a woman of thirty-six in it who has a lanyard on and who does.

The run is a walk-through. About nine people, a floor, nine minutes, a woman of thirty-six calls it from the front and everybody does the thing they did in the last one, and it is a run and not a drill and everybody in that room would tell you it is a run and is not a drill, and there is no form for it and there is no register and the company's copy of the sheet is in a drawer in another building.

It has been called a run on the nine Fridays this volume has and it has been called something else by about four people, in four words, in four different years, and neither phrase has ever been in a document.

---

The room holds about eleven people in nine chairs on a Friday and has held them on every one of the nine Fridays this volume has put in it, and the arithmetic has never once been right and nobody has ever mentioned it. Two of them come late and have come late on all nine. About four of the eleven have been in the room for about four years and about four have been in it for about two. The man of thirty-four is not one of the eleven and has never been one of the eleven and is in the room because he pays for it.

Nobody in that room keeps a register. There is no list of who came and no list of who has been and no list of who has not spoken.

---

**She did not begin it.**

She got as far as standing up. She is the person who calls it and she is the only person in that room who has ever called it, and on this ninth Friday she stood up at about half past seven and did not make a sound for about nine seconds, and about nine people in a room of about eleven watched her not make a sound, and nobody read a number out of a sheet.

**Nobody in that room told her what to say. Nobody handed her anything. Nobody counted for her and nobody counted with her.** The sheet is in a drawer in another building and has been since the last time anybody needed it, and about four people in that room knew that and about four did not.

At about nine seconds, the man of thirty-four who buys the training said one word.

It was the wrong word. It was not the first word of the run and it was not the word she would have used and he is not a person who calls runs and has never called one, and about four people in that room did not move for a second and then about four of them moved.

They finished it. Nine people, a floor, about nine minutes and about forty seconds over, and nobody counted the forty seconds and nobody has counted them since, and the run went.

**Nobody said why it went. Nobody thanked him. Nobody asked him anything about it and he was not asked a fifth time, and about four people in that room noticed the nine seconds and about four of them have said so to nobody.**

---

A woman of about thirty-eight who has been in that room for about two years and who has not spoken in it for a long time did the second half of that run properly, without being told, and has not said that she did it and is not going to be asked.

A man of about twenty-nine who comes on a Friday and who has not spoken in that room in about four years sat down at the right moment without being told either, and about four people noticed that, and about four people did not notice that, and the two groups have not met and are not going to.

Nobody in that room improved on anything that happened in it. Nobody has said one word about the nine seconds to anybody outside it, and the two people who were closest to it in that room have not said one word to each other about it.

They were in the room for about ninety minutes afterwards and about four of the eleven stayed, and the rest went at about nine, and the man of thirty-four left with the others and has not been in that room since and nobody has asked him why he was in it and nobody is going to.

A man of about fifty-eight who has been in that room for four years and who comes for the sake of coming said one thing to about four people in the last half hour, and it was about a car park and a set of steps and how long a person can stand on a set of steps before somebody comes, and about four people in that room understood every word of it and about four did not, and he has not said one word about any of it since.

**Nobody improved on that either.**

---

**That is the whole of what happened in that room on the ninth Friday and this entry is not going to make more of it than nine people made of it, and there is no sentence anywhere in this volume that says what the nine seconds were, and there is no sheet, and no form, and no register, and no line on any hardboard, and nobody has written it down and nobody is going to.**

---

A room off a service road was warm at about half past six that Friday evening and about nine people were in it, and a first-year of nineteen was at the counter and said one small unasked thing in about four seconds about a bus fare and it was not about any of it and nobody improved on it and nobody thanked her.

A man of fifty-one with a tool bag was in it and said nothing and has been asked about nothing for about four weeks, and the asking has not been renewed and nobody has stopped it, which is the standing arrangement and is not going to be changed.

A woman of about thirty was in the chair nearest the door and was not asked anything and was not thanked and was not apologised to, and the reason she is in that room is the temperature, and the temperature costs more than it did, and both of those are correct.

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.

*The run, as a record of hands and seconds.* **Room: a company training room in Crown Terrace. Chairs: about nine. People: about eleven, seven on the company's list, about four on none. Run: a walk-through of one floor, about nine people, called from the front by one person. Company copy of the sheet: in a drawer in another building and not fetched. Time the run was due to begin: about half past seven. Time it began: about nine seconds after that. Cause of the nine seconds, on the page: a woman of thirty-six stood up and did not make a sound. Interventions before the run began: none; no number was read from anything, no hand touched anybody's back, nobody counted, nobody prompted, and about four people in that room knew the sheet was in a drawer and about four did not. Improved on: by nobody. Thanked: by anybody: no. Asked about: by nobody, now or since. Owner: the case, and eleven people, and the two of them closest to it have not spoken to each other about it.**

Any new instrument issued in the last eleven days: none. Persons who rung anybody in this case between about five and about nine: none. The man of about thirty-three present in one of them: yes, and he has said nothing about it to anybody in this case and has not been asked. Stairwells used, doors held open, torches brought down from flats, kettles run: about four of the nine in each of two of the four, and about four of those in the second building had been in a stairwell with somebody in the first building on the earlier occasion. There is no order to it and there was no order to it and nobody in this case has any authority over any part of it. Owner: nobody, and the case records it and does not join it to anything.**

*What the day did not touch.* **Nothing was taught, demonstrated, assessed or assisted, and a company training room is not a practice in that department and the reason is not that he is the exception. No message arrived from anybody. Nobody died. Nobody thanked anybody. Entry 347.**
