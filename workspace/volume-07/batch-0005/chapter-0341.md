# Chapter 341 — Four Hundred Small Decisions

The Monday morning of week one hundred and twenty-two was eleven days after a man of about thirty-three said yes in about four seconds to four buildings and about nine hundred doors, and he has done the work every working day since and he knows what it weighs, and **about nine people in this city can now see that a man of twenty-two is behind, where four of them could see it before this morning, and one of the nine is a person this case has never met and he is not a protagonist of anything.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date. Nine jobs attended and two not attended, both of them in the first of the four districts, and he has chased neither.

The word was at nine and ten. The room off a service road is three hundred and seventy-eight days old and is warm and about four people came in on the Friday evening for the temperature and nobody asked any of them anything.

---

He was rung at about ten past eight on the Monday morning, which is a thing he has never once been rung at, and the man on the other end said his own name and then did not say anything for about nine seconds, and then said the thing he had come to say, and it was about himself and not about anybody in this case.

He said that four buildings and about nine hundred doors is not four things. He said that it is about nine hundred things, one at a time, and that about nine people in this city can do one of them each and that he cannot do nine hundred and has not tried. He said that the first four days went on finding out who he was allowed to ring, and the second four days went on finding out who answered, and that on the ninth day he found out that **about four people in four separate buildings had been standing in their own doorways since about four o'clock on the Friday afternoon waiting for somebody to tell them what to do, and had worked it out on their own by the Monday morning, and that he had put himself in the middle of a thing that was already going.**

He said that he was saying that to a man of twenty-two in a room and not to a person who reports to anybody, and he said it in about four seconds, and then he said the other thing, which took longer.

"**Four hundred small decisions cost this and it is not yours and it is not mine and I am the one who has to live in it. Nobody in those four buildings can tell me what is supposed to happen. Every one of them will tell me what did happen. And about four of them have started telling each other first.**"

Nobody said anything on that call. He has not asked for anything since and nobody has offered him anything.

---

A woman of thirty-four who checks about nine hundred doors came into that room at about four in the afternoon on the Monday and asked about one of the four buildings, in front of about nine people, and the answer came from the man of about thirty-three, who was standing eleven feet away from the counter.

She has telephoned him about nine times in about four months. She telephoned him about two of them on the Friday and about three of them on the Tuesday, and on this afternoon she was in a warm room with about nine people in it and a man of thirty-three had the answer and she asked him for it in front of all of them.

She did not apologise to anybody. She did not explain why she had not telephoned. Nobody in that room said anything about it, and about four people in that room noticed, and not one of them has said so since, and he has not said a word about it and is not going to.

---

He is behind by thirty-two days, and the figure is not a figure anybody gave him.

Thirty-two days is the Thursday of week one hundred and seventeen, when about four districts put about four hundred small decisions through him for about eleven hours and every one of the four districts was right about the arithmetic and the junction held. He attended eleven of eleven this morning and finished eleven of eleven and there is nothing outstanding that he can see from where he is standing.

**What is outstanding is that a man of about thirty-three has been running four buildings for eleven days, and a man of twenty-nine who works on lifts has not telephoned him since the Wednesday, and a woman of thirty-four who checks about nine hundred doors has not telephoned him since the Friday, and a man of fifty-one with a tool bag has not been asked about anything and has not offered anything, and none of the three has told him that they have stopped, and all three of them are right.**

He did not work out what to do about it. There is nothing on this earth he could do about it, and he has known that for about four weeks, and the having-known is the being-behind.

---

A room off a service road was warm at about half past six that Monday evening and about nine people were in it, and one of the nine was a man of about thirty-three who had come in from a gate and had not sat down, and one of the nine was somebody nobody in this case has met.

He is about forty-six and he signs for work that has been done, in one of the four buildings, and he has signed for nine things a day for about eleven years, and he came to that room because the man of about thirty-three brought him and because it is warm, and he had not been in that room before this evening.

He stood at the counter, which is where the man of twenty-two stands, and he was not looking at anybody, and he said two things. The first took about four seconds.

"**I signed for nine on Wednesday and the nine were done. I could not tell you one thing about how any of them got done. I have signed for about nine things a day for eleven years and I can tell you how every one of them got done, and I could not tell you about nine this week, and I am going to sign for nine again tomorrow.**"

About nine seconds. Then the other one, which is the one four people in that room are going to remember and which nobody improved on and nobody thanked him for and he was not asked a question about afterwards and has not been asked one since.

"**And here is the other half and I have been trying to say it since Wednesday. Four weeks ago a piece of paper with a count on it went to four buildings and about four people did exactly what it said and they have not made a decision since and I do not think they are going to this week. Eleven days ago a man of twenty-two said about four sentences in a room and handed four buildings to a man who is standing in this room, and the four of us got on with it. I have been in this trade eleven years and the second one was cheaper, and I have got nobody to say that to, so I am saying it in a warm room to about four people who are not going to be thanked for it.**"

Nobody improved on it. Nobody thanked him. Nobody repeated it. About four people in that room understood that a thing had just been said about a decision and four understood that it was not about the storm, and the four who understood it the first way have not said so and are not going to.

He is on no list. He has no post and no office and no part in anything and he is not in this case again. The fact that he exists is the finding and not a person.

---

A first-year of nineteen was at the counter and said one thing, in about four seconds, to nobody, and it was not about any of it.

"**There is a man on the corner outside who has not been selling anything for about eleven days and I have decided he is not lost, because a lost person walks.**"

Nobody improved on it and nobody thanked her. She said one more small thing on about four of the nine evenings of that week and none of them was about any of it, and she has not been asked about any of them.

**Her one document-shaped finding in this case was spent fifty-four days ago, which is about eight weeks, and this is not it, and there is a half hour of silence from an earlier month that is still not in her mouth and is not to be asked her for.**

---

A cook of twenty-seven was at the sink and said nothing at all. A man of fifty-one with a tool bag came in at about seven, put the bag against the wall, and did not say anything either, and about four people noticed that he came on a Monday, which he does not.

A woman of about thirty was in the chair nearest the door and was not asked anything and was not thanked and was not apologised to, and the reason she is in that room is the temperature, and the temperature costs more than it did, and both of those are correct.

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.
