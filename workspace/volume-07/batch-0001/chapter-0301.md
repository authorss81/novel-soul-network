# Chapter 301 — Two Floors

The Monday morning of week one hundred and nine was the ninth morning in about a month on which a stair fire door in Nine Bridges came up on a docket with a stamped number in the description box that is on the wrong line of a company's list, and a man of twenty-two adjusted the closer for the fourth time, went to the frame twice, and found the thing exactly as he had described it every time, and there is no fault in it that he can put in a box; and on the same morning, on a stair above that door, a lift he has ridden about nine times a week for two years stopped between two floors it has never stopped between, and came back when he pressed the button, and he watched it four times, and he wrote it in his own book and not on the docket, and he said it out loud in a room off a service road at about eight in the evening, and nothing whatever was done about it.

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date. The stair fire door in Nine Bridges is the ninth occasion and it is the same fault number on all nine and the fault number is not his and the box it is in belongs to a company that has never been inside the building.

The word was at nine and ten all morning and reached eleven on no hour of it, and it has been said out loud in front of one person about two months ago and has not been said to anybody since and is not being reported.

The room off the service road is two hundred and eighty-seven days old and is warm. It has been warm since the Wednesday of a week before the one before last, and four names on a lease cost more than they did, and each of the four named that cost out loud before signing.

---

He was in the shop in Lattice Ward from about half past seven, and the shop is a bench, a parts cage, a shutter, a lamp, a counter with a rail of card slots in it and about ten cards in it in nine years, and a wall list in a plastic sleeve that is not the wall and has never been the wall and is not going to be the wall.

He did not open the drawer. He had opened it in the last week of the month before and had looked at a tool roll in it for about nine minutes and had taken nothing out of it, and there are five lines on the thing in the fourth pocket and four of them are full and the fifth is blank, and it has been blank for about two terms now, and nobody in this shop knows it is blank and nobody has ever asked him about it.

---

The lift in Nine Bridges is an old one with a mirror down one side of the car and a smell of hot dust in the summer that nobody in that building complains about, and he has ridden it about nine times a week for two years, and in two years it has stopped between floors four times, and three of those were somebody standing in the doorway of a flat on six.

On the Monday it stopped between five and six, which is a floor it has no door at, and there is no door on five and six anywhere in that shaft.

He was on the stair above it, with his bag on his hip, going up to four, and he heard the cables go slack and he stopped and came back down and looked in.

The car was between the two floors with the doors shut and the panel lit and nobody in it.

He looked at it for about a minute and a half. Then he went up four flights on foot and came back down and pressed the button.

It came.

He did it three more times before eleven, and the fourth time it did not stop and he stood in the car for about nine seconds waiting for it to, and it did not, and he got out at one and went back up the stairs to the yard.

---

The thing he wrote was four lines and it went in the back of his own book and not on the docket, and the reason it did not go on the docket is that a docket is a company's and a company is a thing that can be quoted, and there is a room in this city with a plate on the outside of it and no name on the plate and about nine chairs in it, and he has spent about four years learning that the difference between those two things is the whole of what he knows.

He did not telephone anybody. He had four numbers in his phone that a person could have rung and he rang none of them, and one of the four is a stair fire door on a docket and one of the four is a plate with a letting agent's number on it and one of the four is an address of a ring binder on a shelf that he has never given to anybody and about nine people have.

---

The room was warm by half past six and about nine people were in it, and the bell-push does not ring by itself and did not.

A woman of about thirty was in the chair nearest the door and had been since about two, and the reason she is in that room is the temperature, and the temperature is now something she can afford, and nobody has asked her about it, and she came on a Monday in the last month she could not have afforded it and she has been asked nothing about that either and is not going to be.

A cook of twenty-seven was at the sink and had been at the sink since about half past five, and the gas ring was off, and she turned round.

"**The gas has smelled different since Saturday.**"

"**It is the extract on the stair.**"

"**I have worked in that kitchen nine years and I know what that extract smells like. That is not the extract. That is something sweet and it is not coming off the ring.**"

Nobody took that any further, and about four people in that room looked at each other, and nobody said the sentence that would have connected it to anything.

A man of fifty-eight with a bad hip was in the corner and had been since about half past nine and had not had a cup of tea, as he does not.

A man of fifty-one with a tool bag was at the far side with his back to the wall and was reading the hardboard, and the hardboard is eighteen lines in twelve hands and thirteen names and the newest one is nineteen words long and is about being written down.

---

At about eight he said it.

"**There is a lift in Nine Bridges that has stopped between five and six four times this morning and I watched it four times and there is no door on that floor in that shaft, and it has come back every time I pressed the button, and I did not put it on the docket and I am telling you and not the company.**"

It took about nine seconds and there were about nine people in the room.

Nobody said anything for about eleven seconds.

"**Did you tell them.**"

That was the man of fifty-one with the tool bag and he did not move off the wall when he said it.

"**No.**"

"**Why not.**"

"**Because it is a fault in a lift and a fault in a lift has got a company on it, and if I put it on the docket then in about four days there is a piece of paper in a building in four different places with my name at the bottom of it, and about nine people in that building will know a man in Lattice Ward reported a lift, and none of them will know what I reported and all of them will have an opinion about it.**"

"**You are frightened of a piece of paper.**"

"**I am frightened of a piece of paper. I have been frightened of a piece of paper for about four years and it is the only thing I am frightened of.**"

---

The cook of twenty-seven came out of the sink area at about twenty past eight and stood at the end of the counter with a cloth in her hands.

"**What would you have said if you had rung them.**"

He thought about it for longer than the room expected.

"**That a lift in Nine Bridges has stopped between five and six about four times this morning.**"

"**That is it.**"

"**That is what I would have said. And I have said it in about nine buildings over two years and about four of them have rung me back and asked which four, and one of them asked me to send it in writing, and I did not, and that was a Thursday and I still think about it.**"

Nobody said anything. A cook of twenty-seven went back to the sink. A man of fifty-one with a tool bag read the hardboard and said nothing, as he does, and a man of fifty-eight with a bad hip said nothing at all, which he does about four times a week.

---

Nothing happened for about two hours and nothing happened in the rest of the city either, and that is the part of it that is worth having on the record: a room of about nine people heard a true thing about a lift at about eight in the evening on the Monday of week one hundred and nine and did one thing about it, and the one thing was that about four of them stopped talking and about four of them went home earlier than usual, and neither of those is a procedure.

The shutter came down at about ten. The room was warmer than it had been on the Monday of the week before, and nobody had turned anything down, and the four names on that lease are four names that are paying more for the same four rooms, and each of the four said so out loud before signing, and about four people in this city came into that room on the evenings of the last month because of the temperature and are still coming, and the cheapest thing any of them has ever used is somebody else's money spent on gas, and the man who put it on went round four units on a Saturday and nobody asked him and nobody thanked him. A woman of twenty-four has been in that room on a Thursday about twice a month for about four years and has not been in it this week, and her name is Talia Venn and nobody in that room said it.
