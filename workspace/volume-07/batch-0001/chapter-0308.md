# Chapter 308 — Every Difficult Share

The Monday of week one hundred and eleven was the day a man of twenty-two walked four miles into a workroom and asked a woman of twenty-four for nine accounts that were in a locked drawer in a building with a plate on the outside of it, and she said no in about four minutes, and the reason she gave first was not the one she gave second, and he was wrong in nine words and did not argue, and nothing was resolved and nobody was thanked.

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date. Two jobs attended, one of them in Nine Bridges, and a stair fire door on the docket for the eleventh time, and the list is on the back of a card and the card is in a drawer and the drawer is in a flat.

The word was at nine and ten. The heating is on and costs more. The room off the service road is three hundred and one days old and about four people came in for the temperature and one of them is a woman of about thirty who has not been asked about anything in about six weeks.

---

Talia Venn's workroom is one floor above a corridor with a plate on the outside of the building and about four desks in it, and one of the desks is hers, and there is a consent clipboard on the wall above it that no institution has ever asked her for and that has been there for longer than either of them has worked.

A woman of about forty-three was at the other desk until about ten past five. She said good evening to him in a way that had a meaning in it and then left, and it was a meaning about a room with a plate on it.

He sat down. He had a docket in his hand and he did not put it on the desk.

"**How many were there.**"

"**Nine.**"

"**How do you know there were nine.**"

"**Because there were nine.**"

"**Then how do I know that there were nine.**"

---

She told him in about four minutes and she told it flat and in order, and the order was the whole of the argument and she did not know that while she was telling it.

The first account, a woman of sixty-one and a bias binding and nineteen years. The second, a man of thirty-one and a van and a corner and a prior accident he would not discuss. The third, a girl of nine and a mother and the words *it went faster than the song*. The fourth, fifth and sixth, three women at one table, none of whom knows the others. The seventh, a man of forty-four who asked whether it was new. The eighth, who said nothing and used her hand. The ninth, a man of about seventy who said *stopped* and meant the opposite and said so in about four seconds.

He asked why she had not told anybody. She said they were hers, they had said them to her, and that this was both a fact and a reason, and that she was not going to find a better one before half past five. He said that was not a reason, it was a fact. She agreed with him and did not change her mind.

---

Then she said the real thing, and it came in about four minutes and it did not come in the first minute.

"**What you have done this week is take every difficult share that was going. You have been to the plant. You have been to Nine Bridges twice for a door that has been fine nine times. You told a man of fifty-one with a tool bag that you would go and be useful to two crews and he said do not go and you did not go and you have not mentioned it since, which means you are still thinking about it.**"

He said that all of it was true. She said she knew it was true, and stopped for about nine seconds, and then said the part she had not said yet and said she would say it badly because she had been in that building since half past seven.

"**If I give you the nine, then in about four days you will have arranged them. You will have put them in a sequence. You will have found the thing they have in common and it will be a true thing and it will be a good thing and about nine people said a thing to a stranger and the stranger will have made a list of it and the list will be better than what I have and it will not be mine and it will not be theirs.**"

He said he would not do that. She told him he would do it in about four days and would not do it on purpose, and would tell her afterwards that he had not meant to, and that she would believe him, and that this was the part she could not work around, because he was not doing it to be kind to her.

Then the sentence, in about eleven seconds:

"**You do it because a list is the only shape you have ever trusted.**"

---

She stopped again after that, for about nine seconds, and then said the smaller thing, and said at the start that it was smaller.

"**It is about four o'clock and I have not eaten and I have been in this building since half past seven and I do not want the nine to be shared, and part of that is a preference and part of it is that I have had them for three days and they are the only thing in this city that is only mine.**"

Both of those could be true, he said. She said she knew, and that she was not asking him to do anything about it.

---

He was wrong in nine words. He said them and he did not argue and he has not improved on them.

"**You have decided and you have not told me.**"

She said she had not decided anything. He asked why, then, why she was sitting there with the door shut.

"**Because it is four o'clock and I have not eaten, and because you have just told me that I have decided, which is a thing a person says when they are tired and want the other one to be the one in trouble.**"

He did not say anything for about nine seconds. He was not tired. He had had two hours' sleep and a full day and he had walked four miles to do this, and what he had come with was not the nine accounts at all. He said no, and when she asked what he meant by no, he told her that she was not in trouble and that he had not come to put her in it, and that he had come because on Wednesday nine people said a thing to her and she was the only one who wrote it down, and that since Wednesday he had had four things he could not hand to anybody, and that she was the person he handed things to, and that it had taken him four miles to get here to say it in nine words.

She asked what the four things were. A lift. Fourteen people on a bench. A switch. A plant. She asked whether the nine were in that as well, and he said they were.

---

Then he did the thing she had not expected and she has thought about it since.

"**I am not going to ask you for them again, and I am not going to ask the woman of forty-three, and I am not going to say in a room off a service road that a woman of twenty-four has nine of something. That is yours and I am not going to hold it for you and I am not going to be the reason it is found.**"

She said that was not what she had asked for either. He agreed with her and then said, in about nine seconds, that it was what he had instead of what she had asked for, and he would like her to notice that it was not the same as obeying her. She said it was not the same as obeying her. He said no, and then said what he wanted, which was to know what the difficult share was.

---

She told him that was the whole of the conversation and he had made it into two. Then she said the difficult share.

"**You have nine accounts and you are carrying them by yourself in a locked drawer in a building with a plate on the outside of it, and nobody in this city has a copy, and if you stop coming to work on a Thursday there is no version of that in which anybody else has them. That is not a moral failing. That is a single point of failure and you are the single point of failure, and you have not asked anybody to help you and I am not going to pretend to anybody that I have not noticed.**"

He said he knew. She said then he knew why she had said no. He said she had said no because the nine were hers, and that he was asking about the part that was not hers.

---

She was quiet for about eleven seconds.

She was quiet for about eleven seconds, and then she said it was a Monday, and that they did this on a Thursday, and he said he knew what day it was.

"**I came today because a Thursday was a week away and I could not carry it for a week. I have had it since Wednesday and I have not written it down and I have not said it on a service road, and I know what those two facts are and I am telling you them instead of telling you I am all right.**"

She said that if he came on Thursday then this was a Thursday thing. He said yes. And then she said the thing that is the reason this entry exists:

"**And if it is a Thursday thing then it is a practice, and a practice is two people agreeing to do a thing and then doing it, and I have spent about four years not doing that with you, and the reason I have is that the last time we did it neither of us could get out of it and I have never worked out how to make a thing that can be stopped by the two people in it.**"

He said then he would not come on Thursday. She repeated it back to him. He said no, and she said that was the first thing he had got right this week.

Nobody said anything for about nine seconds. She put her hands flat on the desk and then took them off it, which is the thing her hands do.

"**Say the thing about us.**"

"**There is not a thing about anything.**"

"**There is a thing about you and me and you have been carrying it up and down four miles all week and you have not once said it in four years, so say the rest of it or do not say the first half.**"

She told him that if he said the rest of it then it was a Thursday thing, and that it was already a Monday thing and he had made it a Monday thing by walking in.

"**Then the rest of it is: I do not want to be the reason you are alone with the nine, and I am going to keep being the person you hand things to, and I cannot make those two compatible, and I have not been able to for about four years, and you have known that for about four years, and you have not once asked me to fix it and I would like that recorded as restraint and not as patience.**"

"**It is restraint.**"

"**Good.**"

She wrote the nine words *it is restraint* on a pad and did not read them back and did not give the pad to anybody.

*311. Monday of week 111, at about ten past five in a workroom four miles from a room off a service road, and this entry is nine accounts that stayed in a drawer, four things that went nowhere, nine words that were wrong, and one word used once.*

*Conditions and docket.* **Two jobs attended, one in Nine Bridges. A stair fire door is on tomorrow's docket for the eleventh time, same fault number, and it has not been attended and has not been chased. The rate held at nine and ten. The heating is on and no days-off figure is carried. The room off the service road is three hundred and one days old. A card in a rail is three hundred and five. The hardboard: twelve lines two hundred and twenty-one, thirteen one hundred and seventy-two, fourteen one hundred and thirty-seven, fifteen one hundred and sixteen, sixteen ninety-one, seventeen seventy-three, eighteen nineteen. Wall unchanged. Nine accounts remain in one locked clinical drawer in one room with about nine chairs in it; no copy exists anywhere else; the word *us* occurs once in this entry and it is in a sentence addressed between two people and it is not a declaration; the words *we* and *our* do not occur in this entry at all, and that is walked on the ten files and not asserted, and the count for all ten of them is printed where the ten are counted.**

*The argument, in the order it was had, with the order doing the work.* **Asker: one man, one question, *how many were there*, in about four seconds, standing. Answer: nine, then the source of the number, which was that there were nine. Second ask, *how do I know there were nine*, in about four seconds. He came four miles to ask two questions in about eight seconds and stayed about fifty minutes. Her account of the nine, in order, in about four minutes, flat: nine sources, in seat order, with the four attributes common to all nine and the one thing common to none of them, which is a fault. Her first reason for not giving them to him: they are mine, they said them to me — given, and identified by him as a fact and not a reason, and not improved on by her. Her second reason, given later, in about a minute, and this is the real one: that in about four days he would have arranged them, and would have found the thing they have in common, and the thing would be true, and about nine people would have told a thing to a stranger, and the stranger would have made a better list, and the list would not be hers and would not be theirs. His reply: I would not do that. Hers: you would do it in about four days, you would not do it on purpose, you would tell me afterwards that you had not meant to, and I would believe you. The sentence that carries it, in about eleven seconds: *a list is the only shape you have ever trusted.* Her own addition, unasked, and marked by her as smaller and worse: that she has not eaten, that it is about four o'clock, that she has not wanted the nine shared, that part of that is a preference, and that part of that is that they are the only thing in this city that is only hers.**

*The nine words, and the fact they were wrong.* **Said: you have decided and you have not told me. True: no. She has not decided anything; she has nine accounts and a locked drawer and a preference and a tiredness, and there is no plan in this entry and none in hers. What he came with, and said so afterwards, is not the nine accounts: it is four things — a lift, about fourteen people on a bench, a switch, a plant — and the sentence that goes with them, that since Wednesday he has had four things he could not hand to anybody and she is the person he hands things to, and that it took him four miles and nine words. Not argued. Not improved on. He has not improved on them since. **The wrongness is not carelessness. He said *decided* because a person with a closed door at four o'clock has decided, and because the sentence that works in a stairwell does not work at a desk, and he has not worked out the difference and has not been asked.**

*What he gave up and what he refused, in that order.* **Gave up, unasked for, in about nine seconds: that he will not ask her for the nine again, will not ask the woman of forty-three, and will not say in a room off a service road that a woman of twenty-four has nine of something. Her response, and it is the second refusal in about nine seconds: that is not what I asked for either. His: it is what I have instead of what you asked for, and I would like you to notice it is not the same as obeying you. She agreed that it is not. Refused, in about four seconds: Thursday. Hers: this is a Monday, we do this on a Thursday. His: I came today because a Thursday was a week away and I could not carry it for a week, and I have had it since Wednesday and I have not written it down and I have not said it on a service road, and I know what those two facts are. Hers, in about eleven seconds, and it is the longest thing she says in this entry: if you come on Thursday then this is a Thursday thing, and if it is a Thursday thing then it is a practice, and a practice is two people agreeing to do a thing and then doing it, and she has spent about four years not doing that with him, and the last time neither of them could get out of it and she has never worked out how to make a thing they can both stop. His answer, in about two seconds: then I will not come on Thursday. Confirmed by both. Her last sentence: that is the first thing you have got right this week. Nothing was resolved. No milestone was paid. There was no scene, no declaration and no kiss.*

*What he said, and the word.* **She asked him to say the rest of it or not to say the first half. The rest of it, in about nine seconds: that he does not want to be the reason she is alone with the nine, and that he is going to keep being the person she hands things to, and that he cannot make those two compatible, and has not been able to for about four years, and that she has known that for about four years, and has not once asked him to fix it. Her request for that to be recorded as a category: it is restraint. Hers, after about nine seconds, without softening: good. She then wrote two words on a pad, did not read them back, and did not give the pad to anybody. **That is the second time in this case that somebody has written something down and not read it back and the first was in a room above a line in Saltmarket, and the two women are not the same and have never met.**

*What the day did not touch.* **He taught, demonstrated, assessed and assisted nothing. No share was offered, made, derived or withdrawn, and the word *share* in this entry is a phrase people use about work and is not used in its technical sense by either of the two people in that room. No panel, no marker, no relay. The printed rule was not mentioned. The Exchange did not sit. The secondment is unchanged. The heating is on, costs more, and the fortnight is unpaid and unresolved, and a woman of about thirty was in the chair nearest the door in a room four miles away and was not asked anything and this is the second week in a row she has not been asked anything. The hardboard was not written on. The woman's page is unread. The standing question is not stated in this entry and carries no number. The question written on a service road on a Wednesday in week one hundred and eight is not answered here, is not in anybody's mouth, and neither of the two people in that room referred to it. No message arrived from anybody. Nobody thanked anybody. Entry 311.**
