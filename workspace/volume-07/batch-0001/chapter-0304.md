# Chapter 304 — Four Seconds Early

The Friday evening of week one hundred and nine was the first Friday rota in a term and a half, in a company training room at the end of a passage in Crown Terrace with nine folding chairs in it, and about ten people were in it, and the last run of the evening ended about four seconds before anybody in that room expected it to and nobody said the word; and a man of twenty-nine who works on lifts did the same run again the other way round and it worked, and said why in about nine seconds, and nobody improved on it; and one of about eleven people who get taught it in a week was not in that room and there is no job in this city whose business it is to ring a person.

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date. A shift at a training room is not a job and has never been on this docket and about four years of Fridays are not on any form in this city.

The word was at nine and ten. The room off the service road is two hundred and ninety-one days old and is warm and costs more.

---

The training room has nine folding chairs, a laminated sheet on a post, a counter at the back, and a bell-push of its own that nobody presses, and there is a company name on the door of the building and the company is not the company that assembles anything and is not the author of anything and is a customer.

About ten people were in it at half past five. Seven of them are on the company's list and have been for about four years, and that list is a card in a box on a table and the box is the company's and it has been there about four years. One of the seven is a man of fifty-eight with a bad hip who has a card in date and does not sit in the fourth row. About four more of the eleven are on no list at all, which is the standing figure, and the number is on the back of one woman's own card in a drawer in a flat in Nine Bridges and is on no form.

A woman of thirty-six with a lanyard ran it, and has run it for about four years, and is not a mechanism and holds no post and is owed nothing.

---

He was in the fourth row against the wall and he has been in the fourth row against the wall on about four hundred of these Fridays.

The last run of the evening was the one everybody does at the end, which is a hold with a count in it and a release that is supposed to arrive when the instructor says the word. She says the word at the same point every Friday. The word is not on the laminated sheet. The word has not been on the laminated sheet in about four years and about four people have asked her for it and she has said no four times.

The run ended about four seconds early.

Nobody said anything. Four people in that room had hands up in a position they had put them in, and about nine people finished a fraction after each other, and the room came apart the way it comes apart, and the instructor said the word she says at the end, which is not the release word and has never been the release word, and people got up.

He noticed it. He has been in that room on about four hundred Fridays and he knows where in a count the release arrives the way he knows a stair.

He did not write it down.

---

He has not written anything on a service road since a Wednesday nine days ago, and the last thing he did write on one was a question he has not answered, and the thing he has noticed about the not writing is that he cannot tell whether it is discipline or whether it is that he does not want to be a man who has a book.

He thought about that for about nine seconds in a corridor while about nine other people put their coats on, and then a man of thirty-four who buys training for the company that runs about four of the rooms in an audit came past him and said, without stopping, that he was the only one in there who had not looked surprised, and that this was worth about eleven pounds an hour of somebody's time and he was not going to say it again.

---

At about half past six they did the run again.

Nobody asked to. The man of twenty-nine who works on lifts asked, and the instructor said no, and he said the thing that made her say yes, which is that he wanted to do it with the count started at the other end.

"**You cannot start a count at the other end.**"

"**I have been doing this for nine years and I have never once done it with the count started at the other end, and I would like to do it once, and it is not dangerous, and if it is dangerous then you will tell me and I will not do it.**"

"**If it is dangerous I will tell you and you will not do it and that will be the end of it and not a discussion.**"

"**That is fair.**"

She let him.

---

He did the run with the count backwards and there was no word at any point, and it finished, and the release came at the end of the shape and not at a place in a count, and it was about four seconds later than it had been when the instructor supplied it and it was not late by anybody's measure and about four people in that room said so afterwards in about nine seconds.

He said the true thing about it, and it is the second time he has said a true thing out loud in a room in about four years and he said it in about nine seconds and nobody improved on it and nobody thanked him.

"**It is not the room and it is not the week. I have been doing it the other way for nine years and I have never once done it this way, and the only thing I changed is that I stopped waiting to be told where the end was.**"

"**Then it was never a technique.**"

"**It was never a technique. It was me, and a card in a box, and about eleven people a week.**"

Nobody said anything. The woman of thirty-six with the lanyard looked at him for about four seconds and then said the sentence she says.

"**And I am not writing that down either, and I want to be exact about why, because the reason has changed since the last time I said it. It is not that eleven people is not a finding. It is that the moment I write down that a run can be done backwards then in about four years there is a sheet in a training room somewhere with two columns on it, and one of the columns says supervised and one of them says not, and about four hundred people in this city are going to be handed that sheet by somebody who has never done a run in his life.**"

"**That is a good reason.**"

"**It is my reason and it is about four hundred people and I have not got a better one and I have not got a worse one.**"

---

About half past seven, at the door, the man of thirty-four who buys training for the company said one thing and it was not to anybody.

"**There have been seven of you on that list every Friday for about four years and there are seven of you on it tonight, and about four more of you are in this room on no list at all, and one of the seven was not here tonight, and I am not going to be the man in the lanyard and I am not going to stop buying the training either, and both of those are mine to decide and I have decided them.**"

"**Who is missing.**"

"**I do not know and that is the whole of what I know and I have looked for about four minutes and there is nobody in this city whose job it is to ring a person and find out, and about four people have said that out loud in about four years and nobody has been given anything for saying it, and I am not going to be the person who is given it either, because the day one of us is given it is the day this room is a thing that can be counted.**"

He put a card in the box on the table. He was in it about nine seconds. Nobody stopped him and nobody thanked him and he is not owed anything and he has not been thanked for about two terms and is not going to be.

*307. Friday of week 109, at about ten on a service road, and this entry is four seconds, a run done backwards, and one absence that nobody can chase because nobody's job it is.*

*Conditions and docket.* **A shift in a company training room is not on a docket and has never been on one. About ten people in the room; seven on the company's list, of whom one was absent; about four more on no list, which is the standing figure. One man of fifty-eight with a bad hip holding a card in date and not sitting in the fourth row. The identity of the person who was absent is not established, and no attempt beyond about four minutes is described. The rate held at nine and ten and reached eleven on no hour of it. The heating is on and no days-off figure is carried. The room is two hundred and ninety-one days old. A card in a rail in Lattice Ward is two hundred and ninety-five. The hardboard: twelve lines two hundred and eleven, thirteen one hundred and sixty-two, fourteen one hundred and twenty-seven, fifteen one hundred and six, sixteen eighty-one, seventeen sixty-three, eighteen nine. Wall unchanged. The first four hundred of these Fridays are not on any surface in this file, and about four hundred is a figure that is not on a form. It is not the same figure as the four hundred that means something else entirely, the two are not to be converted into one another, and something in about four days is going to try. **

*The four seconds.* **Run: the last of the evening, a hold with a count, a release supplied by an instructor who says a word at the same point every Friday. The word is not on the laminated sheet and has not been for about four years and has been asked for four times and refused four times. Observed: the run ended about four seconds before the expected point. About four people in that room had hands in the position they had put them in and about nine finished a fraction after each other. Nobody said anything and the instructor said her closing word, which is not the release word. Observer: one, in the fourth row against the wall, who knows where in a count the release arrives. Recorded by him: nowhere — not on a docket, not in his own book, not on a service road, and this is the second occasion in about four years on which he has had something in his hands and not written it. The four seconds of the Friday of week one hundred and two are settled and are not to be re-opened by anybody and are not called solved; **this is a second Friday and is not that Friday**, and the two are not to be joined. The instructor's stated reason for not recording the four seconds is in this entry in her own words and is a different reason from the one she gave for not recording a run count the last time she was asked, and the difference is the whole of what happened in that room. **

*The run done backwards, and the sentence that was not improved on.* **Request: by the man of twenty-nine who works on lifts, unprompted, after the room had come apart. Grounds, in his order: he has done this for nine years; he has never done it with the count started at the other end; he would like to do it once; if it is dangerous he will be told and he will not do it. Refusal given first, in about four seconds: the count cannot start at the other end. Overruled by the requester in the same form he offered it, which is that a second refusal about danger would be final and a third would be a discussion. Consent: given, once, without conditions. Performance: backwards count, no word at any point, release at the end of the shape rather than at a place in a count, about four seconds later than the supervised release had been running, and about four people said afterwards that it was not late. The nine words, said in about nine seconds, and they were said to a room and not to a person: it is not the room and it is not the week; he has been doing it the other way for nine years and has never done it this way, and the only thing he changed is that he stopped waiting to be told where the end was. Response from the instructor, in about nine words, that this was never a technique and that it was him and a card in a box and about eleven people a week. No improvement offered by anybody. No thank you given or withheld. This is the second time this man has said a true thing out loud in a room in about four years.**

*The absence.* **Names on the company's card, which is a card in a box on a table: seven, unchanged for about four years. Present tonight: six. The standing remainder, about four who are on no list at all, was also in the room, so the count for the night is about ten. Who was not there: not identified, not described, not chased beyond about four minutes by the man who buys the training. His own statement of a fact about this city, not improved on by anybody in this file: nobody's job is to ring a person and find out. He added that this has been said aloud by about four people in about four years and that nobody has been given anything for saying it. His own decision, given at the door, unasked for by anybody: he will not be the man in the lanyard and he will not stop buying the training, and both are his to decide and he has decided them. Cost of that decision, named by him: none, and it is the first cost named at that door that has been named by him as his own. Card returned to the box: one, about nine seconds, unobserved by any attempt to stop it. A card in a box is a third object, and it is not the wall and it is not the rail and it is not the master, and no sentence in this case may merge the three.**

*What the day did not touch.* **He taught, demonstrated, assessed and assisted nothing, and a company training room is not a practice in that department and the reason is not that he is the exception; he was in the fourth row and he did not say the four seconds out loud. No panel, no marker, no relay, nothing run, nothing forwarded. The printed rule was not mentioned and nobody in that room knows the number of people in this city who can invoke it, and one man in that room has been in a building where it was discussed and has not been told. The Exchange did not sit. The secondment is unchanged. The heating is on, costs more, and the fortnight is unpaid and unresolved. The hardboard was not written on. The woman's page is unread. The standing question is not stated. The question on the service road is not answered. No message arrived from anybody. Nobody thanked anybody and the man of thirty-four who buys training was thanked for the third time in about four years and was not thanked, and he has not asked to be. Entry 307.**
