# Chapter 307 — It Is On The Counter

The Friday of week one hundred and ten was the day a notice came into a room off a service road in a plain envelope with a district office's mark on it and no number on it. A person of sixty whose job is to ask questions had put it into about nine envelopes, and one of the nine was that room, and the room did not refuse it.

The cost of not refusing it is that there is now a document in that room, and a room with a document in it is a room that can be asked what it thinks.

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date. Nothing attended in that district and one address not attended. The second Friday rota ran in a company training room in Crown Terrace from about half past five and this entry does not go into it.

The word was at nine and ten. Four names on a lease cost more than they did. The room off the service road is two hundred and ninety-eight days old and is warm.

---

The envelope is a large brown one and it is not thick and it came by hand, and the hand was not the post, because the post in this city takes four days and this took one.

Inside it there is one sheet, and the sheet has a district office's mark in the top corner and a line under the mark with no characters on it, and that is the first thing anybody notices about it, and about four people in that room noticed it before they read a word of the body.

The absence of those three things is the first thing anybody says about it, and it is said in the shape people say anything they have stopped arguing about: a rule has a number and a date and a reference, and the person who sent this had left all three off on purpose, and has said as much in a room.

The body is about nine lines and it describes a condition, in the plainest words anybody in this case has read on any piece of paper, and it does not name a cause and it does not name a person and it does not ask for anything.

It asks the reader to note it.

---

Asha Reed read it at about eleven and put it down and picked it up again and read it a second time, and she is nineteen, and she has been the person in that room who has said the least for the longest time.

"**Who sent it.**"

"**Aurelia Sopp. District 3 practice standards. She is sixty and it is her job to ask questions.**"

"**Then it is a woman asking us a question and there is no question in it.**"

"**No. That is what I keep coming back to. There is no question in it. That is the first one of these I have seen in about four years and the four hundred I have sent before them all had a question in them, and the question was always the same question, which is *may we have your name*.**"

---

A man of fifty-eight with a bad hip was in the corner and had been since about ten and had not had a cup of tea.

He asked one question, in about nine words, and it is the only question anybody in that room asked about that sheet all day.

"**Is it going to be on the table on Monday.**"

"**It is on the counter now.**"

"**That is not what I asked.**"

"**No. It is what you asked.**"

Nobody answered him. He did not ask again. He has been asked about five things in about four years and has said one of them, which was that, and has refused one, and nobody has put the refused one to him again.

---

A cook of twenty-seven read it twice, standing up, and put it back down on the counter face up.

"**This is a Friday.**"

"**It is.**"

"**There are about eleven people a week in this city who are in a room on a Friday for a reason that has not changed in four years, and a piece of paper arrived on a Friday, and I am not saying they are connected, and I would like it noted that I said the word Friday before I said anything else about it.**"

About four people heard that and nobody improved on it and nobody thanked her and she went back to the sink.

---

Then Asha said the thing, and it is the thing this entry is for, and it took her about nine seconds and she said it to the room and not to anybody in it.

"**We can refuse this.**"

"**We can.**"

"**And we are not going to, and nobody has asked us to, and I want it said in this room that the not refusing is the whole of the cost and that it is mine, because I am the one who has been talking about rules in here for about four years and nobody has ever once told me that a piece of paper can be a cost.**"

"**What does it cost.**"

"**It costs that there is a piece of paper in this room that came from a building either of us has been inside and it has a mark on it, and the mark is a district office, and a district office keeps a list of premises, and this room is a premises because it has a door and a lease and a landlord, and the plate outside has a letting agent's number on it and no name and about four hundred calls have gone through that number in about four years.**"

"**So somebody sent it to a letting agent.**"

"**Somebody sent it to four hundred people and about four of them had put a forwarding address on a form somewhere and one of those was a letting agent with four units, and nobody chose us. That is worse.**"

---

The address is not a mystery and it is not a conspiracy. A converted shop unit off a service road is a letting. A letting has a tenant, a landlord, a managing agent and a set of premises records held by a district for reasons that have nothing to do with anybody in this case. A person of sixty who is required to be right every time she opens her mouth looked down a list of about four hundred and eleven premises in one district and put a sheet into about nine envelopes, and the reason she did not ask first is that a person of sixty who asks first does not get answers, and that is her experience and not a suspicion.

She has not been thanked. She has not been asked whether it was the right thing to do. She has said, in a room in a district office and not in this one, that she is aware of what she has done, and that it is the first thing in about a term that she has done which her job does not ask for, and that she would do it again on a Thursday.

The other eight envelopes are not accounted for in this case, four of them belong to people this case does not have, and two of them belong to somebody this case does have, and that one is still sealed.

---

The sheet was still on the counter at about half past nine on the Friday, face up, and about nine people had read it and about four of them had read it twice.

No copy of it is on anything in this file. There is no form, no register, no minute, no list, no leaflet and no notice of a notice anywhere in this city that records that it arrived.

There are three places it exists: the copy on that counter, the copy that went to about four other people, and the copy in a drawer in a district office on a Friday night. None of the three is a record anybody can require.

*310. Friday of week 110, at about half past nine at a counter in a room with a plate on the outside of it and no name, and this entry is one sheet with no number on it, nine lines, no question in it, and nine envelopes.*

*Conditions and docket.* **Nothing attended in that district; one address not attended. The second Friday rota ran in Crown Terrace from about half past five and is not in this entry. The rate held at nine and ten. The heating is on and no days-off figure is carried. The room is two hundred and ninety-eight days old. A card in a rail is three hundred and two. The hardboard: twelve lines two hundred and eighteen, thirteen one hundred and sixty-nine, fourteen one hundred and thirty-four, fifteen one hundred and thirteen, sixteen eighty-eight, seventeen seventy, eighteen sixteen. Wall unchanged, eighteen lines in twelve hands and thirteen names. The sheet has been on that counter since about eleven in the morning and is still there. Copies: one in that room, one in a drawer in a district office, and about four in about four other people's hands, and about four of those four are not in this case. No copy is on any surface in this file. No request for it has been made by anybody and no record of it exists that anybody can require.**

*The sheet.* **Object: one large sheet of paper, in a plain envelope, delivered by hand on one day, with a district office's mark in the top corner and a ruled line under the mark carrying no characters. That absence is the first observation anybody made of it and about four people in that room made it before reading the body. Not a rule, therefore: a rule in this case has a number, a date and a reference, and this has none of the three. Length: about nine lines. Contents: a description of a condition, in the plainest words any document in this case has ever been written in, with no cause named, no person named, and nothing asked for. The whole of what it asks: that the reader note it. Author: a person of sixty whose job it is to ask questions, District 3 practice standards, who is right every time she opens her mouth and who said, in a room in her own building, that this is the first thing in about a term she has done which her job does not ask for and that she would do it again on a Thursday. Not thanked. Not asked whether it was right.**

*How a room with a plate on the outside of it and no name on the plate is on a district list.* **Not a mystery and not a conspiracy. A converted shop unit is a letting. A letting has a tenant, a landlord, a managing agent and premises records held by a district for reasons unconnected to anybody in this case. About four hundred and eleven premises in one district, of which this room is one. The nine envelopes were addressed to nine of them. The room is on the list because it has a door, a lease and a landlord, and not because of anything anybody in it did. The plate outside carries a letting agent's number, and a letting agent with four units has had about four hundred enquiries about one of them in about four years, and a forwarding address on a form is the likely route. Stated in that room by a first-year of nineteen in about nine seconds and in this order: we can refuse this; we are not going to; the not refusing is the whole of the cost; it is mine. Four words of hers are the reason this entry exists: *nobody chose us.* Cost as she stated it: a piece of paper is now in this room that came from a building either of us has been inside, and the mark on it is a district office, and a district office keeps a list, and this room is a premises. Not improved on by anybody.**

*The three questions that were asked of it, in order, and the one that was not answered.* **One, from a first-year of nineteen: who sent it. Answered, correctly, in about four seconds, with a name, an age and a job, and no judgement attached to any of the three. Two, from a man of fifty-eight with a bad hip, at about eleven, in about nine words: is it going to be on the table on Monday. What he was answered with was where it was, and he said that was not what he asked, and nobody answered him and he did not ask again; **the reason he asked is not recorded in this file and he has not given it and is not to be asked for it a second time, and about four people in that room believe they know what it is and one of them is right.** Three, from nobody, asked of a cook of twenty-seven, who answered before she was asked: this is a Friday. Her full statement, in order, and the order is the point: about eleven people a week in this city are in a room on a Friday for a reason that has not changed in four years; a piece of paper arrived on a Friday; she is not saying the two are connected; she would like it noted that she said the word Friday before she said anything else about it. Not improved on. Not thanked. **The sentence about a release and a man in a lanyard is said out loud once in this movement, in a bay behind a workshop on the Thursday before, and is not said again by anybody, and the record here does not carry it, because a record that carries it is a second saying. The question that was not asked by anybody in that room all day is whether anybody intends to come back and ask about it, and that question was not asked because in about four years nobody in this city has come back to a room to ask a second time about anything, and nobody in this file has established whether that is a comfort or an absence.**

*What the day did not touch.* **He taught, demonstrated, assessed and assisted nothing and is not in this entry. No panel, no marker, no relay, nothing run, nothing forwarded. The printed rule was not mentioned and about four hundred competent people in this city can still invoke it, and the man of fifty-eight who asked the unanswered question is not one of them and does not know it. The Exchange did not sit. The secondment is unchanged. The heating is on, costs more, and the fortnight is unpaid and unresolved, and a notice from a district office has now arrived in a room that four people are coming into for the temperature. The hardboard was not written on. The woman's page is unread and this sheet is not that page and the two are not to be confused by anybody, and one of the eight other envelopes is believed to be a building and not a room. The standing question is not stated here. The question written on a service road is not answered here. No message arrived from anybody and a notice is not a message and did not come from anybody this case can reach. Nobody thanked anybody. Entry 310.**
