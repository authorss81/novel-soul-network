"**So what is it for.**"

"**It is for a man whose job it is to ring people, and there is no such job, and I have said that sentence in four rooms this year and about four of them were full of people who could have been that job and none of them was, and I am not going to be coy about it at eleven on a Monday morning in front of a room that has spent a fortnight finding out what it will not do.**"

"**I could stop if there was a person to stop, and there is not, and that is the whole thing and I have been saying it for two volumes and this is the first week it has been about a city instead of a room.**"

"**And here is the part that is mine and not yours,**" she said. ---

The man of twenty-two said the thing he could find, and he found it in about eleven seconds and it was not an argument.

"**Then you say the seven words and you go to whoever is running that piece, and if nobody is running it you say that there is nobody running it, and that is a true sentence and about nine people have now said it and it has never once been printed.**"

"**That is a good answer.**"

"**That is the only answer and I have had it since about ten past eleven on Friday and I did not have the nerve to say it to you for a day and a half, and if you want to be angry with somebody, be angry with me about the day and a half and not about the seven words.**"

He said: "**I am not going to be angry with you about the day and a half. I would have taken a fortnight.**"
