# Batch 0005 — Volume 05, Movement V, "Three Districts" (Chapters 241–250)

**This is the last movement of Volume 05 and the last ten chapters of the volume, and it is written to `outline/volume-05.md`, which supersedes `outline/series.md`'s Volume 05 paragraph on four points. All four re-bases were applied in that outline before Chapter 201 was drafted and none of them is re-argued here:**

1. **Stage 3 is reached by delegation and not by teaching, because the teaching half is not available at any price.** He teaches, demonstrates, assesses and assists nobody in any of the ten chapters, and no page may imply that he could. A room off a service road is not a practice in that department and the reason is not that he is the exception.
2. **The recognition is a board in a sign-writer's hand and nine keys, and a key is not a post.** The board is not named over a door, and no name, title, office, post, committee, minute, notice, report, form, leaflet, registration or constitution is created in this movement.
3. **A rule in this volume is a line somebody wrote by hand. It costs the writer something out loud, it binds nobody who did not write it, its own writer may lift it with a thumb, and it is evidence of nothing in any proceeding.** A local council is nine people in a room with a list and no minute is taken of it.
4. **The panel budget for the whole volume is one and it was spent in Chapter 209. There is no second panel and no `* * *` marker in any of the ten chapters.**

## The four debts this batch owns

0. **The fortnight of heating is not paid for by anybody in this volume and is not resolved, and it must not be resolved with a document.** The heating goes off on the Monday of week eighty-three. **It does not come back on the Monday of week eighty-five and it does not come back in this volume, and the room is cold at the close.** No fund, collection, subscription, grant, list, minute, notice or appeal exists and none is proposed. The person it costs is a woman of about thirty who has been in that room on every working day since the Friday of week sixty-nine because it is warm, and who is not asked, and who keeps coming, and about whom nobody apologises, and to whom nobody says anything. Three of the four people who came for the warmth do not come back and their absence is counted and is not mourned in public by anybody.
1. **He is findable, he is asked to be the central owner of a public line, he refuses, and he becomes the route to it anyway.** The refusal is the volume's own argument turned on him — *a man who answers for a room is a key-holder in it* — said about a whole city instead of one room, and the cost is that refusing to own it is what makes him its address. **A first-year of nineteen removes him from it in about nine words in Chapter 247, and nobody thanks her and she goes back to a kettle.**
2. **The four who can run a piece of an evacuation each refuse once, in public, on purpose, and the evacuation still works.** The two refusals are the lift engineer of twenty-nine refusing to read names off his employer's Friday list, and the woman of twenty-four refusing to be the person a public line reaches in a flood. Neither refusal costs anybody their life, and the movement must not make either refusal heroic or cheap. **The piece that works is the cook's and it is a kettle and a chair and about four hundred cups of tea, and she said in Chapter 210 that it is not a technique and she will not have it called one, and it is what four hundred people stood on.**
3. **The concrete resolution, in the outline's own four items and no others:** a real institution that is a small one, being nine people in a room with a list; a public lesson, which he is not in; an explicit opt-out that is a sentence on a wall and not a clause in anything; nine keys of which he holds one and the mechanism by which he can be removed is a person with another key and about nine seconds; the board still a board, rubbed off once and written again by somebody else; the Commons still vulnerable to capture and unable yet to cross a city line, both said out loud by a person who is not the protagonist; and the Exchange's book at forty-six lines with a woman of sixty-one who will still not open it on a Friday.
