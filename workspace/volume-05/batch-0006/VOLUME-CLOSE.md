## 1. What Volume 05 was about

A man spent a volume proving that a room can be useful and refuseable at the same time, discovered that a room with a door is a room a person can be found in, gave away one key and was given nine words that took the last of it off him, and ended up in a cold room in the middle of a city with a board that can be rubbed off with a thumb, nine keys where two are needed, a woman of thirty in the chair who has never been asked why she comes, and about nine people who now know the address of a binder and no paper in the city that says who may ask for it.
