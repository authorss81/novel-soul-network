# Chapter 921 — A Monday Of The Twelfth Week, A Bench Until Two And A Counter Until About Six Before It, Then About Two Hours Of That Evening In That First Floor Above A Line In Saltmarket Where About Nine People Were And Where A Space On A Form Was Found Empty Again That Nobody In That Room Saw Emptied, And Forty Pounds For Four Jobs

**The Monday after that had a bench until two and a counter until about six before it, and then about two hours of that Monday evening went up a stair into that first floor above a line in Saltmarket, where fewer than nine people were and about four of them had been in that room before. Nothing was settled in it and nothing was counted aloud in any room in this city.**

---

Nine callers came into that shop between about seven and about half past nine on that Monday, and the shutter came down at about ten.

**Those four converted units off that service road had stood one thousand seven hundred and eight days by that Monday, which is two hundred and forty-four weeks to the day. The sixteenth of the nineteen ruled lines on that board had been ruled one thousand four hundred and ninety-eight days before that Monday, which is two hundred and fourteen weeks to the day, and nine hands' copies of the front of one page had been taken one thousand two hundred and seventy-four days before that, which is one hundred and eighty-two weeks to the day.**

---

**The nine chairs were in that room on that Monday in the same row and the same gaps, and nobody in that room has said which of them is the one that is not used.**

Nobody was ever told which of them was theirs, and nobody in that room has asked to be told, and nobody has said since how many of the nine people who come into that room sat down in one of them on any given evening.

**And the sentence on the plaster above the rail of that room had not been taken down, and nobody in this city has said who put it there or when.**

About four people in that room have read it. About four of the rest have not. Nobody in that room has asked either of those groups about it, and nobody has taken any of it down with a cloth.

---

**And at about seven on that Monday the woman of about forty-three put that form on its own table and the space on it where a date goes was empty, and on the Friday before that space had something written in it, and nobody in that room saw the thing taken out of it.**

She has said since that she looked at it once and then looked at it again about four seconds later, and that it was empty both times, and that she has not been able to get past that.

Nobody in that room saw it emptied. Nobody in this city has said who emptied it. Nobody in that room has said one word about it to anybody.

**About four people in that room have said since that they were not looking at it at about seven on that Monday, and that they had other things to be doing at about seven on a Monday, and that they have not stopped thinking about it since.**

The woman of about forty-three said one thing into somebody's face about it at about seven and nobody said anything back to her.

She said: somebody has been at that space, and I am not going to sit here and work out who.

---

**A man of twenty-two was in that room on that Monday, asked nothing and signing nothing, and he is not the person who signs anything in this city.**

He has said since that he did not know the space had ever had anything in it, and that he has stood in that room about nine times and had looked at the table every one of them.

He has said since that the part of that Monday he has gone back to is not the empty space and that it is that nobody in that room asked the woman of about forty-three a single question, and that he had a question himself and did not put it.

---

**What was left of that Monday went into about two hours of the same yard and the same building, and four jobs went into it.**

A hand dryer in a washroom had been put in off the lighting with no earth at the fitting at all, so that the case had been holding what it had through a length of conduit clipped twice to a wall.

He earthed it to the metal box behind it and proved the case with a wet cloth and the machine running.

"**Twelve pounds,**" he said. "**A dryer on the lighting with no earth is a dryer whose case is a length of conduit. About four of those dryers in that washroom are on a lighting way, and one of them has been a washroom nobody would put a hand near for about four years.**"

A time clock in a stair had its supply taken off a second board that runs off the first one, so that switching the second board off left the clock standing live with nothing on it.

He gave the clock a way of its own off the first board and proved it with the second board switched off.

"**Ten pounds,**" he said. "**A clock on somebody else's board is a clock that stays live after the power is off. About four of those clocks in that building are put in that way, and one of them has been a clock nobody could trust for about four years.**"

An extractor fan in a kitchen had its earth taken off the clip that held its own conduit to a timber beam, so that the fan was earthed to a piece of wood.

He took the earth off the clip, put it on the building, and proved the case against the housing.

"**Nine pounds,**" he said. "**A fan earthed to its own clip is a fan earthed to a beam. About four of those fans in that kitchen are on their own clips, and one of them has been a smell in that kitchen that nobody could account for about four years.**"

A light in an outbuilding had been put in on the way out that runs to the meter, so that anything done at the meter put that light out as well.

He took it onto a way of its own and proved it with the meter board open.

"**Nine pounds,**" he said. "**A light on the meter way is a light the meter owns. About four of those lights in those outbuildings are on the meter way, and one of them has been a dark yard nobody could explain for about four years.**"

**Forty pounds is what those four came to on that Monday, exact.**

---

**The fourth of those four rooms had its door shut by about half past six on that Monday with a woman of about thirty behind it, and the binder on the shelf at the back of that room did not come out, and this page prints no figure for it.**
