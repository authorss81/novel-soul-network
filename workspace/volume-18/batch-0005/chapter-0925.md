# Chapter 925 — The One Sunday Of This Movement, A Bench Until Two And A Counter Until About Six That Is The Shape Of Every Other Day And Not The Shape Of This One, Two Hours In A Room Off A Road That Is Not The Room Above The Line And Is Not The Four Converted Units, One Of The Nine Come In Without Being Asked And Nobody In That Room Had Been Told That Anybody Was Coming, The Shutter Down At About Two, And Forty-Two Pounds For Four Jobs

**Nothing runs on the one day of this week, a bench until two and a counter until about six is the shape of the other nine days and not of this one, and about two hours of that Sunday went into a room off a road that is neither the room above a line nor the four converted units, and fewer than nine people were in it, and no count was said.**

---

Four callers came into that shop on that Sunday, the last of them at about half past one, and the shutter came down at about two, which is earlier than it comes down on any other day of this movement.

**The card in that rail had been in it one thousand seven hundred and eighteen days by that Sunday, and that is two hundred and forty-five weeks and three days. The seventeenth of those nineteen ruled lines had been ruled one thousand four hundred and eighty-six days before that Sunday, and that is two hundred and twelve weeks and two days, and the post at the end of that corridor had been standing there one thousand two hundred and sixty-two days, and that is one hundred and eighty weeks and two days.**

---

**That room is at the back of a hall off a road on the other side of that city from the line, and it is used on that day of the week and on no other day, and it has been used on that day for about four years.**

There is nothing set out in that room. There are tables against two of its walls and there are chairs that people bring in and stack afterwards, and there is nothing on any of the tables, and there is no list of anybody on any wall of it.

Nobody in that city keeps a list of who comes into that room. Nobody has ever written one down. Nobody has ever sent word to anybody that a person was coming.

**Nobody in that room had known that anybody was coming, and about four people in that room have said since that they had not known a person was on her way up the road until she was in the doorway.**

---

**And one of the nine came into that room at about half past twelve on that Sunday, and nobody in that room had been told that she was coming, and nobody has said since that anybody asked her to come.**

She is one of the nine. Nobody in that room said which of the nine she is, because nobody in that room can say which of the nine anybody is, and nobody in this city has ever been able to say.

She had not been in that room before. She had not been in any room in that city that anybody there knows of, and about four people in that room have said since that they knew of about nine rooms and this was not one of them.

She came in, and she sat down, and she had nothing with her, and she stayed until that room was emptied.

---

**At about a quarter to two she said one thing into somebody's face, and what she said was a question, and nobody in that room answered it.**

Nobody in that room has said since what the question was. About four people in that room were close enough to hear it and about four of them have said since that they have not been able to put it down in the order it was asked in.

**Nobody in that room asked her one question about why she had come on that Sunday. Nobody in that city has asked her since. About four people in that room have said since that they would have asked her and did not, and one of those four has said that asking would have meant making her answer for the room.**

---

**Nothing that was said in that room on that Sunday has been entered anywhere since.**

That sheet was not in that room. That book was not in that room. Nobody wrote anything down in that room that evening and nobody has entered a word of that evening into anything at all.

**That room was not taken by anybody for anything on that Sunday, nobody in that room has said what anybody came there for, and this page does not say it either.**

Whether those nine rows had been dealt with was not said out loud in that room on that Sunday, and the other side of it was not said out loud either.

---

**The man of twenty-two was not in that room on that Sunday, and nobody in that city has asked him about that Sunday.**

He has said since that he was in his own rooms on that Sunday and that he heard about it about a week afterwards from somebody who has not said who, and that he has not been able to get any of it out of that person, and that he has stopped trying.

---

**What was left of that Sunday went into about two hours of the same yard and the same building, and four jobs went into it.**

A freezer in a corner shop had been pushed forward to be cleaned behind and its earth had come away with it, so that it had been standing on its own lead with nothing behind it for months.

He put the earth back where the machine is bolted and proved the case with a meter on it.

"**Thirteen pounds,**" he said. "**A freezer moved out from its earth is a freezer standing on its own lead. About four of those machines in that row of shops get moved for cleaning, and one of them has been a freezer nobody would touch for about four years.**"

A washer in a flat above a shop had tripped its own way at about nine that morning and the person who let themselves in had put it back on the lighting while the floor was wet.

He gave it a way of its own and proved it through a full cycle with the lights off.

"**Twelve pounds,**" he said. "**A machine put back on the lighting in a hurry is a machine somebody will put back on the lighting again. About four of those machines in that block are on a lighting way, and one of them has been a fault nobody could explain for about four years.**"

An outside light over a yard gate had no earth at the fitting at all, because the fitting had been changed for a plastic one and nothing had been done about it.

He earthed it to the gate post's steel and proved the case against that post.

"**Nine pounds,**" he said. "**A light with no earth and a plastic fitting is a light nobody has ever put a meter on. About four of those fittings on that yard are plastic, and one of them has been a gate that could not be lit for about four years.**"

A socket in a kitchen had a rewireable holder in it with nothing at all where the fuse should have been, so that anything on that way had no protection on it whatever.

He put in the rating stamped on that plate and proved the whole way with a load on it.

"**Eight pounds,**" he said. "**A holder with no fuse in it is a way with nothing between it and the fault. About four of those holders in that kitchen are empty, and one of them has been a kitchen nobody would put a kettle on for about four years.**"

**Forty-two pounds is what those four came to on that Sunday, exact.**

---
