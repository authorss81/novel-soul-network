# Chapter 928 — A Wednesday Of The Thirteenth Week That Carried No Sitting, Nine Dates Down A Page In About Nine Seconds Shown By A Man Of About Thirty-Three Who Was Not Asked To Do It And Who Has Not Done It In About Four Years, And Nobody In That Room Able To Use What It Showed, And The Fifth Heading Still Standing Over Nothing On All Nine Rows, And Forty-Two Pounds For Four Jobs

**Nine dates went down a page that Wednesday and did not come out in order, and the day had gone a bench until two and a counter until about six before any of it, and then about two hours of that Wednesday evening went into that first floor above a line in Saltmarket, where about nine people were and nobody said a word back.**

---

Eight callers came into that shop on that Wednesday between about seven and about twenty to five, and the shutter came down at about ten.

**Of those nineteen ruled lines, the eighteenth had been ruled one thousand four hundred and thirty-five days before that Wednesday, and that is two hundred and five weeks to the day. The figure still owed on the open page lying in front of the man of about fifty-one stood at one thousand four hundred and seven days, and that is two hundred and one weeks to the day, and that man himself had been against that north wall one thousand three hundred and twenty-three days, and that is one hundred and eighty-nine weeks to the day.**

---

**That Wednesday was not a sitting. Nobody in that room said anything into the face of that room and nothing was said at about half past six, and about nine people were in that room until the shutter came down at about ten.**

Nobody in that city has said since that that Wednesday should have been one, and nobody in that city has said since that it should not have been.

---

**At about eight o'clock on that Wednesday a man of about thirty-three stood up in that room and did a thing he had not done in about four years and which nobody had asked him to do.**

He had a page in his own handwriting. He had copied nine dates out of a file himself, one under another, in a records room in a second district that is a landlord and a filing system, and he had copied them out twice and got the same thing both times.

He read them down the page from the top in front of the whole of that room, and the fourth of them was earlier than the third of them, and the eighth of them was earlier than the seventh of them.

**That took about nine seconds. He put the page face down on the table when he had finished and did not say one word about what it meant.**

---

**Nobody in that room said that he was wrong and nobody in that room said that the nine dates run in order. Nobody in that room asked him what he thought it meant and he has said since that he does not know.**

Nobody in that room could use it. Not one of about nine people in that room said what anybody should now do differently on account of those nine dates, and about four of them have said since that they would have wanted somebody to say it and did not.

**He has said since that he has not done that in about four years and that nobody asked him to do it and that he would do it again in front of the same room if it came round again and that he does not expect it to come round again.**

---

**And nothing about it made anything reviewable. Nobody in that room said that those nine dates could be looked at again and nobody said that they could not, and about four people in that room have said since that the four of them had all understood what he had shown and had not understood what it was for.**

The sheet with four columns was on its own table from about seven that Wednesday with the fifth heading standing over nothing on all nine of its rows, and it was on that table at about nine.

**Nobody in that room said whether the nine rows on that sheet had been dealt with on that Wednesday, and nobody in that room said they had not, and this page is not saying which of those two is a true sentence.**

---

**The man of twenty-two was in that room on that Wednesday, asked nothing and signing nothing, and he was at the back by the rail and he did not move while it was being read out.**

He has said since that he had read those nine dates before that Wednesday and had got them out of order himself about a month before and had said nothing to anybody then either.

He has said since that he does not know whether that makes two people who have done it or one person who has done it twice.

---

**The rest of that Wednesday went into about two hours of that same yard and that same building, and four jobs went into it.**

A socket in a corridor had been put in on a way that already had a stair light, a pump and a heater on it, so that the three of them together were past what that way could carry.

He took the socket onto a way of its own and proved all three running at once.

"**Twelve pounds,**" he said. "**A socket on a way with three things already on it is a socket that works until somebody boils a kettle. About four of those sockets in that corridor are on ways that are full, and one of them has been a corridor with a burnt smell nobody could account for about four years.**"

An outlet in a workshop had been put in on the way out of a piece of plant that is started and stopped all day long, so that the outlet went off every time the plant did.

He gave the outlet a way of its own and proved it with the plant running.

"**Eleven pounds,**" he said. "**An outlet on a machine's supply is an outlet that is there when the machine is and not otherwise. About four of those outlets in that workshop come off plant, and one of them has been a socket nobody could rely on for about four years.**"

A lamp in a bay had been put in on the way out that runs to the yard gate light, so that the lamp went off whenever that gate light was put out by the timer.

He gave the lamp a way of its own and proved it with the gate light off.

"**Ten pounds,**" he said. "**A bay lamp on the gate light's way is a lamp that works to somebody else's clock. About four of those lamps in those bays are wired off the gate light, and one of them has been a bay that went dark every evening for about four years.**"

A socket in a kitchen had been put on a way with a machine on it that wanted more than the way could give, and had been given a fuse two ratings below the one stamped on the plate.

He put in the rating on that plate and proved the machine through a full cycle.

"**Nine pounds,**" he said. "**A fuse below the stamped rating is a fuse that goes before the fault does. About four of those plates in that kitchen carry the wrong rating, and one of them has been a machine that has kept stopping for about four years.**"

**Forty-two pounds is what those four came to on that Wednesday, exact.**

---

**On that Wednesday the fourth of those four rooms was shut by about half past six and a woman of about thirty was behind that door, and whatever is standing on the shelf at the back of that room stayed where it was, and nothing is printed here for any page in it.**
