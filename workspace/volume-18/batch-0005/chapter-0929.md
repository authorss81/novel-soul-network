# Chapter 929 — A Thursday Of The Thirteenth Week, A Bench Until Two And A Counter Until About Six Before It, Then About Two Hours Of That Thursday Evening In That First Floor Above A Line In Saltmarket Where Two Kinds Of Room Came Up Again And Where One Of The Two Is Still Standing There With Nothing In It And About Four People Have Said Since That They Have Been Doing The Other Thing Since A Man Of About Thirty-Three Said It, And Forty-Four Pounds For Four Jobs

**Two kinds of room came up, one set out for people before anybody went in and one with people already in it, and the day had been a bench until two and a counter until about six, and about two hours of that evening went into that first floor above the line, where about nine people were and about four of them had been counting the first of those two for about four years.**

---

Eleven callers came into that shop on that Thursday between about seven and about half past nine, and the shutter came down at about ten.

**The creased card had stood in that rail one thousand seven hundred and twenty-two days by that Thursday, and that is two hundred and forty-six weeks to the day. The twelfth line of those nineteen had been ruled one thousand six hundred and thirty-eight days before that Thursday, and that is two hundred and thirty-four weeks to the day, and its thirteenth one thousand five hundred and eighty-nine days, and that is two hundred and twenty-seven weeks to the day.**

**Its fourteenth line had been ruled one thousand five hundred and fifty-four days before that, and that is two hundred and twenty-two weeks to the day, and the fifteenth one thousand five hundred and thirty-three days, and that is two hundred and nineteen weeks to the day. The nineteenth and last of them had been ruled one thousand four hundred and fourteen days before that Thursday, and that is two hundred and two weeks to the day, and the hold over nine crates and the ground under them had been standing there one thousand three hundred and fifty-one days, and that is one hundred and ninety-three weeks to the day.**

---

**The first of those two rooms is that first floor above the line in Saltmarket, and there are nine chairs standing in a row in it in the same gaps, and anybody can go up that stair and count them.**

The second of those two rooms is the room off that road that was used on the Sunday and on no other day at all, and there is nothing set out in it of any kind, and there are no chairs in it at all, and anybody can go in there and count the people in it.

**Nobody in that room counted the people in that other room on that Sunday. Nobody in that room has counted them since. Nobody in that city has said since how many people were in that other room on that Sunday.**

Each of about four people in that room has said since that they were in a position to count them on that Sunday and did not, and that none of them has been able to account for that.

---

**And at about half past seven on that Thursday a man of about thirty-three said the two things again into the face of that room, in about nine seconds, flat and at the same speed the whole way through.**

He said: a room somebody has set up for people can be counted.

He said: a room with people in it cannot.

**Nobody in that room contradicted him and nobody in that room agreed with him, and about four people in that room said nothing at all and looked at the row of chairs while he was saying it.**

He has said since that he said it the second time because he had worked out in the week before that he had only ever said it the once, and that he had not thought of that until about four days ago.

---

**About four of those people in that room have said since that they have all been counting the first of those two things for about four years and have gone on doing it ever since he said it.**

One of those four has said since that she had a page of her own with a column on it that was not a column of anybody and that she now knows what that column was counting.

A second of those four has said since that he does not agree that the second of those two things cannot be counted, and that it can be counted by anybody who is prepared to be the one who counts, and that nobody in this city is prepared and that this is the part that took four years.

**Nobody in that room has said since that either of those two kinds of room changes anything, and about four people in that room have said since that they do not think it does and nobody has said that it does.**

---

**That four-columned sheet was on its own table from about seven on that Thursday, the fifth heading standing over nothing on all nine of its rows, and it was on that table at about nine.**

Nobody in that room put it either way on that Thursday, and nobody there said the other half of it out loud either.

**He was in that room on that Thursday, a man of twenty-two, asked nothing and signing nothing, and he stood at the back where the rail is and did not sit down in one of those nine chairs.**

---

**What was left of that Thursday went into the same two hours in the same yard and the same building as it does on the other four days of this week, and four jobs went into it.**

A board in a workshop had two circuits on one way out because the spare had been used by something that had since been taken away, so that one of them was always at the end of what the board could give.

He took the second one onto a way of its own and proved both at their loads together.

"**Thirteen pounds,**" he said. "**Two circuits on one way out is a board that has run out and nobody told it. About four of those boards in that workshop had a spare used and given back, and one of them has been a workshop that cannot run two machines for about four years.**"

A machine in that laundry had been put in on a way that also ran to a wall heater, so that either one of them going on brought the other in with it.

That machine was given a way of its own and proved with the wall heater at full heat.

"**Thirteen pounds,**" he said. "**A machine sharing a way with a wall heater is a machine that runs when a room is being heated. About four of those machines in that laundry share a way, and one of them has been a laundry nobody could explain the bills for about four years.**"

A light in a stairwell had been put in off the lighting board of the flat above it rather than off anything of its own, so that the landlord switching that flat off put it out.

He gave it a way of its own off the building and proved it with the flat's own lights off.

"**Nine pounds,**" he said. "**A stair light on somebody's lighting is a light somebody else owns. About four of those lights in that stairwell are on a flat's board, and one of them has been a stair that goes dark when a tenant goes to bed for about four years.**"

A socket in a corridor had a plate that had come off at some point and been put back with the screw through the cable behind it, so that the screw was the only thing holding that plate on.

He moved the cable out from under it and put the screw where it belonged.

"**Nine pounds,**" he said. "**A plate screwed through its own cable is a plate that is being held on by the cable. About four of those plates in that corridor are back on over the wire, and one of them has been a socket somebody would not let a child near for about four years.**"

**Forty-four pounds is what those four came to on that Thursday, exact.**

---

**That fourth of those four rooms had its door shut by about half past six on that Thursday, a woman of about thirty was behind that door, and the binder on the shelf at the back came off no shelf, and this page puts no figure on it.**
