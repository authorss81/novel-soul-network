# Chapter 930 — A Friday Of The Thirteenth Week, A Bench Until Two And A Counter Until About Six Before It, Then About Two Hours Of That Friday Evening In That First Floor Above A Line In Saltmarket Where About Nine People Were And Where Nothing Was Decided And The Fifth Heading Stood Over Nothing On All Nine Rows And Will Stand Over Nothing On The Next Ordinary Morning, And Forty-Two Pounds For Four Jobs

**The man of twenty-two was in that room on that Friday, asked nothing and signing nothing, and he stood at the back by the rail with the card in it, and he is not the person who signs anything in this city.**

He has said since that he was in that room about nine times in four years and had looked at the table every time, and that there is a fifth heading on that sheet and that he has not seen anybody write in it and that he is not going to be the first one.

Nobody in that room has said anything back to him about that, and nobody in that room has said anything back to him about anything else either.

**What was left of that Friday went into about two hours of the same yard and the same building, and four jobs went into it.**

A supply to a garage had been taken off the house board and had nothing of its own, so that the whole of that garage went off with the house and nothing in it could be worked on alone.

It was given a way of its own off the building and proved with the house off.

"**Twelve pounds,**" he said. "**A garage on the house board is a garage that cannot be worked on while anybody is in the house. About four of those garages in that row are on a house board, and one of them has been a garage that could not be used at evenings for about four years.**"

A machine in the kitchen of that café had been put in on the lighting because there was nothing else free on the board in that kitchen.

That machine was put on a way of its own and proved through a full cycle with the lights off.

"**Twelve pounds,**" he said. "**A machine on the lighting is a machine that only runs in daylight hours. About four of those machines in that kitchen are on a lighting way, and one of them has been a kitchen that cannot do a service at night for about four years.**"

A light in a stairwell had been put in with no earth at the fitting at all, because the fitting that had been taken out of it had been a metal one and the one that went in was a plastic one.

He put an earth on the plastic fitting from the conduit at it and proved the case.

"**Nine pounds,**" he said. "**A fitting changed for a plastic one and nothing done with it is a light with no earth. About four of those fittings in that stairwell are plastic, and one of them has been a stair that has never been tested for about four years.**"

A socket in a yard had been put in on a way out that a previous owner had added to a board with a spare that was never there, so that the board had been over its limit ever since.

He took that socket onto a spare and proved the board at its full load.

"**Nine pounds,**" he said. "**A way added with a spare that was not there is a board that has been over its limit since before anybody moved in. About four of those additions in that yard were done that way, and one of them has been a yard that trips when two things run for about four years.**"

**Forty-two pounds is what those four came to on that Friday, exact.**
