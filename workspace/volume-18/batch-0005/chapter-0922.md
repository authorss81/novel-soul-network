# Chapter 922 — A Tuesday Of The Same Week, A Bench Until Two And A Counter Until About Six Before About Two Hours Went Into That First Floor Above The Line Where About Nine People Were And Where One Of The Nine Was Not One Of The Usual Ones And What Was Said There Was Said Into A Face And Was Not Written Down Anywhere And Has Not Reached The Wednesday, And Forty-Two Pounds For Four Jobs

**Two hours of that Tuesday evening went into a first floor above a line in Saltmarket, where about nine people were and one of the nine had not been in that room before, and the whole of the day before that had been a bench until two and a counter until about six. Nothing was written down in that room that evening and nothing was counted aloud in any room in this city.**

---

That Tuesday brought eight callers into that shop, the last of them at about twenty to five, and the shutter came down at about ten.

**By that Tuesday those four units behind that service road had stood one thousand seven hundred and nine days, and that is two hundred and forty-four weeks and one day. That Tuesday the creased card in that rail had been in it one thousand seven hundred and thirteen days, and that is two hundred and forty-four weeks and five days, and the thirteenth of those nineteen ruled lines had been ruled one thousand five hundred and eighty days before it, and that is two hundred and twenty-five weeks and five days.**

---

**Nobody in that room had been told that anybody was coming on that Tuesday, and there was nothing in that room by which anybody could have been told.**

There is no list in that room. There is no book kept of who comes into it and there never has been, and the door of that room is on a latch that anybody coming up that stair can put up from the landing.

Nobody in that room sent for anybody that day. Nobody in this city has said since that anybody sent for anybody.

**And about nine people came into that room that Tuesday, and one of the nine was not one of the usual ones.**

Nobody in that room had met her there before. She had not been in that room in about four years, and about four people in that room have said since that they had not known she was in this city.

---

**She came in at about half past seven and she sat down in that row of chairs, and at the end of that evening nobody in that room could have said which of the nine she had sat in.**

She said one thing into somebody's face in that room at about nine o'clock. It was said quietly and it was said to one person, and nobody else heard all of it, and it was not long.

**It was not written down. Nobody in that room wrote it down, nobody has written it down since, and nobody in that room has said one word about what it was to anybody outside that room.**

About four people in that room have said since that they heard it and cannot put any of it down, and about four others have said since that they were in that room and did not hear it at all.

---

**And nothing about that Tuesday evening reached the Wednesday.**

Not one person in that room on the Wednesday referred to the evening before, and nobody who was in that room on the Tuesday had said anything to anybody about it in between.

About four people have said since that they noticed that on the Wednesday, and that they have not been able to work out whether anybody had decided not to bring it up or whether there was nothing anybody wanted to bring up.

---

**The man of twenty-two was in that room on that Tuesday, asked nothing and signing nothing, and he stood at the back by the rail.**

He has said since that he had never seen her before that Tuesday and that he did not know then what she was and does not know now.

He has said since that he noticed the thing about the row of chairs and that he did not put it to anybody, and that about four people in that room have since told him they noticed it too.

**What was left of that Tuesday went into about two hours of the same yard and the same building, and four jobs went into it.**

A socket in a workshop had a rewireable holder in it with a fuse two ratings above the one stamped on its own plate, so that a fault on that way had taken the whole of that bench dark twice in about four years.

He pulled the wrong one out, put in the rating on that plate, and proved the whole way with a load on it.

"**Thirteen pounds,**" he said. "**A fuse above the stamped rating is a fuse that lets the fault through on purpose. About four of those holders in that workshop carry a rating above the plate, and one of them has been a bench that has gone dark for no reason anybody could find about four years.**"

A pump in a yard had its earth taken off its own mounting bracket and put nowhere else, so that it was holding itself up and the earth was going back up the same steel.

He earthed it to the yard rail and proved the case against that rail.

"**Eleven pounds,**" he said. "**A pump earthed to its own bracket is a pump earthed to the thing that carries it. About four of those pumps in those yards are bonded to their brackets, and one of them has been a yard that could not be tested for about four years.**"

A lamp in a stair had been put into a holder somebody had filed because it did not fit properly, so that it rattled in that holder every time somebody went past it.

He put a new holder in and proved it with a lamp in it and the stair being walked.

"**Nine pounds,**" he said. "**A holder filed by somebody who did not know what it was for is a holder that does not hold. About four of those holders in that stair have been filed, and one of them has been a light nobody could leave burning for about four years.**"

A bell push in a corridor had been wired to a lighting way, so that the bell rang when the lights were put out at the end of the evening.

He took the bell onto a way of its own and proved it from the push with the lights off and on.

"**Nine pounds,**" he said. "**A bell on the lighting is a bell that goes off when somebody leaves. About four of those bells in that corridor are on a lighting way, and one of them has been a bell that rang in an empty corridor for about four years.**"

**Forty-two pounds is what those four came to on that Tuesday, exact.**

---

**On the fourth of those four rooms there was a shut door from about half past six on that Tuesday, a woman of about thirty was behind it, the binder standing on the shelf at the back came off no shelf that evening, and nothing is printed here for the page in it.**
