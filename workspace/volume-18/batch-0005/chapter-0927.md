# Chapter 927 — A Tuesday Of The Thirteenth Week, A Bench Until Two And A Counter Until About Six Before It, Then About Two Hours Of That Tuesday Evening In That First Floor Above A Line In Saltmarket Where Nobody Has Asked The Person Who Came On The Sunday One Question About Why They Came And Where A Person Who Had Not Been In Any Room In This City For About Four Years Was In That Room, And Forty-Three Pounds For Four Jobs

**Nothing was asked on that Tuesday of anybody about anybody, and it went the way this shop goes: a bench until two and a counter until about six, and then about two hours of that Tuesday evening into that first floor above a line in Saltmarket, where about nine people were and one of the nine had not been in any room in this city for about four years.**

---

Ten callers were into that shop on that Tuesday and the tenth of them at about half past four, and the shutter came down at about ten.

**By that Tuesday the four units behind that service road had stood one thousand seven hundred and sixteen days, and that is two hundred and forty-five weeks and one day. That Tuesday the card creased once had been in that rail one thousand seven hundred and twenty days, and that is two hundred and forty-five weeks and five days, and the twelfth of those nineteen ruled lines had been ruled one thousand six hundred and thirty-six days before it, and that is two hundred and thirty-three weeks and five days.**

---

**Nobody in that room has asked the one who came on the Sunday one question about why she came, and about four people in that room have said since that they would have asked her.**

One of those four has said since that she would have asked it in the doorway before she had sat down, and that she did not, and that she has thought about the doorway since.

Nobody in that room has asked her anything. Nobody in that city has asked her anything. About four people in that room have said since that they do not agree that asking her would have been allowed to be a question, and that they have not been able to finish that sentence.

---

**And the woman of about twenty-nine, who is a relative of one of the nine and is not on that sheet, was in that room on that Tuesday, and nobody in that room had been told that she was coming.**

She is not one of the nine. Nobody in that room has ever seen her name on anything, and nobody has looked for it, and nobody in that room has said that she should be on anything.

**About four people in that room have said since that she had not been in any room in this city for about four years before the Tuesday before, and that they had all assumed she was somewhere else, and that nobody in that room has ever said before that evening that a person simply coming into it was a thing that happened.**

She had been in that room once before, on the Tuesday of the week before, and had said one thing into one person's face at about nine o'clock, and nothing about that evening was written down.

---

**Nobody in that room asked her on that Tuesday why she had come back either, and about four people in that room have said since that they would have.**

She said one thing into the face of the woman of about forty-three at about half past seven on that Tuesday. It was brief and it was about that sheet and nobody in that room has said since what it was.

**Nobody in that room has said that the nine rows on that sheet have been dealt with. Nobody in that room has said that they have not been dealt with either. This page does not say which of the two of them is the true one, and nobody in this city can say either.**

---

**He was in that room on that Tuesday, a man of twenty-two, asked nothing and signing nothing, and he stood at the back by the rail with the card in it.**

He has said since that the row of chairs is still in that room and that he still does not sit in any of them, and that he has begun to think of the row as being about the room and not about the people in it.

Nobody in that room has taken that up since and nobody has said that it is wrong either.

---

**The two hours that were left of that Tuesday went into a yard in another district and into the same building, and four jobs went into it.**

A board in a workshop had a spare way that had been used for two different things at two different times by two different people, and nobody had written down which was which.

It took the two circuits onto ways of their own and proved both at their loads together.

"**Fourteen pounds,**" he said. "**A spare that has been used twice is a board nobody can read. About four of those boards in that workshop have a way that has been handed round, and one of them has been an argument about what a circuit was for about four years.**"

A machine in a yard had its supply taken off the same way as a crane socket, so that the machine could not run with the crane and the yard could not light with either of them.

He gave the machine a way of its own and proved it with the crane socket loaded.

"**Eleven pounds,**" he said. "**A machine and a crane on one way is a yard where nothing runs together. About four of those yards have two heavy things on one board, and one of them has been a yard that could only do one job at a time for about four years.**"

A light over a gate had been put in on the way out that runs to the meter, so that it went off whenever anything was done at the meter.

He took it onto a way of its own with a switch beside the gate and proved it with the meter board open.

"**Nine pounds,**" he said. "**A gate light on the meter way is a light that goes out when anybody opens that meter box. About four of those fittings in those yards run off that board, and one of them has been a gate nobody could see for about four years.**"

An immersion in a flat had been put in with no earth at the element at all, because the maker's plate had never been earthed and the fitting had been changed since.

He put an earth on the element from the pipe it is bolted to and proved the whole thing at full heat.

"**Nine pounds,**" he said. "**An immersion with no earth at the element is a bath in a metal box that nobody has ever tested. About four of those immersions in that block have no earth at the element, and one of them has been a bathroom nobody would let a child near for about four years.**"

**Forty-three pounds is what those four came to on that Tuesday, exact.**
