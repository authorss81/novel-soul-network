**A man of about thirty-three was in that room on that Friday and said four things about nine dates he has not looked at since a Friday about a fortnight ago, and about four people in that room have said since that they did not know he had looked at anything.**

**The rest of that Friday was about two hours in that yard and that building, and four jobs went into it.**

A washdown point in a yard had been put on the same spur as a sump pump, so that neither could be used while the yard was wet.

He took the washdown onto a way of its own and proved it through a full cycle.

"**Twelve pounds,**" he said. "**A washdown sharing a spur with a pump is a yard that cannot be washed and pumped at once. About four of those points in that yard are on a shared spur, and one of them has been a yard that is washed in the dark about four years.**"

A bulkhead light in a stairwell had no earth arriving at the fitting at all and was holding its earth through the conduit behind it.

He brought an earth in, took it off the conduit, and proved the fitting on a tester.

"**Eleven pounds,**" he said. "**A bulkhead with no earth of its own is a bulkhead on somebody else's conduit. About four of those fittings in that stairwell have no earth at the lamp, and one of them has been a light somebody would not stand under for about four years.**"

A small power outlet in a porters' lodge had been put on the lighting board, and the vacuum cleaner had been tripping the stair lights every time it started.

He gave the outlet a way of its own off a spare board and proved both.

"**Eleven pounds,**" he said. "**An outlet on a lighting board is an outlet that trips the lights. About four of those outlets in that lodge are on lighting circuits, and one of them has been a porter who could not vacuum after six in the evening for about four years.**"

A ramp light in a yard had its ring on the same terminal as the line, so that its earth came off the moment the circuit went live.

He moved the ring onto its own terminal and proved it with the circuit off and on.

"**Nine pounds,**" he said. "**A ring on the line is a lamp with no earth. About four of those fittings in that yard are wired that way, and one of them has been an intermittent fault at that ramp for about four years.**"

**Forty-three pounds is what those four came to on that Friday, exact.**
