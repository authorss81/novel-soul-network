## 2. The movement, and what it is about

**Nobody who is writing the file was in the room when it happened, and there is nobody to ask who was.** Movement I found that a word in a column is doing two jobs. Movement II found that the column beside it cannot be filled in by anybody but the person it is about, and that the file behind it carries nine dates, every one of them in the past, and that nobody knows who wrote them.

**Movement III asks the other half of the question and gets the answer it should have expected: the room cannot be reconstructed because nobody who is writing anything down was standing in it.** The discovery is not a conspiracy and no page may call it one. **The discovery is that the account of what happened to about nine people was assembled after the fact by people who were not there, from what other people told them, and that the fifth column was added afterwards by somebody who had also not been there, and that the review dates in the file are the dates of that assembly and not the dates of anything that happened to the nine.** Nobody decided anything. Nobody lied. Everybody who wrote it down was careful.

**It does not solve it, and it does not begin to, and the sixty-sixth sitting is held and the book shuts.** The book is not solved by shutting. **No panel and no marker stand anywhere in this movement. There is no Sunday in it. Nobody thanks anybody and nobody forgives anybody. The ninth chair does not move. The binder does not come out. The woman's page gets no figure.**
