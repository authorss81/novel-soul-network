# Chapter 905 — A Wednesday Of The Seventh Week, A Bench Until Two And A Counter Until About Six, Then About Two Hours Of That Evening In That First Floor Above The Line With About Nine People In It And No Sitting And No Figure Said In Any Room In This City, And A Man Of About Thirty-Three Saying One Thing In About Nine Seconds About The Difference Between What Happened And What Was Written Down Afterwards, And Thirty-Seven Pounds For Four Jobs

**A second Wednesday brought a bench until two and a counter until about six, and then about two hours of that Wednesday evening went into that first floor above the line, with about nine people in it and nothing in that room carrying a figure.**

---

Eleven names and eleven dates went onto that Wednesday, the eleventh of the dates at about twenty to five, and the shutter came down at about ten.

**Of that board's nineteen ruled lines the eighteenth had been ruled for one thousand three hundred and ninety-three days on that Wednesday, which is one hundred and ninety-nine weeks to the day. What the open page lying in front of that man is owed came to one thousand three hundred and sixty-five days, which is one hundred and ninety-five weeks to the day, and that man had been against that north wall one thousand two hundred and eighty-one days, which is one hundred and eighty-three weeks to the day.**

---

**At about ten past seven a man of about thirty-three said four things in about nine seconds, and none of them took longer than the one before it, and he did not sit down afterwards.**

He said: there are two things here and not one.

He said: what happened, and what was written down afterwards.

He said: the second of those is the only one anybody can check.

He said: and nobody has ever wanted to check it.

**Nobody in that room said he was wrong. Nobody in that room agreed with him either, and about four people in that room have said since that they did not want to check it then and do now, and about four people in that room have said since that they want it recorded that they did not want to check it then.**

---

**And nobody in that room has asked what checking it would consist of, and about four people in that room have worked it out for themselves since, and the answer in each case is the same file in a landlord's building in a second district, and nobody has gone back to it.**

It has been about a fortnight since about nine people went and looked at it. About four of them were let in and about five were not.

**Nobody in this city has asked to go back and nobody has been to the front of that landlord's counter about it since, and about four people who were let in have said since that they have not been able to get past the fact that somebody had to have written nine dates and did it alone.**

---

A man of twenty-two was in that room on that Wednesday as well, and nobody asked him a thing and he put his name to nothing, and he has said since that he thought about the second of those two things on the walk to the stop and got as far as the word *evidence* and did not use it.

He said afterwards, at about half past seven, that the piece of paper with the two kinds on it is the only thing he has ever seen that anybody can check, and that nobody has ever asked him to check it.

**Nobody in that room said anything back to that, and about four people in that room have said since that they heard it and have not thought about it since.**

---

The sheet went back onto that table at about nine and the fifth heading stood over nothing on all nine of its rows.

**About four people in that room wanted to ask her on that Wednesday and did not, and not one of them has asked her since, and one of them has said since that she has wanted to every week since and has not once asked.**

---

**What was left of that Wednesday was about two hours in that same yard and that same building, and four jobs were done in it.**

A loading bay light had its supply taken off a socket outlet that a machine was standing on, so that the bay went dark every time the machine ran.

He put the light on a way of its own and proved it with the machine running.

"**Ten pounds,**" he said. "**A bay light on a machine's socket is a bay light that goes out with the machine. About four of those fittings in that yard are on somebody else's outlet, and one of them has been a bay nobody could work in after four in the afternoon for about four years.**"

A gas interlock in a canteen kitchen had been wired off the lighting board, so that a light on the same circuit could take the interlock with it.

He gave the interlock its own supply from a spare way and proved the shut-off.

"**Ten pounds,**" he said. "**An interlock on a lighting board is an interlock somebody can switch off without knowing. About four of those interlocks in that kitchen are on lighting circuits, and one of them has been a room somebody will not sign for in about four years.**"

A pillar light on a gate in a yard had its earth taken off the gate post, which was on its own supply through the post itself.

He brought the earth onto the fitting, made it off, and proved it.

"**Nine pounds,**" he said. "**A light earthed off a gate post is a light on the gate's own supply. About four of those fittings in that yard are earthed off metalwork, and one of them has been a gate that has tripped on a wet morning about four years running.**"

A meter main in a stairwell had been made off with a tail that came round behind the board and back to the same place it came from.

He took the tail off, made each conductor off at the board, and proved the whole.

"**Eight pounds,**" he said. "**A main that comes back to where it started is a main nobody can prove. About four of those boards in that stairwell have a doubled tail, and one of them has been a supply that has read differently from its own meter for about four years.**"

**Thirty-seven pounds is what those four came to on that Wednesday, exact.**

---

**The fourth of those four rooms had its door shut and a woman of about thirty behind it from about half past six on that Wednesday, the binder on the shelf at the back has not come out on any of the ten days of this stretch, and this page prints no figure for it.**
