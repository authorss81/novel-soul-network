# Chapter 904 — A Tuesday Of The Seventh Week, A Bench Until Two And A Counter Until About Six, Then About Two Hours Of That Evening In That First Floor Above The Line With A Sheet Of Somebody's Own Put On The Table With A Heading Written At The Top Of It And Nine Lines Ruled Under That Heading, And Every One Of The Nine Blank, And Nine People Described And Not One Of Them Named, And Forty Pounds For Four Jobs

**By the Tuesday of that week there was a bench until two and a counter until about six, and then about two hours of the evening went into that first floor above the line, where about nine people were and somebody had put a piece of paper of his own on the table.**

---

Nine names went onto that Tuesday's sheet with nine dates beside them, the ninth of the dates at about twenty to five, and the shutter came down at about ten.

**The four converted units off that service road stood one thousand six hundred and seventy-four days by that Tuesday, two hundred and thirty-nine weeks and one day. The thirteenth of those nineteen ruled lines was one thousand five hundred and forty-five days, two hundred and twenty weeks and five days, and the man of about fifty-one had been against that north wall one thousand two hundred and eighty days, which is one hundred and eighty-two weeks and six days.**

---

**A man of about thirty-three brought a piece of paper of his own into that room on that Tuesday and put it on the table at about half past six, face up, and wrote a heading at the top of it, and ruled nine lines under the heading.**

The heading is not printed on this page and this page does not set it out.

The nine lines went down the paper under it. He ruled them in about nine seconds and then he stopped, and about four people in that room have said since that they thought he was going to write something on the first one.

**He did not write anything on the first one.**

---

**And then he asked, one at a time, going along the table, and he asked about nine people, and not one of the nine was in the room.**

He asked the woman who holds that room, and she said that she was in the room, and he ruled nothing and said nothing.

He asked a woman of about forty-three, and she said that she was not, and he put the pen down beside the paper rather than write.

He asked the woman of about thirty-eight, and she said that she had not been in that room in her life, and he nodded at her and wrote nothing.

He asked a man of about thirty-eight who keeps a clinic in a fourth district, and he said that he had come once and he knew which once, and nothing was written down.

He asked a man of about fifty-two who keeps a register, and he said that he was not, and he said it fast.

He asked a man of about fifty-seven, and the man of about fifty-seven said that he had never once been in that room, and the man of about thirty-three said that he had not known that.

**He asked a woman of about twenty-nine who is a relative of one of the nine, and she said that she had never been asked to go into any of those rooms in her life, and she has said since that she was the ninth one and that nobody had told her she was being counted.**

He asked two more people and both of them said that they were not, and nothing was written on either line.

---

**Nobody in that room worked out how anybody would know, and nobody has worked it out since, and the piece of paper went into a coat pocket with nine lines on it and nothing on any of the nine.**

About four people in that room have said since, separately, that they worked out within about nine days how nobody could know it, and that none of the four has said so in a room.

**Nobody in that room asked him what he was going to do with the paper, and nobody has asked him, and he has said since that he does not know and has not thrown it away.**

---

A man of twenty-two was in that room that evening, asked nothing, signed nothing, and watched a man of about thirty-three rule nine lines and fill none of them.

He has said since that he could have told every one of those nine that he was not in the room, and that he did not, and that he does not know what he thought the paper was for.

**Nobody in that room has asked him why he did not.**

---

**What was left of that Tuesday was about two hours in the same yard and the same building, and four jobs went into it.**

A tank pump in a yard was earthed off its own supply pipework, so that the whole of the earth path depended on a joint that somebody could tighten.

He took the earth onto the case, made it off at the gland, and proved it dead and live.

"**Twelve pounds,**" he said. "**A pump earthed to its own pipe is an earth through a joint. About four of those pumps in that yard are earthed off the pipework, and one of them has been a nuisance that arrived with the rain in about four years.**"

A barrier arm in a car park had its supply taken off the same way as the ticket machine at the kiosk, and it had stopped halfway up with a full car park behind it.

He gave the arm its own supply out of the kiosk board and proved it from down to up.

"**Twelve pounds,**" he said. "**An arm and a ticket machine on one supply is a car park that stops halfway. About four of those arms in that yard share a supply, and one of them has been a barrier down and a queue behind it for about four years.**"

A wall heater in a corridor was bonded to a length of steel trunking that ran past it on two brackets, so that it was holding itself up and nothing else.

He earthed it to the building, took it off the trunking, and proved it at full load.

"**Eight pounds,**" he said. "**A heater earthed to trunking is a heater on a bracket. About four of those heaters in that corridor are bonded to the trunking, and one of them has been a corridor somebody would not stand in for about four years.**"

A desk lamp in an office was on a circuit that also carried the machines on the desk, and it had been reported as the thing that dimmed when the copier ran.

He put the lamp on a way of its own and proved the circuit with everything running.

"**Eight pounds,**" he said. "**A lamp sharing a circuit with the machines is a lamp that gets blamed for the machines. About four of those lamps in that office are on shared circuits, and one of them has been called useless by everybody in that room for about four years.**"

**Forty pounds is what those four came to on that Tuesday, exact.**

---

**Behind the shut door of that fourth room there was a woman of about thirty from about half past six on that Tuesday, the binder standing on the shelf at the back of it did not come out on that day, and this page prints no figure for anything standing on that shelf.**
