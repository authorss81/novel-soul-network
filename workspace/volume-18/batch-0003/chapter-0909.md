# Chapter 909 — A Tuesday Of The Eighth Week, A Bench Until Two And A Counter Until About Six, Then The Four Jobs And The Counter And Those Four Rooms Off A Service Road And About Nine People In One Of Them, And A Woman Of About Forty-Three Saying Out Loud The One Thing This Has Been Looking For Since The Beginning Of The Week, That Nobody Who Is Writing That File Was In The Room, And Nobody In That Room Asking Who Is Writing It, And Forty-Three Pounds For Four Jobs

**Tuesday in that shop is a bench until two and a counter until about six, and then the evening went into the yard and the building and the four rooms off that service road, with about nine people in one of those four rooms and nobody in any room of this city saying a figure out loud.**

---

Ten names and ten dates went onto that Tuesday, the tenth of the dates at about twenty to five, and the shutter came down at about ten.

**Those four converted units off that road had stood one thousand six hundred and eighty-one days by that Tuesday, which is two hundred and forty weeks and one day. The line ruled twelfth on that board was one thousand six hundred and one days, which is two hundred and twenty-eight weeks and five days, and the man of about fifty-one had been against that north wall one thousand two hundred and eighty-seven days, which is one hundred and eighty-three weeks and six days.**

---

**And at about ten past seven a woman of about forty-three put down what she had been holding in her hand, which was nothing, and said the thing out loud for the first time in about four years.**

She said: nobody who is writing that file was in the room.

**Nobody in that room said anything for about nine seconds and nobody has said anything about it since, and about four people in that room have said since that they had each worked out something smaller and had not said the smaller thing either.**

She has said since that she has been the person who asks nine questions about nine things for about four years, that she has asked all nine, and that not one of the nine has ever been about the person who wrote anything.

---

**Nobody in that room asked who is writing it. Nobody in that room knows who is writing it and nobody in this city has worked out who is writing it, and the file names nobody on any of its nine rows and there is no minute of anything anywhere.**

**Four people in that room have said since, one to another and separately, that each of them knows a name, and that not one of those four names is any use.**

One of the four has said since that the person she would name first went to a town the bus takes about four days to reach and stopped writing to anybody in between about two years ago.

One has said since that the person she would name is not somebody who writes on forms, and that she has known that about him for about four years, and that she would still put his name first.

One has said since that the person she would name is the one person in this city nobody can ask, and that if he wrote it then nobody was ever going to be told what he wrote.

**The fourth has not said which of them and about four people in that room have said since that they would rather she did not.**

---

**The man of about thirty-three was in that room and said nothing at all about the sentence, and has said since that she is the first person he has heard say it who has no reason to want it said and that he had not thought of that before she said it.**

He was in that room, a man of twenty-two, and was asked nothing and signed nothing, and he has said since that the sentence is true and that he cannot check it and that those two things have been sitting side by side in his head for about four years.

**Nobody in that room has asked him which two things and he has not been asked twice.**

---

**She put the sheet back onto that table at about nine and the fifth heading stood over nothing on all nine of its rows, and about nine people watched her do it, and nobody put anything in it.**

---

**The rest of that Tuesday was about two hours in that yard and that building, and four jobs were done in it.**

A cold store light had a contactor that had welded itself shut, so that the light stayed on whether anybody was in there or not.

He changed the contactor, proved the light out from cold, and proved it again in about nine seconds.

"**Twelve pounds,**" he said. "**A contactor welded shut is a light that is on all day and all night. About four of those fittings in that cold store are on contacts that never open, and one of them has been a compressor that has been running against a light for about four years.**"

A wash basin outlet in a changing room had its earth taken off a stop tap and nothing else, so that the earth came and went with the water.

He took the earth off the tap, made it off at the fitting, and proved it with the circuit off.

"**Twelve pounds,**" he said. "**An outlet earthed to a stop tap is an outlet earthed to a length of pipe. About four of those outlets in that room are earthed off pipework, and one of them has been a fault that only showed on a wet Monday for about four years.**"

A bin store light had been left on its own override since the last time the bins went out, and there was no clock on that circuit anywhere.

He took the override off, put the light on the clock, and proved it.

"**Ten pounds,**" he said. "**A light on an override is a light nobody switches off. About four of those fittings in that yard are on an override with nothing counting them, and one of them has been a bill that everybody has read and nobody has acted on for about four years.**"

A ladder rack outlet in a workshop was drawing its supply off a trailing lead that had been wrapped round a pipe for years, and the pipe was hot in the summer.

He made a fixed outlet on the wall, took the trailing lead out, and proved the new one.

"**Nine pounds,**" he said. "**A trailing lead wrapped round a pipe is an outlet that will not last a winter. About four of those outlets in that workshop are on trailing leads, and one of them has been a trip that everybody put down to the cold for about four years.**"

**Forty-three pounds is what those four came to on that Tuesday, exact.**

---

**That fourth of those four rooms was shut by about half past six on that Tuesday with a woman of about thirty behind the door, the binder on the shelf at the back of it did not come out on that day, and this page prints no figure for it.**
