# Chapter 902 — A Friday Of The Sixth Week, A Bench Until Two And A Counter Until About Six, Then About Two Hours Of That Evening In A Room Off A Road Where A Service Group Meets, Which Is Not A Records Room And Is Not Any Of The Four Either, A Man Of About Fifty-Seven Sitting At A Wall And Being Asked Nothing About Himself, And A Word Beginning To Be Used About An Event By People Who Were Not At It, And Forty-One Pounds For Four Jobs

**On that Friday there was a bench until two and a counter until about six, and then about two hours of the evening went into a room off a road where a service group meets on a Friday and not on any other day of the week. About four of the people who go into that first floor went there instead, and none of them took anything with them.**

---

Ten names went onto that Friday's sheet with ten dates beside them, the tenth of the dates at about twenty to five, and the shutter came down at about ten.

**The seventeenth of those nineteen ruled lines had been ruled for one thousand four hundred and forty-two days on that Friday, which is two hundred and six weeks to the day. The fitting standing at the end of that corridor had been standing there one thousand two hundred and eighteen days, which is one hundred and seventy-four weeks to the day. And the single line inside the one box in that room off the road stood at one thousand and fifty days, which is one hundred and fifty weeks to the day, and describes nothing and has described nothing for a very long time.**

---

**About four of them walked out of that service road at about half past six and turned off it, and where they went they had not gone on any of the twenty days before that, and it was not the records room in the second district and it was not the first floor above the line.**

It is a room with a kettle on a table at one end and about nine folding chairs and a form on a nail. A service group meets in it. Nobody put anything on a table in that room on that Friday and nobody went there to look at anything.

**Nobody in that room knew why they had gone until about nine minutes after they got there, and about four of them have said since that they went because the alternative was going back to a room with a sheet on a table in it and a fifth heading over nothing on all nine of its rows.**

---

**And then a woman who has been in this city about nine years put a sentence into the middle of that room, and it had a word in it that about four people in that room heard and did not hear at the same time.**

She said: we were told the box had changed.

**Nobody in that room stopped her. She has said since that she was talking about a form and not about the nine, and that she has said the same sentence about a form to about nine rooms in about four years, and that she did not know it had a second half to it until she had said it.**

About four people in that room have said since that they have started using the word *we* about an event, and that they did not notice when they started, and one of them has said since that she went home and looked for the moment she started and could not find it and has stopped looking.

---

**A man of about thirty-three asked one thing at about a quarter to eight, and it was the first time anybody had asked it out loud in a room in this city.**

He put it flatly and asked for nothing back.

**Nobody in that room answered that either. About nine people were in it and one of them said *we* about half a minute later, in another sentence, without appearing to have heard the question at all.**

---

**A man of about fifty-seven was sitting against the far wall of that room with a cup and he was asked nothing about himself by anybody all evening, which is what happens on every page of this manuscript that he is on.**

He has been in this city a long time. A woman wrote something about him down once, on a piece of paper, and he came into a shop about nine weeks ago with nothing broken and nothing written and asked whether there was any paper in this city with his name on it and nothing in one of its columns, and he has been at two of these rooms since.

**What was said near him on that Friday was about somebody who was not there. Nobody asked him a question about himself, nobody asked him about the four years he has been thinking about a piece of paper, and nobody has asked him since either.**

He has said since that he was not listening to any of it, and that he was listening to all of it, and that he has not worked out how those two can both be true and that he has not been asked to.

---

**What was left of that Friday was about two hours in the same yard and the same building, and four jobs went into it.**

A fire door in a stairwell had a hold-open magnet on its head that was not the strength for that door, so that the door came back on the last of the closing and stood a finger's width open.

He took the magnet off, put one on that matches the door, and proved it latched four times.

"**Thirteen pounds,**" he said. "**A door with the wrong magnet is a door that never quite shuts. About four of those holds in that stairwell are undersized, and one of them has been a stairwell that has never been quite clear for about four years.**"

A junction box in a ceiling void had two cables arriving with their conductors taped together under one piece of tape, and the only terminal under the tape belonged to the first cable.

He opened it up, made each conductor off on its own, and proved the circuit.

"**Eleven pounds,**" he said. "**Two conductors under one tape is one conductor with a spare. About four of those boxes in that void are taped instead of made off, and one of them has been a light that has gone at about four years' intervals ever since.**"

An emergency light in a car park had a battery of the same shape in it and not the same rating, and it had been reporting itself fit because nothing checks what is in it.

He took the battery out, put one of the rating that belongs there, and proved the whole fitting on its own supply.

"**Ten pounds,**" he said. "**A lamp that checks nothing is a lamp nobody has checked. About four of those fittings in that car park are reported by nobody and tested by nobody, and one of them has been a stairwell nobody wanted to walk in for about four years.**"

A bell transformer in a porch had been mounted on a plastic box with no earth anywhere on it at all, and the bell had worked anyway for about four years.

He earthed the box, made off the earth at the fitting, and proved it with the circuit off.

"**Seven pounds,**" he said. "**A transformer on plastic with no earth is a transformer somebody has had working by luck. About four of those transformers in that building are on plastic, and one of them has been a bell that stopped on a wet night about four years ago and was working again by the morning.**"

**Forty-one pounds is what those four came to on that Friday, exact.**
