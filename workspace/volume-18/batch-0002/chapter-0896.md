# Chapter 896 — A Wednesday Of The Fourth Week, A Bench Until Two And A Counter Until About Six, Then About Two Hours Of That Evening In That First Floor Above A Line In Saltmarket With About Nine People In It And The Count Said Once Into The Face Of That Room, And The Book With A Green Cover Opened Because A Caller Said A Thing About A Form To The Woman Who Holds That Room, And A Man Of Twenty-Two Asking For Nothing And Saying Afterwards That The Fifth Column Of A Sheet Will Still Be Empty On Monday, And Nobody Saying Anything Back To That, And Forty-Two Pounds For Four Jobs

**A Wednesday was a bench until two and a counter until about six, and about two hours of the evening after that went into that first floor above the line in Saltmarket with about nine people in it, and about nine of them had been in that room before and had never once heard what is said at the start of it.**

---

Nine names and nine dates were written on that Wednesday, the ninth of the dates taken at about twenty to five, and the shutter came down at about ten that night.

**The four converted units behind that service road stood one thousand six hundred and fifty-four days on that Wednesday, two hundred and thirty-six weeks and two days. The board on two nails carried a fifteenth line that had been ruled for one thousand four hundred and sixty-nine days, two hundred and nine weeks and six days, and a sixteenth that had been ruled for one thousand four hundred and forty-four days, two hundred and six weeks and two days.**

**The man of about fifty-one against that north wall in that first floor had been in that spot one thousand two hundred and sixty days, which is one hundred and eighty weeks to the day. What the open page lying in front of him is owed came to one thousand three hundred and forty-four days, which is one hundred and ninety-two weeks to the day. And behind that woman's chair there is a space that has had nothing standing in it since a week in the spring, and it is five hundred and thirty-two days, and that is seventy-six weeks to the day, and this page is the only page of this stretch of days that puts a figure on it.**

---

**The count was said once at about half past six, into the face of that room, and it took about nine seconds, and it has been said that way every fourth Wednesday for about nineteen years.**

She said: seventy, of which sixty-five correspond.

She said it once and she did not say it twice and she did not explain it, and a man of about thirty-four said nothing at all, which is what a man in that room has done every fourth Wednesday for about nineteen years.

**Nobody in that room said anything for about nine seconds afterwards, and the woman of about thirty-eight who had asked about the second figure four weeks before did not ask about it this time.**

The second figure is that one less five, and the five are older than that book and are written down on a sheet she has never shown anybody, and she has never said that twice.

---

**And at about twenty past seven something happened in that room that had not happened there in about four years, and it happened because a woman said one thing out loud in front of her, and it was not a question and it was not an argument.**

A woman had come into that room about four times over about nine weeks and had never once been asked what she had come for.

The woman who holds that room asked her. She said, standing at the front of that table, in about nine seconds, that the form asked her one thing twice in two different boxes, and that the two boxes did not mean the same thing, and that she would like one of them taken off it, and that she did not care which one.

She said that it was about the form and not about herself, and about four people in that room have said since that she had said that before anybody had asked her to.

**Nobody in that room said anything at all when she said it, and about four people in that room have said since that they were not expecting anything like that.**

---

**And the book in the green binding was opened at about half past seven on that Wednesday, and it was opened because of a thing a person said to a woman standing in front of her, and it was opened by the woman who holds that room and by nobody else.**

It had a line on it that morning that it had not had the Wednesday before. It stood at sixty-eight lines when that room opened and it stood at sixty-nine lines when the shutter came down at about ten that night.

**Nobody in that room read that line out and nobody in that room has read it out since, and about four people in that room have said since that they saw it and did not take a copy of it and have not been asked for one since.**

The tin beside it was not opened that evening and stood at seventy-three.

**Nobody in this city has worked out what to do with a book that is on sixty-nine lines and a tin that is on seventy-three, and nobody in this city has printed the difference between them, and this page does not print it either.**

---

**Nobody in that room said that the book opening settled anything, and nobody in that room has said anything of the kind since, and about four people in that room have said since that they wanted somebody to say it out loud and that nobody would take the sentence and say it for them.**

**He was in that room that evening, asked nothing at all, signed nothing, and he is not the person who signs anything in this stretch of days. He has said since that he was not asked anything because nobody thought there was anything to ask him, and that this is the fourth time in about four months that it has happened to him and that he has stopped taking it as a thing to work out.**

He said afterwards, at about twenty to eight, that the fifth column of that sheet would still be empty on Monday.

He said it into the back of the room and nobody in that room was facing him when he said it.

**Nobody in that room said anything back to that, and about four people in that room have said since that they had heard it and had not turned round, and one of them has said since that she heard it and understood it and did not turn round on purpose.**

---

**The rest of that Wednesday was about two hours in a yard and a building and four jobs went into it.**

A switch in a passage there had a loop of cable behind the plate, doubling back into its own terminal, so that it held about nine seconds of its own supply.

He took the loop out, re-terminated the tail, and proved it.

"**Twelve pounds,**" he said. "**A switch with a loop behind the plate is a switch that holds itself on. About four of those switches in that passage have loops behind them, and one of them has been a light nobody could put out for about four years.**"

A meter tail in a yard there had one of its two conductors cut back at the gland and pushed inside the sheath

He pulled the tail back out, made both conductors off properly, and proved the board.

"**Eleven pounds,**" he said. "**A tail with a conductor pushed back into its sheath is a tail with one conductor. About four of those tails in that yard have had a conductor turned back at the gland, and one of them has been a reading that came and went in about four years.**"

A socket in a canteen there had its ring on the same terminal as the neutral, so that the earth came off the moment the circuit went live.

He moved the ring onto its own terminal and proved it with the circuit off and on.

"**Ten pounds,**" he said. "**A socket ring on a neutral is a socket with no earth. About four of those sockets in that canteen are wired that way, and one of them has been an appliance that got a shock and nobody found out why in about four years.**"

A fan isolator in a plant room there had been mounted on a plywood backing, and the whole of the unit was standing on a piece of timber about nine millimetres thick.

He took the unit off the timber, made up proper fixings into the wall, and proved it dead and live.

"**Nine pounds,**" he said. "**An isolator on a plywood back is an isolator standing on its own case. About four of those isolators in that plant room are on timber, and one of them has been a unit that has worked loose for about four years.**"

**Forty-two pounds is what those four came to on that Wednesday, exact.**
