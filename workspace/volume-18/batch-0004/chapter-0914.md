# Chapter 914 — A Thursday Of The Ninth Week, A Bench Until Two And A Counter Until About Six Before It, And Then About Two Hours Of That Evening In That First Floor Above The Line, A Form On That Table With A Space On It For A Date, Nine People Asked One At A Time Going Along That Table Whether A Date Can Be Put In, Four Of The Nine Saying It Cannot And Five Of The Nine Saying Nothing, And Nobody Working Out Why, And Thirty-Nine Pounds For Four Jobs

**Thursday of that week began with a bench until two and a counter until about six, and the whole of the evening after that went into that first floor above a line in Saltmarket, where about nine people were. No sitting was held and nothing in this city was said out loud as a figure.**

---

Nine callers came into that shop on that Thursday, the ninth of them at about ten past nine, and the shutter came down at about ten.

**The creased card in the rail by that first door had stood there one thousand six hundred and ninety-four days by that Thursday, and that is two hundred and forty-two weeks to the day. The twelfth of those nineteen ruled lines had been ruled one thousand six hundred and ten days before it, and that is two hundred and thirty weeks to the day, and its thirteenth one thousand five hundred and sixty-one days, and that is two hundred and twenty-three weeks to the day.**

**Its fourteenth line had been ruled one thousand five hundred and twenty-six days before that, and that is two hundred and eighteen weeks to the day, and the fifteenth one thousand five hundred and five days, and that is two hundred and fifteen weeks to the day. The last of the nineteen had been ruled one thousand three hundred and eighty-six days before it, and that is one hundred and ninety-eight weeks to the day, and the hold over nine crates and the ground under them had been sitting there one thousand three hundred and twenty-three days, and that is one hundred and eighty-nine weeks to the day.**

---

**A hearing has to be told in advance what it is, and the telling is a form, and the form was on that table on that Thursday with a space on it for a date and nothing in that space.**

Nobody in that room has said who wrote the form out or who brought it to that table or when either of those two things happened, and the woman of about forty-three, who keeps the file nobody has ever asked her about, picked it up and read it and put it back down.

---

**She asked nine people that Thursday, one at a time, going along the table, whether a date can be put into that space.**

She asked the man of about thirty-three first.

He said: no, a date is a thing you have to ask a person for.

She asked Sera Quill second.

She said: nobody is told anything by being handed a day to turn up.

She asked a man of about fifty-seven third, and he said it slowly, twice over.

He said: you would be writing the day out for them.

She asked a woman of about thirty-eight fourth.

She said: not until somebody has asked them.

**And then she asked five more people and five people said nothing.**

The woman of about forty-three said nothing to herself. The woman of about sixty said nothing. A man of about thirty-eight who keeps a clinic in a fourth district said nothing. A woman of about twenty-nine who is a relative of one of the nine said nothing. The man of twenty-two said nothing.

---

**Nobody in that room worked out on that Thursday why those four people said what they said, and nobody in that room has worked out why the other five said nothing.**

About four people in that room have said since that they did not know at the time and know now, and each of the four has said the same thing about it in a different way: that the four of them were not refusing the form, they were refusing one particular space on it, and that they had not known that was what they were doing until they had said it.

The man of about thirty-three has said since that he went home on that Thursday and worked out what he had actually said, and that it had taken him about an hour to get to it, and that he had been asked in about nine seconds.

**Nobody in that room has said since that either of those two things is a reason.**

---

**The space on that form was empty when the shutter came down at about ten on that Thursday, and it was empty at about nine, and nobody in that room has said when it stopped being empty.**

Nobody in that room has said who put anything in it. Nobody has said it to anybody and nobody has been asked.

**And nobody in this city has said since whether those four had understood what she was asking, and nobody in this city has said since whether the other five had not, and anybody in this city may hold either opinion and put it nowhere in writing.**

---

**A man of twenty-two stood at the back of that room on that Thursday, asked nothing and signing nothing, and he was the ninth person she asked.**

He has said since that he went over the four answers the other four people gave that evening and has not been able to work out why one of the four of them sounded to him like an answer and the other three of them did not.

He has said since that he has thought about which of the two it was, and that he does not want to be asked, and that nobody in that room has asked him.

---

**That Thursday left about two hours of the same yard and the same building, and four jobs went into them.**

A hoist in a yard had its pendant controller earthed through its own cable, so that the control it answered to had no earth of its own beyond the flex it hung from.

He took the earth onto the controller body, made it off at the gland, and proved the pendant at both ends of the travel.

"**Eleven pounds,**" he said. "**A pendant earthed through its own cable is a pendant with no earth at the machine end. About four of those controllers in that yard are earthed that way, and one of them has been a stop that nobody could account for in about four years.**"

A socket in a corridor had been replaced with one that took a different pin size into the same box, so that the earth pin could sit on the face plate instead of in the shell.

He changed the socket for one that put its earth where the box was, and proved it with a load on it.

"**Ten pounds,**" he said. "**A socket in a box that cannot reach its own earth is a socket that has no earth. About four of those sockets in that corridor are that make, and one of them has been a cleaner who would not use that corner for about four years.**"

A light in a stair had been wired off a way that also carried the sockets on that landing, so that a plug heater anywhere on the landing put that light out.

He gave that light a way of its own and proved it with a heater running on the landing.

"**Nine pounds,**" he said. "**A light sharing a way with the sockets is a light that goes out when somebody dries their hands. About four of those lights on that landing are shared, and one of them has been a stair that somebody would not leave a bag in for about four years.**"

An extractor fan in a workshop had been put in on the lighting board because it was the nearest spare, and it came on with the lights rather than with anything else.

He gave it a way of its own and proved it starting on its own.

"**Nine pounds,**" he said. "**A fan on the lights is a fan nobody can run in a workshop with the lights off. About four of those fans in that workshop are on a lighting board, and one of them has been fumes nobody could explain for about four years.**"

**Thirty-nine pounds is what those four came to on that Thursday, exact.**
