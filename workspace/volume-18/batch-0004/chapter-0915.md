# Chapter 915 — A Friday Of The Ninth Week, A Bench Until Two And A Counter Until About Six In Front Of It, And Then About Two Hours Of That Evening In That First Floor Above The Line, A Woman Of About Sixty-Two Putting A Key On A Table Beside A Form And Not Saying What It Is, About Nine People Looking At It And Nobody In That Room Saying Anything About It For About Nine Minutes, And Forty-Three Pounds For Four Jobs

**A second Friday came round with a bench until two and a counter until about six in front of it, and then about two hours of that Friday evening went into that first floor above a line in Saltmarket, where about nine people were. There was no sitting on that Friday, and nothing in this city was said aloud as a number.**

---

Eight callers came into that shop on that Friday and the eighth of them at about twenty to five, and the shutter came down at about ten.

**The seventeenth of the nineteen ruled lines had been ruled one thousand four hundred and sixty-three days before that Friday, and that is two hundred and nine weeks to the day. The post at the end of that corridor had been standing there one thousand two hundred and thirty-nine days, and that is one hundred and seventy-seven weeks to the day, and one line inside one box off that road had been filled in about sixteen months back, which is one thousand and seventy-one days, and that is one hundred and fifty-three weeks to the day.**

---

**The form with the space on it for a date was on that table at about seven with the space still empty, and the sheet with four columns was on its own table with the fifth heading standing over nothing on all nine rows.**

About nine people were in that room. The woman of about sixty-two had come up that stair at about half past six, which she had not done on the Tuesday before, and she sat down at the table about two feet along from the form.

---

**And at about half past eight on that Friday she put a key on that table beside that form, and she did not say what it was, and about nine people looked at it.**

Nobody in that room said anything about it for about nine minutes.

The key was on a loop of brown tape and there was writing on the tape and the writing on that tape has gone so far that nobody in that room could read it and nobody in that room tried very hard.

There were five of those keys. She had kept them about four years.

**She has said since that she did not know she was going to do that, and that she decided about four times in those nine minutes, and that the fourth of those four times was the one she did it on.**

She has said since that the first of the four times was when she sat down, and that the second was about four minutes later, and that the third was after somebody at the other end of that table said something about a bus, and that she has not been able to work out what any of those three decided and that the fourth one decided for her.

---

**Nobody in that room asked her why she has kept it. Nobody asked her on that Friday and nobody in that city has asked her since.**

A man of about fifty-seven, who was at that wall, has said since that he wanted to ask and that he had his mouth open and that he closed it again, and that he has wanted to since.

About four people in that room have said since that they would have liked to ask and that they do not think they would have been the person to ask, and that all four of them have given a different reason for that.

---

**And at the end of those nine minutes she said one thing, and it was said into the middle of that table and not to anybody in it.**

She said: it used to say where, and it does not any more.

She said that once and she did not say it twice and she did not explain it, and about four people in that room have said since that they thought she was going to say what the key opened and that she said something else instead.

**Nobody in that room said anything back to her about that sentence, and nobody in that room has said anything back to her since.**

---

**The key was still on that table when the shutter came down at about ten on that Friday, and it was not on that table when that room was opened the next time anybody went into it.**

Nobody in that room has said where it went and nobody in that room has said who took it, and nobody in this city has said either of those two things.

She has said, to one person and not in a room, that she had wanted it gone before the shutter came down and that she has not been able to remember deciding to want it gone.

---

**A man of twenty-two was in that room on that Friday, asked nothing and signing nothing, and he watched a woman of about sixty-two not put a key down for about four minutes before she put it down.**

He has said since that he noticed her hand move three times and that he has not been able to say what any of the three was, and that the fourth time her hand went towards her purse he looked away.

He has said since that he has not been able to account for why he looked away and that he has gone over it since and has not got any further than that.

---

**What was left of that Friday went into about two hours of the same yard and the same building, and four jobs went into it.**

A washroom in a workshop had a heater above it that was wired to a spur taken off a lighting board, so that the heater ran only when somebody put the lights on over there.

He gave that heater a way of its own off a board with a spare on it, and proved it with the lights over there off.

"**Thirteen pounds,**" he said. "**A heater on the lights is a heater that dries you only in the dark. About four of the washroom heaters in that workshop are on a lighting way, and one of them has been a washroom that was cold every day for about four years.**"

A compressor in a yard had been given a plug and socket that were rated for the cable and not for the load, so that the cable was running at its limit every time the compressor came in.

He changed the fitting for one rated for the load and proved the compressor through a full start.

"**Twelve pounds,**" he said. "**A plug rated below its cable is a plug that decides when the cable gives up. About four of those outlets in that yard are rated below the load standing on them, and one of them has been a compressor that has cut out for no reason for about four years.**"

A time clock in a yard gate had its bell wired into the alarm circuit, so that every clocking on or off rang the alarm in the gatehouse.

He took the bell off the alarm and gave it its own, and proved it clocking on.

"**Nine pounds,**" he said. "**A time clock on the alarm is a gate that alarms all day. About four of those clocks in that yard are on the alarm, and one of them has been a gatehouse that had stopped believing anybody for about four years.**"

A socket in a canteen had been put in behind a counter that was bolted to the floor, so that its earth went back through the counter frame and two bolts.

He earthed it to the floor, took it off the counter frame, and proved it with the kettle on it.

"**Nine pounds,**" he said. "**A socket earthed to a bolted counter is a socket earthed to two bolts. About four of those sockets behind that counter are earthed that way, and one of them has been a kettle that trips the whole canteen for about four years.**"

**Forty-three pounds is what those four came to on that Friday, exact.**

---
