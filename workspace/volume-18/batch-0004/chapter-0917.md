# Chapter 917 — A Wednesday Of The Tenth Week, A Bench Until Two And A Counter Until About Six Before It, And Then About Two Hours Of That Evening In That First Floor Above The Line, A Man Of About Thirty-Three Saying One Thing In About Nine Seconds About The Difference Between A Room That Has Been Set Up For People And A Room That Has People In It, Nobody Saying He Was Wrong, And Thirty-Seven Pounds For Four Jobs

**On that Wednesday somebody said a thing into a face, and there had been a bench until two and a counter until about six before it, and then about two hours of that Wednesday evening went into that first floor above a line in Saltmarket, where about nine people were. It was not a sitting; nothing was counted aloud in any room in this city.**

---

Nine callers came into that shop on that Wednesday and the last of them at about twenty to five, and the shutter came down at about ten.

**The eighteenth of the nineteen ruled lines on that board had been ruled one thousand four hundred and fourteen days before that Wednesday, and that is two hundred and two weeks to the day. What the open page lying in front of the man of about fifty-one is owed was one thousand three hundred and eighty-six days, and that is one hundred and ninety-eight weeks to the day, and that man himself had been against that north wall one thousand three hundred and two days, and that is one hundred and eighty-six weeks to the day.**

---

**A man of about thirty-three had been in the room in a second district on the Tuesday before, where nine folding chairs stood in a row in front of a table with the gaps all the same width, and he had been in this room on the Wednesday, where about nine people were sitting at tables.**

He stood up at about half past seven on that Wednesday and said two things, and he said them flat and at the same speed the whole way through, and it took about nine seconds.

He said: a room somebody has set up for people can be counted.

He said: a room with people in it cannot.

**Nobody in that room said that he was wrong and nobody in that room agreed with him either, and both of those went on past the shutter that night.**

---

**And about four people in that room have said since that they had been counting the first of those two for about four years and had not noticed that they were doing it.**

One of those four has said since that he has gone back through his own notebook for those four years and found a row of figures in it with no reason attached, and that he now knows what the figures were counting and that he had been counting them from something he had seen in a room before he ever came into that room.

A second of those four has said since that she does not agree with all of it, and that a room with people in it can be counted by anybody who is willing to count people in it, and that nobody in that room is willing and that this is the part that took four years rather than nine seconds.

---

**A man of about fifty-seven was at that wall on that Wednesday and was asked nothing about himself, and what was said near him was about rooms and what is done to them before anybody walks into them.**

He has said since that he has been in about nine rooms in this city that had been set up for him and that he has never once been in one that had been set up for anybody else.

---

**Nobody in that room on that Wednesday asked the man of about thirty-three what anybody is supposed to do with the difference between those two, and nobody in that room has asked him since.**

He has said since that he does not know and that he did not expect anybody to say anything back, and that he had worked out beforehand that he would say it once and would not argue about it, and that he has not argued about it.

**And nobody in that room has said since whether the two rooms he was talking about were the same room, and nobody in that room has said whether they were two.**

---

**He was in that room on that Wednesday, a man of twenty-two, asked nothing and signing nothing, and he had carried a chair up a stair on the Tuesday before, and nobody asked him to carry it.**

He has said since that a room with chairs set out and a room with people in it looked like the same thing to him until somebody said the thing he said in that room, and that he has been to that first floor about nine times and had always put it down to the chairs.

He has said since that it is not about the chairs and that he does not yet know what it is about.

---

**What was left of that Wednesday went into about two hours of the same yard and the same building, and four jobs went into it.**

A washing machine in a block of flats had been given a supply off a way out that also carried the lights on that landing, so that the machine tripping at a spin took the landing dark.

He gave it a way of its own off the board in the plant room and proved it at full spin with the landing lights off.

"**Ten pounds,**" he said. "**A machine on the landing lights is a machine that turns the landing out when it spins. About four of those machines in that block are on a lighting way, and one of them has been a landing nobody could light while a wash was running for about four years.**"

A socket in a workshop had been put in on a way that was already at its limit with two heaters on it, so that anything else on that way had nothing left to draw.

He took that socket onto a spare and proved it with both heaters running.

"**Nine pounds,**" he said. "**A socket on a way that is already full is a socket that works until somebody boils a kettle. About four of those sockets in that workshop are on ways with no room left on them, and one of them has been an argument that went nowhere for about four years.**"

A floodlight in a yard had been put in without any earth at all, because the fitting it went into was a plastic one and the plate it was on was painted.

He earthed it to the metal surround and proved it against that surround.

"**Nine pounds,**" he said. "**A light with no earth at all is a light that nobody has ever tested. About four of those fittings in that yard are plastic, and one of them has been a light that was never switched on to find out for about four years.**"

A fan in a workshop had its own way out but had been wired into it with no earth terminal at the motor end, so that the case was doing the work of an earth that was not there.

He put an earth on the motor end and proved the case against the machine.

"**Nine pounds,**" he said. "**A motor with no earth terminal is a motor whose case is its earth. About four of those fans in that workshop have no earth at the motor, and one of them has been a machine nobody would put a hand on for about four years.**"

**Thirty-seven pounds is what those four came to on that Wednesday, exact.**

---

**Nobody opened the fourth of those four rooms on that Wednesday; its door was shut by about half past six with a woman of about thirty behind it, the binder on the back shelf did not come out, and this page prints no figure for it.**
