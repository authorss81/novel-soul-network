# Chapter 932 — The Thursday Of That Same Week, A Bench Until Two And A Counter Until About Six Before It, And Then A Woman Of About Sixty Who Says Out Loud That She Has Copied A Sheet Out More Than Once And That Her Own Copies Of It Do Not Agree, And Nobody In That Room Asking Her Whose Copies They Are, And Forty-Two Pounds For Four Jobs

**Two sheets and a difference between them were the shape of that Thursday, and the day itself was a bench until two and a counter until about six before about nine people had come into one room off a service road to argue about a column of a form that nobody in that room had written anything in.**

---

Eight callers came into that shop on that Thursday between about seven and about half past nine, and the shutter came down at about ten.

**That card had been standing in that rail for one thousand seven hundred and twenty-nine days, and that is two hundred and forty-seven weeks to the day. That twelfth line had been ruled one thousand six hundred and forty-five days before, and that is two hundred and thirty-five weeks to the day. The thirteenth stood directly under it with nothing on it for one thousand five hundred and ninety-six days, and that is two hundred and twenty-eight weeks to the day. The fourteenth below that had been blank for one thousand five hundred and sixty-one days, and that is two hundred and twenty-three weeks to the day. The fifteenth further down had been blank for one thousand five hundred and forty days, and that is two hundred and twenty weeks to the day. The bottom line of that board of nineteen was ruled last of the nineteen, one thousand four hundred and twenty-one days before that Thursday, and that is two hundred and three weeks to the day. The hold laid across nine crates and the floor under all of them had not moved one thousand three hundred and fifty-eight days, and that is one hundred and ninety-four weeks to the day.**

---

**A woman of about sixty said one thing out loud in that room on that Thursday at about ten to eight, into the face of the woman of about forty-three, and nobody in that room asked her one question about it afterwards.**

"I have copied that sheet out twice for myself. The two copies disagree."

**Nobody in that room has asked her whose copies they are, and about four people in that room have said since that they would have asked her that.**

She has been in this city since before the spring and she has never said how many times she has copied it out. Nobody in that room knows whether she is the only person who has, and nobody in that room has ever asked her, and she has not volunteered it.

**And she has said that she copied it for herself and for no one else, and the two copies she made for herself differ in one place, and the place they differ in is the fifth column.**

In one of them the fifth column is ruled. In the other it is not. In both of them there is nothing written in it.

---

**The man of about thirty-three said the thing about two kinds of room once on that Thursday and nobody in that room argued with it and nobody agreed with it.**

"A room somebody has set up for people can be counted. A room with people in it cannot."

He has said that same shape of sentence before in this city, on two earlier days of this year, and he has said he knows he has said it before, and he said it again on that Thursday because he said it had not been finished.

Nobody in that room has asked him what he thinks anybody should now do differently, on that Thursday or on any other day.

**About four people in that room have said since that they had been counting the first of those two things for about four years and had not noticed that they were counting it.**

---

**Nobody in that room on that Thursday asked the woman of about sixty a second question, and nobody in that room asked the man of about thirty-three what he meant, and the two of them left that room separately and neither of them knew the other one was going to say anything.**

Nobody in that room has said since that the two copies are the same copies. Nobody in that room has compared them against each other in this city. Nobody in that room has compared them against anything at all.

**And nobody in that room said one word about the fifth heading on that sheet, and the fifth heading stood over nothing on all nine of its rows for the whole of that Thursday and will stand over nothing on all nine of its rows on the next ordinary morning.**

Nobody in that room has said that those nine rows have been dealt with. Nobody in that room has said since that they have not been dealt with either.

---

**What was left of that Thursday went into about two hours of the same yard and the same building, and four jobs went into it.**

A consumer unit in a stair had been fitted where the previous one had been fitted with its main switch wired into the neutral, so that the whole unit had been turning the neutral off with the live and nobody had ever seen it because the sockets it supplied were all down at the same time anyway.

He rewired the switch to the line and proved the whole of that unit with the load on it.

"**Thirteen pounds,**" he said. "**A switch on the neutral is a unit that puts the live onto earth when it trips. About four of those units in that building are wired that way, and one of them has been a stair where nobody could find the fault for about four years.**"

A floodlight over a yard had come off the same way as an office sign on the far side of that yard, so that the sign could not be lit without the yard going black.

He took the floodlight onto its own way and proved the sign separately at the same hour.

"**Eleven pounds,**" he said. "**Two things on one supply is two things that cannot be on at once. About four of those pairs in that yard are on one supply, and one of them has been a yard that has been dark every evening the sign is lit for about four years.**"

An extractor fan over a sink in a canteen had been put on the lighting, so that it ran only while the lights above it were on and never in the middle of a cooking shift.

He put it on a supply of its own and ran it through a full service with the lights off.

"**Nine pounds,**" he said. "**An extract fan on the lighting is a fan that runs when nobody is cooking. About four of those fans in that canteen are on a lighting way, and one of them has been a kitchen that has stood in its own steam for about four years.**"

A hand lamp on a flex in a workshop had been wired through a switch that was mounted in a metal box bonded to nothing, so that taking the switch off left the lamp live with no way to cut it.

He bonded that box properly and proved the case with a meter on it.

"**Nine pounds,**" he said. "**A switch in a box bonded to nothing is a lamp nobody can switch off. About four of those boxes in that workshop are like that, and one of them has been a lamp that has had to be pulled out at the plug for about four years.**"

**Forty-two pounds is what those four came to on that Thursday, exact.**

**By about half past six on that Thursday the door of the fourth of those four rooms had been shut, and the woman of about thirty was behind it, and nothing came off the shelf at the back of that room, and no figure for the page in what is on that shelf is set down here.**
