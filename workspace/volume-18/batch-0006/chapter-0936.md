**The reason that form carries no names on it was said out loud into a face on that Wednesday at about half past six, by the woman of about forty-three, to the man of about thirty-three, and it is the whole of what that room did that evening.**

"A name on that sheet is an answer nobody gave."

**Nobody in that room argued with it and nobody in that room agreed with it, and about four people in that room have said since that it is true, and not one of them has said what should now be done with a form that carries nine rows and no names on it.**

Nobody in this city has said who decided that the form carries no names. The woman of about forty-three has not said that she decided it, and nobody in that room has asked her whether she did.
