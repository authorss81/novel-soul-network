# Chapter 934 — The Monday Of The Fifteenth Week, A Bench Until Two And A Counter Until About Six Before It, And Then One Question Put To A Man Of Twenty-Two In Front Of About Nine People About Whether He Will Be The Person Who Signs, And A Refusal That Came Back Inside About Four Seconds And Was About Him And Not About The Nine, And Forty Pounds For Four Jobs

**One question put to him in front of about nine people was all of that Monday, apart from a bench until two and a counter until about six, and the answer came back inside a few seconds and nobody in that room asked him for another one.**

---

Ten callers came into that shop on that Monday between about seven and about half past nine, and the shutter came down at about ten.

**Those four converted shop units had been standing there off that service road one thousand seven hundred and twenty-nine days by that Monday, and that is two hundred and forty-seven weeks to the day. That sixteenth ruled line on the board up on two nails had never been touched and had stood blank for one thousand five hundred and nineteen days, and that is two hundred and seventeen weeks to the day. Nine written copies of the front of one page, each of them with a corner torn off, had been written out one thousand two hundred and ninety-five days before that Monday, and that is one hundred and eighty-five weeks to the day.**

---

**A man of about fifty-two who keeps a register put the question to him at about half past seven on that Monday, in that room, with about nine people standing there, and he asked it once and did not ask it twice.**

"Will you be the one who signs it?"

The man of twenty-two did not wait.

"I am not the one whose name goes there."

**About four seconds. Nobody in that room heard a second half to it and nobody in that room has heard one since.**

Nobody in that room argued with it. Nobody in that room softened it. About four people in that room have said since that they heard a man of twenty-two decline to put his own name anywhere near that sheet, and about four people in that room have said since that they would have accepted it if he had said yes.

---

**What he said was about him and not about the nine, and he has not been able to say since whether that was the reason he gave or whether it happened to be the reason and something else was underneath it.**

He has said, to two different people separately, that he does not know. He has said that he was in that room about nine times in four years before that Monday and looked at that table every time, and that he has not seen anybody write in that fifth column, and that whatever the reason was it was already in him before anybody asked him.

Nobody in that room has asked him whether the reason he gave was the reason.

**He is not the person who signs anything in this city and no page of this volume makes him the person who signs anything.**

---

**About nine people were in that room on that Monday, and that fifth heading stood over nothing at all on every one of the nine rows, and it was still standing over nothing at all when that shutter came down at about ten.**

It has not been said in that room that the nine rows on that sheet have been dealt with. Nobody in that room has said since that they have not been dealt with either, and this page prints no difference between the two.

Nobody in that room has asked the man of about fifty-two why he asked. Nobody in that room has asked the man of twenty-two anything else about it, then or since.

---

**What was left of that Monday went into about two hours of the same yard and the same building, and four jobs went into it.**

A ring main in a block of flats had come from a board in a cupboard in a corridor, so that any fault anywhere in the building put the whole of it out and there was no way of finding where.

He put a board in at the stair beside the cupboard and proved the ring from that board with the rest of the building live.

"**Twelve pounds,**" he said. "**One board for a whole building is one board for every fault in it. About four of those buildings are run from a cupboard like that, and one of them has been a block where nobody could find a dead circuit for about four years.**"

A light in a stair had been put in on a supply that came through the ring main and came back on itself, so that the fitting was live whenever the stair switch was off.

He took it off that supply and gave it its own and proved the fitting with the switch off.

"**Eleven pounds,**" he said. "**A light supplied off itself is a light that stays live in the dark. About four of those fittings in that stair are supplied that way, and one of them has been a stair nobody would put a ladder in the dark for about four years.**"

A machine in a small workshop had been put on the same way as a welder about four feet away, so that the welder could not run without the machine cutting out.

He put the machine on a supply of its own and ran the welder with it at full load.

"**Nine pounds,**" he said. "**A machine on a welder's supply is a machine that cannot run near a weld. About four such pairs in that workshop sit joined in just that way, and one of them has been two jobs that could not be done in the same week for about four years.**"

A socket over a sink in a canteen had been put in behind a splashback sheet with a screw through a cable in the wall, so that every time that sheet was taken off the socket came with it.

He took that socket out of the sheet, brought it out to the front of it and proved it with the sheet off.

"**Eight pounds,**" he said. "**A socket screwed through a cable behind a splashback is a socket that cannot be reached without cutting it. About four of those sockets in that canteen are like that, and one of them has never been proved since it was fitted for about four years.**"

**Forty pounds is what those four came to on that Monday, exact.**

---

**From about half past six on that Monday the fourth of those four rooms was shut with a woman of about thirty on the far side of it, and nothing came down off the shelf at the back of that room, and this page prints no figure for the page in it.**
