# Chapter 937 — The Thursday Of That Same Week And The Turn Of This Movement, A Bench Until Two And A Counter Until About Six Before It, And Then The One Thing Nine Separate Dates Would Need And Did Not Have Put To The Only Person In That Room Who Could Have Held Nine Of Them Up At Once, And A Refusal In About Four Seconds About Himself And Not About The Nine, Which Nobody In That Room Beat And Nobody In That Room Softened, And Forty-Two Pounds For Four Jobs

**Nine dates would need nine separate people to ask and there were none of them, and that Thursday was a bench until two and a counter until about six before about nine people stood in one room and one of them said no in about four seconds.**

---

That Thursday took nine callers into that shop between about seven and about half past nine, and that shutter came down at about ten.

**That card had stood in that rail for one thousand seven hundred and thirty-six days by that Thursday, and that is two hundred and forty-eight weeks to the day. That twelfth ruled line had been ruled one thousand six hundred and fifty-two days before, and that is two hundred and thirty-six weeks to the day. The thirteenth stood under it, empty, for one thousand six hundred and three days, and that is two hundred and twenty-nine weeks to the day. The fourteenth below that was blank for one thousand five hundred and sixty-eight days, and that is two hundred and twenty-four weeks to the day. The fifteenth further down was blank for one thousand five hundred and forty-seven days, and that is two hundred and twenty-one weeks to the day. The bottom line of that board of nineteen was ruled last, one thousand four hundred and twenty-eight days before that Thursday, and that is two hundred and four weeks to the day. The hold laid across nine crates and the floor under all of them had not shifted in one thousand three hundred and sixty-five days, and that is one hundred and ninety-five weeks to the day.**

**The thing about nine separate dates was said out loud in that room on that Thursday at about half past six, and it was said into the face of the man of twenty-two, and it took no longer than that and it was true.**

"Nine dates would need nine people to ask. There are none."

Nobody in that room spoke for a moment. About four people in that room looked at the man of twenty-two and nobody in that room had said the thing out loud before, and about four of them have said since that they had each been carrying a piece of it for about four years without putting it next to the other pieces.

**And then somebody in that room asked him whether he would be the one to hold the whole of it, and he did not take any time over it.**

"I have carried one for four years. Not nine."

**About four seconds. Nobody in that room heard a longer version of it afterwards and nobody in that room has asked him for one since.**

---

**He said it about himself and nobody in that room has been able to say for certain whether the reason he gave was the reason he had.**

He has said, separately, to two people, that he does not know. He has said that he has been in that room about nine times in four years and looked at that table every time and has not seen one person write one word in that fifth column, and that whatever is in him now was in him before anybody put the question to him.

Nobody in that room has asked him whether that is the reason.

**Nobody in that room beat the refusal and nobody in that room softened it. About four people in that room have said since that they wanted him to say yes and about four have said since that they are glad he did not, and both of those groups have said both of those things.**

Nobody in that room has said what should now be done instead, and nothing that was said in that room on that Thursday has been written into anything.

---

**About nine people were in that room on that Thursday, and one date for all nine rows was the thing on the table, and it was not on a form and nothing was printed about it and nobody in that room had asked anybody for a day.**

Nobody in that room has ever said that the nine rows on that sheet were dealt with. Nobody in that room has said since that they have not been dealt with either, and this page sets down no distance between them.

**And about four people in that room have said since that a man of twenty-two could have been asked nine times instead of once, and that nobody in this city is prepared to do that, and that this is the part that has taken about four years.**

---

**What remained of that Thursday went into the same yard and the same building for about two hours, and four jobs went in there.**

A socket in a hall had been put in on the lighting, so that the light at the top of that hall went out whenever the cleaner used it, which was most evenings.

He put the socket on a supply of its own and proved the hall with the lights off.

"**Twelve pounds,**" he said. "**A socket on the lighting is a socket nobody can use when the light is wanted. About four of those sockets in that hall are on a lighting way, and one of them has been a hall that could not be cleaned after dark for about four years.**"

A consumer unit in a workshop had been rebuilt with two circuits bridged behind it by somebody who was in a hurry, so that the two circuits could not be isolated from each other at all.

He separated them at the board and proved each one on its own.

"**Twelve pounds,**" he said. "**Two circuits bridged behind a board cannot be worked on separately. About four of those boards in that workshop were bridged that way, and one of them has been a workshop where a fault could only be found by cutting everything off for about four years.**"

A light in a stair had been put in with the earth taken from the conduit at the meter end of the run, which had been replaced about four years earlier and had left the original run with nothing on it.

He earthed that fitting at the conduit above it and proved the case.

"**Nine pounds,**" he said. "**An earth taken from the far end of a re-tubed run is not an earth. About four of those fittings in that stair were earthed that way, and one of them has been a light that has never had a test on it for about four years.**"

A socket in a washroom had been put in on the same way as the lighting in that room, so that nothing could be plugged in once the light was on.

He gave it a way of its own and proved it with the lights switched on.

"**Nine pounds,**" he said. "**One circuit for lights and plugs means the room works or it does not. About four of those washrooms have it on one way, and one of them has been a room where a wash cannot be run after six for about four years.**"

**Those four came to forty-two pounds on that Thursday, and that is exact.**

**From about half past six on that Thursday the fourth of those four rooms was shut, with a woman of about thirty behind that door, and the ring binder stood on its shelf at the back and did not come down, and no figure for the page in it is printed here.**
