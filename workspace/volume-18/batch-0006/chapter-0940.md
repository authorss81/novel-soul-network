# Chapter 940 — The Wednesday Of That Last Week, The Sixty-Eighth Sitting, A Bench Until Two And A Counter Until About Six Before It, And Then A Room In Which A Book Was Opened And Stood At Seventy Lines And A Tin Was Not Opened And A Count Was Said Once Into A Face And Nine Dates Went Into A Fifth Column On All Nine Rows With Nothing In The Other Four And An Objection Stood Printed On A Reverse With Nothing Written Against It And A Word Was Said In A Face About Who Is Holding What, And Forty-Two Pounds For Four Jobs

**One date for all nine of them was what that Wednesday carried, and the day began as a bench until two and a counter until about six and ended in a room where a book was opened and a thing was said into somebody's face and nobody said a word back.**

---

Nine callers came through that shop on that Wednesday between about seven and about half past nine, and that shutter came down at about ten.

**That eighteenth ruled line near the foot of that board had been ruled one thousand four hundred and fifty-six days before that Wednesday, and that is two hundred and eight weeks to the day. The gap on that sheet that a date belongs in had been standing there one thousand four hundred and twenty-eight days, and that is two hundred and four weeks to the day. The man of about fifty-one had not moved off that north wall for one thousand three hundred and forty-four days, and that is one hundred and ninety-two weeks to the day.**

---

**The sixty-eighth sitting was held in that room at about half past six on that Wednesday and the count was said once into the face of that room and was not said anywhere else.**

"Seventy-three, of which sixty-eight correspond."

**At about half past seven the woman who holds that room opened that book and put it down flat and it stayed open, and nothing was written in it by anybody afterwards.**

Nobody in that room was given a reason for either of those two things. Nobody in that room asked for one.

---

**And at about ten to eight the fifth heading on that sheet was filled in on all nine of its rows with one date, and nothing at all was written in the other four columns of it on any row.**

The woman of about forty-three wrote all nine of them herself and she took about a minute over the ninth. Nobody in that room helped her and nobody in that room stopped her.

**About four people in that room have said since that they were waiting for somebody to object and that nobody objected, and about four have said since that they were not waiting for anybody to object and that they would have gone home before about eight.**

Nobody in that room has said that the nine rows have been dealt with. Nobody in that room has said that they have not been dealt with either, and this page sets down no difference between them.

---

**On the reverse of that form there is an objection printed in four lines, of which the first three are the same three that stand at the top of the front of it, and the fourth is the reverse's own, and nothing has been written against it and nothing has been written under it.**

**Nobody was asked about this date.**
**This date is not an agreement.**
**No person on this form was asked anything.**
**Nine people cannot be given one day and be nine.**

That last line belongs to the reverse and is not the fourth line that stands on the front of that sheet, which is a different line and says that nothing on the reverse has been answered. It was added after the three above it and before that form was carried out of that room, and nobody in this city has said who added it.

Nobody has written anything against it. Nobody in that room has asked whether anything is going to be.

---

**A word was said into a face at about half past eight on that Wednesday, by the man of about fifty-two who keeps a register, to the woman of about forty-three, and it is the last thing said in that room on that day and it is not about the sheet.**

"Nobody is holding it. Nobody has ever held it."

**Nobody in that room said one word back to him about that, and nobody in that room said one word of thanks to him for saying it, and he has said since that he did not say it to be thanked and that he would not have said it if he had been thanked for it.**

Nobody in this city has said who is holding that sheet. Nobody in that room has said it either, and about four people in that room have said since that the answer is a thing they have not been able to put in a sentence for about four years.

**He is not the person who signs anything in this city. She is not the person who approves anything. The date went into that fifth column without either of them and neither of them said a word about it at the time.**

---

**On that Wednesday about nine people were in that room, and the ninth chair stood against that wall with its back to that room and its seat to the paint, and it did not move at any hour of that day, and nobody in that room called it one of the nine.**

Nobody in that room has said where a key went. Nobody in that room has said who emptied a space on a form. Nobody in that room has ever said when the next one of these is, and there is nothing in that room that says.

---

**The rest of that Wednesday went into the same building and the same yard for about two hours, and four jobs went in there.**

A socket in a laundry had been put in on the lighting because there was nothing spare at the time, so that a machine beside it could not be run at night without the light coming on over the whole room.

She took that socket onto a supply of its own and proved it with the lights off.

"**Twelve pounds,**" she said. "**A machine socket on the lighting is a machine that only runs in daylight. About four of those sockets in that room are on a lighting way, and one of them has been a wash that cannot be done after dark for about four years.**"

A floodlight in a yard had been put in with its earth taken off the conduit at a point that had been disturbed by a drain and left with nothing to bite on.

He re-made that earth at the fitting and proved the case from outside.

"**Twelve pounds,**" he said. "**An earth taken off a disturbed run is no earth. About four of those fittings in that yard were earthed that way, and one of them has been a light that could not be proved for about four years.**"

An extractor in a workshop had been put in on a supply that came off a welder's way, so that it stopped every time anybody welded.

She gave it a way of its own and ran it through a cycle with the welder going.

"**Nine pounds,**" she said. "**An extract on a welder's supply stops when the welding stops. About four of those extractors in that workshop are supplied that way, and one of them has been a bench with the fumes standing on it for about four years.**"

A switch in a passage had been put in a plastic enclosure on the wall with the conduit entering it directly, so that the cable was pinched where it went in.

She fitted a proper gland and proved the enclosure with the plate off.

"**Nine pounds,**" she said. "**Conduit straight into a box with no gland pinches the cable. About four of those boxes in that passage were done that way, and one of them has been a switch that has worked loose for about four years.**"

**Forty-two pounds is what those four came to on that Wednesday, exact.**

---

**That Wednesday the fourth of those four rooms was shut by about half past six with a woman of about thirty behind that door, and the binder on the shelf at the back of that room did not come off that shelf, and this page prints no figure for the page inside it.**
