# Chapter 935 — The Tuesday Of That Same Week, A Bench Until Two And A Counter Until About Six Before It, And Then A Printed Form Going Up On A Wall In A Passage About Nine People Pass Every Day Carrying At Its Top The Reason It Exists In Four Short Lines, And About Nine Of Those People Reading It Before They Ever Reach The Room, And Forty-Four Pounds For Four Jobs

**Up by two drawing pins went a sheet of paper on that Tuesday, in a passage about nine people went past every day of the week, and the day under it was a bench until two and a counter until about six, the same as every other day of that week.**

---

Nine callers came into that shop on that Tuesday between about seven and about half past nine, and the shutter came down at about ten.

**That card had been standing in that rail for one thousand seven hundred and thirty-four days by that Tuesday, and that is two hundred and forty-seven weeks and five days. That thirteenth ruled line had been sitting blank under the twelfth for one thousand six hundred and one days, and that is two hundred and twenty-eight weeks and five days. One written line inside that box off that road had been filled in one thousand one hundred and ten days before that Tuesday, and that is one hundred and fifty-eight weeks and four days.**

---

**The reason it exists is printed at the top of that form, in four lines, and about nine people read it in that passage before they ever reached the room they were going to.**

**Nobody was asked about this date.**
**This date is not an agreement.**
**No person on this form was asked anything.**
**Nothing on the reverse has been answered.**

**About nine people pass that passage every day and about nine of them read those four lines before they get to the room, and about four of those nine have read them twice, and not one of them has said so to anybody in that room.**

Nobody in this city has said who wrote those four lines. About four people in that room have said since that they would have signed them and nobody has asked them to sign anything.

---

**About nine people were in that room on that Tuesday, and the fifth heading on the sheet on the table stood over nothing on all nine of its rows, and it stood over nothing on all nine of its rows when that shutter came down at about ten.**

Nobody in that room has said, on any day, that the nine rows on that sheet have been dealt with. Nobody in that room has said since that they have not been dealt with either, and this page puts down nothing between them.

Nobody in that room has asked who reads the four lines in that passage. Nobody in that room has asked what the fourth of those four lines means.

---

**The man of twenty-two was not in that room on that Tuesday and nobody in that room knew that he had read the four lines in the passage on his way to work, and about four people in that room have said since that they would have asked him what he made of them.**

He has said since that he read them standing up and has not been able to say anything about them since, and that he has not been able to say whether he would have read them twice.

Nobody in this city has asked him to say more about that.

---

**What was left of that Tuesday went into about two hours of the same yard and the same building, and four jobs went into it.**

A distribution board in a laundry had come off the lighting because there was nothing spare at the time it went in, so that the press and the lights had been on one way for years and a fuse in the lighting had taken the press out of a room full of water twice.

He put the press on a way of its own and proved it with the lights off and on.

"**Fourteen pounds,**" he said. "**A press on the lighting is a press that a light can switch off. About four of those presses in that laundry are on a lighting way, and one of them has been a floor of standing water every time somebody upstairs put a light on for about four years.**"

A socket over a bench in a workshop had been put in on a way that was already at its limit with four machines on it, so that the whole bench went out whenever one of those machines was started.

He took that socket onto a spare and proved the bench with all four machines running.

"**Twelve pounds,**" he said. "**A socket added to a full board is a socket that belongs to whichever machine starts first. About four of those benches in that workshop are over their board, and one of them has been a bench where nothing can be plugged in and worked at once for about four years.**"

A floodlight over a car park had been put in with an earth taken off the conduit at a point two joints back, where the conduit had been painted over three times and had no continuity to the fitting at all.

He earthed that fitting at the conduit immediately above it and proved the case with a meter.

"**Nine pounds,**" he said. "**An earth taken two joints back through three coats of paint is no earth. About four of those fittings in that car park were done that way, and one of them has been a light that could not be proved for about four years.**"

A floodlight in a side passage had been put in on a supply that came off a ring serving four other outside lights, so that the passage went out every time any of them was switched on.

He gave it a spur and proved all five together.

"**Nine pounds,**" he said. "**Five outside lights on one ring is five lights that go out as a lot. About four of those groups in that yard are ringed like that, and one of them has been a passage that has been dark whenever the yard was lit for about four years.**"

**Forty-four pounds is what those four came to on that Tuesday, exact.**

---

**The door of the fourth of those four rooms was shut from about half past six on that Tuesday with a woman of about thirty behind it, and the binder on the shelf at the back of that room came off no shelf, and nothing in this line is a figure for it.**
