# Chapter 887 — A Tuesday Of The Second Week, A Counter Until About Six And Then Four Minutes Of A Corridor Near Civic Spine In Which A Woman Of About Twenty-Four Asked A Man Of Twenty-Two One Question And Got An Answer That Was About The Question, And Twenty-Six Pounds For Four Jobs

**A Tuesday was a bench until two, and after that a counter until about six, and four minutes of it at about ten to nine was a corridor near Civic Spine with a door at one end of it and a door at the other. One question was asked in that corridor and one answer came back and neither of the two of them said another word that night.**

---

Nine names and as many dates were entered on that Tuesday, the ninth of them at about twenty to five. The shutter came down about ten minutes after that.

**The four converted units off that service road stand at one thousand six hundred and thirty-nine days, two hundred and thirty-four weeks and one day. The fifteenth of the nineteen ruled lines is one thousand four hundred and fifty-four days, two hundred and seven weeks and five days.**

**The eighteenth line is one thousand three hundred and fifty-seven days, one hundred and ninety-three weeks and six days, and what that open page is still owed comes to one thousand three hundred and twenty-nine days, one hundred and eighty-nine weeks and six days. That man himself is one thousand two hundred and forty-five days in that spot, one hundred and seventy-seven weeks and six days. A hold has been sitting over those nine crates for one thousand two hundred and seventy-two days, one hundred and eighty-one weeks and five days.**

---

**She had a file under her arm and she did not open it in the corridor, and about nine metres of that corridor is the whole distance between their two doors and there is a light over the middle of it that has been out for about four months.**

She said: will you be the one who ends the nine.

He said: I do not know.

She said: that is about the question and not about us.

He said: yes.

**That is the whole of what the two of them said to each other in that corridor on that Tuesday and neither of them said another word that night and about four people in this city have said since that it was about four minutes long.**

---

**And then she opened the file, in the corridor, with her back against the wall by her own door, and it was a file on him.**

It was about a decision he had made in a year neither of them put a number to, and she had read it in about four minutes and she had three questions in it, and she asked none of them, and she told him she had them.

She said that she had opened it because the terms she works on had reached him in about four years and nobody in this city had ever used them on him, and that this was her using them, and that he was not going to be told what was in it tonight.

**He asked one question, which was whether she had opened anything else, and she said yes, and did not say about what, and he did not ask again.**

---

**Nobody in this city has asked either of them about that corridor and about four people who have been in one of those two buildings have said since that they heard two doors.**

He has said since that she did not use the office to tell him to stop and that he had been ready for her to, and that the not using it was the part he could not get round.

She has said since that she had not used it to tell him to stop because that is not what the office is for, and that an ombud who tells a person what to do is a person who has stopped being an ombud, and that she read that sentence off her own desk in her own writing and has not taken it down.

**Neither of them said anything about that exchange to anybody and it has not been repeated to either of them since.**

---

**He was at a counter at about half past two on that Tuesday and a woman of about thirty-eight asked him whether he had decided anything about a sheet of paper and he said that he had not decided anything.**

She said: you are the third this week to tell me.

He said: I have noticed.

**Nobody in that shop laughed and nobody in that shop has mentioned it since, and he has thought about that sentence perhaps four times.**

---

**The remainder of that Tuesday was about two hours in a fourth district, and four jobs went into it.**

A smoke detector head in the same building had its base twisted off and lying on the shelf beside it, so that the head was not held by anything.

He put the head back on its base and proved it with a tester.

"**Nine pounds,**" he said. "**A detector head off its base is a detector head on a shelf. About four of those heads in that building are off their bases, and one of them has been a ceiling that has been reported as bare for about four years.**"

A pull cord in the same place had its bell mounted outside the room the cord was in, so that anybody in the room pulled a handle in the next room.

He took the bell off the next wall, put it inside the room, and proved it.

"**Eight pounds,**" he said. "**A bell outside the room is a bell the next room answers. About four of those fittings in that building are put in the wrong room, and one of them has been a bell that goes off at about four in the morning for about four years.**"

A bathroom panel light in the same yard had been rewired with a switched live instead of a switched neutral, so that the fitting held a charge with the switch off.

He took it back out, put the switch on the neutral, and proved it.

"**Five pounds,**" he said. "**A switch on the live is a fitting that is on when it is off. About four of those panels in that building are done that way, and one of them has been a complaint about a light that would not go off for about four years.**"

A socket face in a corridor of the same building had been cracked from one corner inward and had been left in place because it still worked on about half of its contacts.

He cut it off, took the box out far enough, and refitted a sound face.

"**Four pounds,**" he said. "**A cracked face left in place is a face working on half its contacts. About four of those faces in that building are cracked, and one of them has been a socket that works and does not for about four years.**"

**Twenty-six pounds is what those four came to on that Tuesday, exact.**
