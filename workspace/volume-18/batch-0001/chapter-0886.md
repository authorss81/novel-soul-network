# Chapter 886 — A Monday Of The Second Week, A Bench Until Two And A Counter Until About Six, A Yard In A Fourth District Where A Man Of About Fifty-Seven Put His Own Two Hands On A Bench And They Were Looked At For The First Time In About Nine Years, And Sixty-One Pounds For Four Jobs

**A Monday was a bench until two, and then a counter until about six, and half an hour of it went into a yard in a fourth district with a man of about fifty-seven and about four tools out of a bag. He had come about the last of his own grip and nothing else, and what was found was ordinary, and it is not a finding.**

---

That Monday's book took ten names and ten dates, and the last of the ten was taken at about twenty to five. The shutter came down at about ten.

**Those four converted units off that service road have stood for one thousand six hundred and thirty-eight days, which is two hundred and thirty-four weeks to the day. The card creased once in the rail by that first door is one thousand six hundred and forty-two days, two hundred and thirty-four weeks and four days, and the rail takes two.**

**The sixteenth of the nineteen ruled lines is one thousand four hundred and twenty-eight days, two hundred and four weeks to the day. The twelfth is one thousand five hundred and fifty-eight days, two hundred and twenty-two weeks and four days. The front of one page has been written out by hand into nine hands for one thousand two hundred and four days, one hundred and seventy-two weeks to the day, and no two of the nine has ever been set one against another.**

---

**He came at about half past five with a hold-up lamp and a bag and he had told the man of about thirty-three on the Thursday that he would, and he had not told anybody else, including the woman who walked to the hall with him.**

He put his two hands flat on the bench at about half past five without being asked and he put the lamp on them.

A grip has gone in two fingers of one hand and the fourth finger on that same hand has curled about half as far as the one beside it, and both of them have been like that for about four years and he has worked round them every day of the four years.

**Nobody in that yard had looked at them before. He has said since that he had not expected anybody to and that he had brought the lamp for his own sake so he could see the end of them.**

---

**And what was wrong with it was ordinary and is not a finding, and this page says so in those words twice.**

It is a grip that has gone and not a nerve that has gone, and there is a difference between those two and the difference is about four hours of a morning, and it does not come back by itself, and it comes back by about four things a day done the way he used to do them.

**He asked, in about four seconds, whether anybody was going to tell him what had happened to it, and Marek told him, and it took about nine seconds and it was not a discovery.**

He said: that is not the same as not being able.

**The man of about fifty-seven looked at his own hand when that was said and did not say anything for about nine seconds, and about four people in that yard have said since that he was waiting to be told it was the other one.**

---

**And the second hand was fine, and he was told that too, in about nine seconds, and about four people in that yard have said since that nobody had expected the second hand to be fine and that this is the part that has been in about nine reports.**

He said: I would rather one of them were broken too.

He said that in about four seconds and then he got on with it and did not say anything else about either hand for about half an hour.

**Nobody in that yard said anything back to that and nobody has said anything back to that since.**

---

**He did not go back to the hall that week and nobody asked him to and nobody in this city has asked him why.**

The woman of about forty-three came to that yard on the Thursday of the following week on her own account and watched him do the last two of the four jobs and said nothing at all about either hand.

**She has said since that she was not going to say anything about them and that she had worked out by about the second minute of standing there that this was not a thing to say anything about.**

---

**The rest of that Monday was about two hours in a fourth district and four jobs in it.**

A motor starter in the same yard had an overload reset pushed in and latched, so that when it had tripped nobody could tell whether it had tripped or whether somebody had put it back.

He unlatched it, proved it, and set it back where the man wanted it.

"**Twenty-six pounds,**" he said. "**A reset that has been latched is a reset that tells you nothing. About four of those starters in that yard have been latched, and one of them has been a motor that has been assumed safe for about four years.**"

A bench light in the same building had a flex coming out of a clamp and trapped under it where the clamp bit onto the outer sheath only.

He freed the flex, made a proper gland, and proved it.

"**Twelve pounds,**" he said. "**A clamp on the sheath is a clamp on nothing. About four of those lamps in that building are clamped that way, and one of them has been a lamp that comes on when the bench moves for about four years.**"

A wall plug in the same place had a face that had been cracked from the corner and had gone warm to the hand.

He cut the plug off, found a good one back in the box, and proved the circuit.

"**Nine pounds,**" he said. "**A cracked face goes warm before it goes anything else. About four of those plugs in that building have cracked faces, and one of them has been a plug that smelt of hot for about four years.**"

A sink trap in the same yard had a compression joint that had been done up by hand and was weeping into the cabinet under it.

He cut it out, made it up with the tool, and proved it.

"**Fourteen pounds,**" he said. "**A joint done up by hand is a joint that is not done up. About four of those traps in that building were done by hand, and one of them has been a cabinet with a wet bottom for about four years.**"

**Sixty-one pounds is what those four came to on that Monday, exact.**

**The fourth of those four rooms was shut and a woman of about thirty was behind it by the time it was half past six, and the binder on the back shelf of that room has not come out, and this page does not say for how long.**
