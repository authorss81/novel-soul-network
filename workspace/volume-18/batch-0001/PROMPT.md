## 2. The movement, and what it is about

**A sheet with about nine rows on it has been on a table in a first floor above a line in Saltmarket since before the spring. It has four columns and a fifth one headed *review*, and nothing has ever been in the fifth column on any of the nine rows. The fourth column holds one word on every row and the word is *load*, and it has been doing two jobs, and nobody has ever asked which of the two it was doing on any given day.**

**Movement I does not resolve that and does not find out. It finds out that the fourth column has a history and that the woman who wrote the form did not know there were two kinds of thing to write in it, and it puts about nine rows in front of a room that has to say something about them, and the something it says is that the review is due and cannot be performed.** The movement closes on a Friday with a man of twenty-two who has refused in about four seconds to be the person who signs a review of nine rows he is not allowed to ask about, and who afterwards cannot say whether the four seconds were the reason.

**No sitting is held on any of the ten days and no number is said in any room in this city on any of them, and the ninth chair does not move, and the binder does not come out, and the place behind the woman's chair is named on one file of the ten and on no other, and nobody thanks anybody.**
