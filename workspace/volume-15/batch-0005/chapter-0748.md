# Chapter 748 — A Monday, A Bench Until Two And Four Rooms Off That Service Road From About Six, A Room Under A Building In A First District, Nine Seconds Said To His Face By A Woman Of Fifty-Three Which Was A Good Bargain And Worked, A Bay On A Road In A Second District Said Out Loud In That Room By A Man Of Twenty-Two Who Had Not Asked Permission To Say It, And Four Times He Asked A Woman Of Forty-Five One Question Over And Over

**A Monday was a bench until two and four rooms off that service road from about six. This one was a stair going down under a building, nine seconds said to his face in the room at the bottom of it, a thing said out loud that he cannot unsay, and a man of twenty-two asking a woman of forty-five one question four times over while the room did what it was doing.**

---

The counter shut at half past six on that Monday and the sheet had twelve names and twelve dates on it, the last of the twelve taken at about twenty to five, and the cage in the round-the-side had gone about nine times in the fortnight.

**That card in the rail is at one thousand three hundred and seventy-six days, one hundred and ninety-six weeks and four days, and the hardboard's eighteenth line is at one thousand and ninety days, one hundred and fifty-five weeks and five days. Those are the two figures the Monday rests on and there is nothing under either of them.**

The separation is at seven hundred and fifty-two days, one hundred and seven weeks and three days, in a one-line box about sixteen months old, and the box is a box and the number in it is a subtraction and neither of those is what happened tonight.

---

**She was already in the room when they got there and she was not standing up and she did not stand up.**

Iona Sorn is fifty-three. She has a coat over the back of one of the chairs and a piece of paper in her hand with about nine words on it that she did not put down.

Sera Quill went in first. Marek went in second. **Nobody else came down that stair and the man of about sixty who keeps that building was at the top of it with a cup in his hand and did not come down and has not come down since.**

**There is a tie on a hook at the end of that table and the hook has a board across it and nothing is fastened and nothing has been fastened, and there is no reason given anywhere in this city for that.**

---

**She said the whole of it in about nine seconds and she said it to his face and he did not get a word in.**

"**Take it. Every release comes to you; nothing waits, nothing is refused without you. It works — I did it and people lived. You would be the only one who can stop anything.**"

About nine seconds. About thirty-two words. She said it the way a woman reads out a delivery that is in the van.

Marek stood there.

"**It holds through the whole of a failure,**" she said, "**which is about nine minutes, and there is nothing behind it and nothing queued behind that, and after that the ordinary work starts again and it is ordinary and it is yours and it is nobody else's.**"

"**That is a good bargain,**" she said. "**I have never offered it to anybody who was not going to be able to refuse it. I am offering it to you because you can refuse it and you have spent a year making sure you could.**"

---

**It was a good one and he has not been able to find the flaw in it since, and he has gone over it on about nine occasions, and there is not one.**

It stops a brace that will not come off the first attempt. It stops a pump that will not balance and is done by hand. It takes about nine minutes off a bus door that is going to take eleven, and then the ordinary work starts. It is faster than anything else in this city and it is not fast in a way that hurts anybody, and about four hundred people have said in the last four months that they would give a great deal for somebody to decide.

**The only thing wrong with it is the shape of it, and the shape of it is the reason it is good, and he has written that sentence down once in a book of his own and has not looked at it since.**

---

He did not say no. He did not say yes. He did not argue with her about a word of it, and she has said since that she noticed and that it is the reason she went on talking to him.

**What he did was say a bay.**

"**The second bay of a garage on a road in a second district,**" he said, "**goes out on a Friday at about four with nothing on it. It has done since before the spring. A woman of about thirty-six works that release and has for about nine years and she is not on anything and nobody has asked her for anything and she is not in this room and she does not know that I am standing in it.**"

Iona Sorn did not move.

"**She is not the only one and I am not saying she is,**" Marek said. "**I am saying that this room has just been asked to believe that one person decides, and about four hundred yards from here a door has been coming out on a Friday on its own for four months, and nobody designed that, and nobody in this city is going to notice it if you take the thing, because a thing that is already happening is not a decision anybody made.**"

**That is what the exit is. Not a threat and not an argument. It is that a room where one person decides cannot tell the difference between a decision and a Tuesday.**

---

**And the price of saying it was that he had used a woman's working shift as a fact in a room, without her, about her, and had not asked her, and could not now, and had said it in front of five people.**

He has spent eleven years refusing to let a person be a route to something. **He has said a bay out loud to a stranger to make his own case and it is the same act with the coat off, and there is no column anywhere for it and he has not got anybody to be complained to.**

He did not take the bargain. The bargain was still on the table in the room at ten past eight and at nine and at half past nine, and it was still good at half past nine, and it is still good.

---

**Then she did the thing she had been in that room to do, and the room came down about four inches.**

Everything in the building that could be held in one line went into one line. Not a wall of it and not a roar. **A pump in a second district that was being balanced by a man and a woman with their hands went to one setting instead of two, and a brace on a floor in a first district that two people were holding in place stopped being held, and about four things within a second of each other and none of them big enough to see from where Marek was standing.**

"**Right,**" Iona Sorn said, and she was not enjoying it, and her hand was not moving.

---

**He asked Sera Quill four times, and the ask was nine words, and it was the same nine words four times.**

"**Sera. Stop the share. I cannot hold it and you can.**"

She said no the first time, in about nine seconds, and the reason was about the work.

"**No. There are about four hundred people in it and about nine of them are the reason it is going well, and I do not know which nine and I am not going to find out in the next four minutes.**"

He asked her again at about ten past eight.

"**No. If it stops now then a pump in a second district does not balance until somebody has walked to it, and that is two hours on a bus and the middle one of that building's three shifts has nobody spare in it.**"

He asked her a third time at about a quarter to nine and she said no in about nine seconds again and then said one more thing, which was not part of the answer.

"**No. And if I say it a fourth time it will not be a reason. It will be a habit. And a habit in a room is a rule, and I have watched a rule come in through a door this year and take about four months.**"

---

**The four of them did the thing then, out loud, in words, and it took about nine seconds to agree and it took the rest of the night to hold.**

Four things and a word. You say the word and he stops. He says the word and you stop. **It is said out loud before it is wanted, and it is said by both, and the saying is the whole of it and there is nothing in it that either of them cannot stop.**

They said it. Then they said it again. Then the room came back about a third of the way, and then most of the way, and the brace was held and the pump was balanced by two sets of hands and the bus door came in under a minute because the man at it was told, out loud, by a woman, what he was allowed to do about it.

**Nobody in that room wrote it down and nobody has written it down since, and the reason is not secrecy. The reason is that the only copy of it is four sentences said out loud by two people in a room with a hole in the floor, and neither of them can produce it, and neither of them would know how to hand it to anybody else in a form that would work.**

---

**At about twenty past nine Sera Quill said the word.**

That is all she did. She did not announce it and she did not stand up and nobody in that room thanked her and nobody has since and she has not mentioned it and will not.

**The room came apart along about nine separate lines at once and held. It is not a story about how it came apart. It came apart in a second and then everything in it was somebody's own again, and about eleven people in a second district found out at about nine o'clock that the thing in front of them had been decided for them and was now not.**

---

**He wanted the woman of about thirty-six to have been in that room, and she was not, and he had to use her from a bay on a road in a second district, and that was the only card he had in his hand.**

He would rather have had the room than the bay. He has not worked out why and he is not going to be told.

---

**Four rooms off that service road and one behind the other three, and a woman of about thirty in the fourth of them from about half past six, sitting with the shut door of that room behind her chair and that chair where it has been all year.**

Her page is the eighth of eight and has been in a ring binder on a back shelf for a hundred and sixty-one days. It did not come out on that Monday, no question was asked about it, and none is going to be.

---

He said none of it.
Twelve dated jobs went out and twelve came in, the shutter came down at about ten, and a woman of fifty-three offered a man of twenty-two the best thing anybody in this city has offered anybody in four months and he did not take it, and a bay on a road in a second district came out of his mouth in a room in front of five people, and a woman of forty-five stopped a share with one word, and about eleven people found out about it at about nine o'clock and none of them will ever know who stopped it.
