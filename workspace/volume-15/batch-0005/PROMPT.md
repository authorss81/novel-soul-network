1. **Chapter 748, where she makes him the bargain.** `outline/ending.md` line 27 is the authority and it is a good bargain and it works inside nine minutes and taking it would end the crisis in a way that costs the whole manuscript its argument. **The trap on one side is a villain's monologue and the trap on the other is a man who is right in a speech. Neither is available. It is nine seconds said by her to his face, it is good, he does not take it, he does not argue with it, and he makes the exit visible by doing something that costs him and that he cannot take back.**
2. **Chapter 747, the one Sunday.** A day off is the easiest filler in this manuscript and the only file in seventy entitled to the about-two shutter. **The risk is that a day off has nothing in it. It has three things: a room in a first district that opens at six and closes at ten and is shut, a woman of about thirty behind a door that nobody opened, and about nine people who find out on the Monday that a thing they had planned for the Sunday did not happen.**
3. **Chapter 749, the chisel.** The whole ending is one authorisation bought across eleven volumes of refusing to be the middle. **The trap is catharsis. It is about nine minutes of physical work, a plate that has not moved since a mapping lapsed on a sheet with three ruled lines and a signature and eleven days on it, a paper that says he is allowed to do it, and a cost that is the last clear trace of his father's practice — and the memory is not erased and the accessible trace is gone, which are two different sentences and both of them are required.**
