# Chapter 746 — A Friday, A Counter Until About Six, A Sheet Of Three Headings Brought To A Two-Man Print Shop On A Road Off Saltmarket By A Man Who Runs A Market And Who Had Been Refused On Thursday, A Going Rate Written At The Bottom Of It That The Copy Did Not Have, And A Man Of About Fifty-Eight Who Took The Money And Did Not Ask And Has Not Asked

**A Friday was a counter until about six. This one was a sheet coming off a press on a road off Saltmarket at about half past four with three headings on it and a going rate at the bottom that nobody had put there, and the man who set it was not told who wanted it and did not ask and has not asked anybody since.**

---

Eleven dated jobs and eleven names on that Friday, the last name of the eleven at about twenty to five, and the cage in the round-the-side went about nine times in the fortnight and the shutter came down at about ten.

**Four rooms off that service road and one behind them stand at one thousand three hundred and sixty-nine days, one hundred and ninety-five weeks and four days, and the hardboard's fourteenth line is at one thousand two hundred and five days, one hundred and seventy-two weeks and one day. Those two are the figures this Friday rests on and there is nothing under either of them.**

The separation is at seven hundred and forty-nine days, one hundred and seven weeks to the day, in a one-line box about sixteen months old.

---

**The man of about thirty-eight who runs a market in a first district had the sheet in his inside pocket where he put it on Thursday and he took it out on the Friday at about half past three and it had not been folded any smaller than it was when it went in, which is a thing about a man and not about a sheet.**

Three headings. What the market can safely do. What it will not share. Who can stop the market's part of it.

The copy he had was made on a machine in the second district. **On the copy, the third heading had lost a word, and what it said was *who can stop it*, and what he wrote on Thursday and what the sheet on a table in a third district says is *who can stop our part of it*.**

---

**The man of about fifty-eight asked one question and it was the only question he asked that afternoon and he asks about one thing in every ten sheets.**

"**Wall or pocket.**"

"**Both.**"

"**Then it's two sheets,**" he said, "**and they are not the same sheet, and the one on the wall wants the weight and the one in a pocket wants the fold, and if I do both off one setting the wall one goes soft by about a month.**"

He did about four hundred of one and about four hundred of the other. He used what he had, which was a heavier stock than the copy, and the wall sheets came off at about four and the pocket ones at about half past four.

**He put the going rate at the bottom of both, which the copy did not have, and nobody in this city has ever had a going rate printed at the bottom of a sheet before and nobody has complained about it.**

---

**He cut a word and he asked before he cut it, which is the second question and it took four seconds.**

"**I can put your part in,**" he said, "**or I can leave it out, and if I leave it out then it is what you brought me and I have not touched it, and if I put it in then it is what the other one says and I have never seen the other one.**"

"**Put it in.**"

"**That'll be four pounds more.**"

"**Put it in,**" said the man of about thirty-eight, and paid it, and was not happy about it, and has not been happy about it and has not gone back.

---

**The invoice has the quantity written in it and the price left blank, and the price is written in underneath in a different hand.**

He did that himself and the man saw him do it and said nothing about it. **The man of about fifty-eight has never asked who wanted a sheet, has never been told, and is not going to be, and there is a woman of fifty-three in this city and he has never heard of her and she has never heard of his shop.**

A plate came in there once, before the spring, and he set about nine hundred leaflets and did not set the last one, and he has told one person about that and it is the only thing he has ever told anybody, and he is not a bad man about it and he is not a good man about it and nobody in this city has asked him which he is.

---

**The rest of that Friday was a shop in Lattice Ward and about nine hundred leaflets, which he did not print and which are not on anything.**

A woman of about thirty-four came in with a bag of them. **She has had them since the spring. They came out of a post box at a firm in a first district, carried by a man of about thirty-one who is not from this city and is not named, and they have the same four steps on them as everything else has the same four steps on, and a paragraph under them that is eight words longer than the one they replaced.**

She wanted to know whether they were still doing it.

"**I have not looked,**" Marek said. "**I have not read one of those since the spring. I do not know who printed them and I do not think anybody does.**"

"**They came in a bag,**" she said. "**I have had them in a bag since the spring and I have moved house twice.**"

"**Then they're yours,**" Marek said, and charged her about nine pounds to look at the boiler, and did not look at the leaflets, and did not throw them out, and did not tell her they were rubbish, and she took them away in the bag.

**Nobody in this city has counted how many of those there are in how many bags in how many houses, and Marek has been asked about them exactly twice in four months and has said the same thing both times and is not going to say anything else.**

---

**One thing he noticed and there is no form for it: the going rate is written on the wall of that print shop in pencil, and it is a different number from the one he charged on the Friday.**

The pencil number is higher by about four pounds. **He said the wall is out of date and that the going rate is what he charges and not what is on the wall, and he did not say when the wall was last right, and nobody asked him, and that pencil number has been on that wall since before the spring.**

**There is also a date on the wall in the same pencil, older than the number, and the date is when he went into that shop, and nobody has ever asked a man why he would put the day he started on the wall where the prices are, and he has never said, and it is not on the invoice.**

---

**He wanted the going rate to have been higher and for the shop's money to have covered it. It was not higher and the shop's money did not cover it, and about six pounds of it came out of his own and it is not on the sheet and it will not be.**

---

**Four rooms off that service road and one behind the other three, and a woman of about thirty in the fourth of them, in the chair, with the door of that room where the door is, and it was shut from about half past six and stayed shut.**

Her page is the eighth of eight and has been in a ring binder on a back shelf for a hundred and fifty-eight days, and it did not come out on that Friday, and there is nobody in this city who has the standing to take it out.

---

**The last of that Friday was a woman of about twenty-six who wanted a card printed for a wedding in the second week of next month and had brought her own wording and would not have it changed.**

He read it, and it was better than what most people bring him, and he said so, and she said her mother had written it, and then stood there.

"**Do you want to know what I would change,**" he said.

"**No,**" she said.

"**I would change one word and I am not going to tell you which,**" Marek said, and printed it as it was, and charged about four pounds, and she took it away in a bag and did not come back and did not need to.

**He has never found out whether the word she left in was the right one and he is not going to, and there is no part of any form on which that would sit.**

---

He said none of it.
Eleven dated jobs went out and eleven came in, the shutter came down at about ten, and about eight hundred sheets with three headings on them and a going rate at the foot are in a market in a first district and in about four pockets, and a bag of about nine hundred leaflets went out of a shop in Lattice Ward in a bag it came in, and nobody in this city has read all of the eight hundred and nobody has written down what a room is for.
