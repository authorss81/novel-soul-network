# Chapter 744 — A Tuesday, A Bench Until Two And A Counter Until About Six, A Two-Man Print Shop On A Road Off Saltmarket Where Four Hundred Of Something Were Printed For Four Pounds, A Room In A Fourth District Where A Card With Two Lines Of Type On It Went Up Over A Pale Rectangle It Had Made There Before, And Nobody In That Room Has To Obey It

**A Tuesday was a bench until two and a counter until about six. This one went to a two-man print shop on a road off Saltmarket at about half past three and then to a room in a fourth district, where a card with two lines of type on it went up on a wall at about five and covered all but two edges of a mark that was already there.**

---

Eleven jobs went on the Tuesday's sheet with eleven names against them, the last of the eleven at about twenty to five. The cage in the round-the-side had gone about nine times in the fortnight and went again at about ten when the shutter came down.

**Line twelve on that board of two nails is one thousand two hundred and eighty-six days, one hundred and eighty-three weeks and five days, and the separation is at seven hundred and forty-six days, one hundred and six weeks and four days. There is no figure on this Tuesday that comes out on a whole number of weeks and there is not one in this page to divide.**

---

**The card is the size of a playing card's smaller half and it is card, not paper, and it has two lines of type on it and nothing else on it.**

The first line says: *Not answering is not a refusal.*

The second line says: *Say it in the room if you mean it.*

**That is the whole of it and it is a rule about who is quiet, and it has been set by a man who is not bound by it, in a shop, for the going rate, and the money has been taken.**

---

**The man of about fifty-eight set it himself and it is the fourth thing this season that he has set himself and he has told nobody that there is anything to be a season about.**

"**Same weight as the leaflets,**" he said. "**I had a box of it. It was the right weight for a wall and wrong for a hand, so I did the hand one and did not do the hand one.**"

"**How much.**"

"**Four pounds,**" he said, "**and that is the going rate and I have not looked at whether it is the going rate.**"

**He took the money. He has not been thanked and has not asked and is not going to be, and he did not ask who wanted the four hundred and Marek did not tell him and was never going to.**

---

**He set the two lines and he set them twice, because the first setting had the second line a half-line too high and he would not print a thing a half-line wrong.**

The first setting went in the press at about four and came off at about ten past four and he looked at it on the bench for about nine seconds and put the whole of the four hundred back in the box, and did not say anything to anybody, and the second setting went in at about half past four and came off at about ten past five, which is about forty minutes and is longer than the first one, and it is longer because he read both lines twice before he locked the forme up, and nobody was waiting for them.

**He charged the going rate once, for four hundred, and the reprint was about nine pounds of his own and he has not mentioned it to anybody and it will not appear on an invoice and did not appear on the one.**

**There is a line of set type in a composing stick on that bench, upside down, facing the door where a stranger can see it, and it is the only line in that shop that is not the right way up and it has been there since the spring.**

He was asked about it, once, by a man who came for a receipt, and he said it was a line he had set wrong and was keeping to remind himself, and it is not that, and Marek has thought about which of the two it is about four times.

---

**The room is on a first floor in a fourth district and it is open from six until nine and about nine people come and go in it, and on that Tuesday the card went up at about five before any of them was in.**

It went up over a rectangle of wall about the size of the card and a little larger, and the wall inside the rectangle is paler than the wall around it, and the paint on the rim is not cracked and is not dirty and has been rubbed back by about nine hands moving a thing up and down in the same place.

**The new card covered all of that except about a finger's width at the top edge and about the same at the left, and everybody who came in that evening could see where it had been before, and not one of them said anything about it, and it is the mark the card makes in a room where nobody has to obey it.**

---

**A man of about forty-one in that room read it standing up and said it was not his to be in charge of and that it was a nice bit of type and that whoever set it could set a good deal more of it for the money.**

"**It's not mine,**" Marek said.

"**I didn't ask,**" said the man of about forty-one, and went and sat down.

**Four of the other people in that room did not read the card at all. They came at about half past six, at about seven and at about eight, and they were waiting for something that was not happening on that Tuesday, and the card is about the size of half a playing card and it is at head height by the door and about four of them walked past it four times.**

---

**The rest of that Tuesday was a shop in Lattice Ward and a job that took two visits and was priced for one.**

A man of about forty-nine wanted a worktop edge trimmed to fit a gap that was not straight, and the gap was not straight because the wall behind it is not straight, and Marek templated it, cut it, brought it back, and found on the second visit that he had templated the wall and not the gap, because the wall is what you put a straight edge against.

**He took the piece back, cut it again out of the same sheet, and put the first piece in his own bin and charged for the second one and not for the first, and told the man about the first piece, and the man said that was all right and it was not all right and he said it twice.**

"**You've done that before,**" said the man of about forty-nine.

"**Once,**" Marek said. "**I have not done it since and I have not worked out how to remember not to.**"

**Nobody thanked him for the second one and he did not expect it and he wrote the first one off against the shop and nobody in this city will ever know that it happened at all.**

---

**The woman of about fifty-two was in that room from about half past six until about nine and she read the card twice and said nothing about it at all, on either time.**

**She had come that evening because she had agreed to come down a stair on the Monday and had been told to bring nothing to read, and she had not asked what was on the wall and nobody told her, and there is nobody in this city entitled to tell her what is on a wall she is standing under.**

She opened her back door at about half five the next morning and at about half five the morning after that and has opened it every weekday since and has not mentioned the card and is not going to.

---

**He wanted the man of about fifty-eight to have charged him more, so that going into that shop would have cost something and he would have had to decide whether to do it.**

Four pounds did not cost him anything. He paid it and it went in a book and he was in and out in about twenty minutes and there was nothing to decide and that is what happened.

---

**Four rooms off that service road and one behind the other three, and in the fourth of them from about half past six a woman of about thirty who came in at the front and went to the back, with the door of that room behind the chair she sits in and that chair where it has always been.**

Her page, the eighth of eight, has been shut in a ring binder on a back shelf for a hundred and fifty-five days. On the Tuesday it stayed where it is, and no question was put to her about it, and none is going to be put to her about it.

---

He said none of it.
Eleven dated jobs went out and eleven came in and the shutter came down at about ten, and four hundred cards with two lines of type on them are now on four walls in four districts, and in one of those rooms you can see where one hung before, and nobody in this city has to do what either line says, and a worktop edge came off a bench in Lattice Ward that had already been templated once against the wrong thing.

---
