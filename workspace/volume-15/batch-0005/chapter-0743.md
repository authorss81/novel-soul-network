# Chapter 743 — A Monday, A Bench Until Two And A Counter Until About Six, Two People Asked To Come Down A Stair In A First District, One Of Whom Said Yes In About Four Seconds And One Of Whom Made Him Say Why In About Nine Seconds Before She Said It, And A Chair With Two People In The Seat Of It And One In The Arms

**A Monday was a bench until two, a counter until about six and four rooms off that service road from about six. This one had two people asked to come down a stair, one of them quick about it and one of whom wanted the reason first and got it, and about nine pounds of his own money went into an evening he did not work.**

---

**Twelve jobs were dated on that Monday and twelve callers came through the counter, the last of the twelve at about twenty to five, and the day did not go the way the book said it would at about half past two and has not gone that way on any other Monday either.**

**What is still owed came to one thousand and fifty-five days, one hundred and fifty weeks and five days, and whatever is in the post at the end of that corridor is at nine hundred and thirteen days, one hundred and thirty weeks and three days. Neither of those two has anything under it and neither of them has had anything under it since before the spring.**

The separation is at seven hundred and forty-five days, one hundred and six weeks and three days, and it is three days longer than it was when she said the thing about a door.

---

**The man of about thirty-four keeps the keys to a building in a fourth district and Marek asked him on the Monday morning outside a shop in a second district, in about four seconds, and the man said yes in about four seconds.**

"**Will it be a long night.**"

"**About an hour,**" Marek said, "**and you can go home at any point in it.**"

"**Right,**" said the man of about thirty-four, and then: "**You should have said the second part first. Nobody ever says the second part first.**"

**He has not been thanked and he was not thanked and he said the second part second.**

---

**The woman of about fifty-two took nine seconds to say yes to and about four of those were Marek's fault.**

She has a key to a back door in a fourth district and she opens it at about half five on a weekday for a man who cannot get into a building before eight, and she is not a member of anything and has never been asked to be and does not want to be.

He said: "**There is a room under a building in a first district and I am going down it in about a fortnight and I would like you to come.**"

"**Whose,**" she said.

That is all she said for a while.

"**Mine,**" Marek said. "**Not the room. Whoever is in it when I get there, and nobody yet.**"

"**How long.**"

"**About an hour. And you can go up at any point and nobody will stop you and nobody will ask you why you went.**"

"**I open a door at about half five,**" she said. "**I have opened that door at about half five for about nine years and there is nobody in this city who could tell me what is under this city, and I have not asked, because asking is a thing you do on your own time and I do not have any.**"

"**Right,**" she said. "**Yes. And bring nothing to read, because I will not read it and I will not pretend I have.**"

**That is about nine seconds and about sixty words and she said none of it twice and it was all of it.**

---

**There is a chair at the top of that stair and it has got two people's weight in the seat of it and one person's in the arms, and the person in the arms is heavier than the two in the seat, and somebody has been through it a great many times in a life that was not long.**

Nobody in this city is responsible for that chair and nobody in this city has a form for it.

---

**The rest of that Monday was a shop in Lattice Ward and a man of about sixty-four who had brought a lamp he did not need.**

He had a table lamp on the counter in a carrier bag and the bag had a second lamp in it, and the first one worked, and he wanted somebody to look at the second one because his wife had said the second one was the better of the two.

**Marek opened the second one and the switch was stiff and there was nothing wrong with it at all, and he put it back in the bag and gave it to him, and charged him for the twenty minutes of looking.**

"**That's fair,**" said the man of about sixty-four. "**I'd have said it was fair if you'd found something.**"

"**You would have,**" Marek said. "**And I would have charged the same.**"

**That is the whole of what happened to that Monday except the bracket in a second district and the two people and the chair, and neither man asked the other anything and the man of about sixty-four has not been back and the twenty pounds is in the shop and nobody is going to query it.**

---

**One thing he noticed and there is no form for it: the arms of that chair at the top of the stair are polished and the seat is not.**

Not dirty the other way round either. The seat has had two people in it and the arms have had one, and the arms have been in use by somebody who came down that stair more often than the two who sat in it, and nobody in this city has ever asked who that is, and there is nobody in this city who could tell him.

**He put his own hand in the left arm on his way down and it was the smoothest thing in that stair, and he took his hand off it and went down, and he has not put it back since.**

---

**About nine pounds of work he had already priced did not get done by him on that Monday, and the difference between what he would have charged and what the man of about twenty-five charged came out of the shop's own money, and it is not on the sheet and it is not going to be on the sheet.**

A call-out in a second district at about half three, a woman of about thirty-nine with a bracket off a door frame that had been done twice and was coming away again. **He was two streets away, asking a woman of about fifty-two to say yes, and when he came back at about twenty past three the work had been done by a man of about twenty-five for about nine pounds less than he would have charged, and she has not said one word about it and has not been told.**

He has written none of it down. **If anybody asks him why the Monday's sheet is light he is going to say it was a light Monday and he is not going to say anything else, and that is a thing he has decided to keep.**

---

**He wanted the woman of about fifty-two to have said yes before he had got to the end of the question, so that he would never have had to find out whether she was the right person to ask.**

She asked. That was better and it took the afternoon off him.

---

**Four rooms off that service road and one behind the other three, all of them lit from about half past six, and a woman of about thirty in the fourth of them who does not turn round when the light in that room comes on, because the door is behind her chair and she knows what the sound of it is.**

Her page is the eighth of eight and has been shut in a ring binder on a back shelf for a hundred and fifty-four days. It did not come out on the Monday and it is not going to come out on the Tuesday, and nobody has asked her one question about it in the whole of this year.

---

**The last of that Monday was a man of about thirty-nine who came in at about ten past five with a bag of tools and asked whether anybody had a spare screw of a size that does not come in any length, and Marek had one, and gave it to him, and would not take money for it.**

"**You will not charge me.**"

"**No.**"

"**Then I will not take it,**" said the man of about thirty-nine, and put it back on the counter, and took it again, and put it back, and left without it, and Marek let him.

"**That is twice now in about a month,**" he said, to nobody, after the door.

**There is no column for a screw and there has never been one and the shop has given away four of them this year and nobody has any way of knowing that except Marek, who has not written it down.**

---

He said none of it.
Twelve dated jobs went out and twelve came in, the shutter came down at about ten, and two people agreed to go down a stair neither of them had been down, one of them inside four seconds and the other inside nine, and about nine pounds of his own money bought an evening he did not work and a bracket in a second district that a man of about twenty-five went in for less and a lamp that was not broken and was charged for anyway.
