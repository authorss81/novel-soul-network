# Chapter 742 — A Friday, A Counter Until About Six And Four Rooms Off That Service Road, A Bus Bay On A Road In A Second District At About Four In The Afternoon Where A Woman Of About Thirty-Six Works A Release And Is Asked For One Thing, And What She Gave Him Was Not The Thing And She Was Not Told What It Was Wanted For

**A Friday was a counter until about six and four rooms off that service road from about six. This one put a man of twenty-two in a bus bay on a road in a second district at about four in the afternoon, asking a woman of about thirty-six for one thing, and she gave him something that is not the thing and was not told what the thing was wanted for.**

---

Eleven callers came to the counter on that Friday and eleven jobs were dated, the last name of the eleven at about twenty to five. The shutter went down at about ten, the cage in the round-the-side went about nine times in the fortnight, and that is the week.

**The man of about fifty-one against the north wall is at nine hundred and sixty-eight days, one hundred and thirty-eight weeks and two days, and the hardboard's fifteenth line is at one thousand one hundred and seventy-seven days, one hundred and sixty-eight weeks and one day. Those are the two figures this Friday rests on and there is nothing under either of them.**

The separation is at seven hundred and forty-two days, one hundred and six weeks to the day, in a one-line box about sixteen months old, and the number went into that box a fortnight before the spring and has not been opened since.

---

**The bay is the second of three in a garage on a road in a second district, and she works the release on it, and the release on it is a lever and a plate and a red flag and nothing else.**

She was about thirty-six and she had been doing it about nine years and she was on her own shift and she was not the only person in the building and nobody had told anybody she was going to be spoken to.

**There is a strip of tape on the column at that bay at about knee height and there has been tape on that column at knee height for years, and nothing has ever been put back up at that height in the whole time Marek has been going about this city.**

---

He asked her for one thing and it took her about four seconds to say yes to it.

The thing was this. **A bus out of the second bay on a day he would name, with nothing on it, about four minutes later than the sheet on the wall says, and nobody told, and it being her own work and not an instruction from anybody.**

"**I could do that,**" she said, "**on a Tuesday. Not a Friday. Fridays that bay is the sheet's.**"

"**Why not a Friday.**"

"**Because on a Friday that door is the sheet and I am not arguing with a piece of paper in front of the others,**" she said, and put her weight on one foot, and did not finish it and did not have to.

Marek said right and wrote Tuesday on the back of his hand and did not tell her why Tuesday and she did not ask why Tuesday.

---

**Then he asked her the question he had come for, which was who else can let a bus out of that bay without asking anybody, and what she gave him was not an answer to it.**

"**Nobody asks me anything,**" she said. "**I am the release. I go out at about half four and I am on this bay till six.**"

And then, without being asked a second time and while she was reaching for the lever to put the bus away:

"**It goes out on a Friday anyway. The second bay. About four. Nothing on it, same as every Friday, and it has done since before the spring, and nobody has ever looked at the sheet to see what it says.**"

"**The sheet says four.**"

"**The sheet says four,**" she said, "**and it has said four since before the spring and the bus has gone out on a Friday at four with nothing on it since before the spring, and that is not anybody letting anybody out. That is a bus.**"

**She said the whole of that in about nine seconds to a stranger and she has not given it a second thought since and she is not going to be asked for a third one and nobody in this city is entitled to ask her for one.**

---

He had come to that bay with a thing he needed. He left it with the fact that the thing he needed has been happening every Friday for about four months to a woman who thought she was going to work.

**He told nobody what it was for. He told her nothing at all, and there is no point in the week on which anybody told her, and the not telling is not a kindness to her and was not done for her.

---

**The rest of that Friday was a shop in Lattice Ward and a fault that was somewhere else than it had been in the morning.**

A man of about forty-three wanted a rail back on a wardrobe and had wanted it back since before the spring, because the far end of it was loose, and by the time the doors were off the rail came away in Marek's hand with about four inches of the carcase still on it, and the plate at the end turned out to be screwed into a stud that somebody had filled in.

**At about half four he told the man it was the fitting and not the rail, and the man said that was what he had been told in the spring and had not believed it then either.**

So it was a rail and a plate and about nine pounds over what he had quoted. The man paid it. It took an hour and a half and the man stayed the whole of it and did not once look at a watch.

"**You said half four,**" he said, at the end. "**It's gone seven.**"

"**It is,**" Marek said.

"**I'm not saying anything,**" said the man of about forty-three. "**I'm telling you that I noticed.**"

**Nobody has ever asked Marek what he does with the hours that are not on a sheet. There is no column for it anywhere and the man of about forty-three noticed, said so, and went home, and there is nothing whatever to be done with it.**

---

**One thing he noticed and there is no form for it: the floor of that second bay is marked.**

Not dirty. There is a rectangle of it, about the length of a bus's nearside door, in a slightly better colour than the rest of the floor, where about nine years of one person standing in one place has kept it clean. **It is not worn. It is clean. Whoever works that bay puts a foot on the same square of floor every working day and the square is better than the floor either side of it because something in that bay is wet about four times a week and somebody mops where it lands.**

---

**He wanted to have been able to leave that bay without turning round at the door, and he turned round, and he has not managed it since, and it is about four seconds each time and there is no reason for it that he has found.**

The fare he paid to get there and back is on nothing. It was twenty-two minutes and a bus and he could have walked and he knows the walk and he took the bus.

---

**Four rooms off that service road and one behind the other three, all of them going from about half past six, and in the fourth of them a woman of about thirty with her back to a shut door and the chair she sits in between her and it.**

The eighth of eight of her pages has been in a ring binder on a back shelf for a hundred and fifty-one days. It stayed in it on that Friday. Nobody has asked her anything about it this year and nobody is going to.

---

He said none of it.
Eleven dated jobs went out and eleven came in, the shutter came down at about ten, and a bus went out of the second bay of a garage on a road in a second district at about four on that Friday with nothing on it, the way it has every Friday since before the spring, and nobody in this city arranged that and nobody in this city has noticed it, and a rail came off a wardrobe in a man's hand with about four inches of the carcase still on it, and the man stayed until seven.
