# Chapter 741 — A Thursday, A Bench Until Two And A Counter Until About Six, A Stair Behind A Door In A First District That Was Not On Any Plan He Had Ever Seen, A Woman Of Forty-Five Beside Him On It And Two People He Had To Ask Before He Went Down At All, And A Room At The Bottom Of It That This Page Does Not Describe

**A Thursday was a bench until two, a counter until about six and four rooms off that service road from about six. This one had a stair going down behind a door in a first district, a woman of forty-five beside him on it, two people he had to ask first, and about an hour and ten minutes at the bottom of it that this page does not describe.**

---

Eleven names and eleven dates went onto that Thursday, the last of the eleven at about twenty to five. The shutter came down at about ten and the cage went about nine times in the fortnight, which is about where it has been sitting for a month now.

**The hardboard's thirteenth line is one thousand two hundred and thirty-two days, one hundred and seventy-six weeks to the day, and the nine hand copies of the front of one page are at nine hundred and twenty-seven days, one hundred and thirty-two weeks and three days. Those are the two figures this Thursday is built on and there is nothing written under either of them.**

The separation is at seven hundred and forty-one days, one hundred and five weeks and six days, in a one-line box about sixteen months old, and it went in there and it stays there.

---

**The stair is behind a door at the back of a building in a first district, and it goes down further than the basement, and it is not on any plan he has ever seen of that building and he has seen four of them.**

It is not locked. It has not been locked since before the spring and nobody in that building carries a key to it and nobody has asked why.

**There is a boot-scraper on the steps at the top of it and there is wet sand in the boot-scraper.** Nobody in this city has explained that stair to anybody and there is no form on which the sand would go.

**He went down it once on his own in the spring, in daylight, with a torch he had borrowed, and got to the bottom and came straight back up, and the door at the top was open behind him when he did it. He has never told anybody he did that and has thought about it about nine times since.**

---

He asked two people on that Thursday and both of them agreed, and he asked them for different reasons and neither of them asked him for one.

The man of about forty-three works a valve in a first district and had been doing it about nine years, and Marek caught him at the end of a shift on the Wednesday and said about nine words and the man of about forty-three said yes before Marek had finished them and then went home.

The woman of about thirty-four keeps a till in a second district and came because she was asked, and she asked one question on the stair, which was whether there was a room with chairs in it, and Marek said he did not know, and she came anyway.

**Sera Quill asked him nothing on the way down. She asked him on the Thursday morning whether he was sure, and he said he was not, and she said right, and they went down.**

---

**He did not describe the room when he came up and he has not described it since, and the reason is not that it is a secret.**

It is that he could not do it. He has tried four times in his own head since and what comes out is a set of measurements and not a room, and a set of measurements is a thing you can take down and a room is not.

What he can say is this. **The ceiling is higher than a room of that kind is.** And: the door at the top of the stair is three inches narrower than the door at the bottom. And: somebody has swept it, and not recently, and the sweep has gone over the same line about nine times, and it has not been the same person.

**He did not see anything in it working and he has not been back with anything to make it work and nobody in this city has been asked to.**

---

**They were down there about an hour and ten minutes and about nine of those minutes were a lock.**

The lock is at the top, on the inside of the door, and it is a new one and it is the wrong kind and it is about eleven pounds fitted by a man who came out from two districts for it, and Marek paid for it out of his own money and put it on no sheet and told nobody in the shop.

**It does not lock. It goes round about two inches and stops. He is going to have it looked at and he is not going to have it looked at this week and he does not know which of those two things he has decided.**

Sera Quill said that was the correct order and did not say it to make him feel better and he did not feel better.

---

**A refusal went out of that room at about four o'clock and it was refused in about four seconds by a man of about sixty who keeps the building and who was on the stair with a cup in his hand.**

Marek asked him whether anybody knew the stair was there.

"**I know,**" the man of about sixty said.

Marek waited.

"**That's the whole of it,**" said the man of about sixty, and went back up, and did not come down again that day and has not come down since.

**He said one more thing from the top step, and he said it to the stairs rather than to anybody, and it took about four seconds and it was this: that the sweep in that room had been done by a woman who worked at the baths and came in on a key, and that she was not in today because it was a Thursday, and that he was not going to be asked about her and had not been asked about her.**

Marek wrote nothing down. He has thought about the Thursday since.

---

**The rest of that day was a shop in Lattice Ward and a woman of about fifty-seven with a shutter that stops four foot off the ground and has stopped there since before the spring.**

It is not the shop. It is the one next door, a hardware place, and the shutter runs in a channel and there is a length of the channel that has been closed up with a folded strip of steel about two years ago by somebody who has not been found.

**He got the shutter up to four foot, twice, in about half an hour, and on the second attempt it stopped at the same place as on the first, and he did not count it out loud and he did not try a third time, and he told her it was the channel and not the shutter and that the channel wanted a fitter and not a repairer.**

"**And how long is that,**" she said.

"**I do not know. He will know. I have not asked him what it is like and I would not have told you the truth if I had.**"

"**Right,**" she said, and paid about eleven pounds, and has not come back, and did not say thank you, and he did not expect it to.

**He has been in about four shops in this city that have closed and he has a list in his head of the ones he told the truth to and he is not going to write it down and he knows what that list is for.**

---

**One thing he noticed and there is no form for it: the finish on the underside of that shutter has been worn back in a band about the width of a hand, from a height of about four foot, and it is worn back evenly, along the whole width of the shutter, and a shutter does not get worn back evenly at one height by anybody opening it.**

He put his own hand in it on the way out. It is not a bag and it is not a dent. It is that about four years of somebody standing at that height with a hand on the bottom edge is in the paint.

He has not thought about what that means and there is nobody he could ask.

---

**He wanted to have gone down on his own, once, without anybody, so that there would be one thing in this city that he had seen and could not describe to a person who had asked him.**

There is nobody he could have asked. That is the reason and it took him the whole of the walk back to work out that it was a reason and not an excuse.

---

**Four rooms off that service road and one behind the other three, and from about half past six on that Thursday a woman of about thirty in the fourth of them, with the door of the room behind the back of her chair.**

Her page, the eighth of eight, has been shut in a ring binder on a back shelf for a hundred and fifty days. It did not come out on that Thursday, nobody put a question to her, and nobody is going to.

---

**The rest of that Thursday, between the shutter and the stair, was about nine calls in a shop in Lattice Ward and a woman of about thirty-five who wanted to know why a tap had gone cold on the first floor of a building where nobody else had reported it.**

**It was her own immersion. She had a heater on that floor and everybody else in that building was on the main, and she had been without hot water for about a fortnight and had been telling the landlord, who has a form and answers in about nine days.**

"**It is not the building,**" Marek said. "**It is on your own floor and it is yours and you have not been told anything that is not true.**"

"**He told me it was the building.**"

"**Then he was wrong and he is not a liar,**" Marek said, "**and you have about a fortnight of cold water and a landlord who will find out in about nine days, and both of those are true at once and I cannot make them not be.**"

She paid about eleven pounds for the visit and did not get anything fixed, and he told her that in advance, and she said she would rather know.

---

He said none of it.
Eleven dated jobs went out and eleven came in and the shutter came down at about ten, and a stair behind a door in a first district has a lock on the inside of it that does not lock, and four people went down it, and about nine of the hour and ten minutes were that lock and a new one, and the room at the bottom of the stair is not described on this page and is not going to be, and the reason given is that the man who was in it could not describe it and not that it is being kept from anybody.
