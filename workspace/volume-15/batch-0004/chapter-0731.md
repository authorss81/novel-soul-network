# Chapter 731 — A Thursday, Nine People In A First-Floor Room Above A Shop In A Third District Writing Down Three Lines And Nine People In A Hall In A First District Writing Down Three Different Ones, And Neither Of The Two Sets Asked The Other A Question, And Three Pieces Of Paper That Nobody Collected

**A Thursday was a bench until two, a counter until about six and four rooms off that service road from about six. This one had about nine people in a first-floor room above a shop in a third district writing on three pieces of paper, and about nine people in a hall in a first district writing on three more, and neither of the two sets knew the other had done it.**

---

Eleven names on the book and eleven dates against them and the last of the eleven at half past five. The shutter came down at about ten and the cage shook about nine times this week, which is about what it has been doing.

**The card in that rail, which takes two, is at one thousand three hundred and fifty-one days, which is one hundred and ninety-three weeks to the day, and it stands four days behind the four rooms off that service road and has done since before the spring. The hold on nine crates standing on a floor in the second of the four towns is at nine hundred and eighty days, which is one hundred and forty weeks to the day, and there is nothing written under either of those two figures and neither of them is about the day.**

The separation is at seven hundred and twenty-seven days, one hundred and three weeks and six days, in a one-line box about sixteen months old, and it is the only line in this book that has never once been surprising to anybody.

---

**The room is the first floor of a shop in a third district and the key to it is kept under the counter downstairs by a woman of about thirty-eight who sells papers and cards and has kept it for about four years and has never once been asked what happens in the room.**

About nine people came up between about seven and about half past eight on that Thursday. There were eight chairs and about nine of them were sat in and one man stood.

There is no chairwoman and there was no vote and nobody took a minute. **What the eight chairs were for was to be sat on while somebody wrote three things down, and the writing was the whole of it.**

The sheet was a sheet of lined paper torn off a pad and it had three headings on it before anybody arrived, because a man of about forty-four had written them on the Tuesday and had not told anybody and had left the pad on the table.

The three headings are these. **What we can safely do. What we will not share. Who can stop our part.**

---

**The first line took about half an hour and it is not a list of anything anybody can check.** Two people could run a warm room between six and ten. One person could drive somebody to a clinic on a Thursday and could not do it on a Wednesday because the clinic is shut on a Wednesday. A man of about fifty-one said his shop had a back room with a kettle in it and that anybody could come and use it and that he was not going to write down that it was his, because a thing written down is a thing that can be taken off you.

**Nobody wrote his name against the back room and nobody asked him to.**

The second line took about four minutes and it is the line everything else on that sheet hangs from. They would not share a list of names. A woman of about thirty-three said, in about nine seconds, that a list of names in a room is a list of names in about two months, and that the reason to keep it out is not politeness and is that nobody in that room can delete a thing once it is written down in a room somebody else is standing in.

A man of about forty-four said that he had found the same fault four times on the same machine since the spring and that he had told about four people and that he had it written on the back of a hand.

"**Then it stays on the back of your hand,**" she said.

"**I can take it round myself,**" he said.

"**Right,**" she said, and that was the whole of it, and about four people in that room said nothing and one of them said that he should not have to.

**He did not write it down and he took it round himself and he has been doing that on and off since the spring and there is no record anywhere of how many times he has done it and he has not been asked and does not want to be.** What it cost him is that the fourth time, the machine went into a stop of about two hours and about four people were waiting for it, and nobody said anything about that to anybody either.

---

**The third line is the one that caused the most talking and it is the only one of the three that anybody in that room would have used the word important about, and nobody did.**

The first suggestion was a set of names. Four people were for it and about four were against it and a woman of about twenty-nine who had not spoken until then said the thing that settled it, which took about nine seconds:

"**If you put names on it you have made a list of people who can stop us, and then the people who are not on the list have to guess whether they can, and the ones who can will not say so out loud in a room, because saying so is how you get asked to do it on a day you cannot.**"

Nobody wrote her name down either.

What went on the third line, eventually, after about half an hour, was a description of a shift and not a person. **Whoever is holding the keys on the night. And if nobody is holding the keys on the night, then nobody.** A man of about thirty-eight said that was not an answer and the woman of about thirty-three said it was the only one that would still be true in a month.

**The sheet has three lines on it and there is no line under any of the three for a name and there is no line at the bottom of the sheet at all.**

---

**The hall in a first district had about nine people in it at about the same time and the three lines on that piece of paper are not the three lines on the other one.**

Theirs went up at about six and their second line is that they will not carry a technique between teams, and their first line is that they will not do anything at all that needs two people in a room, because about nine of the people who came that evening live on their own and about four of them have not been in a room with another person who is not paid to be there since the spring.

A man of about forty-four in that hall wanted the same thing the man in the third district wanted, which is a fault written down with nobody's name on it, and a woman of about thirty-six said no to him in about four seconds and gave a different reason, which is that a fault with nobody's name on it gets carried into a building by somebody who has never seen it happen and will do it the way it was done last, which is the way it was done wrong the first time.

**Neither set of nine knew the other set existed. Nobody sent a sheet anywhere. Nobody asked the other set a question, and neither set has ever been in the other's building, and the two rooms are about four streets apart and neither group knows that about four of its own members have been in the other building on an ordinary day and did not say so.**

---

**The woman of about thirty-eight who keeps the shop came up at about half past eight to ask whether anybody wanted the room on Thursday again, and about four people said that they did, and she wrote the day on a pad by the door and went back down.**

She did not come up. She was not asked. **She has not been told what was written on the sheet and she has not asked and there is nothing in this city that would put it in front of her.**

The sheet is on the table. It is not in a drawer and it is not in a bag and it is not on a list and it has no number on it. About four people in that room have seen it since and one of them has read it and none of them has taken it.

---

**The rest of the Thursday ran.** Eleven names and eleven dates, the last at half past five, and a woman of about forty-four brought in a heater off a bedroom wall that goes on and off and was told that it would go on and off, and she said that is not an answer, and it was the same answer she has had, and she paid about eleven pounds for it and knows what she paid for and has not been back and is not going to be.

---

**The four rooms off that service road were in use from about half past six with one more room behind them, and the woman of about thirty sat in the fourth of them with the chair the door is behind being hers until about ten.**

Her page, the eighth of eight, has been inside a ring binder on a back shelf for a hundred and thirty-eight days. It did not come out on that Thursday. Nobody has asked her a question this year, nobody has said one word to her about the page, and nobody is going to say sorry to her in any form.

---

**Three things on that Thursday, and one of them was a pad somebody had written three headings on on the Tuesday and had not mentioned.**

A kettle in the back room of a shop in a third district that has been on the boil twice and has been emptied into the same sink both times. **A hall in a first district where about nine people wrote down that they would not carry a technique between teams.** And a line of lined paper with three headings on it and nothing under the third one.

---

**He wanted one of those two sheets to have come to him.**

Not the contents. The sheet itself, on his counter, face down, with somebody's handwriting on it, so that he could have had it on a bench on the Friday and read it and been a man who had read it. **Nobody sent it and nobody was asked to send it and there is no arrangement in this city by which nine people in a room off a parade are told that a man who fixes things would like to see what they wrote.** He did not go up. He was in the second of those two rooms for about ten minutes on his way home and stood at the back and about four people noticed him and nobody said anything, and he left before anybody wrote anything down.

He said none of it.
Eleven in and eleven out, the shutter down at about ten, and the counter shut by about ten past ten, and a call-out in a fourth district that he had priced at the going rate on the Wednesday went to a man from that street for about four pounds less, and that is about nine pounds out of this shop and he did not chase it and has not gone after the man.
