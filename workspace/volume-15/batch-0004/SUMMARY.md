## 4. THE INSTRUMENT WAS WRONG SEVEN TIMES, AND EVERY ONE OF THE SEVEN WAS THE INSTRUMENT

**This is the sixth occurrence of that failure in this repository and it is the finding of this batch. The chapters were written and saved before any instrument ran.**

1. **The parser failed its own first assertion.** *One thousand three hundred and forty-seven* parsed as **1,047**, because a tens/hundreds machine that adds on `and` discards the hundreds. It was caught by the assertion, which is the only reason the assertion exists.
2. **The parser had no state for the tens slot** and read *three hundred and fifty-three* as 353 while *three hundred and thirty-three* as 333, and it read a British *and* as a terminator. Rewritten as a four-state machine over a completed total, a current group and a slot.
3. **The boundary-D test was applied to the weeks run.** *One hundred and ninety-three weeks to the day* was reported as a shape defect on 193 % 7 ≠ 0, which is nonsense: `to the day` qualifies the **days** figure. **Thirty-eight findings, every one the instrument's.**
4. **The tail rule was not enforced.** A `continue` intended to skip a consumed run was attached to the shape branch instead of the recording branch, so the walk re-entered *inside* every figure and read 1,351 as 351 and as 51. **It produced figures that were present and wrong and it did not look wrong.**
5. **The walk bridged a colon.** *The rail takes two: one thousand three hundred and fifty-one days* parsed as 1,353. **Forty-five off-row findings on ten files that have none**, and every one of them carried a visible two-word run in the printout, which is how it was found.
6. **The grammar test read the total value instead of the day component**, and a regex for *one days* is satisfied by the tail of *nine hundred and one days*. Forty-four findings, all of them correct English.
7. **The orphan scan's class two was "any word that is not a number"**, which flags *about six* and *about half*. 472 findings, and the honest figure is 2.

**The parser was asserted against eight known renderings before it was trusted, including one hyphenated one, and the assertion is what caught the first failure.** **Every repair pass in this batch hard-failed on any target string that was not found exactly once and wrote nothing at all when one did not match; the first such pass aborted on fourteen targets and the second on four, and a third and fourth were run to clear the residue.** An instrument that cannot be shown to fail safely should not be pointed at prose.
