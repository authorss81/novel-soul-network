15. **Nothing said out loud about what a chapter is for, and no new final enemy, and no institution, office or post invented.** A hall that opens its doors is a hall. Nine rooms are nine rooms. **The Crown Key, on the one day it is named, is a thing on a sheet and nobody in the chapter knows what it is.**

## 4. THE THREE FILES THE WRITER IS LEAST SURE OF, NAMED BEFORE STARTING, AND NOT CLAIMED CLEAN AFTERWARDS

1. **Chapter 704, the hands.** The shape of the damage is stated by the plan of record and the register is wrong, and the wrongness is the whole point, and it is very easy to write a chapter in which somebody realises what has happened and ruins it.
2. **Chapter 707, the sheet.** Two sheets of paper in fifty pages of prose and one of them is the reason the last volume exists, and the temptation to make the second one worth reading is the temptation this manuscript has been refusing for three volumes.
3. **Chapter 710, the sitting.** A new volume's first Exchange sitting, on a book that shut at the last one, in a city where nine minutes of something happened on its first Monday, in a room where nobody has any idea what is going on outside it. **The trap is that the room knows.**

## 5. WHAT THIS BATCH OWES THE NEXT ONE, AND IT IS NINE ITEMS

**Movement II is Chapters 711 to 720, days 1681 to 1694, entries 714 to 723, weeks 256 to 258, and it has NO WEEKDAY GAP AT ALL, which is the first of the four dense movements.** It must be handed: the six words, which a man of about twenty-two heard in a first district on Chapter 709 and said nothing about; the two new people of this movement, who are available and are not to be re-spent; the sheet, which is at more doors and whose number of doors is a number one man keeps; the man of about thirty-four's hands, which are on the page and not written down; the woman's hands that went still, which are on the page and not written down; the four free checks; the book on sixty-four lines; the register at two; the fact that the ninth chair has not moved and the place behind it is empty and neither figure is on anybody's form.
