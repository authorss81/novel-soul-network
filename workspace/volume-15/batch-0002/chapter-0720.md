# Chapter 720 — A Wednesday, A Bench Until Two And A Counter Until About Six, A Man Of About Thirty-Three At A Counter With A Gate Brace He Had Pulled Off A Gate Himself And Would Not Have Put Back, A Second District Where A Valve Would Not Seat And About Four Trips Up A Stair Were Made With A Bucket, A Woman Of About Twenty-Four Who Came In For A Length Of Cord And Said Nothing About Anything, And A Chair On A Landing In A First District That About Four People Walk Past Every Day

**A Wednesday was a bench until two, a counter until about six and four rooms off that service road from about six. This one had a man at the counter with a piece of iron he had taken off a gate himself, and a second district where a valve had to be persuaded, and a woman who came in for a length of cord and said nothing about any of it.**

---

The Wednesday was eleven jobs with a date against each and eleven names on the book, and the last name was taken at half past five, and about two of the eleven were about the day. The shutter came down at ten and the cage shook, and that had happened about nine times in a fortnight.

**The man of about fifty-one against that north wall is at nine hundred and thirty-eight days, one hundred and thirty-four weeks to the day, and the ask is at one thousand and twenty-two days, one hundred and forty-six weeks to the day, and those are two of the four figures in this page that come out as a whole number of weeks, and the other two are the eighteenth line and the place behind the woman's chair.**

The nineteenth line on that board of two nails is one thousand and twenty-eight days, a hundred and forty-six weeks and six days, and the separation stands at seven hundred and twelve days, a hundred and one weeks and five days, and it went into a one-line box in a form about sixteen months ago and there is nothing in it.

**The place behind the woman's chair has been empty two hundred and ten days, which is thirty weeks to the day.** About four people in this city have that figure and about two of them know it to the day, and nobody has said a word about it to anybody and nobody has asked her about it in thirty weeks, and it is not on a form and it is not on anything anybody keeps, and the only person in the world who writes day counts down as a job has it in his head and has not printed it on a load book.

---

**The man of about thirty-three came in at about ten with a gate brace in his hand and a carrier in the other one, and he had put the brace on his own gate by mistake in the spring and taken it off again in about nine minutes with a bar and a spanner, and he wanted it looked at.**

He wanted it put back on.

Marek looked at it for about four seconds and told him the holes had gone oval and that it would not go back on that gate, and the man of about thirty-three said he had thought as much, and brought it in to have it bent back to nothing, which is not a thing anybody can do.

**It took about nine minutes to get it to and about eleven to get it off, and there is a man in this city who does that for a fee and is not in Lattice Ward. He paid for the second one, and he did not get the first one back on, and nobody in this city is going to put it back on for him, and he did not ask anybody to.**

He carried the carrier out with the brace in it and the carrier was lighter going out than it was coming in, and nobody in that shop said one word about the second one.

---

**The flat is on the fourth floor of a block in a second district and the cylinder at the end of the bathroom would not fill, and the reason it would not fill was a valve at the bottom of it that has been cleaned twice by somebody who did not know what it was and has not seated since either time.**

The water had to come up in a bucket. It is about eleven trips, one at a time, up four flights, from a tap on the ground floor, and Marek did six of them and a woman of about fifty-one who lived on the second did four, and a boy of about eleven who lived on the fourth carried two of hers because she told him to and not otherwise.

**They did it in about four minutes each and nobody turned the cylinder on because the point of it is not to turn it on, and nobody put a pan under anything, and the bucket went back down the same four flights between every one of them.**

He was in that flat about two hours. The valve came to seat at about ten past five when somebody who had been told about it came and put a washer on it that cost about four pounds, and the cylinder filled at about half past six, and the boy of about eleven carried the last two trips and put the bucket on the landing outside the fourth-floor door and left it there, and it was still on that landing on the following morning and nobody moved it and nobody put it there.

---

**The woman of about twenty-four came in at about half past four for a length of cord and put it on the counter and paid for it, and the two of them said about nine words to each other in about four seconds, and none of the nine was about a card, and there is a card about four inches by two on a wall in a fourth district in her own name with nine words on it, and he did not ask her about the wall and she did not ask him whether he had heard about it, and neither of them mentioned it and neither of them is going to.**

Nobody thanked anybody in that shop on that Wednesday, and a woman of about twenty-four does not get thanked there, and she had a folder with her and left it under her arm and went out and came back in at about five for a washer and paid and went again, and has not been back.

---

**The rest of the Wednesday ran.** Eleven jobs and eleven names, the last at half past five, and a man of about thirty-eight came in with a lamp that worked and wanted to know whether it was safe, and was told that it was as safe as the other one and that he should not use either of them wet, and he said right and bought a switch and has not been back.

Between about half past six and ten the four rooms off that service road were in use and a fifth was behind the other three, and there was a fifth person in the fourth of those rooms for all of it. She is about thirty and the chair the door is behind is hers. Her page, the eighth of eight of them, has been in a ring binder on a back shelf for a hundred and twenty-seven days, it did not come out that Wednesday, and nobody has put a word to her or apologised to her in any form or ever will.

---

**Three things on that Wednesday and none of them on a form.** A gate brace in a carrier in a shop off Lattice Ward that a man paid for twice and would not have put back on a gate. **A galvanised bucket on a fourth-floor landing in a second district that is not anybody's and was left there by a boy of about eleven who was told to carry it and not to bring it back down.** And a chair standing on a landing in a first district with nothing on it and nothing against it, which about four people walk past every day and not one of them has moved.

---

**He wanted the four people on that landing to have taken the chair in.**

Nobody did. It is a chair and there is room on every landing in that block for about nine of them and it has been standing there since before the middle of the week, and about four people have walked past it every day and not one of them has moved it, and nobody in that building has ever said the word chair to anybody, and he wanted one of them to have put it inside a door and shut the door, and he has wanted that on about nine separate days and it has not happened on any of them and he does not know any of the four by name.

He said none of it.
Eleven dated jobs went out and eleven came in, the shutter came down at ten, and he went home at ten past ten, and he had turned down a call-out at about four o'clock in a third district because his own bench was free for the first time in about eleven days and he did not take the call and he did not use the bench, and that is about eleven pounds and about two hours and neither of them is in a load book.
