# Chapter 755 — A Bay On A Road In A Second District That Goes Out At About Four On A Friday With Nothing On It And A Woman Of About Thirty-Six Standing At It On Her Own Shift And Nobody Knowing She Was There, And Two People He Had To Ask Going Down A Stair To Look At A Room With A Table In It And About Nine Words Face Down On It And Nothing Else At All

**A bench until two. A counter until about six. Four rooms off that service road from half past six. This Friday had a bay on a road in a second district in the middle of it at about four in the afternoon, with a woman at it and nobody who knew she was there, and a room under a building with about nine words face down on a table.**

---

Ten names went on that Friday's sheet and ten dates went with them, the last at about twenty to five. The cage in the round-the-side had been going about nine times in the fortnight.

**Rooms off that service road, four of them and one behind, stand at one thousand three hundred and ninety days, one hundred and ninety-eight weeks and four days, and line sixteen on the board of two nails stands at one thousand one hundred and eighty days, one hundred and sixty-eight weeks and four days. There is nothing under either of them and there has not been since the spring.**

The separation is at seven hundred and seventy days, one hundred and ten weeks to the day.

---

**The second bay went out at about four with nothing on it, which it does on a Friday, which it has done since before the spring, and which nobody has ever looked at a sheet to find out about.**

A woman of about thirty-six works that release. She has worked it for about nine years, she goes out at about half four, and she is on that bay until six.

**Nobody told her anything. Not that a bay had been said out loud in a room in a first district on a Monday in the middle of this year, and not by whom, and not that four people had heard it. Nobody has thanked her. Nobody has asked her a second question. Nobody has said her name to her.**

She is not a second one and she is not a follower and she is not owed anything by anybody in this city, and nothing in this movement is owed to her.

**She is not the woman of about twenty-nine who stood up in a garage on a wide road in the first week of the spring and said one thing nine seconds long, and the two have never been in a room together and are never going to be, and about four people have got them confused and it is going to stay that way for a while.**

---

**He walked past that garage at about twenty to four and did not go in, and he has said since that going in was the only thing he wanted to do and that he did not do it and has not worked out why.**

The shutter was up and the light was on and he could see the second bay from the end of the road and he stood at the end of the road for about four seconds and then went on.

---

**Two people had to be asked and both of them said yes and one of them said why first.**

The man of about thirty-four with the keys to that building in a fourth district came down a stair in a first district at about half past seven, and the man of about forty-three with the folded paper in his coat came with him and did not open the paper.

**The man of about thirty-four said, before he agreed to anything at all, *what for*, and Marek said *to look*, and the man of about thirty-four said *right* and went down.**

The man of about sixty who keeps that building was at the top of that stair with a cup in his hand and did not come down and was not asked to and has not come down.

---

**The room at the bottom of that stair had a table in it and one chair pulled out from it and a cup on the table with about a mouthful in it, and about nine words on a sheet of paper face down, and nothing else that is anybody's.**

There is a cabinet under the floor in that room that is not in this chapter and is not mentioned in it.

**The three of them read the nine words. The man of about forty-three read them twice and did not say what they were. Marek did not turn the sheet over twice and the cup was not emptied and nobody washed it.**

The sheet is still face down on that table and nobody has taken it and nobody has copied it.

---

**He refused in about four seconds to leave the folded paper in that room, and the reason was about ownership and he said it to a man who had not asked.**

"**If it stays here it becomes the room's, and then it becomes mine again through the room. Take it home.**"

The man of about forty-three put it in his inside pocket and said nothing and took it with him, and it is in a drawer in a first district and nobody knows which drawer.

---

**The morning of that Friday was a bench until two, a counter until about six and about nine jobs, and two people came in before half past ten and one of them was made twice.**

A woman of about forty-four brought a worktop edge in a van and wanted it taken off and put back on with the damage made good, and it had been made good in about eleven minutes by somebody else in the spring and it had opened along the joint again by the middle of the summer, and Marek took it off, cleaned the joint, and told her that this fix would hold for about two years and that the next one would be a different fix and a more expensive one, and that he would rather she heard that from him now.

She said right and left it on his counter and came back at about twenty to four for it and paid about nine pounds and said the thing that everybody in this city says, which is that she will think about the second fix.

**That job was done twice that morning and the second time was longer than the first, and about four people who watched said the same thing, and he charged for one of them.**

---

They were in that room about eleven minutes and the light was on for all eleven and then it was off and the door was shut and that is the last time the door of that room is opened in this stretch of days.**

Nobody has a key to it. The man of about thirty-four offered to leave his spare and Marek said no in one word and the man did not press it and has not offered again.

**That room is dark now and it stays dark and nobody has said anything about it and about nine people in this city know where it is.**

---

**The man of about sixty who keeps that building was asked, by the man of about thirty-four, at about half past seven, whether anybody knew the stair was there, and he said he knew, and that was the whole of it.**

The man of about thirty-four said he had not asked that before in about nine years of keeping the keys and he had assumed everybody knew and that it had never occurred to him that it might not be, and the man of about sixty said that was not his business to think about and went and washed a cup.

**There is a name written on a piece of paper inside that cup cupboard on the ground floor of that building and it has been there since before the spring and nobody has ever read it and Marek read it on his way out and put it back and told nobody and has not been able to stop thinking about it.**

**He wanted the woman of about thirty-six to have seen him walk past and not come in, and he does not know whether she saw him, and he has not asked, and asking would have been the thing that told her.**

---

**What it cost was about forty minutes of his own Friday morning, and one bar of his own that was cracked and went into a skip at about half past six.**

---

**Four rooms off that service road and one behind the other three, and a woman of about thirty in the fourth of them from about half past six, with the shut door of that room behind the back of her chair and nothing in her hands.**

The eighth of eight of her pages has been in a ring binder on a back shelf for a hundred and seventy-nine days. It did not come out on that Friday, nobody asked her one thing about it, and nobody ever will.

---

**There is a skip at the end of that yard in Lattice Ward that goes on a fortnight and one bar of his own went into it at about half past six on that Friday with about nine other things that were not his.**

**He watched it go in and did not go and ask what had become of it, and that is the first time in about four years he has put something of his own in a skip without being asked to find out where it went.**

The tip is on a card in the office and the card has not been filled in since before the spring and there are about nine names on the old ones going back years, and he has never looked at the old ones and looked at them for a long time that evening.

He said none of it.
Ten dated jobs went out and ten came in, the shutter came down at about ten, a bay on a road in a second district went out at about four with nothing on it and the woman who works that release was told nothing at all, two men went down a stair and looked at a table with nine words on it face down and came back up, and the light in that room was on for about eleven minutes and then it was not.
