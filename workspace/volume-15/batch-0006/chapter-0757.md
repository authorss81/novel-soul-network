# Chapter 757 — A Counter Shut At Half Past Four So That A Man Could Be On A Second Floor By Half Past Five, About Nine Chairs In That Room, A Register One Woman Has Kept For About Nine Years, Nine Words Said Into A Corner Of A Sheet By The Only Person Entitled To Say Them, And A Question Asked Afterwards In A Corridor That He Did Not Contest

**The counter shut at half past four so that a man could be on a second floor by half past five, and then four rooms off a service road, and then the shutter. In the middle of it: about nine chairs, a register kept by one woman for about nine years, nine words said into a corner of a sheet, and one question asked afterwards in a corridor that he did not contest.**

---

Fifteen names went on that Thursday and fifteen dates went with them, the last taken at about twenty to five. The cage in the round-the-side had been emptied on about the same days it had been going for weeks.

**The man of about fifty-one against that north wall is at one thousand and two days, one hundred and forty-three weeks and one day, and what is still owed is at one thousand and eighty-six days, one hundred and fifty-five weeks and one day. Those are the two the Thursday is measured against and one of them is a decision nobody has ever collected.**

The separation is at seven hundred and seventy-six days, one hundred and ten weeks and six days.

---

**There were about nine chairs in that room and about nine people in it and one of the nine did not sit down.**

The sheet has been going round that table on a Friday like this for about four years with one printed box on it, and the box is not empty any more, and it has not been empty since a Friday in the spring before this stretch of days began, and what went into it that day is on no other sheet in this city.

**Nobody in that room said the words on that Thursday and nobody has said them since and nobody will until the last day of this stretch of days.**

---

**A woman of about forty-eight has kept the register of that body's appointments for about nine years and she is the only person entitled to enter one, and she entered one in about four seconds and read it back.**

"**It holds to its date, not one day past.**"

Nine words. One line. **It decided nothing at all except that the thing already on the sheet could not go on for ever by nobody noticing.**

About four people in that room had expected the thing to be refused. About four had expected it to be softened. It was neither and nobody said either word out loud.

---

**She read the terms out and one of them is the whole of why this page exists.**

That the ombud may look into any decision that body has made, and into any person who made one for it, and may do it without telling that body first.

**A woman of about twenty-four has a key to a room of her own and not to his, and a sheet of paper about four inches by two is taped to a wall in a fourth district in her own name with nine words on it, and about four people have walked past it for about nine weeks.**

Nobody in that room thanked her. Nobody has ever thanked her for that card and nobody is going to and she did not expect it and has not mentioned it.

---

**About four people in that second floor had read something about that appointment before that Thursday and about nine had not.**

A man of about thirty-eight said in about nine seconds that he had heard the thing had been done and had come in to see whether it had been done properly, and the woman of about forty-eight said she would read him the entry and he could check it against the paper, and he did that at about ten past six and did not find anything wrong with it and did not say so and left at about a quarter past six.

**Nobody in that room asked him whether the person appointed could look into him and he did not offer it and it was in the terms and it had been read out.**

A woman of about twenty-nine asked whether the terms could be changed and was told in about nine seconds by the woman of about forty-eight that they could be changed by the same nine people who had changed them last time and that this was the third time and that each change had taken something off the end of it.

**He did not appoint her, did not propose her, was not asked about it beforehand and did not know it was on that sheet until the woman of about forty-eight read the terms out.**

He has said since that the second that mattered most to him on that Thursday was the second before the nine words, which was the woman of about forty-eight turning the sheet round so that the room could see the date before she said anything.

---

**A man of about forty-four asked whether a body can look into its own decisions and the woman of about forty-eight answered him in about nine seconds and the answer was not reassuring.**

"**It can and it has not yet. Being able to is not the same as having done and I am not going to tell this room which of those two it will be in a year.**"

---

**She asked him one question in that corridor at about twenty past five and he answered it in about nine seconds and did not contest a word of it and she wrote nothing down.**

The question was about a decision he made on a Wednesday in a second district about nine years ago and about what he told two people that evening.

He answered it. **He did not ask why, did not ask who had told her, did not offer her a document, and did not say that anybody else had been in the room. That is the first time in this stretch of days that he has not argued with anything anybody has said about him and it lasted about nine seconds and it is not a reconciliation.**

---

**Two o'clock to half past four on that Thursday was the counter and about nine people came through it and two of them were told no.**

A woman of about twenty-nine brought a bicycle lamp that works when it is held at an angle and does not work on its own bracket, and the bracket had been made for it by somebody in the spring who had not asked her what it was for, and Marek took the bracket off, put a washer behind it, and gave the bracket back, and she asked whether it would still work if she left it on, and he said it would.

A man of about forty-four wanted a lock changed on a door in a first floor and had been told by two other men that it was the frame, and it was the frame, and Marek told him so and charged him about nine pounds to look at it and not to do it, and the man said that was the third time this year and he would stop asking.

**Neither of them said thank you. One of them said sorry about the bracket, which was nobody's fault but hers to carry, and he told her so and she said right.**

---

He wanted her to have been given that room before the sentence rather than after it, and she was given it after, and that is the correct order and both of those are true.**

---

**What it cost was nine pounds, exact, out of a counter he was not standing at, because he closed the shop at half past four on a Thursday to be in a second floor by half past five and took the day's till money home in a bag and put it in the drawer on the Friday.**

---

**Four rooms off that service road and one behind the other three, and a woman of about thirty in the fourth of them from about half past six, at her chair, with the shut door of that room behind her.**

Her page, the eighth of eight, has been in a ring binder on a back shelf for a hundred and eighty-five days. It did not come out on that Thursday, nobody asked her one thing about it, and nobody in this city is entitled to open that binder.

---

**About nine pounds of that day's money sat in a bag in his coat overnight because the till was slow and he did not want to leave a float in it, and the drawer it went into on the Friday was the one the float comes out of, which is not the drawer it is meant for.**

**He has done that twice before and both times in the winter and both times he put it back straight, and he did not put it back straight this time, and it is still where he put it.**

There is a chair at the top of that stair in a fourth district that nobody has ever sat in and a coat hook behind it that has nothing on it and about nine people a day go past both of them.

He said none of it.
Fifteen dated jobs went out and fifteen came in, the shutter came down at about ten, a woman of about forty-eight who keeps a register entered one thing in about four seconds and said nine words that decided nothing, a term on a sheet lets one person look into anybody who has made a decision for that body including a man of twenty-two, and that man answered her one question in about nine seconds in a corridor and did not contest it.
