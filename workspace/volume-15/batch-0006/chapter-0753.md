# Chapter 753 — Three Floors Let Separately In One Building, Three Groups Of People In Them Who Until That Year Answered To The Same Woman, Two Hours Of Argument About A Network And Not About A Name, And Then A Woman On The Third Step Of A Stair Saying The One Sentence In That Building That Had Never Been Said And Could Not Be Taken Back

**Three floors let separately in one building, three groups of people in them, and about two hours of argument about what may be done to a network and not about who is in charge of it. At about a quarter to six somebody said a sentence that had not been said before in that building.**

---

Fourteen names went on that Friday and fourteen dates went with them, the last at about twenty to five. The cage in the round-the-side had gone about nine times in the fortnight and the van was out from about eleven and was not back until about half past six.

**What is still owed is at one thousand and seventy-three days, one hundred and fifty-three weeks and two days, and line fourteen on the board of two nails is at one thousand two hundred and nineteen days, one hundred and seventy-four weeks and one day. Neither of those has been on anybody's table since the spring and neither of them is going to be.**

The separation is at seven hundred and sixty-three days, one hundred and nine weeks to the day, and a whole number of weeks is the only thing it has been changed by this year.

---

**There were three rooms in that building and about nine people in the bottom one, about four in the middle one, and about nine in the top one, and all three of those figures were counted by somebody in the corridor on the Friday and written on the back of a docket and never shown to anybody.**

Rafi Pell came with four of the nine from the bottom room. He has a cooperative and about nine buses and is not on a list anywhere in this city and has not been on one in about three years.

He said before he sat down that he was there as one of nine and not as a person, and that if anybody wanted to make him the one he would go out.

---

**The middle of that Friday was four hours of a man of twenty-two's own van and his own back, and about nine chairs and a table.**

The chairs came off a floor in a first district in two loads at about half past four the evening before and two of them came off damp, and Marek carried all of them up himself and nobody asked him to and nobody paid him and nobody has mentioned it since.

The table went up in one piece with the top off, and it took two men off a Friday round to get it round the corner on the second landing, and one of those two men is a man of about thirty-four who keeps the keys to a building in a fourth district and who said *you should have taken the top off and left it* and then held the other end anyway.

**The strap he used to hold the chairs together in the second load was his own and it went into the back of that van and has been used for other things since and has not been returned.**

He was at that building from about half past four until about six and then drove back and put the chairs down in a corridor of a shop in Lattice Ward and went and opened a counter at about half past six with about nine people in it.

---

The argument was about a network and about what a person is allowed to do to it, and it went on for about two hours and it was never once about a name.**

The nine in the bottom room want it kept. They want the returns left where they are and they want a person at each one of them who can say no, and they have already found nine such people and three of those nine have said no and are still counted.

The four in the middle room want about three districts taken off it in a fortnight, and they have said in that room, out loud, that they have the hands to do it and that they are not going to wait for anybody, and nobody has told them they cannot and nobody has told them they can.

**Nobody in this chapter has compared one of those three rooms to another one and nobody in this building has either, and the two positions are not the same and are not the opposite.**

The nine in the top room want it held exactly where it is and have asked for nothing and have offered nothing and would like it noted that they have offered nothing.

---

**He refused twice, in about four seconds each, to say which of the three should have the room on the second floor for a month.**

"**No.**"

"**You have been in all three.**"

"**I have been in all three and that is exactly why I am not the one who decides which of you gets the floor, because the one who does not get the floor will spend a month knowing that I did that.**"

About four seconds the first time and about eleven the second and the second is not counted as a second refusal because he answered the man's question and not the offer.

---

**Nobody in that building ate anything until about half past eight and about four people went out for bread and came back with it and about nine did not eat at all.**

A man of about thirty-three in the bottom room had come in that day expecting the argument to end with somebody appointed and he said, at about eight, that he had a train to think about and no, they did not have trains in this city — he had a bus, and he had a bus to think about, and he went at about eight and left his coat.

His coat was still on the back of a chair at about nine and about four people looked at it and nobody said anything about it and it is on that chair now and it is not his any more because he has been in a second room since and has said nothing about the coat.

**What was said at about a quarter to six had not been said in that building before and has not been unsaid since.**

A woman of about forty-four who was sitting in the corridor on the third step said it, and she had come up from a floor on her own and had not spoken until then.

"**There are about nine of us who are not in this city. Nobody has asked us anything. You have all been two hours over a network that about four of the people in this building have ever been inside, and every one of you has said *the network* as though the nine of us are in it.**"

About nine seconds. About fifty-one words.

**Nobody in that building answered her. About four people looked at the floor. A man of about forty-six who chairs the top room wrote the name of a town on the back of his hand and did not say it out loud and has not written it on anything, and that name has not been sent to anybody and has not been asked about.**

Two hours after she said it, Rafi Pell asked whether anybody knew how many of the nine were in it and nobody knew, and it was the last thing said in that building that evening, and it was not said to her.

---

**He wanted the nine who are not in this city to be sitting at that table, and they were not, and there is no way of asking them from inside this building, and that is not a thing anybody has solved.**

---

**What he gave up was his own van for about four hours and about four of the hours were spent carrying about nine chairs and a table up two flights with two men off a Friday round, and there is no charge for that anywhere in this book and no charge was agreed.**

---

**Four rooms off that service road and one behind the other three, and a woman of about thirty in the fourth of them from about half past six, with the door of that room shut and behind her.**

Her page is the eighth of eight and it has been in a ring binder on a back shelf for a hundred and seventy-two days. It did not come out on that Friday, and there is nobody in this city who has the standing to take it out of that binder.

---

**That van has a rattle in it that comes from the load bed and not from the engine and that has been there since before the spring, and he has been to two people about it and been told twice that it is nothing.**

**He could find it in about nine minutes with the bed up and a torch and a Friday morning he does not have, and he has not looked, and he has decided that he is not going to look this year.**

On that Tuesday the chairs went up in it and the table went up in it and the rattle went the whole way with both, and about four people in that building heard it and one of them said he could do something about it for about nine pounds and Marek said not this week.

He said none of it.
Fourteen dated jobs went out and fourteen came in, the shutter came down at about ten, about twenty-two people argued for about two hours in a building about what may be done to a network and not about who may do it, a woman of about forty-four said in nine seconds that about nine of them were not in this city and had not been asked, nobody answered her, a man of about forty-six wrote the name of a town on the back of his hand and told nobody, and about nine buses and a cooperative went on working and nobody thanked anybody.
