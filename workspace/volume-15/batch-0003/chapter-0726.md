# Chapter 726 — A Thursday, A Landing On The First Floor Of A Block In A Second District, Nine Seconds On That Landing And Nothing Said Back To Them, A Woman Of About Fifty-Three Who Did Not Give A Name And Was Right, And A Man Of Twenty-Two Four Feet Up The Stair With A Screwdriver In His Hand Who Said Nothing And Has Not Said Anything Since

**A Thursday was a bench until two, a counter until about six and four rooms off that service road from about six. This one had a woman of about fifty-three on a landing for about nine seconds, saying a true thing about the shape of the city, and nobody answered her, and Marek was four feet up the stair and said nothing.**

---

The book was closed at half past five for the eleventh working day running, and eleven names were on it and eleven jobs dated, and at ten the shutter came down and the cage shook, about thirteen times in a fortnight.

**The nineteenth line up on that board of two nails is at one thousand and thirty-six days, which is one hundred and forty-eight weeks to the day, and the thirteenth is at one thousand two hundred and eleven days, which is one hundred and seventy-three weeks to the day. Five more figures on this page land the same way, which makes seven in all, and the one that is printed most often on this page is the one that is written on last, and there is nothing under it.**

The separation is at seven hundred and twenty days, one hundred and two weeks and six days. Nobody in this city has opened that box since the spring and the number has not needed anything.

---

**He was called to a block in a second district at about half past three to put two stair lamps back on a landing and to look at a door closer that has been coming open about four inches on its own.**

The landing is on the first floor and there are four flats off it and a stair that turns twice. The lamps had been off for eleven days because somebody had changed them and not put the old ones back and had thrown the old ones out, and the parts for a lamp of that kind do not come in any length and had to be made up.

**The woman of about thirty-six who keeps that floor opened the door to number nine for him, stayed about ten minutes, and told him about the closer and about the man on the fourth who has said he will pay for it and has not paid and has not gone away.**

He was on the fourth step up with a screwdriver in his hand when the door of number four opened along the landing.

---

**She came out and said nine seconds' worth to the woman of about thirty-six and the woman of about thirty-six was standing in her own doorway with her arms folded and did not move.**

She is about fifty-three. She was carrying a folder that was not fastened and there was nothing in it that Marek could see and he did not try.

"**I am not from a firm,**" she said, and the woman of about thirty-six said, "**Everybody's got a card now,**" and he thought that was a lie and did not say so.

"**You do not need to like me,**" the woman said. "**You have not got to stop anything. All you have to do is go on not stopping. That is the whole of it.**"

"**Then what.**"

"**Then nothing,**" she said. "**That is what it looks like from where I am standing. It is not a punishment. It is a shape, and the shape is that every single one of you has to be the only one, and there is nobody in this city who is allowed to be the second one.**"

The woman of about thirty-six did not answer that.

**Nine seconds is about twenty-eight words and she used twenty-eight words and she stopped because she stopped, and not because she had been asked to and not because she had run out of anything, and then she went along the landing and down the stair and out of that block and did not go in at the shop and has not been in any room in this city that anybody has written down since.**

---

**Nobody answered her. The woman of about thirty-six stood in her doorway for about eleven seconds after she had gone and then said, to nobody, "**Well,**" and went inside and shut the door and has not mentioned it since.**

Marek came down the two steps he was on and put the screwdriver in his pocket.

**He had four seconds in which he could have said the thing he thought of, which was that she was describing a shape and he was standing on the step of it, and he did not say it, and it is still true, and it is still there, and it has been true for about four years and about nine people in this city could put it into words and about four of them would be believed.**

She was right. **On that Thursday nothing at that shop failed, and nothing in that block failed, and eleven jobs went out and eleven came in, and the door closer on number nine's door was adjusted by about two inches and now closes, and the two stair lamps were on at about five and about four people in that block noticed them and about nine did not.**

---

**The rest of the Thursday ran.** Eleven names and eleven dates, the last at half past five, and a man of about twenty-six came in with a vacuum and said the suction had gone and would not come back, and was told that it would not come back and that the filter is a pound fifty and about eleven minutes of work, and he said he would think about it and has not been back and has not thought about it, as far as anybody here knows.

---

**The four rooms off that service road filled at about half past six and one more room behind them, and a woman of about thirty was in the fourth of them until ten with her back to a door.**

Her eighth page has been in a ring binder on a back shelf for a hundred and thirty-three days and it did not come out and it was not spoken of and no apology of any kind has been made to her in this city and none ever will be.

---

**Three things on that Thursday, none of them written down anywhere.** A folder with a strap that was not fastened, and nothing came out of it, and about four pages had been in it. **Two lamps on a landing that had been off for eleven days and were on at five and cost about three pounds and were made up from parts that were not the right length.** And a hook in a corridor of a second-district block with a coat on it that is not anybody's and has been there since before the spring.

---

**He wanted to have been standing somewhere else.**

Not somewhere in the room — she was not in the room. **He wanted to have been in a different building entirely and to have heard about this at about nine o'clock from a man of about thirty-eight who runs a market, in the way he hears about everything else, and to have been told it in four seconds and to have been able to say, and to have said, and to have been wrong, and to have been wrong in a room with other people in it.**

He said none of it.
Eleven dated jobs went out and eleven came in, the shutter came down at ten, and he walked home at ten past ten because the last bus had gone, and the two hours of that stair were not on the book because nobody said they were going to be there that long and he has charged for the lamps and not for the stair, which is about four pounds under what he would have charged and is a thing he decided on a landing and has not changed his mind about.
