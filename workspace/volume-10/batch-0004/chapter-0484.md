# Chapter 484 — The Seventh Friday Of Ten, Three Hundred And Twenty-Two Days Of A Post, And A Fourth Copy Written By Somebody Who Cannot Hold A Pen Properly

**The Friday of week one hundred and seventy-eight was spent at a bench in Lattice Ward until about two and he was not in Crown Terrace and the shop did not know and would not have known, and the one thing that is true of it is that about eleven people were in a room on a second floor for about nine minutes for the seventh Friday running and the post by the corridor end has now been bare for forty-six weeks, which is three hundred and twenty-two days, and a fourth copy of nine lines was written on the back of a rent book in a shop in the third of the four towns on the same day, and in the fourth of the nine the figure is right, and about three people have noticed the other figure and about six have not, and the man of about thirty-nine who buys training sat at the back and asked nothing at all for the seventh Friday running.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last of them about six in the evening, and his day was a bench in Lattice Ward until about two and a counter until about six, and then a room from about half past six, and the door on that second floor has a fire door on it that does not shut and has not shut for years. The word was at nine and ten and is told to nobody. **Heating: on, dearer, and four names on a lease cost more than they did, and a fortnight a cook paid for is paid for by nobody and is not resolved.** The room off that service road is seven hundred and seventy-four days old and is warm, and about nine people came in on the Friday evening for the temperature and none of them was asked anything.

The card in the rail is seven hundred and seventy-eight days old, which is four days more than the room is, and the rail holds two, and a rent book in a shop in the third of the four towns with nine lines of handwriting on the back of it is a tenth object and is about four days of bus travel from that building and is not the card and is not the post.

---

The room is the same room. About eleven chairs out of a cupboard and a table at the front and a laptop on it connected to nothing in this case. The chairs were stacked against the wall when the first person came and were unstacked by the first person who came, and that is the seventh week running that two different people have done the two halves of that, and nobody has ever asked which two people those are.

The cupboard the chairs come out of has a lock on it and the key for the lock is on a hook inside the door of the room, and about four people on that floor have said that a room you can lock and then leave the key inside it is not locked in any way that matters, and none of them has said it to anybody who could change it.

**There is a radiator under the window that has been cold on every one of the seven Fridays, and about four of the about eleven have sat away from it, and nobody in that room has ever asked the woman who calls them in why it is cold in a room with a radiator in it.**

About eleven people came, which is the same figure as the six Fridays before, and about four of the eleven are on no rota at all, and the figure is a figure and not a rate and is not on a form.

**The woman of about thirty-six with a lanyard said one word and pointed at the chairs and said nothing else, and about nine minutes went the way they go, and she did not say how long it would be and nobody asked.**

Seven Fridays is not a run. It is seven Fridays, and anybody who wants a run out of it has to wait, and there are three more Fridays after this one of which this case has seen none.

**The post by the corridor end is bare forty-six weeks, which is three hundred and twenty-two days from the Friday of week one hundred and thirty-two, and nobody in that room knows either of those figures.**

**The card is still in it and the number on the card is still the number and the box is at the same height on the same wall, and the second box at the near end of the same corridor is still not empty, and the two are about nine feet apart, and nobody in that building has ever been asked whether the two are a set.**

**The man of about thirty-nine who buys training sat at the back for the seventh Friday running and he asked nothing at all, and about four people on that floor have noticed that he asked nothing, and about four have not, and nobody on that floor has ever kept a note of what he asks on which Friday.**

**Nobody said thank you. The room emptied in about nine minutes the way it does.**

**The two figures in biro on the back of his left hand are still there, eleven and then nine, and there is still no third one, and he has not washed them off and he has not looked at either of them in that room and nobody has ever asked him for either.**

**The corridor outside that room is about four times as long as the room is wide and there are about nine doors along it, of which about four stand open and about four are locked and one is a fire door that does not shut, and nobody in that building has ever gone down it and looked at the backs of the locked ones.**

---

In the third of the four towns there is a shop that sells about nine things and a man of about forty-seven who keeps the rent book in it, and the woman of about thirty-four who keeps the only written record said the nine lines out loud there for the fourth time in about a fortnight on the Friday evening to about four people.

The man of about forty-seven wrote them out on the back of the rent book with a pen he holds in his left hand and which is held wrong, and he wrote them in about nine minutes, and he wrote the fourth of the nine with the right figure in it, and he has never written nine lines out in his life before and has not written any since.

**Nobody in that shop has ever heard the nine lines before and nobody in that shop has told anybody in that town about them, and the rent book went back on the shelf behind the till with the nine lines on the back of it and about nine people in that town have seen the front of that book.**

Nobody told him it was a copy and nobody told him it was not. Nobody asked him to read it back and he has not offered and about four people in that shop have said that the hand is not a good hand and about four have said nothing about the hand at all.

**That is four copies, in four towns, on four different surfaces, and nobody has compared any of them with any other, and about three people have noticed that one of the fourth lines is not the figure they were told and about six have not, and no two of the four people who wrote one has ever met any of the other three.**

---

He went to the room at about half past six and did not stay long. A cook of twenty-seven was at the sink saying nothing and about four people came in for the temperature and the man of about fifty-one had his tool bag at his feet.

In the chair nearest the door was a woman of about thirty whom nobody asked a question, nobody thanked and nobody apologised to, and the page that is hers is the eighth of eight things in a ring binder on a shelf and is unread and was not read on Friday.

A man of about fifty-one with a tool bag was at the wall and had said nothing for fifty-four weeks and two days. A man of about thirty-three, who holds four buildings and about nine hundred doors with no stamp, no reference and no date, was not in that room and is not in this city, and his hold went on for fifty-eight weeks and one day without anything being asked of him and without anything being given to him.

**He had been at the bench in Lattice Ward from about half past seven until about two and at that counter from about two until about six on the Friday, and the shop did not know that a man had said nothing at all in a room on a second floor in another district, and nobody in the room off that service road knew that a post on that second floor has been bare for three hundred and twenty-two days.**

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.

*The post, as a record of a box that has been bare for forty-six weeks.* **What it is: a black post box on a wall at the corridor end of a second floor in another district in this city, at the same height it has been at for years. What is in it: a card, and a number on the card, and no list anywhere of what the number is for. How long it has been bare: forty-six weeks, which is three hundred and twenty-two days from the Friday of week one hundred and thirty-two, and this is a different figure from the one that stood in that corridor four Fridays ago and the two are not to be reconciled with any other count of the same run. Who put the card in: nobody in that building has ever said, and nobody has ever been asked. Whether anybody in that building knows either figure: no. What was asked on this seventh Friday: nothing, by a man of about thirty-nine who buys training, who sat at the back and did not ask, and about four people on that floor have noticed that he did not ask and about four have not, and nobody on that floor has ever kept a note of what he asks on which Friday. What is about nine feet away from it: a second box of a different colour and a different make that is not empty most Fridays. *The fourth hand copy, as a record of nine lines written by a man whose hand is not a good hand.* **Where: a shop in the third of the four towns, on the Friday evening, with about four people in it. Who wrote it: a man of about forty-seven who keeps the rent book in that shop, with a pen he holds in his left hand and held wrong, in about nine minutes, on the back of the rent book. Where the book went: back on the shelf behind the till, and about nine people in that town have seen the front of that book and about four have seen the back. What the fourth line says in his hand: that a room that has not sent anything in nine days has not said no. Whether that is the figure that was said out loud in that shop: yes. Which of the four copies carries a different figure in that line: the one on the side of a chopping board in a kitchen in the second of the four towns, which carries four days, and about three people have noticed on their own and about six have not. Whether any two of the four copies has been compared with any other: no, and the four are on four different surfaces in four towns and about four days of bus travel apart in three directions, and none of the four people who wrote one knows the other three exist. Whether anybody told him it was a copy: no. Whether he has been asked to read it back: no, and he has not offered. Owner: a rent book, and nobody.**

*What the day did not touch.* **Nobody read the ninth line in a ring binder and the folded page at the bottom of that binder is where it was on Friday, and the woman's page is the eighth of the eight and is unread. A drawer stayed shut, a pencil box in a street in another district was not opened, and a folder in a weighbridge hut in the working Ashfields was not turned over. The four rooms in four districts of this city have still never met, and the sheet of about nine lines in a room in the second of the four districts is still not on anything and has not been adopted by anybody.
