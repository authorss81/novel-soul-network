# Chapter 488 — A Woman Of About Thirty-One Who Works Nights Saying Out Loud What Four Days Of A Room Has Cost Her, And Nobody Improving On It

**The Monday of week one hundred and eighty was spent at a bench in Lattice Ward until about two and at a counter until about six, and the one thing that is true of it is that a woman of about thirty-one who works nights and used to sit in a room in the third of the four towns at about four in the afternoon between shifts said out loud, once, in a kitchen in that town, exactly what the room coming out had cost her, and she named it in about nine seconds and did not name a person, and about four people in that kitchen heard all of it and about nine did not, and nobody said she was right and nobody said it was a lot and nobody improved on it and nobody thanked her and she was not thanked because she is the one who lost something.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last of them about six in the evening, and his day was a bench in Lattice Ward until about two and a counter until about six, and then a room from about half past six, and a kitchen in a town four days of bus travel away is a kitchen and is not a room and is not a rail and is not a list. The word was at nine and ten and is told to nobody. **Heating: on, dearer, and four names on a lease cost more than they did, and a fortnight a cook laid out is paid for by nobody and is not resolved.** The room off that service road is seven hundred and eighty-four days old and is warm, and about four people came in on the Monday evening for the temperature and none of them was asked anything.

The card in the rail is seven hundred and eighty-eight days old, which is four days more than the room is, and the rail holds two, and a chopping board with nine lines on the side of it in a kitchen in the second of the four towns is a ninth object and is not the card and is not the rail and is not the wall.

---

**The kitchen in the second of the four towns is the back half of a house on a road that goes nowhere anybody in this case has to be, and the woman of about forty-three who runs it has a bed through a curtain and a boiler that works and about six chairs and a table with a burn ring on it.**

About nine people were in that kitchen on the Monday evening at about half past seven, which is not when it is usually full, and about four of the nine are there because of what the woman of about thirty-four said in the other town about a fortnight ago and nobody in that kitchen has been told that is why.

**The chopping board is on the shelf above the boiler and it has nine lines on the side of it and the fourth of them carries four days, and about three people who have seen that line have said out loud that it is not the figure they were told and about six who have seen it have not said anything.**

The woman of about thirty-four who keeps the only written record was in that kitchen on the Wednesday before last and said the nine lines out loud and would not write them down, and she is not in that kitchen this Monday and has not been since, and nobody in that kitchen has asked her why and about four have asked other people and about four have not asked anybody.

**Nobody in that kitchen has been told that the same nine lines were said out loud in two other towns in about a fortnight and that three people have copied them out on three different things, and about four people there have heard that the woman of about thirty-four goes in and out of this city and about nine have not.**

---

The woman of about thirty-one who works nights came in at about half past seven and had been on since about ten that morning and she had about four hours in her and she sat down on the bad side of the split chair because the good side had a coat on it and she did not ask.

She said it once, and what she said was nine seconds long, and what she said was this:

**"I had four hours of somewhere warm with a chair in it and now I have four hours of nothing and it is not the room's fault. I am saying it because somebody should have to hear it and there is nobody to say it to."**

**Nobody in that kitchen said she was right. Nobody said it was a lot. Nobody offered her a room, and nobody has offered her a room since, and about four people in that kitchen have said since that there is nothing to offer and about four have said nothing at all.**

Nobody improved on it. Nobody has improved on it. Nobody thanked her and she was not thanked and she did not wait to be thanked and did not look round the kitchen afterwards, and about four of the about nine have said since that it was a lot to ask of somebody and none of them has said it to her.

**She works nights and she had used that room at about four in the afternoon for about nine weeks and about four of those nine weeks it was the only warm place with a chair in it that she had been in between shifts, and she has said out loud that the second of those is the part that matters and not the first, and about four people in that kitchen have understood that and about four have not.**

She named the cost and she did not name the room and she did not name the woman who said the room was coming out, and about four people in that kitchen have worked out for themselves which room and which woman and about four have not, and the four who worked it out have not said so to anybody.

**That is the whole of the named cost this week carries, and it is one, and it is named by the person who pays it, and she paid it and was not thanked, and nobody in this city knows her name and this case has not gone and asked for it.**

---

The man of about fifty-two who drove a van to that room on Thursdays and had a lift back came into the shop in Lattice Ward on the Monday at about half past four and bought nothing and said that the lift had not been there on the Thursday and had not been there on the Thursday before and that he had walked, and nobody asked him about either Thursday and he did not mention a room.

**Nobody at that counter has ever asked him what he did about the walking and he has not said and about four people at that counter have said since that nine miles in the dark is a thing you do and about four have said nothing.**

The man of about thirty-four who loads bread was in the shop at about three and bought a box of screws and said nothing at all, and the Thursday he agreed to run once has now been and has not been run and nobody at that counter asked him whether it went and nobody ever will.

**Nobody in that shop knows that a woman who works nights in a town four days of bus travel away said a sentence about four hours of nowhere in about nine seconds on the Monday evening, and the man of about fifty-three at that end with a tray did not look up and has never been asked to.**

---

He went to the room at about half past six and did not stay long. A cook of twenty-seven was at the sink saying nothing and about four people came in for the temperature and the middle one filled.

In the chair nearest the door was a woman of about thirty whom nobody asked a question, nobody thanked and nobody apologised to, and the page that is hers is the eighth of eight things in a ring binder on a shelf and is unread and was not read on Monday.

A man of about fifty-one with a tool bag was at the wall and had said nothing for fifty-five weeks and four days. A man of about thirty-three, who holds four buildings and about nine hundred doors with no stamp, no reference and no date, was not in that room and is not in this city, and his hold went on for fifty-nine weeks and four days without anything being asked of him and without anything being given to him.

**Nobody in that room knew that about four people lost something because one room in a town came out of four, and the number of the four is about four and it is not a count and nobody in this city has the names of any of them and this case has not gone and asked.**

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.
