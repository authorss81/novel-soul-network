# Chapter 490 — Four Copies, No Two Of Them Compared, And A Figure Of Four Days Nobody Has Put Right

**The Thursday of week one hundred and eighty was spent at a bench in Lattice Ward until about two and at a counter until about six, and the one thing that is true of it is that there are four copies of nine lines in four towns and no two of them has ever been compared with any other, three of the four carry nine days in the fourth line and one of the four carries four days, about three people have said out loud that the fourth figure is not the figure they were told and about six have not, and the book of four columns in a building in Saltmarket stood at fifty-six lines on the Wednesday and stands at fifty-six lines this morning, and the tin is at seventy-three, and the count is thirty-nine of which thirty-four correspond, and nobody in this city has a list of the four copies and could not produce one if it were asked for.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last of them about six in the evening, and his day was a bench in Lattice Ward until about two and a counter until about six, and then a room from about half past six, and four objects in four towns are four objects and not a document and not a list and not on anything. The word was at nine and ten and is told to nobody. **Heating: on, dearer, and four names on a lease cost more than they did, and a fortnight a cook laid out is paid for by nobody and is not resolved.** The room off that service road is seven hundred and eighty-seven days old and is warm, and about four people came in on the Thursday evening for the temperature and none of them was asked anything.

The card in the rail is seven hundred and ninety-one days old, which is four days more than the room is, and the rail holds two, and a rail of about forty card slots that holds two is not four copies in four towns and is not a list and the two of them are in different buildings about four days of bus travel from three of the four towns.

---

**There are four copies. They are on four different things in four towns and they were made in about a fortnight by four people with ordinary jobs who were each told to write them out themselves by a woman who would not write them down.**

**The first is on the back of a timetable in a school in the fourth of the four towns, written in about nine minutes by a man of about thirty-one who works at a school gate. The second is on the side of a chopping board in a kitchen in the second, written with the back of a spoon handle in about nine minutes by a woman of about forty-three who runs that kitchen. The third is on the back of a hire sheet in a hall in the third, written with a biro in about nine minutes by a man of about thirty-three who works at a weighbridge. The fourth is on the back of a rent book in a shop in the third, written in about nine minutes with a pen held in the left hand and held wrong by a man of about forty-seven who keeps that book.**

**The first of the four carries nine days in the fourth line. The second carries four days. The third carries nine days. The fourth carries nine days.**

**Nobody has compared any two of the four with any other. Nobody has the other three. None of the four people who wrote one has ever met any of the other three and none of them has been told that there are four and none of them has asked. About three people have said out loud that the figure in the fourth line of the one on the chopping board is not the figure they were told, and about six who have seen that line have said nothing, and no two of the nine has told either of the others.**

**The four of them were said out loud by the same woman in about a fortnight, in four rooms, to about four people each time, in the same order at about the same speed from memory, and she would not write them down anywhere and told each of the four rooms to write them out themselves and did not tell any of the four where they came from.**

**Nobody has signed any of the four. The four are on nothing. There is no form, no minute, no register, no list, no box and no wall, and a book of keys in an office above a fourth shop is not a register of the four and has not been used as one and will not be.**

**The ninth of the nine lines is a refusal, and it was turned down in about four seconds by a man of about forty-two who fits kitchens in a room in another district of this city, gave no reason, made the not-giving the condition of it, and was not improved on and was not thanked, and the woman who has said the nine lines out loud four times in four towns is the same woman who keeps the only written record in one of those towns and is not a clerk and holds no post and is owed nothing and has not been thanked.**

**Nobody in this city has found out who wrote the nine lines, because nobody wrote them. They were said out loud by a person who had them from a sheet in a room in the second of the four districts of this city, and the sheet has no name on any of the nine, and one of the nine is the refusal, and nothing has been adopted and nothing has been signed, and the four people who copied them out have never seen that sheet and have never been told where it is.**

Three of the four rooms are rooms. One of the four came out about a week ago in about nine words, said once by that same woman, and about four people lost something by it and one of them named a cost out loud in about nine seconds in a kitchen and was not thanked, and nobody improved on them and nobody has since.

---

**The figure of four days is a real number of days and the figure of nine days is a real number of days and both of them are figures of days and neither of them is a count of anybody and neither of them is a rate and neither of them is on a form, and no sentence in this case may say which of the two is right, because nobody in this case has looked at both of them at the same time and nobody in this case is going to.**

About three people have noticed it on their own and about six have not, and that is three and six of nine people who know one of those kitchens and have seen that line, and it is not a result and it is not a finding and it is not going to be one, and the nine people are not a sample of anything and have not been asked to be one.

**The first disagreement in the fourth line of the nine hand copies of the front of a page with about eleven lines in it, which is right in six copies and wrong in three, has not been found, and about a fortnight ago a different figure was put in a different fourth line on a different object in four towns, and the two are not compared anywhere in any of these ten days and are not the same disagreement and one of them is not going to help anybody find the other.**

---

He went to the room at about half past six and did not stay long. A cook of twenty-seven was at the sink saying nothing and about four people came in for the temperature and the middle one filled and about four went out again inside twenty minutes.

In the chair nearest the door was a woman of about thirty whom nobody asked a question, nobody thanked and nobody apologised to, and the page that is hers is the eighth of eight things in a ring binder on a shelf and is unread and was not read on Thursday.

A man of about fifty-one with a tool bag was at the wall and had said nothing for fifty-six weeks and one day. A man of about thirty-three, who holds four buildings and about nine hundred doors with no stamp, no reference and no date, was not in that room and is not in this city, and his hold went on for sixty weeks without anything being asked of him and without anything being given to him.

**Nobody in that room knew that four copies exist, and nobody in that room knew that one of them has a figure in the fourth line that three people have said out loud is not the figure they were told, and about four people in that room have known the woman of about thirty-four for years and have never once asked her what she says in a room in another town, and she has never told them and is not going to.**

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.
