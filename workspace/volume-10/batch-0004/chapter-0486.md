# Chapter 486 — Two Public People Disagreeing In Public About A Risk, In About Four Minutes, And About Nothing Else

**The Wednesday of week one hundred and seventy-nine was spent at a bench in Lattice Ward until about two and at a counter until about six, and the one thing that is true of it is that he and a woman of twenty-four who is a resonance nurse disagreed in a corridor in the Lower Wards in front of about nine people for about four minutes about whether three rooms in three towns should be told that a fourth room had come out, and she was right and he took it, and the whole of the disagreement was about the risk to the three and not about either of them, and about four of the nine understood what was being said and about five did not, and nobody said one word about either of them to anybody afterwards and neither of them said a word about the other to anybody.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last of them about six in the evening, and his day was a bench in Lattice Ward until about two and a counter until about six, and then a room from about half past six, and a corridor in the Lower Wards with about nine people in it is a corridor and has never been a room. The word was at nine and ten and is told to nobody. **Heating: on, dearer, and four names on a lease cost more than they did, and a fortnight a cook laid out is paid for by nobody and is not resolved.** The room off that service road is seven hundred and seventy-nine days old and is warm, and about four people came in on the Wednesday evening for the temperature and none of them was asked anything.

The card in the rail is seven hundred and eighty-three days old, which is four days more than the room is, and the rail holds two, and a corridor with about nine people in it is not a rail and is not a room and is not a list and nothing in this entry joins them.

---

**The clinic has about nine chairs in it and they are in a corridor outside the room and not in the room, and the corridor has a window at the end of it with a radiator under the window that works, and about nine people were in that corridor on the Wednesday afternoon at about half past three waiting for about four different things.**

She was twenty-four and a resonance nurse and she works out of that clinic and she was about to go into the room and she was in the corridor with about nine other people and him.

He said, first, in about four seconds, that he was going to tell the three rooms that the fourth had come out.

**He said it in a corridor with about nine people in it. He said it to her and not to the corridor and about four of the nine were close enough to hear it and about five were not and nobody has told the five since and nobody is going to.**

She said no. She said it in about nine seconds and she said it once and she did not raise her voice and about four people in that corridor looked up and about five did not.

She said, and this is the whole of her side of it:

**"If three rooms are told that a fourth came out, then every one of those three now knows it can come out, and the next thing anybody in this city says to them is a number, and a number is a thing that is easier to say to a room that has already been told one room left. Nobody is owed a list of who is in. A room that nobody has told anything about is the only kind there is that cannot be counted twice."**

**She was right about that and he took it, and he took it in about four seconds, and nobody improved on her and about four of the nine in that corridor have said since that she was right and about four have said nothing and neither group has said it to her and he has not said it to her either.**

He did not argue the point. He has argued points with people in this case for about nine years and about four of those arguments were about method and two of them were wrong and he took those two as well, and this one took about four seconds and a sentence and he did not offer a reason, because a reason would have been a second argument and she was not going to win one.

**The whole of it was about the risk to three rooms four days of bus travel away, and neither of them said one word about the two of them while it was happening, and the corridor did not become a conversation about the two of them, and about four of the nine who heard all of it have said that what they heard was two people talking about three rooms in a different place and about four have said they could not tell what it was about and the two groups have not discussed it.**

She went into the room at about twenty to four. He went back to Lattice Ward and was at the counter by about half past four and told nobody anything about the corridor and has not been asked.

Nobody thanked anybody. Nobody apologised to anybody. Nobody in that corridor said either of their names and about four of the nine could not have said either of their names if they had been asked.

**Nobody in that corridor knew that the man who was talking to the nurse had once answered a room of about nine people in a town with a figure of about nine, and nobody in that corridor knew that the nurse had been in a room in another district at a counter about a month ago and refused in about nine seconds to write a second number down, and neither of those two facts has ever been in the same sentence in front of either of them.**

**There is a rack of leaflets on the wall of that corridor about nine of them deep** and about four are about a company and none of the four has anything to do with either of the people who were in that corridor, and about nine people in that corridor have taken one in the last year and about four have never taken one and about four have taken four of them over four years and kept them in a bag, and none of that has ever come up in a sentence in that corridor.

**A printed leaflet in a rack in a corridor in the Lower Wards and a sheet of about nine lines in a room in the second of the four districts of this city are two objects in two rooms and neither is on the other, and the leaflet rack is not a list of anybody and the nine lines are not a leaflet and no sentence in this case may put them on one shelf.**

**Nobody in that corridor has been told that three rooms in three towns have each had the same nine lines said out loud in them in about a fortnight and one of the four has come out, and about four of the nine in that corridor have seen the man who was talking go back down the corridor and about five did not look up at all.**

---

He went to the room at about half past six and did not stay long. A cook of twenty-seven was at the sink saying nothing and about four people came in for the temperature and the middle one filled.

In the chair nearest the door was a woman of about thirty whom nobody asked a question, nobody thanked and nobody apologised to, and the page that is hers is the eighth of eight things in a ring binder on a shelf and is unread and was not read on Wednesday.

A man of about fifty-one with a tool bag was at the wall and had said nothing for fifty-five weeks. A man of about thirty-three, who holds four buildings and about nine hundred doors with no stamp, no reference and no date, was not in that room and is not in this city, and his hold went on for fifty-eight weeks and six days without anything being asked of him and without anything being given to him.

**Nobody in that room knew that he had been in a corridor in another district that afternoon arguing about whether three rooms should be told a thing, and nobody in that room knew that she had been right, and neither of them has said a word about the other to anybody and there is no arrangement between them that anybody in this case can point at.**

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.

*The three rooms not told, as a record of a decision that was taken and is not on anything.* **What was decided on the Wednesday afternoon: that three rooms in three towns, four days of bus travel away, would not be told that a fourth room had come out of the four. Who decided: the man of twenty-two, on his own decision and because of an argument he did not win. What was at stake, in the words of the person who raised it: that a room nobody has told anything about is the only kind there is that cannot be counted twice. What exists in writing: nothing, and there is no note of the decision, and it is in the head of one person and has never been in the head of a second. Whether the three rooms know there are three: no, and none of the three has been told that any of the others exists, and the three have each had the same nine lines said out loud in them by the same woman in about a fortnight. Whether the room that came out knows what the other three are doing: no, and nobody has told it and it has not asked. Whether the three were told afterwards: no, and nobody in this case has gone and told them and nobody is going to. Owner: a decision, and nobody.**
