# Chapter 487 — The Eighth Friday Of Ten, Three Hundred And Twenty-Nine Days Of A Post, And A Woman Who Named A Price Out Loud And Was Refused By Nobody

**The Friday of week one hundred and seventy-nine was spent at a bench in Lattice Ward until about two and he was not in Crown Terrace and the shop did not know and would not have known, and the one thing that is true of it is that about eleven people were in a room on a second floor for about nine minutes for the eighth Friday running and the post by the corridor end has now been bare for forty-seven weeks, which is three hundred and twenty-nine days, and a woman of about thirty-nine who lets offices above four shops in the second of the four districts of this city was asked whether the office above the fourth shop could be the place three rooms put their answers, and she said yes before anybody had finished the sentence and then named the cost out loud, once, and nobody improved on it and nobody thanked her.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last of them about six in the evening, and his day was a bench in Lattice Ward until about two and a counter until about six, and then a room from about half past six, and a room on a second floor in another district has a fire door on it that does not shut and has not shut for years. The word was at nine and ten and is told to nobody. **Heating: on, dearer, and four names on a lease cost more than they did, and a fortnight a cook laid out is paid for by nobody and is not resolved.** The room off that service road is seven hundred and eighty-one days old and is warm, and about four people came in on the Friday evening for the temperature and none of them was asked anything.

The card in the rail is seven hundred and eighty-five days old, which is four days more than the room is, and the rail holds two, and an office above a fourth shop is a fifteenth object and is not the card and is not the rail and is not a list of anybody.

---

**The room is the same room. About eleven chairs out of a cupboard and a table at the front and a laptop on it connected to nothing in this case. The chairs were stacked against the wall when the first person came and were unstacked by the first person who came, and that is the eighth week running that two different people have done the two halves of that, and nobody has ever asked which two people those are.**

**The woman of about thirty-six with a lanyard said one word and pointed at the chairs and said nothing else, and about nine minutes went the way they go, and she did not say how long it would be and nobody asked.**

Eight Fridays is not a run. It is eight Fridays, and anybody who wants a run out of it has to wait, and there are two more Fridays after this one of which this case has seen none.

**The post by the corridor end is bare forty-seven weeks, which is three hundred and twenty-nine days from the Friday of week one hundred and thirty-two, and the two boxes at the ends of that corridor are about nine feet apart, and nobody in that building has ever been asked whether the two are a set and nobody asked on this Friday either.**

About eleven people came, and about four of the eleven are on no rota at all, and the figure is a figure and not a rate and is not on a form.

**The man of about thirty-nine who buys training sat at the back for the eighth Friday running and he asked nothing for the second Friday running, and about four people on that floor have noticed the two Fridays together and about four have not, and nobody on that floor keeps a note.**

**The two figures in biro on the back of his left hand are still there, eleven and then nine, and there is still no third one, and nobody has ever asked him for either of them or which room either of them is for.**

**A woman of about thirty-one who works nights has been at the back of eight of those Fridays now and has said nothing on all eight, and about four people on that floor know which eight and have never asked her and have not told her that they know.**

Nobody said thank you. The room emptied in about nine minutes the way it does, and about four people on that floor have said since that the eight Fridays had been the quietest they can remember, and nobody on that floor knows what the quiet was about.

---

The woman of about thirty-nine who lets offices above four shops in the second of the four districts of this city has done that for about eleven years and has a book of keys and a book of rents and has never offered either of them to anybody.

She was asked on the Friday whether the office above the fourth shop could be the one place three rooms put their answers, and the person who asked was a man of about twenty-seven who drives a van in the third of the four towns, and he asked it in about nine seconds and he asked it standing up in a room over a shop in that town and not in writing and not through anybody else.

**She said yes before he had finished the sentence, and she said it in about four seconds, and about four people in that room heard the whole of the question and the whole of the answer and about nine were talking.**

Then she named the cost out loud, once, and she said it after the answer and not before it, and she said it to the room and not to the man.

**She said that it is two evenings a week on the top of a full week. She said that the office above the fourth shop is the only one of the four she lets with a window in it and that the other three are on a landing with no window and that a person asking for a room is going to ask for the one with the window. She said that she is not going to raise the rent on any of the three rooms to pay for it. She said that the three rooms have not been asked whether they can pay anything at all and that she is not going to ask them. And she said that nobody is going to thank her for it and that she is not asking anybody to.**

**Nobody said she was right. Nobody said it was a lot. Nobody offered to do any part of it, and nobody has offered since, and the man of about twenty-seven who asked did not thank her and did not say she was right and has not been back to ask about it and has not said that he would be.**

The man of about twenty-seven who drives a van in the third of the four towns has been in three of the four towns in his working life and has been in none of their rooms, and he said one ordinary sentence after she had finished, which was that he had not expected the answer to be that fast, and about four people in that room have said since that the fastness was the whole of it and about four have said nothing.

**The two evenings a week are on no rota in this city and are on nothing, and nobody in this case has asked her to be on a rota and nobody is going to, and about four people at a counter in another district have said since that a letting office is a strange place for it to land and that it is not strange once you say it out loud.**

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.
