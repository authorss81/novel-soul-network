# Chapter 487 — The Eighth Friday Of Ten, Three Hundred And Twenty-Nine Days Of A Post, And An Office Above A Fourth Shop That Two Rooms Have Been To And A Third Has Not

**The Friday of week one hundred and seventy-nine was spent at a bench in Lattice Ward until about two and he was not in Crown Terrace and the shop did not know and would not have known, and the one thing that is true of it is that about eleven people were in a room on a second floor for about nine minutes for the eighth Friday running and the post by the corridor end has now been bare for forty-seven weeks, which is three hundred and twenty-nine days, and a woman of about thirty-nine who lets offices above four shops in the second of the four districts of this city has been letting rooms come to an office above a fourth shop on two evenings a week since the Wednesday of week one hundred and seventy-seven, and two of the three rooms that are left have been to it, and about four people came to it on this evening and about four have never been told that it exists, and one of the four asked her whether there was any paper in it and she said there was some in a drawer and took none of it out, and nobody improved on it and nobody thanked her.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last of them about six in the evening, and his day was a bench in Lattice Ward until about two and a counter until about six, and then a room from about half past six, and a room on a second floor in another district has a fire door on it that does not shut and has not shut for years. The word was at nine and ten and is told to nobody. **Heating: on, dearer, and four names on a lease cost more than they did, and a fortnight a cook laid out is paid for by nobody and is not resolved.** The room off that service road is seven hundred and eighty-one days old and is warm, and about four people came in on the Friday evening for the temperature and none of them was asked anything.

The card in the rail is seven hundred and eighty-five days old, which is four days more than the room is, and the rail holds two, and an office above a fourth shop is a fifteenth object and is not the card and is not the rail and is not a list of anybody.

---

**The room is the same room. About eleven chairs out of a cupboard and a table at the front and a laptop on it connected to nothing in this case. The chairs were stacked against the wall when the first person came and were unstacked by the first person who came, and that is the eighth week running that two different people have done the two halves of that, and nobody has ever asked which two people those are.**

**The woman of about thirty-six with a lanyard said one word and pointed at the chairs and said nothing else, and the nine minutes went the way the seven of them before them went, and she did not say how long it would be, and nobody has ever asked her, and about four people on that floor have said that she is the only person in the room who has never once been asked anything.**

Eight Fridays is not a run. It is eight Fridays, and anybody who wants a run out of it has to wait, and there are two more Fridays after this one of which this case has seen none.

**The post by the corridor end is bare forty-seven weeks, which is three hundred and twenty-nine days from the Friday of week one hundred and thirty-two, and the two boxes at the ends of that corridor are about nine feet apart, and nobody in that building has ever been asked whether the two are a set and nobody asked on this Friday either.**

About eleven people came, and about four of the eleven are on no rota at all, and the figure is a figure and not a rate and is not on a form.

**The man of about thirty-nine who buys training sat at the back for the eighth Friday running and he asked nothing for the second Friday running, and about four people on that floor have noticed the two Fridays together and about four have not, and nobody on that floor keeps a note.**

**The two figures in biro on the back of his left hand are still there, eleven and then nine, and there is still no third one, and nobody has ever asked him for either of them or which room either of them is for.**

**A woman of about thirty-one who works nights has been at the back of eight of those Fridays now and has said nothing on all eight, and about four people on that floor know which eight and have never asked her and have not told her that they know.**

Nobody said thank you. The room emptied in about nine minutes the way it does, and about four people on that floor have said since that the eight Fridays had been the quietest they can remember, and nobody on that floor knows what the quiet was about.

---

The woman of about thirty-nine who lets offices above four shops in the second of the four districts of this city has done that for about eleven years and has a book of keys and a book of rents and has never offered either of them to anybody.

**She agreed it herself on the Wednesday of week one hundred and seventy-seven, at a counter in this city, after a man there had said no to her in about four seconds, and that was two weeks and two days ago and nobody has mentioned it since and she has not mentioned it either.**

The two evenings a week are on no rota in this city and are on nothing, and nobody has asked her to be on a rota and nobody is going to, and about four of those evenings have been and nobody has a list of which four and neither has she.

**About four people came to that office on this evening and two of them were out of the same room, and nobody who came was out of the room that came out of the four, and that room has never been told that there is an office.**

The man of about twenty-seven who drives a van in the third of the four towns has been in three of the four towns in his working life and has been in none of their rooms, and on the Friday before this one he asked her one question in about nine seconds, standing up, in a room over a shop in that town, and not in writing and not through anybody else.

**He asked whether there was any paper in that office. She said there was some in a drawer. She did not say which drawer and she did not take any out and she has not mentioned paper since, and nobody in this case has asked her for a sheet of anything and nobody is going to.**

**Nobody who has ever been in that office has been told how many rooms there are. About four people know that it is the one place and about nine do not, and nobody has taken a count of either of those two numbers and the two of them have never been in the same sentence in front of anybody.**

Nobody said she was right about anything. Nobody said it was a lot. Nobody offered to do any part of it, and nobody has offered since, and about four people at a counter in another district have said since that a letting office is a strange place for it to land and that it is not strange once you say it out loud.

---

He went to the room at about half past six and stayed longer than he had stayed on the Thursday, and there was nothing in that room that needed doing. A cook of twenty-seven was at the sink saying nothing and about four people came in for the temperature and one of the four stayed about twenty minutes and asked him nothing.

**He thought about the office above the fourth shop for about as long as it took the man of about fifty-one to put a bag down, and he did not think about it for longer than that, because there has been nothing for him to do about it since the Wednesday of week one hundred and seventy-seventh, when a woman he had spoken to once said she would do it herself and he let her say it.**

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.
