# Chapter 493 — Nine Seconds, And A Week Of Two Loads That Is Not Coming Back

**The Thursday of week one hundred and eighty-one was a bench until about two and a counter until about six and then a room, and the one thing that is true of it is that two people in two towns were asked the same question on the same morning and both of them said no, and one of them said out loud what the no would cost her, and he was told none of it and was asked none of it.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last of them about six in the evening, and his day was a bench in Lattice Ward until about two and a counter until about six, and then a room from about half past six, and a haulage firm in the third of the four towns is a haulage firm and is not a shop in this city and nothing in this entry joins them. The rate was at nine and ten, uncorrected, and had been since the spring. **On, and dearer, and the four names on that lease are dearer than they were. The fortnight a cook laid out is not on anybody's account and nobody has settled it.** The room off that service road is seven hundred and ninety-four days old and is warm, and about four people came in on the Thursday evening for the temperature and none of them was asked anything.

The card in the rail is seven hundred and ninety-eight days old, which is four days more than the room is, and the rail holds two, and a rent book on a shelf behind a till in a shop about four days of bus travel away with nine lines on the back of it is not the card and is not the rail and has never been in either.

---

The third of the four towns has about four firms that take a load out of it and about nine vans, and one of the firms is run by a woman of about thirty-seven who has run it for about four years and who has never been asked by anybody in this city what is in it or how much of anything it is.

**She was offered the work on the Thursday morning in a yard, in about nine seconds, by a person with an ordinary job in a company, and the offer was on a form and the form had two columns on it and one of the two columns was the same column that is on nine forms in a drawer in a building in the second of the four districts of this city, and nobody in that yard has ever seen the drawer and nobody in that building has ever heard of that yard.**

The work was about a week. Two loads a day for five days, out of that yard and back, and a driver, and the driver was a man of about forty-five who had done that run for about two years and who was told on the Thursday morning that he would keep it if the form was signed.

**She said no in about nine seconds. She did not raise her voice and she did not put the clipboard down and she did not ask for a moment.**

Then she said what it was going to cost, and she said it once, and it took about nine seconds, and about four of the about nine people in that yard heard all of it and about nine did not.

**"Two loads a day for a week and the driver who does that run, and that is a week, and you do not get the week back after the no, and I am not saying it to make anybody feel better."**

Nobody said she was right. Nobody said it was a lot. Nobody said there would be other work, and nobody offered her other work, and nobody has offered her other work since, and about four people in that yard have said since that there is nothing to offer.

**She was not thanked. She did not wait to be thanked and did not look round the yard afterwards, and about four of the about nine have said since that it was a lot to ask of somebody and none of them has said it to her.**

Nobody improved on it. Nobody is going to. The form went back in the folder and the folder went into a bag and the bag left the yard in the same van it came in, and the man of about forty-five was told on the Friday that the run was not there any more and he has not said anything about it to anybody and about four people in that town know it was not there and about four do not.

**Nobody in that yard was asked to sign anything and nobody signed anything, and the second column of the form that was in it is empty, and it is one of about nine tables in about four places in this city that carry a version of the same form and the second column is empty in every one of them.**

**The van that left that yard at about ten went out with about nine things in it and came back at about four with the same about nine things in it, and about four people in that town who watch that yard have said so, and the person from the company was in the cab of the first one for about four minutes and the folder went in with it and came out with it.**

Nobody in that yard has ever been told that the same week of work was offered to a woman of about thirty-seven in a kitchen in another town in about four days, and she was in that kitchen on the same Wednesday morning, and neither of them has been told about the other and this case has not gone and asked either of them.

**The second column of that form is one field on a piece of paper and it has been carried to about nine tables in about four places in this city and to a rack in a corridor and to a yard, and it is empty in every one of them, and nobody has ever drawn a line under it and nobody has ever been asked what the line would be for.**

She runs about four days of work in that week that she could not take, and about four of the people who would have used the firm in that week went without it, and about nine of the people who work out of that yard have heard the whole of it, and neither group has been asked anything.

---

In the first of the four towns there is a shop that sells about nine things and a man of about forty-seven who keeps the rent book in it, and there are nine lines of handwriting on the back of that book, and about four people in that shop have asked him for the book on two separate mornings and he has not said yes and he has not said no.

**"Third time I'm asking," the man of about thirty-seven said, with his tray up against his chest. "I only want the back of it. I'll bring it back before I open my shop."**

**"No," the man of about forty-seven said, to the tray and not to the shop.**

**"You don't know what I'd do with it."**

**"No," he said again, and laid his hand flat on the cover of the book without opening it, and the woman who works nights, who had been in the shop since before either of them, said afterwards that she had never in her life seen a man refuse a piece of paper and then put his hand flat on top of it.**

**"It's the back," she said. "That's all any of us want. It's the back of the rent book, not the rent book."**

**"It's the same book," he said, and turned the shop sign round so that it said closed, and it was not closed, and the four people who had asked before it went out into the street and stood about it.**

**On the Thursday morning he was asked a third time and he said no in about four seconds. He said it to the person who asked and not to the shop, and he did not put the book down, and nobody improved on him and nobody thanked him, and about four people in that shop have said since that he should have handed it over and about four have said he was right not to and neither group has asked him why.**

The book went back on the shelf behind the till with the nine lines on the back of it and it is there now, and about nine people in that town have seen the front of it and about four have seen the back, and nobody in that shop has been told that there are other nine lines written down in three other towns, and about four people in that shop would not care and about four would, and neither group has said so to anybody.

**Nobody in that shop has ever read the fourth of those nine lines out loud, and nobody in that shop has compared it with any other fourth line, and nobody in that shop has compared it with the fourth line of a hand copy of a page with about eleven lines on it that is right in six copies and wrong in three and has not been found.**

Nobody in this case has ever compared any two of the four hand copies with any other, and the four are in four towns, and none of the four people who wrote one has ever met any of the other three.

---

Nobody in this case has ever asked the woman of about thirty-seven what her firm is called and this entry does not say. Nobody in this case has ever asked the man of about forty-five who lost a run what he thought about it and this entry does not say, and he is owed nothing and nobody in this city knows his name.

**Nobody in this city was told any of it. He was at a bench in Lattice Ward from about half past seven until about two and at that counter from about two until about six, and the shop did not know that a woman in a yard four days of bus travel away had named a week out loud, and nobody in the room off that service road knew either.**

**What he had instead, on the Thursday, was a fortnight of a habit he had not examined: he was the one who said no in about four seconds, and he had said it four times in ten days, and every one of the four had been the same no, and the sameness was not a policy because he had never once decided on it.** He turned the thought over at about five, at the counter, with a bracket in his hand that he had already found once, and then he put the bracket in the bag and gave the bag over, and did not turn the thought over again for a week.

---

He got to the room at about half past six, and he did not stay. A cook of twenty-seven was at the sink saying nothing and about four people came in for the temperature and one of the four was in the chair by the radiator and stayed longer than the other three and said nothing.

In the chair nearest the door was a woman of about thirty whom nobody asked a question, nobody thanked and nobody apologised to, and the page that is hers is the eighth of eight things in a ring binder on a shelf and is unread and was not read on Thursday.

A man of about fifty-one with a tool bag was at the wall and had said nothing for fifty-seven weeks and one day. A man of about thirty-three, who holds four buildings and about nine hundred doors with no stamp, no reference and no date, was not in that room and is not in this city, and his hold went on for sixty-one weeks without anything being asked of him and without anything being given to him.

**Nobody in that room knew that about four days of work in a firm in a town had gone, and nobody in that room has ever been asked what it costs to say no to a company, and the man of about fifty-one in that room has said no to nothing in fifty-seven weeks and one day and has been asked nothing in that time either.**

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.
