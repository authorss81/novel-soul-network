# Chapter 497 — The Tenth Friday Of Ten, And Nine Seconds From A Man Who Works On Lifts

**The Friday of week one hundred and eighty-two was a bench until about two and a counter until about six and then a room, and the one thing that is true of it is that thirty-six hours ran out unused, that a man said no to them in about nine seconds without saying why, and that the one person who could have held all three rooms together was asked to and could not, and that he was in neither building.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last of them about six in the evening, and his day was a bench in Lattice Ward until about two and a counter until about six, and then a room from about half past six, and the door on that second floor has a fire door on it that does not shut and has not shut for years. The rate was at nine and ten and the cage man worked with his back to it. **On and dearer. Four names on one lease, dearer than they were. The fortnight that cook laid out is still on nobody's account.** The room off that service road is eight hundred and two days old and is warm, and about nine people came in on the Friday evening for the temperature and none of them was asked anything.

The card in the rail is eight hundred and six days old, which is four days more than the room is, and the rail holds two, and a sheet of paper with one name on it in a drawer in a van in a town is neither the card nor the rail and has never been in either.

---

The room is the same room it was on the ninth Friday, and it is still eleven chairs out of a cupboard, and a table at the front, and a laptop on it that is connected to nothing in this case. The chairs were stacked against the wall when the first person came and were unstacked by the first person who came, and that is the tenth week running that two different people have done the two halves of that, and nobody has ever asked which two people those are.

**The radiator under the window has now been cold on ten Fridays out of ten, and about four of the about eleven sat away from it tonight, and nobody in that room has ever asked the woman who calls them in why a room with a radiator in it is cold.**

About eleven people came, which is the same figure as the nine Fridays before, and about four of the eleven are on no rota at all, and the figure is a figure and not a rate and is not on a form.

**The woman of about thirty-six with a lanyard said one word and pointed at the chairs and said nothing else, and the nine minutes went the way the nine of them before them went, and she did not say how long it would be and nobody asked her and nobody has ever asked her.**

Ten Fridays is a run and it is not a result, and nobody on that floor has said it is a result, and about four people on that floor have said that ten is a number, and that is as far as it has gone on that floor.

**The post by the corridor end is bare fifty weeks, which is three hundred and fifty days from the Friday of week one hundred and thirty-two, and nobody in that room knows either of those figures, and this figure is a different one from the one that stood in that corridor two Fridays ago and the two are not to be reconciled with any other count of the same run.**

**The card is still in the box and the number on the card is still the number, and the box is at the same height on the same wall, and the second box at the near end of the same corridor is still not empty, and the two are about nine feet apart, and nobody in that building has ever been asked whether the two of them are a set.**

**The man of about thirty-nine who buys training sat at the back for the tenth Friday running and asked nothing at all, and about four people on that floor have noticed that he asks nothing, and nobody on that floor has ever kept a note of what he asks on which Friday.**

**Nobody said thank you. The room emptied in about nine minutes the way it does.**

**The two figures in biro on the back of his left hand are still there, eleven and then nine, and there is still no third one, and he has not washed them off and he has not looked at either of them in that room and nobody has ever asked him for either.**

**Nobody who was in that room on the Friday has ever been in a room in any of the four towns, and nobody in that room knew that a man who works on lifts had said no in about nine seconds about four days of bus travel away that afternoon, and nobody has introduced them and nobody is going to.**

---

The fourth of the four towns has a room over a shop that four people use after a shift and a man of about twenty-nine who works on lifts in that town has been in it about nine times in about two years, and on the Friday afternoon at about two a person from a company came into it with a folder and said that thirty-six hours were left.

**He said no in about nine seconds. He did not say why and he was not asked and nobody improved on him and about four of the about nine in that room heard all of it and about four did not.**

**"Thirty-six hours," the woman from the company said, standing up. "That's the whole of it. One address. You keep the room. You keep your two evenings. Somebody just holds the paper."**

**"No," the man of about twenty-nine said, in about nine seconds, flat, without putting his cup down, and did not say why and was not asked and nobody improved on him and about four of the about nine in that room heard all of it and about four did not.**

**"May I ask why not?"**

**"You may," he said, and did not, and the woman from the company stood there with the folder under her arm for a moment longer than the answer needed, and then went out, and the bell over the shop door has not worked since before either of them started coming, and nobody in that room has ever reported it.**

Nobody thanked him. He did not wait to be thanked. About four people in that room have said since that it was quick and about four have said he should have heard what the offer was first, and neither group has asked him and he has not said and about nine people in that town have heard about it.

**He is owed nothing. He holds no post. He has never copied nine lines out by hand, he has not been asked to, and nobody in that room knows whether he has or not, and nobody in that room has been told that there are four copies.**

The thirty-six hours went at about two the next afternoon. Nothing was sent. Nobody wrote anything and nobody sent anybody, and the one address was not taken, and the second column on the form in the folder is empty, and the folder went back into a bag and the bag went out in the same van it came in.

**Nobody was suspended from anything, because there was nothing to be suspended from. The three rooms are three rooms and the three things are said in three rooms and no address has been given to anybody and no room has been signed out of anything.**

The office above a fourth shop has been letting rooms come to it on two evenings a week for about five weeks and is now being used by about four people who were already coming to it, and about four who had been coming have not come since the Friday, and about four of those four are on no rota at all and have not been on one before and have not been asked to be.

**Nobody was told by anybody that they were not on a rota, and nobody has been given a reason, and the woman of about thirty-nine who lets that office has not asked any of them why they stopped and has not been asked by any of them and has said nothing to any of them about it.**

---

A sheet of paper with one name on it is in a drawer in a van belonging to a man of about thirty-eight who repairs refrigeration in the first of the four towns, and he put it there on the Friday morning, and he said yes in about four seconds when he was asked, and nobody asked him why and nobody improved on him and nobody thanked him.

**"Four weeks," the woman from the company said. "Then it moves. It goes in a drawer and it comes out when the four weeks are up."**

**"Yes," the man of about thirty-eight said, in about four seconds, and wrote his name on the sheet in the cab of his own van, and put the sheet in the drawer, and shut the drawer, and the key to that drawer stayed in his pocket, and he has not told anybody in that town which pocket, and nobody has asked him.**

**The name on that sheet is his and it is the only name on it, and it stays there for four weeks and then it comes out and goes to somebody else, and nobody in that town has been told whose name it is and nobody in this city knows it, and a sheet of paper in a drawer in a van is not a rota and a drawer is not a registry and a name in one head is not a list.**

Nobody has been told how he would find out that something had gone wrong, and a woman of about twenty-five who washes up in a kitchen in another town said on Wednesday that there was no way, and nobody has carried that anywhere, and the man of about thirty-eight who repairs refrigeration has not been told that she said it and about nine people in that town have not been told either.

---

He was asked, on the Friday, by a person who has not been named to anybody, whether three rooms in three towns could be asked to do one thing and would do it because he asked. He said yes and then he heard the rest of it, and the rest of it was that one of the three had already said no and had not said why and was not going to be asked, and the two others had not been asked at all.

**"No," he said. "I can't."**

**"You can. You're the only one of the three of us who could."**

**"One of them's stopped," he said. "That's it. That's the whole of it. One of them's stopped and I'm not going round it, and if they want me they can ask me again after they haven't gone round it, and if they still want me then, fine."**

**"That's not what I asked you."**

**"No," he said, and what he wanted to say next, and did not say, was that he had been the one holding it up since he was nineteen and that putting it down was the first thing he had ever been able to do that nobody would later call a favour, and the person who asked him looked at him for a second longer than was comfortable on either side and then let it go.**

**He said it in about nine seconds, and the reason was not the reason anybody expected, and the reason was that the one room had stopped, and a room is allowed to stop, and that was the second of the three things said out loud on the Monday a week before. Nobody improved on him. Nobody said it was a pity. Nobody thanked him, and the person who asked has not asked him anything since and about four people have said since that he could have gone round the room that stopped and about four have said that going round it is the whole of what is not allowed, and neither group has said it to him.**

For about the length of a cup of tea, and no longer, he could have done it. Three rooms, three towns, and a man in this city who could hold a thing up across all three of them at once while it ran.

**He has thought about that cup of tea more since than about anything else in the ten days, and not with any pride in it at all, and the part he keeps coming back to is that for the length of the tea he was the useful one. There was a shape in front of him that was exactly his shape, and it had his hands in it, and it would have worked, and the reason it was his shape is that nobody in any of the three rooms had ever asked to be held up and the asking would have had to start with him.** He did not want it. He has not been able to explain to himself, in nine days, why not, and he has stopped trying, and the not-explaining is the first thing he has done in a long time without deciding to.

**Then one room had stopped and he could not, and that is not a thing that was taken from him and it is not a cost anybody named and it is not in any record, and about four people in this city have said since that he was the wrong person to ask and about four have said he was the only person anybody would have asked.**

He reached the room at about half past six and did not stay. A cook of twenty-seven was at the sink saying nothing and about four people came in for the temperature and the middle one of the four said the word warm and said it to the room and not to him.

There was a woman of about thirty in the chair nearest the door whom nobody asked a question, nobody thanked and nobody apologised to, and the page that is hers is the eighth of eight things in a ring binder on a shelf in that room, and it is unread, and it was not read on the Friday either.

A man of about fifty-one with a tool bag was at the wall and had said nothing for fifty-eight weeks and two days. A man of about thirty-three, who holds four buildings and about nine hundred doors with no stamp, no reference and no date, was not in that room and is not in this city, and his hold went on for sixty-two weeks and one day without anything being asked of him and without anything being given to him.

**He had been at the bench in Lattice Ward from about half past seven until about two and at that counter from about two until about six on the Friday, and the shop did not know that a man had said nothing at all in a room on a second floor in another district, and nobody in the room off that service road knew that a sheet of paper with one name on it was in a van in a town, and neither building knows that the other one has a board or a box in it.**

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.
