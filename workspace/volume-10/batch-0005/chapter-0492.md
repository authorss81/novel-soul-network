# Chapter 492 — A Proposal With A Deadline On It, And A Form With Two Columns

**The Wednesday of week one hundred and eighty-one was a bench until about two, a counter until about half past two, and then a room, and the one thing that is true of it is that a company put a form with a second column on it on a table in a room above a fourth shop and gave it until the Friday morning, and that a woman who lets that office read it twice and then said one sentence.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last of them about six in the evening, and his day was a bench in Lattice Ward until about two and a counter until about two and a half, and then a room from about half past six, and he was not in the office above the fourth shop and has never been in it. The rate was at nine and ten and it went on the wall list and nowhere else. **Heating on, dearer than the quarter before, and four names on a lease dearer than they were. The fortnight that cook laid out is on nobody's account and has not been settled.** The room off that service road is seven hundred and ninety-three days old and is warm, and about nine people came in on the Wednesday evening for the temperature and none of them was asked anything.

The card in the rail is seven hundred and ninety-seven days old, which is four days more than the room is, and the rail holds two, and a printed form on a table in an office above a fourth shop is neither the card nor the rail and nobody has ever put one in the other.

---

The office above a fourth shop has two rooms and a kettle and a table and about nine chairs and a drawer, and the drawer is where the paper is, and the paper is about nine forms with a company mark on them and a second column on all nine that has never been filled in by anybody in this city.

**There is a woman of about thirty-nine who lets that office and who agreed on a Wednesday about two weeks ago that three rooms could bring their answers there on two evenings a week, and two of the three have been, and the third has not and has never been told it exists.**

She was in that room on the Wednesday morning at about eleven and she read the proposal through once and then again, and about four people were in it, and the person who had put it there sat with a folder and did not say a word while she read it the second time.

The company is not new to this case. Its training materials go out to four towns under four names on the same envelope, and about eleven people a week in this city sit in a room on a Friday afternoon in a company building, and nobody in that shop has ever been told what happens in that room and nobody in that shop has ever been asked.

**The offer, in what a person with an ordinary job in a company said out loud, was this. One address. One place. Everything three rooms decide goes there and comes back out of there, and it comes back out faster than it would go between three towns on a bus that goes twice a week, and there is no charge for it, and there has never been any charge for anything.**

**The price was the form. One column for what the room has decided this week. One column for whether the room still wants to be doing this at all, and the second column is the one that matters and the second column is a yes or a no to a room that has never been asked.**

The man with the folder put a finger on it and did not push it across.

**"It isn't a merger," he said. "Nothing merges. You keep the room. You keep the two evenings. Somebody just holds the paper."**

**"Whose paper," she said.**

**"Ours to start with. Then whoever is holding the week, and that rotates, and the name of whoever it is goes in the first column."**

**"And the second."**

**"The second is four words," he said. "Whether you still want to be doing this. People put it in and it works. That is the whole of what we are asking anybody to do."**

**"That is a yes or a no to a room that has never been asked," she said, and did not say anything else, and picked the pages up and squared them and put them back down in the same order.**

Nobody in that room said the word covenant. Nobody in that room said the word merger. Nobody in that room said anything at all for about four minutes after that, and about four of the about nine people who have been in that office have said since that the four minutes were the whole of the morning and about four have said it was longer.

The woman of about thirty-nine said one sentence about it and then did nothing else about it at all.

**"I have a drawer for the paper and not a room for the answer, and if somebody wants to put a room in my drawer they can come and do it themselves."**

**Nobody thanked her. She was not thanked. She did not wait to be thanked and did not look round the room afterwards, and about four people in that building have said since that it was a lot to refuse and none of them has said it to her, and about four others have said that a drawer is a drawer and neither group has spoken to the other about it.**

She put the nine forms back in the drawer with the second column still empty, and about four people in that building have said that the drawer has had them in it for about a year and a half and about four have said it was longer, and nobody has asked her and she has not said.

**Nobody filled in either column of any of the nine forms. Nobody has ever filled in either column of any of them, and about nine tables in about four places in this city carry a version of the same form, and the second column is empty in every one of them, and nobody has drawn a line under it.**

---

**Nobody asked him to speak for the three rooms and he was not asked to. What he was asked, on the Wednesday, by a person who has not been named to anybody, is whether the three rooms would do better with one answer than with three, and he said no in about four seconds, gave no reason, made the not-giving the condition of it, and went back to Lattice Ward and was at his bench by about one, and nobody improved on him and nobody thanked him.**

He was not told the content of the offer and was not asked for it and did not ask for it. About four people in that office have said since that he would have been the obvious person to ask and about four have said that asking him would have been the way to lose it, and the two have never spoken about it and are not going to.

**There were four seconds in the answer and the four seconds were not hesitation. What he was doing in them was working out that the only reason he was being asked was that he was the person in this city who would be believed, and that being believed about three rooms he had never been inside was a thing he could do in one sentence and could not undo, and so he said no, and gave no reason, and has not given one since, and it is the only refusal in this batch that he has never once wished he had made differently.**

He got back to Lattice Ward at about one and ate something standing at the bench, and the unit from the night before was still on the mat where he had left it, and he looked at it without turning a screw.

**Nobody in that room in the second of the four districts was told that the three rooms have never met each other, and nobody in that room has ever been told how many rooms there are, and the two rooms that have been to that office do not know the names of each other and have not asked.**

**About four people who have been to that office in about five weeks have said since that it is the only room anybody has offered them and about nine have said that they had not been asked, and about four of the first set have said that they would go somewhere else if it were closer, and about four have said they would not, and neither group has been asked where the somewhere else would be.**

The two rooms that have been to that office have been on two different evenings and have not been in it on the same evening, and the woman of about thirty-nine who lets it has not arranged that and has not tried to, and about four people in that building have said that she could and none of them has said so to her.

**The office above a fourth shop is a room with a drawer in it. It is not an office in the sense of a department and it is not on a rota in this city and it is not a registry, and nobody who has ever been in it has been asked to fill anything in, and nobody has.**

---

Four hundred and one replies are in a company's postbox in a file somewhere in this case and about nine of them got an answer, and a minute somewhere says about nine again, and neither of those figures is a count of anybody and neither of them is a rate and neither of them is on a form, and the nine forms in a drawer in a building in the second of the four districts have never been in the same room as either of those figures, and nobody has put them together.

The envelope the four names go out under is one envelope. About four people in four towns have seen that envelope and about four in each town have not, and nobody in this city has a list of the four names and could not produce one if it were asked for, because a company has a list and the list is the company's and not anybody's here.

---

He went to the room at about half past six and stayed a little longer than he had stayed on the Monday, and there was nothing in the room to be done about any of it and he did not do anything about any of it.

**He thought about the second column for about as long as it took the man of about fifty-one to put his bag down, and not longer, and then he stopped, and about four people in that room have said since that he looked at the door for a while, and about four of the eleven have not noticed him at all, and neither of those two groups has ever said anything to him.**

A cook of twenty-seven was at the sink saying nothing and about four people came in for the temperature and about four went out inside nine minutes.

In the chair nearest the door there was a woman of about thirty whom nobody asked a question, nobody thanked and nobody apologised to, and the page that is hers is the eighth of eight things in a ring binder on a shelf, and it is unread, and it was not read on Wednesday.

A man of about fifty-one with a tool bag was at the wall and had said nothing for fifty-seven weeks. A man of about thirty-three, who holds four buildings and about nine hundred doors with no stamp, no reference and no date, was not in that room and is not in this city, and his hold went on for sixty weeks and six days without anything being asked of him and without anything being given to him.

**Nobody in that room knew that nine forms with a second column on them had been on a table about four days of bus travel away that morning, and nobody in that room has ever been asked what is in a second column, and the cook of twenty-seven at that sink was not asked.**

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.

*Conditions and docket.* **Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last of them about six in the evening, and his day was a bench in Lattice Ward until about two and a counter until about two and a half, and then a room from about half past six, and he has never been in the office above the fourth shop. The shop: nine feet of counter, a bench, a parts cage and the man in it who works with his back to the room all day, a shutter, a tool roll with a fourth pocket, a wall list in a plastic sleeve, a shelf of finished work along the far wall, and about forty card slots along the front of the counter, of which two hold a card. People who came to that counter: about four, all four of them told no to something. The day's work: eleven things, four of them no, and nothing was posted and nothing was collected off the shelf. *The offer, as a record of one place offered in exchange for one column.* **What was offered: one address and one place, and anything three rooms decide goes there and comes back out of it faster than it would go between three towns on a bus that goes twice a week, and no charge for it. What was asked for: one form with two columns, filled in by every room every week, the first for what the room has decided and the second for whether the room still wants to be doing this at all. When it had to be answered by: Friday morning of the following week. Who read it: a woman of about thirty-nine who lets an office above a fourth shop, twice, with about four people in the room and a person from the company sitting with a folder and saying nothing. What she said, once, in one sentence: that she has a drawer for the paper and not a room for the answer, and that somebody who wants to put a room in her drawer can come and do it themselves. What she did next: nothing. Whether anything was signed: no. Whether any column of any of the nine forms has been filled in: no, and none has ever been, and about nine tables in about four places in this city carry a version of the same form with the second column empty. What the company is: a company whose training materials go out to four towns under four names on the same envelope, and it has a list of the four names and the list is the company's. Whether he was asked to speak for the three rooms: no, and what he was asked was whether three rooms would do better with one answer than with three, and he said no in about four seconds and gave no reason and was not thanked and nobody improved on him. Whether the two rooms that have been to that office know each other: no, and neither knows the name of the other and neither has asked. Owner: a drawer, and nobody.**

*The second column, as a record of a field nobody has ever filled in.* **What it is: the second of two columns on a form with a company mark on it, and it asks a room whether it still wants to be doing this at all, and the first of the two columns asks what the room has decided this week. Where the forms are: nine of them in a drawer in an office above a fourth shop, on about nine tables in about four places in this city, on about four hands on a rack in a corridor in another district, in a yard in a town four days of bus travel away, in about nine buildings a man of about twenty-nine who works on lifts can get into, and on the back of a woman's own card somewhere in this case. How many times it has been filled in: never, by anybody, in this city or in any of the four towns. Whether anybody has drawn a line under it: no. What it was offered for: one address and one place, quicker than three towns and a bus that goes twice a week, and no charge for it, in exchange for every room filling in both columns every week. Whether the offer had a deadline: yes, Friday morning, and it passed at nine on that Friday with the second column empty. Whether a company has a list of the four names on one envelope: yes, and the list is the company's and is not in this case. Whether any count of anybody appears on the form: no. Owner: a column, and nobody.**

*What the day did not touch.* **The ninth line in the ring binder went unread again on Wednesday, and the folded page at the bottom of that binder is where it was when the binder went back on the shelf, and the woman's page is the eighth of the eight and is unread. One drawer remained shut, the pencil box in a street in another district was not opened, and the folder face up in a weighbridge hut in the working Ashfields was not turned over. The four rooms in four districts of this city have still never met, and the sheet of about nine lines in a room in the second of the four districts is still not on anything and has not been adopted by anybody, and the four hand copies of it in four towns are on nothing and have not been introduced to each other. The nine hand copies of the front of a page with about eleven lines in it are past their fifty-second week, and about eight of the nine are still unfinished, and the first disagreement in the fourth line of those has not been found, and a figure of days in the fourth line of a timetable or a chopping board or a hire sheet or a rent book is a different fourth line and is not the same disagreement and is not compared with any of them anywhere. There are nine keys wanted and two in existence, and the first of the nine is still not a key anybody can ask back for. The nine doors on that road on the far side of the city are still on one hinge apiece and no mender is paid for, and the week about eleven people lost is not back with them. A bar is still across the inside of a door in each of four buildings in four towns, on nothing, in no list. The leaflets in a rack in a corridor in the Lower Wards are still in their rack, and the second column under every one of them is empty. The fortnight a cook laid out is still on nobody's account. No sitting this week at the Exchange; the book is at fifty-six lines and the tin has had nothing put in it. Nobody died. Nobody thanked anybody. Entry 495.**
