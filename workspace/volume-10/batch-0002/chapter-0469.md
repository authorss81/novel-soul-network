# Chapter 469 — No, And Four Answers That Do Not Know Each Other

**The Wednesday of week one hundred and seventy-four was spent at a bench in Lattice Ward until about two and at a counter until about six, and the one thing that is true of it is that the fourth of the four rooms said no, in about nine words, and did not answer the question at all, and the woman who carried the question was not asked it again, and the four answers now stand at eleven, about nine, who is asking, and no, and no two of the four rooms knows that the other three exist and nobody has introduced them.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last of them about six in the evening. The word was at nine and ten and is told to nobody. **Heating: on, dearer, and four names on a lease cost more than they did, and a fortnight a cook paid for is paid for by nobody and is not resolved.** The room off that service road is seven hundred and forty-four days old and is warm, and about four people went in on the Wednesday evening for the temperature and none of them was asked anything.

The card in the rail is seven hundred and forty-eight days old, which is four days more than the room is, and the rail holds two, and four answers in four towns are on four pieces of paper and in four mouths and none of them is on that rail and none of them is in this city.

---

The fourth room is at the back of a hall on a Thursday and about four people are in it and about nine come in a year.

The woman of about thirty-four who keeps the only written record in one of the four towns went to it on the Wednesday, and she said the eleven words, and she said them in the ordinary way a person says a thing they have said three times that week.

**It said, in about nine seconds, in about nine words: no. Nobody counts us and we are not numbers.**

She did not argue. She did not ask it a second time and she did not ask a different question instead, and about four people who were in that room have said since that the woman was not rude and was not trying to be clever, and about four people in it have said that they would have said the same thing.


The room is at the back of a hall and the hall is used for something else on a Wednesday and the room is the room you get when you take the corridor past the kitchen, and about four people are in it and the other about nine of the year are the same four.

**The person who spoke for that room is not its secretary and holds no post and has never been asked to speak for it and was speaking because the woman was standing in the doorway and the question was asked at her and not at anybody else.**
**Nobody said she was wrong. Nobody said the room was being difficult. Nobody said it would come round. Nobody wrote down what the room said and nobody repeated it to anybody, and the woman who heard it has not told the other three rooms and has not been asked to and will not.**

---

The four answers are as follows and they are given here in the order they were made and the order is not a ranking and nobody chose it.

The first is eleven, and not nine, in one line, in one hand, on a card the size of a nail box, from a room four feet of shop deep with a door that sticks and about eleven people in it on a Wednesday evening.

The second is about nine of us, one room, Wednesday evenings, only, in about nine words, from a room above a kitchen, said by somebody who has read those words off a folded sheet in a drawer and has not counted anybody.

The third is who is asking, nobody has said who is asking, in about nine words, from a room at the end of a corridor in a school, and it is the only one of the four that is a question.

The fourth is no, nobody counts us and we are not a number, in about nine words, from a room at the back of a hall on a Thursday, and it is the only one of the four that is a refusal.

**One of the four counted. One of the four read. One of the four asked. One of the four said no. The only one of the four that anybody in this case can say is right is the one that did not count, and the only one of the four that has never produced a figure at all is the one that said no.**

---

**No two of the four rooms knows that the other three exist. Two of them have spoken, in one direction only, and the other two have not spoken to anybody. Nobody has introduced them. There is no list, no sheet, no address, no envelope, no telephone number held by anybody who has called it, and there is no column anywhere for four.**


There is one woman who has been to three of the four and she has kept the only written record in the town she lives in for about two years, and the record is a hardback notebook with about nine things in it that are about that room and not about the other three.

**None of the three rooms is in that notebook. About four people in that town have asked her whether she wrote any of it down and she has said she wrote down what happened in her own room and nothing else, and that is true.**
There is one woman who has been to three of the four, and she is not a clerk and holds no post and is owed nothing, and she has not been thanked and has not been asked to keep doing it and has not said she will.

**Nobody in this city knows that four rooms exist, and nobody in this case is going to put them together, and the not-putting-together is not a rule anybody has written down and it is not a decision anybody has taken, it is what happens when four rooms are told a question by a person who was told it by a room.**

---

He was at a bench in Lattice Ward from about half past seven until about two and at a counter from about two until about six and about nine people came to that counter and about four of them were told no to something.

Nobody at that counter knows that a room at the back of a hall said no on the Wednesday. Nobody at that counter was told and nobody asked and the docket says nothing about it, and the docket is the only account of the day and it is not sent to anybody.

**Nobody at that counter was asked to do anything about the four, and if he had been he would have said no in about four seconds, and there is no evidence in this case that he would have been and none is going to be manufactured.**

He went to the room at about half past six and did not stay long. A cook of twenty-seven was at the sink saying nothing and about four people came in for the temperature and the shutter on that service road came down at about ten.

In the chair nearest the door was a woman of about thirty whom nobody asked a question, nobody thanked and nobody apologised to, and the page that is hers is the eighth of eight things in a ring binder on a shelf and is unread and was not read on Wednesday.

A man of about fifty-one with a tool bag was at the wall and had said nothing for about fifty weeks. A man of about thirty-three, who holds four buildings and about nine hundred doors with no stamp, no reference and no date, was not in that room and is not in this city, and his hold went on for about fifty-three weeks and six days without anything being asked of him and without anything being given to him, and it is going to go on.

**The woman of about thirty in the chair nearest the door has been in that room on every one of the ten days of this fortnight and has not been asked a question on any of them, and the page that is hers is the eighth of eight things in a ring binder and it is unread, and the fortnight the cook of twenty-seven laid out is on nobody's account and has not been mentioned in that room since the last time it was mentioned, which is not this week.**

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.

The hall at the back of that building is used on a Wednesday for something that about four people come to and about four people do not, and the room at the end of the corridor past the kitchen is not that thing and has never been that thing.

**Nobody has ever asked the room why it meets, and it does not meet for a reason anybody has written down, and about four of the four people in it have given four different reasons and about four of them have said it is about the room.**

About four of the nine who came in said nothing and stood by the door and then left, and nobody asked them why.

**Four rooms in four towns and four rooms in four districts are eight rooms and not four, and the two sets have never met each other and are not compared anywhere in this record and are not going to be.**

The middle one is the one that fills and about four people came in on the Wednesday evening for the temperature and about four went out again, and the shutter on that service road came down at about ten and the light over the bench in the other building was the only light on that street he could see.

Nobody in that room knew that four rooms in four towns had been asked the same eleven words and had answered four different things, and about four people in that room have said since that rooms are all the same, and the four of them have been in four different towns and have not met.

**Nobody has written the four answers down anywhere in this city. They are on a card in a town, in a mouth in a town, on a piece of paper in a town and in the memory of a woman who keeps one record, and not one of the four is in this case's own hand, and this record is not a place any of them is written down.**
