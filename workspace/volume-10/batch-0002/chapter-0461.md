# Chapter 461 — One Question In Four Rooms, And Eleven Where The Sheet Said Nine

**The Monday of week one hundred and seventy-two was spent at a bench in Lattice Ward until about two and at a counter until about six, and the one thing that is true of it is that the same question was put to four rooms in four towns in the same eleven words, by four people with ordinary jobs, none of whom knows the other three exist and nobody having introduced them, and the first of the four answers came off the morning bus with eleven on it where the only number that has ever been written down about any of this says nine, and a woman of about thirty-four who keeps the only written record in one of those four rooms said out loud, once, that carrying the question to three rooms cost her two days of work and a bus fare she is not going to get back, and nobody improved on it and nobody thanked her.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last of them about six in the evening. The word was at nine and ten and is told to nobody. **Heating: on, dearer, and four names on a lease cost more than they did, and a fortnight a cook paid for is paid for by nobody and is not resolved.** The room off that service road is seven hundred and twenty-eight days old and is warm, and about nine people came in on the Monday evening for the temperature and none of them was asked anything.

The card in that rail is seven hundred and thirty-two days old, which is four days more than the room is, and a card's age does not stop because a card has been picked up and does not start again when a card has been put down, and the rail holds two, and the slip of paper that came off the bus that morning is a ninth object and is not the card and is not the wall and is not the sheet that came before it.

---

The question is eleven words long. It is: how many of you are there, and who is it that you would like to be able to say it to.

There is no room on it. There is no name on it. There is no town on it and no place-name with a spelling on it, and it is not on any paper this city has ever handled, and nobody in this city has ever seen it before the Monday of this week.

**It got to four rooms because a room in one of the four towns had been asked something in almost the same words, and somebody in that room told a room in the next town what the question was, and that room told the next, and the next told the last, and nobody introduced anybody to anybody at any point in that.**

**The question travelled. The number did not. That is the whole of what happened in four towns over about four days and it is the reason the four answers disagree with each other, and nobody in any of the four rooms has any way of knowing that the other three exist.**

A woman of about thirty-four kept the only written record in one of those four rooms. She is not a clerk and holds no post and is owed nothing by anybody and has not been thanked by anybody in this case and is not going to be.

She asked it in her own room on a Sunday afternoon, which is the one day the room is shut, and then she carried it to three other rooms herself, on foot and on a bus that goes twice a week, and she did it on a Sunday and a Monday and gave up a shift on the Monday.

**She named the cost out loud, once, to about four people, and it was that it cost her two days of work and a bus fare she is not going to get back, and she said it as a fact and not as a complaint, and nobody improved on it and nobody said she was right and nobody has asked her about it since.**

She did not say the question was a good idea. She did not say it was a bad one. She has never heard of the man at the bench and does not know that the number about nine came out of this city and she would not be interested if she did, and nobody in this case has told her and nobody is going to.

---

The first of the four answers came off the bus at about ten on the Monday, and it was not folded, and it was about half the size of a folded sheet of paper, and it was on a piece of card the kind that comes in a box of nails.

**It said eleven, and it said not nine, and the not nine was written in the same hand and in the same line and there was no gap between them.**

Nobody brought it in. Nobody signed for it. Nobody asked who had brought it, and about four people at that counter handled the pile it arrived on and none of them looked at it until about eleven because it was face up and because it was small.

The room that wrote it is a room with four feet of shop behind it and a door that sticks, and it has a kettle in it, and about eleven people are in it on a Wednesday evening. It has never seen a sheet of paper from this city. It has never seen a number about itself. It was asked a question and it counted.

**It is the first of the four answers and it is the only one of the four that had to count anything, and the counting is the reason its answer is different from the others, and nobody anywhere has put those two facts next to each other.**

---

He read it at about eleven and then read it again at about half past two with the light on it and the parts cage man working with his back to the room the whole of both.

The only number that has ever been written down about any of this is the nine. It is on the back of a folded sheet in about nine seconds of handwriting and it came back four days later as four hundred of them with a second line under it saying four hundred rooms, and neither of those two was ever put back on the same paper as the nine.

**Eleven and nine are on two objects in two towns and no sentence has ever joined them, and a person holding both of them is a person this case has made and not a person anybody in either town is.**

He said nothing about it to anybody. He did not write it down. The docket is the only account of the day and it is not sent to anybody and it has no column for what a piece of paper is about.


The eleven words are the same eleven words in all four places and they are not the eleven words on the ninth line of the sheet, and the two sets of eleven were arrived at separately by people who have never spoken and never will.

**The question on the ninth line of that sheet asked how many. The eleven words ask how many and who it is that you would like to be able to say it to, and the second half of them is the part that nobody in any of the four towns has ever heard before.**

Nobody added the second half as far as anybody in this case knows. The woman of about thirty-four who says the eleven words in three of the four rooms says she heard them whole and has never asked where the whole of them came from, and nobody has ever asked her either.

The first room counted. It has four feet of shop behind it and a door that sticks in the wet and a kettle and about eleven people in it on a Wednesday evening, and nobody helped it count and nobody was standing in the room while it did it.

**It took about four minutes and it did the count twice and the second count gave the same figure, and nobody in that room has said out loud that it was done twice, and one of the eleven has since said so to a person who has not repeated it.**

It wrote the figure on a piece of card about the size of a nail box and it wrote the word not after it in the same line, and it did not write a date and it did not write a name and it did not put it in an envelope, because there has never been an envelope in that room.

**A woman of about twenty-three with a ring on a chain came in at about half past three and asked whether a drawer in a house two streets away was open, and she was told no in about four seconds, and she did not say which street and nobody asked her and she has not been back.**
---

A man of about thirty-four who loads bread came in at about four and asked whether the four rooms were the four towns.

He asked it well. He said he had four vans and that the four towns were the four places his vans went and that if the four rooms were in the four places then there was a thing he could carry for them and he wanted to know before the weekend.

**He was told no in about four seconds, with no reason, and no condition, and no version that said come back and ask somebody else.**

There was no reason given. There was no suggestion that somebody else might know. There was no request to write it down instead, and there was no form, and the man did not ask twice and did not say why he had asked.

Nobody at that counter enjoys it. There is nothing in this case to enjoy and nobody at that counter takes anything off anybody.

**He said, once, at the end of the counter, to the counter and not to the man, that he had asked because he would have done it for nothing, and that nobody had said he could and nobody had said he could not, and that that was the part he had not expected.**

Nobody said that was generous. Nobody said that was not the point. Nobody named a person who should have asked him and nobody named a person who should have answered him, because there is nobody in this case who can be named as the person to ask.

**He went back to his van and the four vans went to the four places on the Thursday and he told nobody anything and he has not mentioned it since and is not going to.**


Nobody said that four vans going to four places is a network and nobody said that it is not one either, and the four places are places his vans go and not rooms anybody has introduced him to, and he has never been inside any of the four and he was not asked to be.

**He had said that he would have done it for nothing and that nobody had said he could and nobody had said he could not, and both halves of that are the same half, and he has not worked that out and nobody is going to work it out for him.**

About four people at that counter between about two and about six did not know any of it had happened. A man of about fifty-three was at that end with a tray and did not look up, and the parts cage man worked with his back to the room the whole of the afternoon and would not have seen it and would not have said anything if he had.
---

He went to the room at about half past six and sat at the end of the second bench and did not stay long.

A cook of twenty-seven was at the sink saying nothing. About four people came in for the temperature and about four went out again inside twenty minutes and the kettle went on twice and one cup was made and it was not made for anybody in particular.

In the chair nearest the door was a woman of about thirty whom nobody asked a question, nobody thanked and nobody apologised to, and the page that is hers is the eighth of eight things in a ring binder on a shelf and is unread and was not read on Monday.

A man of about fifty-one with a tool bag was at the wall and had said nothing for about forty-seven weeks and five days, and nobody had renewed the asking and nobody had stopped it.

A man of about thirty-three, who holds four buildings and about nine hundred doors with no stamp, no reference and no date, was not in that room and is not in this city, and his hold went on for about fifty-one weeks and four days without anything being asked of him and without anything being given to him, and it is going to go on.

**Nobody in that room knew that a question had been put to four rooms in four towns over four days, and about four people in that room have since wondered how many rooms there are, and none of them has asked anybody and nobody in this case is going to tell them.**

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.

About four of the nine who came in said nothing at all and stood by the door for about nine minutes and then left, and nobody asked them why and nobody has ever asked anybody in that room why they came.

**The bench at the end of the second one is the bench he sits on and it is the only bench anybody in this case has ever been asked to move, and nobody asked him to and nobody is going to.**

The room is four rooms and a back one and the middle one is the one that fills, and about nine people came in on the Monday evening and about four of them were the same four as the Monday before and about four of them had not been in it in a month, and nobody has ever kept a list of which.

A cook of twenty-seven was at the sink saying nothing and washed about nine things and made no cup, and the shutter on that service road came down at about ten, and nobody in that room knew that eleven had come off a bus in the morning and gone back into a pocket on the Tuesday.

**About four people in that room have said since that a room like that one is a good thing and about four of them have said since that it is not going anywhere, and neither of the two has been said to the other and neither has been said out loud.**

*The question, as a record of eleven words that got further than the number did.* **What it says: how many of you are there, and who is it that you would like to be able to say it to. How long it is: eleven words. What is not on it: a room, a name, a town, a place-name, a date, a signature, an address, and a sender.
