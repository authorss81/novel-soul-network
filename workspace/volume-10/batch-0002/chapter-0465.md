# Chapter 465 — The Second Answer, And The Nine Words Coming Back Word For Word

**The Monday of week one hundred and seventy-three was spent at a bench in Lattice Ward until about two and at a counter until about six, and the one thing that is true of it is that the second of the four rooms answered in about nine words and the nine words were the nine words that had gone out of this city on the back of a folded sheet, word for word, in the writing of a man at a bench, and the room that gave them back had not counted anybody and had read them off a piece of paper in a drawer and nobody thanked anybody.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last of them about six in the evening. The word was at nine and ten and is told to nobody. **Heating: on, dearer, and four names on a lease cost more than they did, and a fortnight a cook paid for is paid for by nobody and is not resolved.** The room off that service road is seven hundred and thirty-five days old and is warm, and about nine people came in on the Monday evening for the temperature and none of them was asked anything.

The card in the rail is seven hundred and thirty-nine days old, which is four days more than the room is, and the rail holds two, and the folded sheet that is in a drawer in a kitchen four days of bus travel away is a ninth object and is not the card and is not the wall and is not the page of about eleven lines and is not the master's original and is not the wall list in its plastic sleeve.

---

The second room is above a kitchen in one of the four towns, and it meets on a Wednesday evening, and there are about nine people in it, and it has never been asked anything by anybody with a job.

It is the only one of the four rooms that has ever held the sheet.

**The sheet is in a drawer. It came off a bus in this city in a folded state, it was written on in the middle of the back of it in about nine seconds, it went back on a bus, and it came back four days later with two lines on the front of it in a different hand, and it is now in a drawer in a kitchen four days of bus travel away and it has been out of that drawer about nine times.**

The eight lines on the front of it are a list a room made for itself. The ninth line is a question. The back of it has nine words in the middle of it, and those nine words are about nine of us, one room, Wednesday evenings, only.

**And the two lines that came back on the front of it in a different hand say four hundred of them, and, in a second line underneath, four hundred rooms.**


The drawer it is in is the second drawer under a table in a kitchen and it is not locked and there are about nine other things in the drawer that are not it, and nobody in that kitchen has ever asked why a piece of paper is in with the string and the fuses.

**About nine people have read that sheet and about four of them have read it more than once, and one of those four has read the back of it and not the front and thinks the whole thing came out in a burst of handwriting rather than three people at a month apart.**

There is no second copy of it anywhere. There is no carbon and no photograph of either state of it and there is no way in that town to find out what is on the front of a piece of paper that four people have read.
About nine people have read that sheet. Nobody in this case has any way of knowing how many, and nobody in that kitchen knows how many other people have read it either.

---

It was asked the same question. Eleven words, the same eleven words, put to it by a person with an ordinary job who had been told the question by a room that had been told it by a room.

It answered in about nine seconds, in about nine words, and what it said was:

**"About nine of us, one room, Wednesday evenings, only."**

**That is word for word what was written on the back of a folded sheet at a bench in this city about a month ago, in the handwriting of the man at the bench, and it has come back to a town four days of bus travel away in the mouth of somebody who has read it, and neither of those two things is a mystery and both of them are things nobody in this case can explain.**

Nobody in this city was told. The sheet went out folded and it came back with two lines on the front of it and nobody at that counter knew where it had gone and nobody has ever been told and the man who wrote on the back of it has not been told that it has been read four days of travel away by about nine people.

**He read a card about the size of a nail box on the Monday before this one that said eleven and not nine, and he has had it on a shelf under the lamp since, and the card and the sheet are on opposite sides of a road and nothing in this case has put them together and nothing in this case is going to.**

---

**The second of the four answers agrees with the number. That is the whole of what it does and it is the only one of the four that does it, and agreeing and being right are two different things and on this Monday they happened to be the same thing and nobody will ever be able to say which.**

The room has about nine people in it. That is what about four people who have been in it will say, and nobody has counted, and the figure is in a drawer and on the back of a piece of paper and in one mouth.

Nobody in that room has been asked to count. Nobody in that room was told that anybody wanted a number. The person who asked the question waited about nine seconds and wrote down what was said and neither of them knows that the nine words came out of this city.

**The first room counted and said eleven. The second room read and said nine. They are in two towns and neither of them has heard of the other, and the second one is the only one of the four that is right, and it is right because of a piece of paper and not because of anything anybody in it did.**


The person who put the question in that room waited about nine seconds and wrote down what was said and put a full stop after it, and did not ask it a second time, and did not say where the words had come from, and was not asked.

**Nobody in that room knew that the nine words had been written by anybody. Nobody in that room knew that they had been written anywhere at all, and one of the about nine has since asked another of the about nine where the sentence came from, and the answer was that it was always the sentence.**
---

He did not write the nine words down. He did not write the eleven down. The docket is the only account of the day and it has no column for what a thing in another town said.

About nine people came to that counter between about two and about six. About four of them were told no to something, and one of the four was told a thing could be had on Thursday and not before, and that went on the docket as well.

The man of about fifty-three was at that end with a tray and did not look at the card on the shelf and has never asked what is on it and is never going to.

**Nobody thanked anybody, and about four people at that counter over the day have said since that two rooms cannot both be right, and neither of them has ever been to either of the rooms and neither of them is going to.**

He went to the room at about half past six and did not stay long. A cook of twenty-seven was at the sink saying nothing and there is a fortnight on nobody's account that has her handwriting on the envelope and nothing else.

In the chair nearest the door was a woman of about thirty whom nobody asked a question, nobody thanked and nobody apologised to, and the page that is hers is the eighth of eight things in a ring binder on a shelf and is unread and was not read on Monday.

A man of about fifty-one with a tool bag was at the wall and had said nothing for about forty-eight weeks and five days. A man of about thirty-three, who holds four buildings and about nine hundred doors with no stamp, no reference and no date, was not in that room and is not in this city, and his hold went on for about fifty-two weeks and four days without anything being asked of him and without anything being given to him.

**The separation is four months and twenty weeks old on that Monday, and it is not shorter and it is not ended, and the figure is counted from its own chain of figures and not from anything that happened in a kitchen four days of bus travel away, and the cook of twenty-seven at that sink has not been asked about a fortnight that she paid and nobody has paid back.**

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.

The room above that kitchen has a window that does not open and about nine chairs and a table with a cloth on it that has been there longer than anybody can remember, and the about nine who meet there on a Wednesday have met there for long enough that nobody has to be asked twice.

**Nobody in that room has ever been asked where the words came from and about four of the about nine could not say if they were asked tomorrow, and one of them could and has not been asked.**

About four of the nine who came in said nothing and stood by the door and then left, and nobody asked them why.

**Eleven and nine are on two objects in two towns and the nine words are on a third object in a fourth place, and the three have never been on a table together and are not going to be.**

The middle one is the one that fills and about nine people came in on the Monday evening for the temperature and about four went out again, and the cook of twenty-seven washed about nine things and made no cup and did not say anything.

**Nobody in that room knew that nine words had come back word for word from a kitchen four days of bus travel away, and about four people in that room have said since that a number is a number, and the two of them have not been in the same conversation and never will be.**
