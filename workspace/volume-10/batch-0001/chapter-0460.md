# Chapter 460 — Nine Minutes Alone In A Lift, And About Four Seconds At A Counter

The Thursday of week one hundred and seventy-one was spent at a bench in Lattice Ward until about two and at a counter until about six, and the one thing that is true of it is that **a man of about twenty-nine who works on lifts asked to have somebody on the other end of nine minutes in a lift in a building in one of the four towns, and was told no in about four seconds with no reason and no condition, and said out loud that he would do it alone and that he had not planned to, and nobody improved on it and nobody thanked him.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last of them about six in the evening. The word was at nine and ten and is told to nobody. **Heating: on, dearer, and four names on a lease cost more than they did, and a fortnight a cook paid for is paid for by nobody and is not resolved.** The room off that service road is seven hundred and twenty-four days old and is warm, and about four people came in on the Thursday evening for the temperature and none of them was asked anything.

The card in the rail is seven hundred and twenty-eight days old, which is four days more than the room is, and the rail holds two, and it is in a building in this city and the lift is in a building four days of bus travel away and nothing on that Thursday joined the two of them.

---

He came in at about three with a clipboard he did not put down and did not open.

He put his weight on the wrong side of the counter and moved it back without appearing to notice, and he was not the only man who has done that at that counter and about four people a day do it.

**Nine minutes at the counter, and the bus at about six, and the whole of the conversation fitted inside the nine minutes with about a minute to spare, and he had done the arithmetic on the way in and knew it.**

He works on lifts. He has been doing it since he was about nineteen. He came off the bus that morning and he was going back on the one at six and he had about nine minutes at the counter before he had to be at the stop, which is exactly as long as it took.

He said it in the order a person says a thing they have already said to themselves.

He said the roof first and the car second and the four hours third, and the order was not the order he would have used if he had not already said it to himself, and about four people at that counter have noticed the order of things and none of them has said so.

He said he had asked two other people before the bus and both of them had said a different thing, and he gave no names and was not asked for any, and the two of them were not in that shop and never have been.

There is a lift in a building of about nine flats in one of the four towns. It is old. On Monday he is going to be on the roof of it for about two hours, and he will be out of sight, and if the car stops with him in it he will be in it until somebody notices, and somebody notices on a Monday in a building of about nine flats when the lift does not come back, and that is about four hours, and it might be longer at six in the evening.

**He works on lifts and has done since he was about nineteen and he is about twenty-nine, and he came off the bus that morning and he was going back on the one at six, and the whole day was arranged around a lift in another city.**

**He asked whether there would be somebody on the other end. He did not say a name and nobody in that shop has ever been asked for a name by a person with a job.**

**He asked it as a job question and not as a favour, and that is the whole reason it was answerable in four seconds, and a favour would have taken longer and would have got a different answer.**

Nobody named anybody. Nobody said that somebody in another building might be able to, and nobody said that a phone would do, and nobody at that counter owns a phone that anybody has ever been shown.

**Nobody has ever written down the address of that building and it is not on the docket and the docket has no column for it, and there is no way on earth to find that lift from anything written down in this city.**

He was told no in about four seconds.

There was no reason. There was no condition, and there was no version of it that said come back on another day, and nobody said that somebody else could be found, because that would have been an offer and there is no version of this counter on which anybody is offered.

---

He did not argue for about nine seconds, which is longer than the answer took.

The nine seconds were about the shape of a man deciding whether to be angry, and about four people at that counter have seen that nine seconds happen in front of them before and have not seen it end the same way twice.

**He did not raise his voice and he did not put the clipboard down and he did not say the word please, and the not-saying of please is not a thing anybody at that counter noticed at the time.**

Then he said, at the end of the counter, once, and he said it to the counter and not to the man:

**"All right. Then it is nine minutes on my own and then it is four hours on my own, and I had not planned on either."**

Nobody said that it was a risk he should not take. Nobody said that a lift man does that every week. Nobody said that somebody would notice sooner than Monday, and about four people at that counter have wondered since whether somebody would notice sooner than Monday, and none of them has ever asked him.

**Nine minutes alone on a roof and about four hours alone in a car, and both figures were named by the man who was going to have to spend them, once, after the answer, and not before it.**

**A fourth thing was also not said, which is that the answer was right, and nobody at that counter has any standing to say that a thing is right, and the standing is not a rule and is not written anywhere.**

**He had asked two other people before the bus and got a different answer from each of them and had come about four days of travel to ask a third, and the third was the shortest of the three and there was no reason on earth for that to be a coincidence.**

**He was not thanked. He was not repaid. He put a bar across the inside of the door of a room in a different building in a different week of another year, and nobody at that counter connected the two things then and nobody is connecting them now, and the two objects are not one object and are not to be described as one.**

He left at about ten to six to be at the stop.

**He got the bus, and about four people in a kitchen four days of travel away were not told that a man had been asked and refused, and the man who asked has not mentioned it to any of them, and will not.**

The shutter on the service road was half down by the time he had gone and the light over the bench was on, and about four people came in for the temperature between about six and about half past six, which is later than they usually come on a Thursday.

---

The twelve lines on a hardboard in a room off that service road are six hundred and forty-four days old on that Thursday, and six hundred and forty-four is the day the eighteen lines were written on that same board. **The eighteen lines are four hundred and forty-two days old on the same day, and four hundred and forty-two is the day the twelve were written.** Both figures are ages and not dates, and each of them is also the day a different line went on that board, and a person reading either of them is holding a day and an age at the same time and the two are about the same piece of hardboard and not about a lift in a building of about nine flats.

He went to the room at about half past six. A cook of twenty-seven was at the sink saying nothing. In the chair nearest the door was a woman of about thirty whom nobody asked a question, nobody thanked and nobody apologised to, and the page that is hers is the eighth of eight things in a ring binder on a shelf and is unread and was not read on Thursday.

A man of about fifty-one with a tool bag was at the wall and had said nothing for about forty-seven weeks and one day. A man of about thirty-three, who holds four buildings and about nine hundred doors with no stamp, no reference and no date, was not in that room, and his hold went on for about fifty-one weeks without anything being asked of him and without anything being given to him.

**The man of about thirty-three is not in that room and is not in this city, and the fifth week of his hold began on the Monday, and about nine hundred doors in four buildings went through that week with nobody's authority behind anything that happened to them.**

**The man of about fifty-one put his tool bag down at about half past six and picked it up again at about ten, and between those two times it was not on the floor, and nobody has ever asked where it went, and it went where it always goes and that is not in this case.**

Nobody in that room knew that a man of about twenty-nine had been refused in about four seconds that afternoon, and nobody in that room was asked and nobody in that room is owed anything by anybody in it.

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.
