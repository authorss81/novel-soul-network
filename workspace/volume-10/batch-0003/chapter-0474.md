# Chapter 474 — The Fifth Friday Of Ten, Forty-Three Weeks, And A Question Nobody Answered

**The Friday of week one hundred and seventy-five was spent at a bench in Lattice Ward until about two and he was not in Crown Terrace and the shop did not know and would not have known, and the one thing that is true of it is that the post by the corridor end has now been bare for forty-three weeks, which is three hundred and one days, and about eleven people were in a room on a second floor for about nine minutes, and the man of about thirty-nine who buys training asked one question for the first time in a fortnight and it was about the post and nobody answered it.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last of them about six in the evening, and his day was a bench in Lattice Ward until about two and a counter until about six, and then a room from about half past six, and nobody in that shop knew that anybody had asked a question on a second floor in another district. The word was at nine and ten and is told to nobody. **Heating: on, dearer, and four names on a lease cost more than they did, and a fortnight a cook paid for is paid for by nobody and is not resolved.** The room off that service road is seven hundred and fifty-three days old and is warm, and about four people came in on the Friday evening for the temperature and none of them was asked anything.

The card in the rail is seven hundred and fifty-seven days old, which is four days more than the room is, and the rail holds two, and a black post box on a wall on a second floor in another district is a different box from a second one at the near end of the same corridor and the two are about nine feet apart and nobody in that building has ever been asked whether they are a set.

---

The room is the same room. About eleven chairs out of a cupboard and a table at the front and a laptop on it connected to nothing in this case. The chairs were stacked against the wall when the first person came and were unstacked by the first person who came, and that is the fifth week running that two different people have done the two halves of that, and nobody has ever asked which two people those are.

About eleven people came, which is the same figure as the four Fridays before, and about four of the eleven are on no rota at all, and the figure is a figure and not a rate and is not on a form.

**The woman of about thirty-six with a lanyard said one word and pointed at the chairs and said nothing else, and about nine minutes went the way they go, and she did not say how long it would be and nobody asked.**

Five Fridays is not a run. It is five Fridays, and anybody who wants a run out of it has to wait, and there are five more Fridays after this one of which this case has seen none.

The post by the corridor end is bare forty-three weeks, which is three hundred and one days from the Friday of week one hundred and thirty-two, and nobody in that room knows either of those figures.

**The card is still in it and the number on the card is still the number and the box is at the same height on the same wall, and the second box at the near end of the same corridor is still not empty, and nobody in that building has ever been asked what either of them is for.**

**The man of about thirty-nine who buys training sat at the back for the fifth Friday running and he asked one question, and it was the first he had asked in a fortnight, and what he asked was what the number on the card in that box is for.**

Nobody answered him. The woman of about thirty-six with the lanyard said the one word and pointed at the chairs and said nothing else, and about four people on that floor have wondered what the number is for for years and none of them had ever asked.

**He asked it in about nine seconds and he did not ask it twice, and about four people in that room heard it and about seven did not, and nobody has told the seven since and nobody is going to.**

**The corridor goes past both boxes and past a window at the end of it, and about four people have looked at the first box while walking to that window and about four have looked at the second one, and neither group has ever looked at the other one at the same time.**

**The number on that card has not changed for as long as anybody on that floor can remember, and about four of them have said, to each other and not to anybody else, that whatever the number is, it cannot be a count of people, because it has not moved.**

**The two figures on the back of his left hand are still there, eleven and then nine, and he did not write a third one on this Friday, and he has not looked at either of them in that room, and nobody has ever asked him for either and nobody has ever asked him why he wrote them down at all.**

---

The first of the four towns has a school in it, and the room at the end of a corridor in that school has about nine chairs in it and a table and a wall with nothing on it, and it is the room that asked who is asking, in the same eleven words, in the same fortnight as the other three.

There were about four people in it on the Friday afternoon at about four o'clock, which is earlier than the room usually meets, because a heating in that building has been off since the Wednesday and nobody has said so to anybody.

A woman of about twenty-nine who works at that school and who is not a teacher said it once, and what she said was nine words long, and what she said was this:

**"Nine minutes and nine seconds are the same thing."**

**She said it to about four people and then she said, to nobody in particular, that somebody was going to have to ring somebody about the heating and that she was not going to be the one, and about four people in that room have said since that they were glad it was not her.**

Nobody in that room wrote it down. Nobody in that room wrote anything down on that Friday, and about four people were holding hot water in paper cups because the heating had gone off on the Wednesday and nobody had rung anybody until about four o'clock on the Friday.

The woman of about twenty-nine counted the cups out of a cupboard and put one on the table in front of each person and did not say whose was whose, and about four people in that room have said since that the room has never had cups in it before and that the woman of about twenty-nine put them there because the room had no way of saying that it was cold.

**Nobody in that town knows that about nine rooms in four towns have said the same nine words in a fortnight, and this room does not know that the three other rooms it was asked the same question as do not know it exists either, and nobody has introduced them and nobody is going to.**

**A heating in a school in the first of the four towns is not the heating in a building in this city that went off on a day this case has counted, and no sentence in this case may put the two on the same sheet, and this is not the same thing.**

---

He went to the room at about half past six and did not stay long. A cook of twenty-seven was at the sink saying nothing and about four people came in for the temperature and the man of about fifty-one had his tool bag at his feet.

In the chair nearest the door was a woman of about thirty whom nobody asked a question, nobody thanked and nobody apologised to, and the page that is hers is the eighth of eight things in a ring binder on a shelf and is unread and was not read on Friday.

A man of about fifty-one with a tool bag was at the wall and had said nothing for about fifty-one weeks and two days. A man of about thirty-three, who holds four buildings and about nine hundred doors with no stamp, no reference and no date, was not in that room and is not in this city, and his hold went on for about fifty-five weeks and one day without anything being asked of him and without anything being given to him.

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.

**Nobody in that room knew that a post on a second floor in another district has been bare for three hundred and one days, and nobody in that room knew that about four people on that floor had wondered for years what the number on the card in it is for, and one of them asked on the Friday and was not answered.**

**A woman of about thirty-one who works nights and a woman of about thirty in a chair nearest a door in another district are two people and not one, and the two have never met, and a man of about fifty-one at a wall in a room off a service road is a third and not a fourth.**

**A man of about thirty-nine at the back of a room in a second district has said nothing to the man at that counter and does not know he exists, and the campaign that is being said in about nine rooms on the far side of the road has never once been said to him, and nobody in this case has any way of finding out that it is being said at all.**
