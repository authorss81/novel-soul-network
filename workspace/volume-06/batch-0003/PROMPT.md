## THE MEASUREMENT AND THE TWO CEILINGS

**The two ceilings are the bold emphasis ceilings: hold the batch average at or under 10.75 per thousand and every chapter at or under 12.4. Only the bold figure is measured against them. The hedge figures are printed whatever they are and no ceiling is set on either anywhere in this repository, and this pass is not one that gets to invent one.**

**Movement I closed at 9.20 and Movement II at 7.79, and the batch that has to work hardest is this one, because the midpoint chapter is a chapter about a recording of a person saying four things, and a chapter like that is exactly the shape that a writer fills with bolded speech until the emphasis means nothing.** The way to hold it is fewer and longer speeches and not fewer words, and the rule that worked in both previous movements is the one to reuse: **the interrogative frame and the connective replies go plain, and the payload keeps the span.** A chapter in which a woman's voice is transcribed for eleven minutes should transcribe it and let it be plain, and bold the one sentence that changes the argument, and bold nothing else in that chapter. **Do not spend the budget on the recording. Spend it on the four lines around it.**

**THE MATCHERS ARE PRINTED BEFORE A WORD IS WRITTEN, because three passes of Movement I published a figure without one and the fourth had to withdraw all of them.** Words are whitespace-separated tokens, comparable with `wc -w`. A bold span is one match of `\*\*(?!\s)(.+?)(?<!\s)\*\*` read a line at a time, so DOTALL is never in play. The three cross-checks are `raw.count('**') // 2`, the same regex with `re.S`, and a non-overlapping `find('**')` pair count; all four must agree span for span on all ten finished files, and if they do not the table is wrong and not the prose. **Raw hedge is one match of `\babout\b`; prepositional is one match of `\babout\s+(?:it|what|the|which)\b`; true is raw minus prepositional. All three are case-sensitive, and the case-insensitive reading must be printed beside them, because a reader is entitled to know how many sentence-initial capitals the case-sensitive reading excludes.** Movement II's figures for comparison, all reproducible: **212 bold spans in 27,230 words at 7.79 per thousand, worst chapter 10.89; hedge 537 raw, 56 prepositional, 481 true at 17.66, and 566 raw / 56 prepositional / 510 true on a case-insensitive reading.**

**THE EIGHT-TOKEN OVERLAP IS PRINTED WITH ITS MATCHER AND WITH BOTH BOUNDARY DEFINITIONS, because Movement II found that two of them were in circulation and that only one of them was the house one.** The overlap is the number of **distinct eight-word shingles shared between a load book and the body prose above it.** Normalisation: delete every `**`, lower-case, split on whitespace. No punctuation is stripped and no italic frame heading is removed. **Definition A, the one Movement I published, is the text after the last `---` separator line in the file. Definition B, which is the correct one for this manuscript's form, is the text from the italic line that opens the entry — `*274. **Nothing in the narration is bolded.** All emphasis is inside speech or inside a load-book entry.
