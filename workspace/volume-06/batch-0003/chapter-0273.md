# Chapter 273 — The Thing Nobody Can End

The Monday of week ninety-five was the Monday after the Friday, and it was the morning a family of two women and a mother of seventy-nine came into a cold room off a service road to say that a care-link had worked for about four years and could not be ended by anybody in that family, and neither of them was thanked and neither of them was described as a case and neither of them became a resource, and about nine people in that room wanted to make it into a thing and one of them said so out loud and was told no.

---

Ten on the docket and nine in date, and a job he did not go to and did not reassign and did not tell anybody he had not gone to.

The word was at nine and ten. The heating in that room was on its ninety-first day and it is a hundred and ninety-six days old.

---

They came in at about eleven and they had not telephoned, and one of them had worked out on a bus why they had not telephoned, and she said it in the doorway before she came in, and nobody had asked her.

The elder is thirty-nine and the younger thirty-six and they are sisters and they live about four streets apart. Their mother is seventy-nine, is at home, cannot manage the stairs after dark, and has been at home since the spring before the one before last.

Nobody in that room asked for a name and neither of them gave one.

---

The care-link is a thing a person pays for and a company sends somebody to do. It is a telephone that is answered at night by a person who is not in the house, and who comes if it is rung and cannot be rung after about eleven at night on a Sunday.

It has been in place about four years. It cost about nine pounds a month when it started and about eleven now.

The younger one had a sheet of her own paper with the payments on it. She did not take it out.

---

She said the whole of what she had come to say, and it took her about four minutes, and it was not a complaint and she said twice that it was not a complaint.

"We pay it. She would not be at home without it. In the spring before the one before last she was in a ward for eleven weeks and the ward told us to expect her home in about three days and we could not manage three days. That is the entire history of it and there is nothing else anybody would want to know."

"**Then what have you come to ask.**"

"We have not come to ask anything. We have come because it is Monday and it is warm in here."

Somebody said, "**It is not in here.**"

"It is not in here, " she said, " and I know. I have been in four rooms in this city in nine years where a person was told the temperature was not a matter and in three of the four the person was right. I am not here about the temperature and I would like it noticed that I am not here about the temperature."

---

The elder one said the other half of it and said it slowly, and about four people in that room understood what it was before she had finished the first sentence.

"The thing we cannot end is not the care-link. She can end the care-link. She has a telephone in her hand and there is a number on a card in a drawer and she could ring it on a Tuesday and there would be no charge and nobody would come."

"Then what cannot be ended."

"Everything after that. If it stops then she is seventy-nine and at home and on her own at night, and the only thing that changes is that we find out about it about four days later, and we find out about it because somebody rings the district, and there is no job in this city whose business that is. I have looked. I looked for about four weeks before I came here."

---

A cook of twenty-seven, from a sink, without turning round, said the thing that has been said in that room in about four shapes over two volumes.

"**About four years is not a thing anybody can do with a telephone.**"

Nobody improved on it and nobody thanked her for it and she has not said a fifth and has said four.

---

A first-year of nineteen asked one question and it was the only question anybody asked all morning, and it was the right one, and it was asked at about half past eleven to the older of the two and not to the man of twenty-two.

"**Are you two alright.**"

"We are the two of us and we are in here on a Monday morning and we are not here to be helped, so yes."

"**That is not what I asked.**"

"I know what you asked. We are all right in the way that matters today and we will be all right on Thursday and I do not know what we will be in about four years and neither does she, and that is the thing we have come to say, and you have it now and we are not going to be asked anything else."

---

The man of twenty-two did not ask a question, and he had one, and he did not ask it, and the reason he gave afterwards, on a service road, standing up, in the wind, was that she had told the room what she had come for and that she had said twice that nobody was going to ask her anything else, and that she was the fourth person in about a term to say that in that room and that he was not going to be the one who did it anyway.

"**I have wanted to ask somebody in this case for about four months who rings a person whose care-link has stopped. I have got as far as wanting to. I have not got as far as asking and I am not going to describe wanting as asking.**"

---

At about one the man of about thirty-four who buys training was in that room for about nine minutes on his way to a building in Crown Terrace, and he said the only sentence he said, and he said it to the two of them and not to anybody else.

"**I buy training for a company and I have been trying for about two years to find out who signed off a thing that is on about nine hundred cards, and nobody will tell me, and I stopped asking and I did not stop noticing, and the reason I am telling two strangers that on a Monday is that it is the same shape as their thing and I have never found anybody else with the shape.**"

The younger of the two women said: "**Then you have found one and we are not going to help you, because if there were nine of us you would have nine of us in about four years and we would be on a page.**"

"**That is exactly right and I would have said it myself in about four days.**"

Nobody thanked him. He is owed nothing. He was not thanked for a sentence and the two of them were not thanked for being in the room.

---

At about half past three a woman of twenty-four was in that room and had been for about an hour and she said four sentences and all four of them were about her own position.

"**A care-link is not a service in this city. There is no service, there is a company, and the company is right that it works, and nobody has ever written down anywhere what happens on the morning somebody cancels one, and the reason nobody has written it down is that the person who cancels it is the only person who could tell you and the person you would have to ring to find out is the person who would not have been rung.**"

Nobody asked her a fifth question. Nobody said that the two women had been lucky. Nobody said that about nine families in this city were in the same position and nobody counted them, because counting them is a page and a page is what she was describing.

She is Talia Venn and nobody in that room said her name and she did not give it to anybody.

---

At about seven there were about nine people in that room and a woman of about thirty was in the chair nearest the door with a bag on her lap and had been since about two, and a cook of twenty-seven put a cup of tea in front of her and said nothing at all, and about four people in that room wanted to ask her why she comes and nobody did.

The two sisters left at about half past four. Nobody asked them whether they would come again and nobody said that they should, and about four people in that room wanted them to come again and about four of them had a reason that would have been about four pages.

He wrote one thing on a service road, in the wind, at about ten.
