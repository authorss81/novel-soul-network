# Arithmetic and calendar — Volume 06

**`workspace/volume-06/ARITHMETIC-AND-CALENDAR.md`, at the volume root, beside `batch-0001/` and not inside any phase directory. It is a volume-level working file and not a phase: it holds no `PROMPT.md` and no marker, so the controller's selection rule — the first directory under `workspace/` holding a `PROMPT.md` and no `.done` — is unaffected by it. `workspace/volume-04/ARITHMETIC-AND-CALENDAR.md` and `workspace/volume-05/ARITHMETIC-AND-CALENDAR.md` are the precedents and sit in the same place.**

**This file exists from Chapter 251 onwards, not from the volume close. That is the point of it.** Four state files and five batch prompts in Volume 05 pointed at a Volume 05 calendar that did not exist, the Volume 05 close had to build one after the fact, and the seventh document in this repository to name a missing file was a hand-off. A Volume 06 writer gets the table on the first day of the volume and the volume close will extend it, not invent it. **The volume close owns the sections marked as its own below and no writing pass may write them.**

**It is a table and a set of series. It is not a prompt, it plans nothing, and it derives nothing.**

**Four rules, and they are the same four as Volume 04's file and Volume 05's file.**

1. **No day number may be printed in a chapter.** No month-name, no month-date, no day-date, no year. The only calendar nouns permitted are the season words, the week and the day inside the week, and the two inherited years from `chapter-0053.md` and `chapter-0056.md`, which stand and are not extended.
2. **A day is assigned only by the live batch prompt's day map, which is fixed and used and is not to be derived.** Where this file and a batch prompt disagree on a day, **the batch prompt is right for its ten days and this file is right for the run**, and a batch prompt that disagrees with the table below on a day is a defect to be corrected in place and recorded, as Volume 05's Movement V prompt was.
3. **Weeks run Monday to Sunday. Week 88 = Mon 502 – Sun 508. Week 89 = 509–515. Week 108 = Mon 642 – Sun 648.** Volume 05 ended on the Wednesday evening of week eighty-eight. Volume 06 opens on the Monday morning of week eighty-nine and closes on the Wednesday morning of week one hundred and eight.
4. **The interval map's two-day offset from the Monday of week thirty-one is inherited, is recorded for the thirteenth time, and is not to be re-derived from the anchor. The Thursday of week 36 is day 141 and not 139, and that correction stands. No chapter in this manuscript has ever printed a day number, so no prose depends on any of it.**
