# Chapter 294 — Three Districts

The Monday morning of week one hundred and two was the morning a civic sequence ran in three districts for reasons that were correct under a rule that had been restricted a fortnight before and not repealed, and nobody in this case knows who ran it, nothing arrived from anybody, nothing was announced and nothing was minuted, and the only finding anybody in this case got was a count, and the count was four, and none of the four has ever heard of the other three.

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date. Four jobs attended, two of them in districts this case has not worked in, and the man with a clipboard in a building in Crown Terrace did not ask him on the Monday because the man was on leave.

The word was at nine and ten. The heating in that room is on and it costs more. The room is two hundred and thirty-eight days old.

---

He found the first one at about eleven in the morning because he was standing in a car park in a district this case has not been in with a torch in his hand.

The ventilation plant in that car park runs on a photocell and a clock and a signal from a node. At eight o'clock on a Monday morning it should be off, and it has been off every weekend for about nine years, and on that Monday morning it had been running since about eleven the previous night, and there was no fault on it and there was no fault code and there was nothing broken.

He stood under it for about four minutes and then he went and asked the caretaker, who was sixty-one, and who said, before he had finished the question, that yes, she had run it, on the Friday, on the telephone, and that it is what you do when the node is not answering, and that she had not thought about it since and had two hundred other things.

---

He found the second one at about half past two in a stairwell in a second district, where a stair light that had been off since the middle of the afternoon on the Friday was still on on the Monday, and a lift that had been parked on the roof since the Friday was at the ground floor and would not come up when it was called.

He found the third one at about four in a third district, in a lobby, where a door that is held released on a signal had been shut and locked at about six in the morning and was still shut.

Three districts. Nothing broken anywhere. Nothing alarming anywhere. Nothing announced.

---

He asked one question in each of them, the same question, to a competent person: who ran it.

The first answer was one person. The second answer was one person. The third answer was two people, because neither of them would say which of them had done the half that mattered.

The count was four.

Not one of the four has ever heard of the other three. They work in three districts, they have three different employers, two of them are on no list and two of them are on a list that belongs to a company and is not ours, and there is no body in this city that they are all members of, and they were not chosen and they did not choose each other and there is nothing to join.

The finding, then, was a count, and it was four, and it was not a name, and nobody in this city is going to be the person who ran it on a Monday, because running it is a job and there are about four hundred people in this city qualified to do it and not one mark on any of it.

---

At about half past six in a room off a service road there were about nine people and somebody asked him who did it.

He had been asked that four times since eleven o'clock by people who did not know the answer either, and he had said the same thing each time, and he said it again.

"**I do not know, and I am not going to find out, and I want to be careful about the reason.**"

"**You are going to say there is no way to find out.**"

"**There is a way. Somebody could go and ask the four, one at a time, in three districts, and it would take about a term, and if one of them said a name then the name would be in this room in about four months and every one of the four hundred would know it in about four years, and then the only people in this city who could run a sequence would be four people who know each other's names.**"

"**So the four hundred is the protection.**"

"**The four hundred is the accident. I did not arrange it. Nobody arranged it. It is a rule that is printed and dated and has been in force for about eleven years and it is not hidden and there is a wall binder in a district office with about nine rooms of paper behind it.**"

"**Then somebody wrote it.**"

"**Somebody wrote it. That is a true sentence and it is about nineteen years old and it is the only true sentence anybody has said about it today, and I am not going to be the one who carries the rest of it around, because I have been carrying things around for four years and the thing I carry does not even work.**"

Nobody thanked him. Nobody asked him a second question. Nobody in that room said a name, and there was a moment at about half past seven when the whole room wanted to say one particular name, and nobody said it, and afterwards about four of them said privately that they had wanted to, and that wanting to is not the same as knowing.

---

About four hundred competent people in this city can still invoke that rule. The number of them is not a conspiracy, is not on a list, is not reducible to a smaller number by anybody who has tried, and nobody in this case can produce it. A person of sixty in a district office has been trying for about a term and has said so out loud to about nine people and has not thanked anybody for not helping.

---

The clerk of forty-four noticed something at a lift.

It was at about quarter to nine in the morning in a building in Crown Terrace where she is seconded to a secretariat that nobody in that building has been told she is in, and it is the second time in about a month that she has noticed something in that building and not done anything about it, and the first time was in a lift with a man of twenty-two, and neither of them told the other.

Two people got into the lift ahead of her who she did not know, and one of them said the name of the rule out loud into a telephone, in an ordinary voice, while the doors were closing, the way a person says the name of a thing they use every day of their working life.

The other one did not react. She did not react. She went out on a floor she does not work on, which is not a thing she does, and walked to the stairs and down nine flights.

She did not raise it with the chair of forty-nine. She did not raise it with the man of twenty-two, who would have listened and said nothing and written it down. She wrote it on a piece of her own paper, in her own hand, once, and put it in her bag, and the paper is not ours and is not in a ring binder and is not on the wall.

The reason, which she gave to nobody and is not going to give, is that a clerk who has heard a rule named in a lift in the building she is seconded into has to decide whether that is a fact about a case or a fact about her own secondment, and nobody has asked her to hold both at once, and she checked twice in the first week of the secondment and once in the last week and has not checked since.

---

At about half past nine a woman of about thirty was in the chair nearest the door with a cup of tea in front of her and had been since about two, and a cook of twenty-seven was at the sink and said nothing at all, and a man of fifty-one with a tool bag came in at about ten and read the hardboard from the far side of the room and said nothing, as he does.

The shutter came down at about ten and the day ended on a service road in the wind.

---

He wrote one thing on a service road, in the wind, at about ten, standing up.

*297. Monday of week 102, at about ten on a service road in the wind, and this entry is three districts, four people, and one lift.*

*Conditions and docket.* **Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; four jobs attended, two of them in districts this case has not worked in; the man with a clipboard on that building's staff was on leave and did not ask. The rate held at nine and ten and reached eleven on no hour of it. The heating in the room off the service road is on, restored by a landlord, and the days-off series is closed and no figure of days is carried forward. The room is two hundred and thirty-eight days old. A card in a rail in Lattice Ward is two hundred and forty-two days in the rail, a third object, and is not in that room, is not in a car park, and is not in a secretariat. The twelve lines on the hardboard are a hundred and fifty-eight days old, the thirteen a hundred and nine, the fourteen seventy-four, the fifteen fifty-three, the sixteen twenty-eight, and the seventeen eight. Eight items in a ring binder, unopened, unread and unchanged. The wall is seventeen lines in eleven hands and twelve names, the first line a cook's and still the first, no line removed in this run of days and none rubbed off.**

*What ran, in three districts, and what it looked like.* **A civic sequence, run under a printed rule that is in force and was restricted a fortnight ago and not repealed. One: ventilation plant in a car park, off every weekend for about nine years, running from about eleven o'clock on the Sunday night, no fault, no fault code, nothing broken. Two: a stair light out since the middle of the Friday afternoon still lit on the Monday, and a lift parked on a roof since the Friday at the ground floor and not answering a call. Three: a door that is held released on a signal, shut and locked at about six in the morning and still shut. Three districts, about four buildings known to this case at the close of the day, nothing broken, nothing alarming, nothing announced, nothing minuted, and no message from anybody. Nobody in this case knows what started it, in the sense that nobody knows what to call it, and the entry records that the rule under which it ran is a rule any competent person may pick up on a Monday.**

*The count, which is the whole of the finding.* **One question asked three times, in three districts, to a competent person: who ran it. Answers: one, one, and two. Total four. None of the four has ever heard of the other three; three districts, three employers, two of them on no list at all, two of them on a list that belongs to a company and is not ours; no body that all four belong to; not chosen by anybody and did not choose each other; nothing to join. Nobody in this city is going to be the person who ran it on a Monday, because running it is a job and there are about four hundred people in this city qualified to do it and not one mark on any of it. The number of about four hundred is not a conspiracy, is not on a list, is not reducible by anybody who has tried, and cannot be produced by anybody in this case; a person of sixty in a district office has been trying for about a term and has said so out loud and has not been thanked by anybody for failing to.**

*What was not said in a room, and why.* **Asked four times on the day by persons who did not know the answer and once more at about half past six by a room of about nine people, and answered the same way each time: that nobody in this case knows, that a person could find out in about a term by going to the four one at a time, and that the finding of such an exercise would be that the only people in this city able to run a sequence would be four people who know each other's names. Also said, and true, and about nineteen years old, that somebody wrote the rule; and nothing else, and no reason given beyond that a man who has been carrying something for four years does not intend to start carrying another thing. At about half past seven the whole room wanted to say one particular name and nobody said it, and about four of them said afterwards that they had wanted to, and wanting to is not knowing. A name that exists, is not findable, has not been met, is not to be met, and was named once in this case by herself and by nobody else, is not attached here to anything that happened on this Monday, and no character in this run of days could know that it should be.**

*The only record of the Monday, and it is not ours.* **One company's own system, in a building in one of the three districts, which shows that a call was made on the Friday and by whom, in the ordinary way its own kind of system shows a call was made. It is that company's, it was not written for this case, no page of it is in this file, in that room or in the ring binder, and nobody in this case asked for it, copied it, or was shown it. It is recorded here only so that a later reader does not think the Monday left nothing at all behind it.**

*The lift, second time in about a month.* **A clerk of forty-four, seconded to a secretariat in a building in Crown Terrace that nobody in that building has been told she is in, at about quarter to nine in the morning, went up in a lift with two people she does not know, and one of them said the name of the printed rule out loud into a telephone in an ordinary voice while the doors were closing, the way a person says the name of a thing they use every day. The other one did not react. She did not react. She went out on a floor she does not work on and down nine flights. She did not raise it with the chair of forty-nine and did not raise it with the man of twenty-two, who would have listened, said nothing, and written it down. She wrote it once on a piece of her own paper, in her own hand, and put it in her bag; the paper is not ours, is not in the ring binder and is not on the hardboard. This is the second time in about a month she has noticed something in that building and not acted, and the first was in a lift with him. She has given the reason to nobody and is not going to: that a clerk who hears a rule named in the building she is seconded into has to decide whether that is a fact about a case or a fact about her secondment, that nobody has asked her to hold both, and that she checked twice in the first week and once in the last week and has not checked since. The secondment is unchanged and the chair has still not been told.**

*People, and the count of them.* **About nine in a warm room off a service road at about half past six and about nine at about ten, with a woman of about thirty in the chair nearest the door from about two, described by a count, not asked anything, not thanked, not apologised to. A cook of twenty-seven at a sink, silent for the thirteenth time in about a fortnight. A man of fifty-one with a tool bag in at about ten, who read the hardboard from the far side and said nothing. Three competent people in three districts who each ran a thing and each answered one question and none of whom has been thanked, because thanking them would require a name and the count is four. A clerk of forty-four, who is one of the four people in this case who has made herself findable on purpose in order to do a thing correctly, and who went out of a lift on the wrong floor and wrote one line on her own paper. Counts: three districts, four buildings known, four people who ran it, one lift, one line of handwriting that is not on a wall, and zero thanks.**

*State at the close of Monday.* **No panel and no marker. No relay, nothing run, nothing forwarded, and nobody ran anything; the machine in a basement in a building in Nine Bridges is a recording machine that has been stuck for about four years. No message from anybody and nothing arrived from anybody, and the recording of eleven minutes has not been copied, not been read out and not been destroyed, and the person on it is not met. No authorship identified by anybody. He taught, demonstrated, assessed and assisted nothing and a room off a service road is not a practice in that department and the reason is not that he is the exception. Nine keys are needed and two exist, in this entry and in no room; the first cannot be asked back by anybody, the second is in the pocket of a woman of thirty-one, and the answer to the question on the back of a docket is on nothing. The licensor is unnamed and the field is blank. The master's fifth line is still blank in the fourth pocket of a tool roll and nobody has ever asked him to fill it in. The board over a door, the hardboard on two nails, a wall list in its plastic sleeve in a repair shop in Lattice Ward, a sheet of about two hundred and forty lines on a wall in a building in Crown Terrace, a day room next door that is shut, and a door in Nine Bridges with nothing on it are six surfaces and none has been merged with another. No page, minute, notice, report, register, form, leaflet, registration or constitution was created by anybody in this case in this run of days. The standing question is not stated here, carries no number, and no rule about who may be asked was invented. Entry 297.**
