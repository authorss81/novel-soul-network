**A heading, an opening paragraph that says what the day was in about a hundred words, then the day in scenes separated by rules, then one thing he wrote standing up on a service road, then a load-book entry numbered 294 to 303 whose opening line names the day and says what the entry is in three nouns. The previous forty chapters were believed to contain none of them; Movement IV's review repair pass found the construction in the prose of six of its own ten files in a form this list does not name, and the belief was wrong, and the corrected claim is the next paragraph.** The house form for emphasis is **one bold span per load-book paragraph and no emphasis inside it**; a second span inside a frame breaks the even-count rule on that line and is a defect this repository has found in three of five movements, and the last one was found by a rewrite rather than by a first draft, **so the fix is a check at the line while the file is written and not a review afterwards.** **Nothing in the narration is bolded. All emphasis is inside speech or inside a load-book entry. Every line carries an even number of `**` and every line carries an even number of `"`, every `**` span resolves with nothing left over, no span opens or closes with a space inside the delimiter, there is no empty `****`, and no line may carry a bold span on both sides of one speech tag.** The test for the last of these and for the narration rule, which has no line-level counter, is: **for every bold span in the body, the number of `"` characters before the span must be odd.** Movement IV's own first draft returned two on that test and both are now printed inside a sentence that says who wrote them, which is the correct form for a line on an object in this manuscript.

**THE LIST OF DRAFTING REFERENCES ABOVE IS A LIST OF PHRASES AND NOT A RULE, AND A PHRASE LIST CANNOT FIND A WORD IT DOES NOT CONTAIN. Movement IV carried the unit *volume* into the narration of six of its ten files, ten times, as *the whole of the improvement in six volumes* and as *five sentences of one shape in two volumes*, and neither is a thing anybody in a room can know.
