# Chapter 292 — A Restriction And Not A Repeal

The Wednesday morning of week one hundred and one was the morning a person of sixty whose job is to ask questions read out loud, in a room with about nine people in it, the name of a printed rule, and then read out the slip that had been added to the binder that restricts how the rule may be used, and the slip is a restriction and not a repeal, and about four hundred competent people in this city can still invoke the thing, and a man of twenty-nine who works on lifts asked the only question anybody asked and was told the true thing, and nobody thanked anybody.

---

Eleven on the docket and ten in date. A job in Crown Terrace ran long and was not chased, and a man with a clipboard in the same building asked him on the Tuesday whether he was from the company and was told he was not, and the basement in an unnamed district was not reported.

Nine and ten a minute all day, and eleven on no hour of it. Four names on a lease and the heating on and costing more, in a room two hundred and thirty-three days old, and a slip in a binder in a district office across the city that is not ours.

---

The office was on a floor with about nine rooms' worth of paper in it and the paper is other people's. There was a counter with a queue at it. There was a wall binder with printed standards in it, standing open, with numbers and dates on the things in it, and it is not ours and it is not in this case and nothing in this case has ever been put into it or taken out of it.

Aurelia Sopp is sixty and her job is to ask the questions, and she has been right every time she has opened her mouth in that building for about nineteen years, and being right is the difficulty and not the qualification, and she has said so once, out loud, to about nine people, and it is in a record and not in this file.

She had about nine people in the room. Four of them were from the district. Four of them had come about something else entirely and were going to be late. The fifth was a man of twenty-four from a company that supplies about eleven sites and has not been told anything.

Nobody in that room was told why they were there. She had not said a name and was not going to.

---

She took a slip out of the binder and put it on the counter, face up, and did not read from it for about four seconds, which is longer than it takes.

Then she said the name of the printed rule out loud, in the room, to about nine people, and about four of them had never heard it and about four had and did not look up.

Then she said what the slip does, and the slip is a printed thing with a number and a date on it and it belongs to whoever put it in the binder, and no page of it is in this case.

"**From this morning, a person who uses this has to do four things and then they have to stop. Say out loud the name of the person who is not answering. Say out loud how long it has been. Say out loud to one other person that they have said it. And then go and do the next thing that does not need that person.**"

"**That is all it is.**"

"**That is all it is and it is four sentences and none of them is about consent and all of them are about arithmetic.**"

Nobody said anything for about nine seconds. A man of twenty-four from a company that supplies about eleven sites said that the four sentences were fine, and that the difficulty was the third one, because the third one is the only one that requires a person, and about four hundred competent people in this city can say the first, the second and the fourth on their own, and a person standing in a lobby at about two in the morning is one person and the third sentence is the whole of the restriction and it costs one phone call.

Aurelia Sopp said yes, and that he was right, and that she had not thought of that and would not be saying it better than him.

"**And I am not going to pretend the third sentence holds. It will be said about four times in ten and not at all in six, and in about four years there will be a district that has a number for it and the number will be a form.**"

---

Then a man of twenty-nine who works on lifts stood up at the back, and he is on a rota in this city that has been a Tuesday for about nine years and he has not been asked to be on anything, and nobody thanked him then and nobody has.

He asked one question and he asked it well and it took about eleven seconds and it is the only question anybody asked in that room.

"**If it is a Monday and the node in the building has been silent since Sunday and I am the only person in it, am I still allowed to run it.**"

She did not answer for about four seconds, and the four seconds were correct.

"**Yes.**"

"**Say the rest of it.**"

"**The rest of it is that you are still allowed, and that you still may, and that if you do it correctly you have broken nothing that anybody can be suspended for, and that this slip does not change that, and that in about four hundred buildings in this city there is a person who could run it this afternoon and will not have been asked to by anybody.**"

"**Then what is the slip for.**"

"**The slip is for the people who were not going to anyway. It is not aimed at you. It is aimed at about four people a year who run it in about nine seconds without saying a name, and there are about four hundred people in this city who can do that, and the number of them is not a conspiracy and is not a list and is not reducible, and nobody in this room can produce it and I have tried for about a term.**"

He sat down. Nobody said anything. Nobody thanked her.

"**You are owed that answer and you are not getting a second one and you did not have to stand up to get it.**"

---

A clerk of forty-four was in the room and she is the woman who read a reference off a wall binder in about nine seconds in the spring and took about nine hundred cards out of use across about eleven sites in about four days, correctly, and who is seconded to a secretariat in a building in Crown Terrace that nobody in her own building has been told she is in.

Nobody thanked her for that either. Nobody mentioned it. She had come to give evidence about a thing she had done and was asked two questions about a thing she had not done and answered both of them in about nine seconds, and at the end of it a person of sixty said, to the room and not to her, that the person who had done the original thing was not in the building any more and that the slip existed because of her, and that it was the first time in about a term that a thing done in about four days had come back as a change in how a rule is used, and that nobody was to draw a conclusion from that.

The clerk of forty-four said nothing. She has been asked about that silence by nobody, including him.

---

Talia Venn was in that room for about forty minutes and said one thing in it, and her name went to nobody there, and the thing was about the number.

"**You have all got the four sentences and about four of you have the third one and the other four of you have the first, the second and the fourth. In about four years the four who have the third one will be the only four who can do it properly and they will be the only four anybody can find, and you will have solved a version of it that nobody asked for.**"

Aurelia Sopp said that she had about four people in her own building who thought the same way and had not said it out loud because saying it out loud in that building is how a thing becomes a form, and that a woman of twenty-four saying it in a room off a service road and a person of sixty not saying it in a building with about nine rooms of paper in it is not a contradiction.

Nobody thanked either of them. Nobody improved on either of them.

---

At about half past six in a room off a service road there were about nine people and a woman of about thirty was in the chair nearest the door and had been since about two, and a cook of twenty-seven put the ring on at about half past eight and said nothing at all, which was the eleventh time in about a fortnight.

A man of fifty-one with a tool bag read the hardboard from the far side of the room and said nothing, as he does, and has said he has no comment on lines and has not been asked twice.

The heating in that room is on and it costs more, and the number of names on the lease is four, and each of the four named the cost out loud before signing in a week in the spring before last, and nobody has asked any of the four to name it again, and the fortnight that a cook paid for in the winter is not paid for by anybody and is not going to be, and no fund, collection, subscription, grant, list, minute, notice, leaflet, registration, appeal or form was proposed on that evening by anybody.

The shutter came down at about ten.

---

At about ten, in the dark, on a service road, he wrote one thing standing up with his coat still on, and a bus went past and did not stop.

*295. Wednesday of week 101, at about ten on a service road in the dark, and this entry is one slip, four sentences, and about four hundred.*

*Conditions and docket.* **Eleven on the docket and ten in date; one job in Crown Terrace ran long and was not chased; the basement in an unnamed district not reported. The rate held at nine and ten and reached eleven on no hour of it. The heating in the room off a service road is on, restored by a landlord, and the days-off series is closed and no figure of days is carried forward. The room is two hundred and thirty-three days old. A card in a rail in Lattice Ward is two hundred and thirty-seven days in the rail, a third object, and is not in that room. The twelve lines on the hardboard are a hundred and fifty-three days old, the thirteen a hundred and four, the fourteen sixty-nine, the fifteen forty-eight, the sixteen twenty-three, and the seventeen five. Eight items in a ring binder, unopened, unread and unchanged. The wall is seventeen lines in eleven hands and twelve names, the first a cook's and still the first.**

*What was in the binder, and who put it there.* **One printed slip, with a number and a date, belonging to whoever maintains that binder, standing in a wall binder of printed standards in a district office in District 3. It is not ours, it was not written by anybody in this case, and no page of it is in this file, in that room, or in the ring binder. It is a restriction on how a printed rule may be used and is not a repeal of it, and about four people in that room on the Wednesday morning had heard the name of that rule before and about four had not, and the name was said out loud by a competent person doing her job and is in no narrator's sentence, in no heading, in no load book and in no document of this case. The name of the rule is not repeated in this entry.**

*The four sentences, and what they cost.* **Instrument: one printed slip, numbered and dated, added by whoever maintains that binder. Effect: invocation now costs four acts in a fixed order — name the person who is not answering, state how long, tell one other person that you have, then move to a task that does not depend on them. None of the four touches consent. All four touch arithmetic. Persons requiring one: the third act only. Acts performable by a single competent person with no other person present: three of the four. Finding one, by a man of twenty-four supplying about eleven sites, untold of anything: the restriction is therefore one telephone call wide, because the other three acts are solo. Finding two, by the person who read it aloud: expected compliance about four times in ten, non-compliance about six, and a predicted district number for the third act inside about four years, that number being a form. Acceptances of either finding: none. Thanks: none, and the man of twenty-four has not been thanked and will not be. What the slip does not touch: whether the person may be reached at all.**

*The question that was asked, and the true answer.* **One question, asked by a man of twenty-nine who works on lifts, standing up, in about eleven seconds, on a Wednesday morning, in a district office. It concerned a Monday, a node that had been silent since Sunday, and his being the only person in the building. Answer: yes, in four seconds, which the entry records as four correct seconds. He asked for the rest of it and got about nine more: that he still may; that doing it correctly breaks nothing anybody can be suspended for; that the slip does not change that; and that in about four hundred buildings in this city there is a person who could run it that afternoon and will not have been asked to by anybody. Then, to the room: the slip is aimed at people who were not going to anyway, and not at him; it is aimed at about four people a year who run it in about nine seconds without saying a name; there are about four hundred in this city who can do that; that number is not a conspiracy, is not a list, and is not reducible; and nobody in that room can produce it, and she has been trying for about a term. He was told he was owed the answer and would not get a second one and did not have to stand up to get it. He sat down. Nobody thanked her. Sources: her own knowledge and about four years of other people asking her. Refusals: one, the second answer, which was hers to give and was refused by her.**
*The clerk, and the four seconds.* **Present from the start to the close. Not thanked: zero times. Not mentioned by name in that room: zero times. Questions: two, both about work of hers, both answered in about nine seconds, the second one into four seconds of silence that are hers and not a room's. Her own reason for being in the room: to give evidence about something she had done. What she was asked about instead: something she had not done. At the close, addressed past her to the room: she has left that building; the slip exists because of her; it is the first time in about a term that work finished in about four days has come back as a change in how a rule is used; and nobody is to draw a conclusion from that. Her reply: none. Questions to her about the reply: none, from anybody, including him. Her own record of that spring: a reference taken off a binder in about nine seconds, about nine hundred cards, about eleven sites, about four days, correctly. Secondment: in place, third building, secretariat, floor above a district office, unknown to her own building, unchanged, and endable by nobody in this case. The chair of forty-nine: not told, second occasion. A clerk is not a mechanism and an ordinary job is not a post; she holds nothing, claims nothing, and is on no list.**
*Two statements about a number, from two rooms, and the disagreement about which one to say out loud.* **Speaker one: Talia Venn, twenty-four, about eleven seconds, in the district office, with her name given to nobody in that room and carried here and on no document. Content one, in short: an expectation that the holders of the third sentence will be the only people who can do it properly and the only people who can be found, and a prediction that the city will then have solved a version of the problem nobody asked for. Speaker two: the person of sixty, in the same room, about nine seconds, content two: about four people in her own building hold the same view and have not said it aloud, on the ground that saying it in a building with about nine rooms of paper in it is how a thing becomes a form, and that the two positions are not a contradiction. Neither thanked, neither improved on, and no number is attached here to a rule about who may be asked.**
*What the day did not settle, and what it did not contain.* **Nothing was defeated, nothing was turned, and the rule stands, restricted and in force, and the person who read the slip is right that about four hundred people in this city can still invoke it and the number is not reducible and no one in this case can produce it. Nobody was thanked. He taught, demonstrated, assessed and assisted nothing, said no to nothing, and a room off a service road is not a practice in that department and the reason is not that he is the exception; the condition is neither quoted nor paraphrased and one request to run a procedure once, made in a passage some weeks ago, was not staged, not rehearsed, not priced and not referred to. No panel and no marker. No relay, nothing run, nothing forwarded, and the word does not appear in this entry. The plate in a service cupboard on a landing in Nine Bridges is not explained, not removed and not blamed on anybody, and the man of thirty-four who found it was not in this building and is not named. No authorship of anything was identified by anybody; the one identification of the authorship of the rule in this case was made once, on a recording, by the person it concerns, and by nobody else and by no character in any of these days, and that recording has not been copied, not been read out and not been destroyed. No message arrived from anybody. Nine keys are needed and two exist, the count is not said aloud in any room, the first cannot be asked back by anybody, the second is in the pocket of a woman of thirty-one, and the answer to the question on the back of a docket is on nothing.
