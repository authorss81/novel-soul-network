# Chapter 299 — One Line Went In

The Wednesday morning of week one hundred and four was the fifteenth sitting of an Exchange that has run on four-week spacing for nineteen years, four weeks to the day after the fourteenth, and the book opened, and one line went into the third column in about nine seconds because a woman of thirty-eight said a thing to the holder of the book and not to the room, and the telephone count moved from fifty-two to fifty-four and was announced for the twentieth time, and the ninth chair was against the wall and was not moved back, and not one word of the four minutes is in this file.

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date. One job in a district this case has not been in, one address in Nine Bridges not attended and not chased, and nothing has been reported in three districts since the Friday before last.

The word was at nine and ten. The heating is on and costs more and the room is two hundred and fifty-four days old, and a book above a line in another district was opened for the fifteenth time that morning for about nine seconds of a woman's own words.

---

The room is above a line in a building in Saltmarket and the holder of it says so at the start of every sitting, and the shutters were up, and the light in it was the flat light of a room that has been on the same side of a street since before anybody in it was working.

Nine chairs in a curve with the two ends turned in. The ninth stands against the wall facing away from the room and has been where it is since the holder of it was fifteen, and nobody has asked her about it in nineteen years and nobody is going to.

There is a table, a tin that has never had a lock on it, and a book with four columns and forty-seven lines in it. The last line had gone in fifty-six days earlier, at the thirteenth sitting, and had not been read since.

---

The woman of thirty-eight came in at about half past nine and had telephoned twice in about a term and had not been rung back either time, and she said that in the first four seconds, and then she asked for four minutes alone with the holder of the book, and the room agreed to that without a vote, because there is no vote for anybody in that room.

She was given about four minutes. What she said in them is not in this file and is not going to be in anything, and the holder of the book has not repeated it to one person, and the young man of twenty-one in that room, who has been coming since he was seventeen and who keeps a count he has never given her, was in the room and has not asked her about it and was not asked himself.

She said the words out loud first, in her own words, to the holder of the book and to nobody else, and then she wrote one line herself in the third column in about nine seconds, which is not required, and about nine of the nine people in that room noticed the order of it and did not say so.

The book is at forty-eight lines.

---

The count came at about ten past ten, unasked, as it comes, with the arithmetic done in the head about four minutes earlier and refused in the room.

"**Fifty-two to fifty-four. Two in four weeks. The telephone is ahead of the book and it was ahead of the book four weeks ago, and it was behind it before that, and I am telling you the direction and not the difference, and I did the difference in my head about four minutes ago and I am not going to do it in front of nine people.**"

"**You have announced that count how many times.**"

"**Twenty. Fifteen of those are the fifteen Wednesdays this book holds, and five predate it, and there is a sheet with the five on it that I have not shown anybody and that I am not going to.**"

"**And nobody converts anything.**"

"**Nobody in this room converts a call into a line or a line into a sitting or any of the three into either of the others, and I have asked for that about nine times in nineteen years and I have been given it every time and I have never once been given it by anybody who needed it.**"

---

The man of about forty-four came for the fifth time and asked one question, and it was the first question anybody in that room has asked in about two years that was about a person instead of about the book.

"**Is she all right.**"

Nobody said anything for about nine seconds.

"**No.**"

"**You cannot know that.**"

"**I cannot know that either, and I have said no because no is the true answer and not because I am refusing to help you. I do not know whether she is all right. I know that she came, and that she said a thing to me, and that she wrote one line in her own words, and that I am not going to tell you one word of it, and that is all I have got and I have had it for about four minutes.**"

"**You have had it for nineteen years.**"

"**Some of it.**"

No thanks went to her and he was not asked a second question. Nobody in that room asked which line, or whether it was a good one, and he has not asked either.

---

The ninth chair was against the wall at the close and was not moved back. Its mover is not named here and is not to be named by anybody.

The answer about furniture was given for the eighth time and was not confirmed and was not to be confirmed, because confirming it is a minute and she does not take minutes.

A young woman of twenty-one has been in that room since she was fifteen, and is not the first-year of nineteen in a building four miles away, of whom there is one, and she was not asked anything on that Wednesday, has not been asked anything in four years, and has given no reason and is not to be given one.

---

At about half past six in a room off a service road there were about nine people and the room was warm, and a woman of about thirty was in the chair nearest the door and had been since about two, and a cook of twenty-seven was at the sink and said nothing at all.

Talia Venn was in that room for about an hour and said one thing, and her name went to nobody in it, and the thing was about a book and not about a person.

"**A book that only opens when a person is standing in front of the person holding it is the only kind of book that can hold a person's own words for nineteen years, and you have just watched it work, and in about four years somebody is going to offer that woman a system, and the system is going to be correct, and she is going to say no, and she is going to be the only person in whatever room she is in who has understood what the thing is for.**"

"**And if she says yes.**"

"**Then you will have got what you wanted and you will have lost the only version of it that was any use, and I am not going to be able to tell the difference afterwards, and neither will you, and that is the risk and not the argument.**"

Nobody improved on it. Nobody asked her a second question. She volunteered no second thing and she did not say her name to anybody in that room.

The heating in that room is on and it costs more. There are four names on the lease and each of the four named the cost out loud before signing in a week in the spring before last and nobody has asked any of the four to name it again. The fortnight that a cook paid for in the winter is unpaid by anybody, unresolved, and was not mentioned in that room and is not going to be.

The shutter came down at about ten, and the ninth chair went back against the wall in that room too, and that is a different chair in a different street and nobody has ever mixed the two up.

---

He wrote one thing on a service road in the dark at about ten, standing up, and it took him longer than the sentence in it was long.

*302. Wednesday of week 104, at about ten on a service road in the dark, and this entry is one line, two calls, and one question about a person.*

*Conditions and docket.* **Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; one job in a district this case has not been in; one address in Nine Bridges not attended, unreported and not chased; nothing reported in three districts since the Friday before last. The rate held at nine and ten and reached eleven on no hour of it. The heating in the room off the service road is on and the days-off series is closed and no figure of days is carried forward. The room is two hundred and fifty-four days old. A card in a rail in Lattice Ward is two hundred and fifty-eight days in the rail, a third object, and is not in that room above a line in Saltmarket and is not in a room in Crown Terrace. The twelve lines on the hardboard are a hundred and seventy-four days old, the thirteen a hundred and twenty-five, the fourteen ninety, the fifteen sixty-nine, the sixteen forty-four, and the seventeen twenty-six. Eight items in a ring binder, unopened, unread and unchanged. The wall is seventeen lines in eleven hands and twelve names, the first line a cook's and still the first, no line removed in the days this entry covers and none rubbed off.**

*The sitting.* **The fifteenth, four weeks to the day after the fourteenth, in a room above a line, on a Wednesday, as it has run for nineteen years. Nine chairs in a curve with the two ends turned in and the ninth against the wall facing away from the room. A tin that has never had a lock. A four-column book at forty-seven lines at the start of the morning, the last of them fifty-six days old and put in at the thirteenth sitting. Present: the holder, sixty-one; a man of about forty-four, fifth attendance; the young man of twenty-one in attendance since seventeen, who keeps a count and was not asked for it; a young woman of twenty-one in attendance since fifteen, unnamed, unasked, given no reason, and not the first-year of nineteen of another building, of whom there is one; a woman of thirty-eight who had telephoned twice in about a term and been rung back neither time; and about four others not described here. The book opened. The ninth chair was against the wall at the close and was not moved back, its mover is not named here, and the answer about furniture was given for the eighth time and was not confirmed, because confirming it is a minute.**

*One line, and the four minutes.* **A woman of thirty-eight asked, in the first four seconds of arriving, for four minutes alone with the holder of the book, and was given them without a vote, there being no vote for anybody in that room. She said a thing to the holder of the book and not to the room for about four minutes. Not one word of the four minutes is in this file, is in this entry, is in the ring binder, is in any load book, and is not going to be anywhere. She then wrote one line herself, in the third column, in about nine seconds, in her own words, after saying the words out loud first, which is not required and which about nine of the nine people present noticed. The holder has not repeated any of it to one person. The young man of twenty-one was in the room, has not asked about it, and was not asked. The book is at forty-eight lines, the second time since the Monday that it has grown, and it has not grown at any other sitting in the last five.**

*Four weeks on, and the figures.* **The telephone count moved from fifty-two to fifty-four, two in four weeks, and the book from forty-seven lines to forty-eight, one. Stated by the holder, in about nine seconds, and not converted by anybody: that the telephone is ahead of the book; that it was ahead four weeks ago; that it was behind the book before that; and that the direction is given and the difference is not, the difference having been done in her head about four minutes earlier. The count has now been announced twenty times, fifteen of those being the fifteen Wednesdays this book holds, and five predating it on a separate sheet she has never shown anybody and is not going to. Her request, given for about the ninth time in nineteen years and granted for about the ninth time: that nobody in that room convert a call into a line or a line into a sitting or any of the three into either of the others. These four figures are not to be converted into one another or into a number of sittings by any person, and the direction and not the difference is the whole of what this entry reports.**

*The fifth question, and the first one about a person.* **A man of about forty-four, fifth attendance, asked whether the woman of thirty-eight was all right. Answer: no, given in one word. Pressed, the holder said in about nine seconds that she cannot know that either, that no was given because no is the true answer and not as a refusal to help; that what she has is that the woman came, said a thing to her, and wrote one line in her own words; and that she is not going to tell him one word of it. He said she has had some of it for nineteen years and she agreed and did not say how much. Nobody thanked her, nobody asked him a second question, nobody asked which line, and he did not ask. He has not asked since. The question of whether a person is all right is a different object from the question of what a book holds, and this sitting is the first in nineteen years in which that has been said out loud in that room, and it was not improved on by anybody.**

*An hour in another room, and what a woman of twenty-four said about a book.* **Talia Venn, twenty-four, about an hour in a room off a service road, one statement, about a book and not about a person, her name given to nobody in either room and carried here. Content: that a book opening only when a person is standing in front of the person holding it is the only kind that can hold a person's own words for nineteen years; that in about four years somebody will offer that woman a system; that the system will be correct; that she will refuse it; and that she will be the only person in whatever room she is in who has understood what the thing is for. Asked for the consequence of her saying yes, she gave one: the case gets what it wanted and loses the only version that was any use, and neither she nor anybody else can tell the difference afterwards, and that this is the risk and not the argument. Nobody improved on it, nobody asked a second question, no second thing was volunteered, and no name was given by her to anybody in that room.**

*The shape of a Wednesday night, and what it did not hold.* **About nine people in a warm room off a service road at about half past six, with a woman of about thirty in the chair nearest the door from about two until ten, described by a count, not asked anything, not thanked, not apologised to, and still the person a fortnight of unpaid heating was paid for. A cook of twenty-seven at a sink, silent for the seventeenth time in about a fortnight. Four names on a lease, each of which named its cost out loud before signing in a week in the spring before last; nobody asked any of the four to say it again. The fortnight is unpaid by anybody, unresolved, was not mentioned in that room and is not to be mentioned in a later one, and no fund, collection, subscription, grant, list, minute, notice, leaflet, registration, appeal or form exists anywhere near it or is proposed by anybody. No page, minute, notice, report, register, form, leaflet, registration or constitution was created by anybody in this case in the days this entry covers, and the only object this case has made in it is a line of handwriting that has not yet been written. No panel and no marker. No relay, nothing run, nothing forwarded; the machine in a basement in a building in Nine Bridges is a recording machine stuck for about four years and nobody went near it; the plate in a service cupboard on a landing in Nine Bridges is not explained, not removed and not blamed on anybody, and the man of thirty-four who found it is not named here and is not to be named. No message arrived from anybody. No authorship identified by anybody. He taught, demonstrated, assessed and assisted nothing, and a room off a service road is not a practice in that department and the reason is not that he is the exception. Nine keys are needed and two exist, in this entry and in no room; the first cannot be asked back by anybody and the second is in the pocket of a woman of thirty-one, who has not been asked to be carried anywhere since she said no and is not to be asked; the answer to the question on the back of a docket is on nothing. The licensor is unnamed and the field is blank. The master's fifth line is still blank in the fourth pocket of a tool roll and nobody has ever asked him to fill it in. Six surfaces and none merged with another here. The standing question is not stated here, carries no number, and no rule about who may be asked was invented. Nobody thanked anybody. Entry 302.**
