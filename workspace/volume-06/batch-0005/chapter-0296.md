# Chapter 296 — Eleven Buildings

The Thursday morning of week one hundred and two was the first day after, and about eleven buildings in three districts had lost something for a day, and nobody died, and a first-year of nineteen said out loud in a room that nobody in this city was to be allowed to make anybody nearly die, and four people each ran one piece of what happened without a central owner and not one of the four had been taught by him, and the fifth piece was a number on a plate and a cook and a man of twenty-nine and a woman of twenty-four who is not on a telephone, and nobody thanked anybody.

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date. A man with a clipboard in a building in Crown Terrace did not ask him on the Wednesday or the Thursday, and the basement in an unnamed district was not reported.

The word was at nine and ten. The heating in that room is on and it costs more. The room is two hundred and forty-one days old.

---

Nobody died.

That is the first fact and it is a fact, and by about eleven o'clock on the Thursday morning it had already been made into something else by three people who were owed nothing, and the first-year of nineteen stopped all three of them inside about four minutes, in a room off a service road, on the ground floor, with a jug of water and about nine people in it.

"**Say what you have.**"

"**Fourteen people could not get out of one of the buildings.**"

"Could not, or did not want to."

"Could not."

"**Then a lift did not come when it was called. Write that down and write it in those words. If you write fourteen people then in about four years there is a number about people in a building that somebody made up on a Thursday, and the made-up number will be in a system and the real number will be the thing you did not write down.**"

"That is not fair."

"**It is completely fair and I am not being kind. A lift that does not come is a fault and a fault belongs to somebody who maintains lifts. A person who could not get out of a building is a claim about a person, and I have been inside a room for a term and a half where people make claims about persons out loud and about nine of them have been proved wrong and about four of them were the people making them.**"

Nobody thanked her. She is owed nothing. She holds no post and is not a deputy and is on about eleven hundred leaflets with her own name at the bottom of them by her own choice.

---

The count was done by two people who owed nothing and neither of whom had been taught anything by him.

A man of thirty-four who buys training for a company sat down at about eleven with a pen and did it in about forty minutes: three districts, every building anybody in that room could name, a list of what each one had lost, and a total.

Eleven buildings. Nine of them had a lift in them that did not answer a call for about a day. Two of them had a stair door that is held released on a signal and was shut. One of them had ventilation plant running that has not run on a Sunday morning in about nine years, and that one is the same car park, and he is the caretaker's is the caretaker of it.

Four objects. Eleven buildings. Nobody dead. Nobody hurt. Nobody trapped, because in every one of the eleven there was a stair and every stair worked, and that is the sentence the count is actually made of.

"**Eleven is not a number anybody can act on.**"

"No."

"**And it is not a number anybody can reduce, because I have asked three districts and the answer in two of them was that they stopped asking once it was over, and in the third it was a woman of sixty-one who did it in a book and has not shown anybody the book.**"

"Then it is a number that will be eleven forever."

"**Then it is a number that will be eleven forever, and in about four years somebody will ask me what happened on the Thursday and I will say eleven buildings, and they will write down eleven buildings, and that is the correct thing and it is also the whole of it.**"

---

The four pieces were run by four people and not one of the four had been taught, shown, assessed or assisted by him, in this case or in any other, and he was in all four and he is the subject of none of them.

A man of twenty-nine who works on lifts ran the lifts. He went into about nine of the eleven in about a day and did not go into two of them, and has named the two out loud, and the two are not buildings he will not do on his own ground; they are the two where a company has a maintenance contract and a person with a key, and he does not go into a building where somebody else has the key, and he said that out loud and nobody improved on it.

A first-year of nineteen ran the counting, and ran the refusal to make anybody nearly die, and has said that neither of those is a piece and that she is doing both of them because nobody else in the three districts was going to.

A man of fifty-one with a tool bag ran the two stair doors. He got one of them open at about four in the afternoon on the Thursday and the other one at about eleven the following morning, and he has said that the second one took longer than the stair doors he usually works on and that he has not worked out why, and he does not want it worked out, and a man of twenty-nine who works on lifts said that is the right answer and was not thanked for it.

A woman of thirty-four who has been checking about nine hundred doors in four buildings on her own time for about four years, unpaid, ran the part where somebody went to every one of the eleven and told whoever answered the door what had happened, and she did it in about a day, and she did not telephone any of them first.

"**I have been in about nine of those buildings before and about four people recognised me and asked me about a sign.**"

"Did you answer."

"**I said no and I went away. That is the same answer I gave on a stair in Nine Bridges and I am not going to improve on it for a stranger.**"

Nobody thanked her. She has not been thanked for the four years either.

---

The fifth piece was a number on a plate.

The plate is on the outside of that room and carries a letting agent's telephone number and no name, and it rang about nine times over about a day and a half, and about four of the nine rang on the Thursday, and a cook of twenty-seven answered about four of them and told about four people, in about four seconds each, that they should go and use a stair.

"**You could have given them the number of the man of twenty-nine.**"

"**I have it on a card in a drawer in Nine Bridges and it is about nine years old and it is the number of a man I have spoken to twice.**"

"And the number of the woman of thirty-four."

"I have that one too."

"Then why not."

"**Because both of those people would have had to stop what they were doing, and one of them has about nine buildings in her and the other one has a lift in about nine of them, and there was nothing at that door that a person on a telephone could do that the person who came through the door could not do.**"

"**Except that about four of them were about four minutes away from somebody who would have been able to.**"

The cook did not answer that and washed a mug.

---

The woman of twenty-four was not on a telephone and about four minutes of the whole thing went by in which nobody could have rung her, and she named that herself in an earlier run of days, in her own words, and has carried it since and has said she will not check it and was not asked to.

A first-year of nineteen found the gap at about four in the afternoon on the Friday and said it out loud in a room, and nobody looked at anybody, and the man of twenty-two did not look up from the counter.

"**Four minutes, at about a quarter past eleven, on the Thursday, when four of the nine buildings had not been told by anybody and the only four people in this case who could have told them were about eleven buildings apart.**"

"**Say her name.**"

"No."

"**Why not.**"

"**Because she said in the spring that she is not going to be on a telephone and gave a reason about four seconds long that I did not hear, and I am not going to be the person who makes her argument for her in a room. She named it. She carries it. I have written down that there were about four minutes and I am not going to write down anything else about it.**"

Nobody said anything. Nobody thanked her. Nobody improved on it, and nobody has asked her about the four minutes since, and she has not offered them.

---

At about half past six in a room off a service road there were about nine people and the room was warm and a woman of about thirty was in the chair nearest the door and had been since about two, and a cook of twenty-seven was at the sink and had said more words to one person in about a day than she has said to a room in about a fortnight, and did not mention it, and was not asked.

The heating in that room is on and it costs more. The number of names on the lease is four and each of the four named the cost out loud before signing in a week in the spring before last. The fortnight that a cook paid for is unpaid by anybody and unresolved and was not mentioned in that room on that evening and is not going to be.

The shutter came down at about ten.

---

He wrote one thing on a service road, in the dark, at about ten, standing up.

*299. Thursday of week 102, at about ten on a service road in the dark, and this entry is one count, four hands, and about four minutes.*

*Nobody nearly died, and three attempts to say so.* **Three people, none of them owed anything to anybody in this case, converted a fault into a claim about persons within about six hours. A first-year of nineteen stopped all three in about four minutes in a room off a service road, on the ground that a lift that does not come is a fault and belongs to whoever maintains lifts, and that a person who could not get out of a building is a claim about a person, and that claims about persons made out loud in rooms have been proved wrong in this city and that about four of the people who made them were the people making them. Nothing about this is to be written down again by anybody in a later run of days as a number of people.**

Lifts: a man of twenty-nine who works on lifts, into about nine of the eleven in about a day, refusing the other two on a stated ground of his own, being that he does not go into a building where somebody else holds the key, which he said out loud and which nobody improved on.
