# Chapter 298 — One Sentence

The Monday morning of week one hundred and three was the morning a man of twenty-two said one sentence out loud in a room off a service road, in front of about nine people, and it was that four people were each findable and he was not one of them, and it is the answer to a question this room has been asking for about four years and it is not the answer to the one it should have been asking, and nobody improved on it, and nobody thanked anybody.

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date. Two jobs attended and one address in Nine Bridges not attended and not chased. Nothing has been reported in three districts for about a day and a half and nothing has been reported about the eleven buildings since the Friday.

The word was at nine and ten, and it was nine and ten in nine rooms that morning and nobody said so. Four names on that lease cost more than they did and the room they are paying for is two hundred and forty-five days old.

---

He said it at about eleven in the morning, standing up, with a docket in his other hand, in about nine seconds.

There had been no lead into it. A man of fifty-eight with a bad hip was in the corner and had been since about half past nine. A man of fifty-one with a tool bag was at the far side. A cook of twenty-seven was at a sink about nine feet away with her back to the room and did not turn round. A first-year of nineteen was at the counter. A man of twenty-nine who works on lifts was in the fourth chair. A woman of thirty-four was not in that room and had not been in it since the Friday. The chair nearest the door had a woman of about thirty in it and had had one in it since about two, and nobody had asked her anything in all that time and nobody was going to.

"**Four people ran four things last week and every one of the four of them could have been found by somebody who wanted them, and I was not one of the four, and neither was anybody in this room.**"

That is the sentence. It is thirty-nine words, counted twice, and it took about nine seconds and it was said once.

---

Nobody said anything for about eleven seconds.

Then the man of twenty-nine who works on lifts said the thing that is actually true about it, and he said it in about nine seconds, and nobody thanked him for it and it changed nothing.

"**Then next time it will not be those four.**"

"**No.**"

"**Then in about four months there will be a building in this city where four things are going wrong and the four people who could do them are four different people, and the four people who know all four names are sitting in a room off a service road with nine chairs in it.**"

"**Yes.**"

"**And what happens on the Tuesday.**"

"**Nothing. I have a docket. I go to whatever is on the docket. And the four of you are wherever the four of you are, and about four of those places are eleven buildings apart, and one of them is a stair door on the Monday.**"

"**So the sentence you have just said is about last week.**"

"**It is about last week. It is the only week I have had.**"

Nobody thanked him. Nobody improved on it. Nobody wrote it down except him, on a service road, at about ten that night, and he wrote it as a sentence and not as a finding.

---

A first-year of nineteen said the other half of it at about half past eleven, and she stood up to say it, and she said about four seconds of it and then stopped, on purpose, and everybody heard her stop.

"**That is the answer to a question this room has been asking for about four years, and it is not the answer to the one it should have been asking, and I am not going to say the other one out loud in here either, because if I say it then about nine of you will write it down and I have watched that happen to a better sentence than that.**"

"**What is the one it should have been.**"

"**No.**"

"**You have just described the shape of it.**"

"**I have described the shape of it and I have said no, and those are two sentences and both of them are mine, and if anybody in this room would like the second one said out loud then that is a thing about them and not about me and I am not going to be the instrument of it.**"

Nobody said anything. Nobody thanked her. She is owed nothing, holds no post, is not a deputy, and is the only nineteen-year-old in this case, and she went back to the counter and washed a cup and did not say anything else about it for the rest of the day or after.

---

A man of fifty-eight with a bad hip was in that room from about half past nine until about one and he did not say the thing about the paper.

He has been in that room on and off for about a term and has said two sentences in it in about a term, and one of the two was the correct one in the spring and he said then that he would not say it again, and he has not said it since, and this Monday was the fifth occasion anybody in that room has had in mind and it was the fifth occasion he did not say it.

About four people in that room looked at him at about one o'clock when they thought he was going to.

Nobody asked him to. Nobody has ever asked him to, including the man of twenty-two, who wanted to, on about four occasions, in about four months, and did not, and has recorded that he did not and is not going to.

---

Talia Venn was in that room for about an hour and said one thing in it, and her name went to nobody in it, and the thing was about the sentence and not about him.

"**You have said in about nine seconds the thing that about nine people in this case have been trying to get at for four years, and every one of them got to a different half of it, and none of them got to both halves at once, and about four of them are going to be annoyed with you on Monday because you said it in a room instead of in a book.**"

"**Should I have said it in a book.**"

"**No. There is no book. That is the other half and you have just said it without noticing and I am not going to be the one who points at it.**"

"**Point at what.**"

"**Nothing. Ask me on another day.**"

Nobody improved on either of them. Nobody thanked her. She did not thank him and she did not say his name in that room and she has not said it in any room.

---

About eleven people a week in this city are still going to be taught that a release waits for a man in a lanyard. It was said out loud once in a public room in the last four days, by the man it is about, in about nine seconds, in front of about nine people, and nothing has come of it and the number is not smaller.

He did not say the other thing on that Monday. He has said it out loud to one person in six years and she is not going to be told about the four sentences and he is not going to tell her.

---

At about half past six in that room there were about nine people and it was warm and a woman of about thirty was in the chair nearest the door with a cup of tea in front of her and had been since about two, and a cook of twenty-seven was at the sink and said nothing at all, which was the sixteenth time in about a fortnight.

The heating in that room is on and it costs more, and the number of names on the lease is four, and each of the four named the cost out loud before signing in a week in the spring before last, and nobody has asked any of the four to name it again. The fortnight that a cook paid for in the winter is unpaid by anybody and unresolved and was not mentioned in that room and is not going to be, and no fund, collection, subscription, grant, list, minute, notice, leaflet, registration, appeal or form was proposed on that Monday by anybody.

The shutter came down at about ten, and about four people who had heard the one sentence said nothing about it on the way out, and two of them said something about it in a car park afterwards.

---

One thing, on a service road, in the dark, at about ten, standing up, and nothing else that night and no second version of it anywhere.

*The fifth occasion, and no question.* **A man of fifty-eight with a bad hip was in that room from about half past nine until about one. He did not say the thing about the paper. Nobody asked him to, nobody has ever asked him to, and the man of twenty-two wanted to on about four occasions in about four months and did not, and has written down that he did not and is not going to. *One hour, one sentence, and a question that was put off.* **Talia Venn, twenty-four, about an hour, one statement, about the sentence and not about the man, her name given to nobody in that room and carried here. Content: about nine people have been after this for about four years; each reached a different half; none reached both at once; about four of them will be annoyed on the following Monday because it was said in a room rather than in a book. Put back to her as a question, the answer was no, and the reason was that there is no book, that this is the other half, and that he had said it without noticing and she was not going to be the one to point at it. Asked to point at it, she declined and said to ask her on another day. Nobody improved on either of them, nobody thanked her, she did not thank him, and she did not say his name in that room.**
