# Chapter 283 — The Word Came Early

The Friday of week ninety-eight was the first Friday rota of this run of days, and it ran in a company training room at the end of a passage in Crown Terrace with nine folding chairs in it and a laminated sheet on a post, and eleven people were on the week's list and about four of the eleven were not on it, and the cards have been out of use for about nine weeks, and on the last two runs there was no gap at all, and a man of twenty-two told a woman of thirty-six with a lanyard so, once, in a doorway, and she said she knew, and that was the whole of what that question has produced in about four months and nobody thanked anybody.

---

Grade three, twenty to eight, on a card in date, with the exception for the term recorded and renewed in a system in a box that says *No reason given by holder*. Eleven on the Friday list for the week and about four of the eleven not on it.

The word was at nine and ten. The heating in that room was on its hundred and ninth day and it is two hundred and fourteen days old.

---

The room is at the end of a passage and not on the way to anything, and about four people who have come to that building for other reasons have come the wrong way down the passage in four years and not one of them has been asked to leave.

The chairs are folding and there are nine of them and the laminated sheet on the post has been on that post since the spring before last and has a beat printed on it that a person has to say out loud and no person has ever read off it.

---

The cards have been out of use for about nine weeks. About nine hundred of them came out of use across about eleven sites in about four days in the spring of this year because a printed rule says a practice card cannot be issued unless it carries the beat that performs the boundary, and about nine hundred of them did not carry it, and that was correct, and the instructor's hands have been the only thing in that room since.

She raised it, once, with the company, in about nine words, and was told that nothing is coming, and did not raise it again.

That is a fact about a room and about about eleven people a week and it is not a fact about a rule, and nobody in that room put the two together on the Friday and nobody in that room has put them together on any Friday.

---

On the last run the woman of about thirty-four in the second row, four years on that list, reached the end and finished, and there was no gap.

Not a short one. Nothing. The instructor said the word and the room went back to the start and the whole of it was one beat.

On the run before that there was no gap either. On the three before those there was a gap of about four seconds, and on the two before those there was a gap of about four seconds, and nobody in that room has ever said out loud what it is, and the man of twenty-two was in the fourth row against the wall on all five of those Fridays with the word available and did not say it, and was not asked to, and is not to be.

---

A man of about forty-four came on the Friday. He is not on that week's list and he has not been on a list for about a year, and he was in that room on the Friday of week ninety-five and has been in a doorway after it twice, and he came in at about half past five and stood at the back and did the whole run with the other eight.

Nobody asked him why he was there. He has been on that list nine years. He offered an explanation for the gap twice in three weeks in a doorway and was told by the man in the lanyard that he was the man in the lanyard and that asking a stranger for a fact is a thing he does about eleven times a week for money, and the answer he got was that he does not have the reason either, and he has not offered it a third time.

He left before the last part and nobody asked him to stay and he is not owed anything by anybody in this case.

---

Afterwards, in the doorway, with nine people behind him coming out and about four of them still talking, the man of twenty-two said one thing to the instructor and it took about four seconds and he said it once and he did not improve on it.

"**It was not you on the last two Fridays.**"

"**I know it was not me on the last two Fridays.**"

"**I know you know. That is not an answer. I have got about nine of the eleven and none of the other two and I do not know what those two are and I have not got round to it.**"

"**Then it is not an answer and I have not got one either, and the reason I have not got one is that I have never once asked anybody in this room what they are waiting for, and you asked a woman of twenty-three that in about four weeks and you were in the fourth row and she was in the front row and I heard you ask it and I did not hear the answer, and that is the only thing I have got.**"

A man of twenty-nine who works on lifts was in that room and has been on a Tuesday rota for about nine years, and he said one sentence in the doorway on his way out and it was not about the gap.

"**I have been in four of these since the spring and the word has been early in three of them and I have never once worked out why and I have stopped working on it, and I would like that noticed before somebody in this building decides that I have.**"

Nobody thanked him. Nobody wrote it down. It is in this entry and nowhere else.

Nobody thanked either of the two. She is not a mechanism and holds nothing and is a woman of thirty-six with a lanyard and a card on a table in an office he has never been inside.

He was not asked for anything else and he did not offer anything else and he has not said the word in any of those rooms in about four years and is not going to start because of about four seconds.

---

At about half past nine in the room off a service road there were about nine people and the heating was on its hundred and ninth day and a woman of about thirty was in the chair nearest the door with a cup of tea in front of her and had been since about two, and a cook of twenty-seven was at the sink and has said five sentences of one shape in two volumes and has said she will not say a sixth and about four people in this case are keeping the count.

Nobody thanked anybody at the end of the Friday and nobody has rung anybody since and the room is not going to be rung about a gap in a company training room at the end of a passage.

He wrote one thing on a service road, in the dark, at about ten.

*286. Friday of week 98, at about ten on a service road in the dark, and this entry is one list, no gap, one doorway, and a hundred and nine days.*
