# Chapter 289 — It Was Not An Apology

The Thursday afternoon of week one hundred was the afternoon the word was said out loud, once, to one person, in a warm room off a service road with about nine people in it, and it was nine, ten and eleven a minute and it was not corrected, and it is not an apology and it is not a request, and she is not going to report it to anybody and that is her decision and not his, and he did not ask her to and did not ask her not to, and there is no version of that he can get back.

---

Eleven on the docket and ten in date, and a stair light in a block in Nine Bridges was reset at about eleven by a man in a district jacket who wrote the fault down as the fault not being the lamp.

The word was at nine and ten. The heating in that room is on. The room is two hundred and twenty-seven days old.

---

He had been carrying it since the spring before the one before last and had never once given it away, and about four people in this case know that he carries something and not one of them knows what it is, and one of the four is a woman of twenty-four who asked him a four-word question in this room a fortnight ago and has not asked him anything since.

What made him say it on that afternoon was not a decision. It was a valve.

On the Saturday before last a landlord went round four units and opened one, and about four people came back into that room who had not been in it since a door next door was locked, and the whole of the improvement in about four years is a thing one man did on a Saturday in about four minutes to a building he has never been inside and about which he knows nothing, and the only part of it that was not luck was that somebody had put a valve on a wall in the spring and left it.

---

He said it at about half past four, in about nine words, to one person, at a table, with about nine people in the room.

"**I talk at about nine or ten a minute and when I am tired it is eleven.**"

Talia Venn did not move and did not put her tea down and about four other people in that room were close enough to hear it and about four were not and he did not check which was which and did not look round and did not do it on purpose either way.

"**How long.**"

"**About six years. It has not changed and it is not going to be changed by anybody being careful.**"

Nobody said anything. A cook of twenty-seven was at a sink about nine feet away with her back to the room and did not turn round, and nobody in that room was waiting to be shown anything.

---

"**Is that the thing you have been carrying.**"

"**It is the thing I have been managing, and I have been managing it since the spring before the one before last, and managing it is the same shape as the other thing and I have never once said that out loud.**"

"**What other thing.**"

"**About eleven people a week in this city are still going to be taught that a release waits for a man in a lanyard, and I have been the man in the lanyard for four years in about nine different buildings without ever once being in the room.**"

Nobody thanked him for either of those and nobody in that room had ever heard the first one and about four of them had heard the second one many times.

---

Then she said the part, and it took about eleven seconds, and she did not make it kind, and it was the correct thing and it cost her something to say and she said it anyway.

"**I am not going to do anything with it.**"

"**You do not have to.**"

"**I know I do not have to, and that is not why I am saying it. I am saying it because you have about four seconds to decide whether you are going to ask me to keep it or ask me not to, and both of those are a request, and both of them hand it over, and in about four years somebody is going to find out that a man in a room off a service road has said a thing like that to somebody, and if I have been asked either way then it is a thing that was passed between two people and you will have made it that.**"

He did not ask her to keep it. He did not ask her not to.

"**Then that is the whole of what has happened and you will have to live inside it.**"

"**Yes.**"

"**I am not reporting it. That is mine and it is not yours and you do not get a copy of it and you cannot ask me for one.**"

---

He wrote the part down. That is the whole of what he did about it.

Not the sentence — the sentence was in a room and about four people were in it. The part down is: that he did not look round, that about four people were close enough, that he did not check, that the not checking was the decision, and that a decision not to check is the same shape as every other thing he has done for four years and he made it in about four seconds and he would make it again.

He has not said it to anybody else. He has not said it to the officer of sixty, and he has not said it to the man of fifty-eight with a bad hip, and he has not said it on a service road, which is where he has said everything else for about four years and where he is not going to say this.

---

At about half past six the room was warm for the third time in about nine days and about nine people were in it and a woman of about thirty was in the chair nearest the door with a cup of tea in front of her and had been since about two, and nobody in that room asked him about it and about four people in it had heard it and nobody apologised to anybody and nobody thanked anybody.

A first-year of nineteen, who was not close enough to hear it and who has not been told, said one thing at about half past seven that was not about it and that four people wrote down afterwards and nobody acted on, and it is in the next entry and it is not the thing she said in this room two weeks ago about there being no post in this city whose business it is to ring a person, and the two are about different objects and nobody in that room is going to be told they are the same.

He wrote one thing on a service road, in the wind, at about ten.
