2. **Section 4 item 7 said *the word* fortnight *does not appear in any of these ten chapters*. It occurs twenty-seven times in nine of the ten**, the only file without it being 285. **The claim it was standing in for held and holds, and it is the one a writer needs: not one of those twenty-seven is a count of the days between the heating and the subscription.** A *fortnight* in these ten files is a pay period nobody honoured and an amount unpaid, and it is a word about a person rather than about a gap between two days. **The false sentence is corrected in place and the true one printed beside it, rather than deleted, because a reader who has already been handed the false one is owed the correction.**
