# Chapter 285 — A Landlord On A Saturday

The Wednesday of week ninety-nine was the morning the heating in that room came back, and it came back because a landlord went round four units on the Saturday and put it on in three of them and then came to the fourth and did that one as well, and nobody in this case asked for it and nobody in this case paid for it and nobody in this case knew until about eleven, and about four people who had been coming in for the temperature of a shut day room came back, and nobody thanked anybody, and there is no document anywhere near it.

---

Eleven on the docket and ten in date, and a stair fire door in Nine Bridges was attended at about ten and reset and is expected back, which is the same fault in a box that does not hold the room number.

The word was at nine and ten. The heating in that room was on its hundred and fourteenth day and it came back at about a quarter to ten, and it is two hundred and nineteen days old.

---

The unit next door is shut. It has been shut since the Monday and the man who locked it went home and has not thought about it since.

The room off the service road was cold at about a quarter to eight and was not cold at about eleven and nobody in it worked that out, because the only person who could have worked it out was in a stairwell in Nine Bridges at about nine and came in at about half past ten.

---

A landlord with four units on that stretch went round them on the Saturday, in the order he goes round them, which is the order they are in, not the order he likes them in.

Three of them have people in them in the evenings. He put the heating on in those three in about four minutes each and did not go in and did not knock and did not turn anything off that somebody had turned on.

The fourth is a converted shop unit with a shutter and about nine chairs in it and nobody in it at any hour of any day that he has ever been there. He came to it last. He went in, he opened the thing on the wall, he closed it again, and it took him about four minutes, and he did not know anybody who used it and did not ask and has not asked since.

He has not been told anything about that room. There is nothing on the lease about a room inside a room and no name on the door but a telephone number belonging to a letting agent.

---

At about half past ten the man of twenty-two came in off a service road with a docket under his arm and the room was warm and he stopped in the doorway for about four seconds.

Nobody told him. There was nothing to be told. A woman of about thirty-four who had been past about nine times this year and had left a sign up in a doorway in another street said, to nobody, that the place is warm, and a man of about fifty-one with a tool bag said that it is, and about nine people in that room worked out between about half past ten and about eleven that a person had gone into a building at the weekend and put a valve open.

Nobody in that room knows who. Nobody in that room is going to find out.

---

A first-year of nineteen said one thing about it, at about half past eleven, and it is the correct thing and it changed nothing.

"**Nobody in this room is to thank that landlord.**"

Somebody said why not.

"**Because a landlord who has been thanked is a landlord who has been asked, and then it is a favour, and in about four years there will be about four people in this city who are somebody's kind landlord and every one of them will have a reason.**"

"**It is not a favour. It is a valve.**"

"**It is a valve and it is also the reason he came to this one last and it is also the reason he did not knock, and if I stand on that step and say thank you then in about four years I am a person who thanks landlords, and that is a thing about me and not about him.**"

Nobody thanked him. Nobody thanked anybody. The finding was given and it was correct and it was acted on by nobody at all, and the man of twenty-one who works on lifts was not in that room and is not to be asked about it.

---

About four people came back into that room over the following three days who had not been in it since the Monday.

One of them was a woman of about thirty who has been in that room on every working day since the Friday of week sixty-nine, and who has been asked nothing since, and who was not asked anything on the Wednesday, and who was not asked why she comes, and who was not thanked, and who was not apologised to.

The other three are described by a count and are not described by a name and are not asked anything either.

A cook of twenty-seven put the ring on at about half past eight and did not mention it, and about four people in that room noticed that she did not mention it, and she has said five sentences of one shape in two volumes and has said she will not say a sixth.

---
