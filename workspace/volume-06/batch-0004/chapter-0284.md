# Chapter 284 — Right About The Water

The Monday of week ninety-nine was the day a local council's ordinary business was suspended in writing, with a count, on the ground of an emergency, by a man of fifty-eight in a coat that has never been in a workshop, and every sentence he said that morning was a sentence a competent person says, and he was right about the water and wrong about the room, and the day room next door shut at about two in the afternoon and about four people who had been coming in for its heat have not been in that street since, and nobody thanked anybody.

---

Ten on the docket and nine in date, and a shared lighting circuit on a fourth floor in Nine Bridges came up for the second time in a fortnight and was the same fault and was fixed the same way.

The word was at nine and ten. The heating in that room was on its hundred and twelfth day. The room is two hundred and seventeen days old.

---

The instrument is two sides of a sheet and it belongs to the people who wrote it and it is not ours, and nothing in this file is a copy of it or a part of it.

It is headed with the name of a local council. It says that the ordinary business of that council is suspended, and it says why, and the why is a number and the number is a count.

About eleven hundred premises. About nine hundred people on a list kept at a school hall by three people on a rota that was written on the back of a piece of card. A pump that has been running since the water went down, and a standpipe that has not, and a fortnight.

The fortnight is in it. The fortnight is a decision and not a punishment and the man who wrote it said so out loud, to a room of about nine people, unasked, before anybody asked him anything at all.

---

He is fifty-eight. He is Iven Sore. He has a coat that has never been in a workshop and a soft voice and he has declined twice, in writing, to have a table moved into a locked room upstairs, and he has agreed to nothing in fourteen years and refused nothing, and those two are not the same, and there is nobody in this city who has to make them the same.

He is not a post. He is a person with an ordinary job and an ordinary coat and a piece of paper of his own about a pump.

He said a great deal, in about eleven minutes, in a room, and nobody improved on any of it and nobody has yet said he was wrong about any of it.

"**A council's ordinary business is the first thing that stops. It stops before anybody has decided that it is going to. If it does not stop then nothing stops, and I have never in fourteen years found that to be in dispute.**"

Somebody asked him what the emergency was.

"**A pump has been running since the water went down and a standpipe has not, and about nine hundred people are on a list at a school hall, and three people are keeping that list on the back of a card.**"

"**And how long.**"

"**A fortnight. Fourteen days of not doing the other thing. It is not a sentence and it is not a punishment, and anybody in that building who uses it as one will find out in about four weeks that the people in that district do not come back afterwards. That is not an argument. I have seen it twice.**"

"**Do you have any view on how people are feeling about it.**"

"**No. I was not asked for one and I would not give you one, and if I gave you one you would put it in something and in about four years it would be the sentence everybody remembers about this instead of the count.**"

---

Then he said the part that is the whole of the finding, and he said it about himself, unprompted, in one sentence, which is the mark of a competent person.

"**I am right about the water and I know nothing whatever about a room off a service road and I have not been asked and I am not going to find out this week.**"

The emergency is four streets from that room. The boundary the instrument draws is drawn around premises, because a boundary drawn around anything else cannot be counted and a thing that cannot be counted is not a boundary, it is a mood. The room is inside the boundary. He is right about the boundary. He does not know what is inside it and nobody in that room told him and nobody was going to.

About four people in that room thought the last part was a fault. Nobody said so, because it is not a fault, and because a man who says out loud which half of his own answer he is standing behind is not a man who needs correcting.

---

What it cost this case is four people and one door.

The day room in the unit next door is run by the council that has just been suspended, and it shut at about two in the afternoon, and the man who locked it was told to lock it and did and went home and has not thought about it since. The day room is a warm room with about eleven chairs in it and it has been open on weekdays for about six years, and the room off the service road has been cold for a hundred and twelve days, and about four people had been coming into the cold room for about nine weeks because the warm one was through the wall.

About four of them were not in that room on the Monday and have not been in it since.

One of the four has been in it on every working day since the Friday of week sixty-nine and is in it on the Monday, in the chair nearest the door, with a bag on her lap, described by a count, and nobody asked her why she comes and nobody thanked her and nobody apologised to her, because an apology would have been a question and it would still have been one.

She was not asked about the day room. Nobody in that room mentioned the day room.

---

Nobody in that building is a villain and nobody was defeated on the Monday and nobody was turned and nobody agreed to anything.

The instrument is the office's and another party's. No minute, no form, no report, no register, no notice, no leaflet, no registration and no constitution was created by anybody in this case on that day, and the only new object made by anybody in this case in this run of days is one line of handwriting on an existing hardboard, which is not this.

A man of twenty-two read about nine lines of it on a service road and wrote the rest of it down and has not seen the sheet.

He wrote one thing on a service road, in the wind, at about ten.

*287. Monday of week 99, at about ten on a service road in the wind, and this entry is one count, one boundary, one locked door, and a hundred and twelve days.*

*Conditions and docket.* **Ten on the docket and nine in date. Ten jobs, one short of them in date. That room is inside it. Counts: one boundary, one room inside it, one man who is right about one half of it and says so, and four streets between the emergency and the wall.**
