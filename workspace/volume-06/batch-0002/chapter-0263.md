# Chapter 263 — Four Times Over

The Thursday of week ninety-two was a delivery stoppage over a company requirement and about nine people were on it, and one of them came into a room off a service road because the room is open and it has a plate on the outside of it with a telephone number on it and no name, and the openness cost a first-year of nineteen the ability to be somebody's friend for a term, and she named the cost at a sink about an hour later, and nobody thanked her, and about four people in that room wanted to say it had not been her choice and none of them did.

---

Eleven on the docket and ten in date, and the standing water in a basement in an unnamed district came back up to about four inches without anybody having done anything to it, which he wrote down and attributed to nothing.

The word was at nine and ten. The heating in that room was on its sixty-sixth day and it is a hundred and seventy-one days old.

---

The requirement belongs to a company and is about a company's own deliveries. A parcel or a prescription or a bag of food cannot be left at a door with a neighbour, or put inside a door, or signed for by whoever comes down, unless the person who takes it is recorded on a hand-held by the person who hands it over, with a name and a time and a button.

It is not an unreasonable requirement. Two of the about nine people on the stoppage said in the first week that it is the most sensible thing their employer has ever asked of them, and one of those two is a woman of about forty-four who has driven for that company for six years and who has been in a room off a service road four times and has never once asked that room for anything.

The stoppage began on the Tuesday. The four days before it, the company had issued a hand-held to about nine drivers and told them in writing that a delivery not recorded is a delivery not made, and that a delivery not made is a delivery refused, and that this applies to neighbours and to doorsteps and to anybody who is not the person named on it.

Nobody in that company is a villain. Nobody in that room said they were.

---

She came in at about half past six on the Thursday and she stood in the gap between the counter and the hardboard, which is where about four people a week stand, because there are nine chairs and about eleven people.

She said: "**Can I sit.**"

And a first-year of nineteen, who is nineteen, said: "**You can sit.**"

That is the whole of what was said to her for about forty minutes. A cook of twenty-seven put a cup of tea in front of her. A man of fifty-one with a tool bag read the hardboard from the other side of the room and said nothing. Somebody's telephone went off in a bag on the counter and nobody answered it.

Nobody asked her why she was on the stoppage. Nobody asked her what she intended to do about it. Nobody in that room had anything to give her and did not try to.

---

The cost was named at about half past seven, at a sink, by the person it cost, and it is a first-year's cost and it is in her own words and nobody improved on it.

"**I have been her friend four times over. Every one of them was in this room, and every one of them was a Thursday, and she came because there is a plate on the outside of this door with a number on it and no name and she telephoned the number and somebody let her in.**"

She was at the sink with her hands wet.

"**If I am her friend then anybody who wants to know what she is doing on a Thursday afternoon has to find one person, and the one person is me, and I am on about eleven hundred pieces of paper already and I put my own name at the bottom of those on purpose when I was eighteen.**"

"**Say the rest of it.**"

"**So I am going to stop being her friend for about a term. That is the cost, and I have not decided it in a moment, I worked it out on Wednesday on a bus and I have been carrying it since about four o'clock this morning, and I am naming it at this sink because that is what happens in this room, and because I would have done it anyway and I wanted it said in front of people.**"

A cook of twenty-seven said, without turning round: "**You will not say a fifth thing of that shape. You said four in a fortnight and you said you would not say a fifth, and this is not a fifth, because a fourth person is not a shape.**"

"**Then it is the fourth thing of a shape, and I have said four, and I am not going to be a person who has said five.**"

She said that and did not say anything else about it that evening.

---

The woman of forty-four did not know. Nobody told her. She sat for about forty minutes and drank one cup of tea and did not ask about the hardboard, and she said one thing at about twenty to seven which was that she had come because it is the only room in this city where nobody has ever asked her to justify being in it, and that is not the same as the stoppage and she knows it is not.

A man of twenty-two was in the room and he did not ask the first-year of nineteen to change her mind, and one other person came close to it at about nine o'clock and did not, and the first-year of nineteen said, unprompted, to the room:

"**Nobody is going to talk me out of it and I would like that written down nowhere, and I would also like everybody in here to know that I am not doing it because anybody in this room did anything, because you did not, and if one of you had I would have said so.**"

"**You have not asked why either,**" said the man of fifty-one with a tool bag, from the other side of the room.

"**No. Because I have got about nine words left in me tonight and they are all about me.**"

---

The stoppage went on for another nine days and then it was settled at about four in the afternoon on a day in the following week by the company and the drivers and not by anybody in that room, and the terms were that the hand-held stays and the neighbour rule stays and a person may nominate a neighbour once, in writing, in advance.

Nobody in a room off a service road was in that settlement. Nobody heard about it. The woman of forty-four went back to work and did not come to that room, and she is not owed an explanation by anybody, and she was not given one.

He wrote one thing on a service road, in the wind, at about ten, with a cup in his hand that he had not wanted tea from.

*266. Thursday of week 92, at about ten on a service road in the wind, and this entry is one stoppage, four visits, and sixty-six days.*

*Conditions and docket.* **Eleven on the docket and ten in date. The stair fire door in Nine Bridges was attended at about two, the closer was reset again, and the frame and the door were found exactly as he has described them on eleven previous occasions; the entry records the finding and attributes it to nothing. The standing water in a basement in an unnamed district returned to about four inches from about two without intervention by any person, valve or stopcock. The rate held at nine and ten. The heating in the room off the service road is on its sixty-sixth day off, unpaid, and is not resolvable by any document and none is proposed. The room is a hundred and seventy-one days old; a card in a rail in Lattice Ward is a hundred and seventy-five days in the rail and is a third object and is not in that room. The twelve lines on the hardboard are ninety-one days old, the thirteenth is forty-two, and the fourteenth is seven.**

*The requirement, and who is on the stoppage.* **A company's own deliveries may not be left at a door, left inside a door, or signed for by a person who is not the person named, unless the handover is recorded on a hand-held by the person handing over, with a name, a time and a button. Issued in writing after four days of operation; an unrecorded delivery is a delivery not made and a delivery not made is a delivery refused. About nine drivers are on the stoppage, which began on the Tuesday. Two of the nine stated in the first week that the requirement is the most sensible thing their employer has ever asked of them, one of them being a woman of about forty-four with six years of driving for that company. No person in that company is described here as a villain and none was so described in that room.**

*The visit, and the four words.* **A woman of about forty-four came in at about half past six, stood in the gap between the counter and the hardboard where about four people a week stand, asked whether she could sit, was answered in four words that she could, and sat for about forty minutes. A cup of tea was put in front of her. A man of fifty-one read the hardboard from the other side of the room and said nothing. A telephone in a bag on the counter rang and was not answered. Nobody asked her why she was on the stoppage, what she intended to do about it, or anything else, and nobody in that room had anything to give her and did not try. She said one sentence before leaving: that she had come because it is the only room in this city where nobody has ever asked her to justify being in it, and that this is not the same as the stoppage and she knows it is not.**

*What the openness cost, named by the person it cost.* **A first-year of nineteen, who is nineteen, at a sink at about half past seven, in her own words and about a term: that she has been the woman's friend four times over; that every one of the four was a Thursday and every one was in that room; that she came because there is a plate on the outside of the door carrying a telephone number and no name and somebody let her in; and that if she is the woman's friend then anybody who wants to know what that woman is doing on a Thursday afternoon has to find one person, and the one person is the first-year, who is on about eleven hundred pieces of paper with her own name at the bottom of them by her own choice since she was eighteen. She stated that she had worked it out on a bus on the Wednesday and had carried it since about four that morning, that she is naming it because that is what happens in that room, and that she would have done it anyway and wanted it said in front of people. A cook of twenty-seven told her from the sink that this is not a fifth thing of that shape, because a fourth person is not a shape, and was told that it is the fourth thing of a shape and that the speaker is not going to be a person who has said five. Nobody improved on the cost. Nobody thanked her. About four people in that room wanted to say that it had not been her choice and none of them said it, and the not saying is in this entry because it happened.**

*What nobody did.* **The man of twenty-two did not ask her to change her mind. A second person came close to it at about nine and did not. The first-year said, unprompted, that nobody was to talk her out of it and that she would like it written down nowhere, and that she was not doing it because anybody in that room did anything, because nobody did, and that if one of them had she would have said so. A man of fifty-one asked whether she had asked why, and was told no, and that she had about nine words left in her and they were all about herself. The woman of forty-four was not told. She did not come again, and is owed no explanation by anybody, and was given none.**

*The settlement, and who was in it.* **The stoppage ran nine more days and was settled in about four minutes on an afternoon in the following week between the company and its drivers. The hand-held remains, the neighbour rule remains, and a person may nominate a neighbour once, in writing, in advance. No person in this case was in that room and nobody in this case heard of the terms, and the four figures in this entry are not on any paper that belongs to anybody in this room.**

*State and boundaries.* **About eleven people in a cold room and a woman of about thirty in the chair nearest the door from about two, described by a count and not by a name, an age, a district or an occupation, not asked anything, not thanked, and not the subject of a question. Fourteen lines on a hardboard on two nails, the first a cook's and still the first, and one of the fourteen is a first-year of nineteen saying she is not going to be the person who is asked about this room. Four words in chalk and one unremovable grey ghost over a door. Eight items in a ring binder, unopened and unchanged. The company's requirement and its written warning are a company's and predate this case, and no page, minute, notice, report, register, form, leaflet, registration or constitution was created by anybody in this case. No teaching, demonstration, assessment, assistance or relay by him, and a room off a service road is not a practice in that department and the reason is not that he is the exception, and the condition is neither quoted nor paraphrased. No panel and no marker. Nine keys needed and two existing, count not stated. The licensor is unnamed and the field is blank. No message has arrived from anybody. The standing question is not stated here and carries no number. Entry 266.**
