# Chapter 264 — Cards Go In Pockets

The first Friday of this rotation ran in a company training room off a corridor in Crown Terrace with nothing to hand out and nothing changed, and eleven people sat in nine folding chairs, and a woman of about thirty-four asked for the fourth time in a term whether the ending of a practice could be written down for her to check herself against, and was refused in four words, and the four words are the whole of the reason because the reason was given in full on the Friday before last and has not been given since. About eleven people a week in this city are still going to be taught that a release waits for a man in a lanyard, and the figure is on the back of a woman's own card, and it is not a rate.

---

Grade three, twenty to eight, on a card in date, with the exception for the term recorded and renewed in a system in a box that says *No reason given by holder*.

Eleven on the Friday list for the week and about four of the eleven not on it.

Four jobs, of which the last is a stair fire door in Nine Bridges, reset for the ninth time in about a fortnight and expected back.

The word was at nine and ten. The heating in that room was on its sixty-seventh day and it is a hundred and seventy-two days old.

---

The training room is off a corridor on a floor with about nine other rooms on it, and there is a laminated sheet on a post by the door carrying nine words with no name, no date, no company and no version, and he has seen that sheet in about nine other places in this city and it is the same nine words in all of them.

Nine folding chairs and eleven people, two of them against the wall. A woman of thirty-six with a company lanyard and a card of her own on the table in front of her. A man of fifty-one in the second row with a bad wrist who comes on Fridays and is not on the list. A man of twenty-one near the door who has been near the door for about a term and has not spoken in that room.

He was there to confirm scope, which is the whole of a grade three Friday, and his name is on the list for the week in a system.

The man of twenty-nine who works on lifts was not in that room on that Friday. Nobody asked him whether he was coming and he was not asked again.

---

The cards are still gone.

They have been gone for about two weeks, and the four runs went faster, and the reason they went faster is the same reason they went faster the first Friday they went faster, which is that about nine people were not holding a piece of paper and looking down at it.

The runs still ended on her word. One syllable, said at the end of each run, and the eleven people stopped. It is the beat that ends a practice and hands the choice to act back to the person doing it, and it is not on a card, and it has never been on a card, and it is not in the box on the post by the door, and nothing in this case has ever touched it and nothing in this case could.

The woman of thirty-four asked her question in the gap between the third run and the fourth.

"**Can I have the ending written down. Not to keep. Just so that I know I have got it right.**"

"**Cards go in pockets.**"

Nobody in that room said anything for about four seconds.

"**That is four words.**"

"**It is the whole of the reason and I gave you the whole of the reason on the Friday before last in about thirty words, and you told me it was three refusals and one reason, and I said then that I would rather it were one reason than three, and it is, and I am not going to say it again in thirty words so that you can keep it.**"

"And if I write it on a sheet and put it in my own bag."

"**Then it is a sheet in a bag, and in about four years it is a card, and in about four years after that it is in a pocket on a person who was not in the room, and then somebody runs a session for a person who has not got the pocket.**"

"That is the long one again."

"**That is the short one again. You have had both and I am not going to give you a third.**"

---

Nobody thanked her. The woman of about thirty-four said that she had asked four times in a term and would ask a fifth, and that she was not asking because she thought it was wrong, and that she was asking because every Friday for about four years she has finished a run by waiting about eleven seconds for somebody else to say a word, and that she would like to be able to stop doing that, and that she now knows she cannot.

The man of twenty-one near the door said nothing at all. People looked at the woman of thirty-four and the man of twenty-one did not look at anybody.

---

On the last run nobody waited.

The person in the front row got to the end of the hold, stopped, and finished, and the whole of the gap was about four seconds, and then the room went back to the start.

The man of twenty-two counted it twice. He was in the fourth row against the wall and he had the word available and he did not say it, as he has not said it on any Friday this term.

He could not work out afterwards, and did not say that he could not work it out, and did not say the other thing either: whether the four seconds was because the cards have been gone for about two weeks, or because the person in the front row is not the person who was in the front row on the Friday before last. Nobody in that room asked him and about four people in it were thinking about it and none of them was going to be the one who said so.

The woman of thirty-six said, at the end, to the room: "**The cards going has not changed what happens in this room. It has changed what is in your hands, and I am not sure anybody in here has ever been paid to notice that.**"

She has said that once before, in the same room, to the same people, on the Friday before last.

---

At about nine in the cold room off the service road there were about nine people and a woman of about thirty was in the chair nearest the door and had been since about two, and a cook of twenty-seven put a cup of tea in front of her and said nothing, which is what has happened on every working day for sixty-seven days.

He wrote one thing on a service road, in the dark, at about ten.

*267. Friday of week 92, at about ten on a service road in the dark, and this entry is one question asked four times, one refusal of four words, and sixty-seven days.*

*Conditions and docket.* **Grade three, twenty to eight, on a card in date, the exception recorded and renewed for the term in a system in a box that says *No reason given by holder*. Eleven on the week's list, about four of them not on it. Four jobs attended, the last a stair fire door in Nine Bridges, reset for the ninth time in about a fortnight, expected back. The rate held at nine and ten. The heating in the room off the service road is on its sixty-seventh day off, unpaid, and unresolved. The room is a hundred and seventy-two days old; a card in a rail in Lattice Ward is a hundred and seventy-six days in the rail and is a third object and is not in that room. The twelve lines on the hardboard are ninety-two days old, the thirteenth is forty-three, and the fourteenth is eight. Eight items in a ring binder, unread. The city's standing weekly figure is unaltered: about eleven people a week in this city are still going to be taught that a release waits for a man in a lanyard, the number is on the back of a woman's own card, it is on no form, and it is not a rate.**

*The room, and what is not in it.* **Nine folding chairs and eleven people, two against the wall. A laminated sheet on a post by the door with nine words and no name, no date, no company and no version, identical to a sheet seen in about nine other places and never explained by anybody. The instructor is a woman of thirty-six with a company lanyard and her own card on the table. A man of fifty-one with a bad wrist attends and is not on the list. A man of twenty-one near the door has been near the door for about a term and did not speak. He was present to confirm scope, which is the whole of a grade three Friday, and his name is on the week's list in a system. A man of twenty-nine who works on lifts was not in that room and was not asked whether he was coming and is not to be asked again.**

*The cards and the runs.* **Cards out of use about two weeks; nothing to hand out. Four runs, faster, on the ground given the first time they went faster, which is that about nine people were not holding a piece of paper. All four ended on the same one-syllable spoken word, which ends a practice and hands the choice to act back to the practitioner; on no card, never on a card, not in the box on the post, untouched by anything in this case. The instructor restated at the end a sentence of her own from the Friday before last, about the withdrawal having changed what is in people's hands and not what happens in the room.**

*The fourth asking and the four words.* **A woman of about thirty-four, second row, four years on the list, asked for the ending in writing. Refused in four words: cards go in pockets. Four occasions in a term. The long form of the ground was given in about thirty words on the Friday before last, was given a second time on being asked whether it had changed, and was not given a third. Nobody thanked the instructor. The asker gave her own account of why she keeps asking and it is not that she thinks the refusal is wrong: four years of finishing a run by waiting about eleven seconds for another person, and a wish to stop. Not thanked either, and not asked to stop.**

*The four seconds, and what is not known about them.* **On the last run the person in the front row reached the end of the hold, stopped, and finished, in a gap of about four seconds, and the room restarted. The man of twenty-two counted it twice, was in the fourth row against the wall with the word available, and did not say it, as he has not said it on any Friday this term. He did not establish and did not state whether the four seconds is a consequence of the cards having been out of use for about two weeks or of the person in that chair not being the person who was in it on the Friday before last, and the entry records the not knowing rather than settling it. He was thinking about it, and so was the man of twenty-one near the door, and neither of them was going to be the one who said so, and he was not asked.**

*State and boundaries.* **Nine chairs and a cold room, about nine people, and a woman of about thirty in the chair nearest the door from about two, described by a count and not by a name, an age, a district or an occupation, asked nothing, thanked by nobody. Fourteen lines on a hardboard on two nails, the first a cook's and still the first. Four words in chalk and one unremovable grey ghost over a door. Eight items in a ring binder, unopened. No page, minute, notice, report, register, form, leaflet, registration or constitution was created by anybody in this case; the five duties on a laminated post are a company's and are not ours. No teaching, demonstration, assessment, assistance or relay by him: he confirmed scope, which is the whole of what a grade three Friday is, and did not say the word. A room off a service road is not a practice in that department and the reason is not that he is the exception, and the condition is neither quoted nor paraphrased. No panel and no marker. Nine keys needed and two existing, count not stated. The licensor is unnamed and the field is blank. No message has arrived from anybody. The standing question is not stated here and carries no number. Entry 267.**
