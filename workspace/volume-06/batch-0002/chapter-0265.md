# Chapter 265 — I Am Not In The Book

The Monday of week ninety-three was the day an item came back to the person who found the fault in it, and the only person in this city who could have answered it said no in about ten words, and the ten words were not the words she has been refusing in for nineteen years, and she was thanked by nobody, she was not owed anything, she was not carried anywhere, she was not put on a stage, she was not described as a resource, and she is not going to be asked again.

---

Ten on the docket and ten in date, and the standing water in that basement was reported for the tenth time inside about four weeks and stands at about four inches, and nothing has been fitted to it by any person.

The word was at nine and ten. The heating in that room was on its seventieth day and it is a hundred and seventy-five days old.

---

The room above the line in Saltmarket has no sitting on a Monday. It has a woman of sixty-one in it, and a book with four columns and forty-six lines, and a tin with no lock on it, and nine chairs of which the ninth is against the wall.

There were about four people. The shutters were up. Nobody took a minute and nobody made a note of anything.

Aurelia Sopp, who is sixty and whose job it is to ask the questions, came in at about half past ten with a folder under her arm, and she said what she had come for in about eleven seconds, and she did not sit down.

"**The item came back to me on Friday. A person has to ask, and I am the person, and I have had a weekend about it and I am not going to pretend I had more.**"

"**Say what you want.**"

"**I want you to come to a room in Crown Terrace one morning and say what happens on the other side of your table. Not the rule, not the card, not the district. What happens to people who come in here and say they cannot go back to where they were. I have about forty-one people and no account of any of them, and you have nineteen years of them and you are the only person in this city who does.**"

---

Bea Nunn said no in about nine seconds and then said the same thing twice more, in the same words, over the next eleven minutes, and nobody in that room improved on it.

"**I keep the book. I am not in the book.**"

It is ten words. It is not the sentence she has been refusing in for nineteen years, and she made sure of that before she said it. A man of twenty-two in that room heard the difference, has never told anybody what the other sentence is, and has never been told.

Aurelia Sopp said: "**Say more than that.**"

"**No.**"

"**You do not have to say more than that. I am asking because I would like more than that and not because I am entitled to it, and I am going to say the rest of it once and then I am going to stop, and I would like it noticed that I am stopping.**"

"**Go on.**"

"**In about four years, if I stand in a room and describe nineteen years of people, then every one of them becomes a thing that was described. Not by me. Described. There is not one of them in a position to say anything about that, because the whole arrangement is that they said it to me and nobody else was listening, and I am not going to be the person who took that and carried it into a building in Crown Terrace and put it on a table.**"

"**And if you came and you refused, after you had looked at it.**"

"**Then I would have looked at it, and I would be a person who came to look at it, and in about four years somebody would ask me what I saw, and I would have to remember that I went. No.**"

---

Nobody thanked her. Nobody asked her a second question. Nobody asked her whether she would come if it were a smaller thing, and nobody suggested a smaller thing, and nobody offered her a car, and nobody offered to come to her, and nobody wrote down that she had been asked.

A man of twenty-two was in that room and had been for about nine minutes and he did not ask her to change her mind, and he was asked afterwards, in the corridor, by a first-year of nineteen, why he had not, and he said:

"**Because I have been the man in the lanyard since the spring before the one before last and I know exactly what it costs to be asked twice, and because she has said no to being carried anywhere by anybody who has not asked her first since before I was in that building, and asking her is not asking her twice, it is asking her once, and it is the same thing.**"

Nobody thanked him for that either. There is no mechanism in this case by which anybody is thanked for not asking.

Aurelia Sopp said one more thing before she left and she said it to the room and not to the woman of sixty-one.

"**I have been right every time I have opened my mouth in this building for nineteen years. This morning the right thing to do was to be right, and doing it would have cost her the only thing she has, and I have had to hold being right back on purpose twice — once last Monday in a room in Crown Terrace, where it would have cost a clerk of forty-four something, and once this morning — and this morning was the harder of the two. I am not going to be thanked for either and I would like nobody to try.**"

---

The young man of twenty-one said, later, to the room, that she had now been asked to come to a building in Crown Terrace and had said no, and that he was keeping a count of things that had happened to her in that room that had not happened to anybody else in it, and that he was not going to tell her what the count was, because it was hers and not his, and that this was the first use he had ever found for it.

The young woman of twenty-one was in the room for about nine minutes and was not asked anything and was not given a reason for anything and her chair is where it is.

Nobody described the woman of sixty-one as a resource. Nobody said that somebody ought to go and get her, and nobody said that she was the person who could unlock it, and nobody said the word *invaluable*, and about four people in that room noticed that none of those sentences had been said, which is not a thing anybody in this case has ever had to notice before.

---

At about half past six in the cold room off the service road there were about nine people, and a woman of about thirty was in the chair nearest the door and had been since about two, and a cook of twenty-seven put a cup of tea in front of her and said nothing at all, and about four people in that room wanted to ask her why she comes and nobody did and nobody apologised to her, because an apology would have been a question and it would still have been one.

He wrote one thing on a service road, in the wind, at about ten, and the shutter had been down for about four hours and the cold came off the brick.

*268. Monday of week 93, at about ten on a service road in the wind, and this entry is one refusal of ten words, and seventy days.*

*Conditions and docket.* **Ten on the docket and ten in date. The standing water in a basement in an unnamed district was reported for the tenth time inside about four weeks and stands at about four inches; no person, valve, stopcock or inlet has been fitted to it by anybody, and the entry records the tenth report and attributes it to nothing. The rate held at nine and ten. The heating in the room off the service road is on its seventieth day off, unpaid, and is not resolved and nothing in this entry resolves it. The room is a hundred and seventy-five days old; a card in a rail in Lattice Ward is a hundred and seventy-nine days in the rail and is a third object and is not in that room and is not in Saltmarket. The twelve lines on the hardboard are ninety-five days old, the thirteenth is forty-six, and the fourteenth is eleven.**

*Why she was asked, and by whom.* **A hearing produced one thing on the Monday of last week and the thing was that a room cannot ask to be heard again and a person has to, and the item therefore came back on the Friday to the practice standards officer of sixty who found its second fault a fortnight before the hearing and did nothing about it. She went to the room above the line in Saltmarket on the Monday morning at about half past ten with a folder, and said in about eleven seconds that she had had a weekend about it and was not going to pretend she had had more, and then said what she wanted: that the woman of sixty-one should come to a room in Crown Terrace one morning and say what happens to people on the other side of that table, not the rule and not the card and not the district, on the stated ground that there are about forty-one people in her item and no account of any of them and nineteen years of them in that room and one holder in this city. She was not entitled to the answer and said so, and asked once.**

*The refusal, and the shape of it.* **Given in about nine seconds and repeated in the same words twice more over about eleven minutes by the woman of sixty-one: that she keeps the book and is not in the book, which is ten words and is not the sentence she has been refusing in for nineteen years, and she made sure of that before she said it; one man in that room heard the difference, has never told anybody what the other sentence is, and was never told. Asked for more, she declined. The rest was given once and then withdrawn: that in about four years a description of nineteen years in a room in Crown Terrace would make every one of those people a thing that was described, that not one of them is in a position to object because the whole arrangement is that they said it to her and nobody else was listening, and that she is not going to be the person who carries it into that building. Asked what she would do if she came and refused after looking at it, she answered that then she would have looked at it, and that in about four years somebody would ask her what she saw, and that the answer is no.**

*What was not done, and is the point of the day.* **Nobody thanked her. She is owed nothing by anybody in this case. Nobody asked her a second question, nobody asked whether she would come if it were a smaller thing, nobody proposed a smaller thing, nobody offered a car, nobody offered to come to her, and nothing was written down. She is not carried anywhere, is not put on a stage, is not described as a resource, and is not asked again. A man of twenty-two in the room for about nine minutes did not ask her to change her mind and was asked why by a first-year of nineteen, and gave as the ground that he has been the man in the lanyard since the spring before the one before last and knows what it costs to be asked twice, and that she has said for nineteen years that she will not be carried anywhere by anybody who has not asked her first. Nobody thanked him; there is no mechanism in this case by which anybody is thanked for not asking or for not arguing.**

*What the officer of sixty said once.* **Unasked, to the room and not to the woman of sixty-one: that she has been right every time she has opened her mouth in that building for nineteen years; that the correct thing to do that morning was to be right; that being right would have cost the holder of that book the only thing she has; that this is the second time in nineteen years she has had to hold being right back on purpose, the first having been in a room in Crown Terrace on the Monday of week ninety-two, where being right would have cost a clerk of forty-four something; that the second was the harder of the two and she says so; and that she is not going to be thanked for either and would like nobody to try. She was not thanked.**

*The two young people, neither of them asked anything.* **A young man of twenty-one in attendance since seventeen said he was keeping a count of things that had happened to that woman in that room that had not happened to anybody else in it, and that he was not going to tell her the number because it was hers and not his, and that this was the first use he had found for the count. A young woman of twenty-one in attendance since fifteen was in the room about nine minutes, was not asked anything, was not given a reason for anything, and her chair is where it is. About four people in that room noticed that nobody had described the woman of sixty-one as a resource and that no sentence of the shape that somebody ought to go and get her had been said, and the entry records the noticing and does not make anything of it.**

The standing question is not stated here and carries no number. Entry 268.**
