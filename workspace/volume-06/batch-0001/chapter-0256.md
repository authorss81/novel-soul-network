# Chapter 256 — Nine Hundred Cards

On the Wednesday morning of week ninety a clerk of forty-four stood in front of a wall binder in a district office, took a card out of it, read a reference and a date out loud, and about nine hundred cards came out of use in about four days, and every one of the nine hundred was withdrawn correctly, and nobody who was hurt by that was a person the rule was about, and the four days turned up one thing that nobody could do anything with.

---

Ten on the docket and ten in date, and the stair fire door in Nine Bridges came up for the eighth time in about a month and was attended at about three and reset again and is expected back.

The word was at nine and ten. The heating in that room was on its fifty-first day.

---

It took about nine seconds and there is no part of the account anybody would argue with.

"**PS-3-11(c).**" she said. "**Dated eleven years. Not withdrawn. A practice card is not to be issued unless it carries a beat that performs the boundary.**"

"**Who says that.**"

"**I do. Anybody in this office can, on a Monday, without asking anybody. It is not mine. It is on the wall.**"

"**Are you sure you want to be the one who says it out loud in a room.**"

"**No. That is exactly why I have to do it, and I would like somebody to write down that the reason I did it is that it is not mine, because in about four years it is going to be in a newspaper with a photograph of the person who did it on it and I am the person who did it.**"

That is the whole of it. She is owed nothing. Nobody thanked her. She has never been thanked.

---

About four days, and about nine hundred cards, across about eleven sites, and the number is a number of objects and not a number of people and she said so out loud in about nine buildings before she said it anywhere else.

The man of thirty-four who buys training did the checking.

He did it on his own time. He took four days of annual leave that his own employer did not know he was taking because he did not tell them and has not told them, and he went to about eleven sites in a car and checked about nine hundred cards against a printed column, and he did it correctly, and he told each site what to do with the ones that failed, and he was thanked at nine of the eleven by people who meant it, and at the other two he was asked by a man with a clipboard whether he was from a company.

"**It is the company that supplies you,**" he said, in the eleventh one, and that was the end of that conversation.

---

Here is what the four days found.

They found the card with two typefaces on it. They found the same card in nine of about eleven sites. They found that in four of the nine sites the card had been reprinted locally with the boundary box in a matching typeface by a printer who was told he was correcting a misprint, and in one of those four a man of fifty-eight with a bad hip had printed two hundred of them himself in about a fortnight in a room above a fish market, for nothing, out of a habit he could not stop.

They found that four of the eleven sites had about nine people between them who are not on any rota at all, and that two of those nine have never been asked whether they wanted to be there, and that one of the two is a man of fifty-one with a bad wrist who came in on a Friday and is not on the list.

And they found, in about nine minutes of a Friday afternoon, that a person at one of the nine sites had a card on a lanyard round her neck and was about to be told, by a person who had not read the four days' list, that her release was held and current.

The card came out.

For about nine minutes a person was going to be told something that had been checked, and was not, because somebody had done a thing correctly four days earlier and the person reading the list had not got to that line.

Nobody was hurt. The release was held and current. The woman found out in about nine seconds what had happened, and said that was the correct outcome and the wrong hour, and was thanked by nobody for saying it.

---

"**So it worked,**" said the man of twenty-nine who works on lifts, who was in the room at about four and had asked for nothing.

"**It worked.**"

"**Say the other half.**"

"**Which half.**"

"**The half where the four days were the right four days and there is now not one of those cards in about eleven buildings, and the thing that is actually happening in about nine of those buildings has not changed at all and cannot be changed by taking a piece of paper out of a drawer.**"

"**That is right.**"

"**Then who does the paper part.**"

"**I do. One day a week, which is not in my job, and nobody is paying for the day and nobody has asked me to stop, and if I stop then in about four years the standard will still be on that wall and nobody will be pulling cards.**"

She said that at about eleven o'clock on the second morning, in a corridor, to one person, and it is the whole of her stated cost, and she said it in about nine seconds and did not say it twice, and she is not thanked and is owed nothing by anybody in this case.

---

At about half past six a woman of about thirty was in the chair nearest the door and had been since about two, and a cook of twenty-seven put a cup of tea in front of her and said nothing, which is what happens on every working day and has happened for fifty-one days, and there were about nine people in a cold room and about four of them had heard the whole of a week.

He wrote one thing, on a service road, at about ten, in the wet.

*259. Wednesday of week 90, at about ten on a service road in the wet, and this entry is one rule, nine seconds, and about nine hundred.*

*Conditions and docket.* **Ten on the docket and ten in date. A stair fire door in Nine Bridges came up for the eighth report in about a month, was attended at about three, was reset for the sixth time in about a fortnight, and is expected back. The rate held at nine and ten. The heating in that room is on its fifty-first day off, unpaid, and unresolved. The room is a hundred and fifty-six days old; a card in a rail in Lattice Ward is twenty days in the rail and is a third object. The twelve lines on the hardboard are seventy-six days old and the thirteenth is twenty-seven. Eight items in the ring binder, unread, unchanged.**

*The invocation, and its cost.* **PS-3-11(c), dated eleven years, not withdrawn, requiring a practice card to carry a beat that performs the boundary before issue, was invoked from the wall binder by the clerk of forty-four in about nine seconds on the Wednesday morning. She stated before invoking it that she did not want to be the one who said it out loud, that this was the reason she had to, and that in about four years it would appear with a photograph of the person who did it on it. It is not her rule. Anybody in that office may invoke it on a Monday without asking anybody. Nobody thanked her. She is owed nothing by anybody in this case.**

*What the four days were.* **About nine hundred cards across about eleven sites, withdrawn correctly, the figure being a figure of objects and not of people, and the clerk said so aloud in about nine buildings before she said it anywhere else. **The checking was done by a man of thirty-four who buys training, on his own time, on four days his own employer does not know he took and has not been told, driving between about eleven sites against a printed column. He was thanked at nine sites by people who meant it. At two sites he was asked by a man with a clipboard whether he was from a company, and at the eleventh he said that it is the company that supplies them, and that ended the conversation.**

*What the four days found.* **The card with two typefaces, in nine of about eleven sites. The same card locally reprinted in four of the nine by a printer told he was correcting a misprint, and in one of the four, two hundred copies printed by a man of fifty-eight with a bad hip, above a fish market, for nothing, out of a habit he cannot stop. **Four of the eleven sites hold between them about nine people who are on no rota, two of whom have never been asked whether they want to be there, one of the two being a man of fifty-one with a bad wrist who attends on Fridays and is not on the list. And, in about nine minutes of a Friday afternoon at one of the nine sites, a card that had already been withdrawn was still on a lanyard round a woman's neck while a person who had not read the list was about to tell her that her release was held and current. Nobody was hurt; the release was held and current; the woman established that the outcome was correct and the hour was wrong, and was thanked by nobody for saying so.**

*What the four days did not find.* **Stated first by a man of twenty-nine who works on lifts, unprompted, in about nine words: that the four days were the right four days, that not one of those cards remains in about eleven buildings, and that what is actually happening in about nine of those buildings has not changed and cannot be changed by taking a piece of paper out of a drawer. He was answered yes and did not say anything else. **The standing refusal to go and look was not spent again and was not asked about.**

*The cost of the day nobody is paying for.* **Stated by the clerk of forty-four, in a corridor, on the second morning, to one person, in about nine seconds and once: that the pulling is one day a week, that it is not in her job, that nobody is paying for the day, and that nobody has asked her to stop, and that if she stops then in about four years the standard will still be on that wall and nobody will be pulling cards.**

*State and boundaries.* **A cold room, about nine people, and a woman of about thirty in the chair nearest the door who has been there since about two, described by a count and not by a name or an age or a district. No page, minute, notice, report, register, form, leaflet or constitution was created by anybody in this case; the printed column and the list of withdrawn cards are a company's and a district's and are older than this case. No teaching, demonstration, assessment, assistance or relay by anybody. No panel and no marker. Nine keys needed and two existing, count not stated. The licensor is unnamed and the field is blank. No message has arrived from anybody. Entry 259.**
