# Chapter 257 — Two Sets Of Nine Hundred

The second Friday of the rota ran in a company room off a corridor in Crown Terrace with nothing to hand out, and the rate stood at eleven for about four minutes at ten past four in the afternoon and nobody was told, and in a cold room off a service road a man of fifty-one with a tool bag found out that the same figure had come out of two different drawers on the same Wednesday and that nobody in any building can tell the two apart.

---

Grade three, twenty to eight, on a card in date. Eleven on the Friday list for the week and about four of the eleven not on it, and the number eleven is on the back of a woman's own card in a flat in Nine Bridges and is on no form anywhere and is not a rate, and about eleven people a week in this city are still going to be taught that a release waits for a man in a lanyard.

The stair fire door in Nine Bridges came up for the ninth time in about a month, and he went to it at about half past three, and the closer was reset again, for the seventh time in about a fortnight, and the frame was as he had described it, and there is no fault in it that will go in a box.

The word was at nine and ten all day and it was at eleven for about four minutes at about ten past four. The heating in that room was on its fifty-third day and it is a hundred and fifty-eight days old.

---

Nine folding chairs and eleven people in them and against the wall, which is two more than the chairs, and a laminated sheet on a post by the door with nine words on it and no name and no date and no company and no version.

The cards were gone.

They had been gone since the Wednesday, because a clerk of forty-four read a reference off a wall binder in about nine seconds and about nine hundred of them came out of use across about eleven sites, and the woman of thirty-six with the company lanyard had handed in her own copy on the Thursday along with everyone else's and had done it without one word of complaint, which she mentioned twice.

She said it with no heat in it at all. "**I want it on the record that I did not object. Not for a second. A card is a note. You take the note.**"

"You have not said what you are going to do without it."

"**I am going to say the five things out loud. I have been able to do that for nine years and I have never once had to.**"

---

She was right about that, and it turned out to be the whole of what a room of about nine people could find.

The four runs went faster. They went faster because about nine people were not holding a piece of paper and looking down at it, and because the pause where somebody found the box with the beat in it was a pause that had been paid for every Friday for nine years by about nine people who did not know they were paying it.

The runs still ended on her word. They ended on her word because they had always ended on her word, and the beat that ends a practice and hands the choice to act back to the person doing it is not on a card, and has never been on a card, and is not in the box on the post by the door.

She said it at the end, to the room. "**The cards going has not changed what happens in this room. It has changed what is in your hands, and I am not sure anybody in here has ever been paid to notice that.**"

---

A woman of about thirty-four in the second row asked her a question that was entirely reasonable and that nobody in that room had asked in nine years.

"**Can I have the ending written down? Not to keep. Just so that I know I have got it right.**"

"**No.**"

"**Why not.**"

"**Because it is not written down anywhere. It has not been written down anywhere since I started, and if I put it on a sheet for you then in about four years somebody will put it on a card and in about four years after that it will be a thing that is on a card, and then it will be a thing that is in a person’s pocket, and then somebody will run a session where a person does not have the pocket.**"

"**That is three refusals and one reason.**"

"**That is one reason used three times and I would rather it were one reason than three.**"

Nobody said anything. About four people in that room had been in a job where a practice was handed to them on a card and had never once been shown the ending, and about four people in that room understood that the withdrawal had not taken anything away from them and had only taken away the paper that had been hiding the fact.

---

A man of twenty-nine who works on lifts, in the fourth chair, on the employer's Friday list, said the thing that the day actually produced, and he said it to the floor.

"**There were nine of us last Friday and there are eleven this week. Nobody was told. Nobody came because of a card. I do not know why they came and I have looked at all eleven faces and I do not know one of them from any other Friday.**"

"You have not asked them."

"**No. And I am not going to, and I want that on whatever it is you write, and I am aware of what it is you write and I do not care, because if nine hundred people stop being handed a piece of paper and eleven of them turn up instead, then that is not an improvement and it is not nothing, and we will not find out which for about four years.**"

Nobody thanked him. A man of twenty-one near the door said nothing at all, and about four people looked at the man of twenty-nine, and he looked at the floor.

---

The room off the service road was cold and had been cold since seven in the morning on the Monday of week eighty-three, and a man of fifty-one with a tool bag was in it at about half past one and had been in it twice this month.

He had a folded piece of paper and he put it on the counter and unfolded it and it was the company's own list of the eleven, folded in four, and it is not a good list.

He put one finger on the paper, on the fourth line. "**Those are the eleven. That is what came out on Wednesday. And I know four of those eleven buildings, because I have put a number on a door in each of them, and the number on the door is off a different line of the same list.**"

"How many doors."

"**About nine hundred. Four buildings. Same figure, and it has been the figure for about four years and I have said it out loud in about four rooms and I have never once been thanked.**"

"**And the same figure came out on Wednesday.**"

"**The same figure came out on Wednesday and it is a different nine hundred and there is nobody in this city who can tell the two apart in under about four minutes, and I have wanted that written down for about four years and I have never once wanted it written more than I wanted it not to be true.**"

---

A man of twenty-nine who works on lifts came in at about half past two, and the man of fifty-one asked him, before he had sat down, whether he would go to two of the eleven sites and stand in them for one session each.

He said: "**No. I said why once and that is all.**"

That is nine words, and it is not the reason. He gave the reason the first time in this room on a Thursday a fortnight ago, unprompted, and it is that a list of buildings is a list of people, and he has given it since in a district office on a Monday before anybody had asked him. He was asked, about four minutes later, whether the reason had changed, and said that a reason does not change because four days have passed, and that this is a different sentence and is not the reason. He is not to be asked again and he is not to be given a shorter answer and he is not to be thanked.

He has a page of his own in the ring binder. He has eleven buildings he cannot price, and the clerk of forty-four pulled cards out of nine of them, and he has not gone to any of them, and nobody in that room suggested that he should, except one person, who was told no in nine words.

---

He wrote one thing on a service road, in the dark, at about ten, and the shutter was cold behind him and there was a puddle in the road from the morning.

*260. Friday of week 90, at about ten on a service road in the dark, and this entry is two figures of nine hundred, one question nobody had asked in nine years, and fifty-three days.*

*Conditions and docket.* **Grade three from twenty to eight on a card in date, with the exception for the term recorded and renewed in a system in a box that says *No reason given by holder*. Eleven on the Friday list for the week, of whom about four are not on it. The stair fire door in Nine Bridges was attended at about half past three, was reset for the seventh time in about a fortnight, and was found as described; the description box carries a stamped number and nothing in the frame supports a fault. The rate held at nine and ten and was at eleven for about four minutes at about ten past four. The heating in that room is on its fifty-third day off, unpaid, and is not resolvable by any document and none is proposed. The room is a hundred and fifty-eight days old; a card in a rail in Lattice Ward is a hundred and sixty-two days in the rail and is a third object and is not in that room. The twelve lines on the hardboard are seventy-eight days old and the thirteenth is twenty-nine. Eight items in the ring binder, unread and unchanged. The city's weekly figure stands unaltered, is not a rate, and is on the back of a woman's own card.**

*The cards, and the four runs without them.* **About nine hundred withdrawn across about eleven sites on the Wednesday. The instructor in Crown Terrace handed in the last copy on the Thursday, unobjected to, and reported the handover twice in a room of eleven people. She then ran four runs with no card in any hand and no copy of her own, and stated that she has been able to name the five parts of the practice aloud for nine years and has never had to. The runs went faster and the reason is hers and is correct: nine people were not looking at a piece of paper. The runs ended on the same spoken word they have ended on for nine years. That word is the beat which ends a practice and hands the choice to act back to the practitioner, and it is on no card, has never been on a card, and is not in the box on the post by the door. Nothing in this case touched it and nothing in this case could.**

*The question that had not been asked in nine years, and the three refusals built on one reason.* **A woman of about thirty-four in the second row asked whether the ending could be written down for her to check herself against, and was refused, and gave the reason in the form of three consequences and not one: a sheet becomes a card, a card becomes something in a pocket, and a pocket becomes a session somebody runs for a person who has not got the pocket. She was told that one reason used three times is preferable to three reasons and that this is a preference and not a policy. **The withdrawal removed from that room a piece of paper and did not remove from it anything anybody was relying on, and what it exposed instead is that about four people in that room had been given a practice and never shown the ending, and had not known to ask.** No object was made and nothing was written down and nobody in that room was thanked and nobody was angry.**

*Two nines hundred.* **The figure about nine hundred is carried in this case by two different people and means two different sets of objects. It is about nine hundred practice cards withdrawn across about eleven sites, and it is about nine hundred doors in four buildings carrying a number off a different line of the same company's list, put on by a field service engineer of fifty-one who has said the figure in about four rooms in about four years and has never been thanked. He established on this afternoon that four of the eleven sites are buildings he has worked in, that no person in this city can tell the two sets apart in under about four minutes, and that this is the second time he has asked for the fact to be written down. Nobody wrote it down. The second nine hundred cannot be reached by anybody in this case and its author cannot change one of them.**

*The refusal, and the sentence that is not the reason.* **A man of fifty-one with a tool bag asked a man of twenty-nine who works on lifts, before he had sat down, whether he would go to two of the eleven sites and stand in them for one session each. He refused in nine words, and the words are in the room and are not repeated here. When asked, about four minutes later, whether the reason had changed, he answered that a reason does not change because four days have passed, and that this is a different sentence and is not the reason. The standing ground, given unasked in this room on a Thursday and restated in a district office on a Monday before the question was put, is that a list of buildings is a list of people; it is not to be shortened, a second ground is not to be given, and he is not to be thanked. **Nine of the eleven sites had a card pulled out of them by a clerk of forty-four who is owed nothing; he has gone to none of them; nobody in that room proposed it except one person, who was told no.** He holds a page of his own in the ring binder and cannot price eleven buildings and did not price them today.**

*The rate.* **Eleven for about four minutes at about ten past four, the second time in about a term and a half. The first was on a Friday nine days ago, while about eleven buildings were being cleared in standing water. **He told nobody, then or now. At about ten on a service road he said the number out loud once to an empty road, which is the only time in six volumes he has said it to anything that cannot answer, and he has now done it twice, and both occasions are in this entry and neither was said to a person. He records that he does not know why a fortnight of water and a room that got two people through the door are the same size, and that the not knowing is the part of it he has.** Nobody in Crown Terrace and nobody in the room off the service road was told any of this, and nobody thanked him for not saying a word in a company room on a Friday, which is what he did on the Friday nine days ago when he was the only other person in that building who could have finished a woman of twenty-two's run, and there was nothing there for anybody to notice.**

*State and boundaries.* **Nine chairs and a cold room. A cook of twenty-seven who put the gas ring on at about half past eight, as she has done for about fourteen weeks before that at the same time on the same mornings, and who said a thing of that shape on the Monday for the fourth time in this hearing and did not say it again today, having said she would not say a fifth. A woman of about thirty in the chair nearest the door, described by a count and not by a name or an age or a district, who has been in that room on every working day since the Friday of week sixty-nine because it is warm, and who was not asked anything today, and about four people wanted to. Four words in chalk and one unremovable grey ghost over a door. Thirteen lines on a hardboard on two nails, the first of them a cook's and still the first. Eight items in a ring binder and no page read and no page taken out. No page, minute, notice, report, register, form, leaflet, registration or constitution was created by anybody in this case; the five duties on a laminated post are somebody else's and are not ours. No teaching, demonstration, assessment, assistance or relay by him, and a room off a service road is not a practice in that department and the reason is not that he is the exception. No panel and no marker. Nine keys needed and two existing, count not stated. The standing question is untouched, is not stated, and carries no number. The licensor is unnamed, the field is blank, and no message has arrived from anybody. Entry 260.**
