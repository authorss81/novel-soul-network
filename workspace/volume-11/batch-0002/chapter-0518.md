# Chapter 518 — A Question Asked In An Office Above A Shop, And A Man Saying He Cannot Answer It

**A Friday, and the shape of it was two rooms above a shop with a drawer in them, where a man holding a notice was asked a question about nine flats and answered that he could not answer it, and a woman asked the wrong person anyway, and the wrong person said no in about four seconds.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in the date column and eleven attended, the last of them about six in the evening; the hours were a bench in Lattice Ward to about two, that district's counter to about six, and the four rooms off the service road from about half past six. The rate is nine and ten, wrong, left as it is, said to nobody. **The heating in the tenement above the road is on and dearer, four names on one lease are carrying it, and a fortnight that one cook laid out of her own money is on nobody's account and is expected in the spring to be the same.** The room off that service road is eight hundred and seventy-nine days old, which is one hundred and twenty-five weeks and four days, and is warm, and is four rooms and a back one.

The card in the rail along the counter front is eight hundred and eighty-three days, four days beyond the room, and the rail holds two. A drawer in a room above a shop with nine forms in it and nothing in eight of the second columns is a fifth object in this entry and is not that card and is not in that rail.

---

There are two rooms and a kettle and a table and about nine chairs and a drawer, and the drawer is where the paper is, and the paper is about nine forms with a company mark on them and a second column on all nine.

The woman who lets the room is about thirty-nine and she agreed about three weeks ago that three rooms could bring their answers there on two evenings a week, and two of the three have been, and the third has not and has never been told that it exists.

The rota man came in at about two on the Friday afternoon with the printed sheet in an inside pocket and sat down in the chair nearest the door, which is a chair he chose on purpose because it is the one you can leave from without walking in front of anybody.

**"I am not here about the forms."**

**"Nobody is ever here about the forms. Say what you are here about."**

**"I am here because a man in a corridor made a question smaller at me and I have not been able to put it down since, and I want to know if this room is a room or a corridor."**

**"It is a room, and it is not a corridor, and those are not the same as safe."**

**"Then what is it."**

**"It is a room with a table and about nine chairs and a woman who lets it, and I have never once in this room told anybody that a thing was fine."**

---

**"How many of your nine flats would object to that setting."**

She said it the way a person reads a line off a list, and she said it before he had taken the sheet out, and Marek, who had come in behind him and had not said that he was in the room, understood that she had been expecting him since the Monday of the week before.

**"I cannot tell you that."**

**"You keep the rota. You know the building."**

**"I keep the rota. That is not the same as knowing what nine people would say about a relay setting, and the difference between those two is the whole of your question and it is the reason the question is not a fair one."**

**"It is a normal question."**

**"It is a normal question in the same way that a doctor's waiting room is a normal place to be asked what your next of kin is called. The normalness is not the difficulty. The difficulty is that the answer would be made up and you would carry it."**

**"I have carried made-up answers for eleven years. I carried one about a gas check in a flat on the second and somebody did not call a man for nine days."**

**"Then you know exactly what a made-up answer costs and you are asking me to hand you one, and if I do that then I am not a room, I am the corridor with a better chair."**

---

The rota man put the sheet on the table face up and turned it round so that the nine lines were facing her and did not say anything for about eleven seconds.

**"Then tell me what I am supposed to do with this."**

**"You are supposed to take it to whoever set the setting and ask them to write down what it is for and what it changes, and if they will not write it down, you do not put it on the board, and your inspection at the end of the month will be an inspection of a board with nothing on it, and I do not know what happens then and neither do you."**

**"It happens with my name on it."**

**"Yes."**

**"And a flat on the fourth floor that I found last year."**

**"Yes. And I am not going to soften that either, and I have said so before in a different room and I will say it again."**

**"You said it in a room where five people could hear you. You are saying it in a room with about nine chairs and a drawer and a woman who lets it and I do not know which of those is worse."**

**"It is worse. And I would still rather say it here where a woman of thirty-nine can hear me say it than in a room with five strangers in it where the only person who is listening is the person I am talking to."**

---

She turned round in her chair then, and looked at Marek, and made the mistake, and made it in a way that made it clear she had been about to make it since the Monday.

**"You are the one people go to. Is that setting safe in a building with nine flats."**

Marek put the tool he was holding down on the bench, which he does not do, and which took him about a second and a half longer than it should have.

**"No."**

**"You have not looked at it."**

**"I have not looked at it and I could not tell you if I had, and that is the whole answer, and I am not going to decorate it."**

**"You could say the honest thing and let her decide."**

**"She is not mine to send things to, and I am not hers to send things from, and if I bring you a question from this room and she answers it then this room has found a way in to an office that was set up so that it could not, and it would have got there in about eleven minutes because I am the person people go to, and that is the one thing about me that has never once been a qualification."**

**"Then what are you."**

**"I am a man in a room who has just been asked to do the only thing I am good at, and I have said no to it in about four seconds, and the four seconds were not me thinking."**

**"The green stair,"** the rota man said, from the chair nearest the door.

**"The green stair is not a shortcut to me and I have told you that in a warm room and I am telling you here."**

**"Then what is the office for."**

**"The office is for a person to bring a thing they were asked to agree to, and it is not for a person to bring a thing to me, and I have eleven weeks of not being allowed to speak for her and I am not going to start on a Friday afternoon in a room with a drawer in it."**

---

Nobody in that room thanked anybody. The woman of about thirty-nine said one thing before the rota man left and it was to Marek and it was about nine words long.

**"That was the correct answer and I want you to know that I am not going to act on it either, and I am not going to tell you why, and you will not be able to guess."**

**"I can guess."**

**"Do not."**

The rota man left at about twenty past three with the sheet in the same inside pocket it was in on the Thursday of the week before, and the inspection at the end of the month is in about two and a half weeks, and the board in the entrance of a block of four storeys has nothing on it, and that is now a decision, and at about two o'clock this afternoon it was only a sheet in a coat.

**He named the cost out loud, in the room, to two people, and he had named it twice before in two other rooms, and the third time it was shorter and it was the only one of the three that he believed.** The fourth floor. Nine days in a flat on the second. And a man of thirty-one on the same landing who will get the Friday and does not want it and has still not been told.

---

He came into the room off the service road at about half past six and there were four people in it for the temperature and one of them was the man of about fifty-one, whose bag was on the floor at his feet, on the four hundred and eighty-fifth day of his saying nothing, which is sixty-nine weeks and two days.

**What he wanted on the Friday evening, and did not have, was to have been asked in a room above a shop and been able to say the thing he is good at, and the not being able to is the arrangement working and it cost him the only thing he wanted that day.** He has been the person who knows for about two years and he has been the person who is asked for about two years, and on the Friday the two came apart in a room with nine chairs, and the coming apart took about four seconds, and he has thought about the four seconds every hour since.

**The decision he took on the Friday evening cost him a Friday evening of not saying anything at all.** He could have written the nine lines out in his own words in about six minutes and the woman of thirty-nine would have put them on the table and the rota man would have taken them to a board in a block of nine flats and the inspection at the end of the month would have been satisfied and nobody would have learned anything about a relay. He did not do it and he did not go back to the second floor of the office to say that he had been asked to. He went to the counter and did the two hours and said nothing to anybody, and the two hours are the cost, and the cost is his and not the rota man's and not the nine flats'.

The woman of about thirty in the chair nearest the door was in the room for about an hour. Nobody asked her anything, nobody said thank you to her, and nobody said sorry to her, and the page that is hers is the eighth of eight in a ring binder on a shelf and was not read on the Friday.

On the far side of the road there is a man of about thirty-three who holds four buildings and about nine hundred doors, and not one of the four has a stamp, a reference or a date on it, and he was not in this city, and his hold ran on for seventy-three weeks and one day with nothing asked of him and nothing given to him, and he is not the man who came in off a bus on the Monday and has not been told that this one exists. The ask is at five hundred and sixty-nine days, which is eighty-one weeks and two days, counted from the day it was asked.

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.
