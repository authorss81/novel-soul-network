**And that is the whole of the reason, and it is not a good one, and he said so to the woman on the counter in about four seconds and she did not argue with him about it.**

**"I have not told you anything."**

**"Then that is five people and I have not asked you for your name and I am not going to."**

**"Then ask me for the two days and I will tell you whether they were worth it."**

**What he wanted, and did not say, on that Thursday at about ten past seven, was to be asked a second question, and to be given about nine minutes in that shop, and to be the person who explained the four towns to a woman who has been in none of them.**

**So he did not say it. He asked her one question and she answered it and it was not the answer he came for and he will be able to hear it for about four years, and the cost of not saying it is that a woman of about fifty-eight with a shop and a drawer and a bin behind her is still behind a counter with no cold behind her and nobody in this city has her number, and he could have had that number on the back of a job ticket in about nine seconds on the Thursday night, and he has not written a fifth thing on the back of a job ticket in this city in about four months and he is not going to start on a Saturday in a town.**

**It is about nine seconds. It is asked in a face by a person who has an ordinary job and who is not on anything, and it is answered in a face by the person who paid.**

**"Were the two days worth it."**

**"No."**

**"Right."**

**"Do not stop there, because in about four days I am going to be in a room and somebody is going to stand up and say the two days were worth it, and I have come four days on a bus to be able to say it to a face before they do it to a room."**

**"Say it then."**

**"The two days were not worth it and the man with the bar is a careful man who did the right thing in four minutes and I have got seventy-eight pounds and a lift that has been running since the Monday and I would do the same two days again and I am not going to pretend to a stranger on a Thursday that I would not."**

**"Those are two answers."**

**"They are one answer with the top of it taken off and the bottom of it left on, and if you ask me to do it in one word I will do it in one word for you and it will be the wrong one, and I have four days of bus travel to think about which one I actually believe and I have not got to the bottom of it and I am not going to pretend to."**

A man of about thirty-four who mends pumps is in that town for the last four days of the week and is not in the shop and has not been told that anybody has come, and about nine houses in that week have had a visit from him and the last of them was on the Tuesday and none of the nine had asked him anything either.

**The man at the counter tried, twice, to give her a sentence she could use in a room, and she took neither of them, and the second time she told him why, and the reason is the sentence he is carrying four days on a bus back with.**

**"Say the first one."**

**"You can say the man with the bar did the right thing in four minutes and the two days after that were the correct process arriving too late, and that is true and it will sound like a complaint about a firm."**

**"I will not say it."**

**"Then say the second one."**

**"You can say the institution is too slow and the answer is a faster institution, and about nine people in that room will agree with you and about four hundred competent people in your city will be able to say the paragraph and not one of them will have anywhere to put an objection to it."**

**"I will not say that one either."**

**"Why."**

**"Because the second one has a plan hidden at the bottom of it and I am not going to be the person who carries a plan into a room in a town four days of bus travel away, and I have read what a plan does to the people who are not in the room and I am one of them and so are you."**

**"That is a fair thing to say and it is not the thing I came for."**

**"No. It is the thing you are getting. The thing you came for is a woman saying no in nine seconds and you have got it and it is a worse thing than the two sentences and I am sorry for you and I am not going to improve it."**
