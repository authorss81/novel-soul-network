**"Say the second thing again and I will tell you whether it is right."**

**"It is that the office should say, in public, this week, that one place for every answer in this city is a bad idea, and say why, in about nine words, and not take a side about anything else."**

**"No."**

**"You have not said why."**

**"That is about one person a week against about four hundred people who watched a thing get refused and are waiting to hear which way to read it."**

**"That is the number and I have done the same arithmetic you have, and I have got a different answer, and I am not going to soften it, and if you want me to soften it you can ask me on a different day and I will not."**

**"I am not asking you to soften it. I am telling you that a person cannot be expected to do that arithmetic at eleven at night with nothing in front of them."**

**"Then a person is going to be hurt by a thing that is not the office's fault, and about nine people in this corridor are going to hear the whole of this, and I am not going to say it more slowly for them."**

**"You are not the reason that office exists."**

**"No. And nobody in this city is going to be able to say that either, and that is the part I would like you to hear in a corridor and not in a room: a committee of about nine people decided in a room with nine chairs in it, on a Thursday, in a building with a shuttered window, and the reason was four rooms in four districts and about four hundred competent people and a company with a form, and none of those was a man of twenty-two and none of those is going to be a man of twenty-two. If anybody tells you it was you, ask them for the date on the paper, and I will put it in front of them myself and I will do it in about nine seconds and I will not enjoy it."**

That is nine words over four sentences and it is the whole of the thing he came to say and the whole of the thing he is allowed to say about it, and it is said in a face in a public corridor with about nine people in it, and the corridor did not stop.

**"The risk of my position is not the one you are worried about and I will name mine out loud because you have named yours twice. If I say it this week then a person who is frightened will bring it to me, because I am the only thing in this city that has been put in a room and been asked, and I will have to say the sentence that says it is not mine, and there is a woman in this city who has been saying that sentence for about four years for nothing and she is better at it than I am and she has got no office and no limit and no eleven weeks, and that is the cost of my taking a position, and it is not on me, it is on her, and she does not know I am talking about her."**

**"Then do not take a position."**

**"I am not going to take a position and I am also not going to tell you that I agree with you, because I do not agree with you, and because a person who has been doing this for eleven years has learned that agreeing with a person in a corridor is cheaper than disagreeing and that the cheap version is the one that costs somebody else."**

**"Say that again."**

**"No. I said it once and I meant it and I am not going to be a person who repeats a thing to be agreeable."**

About four minutes. Neither of them raised her voice. About nine people went along that corridor in those four minutes and about four of them heard the whole of it and about four of those four have given their own account of it since and every one of the four accounts is a different account of which of the two was talking most.

**It was not resolved. It was not softened. It was not a scene. **"Nothing. I would like you to write down that I came a fourth time, and that I am not coming again, and put it nowhere, and that is the whole of what I have come for and I have said it out loud to a person who is not the office and you are not the office."**

**"That is three things."**

**"It is one thing in three parts and you have counted them, and that is the fourth time this year somebody has counted my sentences for me."**
