- **590 (Thursday 1408, entry 593, the close).** A man of about fifty-eight who drives a van, owed nothing, says in a face that the count will hold on a board with a gap in it — and that the holding is worse for everybody including himself; nobody thanks him; the shutter comes down at about ten with no Sunday on the day; the drawer is not opened and the nine lines are not written for the sixth time.
