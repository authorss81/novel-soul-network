# Chapter 557 — He Was Asked To Help By A Man Of About Thirty-Eight With A Cold Store On A Trade Route And He Said No In About Nine Seconds And Gave A Reason That Was Correct, And The Man Accepted It And Did Not Ask Twice, And Something Still Broke On The Tuesday

**The Friday was a bench in Lattice Ward until about two, a counter until about six, and a room off that service road from about half past six, and its shape was a question with a technical answer in it, put by a man who had come four hours on a bus with the answer written down and put away before he got to the counter.**

---

The card in the rail along the front of that counter is nine hundred and seventy-four days, one hundred and thirty-nine weeks to the day, four days more than the room, and the rail takes two.

---

**He had a piece of paper with a diagram on it and the diagram was good. It was a door, a compressor, a thermostat, and a clip, and there was a mark on the thermostat and a mark on the clip and an arrow from one to the other, and the arrow was wrong.**

**"Is that right,"** the man said, **"that the clip's letting the thermostat know."**

**"No."**

**"Right."** He folded the paper in half. **"That's what I came for. I've been to two of these and one of them told me it was right and one of them told me to write to the office on the card and I've been in a bad way about what to do for about nine days."**

**"What's the store."**

**"Two chests and a lorry box, on a trade route, about nine hours' drive from here, and the box runs on a diesel set and the set is a noise the whole time."**

**"And the door's been open."**

**"The door's been open about four minutes twice a day for about nine years,"** the man said. **"It's a trade route, the lorry's there about six hours, it's not a farm, you're not going to stand in a doorway for nine years waiting for a bus."**

**"And the seal's held."**

**"The seal's held and the compressor's short-cycled about four times a day since about August, which it doesn't do, and I've had a man from the firm up in October who looked at it and wrote on a form that the seal was serviceable."**

**"And you want to know whether that's the seal or the thermostat."**

**"I want to know whether that's the seal or the thermostat, and I'd have gone out there and taken it apart and I'd have known by the Tuesday, and I have not got a Tuesday, and I've got a Wednesday when a man's coming, and if the compressor's the thing that's been going, I've got two dead ones sitting in a lock-up in this city and about nine days of no cold and about nine people on a run who'll have nothing to put in a box."**

---

**He could have said yes. There were about four versions of yes available and every one of them would have been competent, and he has been able to take a thing apart and say which part of it is tired since he was seventeen, and this is a man who knows it.**

**He said no in about nine seconds and he gave a reason and the reason was correct.**

**"No. And the reason is that if I go, I will come back and tell you it is the thermostat, and you will believe me, and you will change it, and if I am wrong then in about four months the compressor is going to be the thing and it will have gone short-cycling for four months with a man in this city having told you it was something else."**

**"That is fair."**

**"And the other reason is worse and I'm not going to pretend it isn't. I have been asked for things by people in four towns for about four years and I have said yes to a number of them and every one of those people has told somebody else afterwards that I looked at it, and there are now about nine people in four towns who think I have been in their building."**

**"Have you been in their building."**

**"I have been in one of them and I was twenty-one and I went to look at a pump."**

**"Right."**

**"So no. And I am not going to soften it and I am not going to give you half of it, and I am not going to write it down for you, and if you want a name, the man in a yard on a wide road can tell you what a card is worth and it is nine days, and a firm with a counter and a yellow strip on it will hand forty of them to anybody who turns up with a carrier bag."**

**"That's the answer."**

**"That is not the answer to your question. That is the answer to the other thing you didn't ask me."**

---

**The man of about thirty-eight put the folded paper in his pocket and looked at the wall list for about four seconds and then said the thing that made this the hardest of the ten days so far.**

**"I've been in this shop three times. My first time, about two years ago, I asked a man who was on the bench whether the seal on my box was all right and he said come back and see me on a Thursday and I came back on a Thursday and he was busy and I waited forty minutes and he did about nine minutes on it and he charged me nothing and he told me a thing about short-cycling that I still use."**

**"I remember."**

**"I know you remember. I want to say it anyway,"** the man said, **"because you have just told me no and told me why and told me what a card is worth, and both of those cost you something and I have got about nine people on a run and about two dead compressors, and I'm not going to be the man who says you were no help. You were no help. I want that on the record in a shop, from me, on a Friday."**

**"That's not a thing that goes on a record in a shop."**

**"It's a thing that goes on a record in a shop,"** the man said, **"and you have got a drawer about nine feet from me and I am not asking you to open it."**

---

**He did not ask twice. He asked the woman of about thirty-four about a firm on a wide road and she wrote down four lines on the back of a docket and gave them to him and said the first one is the one that will not want to speak to him and the other three will.**

**And on the Tuesday, four days later, at about eleven in the morning, a compressor on a trade route four hours' drive from this city cut out for about nine minutes, and a lorry box on a trade route four hours' drive from this city came up to about eleven degrees over about nine hours, and about nine people who should have had something in a box did not have it in a box, and the man of about thirty-eight found out at about two in the afternoon and did not tell anybody about it because there was nobody within about nine hours' drive to tell, and he fixed the compressor himself on the Thursday with a part that cost about nine pounds.**

**He did not blame the man at the counter. Nobody in that trade route has ever said the name of this shop out loud. The refusal was correct and the Tuesday happened, and neither of those cancels the other, and both of them are on the same page.**

---

**What the man of about twenty-two wanted, and did not say, on that Friday, and did not say on the Tuesday, was to be somebody whose no is worth having. A man's no is worth having when there is about a hundred good yeses on the other side of it. He has about four, and one of the four was a pump in a building in a town when he was twenty-one, and the ninety-nine have not arrived yet, and he is twenty-two, and there is no arrangement under which they arrive in a year.**
