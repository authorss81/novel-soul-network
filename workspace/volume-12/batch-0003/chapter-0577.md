# Chapter 577 — A Pavement In A Second District At Ten In The Morning With About Nine People On It Waiting For A Signature In A Box, A Man Of About Sixty-One Who Signs Four Hundred Of Them Without Reading One, And Two Figures That Are Each Other On The Same Morning

**The Thursday was a bench in Lattice Ward until about two, a counter from about half past ten until about six, and a pavement in a second district between about eleven and one, and its shape was a queue for a pen, and nobody standing in it was angry.**

---

Eleven jobs on the Thursday, every one of them dated, eleven people through that counter, and the last of them at about five. The bench until two, and then a counter from about half past ten rather than two, because the bench had a machine on it that would not go out until the middle of the afternoon. **The heat in that flat is on and dearer than the winter's, four names on that tenancy are carrying the difference, and a fortnight a cook laid out of her own wages is owed by nobody and is going to be owed by nobody at the end of this month.** The rooms off that service road are one thousand and eleven days, one hundred and forty-four weeks and three days, four of them and one behind, and they are warm.

The card in the rail along the front of that counter is one thousand and fifteen days, one hundred and forty-five weeks to the day, four days past the room, and the rail takes two.

**The eighteenth line on two nails above the Lattice Ward bench is seven hundred and twenty-nine days old, one hundred and four weeks and one day, and seven hundred and twenty-nine is also the day the hold of the man of about thirty-three began, and that hold is six hundred and forty-four days, ninety-two weeks to the day, and six hundred and forty-four is also the day the eighteenth line was written on that board.** One of those two figures is an age and one of the two is a day something happened, they stand on the same morning of this Thursday, and neither of them is a day anything happened to the heating in this city, and the heating in that case is a closed thing with no figure of days off for it carried for it anywhere in this entry.

**The man of about fifty-one at the north wall of those rooms is six hundred and seventeen days, eighty-eight weeks and one day, and the ask is seven hundred and one days, one hundred weeks and one day, and the post at the corridor end is five hundred and fifty-nine days, seventy-nine weeks and six days. None of those three is a day anything happened to the heating either, and two of them are in a different district from each other and that is the whole of what the three of them have in common.**

---

**The office is a first floor above a line of shop units and it opens at nine and it is the office that signs the box on the back of a form, and about nine people were on the pavement outside it at ten in the morning, and four of the nine had come up four days.**

There is no list. There is a pad on a desk by the window, a pen on a string, and a man of about sixty-one who has signed the box on the back of a form since before the four towns had a card that took nine days.

**He does not read them. Not one of the about four hundred he has signed, and he is not slow and he is not careful and he is not tired, and about four people in this city have asked him about it and he has given the same answer to all four of them: I am not paid to read it and if I read it I am a person who decides and I do not want to be that.**

**Nobody in that queue was angry. That is in this entry because it is the reason the queue works and because a queue of about nine people on a pavement at ten in the morning with four of them four days into a journey is not a thing anybody in this city expected, and about four of them had told about four other people in four towns that there would be a queue and had not said what kind.**

At about eleven a man of about thirty-eight got to the front of that pavement, had his sheet signed in about four seconds, and said, in about nine seconds, to nobody in particular and to everybody:

**"One pen. About four hundred of us. Four towns. And nobody in this queue is angry, and the reason nobody in this queue is angry is that every one of us is waiting for the same four seconds."**

Nobody agreed with him. About four of the nine looked at him and one of them said, in about four seconds, that the four seconds were not the point, and the man of about thirty-eight said he knew, and the queue moved on.

---

**Marek was in that queue from about eleven and got in at the back and nobody moved him and nobody asked him to and there was no room on that pavement for eleven people at ten in the morning in this weather and he was the eleventh.**

**About four people in that queue knew who he was and none of them said so out loud until a woman of about forty-six, who had been in the front four by the time he was halfway back, turned round and said his name.**

**"You've not got one of these."**

**"No."**

**"Then you'll not get it signed."**

**"I know."**

**"Well you can come up,"** she said, **"and I'll not say you've come, and he'll not ask you, and if he asks you then you can tell him you've come to see whether a person signs without reading, which is what you've come for, and I don't know anybody in this queue who hasn't come for that."**

**He went up. The office is two rooms and a landing and a window with a rail on it. A woman of about thirty-four was at a desk in the first room. The man of about sixty-one was in the second at a table with the pad and the pen on the string.**

**"Have you got one of these?"**

**"No."**

**"Right,"** the man said, and went back to the pad, and it took him about four seconds to look up and about four seconds to look down, and he did not ask where the man had come from and he did not ask what he was and he has not asked since and will not.

**Marek was in that building for about eleven minutes and then he was on the pavement again and he said goodbye to nobody, because the woman of about forty-six had gone back to the front of the queue.**

**What he noticed on that Thursday morning, and did not say to anybody, and has not written down anywhere, is that about four of the nine on that pavement had come four days for a signature and could not have said what was in the box they were getting signed, and that not one of the nine was angry, and that a queue of nine people who are not angry keeps its own order without anybody organising it, and that this is the same thing as the four towns and it is the reason the four towns are in a mess and the reason nobody in them is furious, and both of those are true at once and about four people in this city have noticed the first half and nobody has noticed the second half.**

**What he wanted and did not say, and did not say to the woman of about forty-six and will not say to her, was to be at the front of that pavement, and to be there as one of the nine and not as the man who turns up in rooms. He wanted it in about the first four minutes and after that he did not want it, and the after is the part he has not told anybody about, and the after is that a man at the front of that queue has a sheet with a box on it, and a man at the back of it has nothing at all, and the only difference between the two is about four seconds with a pen.**

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.
