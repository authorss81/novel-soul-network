**He came in at about ten past four on the Thursday with nothing in his hands, and he had come up from the bus depot that morning, and he had a folded sheet in the inside pocket of a coat that has been in that coat since the spring.**

**"I drove four days on Monday to stand in a yard in a town four days of bus travel away,"** he said, **"and I am not going to stand in a yard, I have done that, and I am going to stand here for about nine seconds and say one thing a woman said to me and then go and catch the bus."**

**"Go on."**

**"She said: the four sentences on the back of the forms are the right sentences and I have been saying one of them for about four years and I have never once said it out loud to anybody who wanted writing, and the reason my clip is not coming is that I have never asked for it, and if I ask now, after four years, it will cost the four people who have been covering for me since the spring, and I have done that sum on a piece of paper and I am not going to ask, and the stoppage is not going to end."**

**"That's the whole of it."**

**"That's the whole of it,"** the man of about thirty-three said. **"And she said it standing at that gate with a dog coming across the yard behind her, and she said it in about nine seconds, and she did not ask me to remember it, and she did not ask me to write it down, and when I said I would write it down she said don't."**

Nobody thanked anybody.

**He was not thanked. She was not thanked, and she does not know that he drove four days, and about four people in this city have since repeated the nine seconds and none of the four has said thank you to either of them, and the man of about thirty-three has told the story about four times and has told it the same way every time, and about four of the people who heard it have said that it is the worst thing anybody has said to them this year, and none of them has said it to her.**
