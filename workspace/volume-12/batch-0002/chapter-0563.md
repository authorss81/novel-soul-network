# Chapter 563 — A Clinic In The First Of The Four Towns Turning Down A Thing This City Offered It, Correctly, And The Reason In About Nine Words From A Person Who Is Not Named, And About Nine People On Wages On The Wrong Side Of Being Right

**The Thursday was a bench in Lattice Ward until about two, a counter until about six, and a room off a service road from about half past six, and its shape was a building in a town four days of bus travel away, put here from a bus window by a person who was not on any of the things that happened in it.**

---

The Thursday was eleven long and the eleven of them all carried a date, and eleven people came through that counter and the last of them at about ten past five. Bench until two, the counter two to six, four rooms off that service road from about half past six with the heat on in one of them. **The heating there is on and above what it was through the winter, four names on that tenancy are carrying the rise between them, and a fortnight of a cook's own wages is still owed by nobody and is going to be owed by nobody at the end of this month.** The service-road rooms are nine hundred and eighty-three days, one hundred and forty weeks and three days, four of them and one behind, and they are warm.

The card in the rail along the front of that counter is nine hundred and eighty-seven days old, one hundred and forty-one weeks to the day, four days more than the room, and the rail takes two.

**The thirteenth line on two nails above the bench in Lattice Ward is eight hundred and fifty-four days old, one hundred and twenty-two weeks to the day, and the eighteenth is seven hundred and one days, one hundred weeks and one day, and the twentieth line on that board has never been started and is not going to be started in the ten days this entry is one of.** The man of about fifty-one at the north wall of those service-road rooms is five hundred and eighty-nine days old, eighty-four weeks and one day, and the ask is six hundred and seventy-three days, ninety-six weeks and one day, and neither of those two figures is a day anything happened to the heating in this city, and the heating is a closed thing in this case with no figure of days off for it in it anywhere.

---

**It is put here from the outside of a gate in the first of the four towns, and the person putting it here is a man of about thirty-four who drove up in a van on the Wednesday and sat in a bus on the Thursday morning and looked out of a window, and he has never been inside the building and has never been asked anything by anybody in it.**

The building is one room and a store room and a room behind the store room, and it has a green door that was repainted in about the spring and has a bench outside it that about four old men sit on in the afternoons for no reason anybody has established.

**On the Monday of ten days ago a man of about forty-four in this city had written a thing on a sheet and had put the sheet through a door in a building on a wide road, and the sheet went to about nine places, and one of the nine places was that building, and what the sheet offered was a place on a list of about eleven buildings that could be asked a question and be answered in a day.**

**The clinic said no. It said no to the list, and it said no to a card, and it said no to a man of about thirty-three who came up on the Sunday night with a hired van's key and a folded docket and about four days of bus travel behind him and who asked, in about nine seconds, whether it wanted to be on a list, and it said no to him as well, and it said no to a woman of about thirty-four who came up on the Wednesday with four boxes she could not sell, and it said no to all four, and it did not say no to the office of the woman of about twenty-four because that office has never asked it anything and would not.**

**Nobody in that clinic is a villain. Nobody in that clinic is a hero. The woman who runs it is about thirty-one and has run it for about nine years and she is the reason it is a clinic and not a room, and the nine people on her wages are the reason it is open on a Wednesday, and both of those are ordinary and neither of them is a decision anybody made this month.**

---

**And here is the reason, in about nine words, said out loud, on a Wednesday afternoon in the spring, in a face, to a man of about forty-four who had come down from this city for it, by a person who is not named in this entry and is not going to be named in any of these ten.**

**"In April we had a man in a bed for nine days."**

That is the whole of it. It is eleven words and it took about four seconds and the man of about forty-four did not say anything for about nine seconds afterwards, and then he asked the question he had come four days to ask, and she answered it, and the answer is not the reason.

**"Then put us on the list."**

**"No."**

**"But the list is eleven buildings and a question asked of eleven is a question asked of about nine hundred people, and you are one of nine that are open, and the other eight are in this city."**

**"Yes."**

**"And being one of nine is why you should be on it."**

**"No,"** she said. **"It is why I have said no four times this week and I am going to say it a fifth time to a man in a van on Sunday and he will take it well and he will not ask me why, and I would like to know whether you can see the shape of that from where you are standing, because I cannot."**

**"I can't."**

**"Then you have come four days to be told no by a person in a room with a bed in it, and I am sorry about the four days, and I am not going to change it, and you may write that down or you may not, and I would rather you did not."**

---

**Here is what is on the wrong side of being right, and it is about nine people, and they are on wages, and each of the nine is named by a job and not by a name, and none of the nine has been asked about the list by anybody including the woman who runs the clinic.**

**The woman who does the injections, who has done them for about nine years and who is about forty-four. The woman who keeps the book, who is about twenty-six and who has kept it for about two years and who asked, in the spring, whether the list would put the book somewhere, and was told it would, and has not asked again. The man who drives the van, who is about thirty-three and who is on the road four days of this week. The two women who do the washing up and the beds, who are sisters, who are about thirty-one and about twenty-nine, and who are on the same rota and have never once been asked what they think a list is. The young man of about nineteen who carries the stores and who asked the woman who keeps the book, in about nine seconds in a corridor last month, whether being on a list would mean people knew the clinic was there, and was told yes, and said **"Right"** and carried on. The woman of about fifty-one who does the Friday morning hours alone. The man of about thirty-eight who does the building's other side, an office with a door that is locked on a Friday, and who is on that clinic's wages four days a week and on somebody else's the other three.**

**That is eight. The ninth is a woman of about thirty-six who does two days a week and who was not in that room on the Wednesday and who has said, in about nine seconds, in a corridor, to a man who had come down from this city: we have a list of eleven and we are the one that says no, and when the other ten are asked a question in a year we will be the one that is not asked, and I have thought about that for about four days and I have not told anybody, and you may go back on the bus.**

**Nobody thanked the woman of about thirty-one for saying no. Nobody thanked the eight or the nine. The man of about forty-four wrote it down anyway, because he was told he might, and he wrote it down in a hand that is not his own, and the sheet went into his coat, and the coat went onto a bus, and the bus took about eleven hours, and he was in this city by about seven in the evening and did not come to the counter and has not come to the counter since and has sent nobody.**

**What he noticed, on that bus, and did not say, and has not put on anything, was that in about nine days he had heard four different people in four different towns say the same sentence in four different mouths, and the sentence was a refusal, and every one of the four was correct, and the four of them together had made a shape with about nine people on wages inside it and no way out of it at all, and that he was the reason the four of them had been asked at all, and that being the reason was not the same as being able to do anything about it, and he has known that distinction for about eleven years and has never before had four examples of it in a row on four days.**

**What he wanted and did not say on that Thursday, and did not write down, and did not send anybody, was to go back on the Sunday and stand in that room with the bed in it for an hour and say nothing, which is a thing he has never done in eleven years of work and which would have cost him two days of a bench and about eleven hours of a bus each way, and which would have changed nothing at all, and he wanted it, and the wanting is the entire entry.**

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.

*The clinic, and four refusals in a week, and the eleven words.* **The building: one room, a store room, and a room behind the store room, with a green door repainted in about the spring and a bench outside it that about four old men sit on in the afternoon for no established reason. The offer: a list of about eleven buildings, made on a sheet by a man of about forty-four in this city and put through a door on a wide road ten days ago, on which a place could be asked a question and be answered in a day. *What the day did, and what it did not touch.* **A clinic that is right turned down four correct things in a week and about nine people on wages are on the wrong side of that rightness, and none of the nine was asked about it by anybody, and the person who said the reason is not named in this entry.** Nobody thanked the clinic, nobody thanked the nine, nobody thanked the man of about thirty-four who drove up and asked in nine seconds, and nobody thanked the woman of about thirty-four with four boxes. He taught nobody, demonstrated nothing, assessed nobody and assisted nobody on this Thursday, and nobody asked him to.
