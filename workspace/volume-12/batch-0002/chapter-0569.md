# Chapter 569 — Told Second-Hand By A Person Who Was In A Room What The Woman Of About Twenty-Four Said About The Clinic, And It Was About Nine People On Wages And Not About Him, And He Is The Reason She Was In That Room, And He Said Both Of Those In About Nine Seconds In A Yard And Was Not Agreed With

**The Monday was a bench in Lattice Ward until about two, a counter until about six, and a yard behind a firm on a wide road from about half past six, and its shape was about nine seconds in a yard in which a man of twenty-two said a true thing about himself to a stranger and was told, in a face, that it was the second true thing and not the first.**

---

The Monday was eleven on the docket and every one of the eleven carried a date and eleven people came through that counter and the last of them at about ten past five. A bench in Lattice Ward took the morning, the counter had the middle four hours, and the rooms off that service road had the evening from about half past six. **The heating in that flat is on and dearer than it was through the winter, four names on that tenancy are carrying the rise between them, and a fortnight a cook laid out of her own wages is owed by nobody and is going to be owed by nobody at the end of this month.** The service-road rooms are nine hundred and ninety-four days old, one hundred and forty-two weeks to the day, four of them and one behind, and they are warm.

The card in the rail along the front of that counter is nine hundred and ninety-eight days, one hundred and forty-two weeks and four days, four days more than the room, and the rail takes two.

**The nineteenth line on two nails above that bench is six hundred and ninety days, ninety-eight weeks and four days, and the man of about fifty-one at the north wall of those service-road rooms is six hundred days, which is eighty-five weeks and five days, and the hold is six hundred and twenty-seven days, eighty-nine weeks and four days. Not one of those three figures is a day anything happened to the heating in this city, and the heating is a closed thing in this case with no figure of days off for it in it anywhere.**

---

**He was in the yard behind the firm on the wide road at about half past six on the Monday evening because there were nine crates in it and about four days left on them and about four men standing about in it, and about two of the four had heard him ask a question on the Friday of a fortnight ago and had not stopped talking about it since, which is a thing that happens to a man who says a true thing in a yard and is a cost and not a reward.**

**A man of about thirty-three who drives for a firm had come in at about ten past five with a folded sheet of paper in his coat, and it was folded twice and it was not a docket and it was not a form, and he put it on the counter and said four words.**

**"Somebody sent me this."**

**"Who."**

**"A woman in the first of the four towns. She gave it to a man in a kitchen in the second of them and he gave it to me and I've had it since Tuesday and I haven't read it in front of anybody."**

**"Then read it in front of me."**

**"No,"** the man said. **"I'll read it to you and then you'll have heard it and that's worse for me."**

---

**And he read it out in a shop in this city, and it took about nine seconds, and it is what the woman of about twenty-four said about the clinic in a room in the first of the four towns, and he was in that room, and it is not the long version and it is not the short version and it is a third thing that has never been given to anybody in this city.**

**"She said: the clinic is right. The clinic has been right four times in a fortnight and I have been in this city for four years telling about four hundred people they may refuse, and the clinic is the first place I have ever heard a refusal that was going to cost somebody something, and she did it in a room with a bed in it."**

**"And then."**

**"And then she said: don't come and fix it. That's the whole of what she said about the clinic and she said it before anybody asked her a question about it, and about four of us were in that room and not one of us has been able to say anything else about the clinic since."**

**"Anything else."**

**"Anything else,"** the man said, **"because she said the second half about four seconds after the first half and the second half was about nine people on wages and one of them does the injections and has done them for about nine years, and the second half was that those nine people are the ones the refusal is about and not her, and that the whole of the room heard it and about four of the nine were not in the room, and that she would not be able to say that to the five who weren't there for about a month, and that a month is a long time to be the only person who knows what a decision cost."**

He put the folded sheet back in his coat. He did not leave it on the counter and it does not go in the drawer and there are still three sheets in that drawer and this was not one of them.

---

**And here is where it turned, and it turned in about nine seconds, in a shop, with a man of about thirty-three who had been in a room, and the man of twenty-two said two things and both of them were true and he said the second one out loud on purpose and he was not agreed with.**

**"That is about nine people on wages and not about me."**

**"Yes."**

**"And I am the reason she was in that room."**

**"You are what?"**

**"I am the reason she was in that room,"** Marek said. **"Not the office. Not the committee. Me, in this city, in about four years of saying a thing in about nine hundred rooms that a person four days of bus travel away may refuse whatever they are asked, and she heard it, and she came here to be asked a question she could say no to, and she said no, and about nine people on wages are inside the answer, and that is what she said, and I am the reason she was in the room, and none of those two things is the other's excuse, and both of them are on this counter at about ten past five on a Monday and neither one of them is going to be put anywhere."**

**"You'd not have said any of that if I hadn't read it out."**

**"No."**

**"Then it wasn't nothing."**

**"It wasn't nothing,"** Marek said, **"and it is not a thing I did. You carried it four days and you read it out in a shop on a Monday, and the carrying was yours and the reading was yours, and I have been in this shop for about eleven years and I have never once been the person who brings a thing into a room, and I have wanted to be, and I am not, and the reason I am not is four days of bus travel in each direction and one rule that I helped write and broke once."**

**"That's two."**

**"That's two,"** Marek said, **"and they're both true and about four people have now told me in about four weeks that I say true things, and I have not said one of them back to the person who told me the first one, and that is also true, and I am not going to make it a third."**

The man of about thirty-three did not agree with him. He said, in about nine seconds, and without any heat at all, that he had driven four days with a piece of paper in his coat expecting to be told something about a machine, and that what he had been given was a man telling him about himself in a shop, and that he was going to have to think about which of the two was worse and that he did not think it was the machine.

---

**What he noticed, and did not say, and told nobody, was that the man of about thirty-three did not argue with any part of it, and that a man who does not argue with a true thing has already decided what to do about it, and that whatever the man of about thirty-three is going to do about it, he will do it in a town four days of bus travel away with a piece of paper he did not write, and that this is the first time in about eleven years of work that something he said in about nine hundred rooms has come back to him with a name on it that is not the office's, and that the name on it is a man with a van.**

**What he wanted and did not say, on that Monday evening, was to be wrong about the second thing. He wanted the reason she was in that room to be the committee, or the office, or a woman of about thirty-four with a hired van, or the weather, and he worked out in about nine seconds that every one of those is available and that he said the true one out loud because the true one was the one that made him the man of this, and that a man of twenty-two who is the man of this in a room four days of bus travel away has been the man of this in about nine hundred rooms already and has not once been asked to carry anything out of one of them, and that is what he wanted on this Monday, and that wanting it is the entire cost of this entry and it is not going to be paid and is not going to be forgiven.**

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.
