# Chapter 564 — A Yard Behind A Trade Counter On A Wide Road, And A Man Of About Thirty-Three Who Was Asked Which Of The Four He Was In And Answered Three Of Them In About Eleven Words, And The Fourth Is The One He Is In And The Fourth Is On Nobody's List, And Seven Hundred And Fifty-Six And Five Hundred And Ninety Are On The Same Afternoon

**The Friday was a bench in Lattice Ward until about two, a counter until about six, and a yard behind a firm on a wide road from about half past six, and its shape was about nine crates and a man standing in the middle of them, and one question put to him that no form in that yard had ever asked anybody.**

---

The Friday was eleven long and every one of the eleven was dated and eleven came through that counter and the last of them at about five. Bench until two, the counter two until six, a wide road and a yard behind a firm on it from about half past six. **The heating in the rooms off that service road is on and it is dearer than the winter's, four names on that tenancy are between them paying it, and a fortnight that a cook laid out of her own money is owed by nobody and will be owed by nobody when the month turns.** Those rooms are nine hundred and eighty-four days, one hundred and forty weeks and four days, four of them and one behind, and they are warm.

The card in the rail along the front of that counter is nine hundred and eighty-eight days old, one hundred and forty-one weeks and one day, four days beyond the room, and the rail takes two.

**The seventeenth line on two nails above the bench in Lattice Ward is seven hundred and fifty-six days old, one hundred and eight weeks to the day, and the man of about fifty-one at the north wall of a room in another district is five hundred and ninety days old, eighty-four weeks and two days. One of those two figures is the day a man first sat down against a wall in this city and one of the two is the day the seventeenth line was written on a board in it, and they are on the same afternoon of this day, and neither of them is a day anything happened to the heating in this city, and the heating is a closed thing in this case with no figure of days off for it in it anywhere.**

---

**He had nine crates in a yard and about four days of waiting left on them and he had come to this city on the Wednesday with them, and he was about thirty-three, and he drives for a firm, and the firm is not the firm with the yellow strip on it, and he had said that clause out loud in a yard in this city on the Thursday of the week before last and had been right and had not been thanked.**

**Nobody in that yard had asked him a single question about the crates since the Wednesday. They had asked him where the yard was, and they had asked him whether the gate was open at the weekend, and about four people had asked him whether the firm was still running, and every one of those three questions has an answer on a form somewhere. None of them had asked him what he was.**

**He was standing in the middle of nine crates at about ten past seven on the Friday evening with his hands in his pockets because it was cold enough for hands in pockets and not cold enough for anything else, and Marek was at the end of that yard with a bag of tools because there was a machine in the corner of it with a bearing going and the man of about forty-six who ran the place had asked him to look, and looking is not teaching and looking is not demonstrating and he did not demonstrate it.**

---

**"Do you mind if I ask you something odd."**

**"Go on."**

**"There are four ways a person can be in a thing like this,"** Marek said, **"and I'm not asking you which one you want to be. I'm asking you which one you've been put in."**

**"That's not a question anybody's asked me."**

**"No."**

The man of about thirty-three thought about it for about nine seconds, which is longer than the question took to ask, and then he answered three of the four.

**"I'm asked. I'm on the sheet. I'm left to it."**

Eleven words, and he said them flatly, in the way a man says a thing he has been asked a version of before without knowing he had, and then he waited, because there were four and he had said three.

**"And the fourth."**

**"There's a fourth."**

**"There's a fourth one."**

**"There's a fourth one I'm in,"** the man said, **"and it isn't on your list, and it isn't on the firm's sheet either, and it isn't on the card, and I've been in it since about the spring and the only thing anybody has ever said to me about it is the number nine days."**

**"Say the fourth one out loud so that I have got it."**

**"I do the ordinary thing and I wait,"** the man said. **"That's all it is. I do the ordinary thing and I wait, and nobody decided that for me and there's no box for it, and about four hundred people in four places are in it and it's not a category, it's a Tuesday."**

---

**And Marek said the thing, out loud, in a face, to a man of about thirty-three in a yard, and it took about nine seconds, and nobody thanked him for it, and there is no form anywhere in this city on which it can be written down.**

**"You're in the second one and I want to say so out loud, because about nine people on wages in a town four days of bus travel away are in the second one right now and they have not been asked, and the second one is the one where somebody decided in about the spring that you were in it and it was correct and nobody has to be told."**

**"That's the one I'm in?"**

**"You're enrolled and it's correct by default. Nobody chose it for you. It was correct when it was decided and it has been correct every day since and about nine people in four days' travel are inside it and about four of them have told nobody, which is a different thing from it not mattering."**

**"Right,"** the man said, and then he said the part that made the whole evening, and he said it without any heat at all. **"Then which of the four is the one that isn't on a list."**

**"Yours."**

**"Right,"** the man said. **"Well, I've been trying to work out for about four days who to tell that to and I have not got anybody, and you've asked me the question in a yard in about nine seconds, and I'm not going to forget it, and I want you to understand that I'm not going to do anything about it either, and I don't want you to think I'm going to."**

**"I don't."**

**"That's the correct answer and I want it said that it's the correct answer, because if it's the wrong answer I want to hear that instead and I haven't heard it."**

---

**What he noticed on that Friday evening, and did not say, and told nobody, and has not written down anywhere, was that the man had said *nobody decided that for me* about the fourth one, and that this was the first time in about eleven years of work that he had heard a person say a thing was nobody's decision in a tone of total indifference rather than in a tone of complaint, and that the man was not angry, and that about four hundred people are in that fourth thing and every one of them is not angry, and that the shape does not need anybody to be angry in it to work.**

**What he wanted, and did not say, on that Friday, was to write the fourth one on a piece of paper and give it to that man, and he did not, and the reason he did not is that the man did not ask for it, and a piece of paper with four words on it in a man's hand is a document, and about four hundred people would then be in a category, and the man of about thirty-three had just spent about nine seconds telling him that the whole of what is wrong with the second one is that somebody decided it, and a piece of paper is somebody deciding it again with a smaller pen.**

He did the bearing. It was not the bearing, it was a belt, and the belt cost the man of about forty-six about nine pounds, and Marek told him what was wrong with it in about nine seconds and did not show him the belt and did not hold it and did not teach anybody anything, and the man of about forty-six replaced it himself and was not thanked by anybody including the man who paid for it.

**About four people were in that yard at about half past seven. All four of them had heard the question. All four of them had heard three of the four answers out loud, in a voice, from a man who drives for a firm. And not one of the four of them said a word about it then or afterwards, and about two of them have described it since to people who were not in that yard, and both of the two gave it in a way that put the fourth answer last, when in the yard the fourth answer came before anybody spoke.**

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.
