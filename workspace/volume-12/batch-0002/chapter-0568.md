**It is put here from the wrong side of a street in the second of the four towns, and the person putting it here is a man of about thirty-three who drives for a firm and who stood in that street for about nine seconds on the Sunday night of a fortnight ago, at about eleven at night, in the rain, waiting for a woman of about thirty-four to finish shutting a shop, and who has not been back and is not going to.**

There is a row of shops in that town. There are about eleven of them and a bus depot at one end and a pumping station at the other. Four of the eleven have a freezer.

**ONE. **TWO. She had a list of nine names on a docket in her own hand. Nine names and about four of them have a freezer, and the other five do not, and the list is not a list of shops, it is a list of people, and she made it in about eleven weeks out of things people had said to her across a counter.**

**THREE. On the Thursday before that Sunday she did a thing that took her about four seconds and that she has described twice since and to nobody who has written it down. She told the four. Not the clinic. **FOUR. **SIX. Nobody has asked her about any of it. **EIGHT. **NINE. Four of nine, and the four of the nine are not the four anybody in this city would have chosen, and the woman of about thirty-four did not choose them either, she chose the four that had freezers, and the reason she chose those four and not the clinic is on the record of nobody and is hers.**

**And the woman of about thirty-four is not in this city and has not been in this city since that Sunday night bus, and this entry is the ninth paragraph of nine and it is the only one of the nine that says anything about her, and what it says is that she is owed nothing and that nobody has asked her a single question about any of it, including by the man who stood in the street in the rain, including by the man at the counter in this city who she has spoken to four times in about four weeks and who has never once asked her what she did on the Tuesday.**

**What he wanted and did not say, and has not written down, and has not sent anybody about, was to be the person she says the six words to next, and the reason he cannot be that person is that the six words have to be said to a person in a shop by somebody who has been in that town, and he has been in none of them, and the only person he has ever been in anything in, in four days' travel, is a building in the first of the four towns when he was twenty-one, and he went to look at a pump, and about nine people in four towns still think he has been in their building, and he is the reason they think it.**
