# Chapter 593 — A Thursday With Eleven Jobs And A Woman Of About Thirty-Six Who Opens A School In The Fourth Of The Four Towns At Eight In The Morning And Who Has Been Asked To Keep Nine Other Households On A List And Who Takes One Of Them Off It On Purpose And Says Out Loud What That Household Will Not Be Getting In The Spring, And Nobody In That Town Knows Yet Including The Two Of Them

**The Thursday was a bench in Lattice Ward until about two, a counter until about six, and four rooms off a service road from about half past six, and its shape was a woman crossing out one line with a pen and then saying the sentence that goes with the crossing out.**

---

Eleven jobs on the Thursday, every one of them dated, eleven people at that counter, the last at about ten past five. The bench until about two, the counter from two to six, four rooms off that service road from about half past six. **The heat in that flat is on and past the winter's figure, four names on that one lease are carrying what is left of it, and a fortnight a cook laid out of her own wages is owed by no one and is going to be owed by no one at the end of this month.** Those four rooms are one thousand and fifty-three days, one hundred and fifty weeks and three days, four of them and one behind, and they are warm.

The card in the rail along the front of that counter is one thousand and fifty-seven days, one hundred and fifty-one weeks to the day, four days on from the rooms, and the rail holds two.

**The nineteenth line on those two nails is seven hundred and forty-nine days, one hundred and seven weeks to the day, and it is the only line on that board which has ever carried a zero in the last place and it is not the only line of a kind in this entry. The hold of the man of about thirty-three in another city is six hundred and eighty-six days, ninety-eight weeks to the day, the ask is seven hundred and forty-three days, one hundred and six weeks and one day, and the man of about fifty-one against that north wall is six hundred and fifty-nine days, ninety-four weeks and one day. None of those three figures is a day anything happened to the heating in this city, and that heating is finished with in this entry and no number of days off is held for it anywhere in it.**

---

**The fourth of the four towns has a school in it with about two hundred and ten children in the day and about nine in the morning, and the school is the only building in that town with a door that is unlocked at eight.**

A firm four days of bus travel away asked the school in the spring of a year nobody there can now date whether it would hold a list. It is a polite request. It is on a printed sheet. It came with about four free boxes of nothing, and the firm meant it kindly, and the firm is not a villain and the sheet was not a trick.

**She is about thirty-six. She opens that school at eight. She has been asked, kindly, by a woman of about forty-four in a second district of a city four days away, to keep a list of about nine households, and she has done it for about two years, and she is not a teacher, and nobody pays her for the list, and she has told nobody in that town what is on it.**

The nine households are on it because about nine of them cannot get to a counter. There is a bus that goes in at about ten and comes back at about two, and the counter where the cards are handed in is a two-hour walk from the far end of that town from the bus stop, and in the winter the two hours each way is four hours each way, and about nine households in that town have somebody in them who cannot do four hours each way.

So the cards went to the school, and the school opened at eight, and the school was there.

**On the Thursday morning of this week she crossed one of the nine out.**

Not all nine. One.

**"I am not taking them off because of them,"** she said, to about nine people, in a hall, before the first bell. **"I am taking one off because in about four years there will be forty on it, and the fortieth will be on it for the same reason the ninth is on it now, and I will not be the person who says no, because by then I will have said yes nine times and once is a favour and forty is a habit, and a habit that lives in a school is a thing that cannot be closed."**

A man of about twenty-nine who teaches there said: **"Which one."**

**"The two in the four rooms above the bakery,"** she said. **"Man of about seventy-four and woman of about seventy. Thirty-one years in those rooms. He has never been to a counter in his life and he is not going to start now. Their card goes back in the bag in the spring, and nobody in this town knocks on their door between now and then, and neither do I, and you are not to either, and if anybody in this hall hears about this from anybody else, that is a conversation we will be having in this hall and not in a kitchen."**

About nine people in that hall said nothing.

**"They will not be found,"** she said. **"That is the whole cost and I am saying it in this hall so that it is said. In the spring there is a van that comes to this town about four times and it will come for the number on the sheet, and the number will be the number, and there will be a box in a cold store on a road four days from here with a household on it that does not exist, and that is what happens. It is not a punishment. It is a shape."**

**"Who decided,"** a woman of about thirty-one said.

**"I did,"** she said. **"About four seconds, standing at that door at about ten past eight with a pen. It took about four seconds and there was nobody else in the building."**

She read the rest of it out. The remaining eight stay. The school keeps opening at eight. The school will not take anything else, and the school will not ask the firm for anything, and the school will not be the reason anybody comes to that town in the spring or does not come.

**About nine people heard that and about four of them did not agree with it and about five said nothing at all, and nobody in that hall thanked her, and she was owed nothing before the Wednesday and is owed nothing now, and the bell went at about nine and the two hundred and ten came in.**

---

**Marek was not in that town and did not know any of that on the Thursday, and this is the third Thursday running in which correct information existed in a place he could stand in and did not reach him for four days, and that is not a failure of the arrangement, it is the arrangement.**

He was at a bench in Lattice Ward until two. Then at a counter until six with eleven people through the door, one of whom was a man of about thirty-one with a meter under his arm who said, in about nine seconds, that the fourth of the four towns had stopped answering anything that was not in writing since the spring, and that this was because of a form, and not because of anybody, and that he had been told so by a woman of about thirty-four who types for a firm and who was not the woman who decided anything.

Then four rooms off a service road, the heat on, about four people in them who were not asked anything.

**What he noticed on the Thursday evening, and told nobody in those four rooms, is that the school list and the firm's form have the same printed box on them, and that in the school's case nobody had ever written anything in it and nobody knew it was there until the Wednesday of the week before.**

The box has no date under it and no name above it. It is the only part of that sheet with neither. He has known that for a year and has said it in about four rooms to about four people, and every time he has said it the person he said it to has looked at the box and then looked at him, and the look has meant one of two things and he has never been able to tell which.

**The second thing is that the woman who opened that school and crossed a line out is the only person in four towns in about two years who has taken something out of an arrangement instead of putting something into it, and she did it standing up at a door at about ten past eight with a pen, in about four seconds, alone, and she told nine people afterwards in a hall and asked none of them whether they agreed.**

**What he wanted, on the Thursday night, was for somebody to have told her before the Thursday that a person crossing a line out in a school gets the same four seconds as a person crossing a line in anywhere else, and that four seconds at ten past eight in an empty building is the correct amount of time to need and not one second more. Nobody told her. There is no carrier in this city that carries a sentence to a school in the fourth of the four towns by Friday, and he did not write one, and the reason he did not write one is that the sentence would have arrived on the Monday and the Thursday would still have been the Thursday, and the only thing his sentence could have done was to have taken four seconds off hers, and those four seconds were hers.**

**And the small thing he gave up on the Thursday, which is on no form: a bus ticket from the Sunday night was in his coat pocket, and on the Wednesday he put it in the bin by the bench, and on the Thursday it was in the pocket again, and on the Friday morning it came out of a pocket soft and folded in two and went into the wash with a shirt, and on the Saturday he had to buy a second one from the woman at the counter, and she wrote it in the book and then looked at the book and looked at him, and neither of them said anything, and the book is one short from this week and it will be one short from next week, and it is not on any docket and there is no form for a person buying the same ticket twice in four days.**

Nobody thanked anybody, in a hall with two hundred and ten children in it or at a counter with eleven jobs dated against it, and the shutter came down at about ten, and a Thursday is not a Sunday and there is no shop that opens on a Sunday.

*What the day did, and what it did not touch.* **A woman of about thirty-six removed a household from a list she had never been paid to keep, in about four seconds, alone, at a door, and said the cost of it out loud to about nine people before the bell, and about four of them disagreed and nobody thanked her.** Nobody thanked anybody that day in that town or in that shop or on that counter. The shutter came down at about ten and this entry contains no Sunday. He taught nobody, demonstrated to nobody, assessed nobody and assisted nobody, and no such request was made of him in the course of it. **The running count of things done properly that altered nothing, done by people owed nothing, is unmoved at two — a clipboard on a nail inside a cold-store door in the first of the four towns, and four torn pieces of the same list in a coat pocket at that same gate, both belonging to one woman of about fifty-two — and a woman removing a household from a list in four seconds is not a third of it, because it was not a correct thing done in about nine seconds, it changed a household's spring, and she is owed nothing by it.** The card in the rail is one thousand and fifty-seven days and was in the rail all day in a rail that takes two, and a two-box return card with a clip is another object entirely and is in none of this. The rooms are one thousand and fifty-three days and warm. The board stands at nineteen lines with the twentieth untouched. The drawer under the bench is shut and holds four sheets, the fourth of them off a driver who was on that road anyway.
