# Chapter 876 — A Friday Two Days After A Wednesday That Produced Nothing At All, A Counter And About Two Hours Of It, And The Whole Of The Day Carried By About Nine People Who Each Had Something To Do And Who All Of Them Had Brought With Them From A Sitting Where Nothing Had Been Decided

**A counter and about two hours at it was the shape of that Friday, and the day after it was a Saturday, and the day before it was a Wednesday on which a number had been said once in a room and nothing had come of it. About nine people in this city each carried a piece of that Wednesday with them into that Friday and not one of them said so.**

---

Six names and six dates went onto that Friday's sheet, the last of the dates at about ten to five, and the shutter came down at about ten.

**And no figure was said in any room in this city on that Friday and none was announced anywhere, and the Wednesday of every fourth week that carries one was two days behind and not one of them.**

---

**A Friday after a sitting that produced nothing is an ordinary Friday, and about nine people in this city have found that out in about four days rather than about nine years.**

Nothing had been decided on that Wednesday. Nothing had been refused and nothing had been agreed and no name had gone anywhere, and about nine people went to their ordinary work on that Friday and did the ordinary work.

A woman of about thirty-eight said in about nine seconds that she had expected the Wednesday to make about four things easier and that it had made none of them easier and that this was the first time in about nine years that a Wednesday had done neither.

**Nobody at that counter said she was disappointed and nobody said she was not, and about four people at that counter have said since that they have not been able to tell the difference any more.**

---

**The man of about thirty-four who owns four vehicles came through that counter at about eleven in the morning and was not asked about the practice and did not talk about the practice.**

He said nine words to the man behind that counter and then went out again, and the nine words were about a road and about a time of day.

He said: I pass it at six and I do not stop.

**Nobody at that counter asked him why he had said it, and nobody in this city has asked him why, and about four people in that city have said since that they would have asked him and did not.**

He has said since that he said it so that somebody would write it down somewhere, and that he has no way of knowing whether anybody did, and that this is a thing about him and not about that road.

---

**The woman of about forty-four who works at that trade had been at that counter for about nine weeks on the weekday and about nine years in all, and on that Friday she said the thing out loud for the first time.**

She said that a person told a thing in a doorway gets the whole weekend to sit with it, and that the man who owns that trade had opened on the Sunday as he opens on every Sunday, and that about nine weeks is a long time to be the only person in a building who knows something.

She said in about nine seconds that she has not told him and is not going to tell him, and that she has been at that counter on about nine weeks of mornings with a thing in her bag.

**Nobody at that counter asked what the thing in the bag said and nobody in this city has asked her, and about four people who know there is a bag have said since that a road is not a person.**

She said afterwards, at about half past four, that nobody in this city has thanked her for the nine weeks and that she said about nine weeks ago that she would not expect it, and that saying it has not made the mornings any different.

**Nobody at that counter said anything about that and nobody has since, and she went out at about ten to six the way she goes out every weekday.**

---

**A man of about thirty-four who has been over there and done it twice was at that counter on that Friday and was not asked about the practice, and he was the one who said what about nine weeks costs.**

He said in about nine seconds that in about nine weeks nobody over there has changed anything, that he has been over a border twice and done it, and that he still cannot say whether either time was safe.

He said that what has changed in about nine weeks is that about nine people in this city know a thing about it, and that is not the same as anybody over there knowing anything.

**Nobody at that counter asked him to say which of the two he thought was more useful and he was not asked.**

---

**A man of twenty-two was at that counter from about half past four and was asked nothing at all, and the one thing he said was about a mat.**

He said that the mat behind that counter has been cut and re-laid so many times that about four layers of it are showing at one corner, and that the layer underneath is a different colour and has been there since before the shop took the lease.

**Nobody at that counter had any idea what to do with that and nobody came back with a second question about it, and he said afterwards that a floor tells you more about a trade than a person does.**

Nothing was signed by him in this stretch of days and no sheet went out of that shop with his name on it on that Friday, and there is nothing in that shop that wants a signature.

---

**What was left of that Friday went into about two hours of work in a fourth district.**

**A starter on a motor in the same yard had its contactor set to about nine hundred amps for a motor that pulls about four hundred, so that it had been hunting on and off for about four years.**

He set the contactor to what the plate on the motor says, proved it against its own gauge, and wrote the setting on the door.

**Thirty-one pounds. He told the man that a contactor set too high is a contactor hunting, and that about four starters in that yard are set against their own plates, and that one of them has been reported as a motor that will not start when it is wanted for about four years.**

---

**A coupling on a pump in the same building had a guard fastened with a bolt that went through about nine millimetres of air and nothing else.**

He took the guard off, cut new holes, and fixed it back with bolts of the length that belongs there.

**Twenty-six pounds. He told the man that a guard fastened through air is a guard somebody removed in a hurry, and that about four guards in that building have been refastened that way, and that one of them has been reported as a guard that will stay off for about four years.**

---

**A light switch on a landing in the same place had been put in upside down, so that it was up for off.**

He turned it round, put a mark on the plate so that it can be found again, and left it.

**Nineteen pounds. He told the man that a switch fitted upside down teaches everybody in the building to stop believing switches, and that about four switches on that staircase were fitted that way, and that one of them has been reported as a dark stair for about four years.**

---

**A pipe clip on a run in the same yard had been re-fixed with a screw about nine millimetres too long, so that it had cut a slot in the pipe and the pipe was weeping at that slot.**

He packed the clip out, cut a new screw, and left the pipe round.

**Nine pounds. He told the man that a screw too long in a clip is a leak that has not started yet, and that about four clips in that run were re-fixed that way, and that one of them has been reported as a drip for about four years.**

**The four of those prices came to eighty-five pounds on that Friday, exact.**

**Half past six on that Friday and the door of the fourth of those rooms was shut, and behind it was a woman of about thirty, and on the back shelf of that room the eighth of eight lay at three hundred and eighty-nine days.**

**Nobody in this city has asked her anything about that page and nobody is going to. What is on that page is not given here.**
