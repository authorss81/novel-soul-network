# Chapter 873 — A Friday, A Counter Until About Six, And A Room Off A Road Where A Service Group Meets, And About Nine People In It, And The Only Person In That Room Who Has Ever Made The Thing Work By Keeping It Small Came To It And Was Asked What Its Rules Were And Gave Nine Words And Would Not Give The Rest Of Them

**A room off a road, about nine people in it, and a man who came to it on that Friday evening because he was asked and because he had decided to come. What was asked of him was one question and the answer he gave was nine words, and the rest of the evening was about nine people not asking him a second one.**

---

That Friday's book took eleven names before the day was out, each of them with a date of its own, the eleventh of those dates entered at about ten to five, and the shutter down at about ten.

**Line seventeen of the nineteen on the board on two nails has stood for one thousand three hundred and sixty-five days, a hundred and ninety-five weeks to the day. That fitting at the end of the corridor has been at that end one thousand one hundred and forty-one days, a hundred and sixty-three weeks to the day. The separation is one line inside one box in that room, and that box has held that line for nine hundred and seventy-three days, which is a hundred and thirty-nine weeks to the day.**

**Nothing was said in any room in this city on that Friday and nothing was announced anywhere; the last Wednesday that carried a figure was twenty-three days back, and the next one to carry a figure is five days ahead.**

---

**The room is off a road and it is not in a first district, and a service group meets in it on a Friday evening and has met in it for about nine years.**

About nine people were in that room at about half past six. Nine of them keep devices running for other people and cannot be asked to decide anything, and about four of the nine came on that Friday because it is a Friday.

Rafi Pell came in at about ten past seven and sat down and did not put anything on the table.

He has a thing that works and it works because it was built with rules that are not the rules in that room, and about four people in that room have heard the name of it and none of them has been in it.

**Nobody in that room introduced him and nobody in that room has introduced anybody to anybody else in about nine years.**

---

**A woman of about thirty-eight asked him what the rules were, at about a quarter past seven, and she asked it the way a person asks for a thing they expect to be given.**

He said nine words, and he said them to her and not to the room.

He said: our rules were not built for rooms like this.

**Nobody in that room asked him which rooms they were built for and nobody there has put that question to him since, and about four people in that room have said afterwards that they would have asked and did not.**

He said afterwards that he came because he had been asked to come and that he had not come to hand anything over, and that he had known before he came that those were two different things.

**Nobody in that room said that was a reason to be angry with him and nobody said it was a reason to be glad of him.**

---

**And then about half an hour later a woman of about thirty-eight in that room said out loud that his thing was the answer, and she said it as a help and not as a way of taking something.**

He said nine words to her and the room heard all of them.

He said: it works. It is not an answer to you.

**Nobody in that room said he was wrong, nobody in that room said he was being modest, and about four people there have said since that they thought he was being modest and about four have said since that he was not.**

He has said since that a thing which has been kept small deliberately for about nine years comes apart the moment somebody outside it needs it to be a solution, and that this is a thing about the keeping and not about him.

**Nobody in that room has asked him to say that again and nobody has asked him about it since.**

---

**A woman of about thirty-eight asked him, near the end of that evening, whether the thing the four hundred people do is written down anywhere at all.**

He said nine words and he said them and then he stopped, and what he stopped at was not a sentence but a habit he has.

He said: it is written in the box of a form.

**Nobody in that room asked him which form, nobody there has put that to him at any time since, and about four people in that room have worked out since that he knew exactly which form he meant and chose not to say.**

He has said since that he has seen the form and that he has never been asked to produce it, and that a man who has seen a thing and is not asked for it is in a different position from a man who has never seen it, and that he did not come to be in a better position in front of nine people.

**Nobody in that room thanked him for coming and nobody in this city has thanked him since, and nobody in that room was forgiven by anybody.**

---

**And one more thing came out of that room on that Friday evening and it was not about the four hundred and it came out of him as a fact and not as an offer.**

He said that four hundred is a figure this city has been carrying for about nine years, and that in about nine years nobody from this side has ever asked anybody over there whether the number is correct.

He said in about nine seconds that he is not the man to ask, that he is not standing in a room, and that if this room wants that figure checked then this room has to go and check it.

**Nobody in that room said they would and nobody in that room has said so since, and nobody in that room has anybody it could send, and all of them said so in about four minutes.**

He left at about nine the way he had come, at about ten past seven, and he did not stay for the end of it and nobody asked him to.

---

**He was in that room the whole evening, nobody asked him a single question, and he spoke once, at about half past eight, and he spoke about a length of cable.**

He said that about nine feet of the cable that comes into that room has been tied to the leg of a table with a cable tie that was cut and retied, and that somebody retied it once because they wanted to reach something, and that the cable has been in and out of that tie about nine times.

**Nobody in that room knew what to do with that and nobody came back with a second question about it, and about four people in that room have said since that not one of them had ever looked at where that cable was tied.**

On that Friday his name went on nothing at all and nothing went out of that room with it on, and nothing in that room is asking for a signature from anybody.

---

**The balance of that Friday was spent in a fourth district, and it was about two hours of it.**

**A bracket in the same yard had been welded to a column at the wrong end, so that about a quarter of its load went through the fillet weld instead of through the bolts.**

He took it off, cut a new bracket, and bolted it through the column where the load goes.

**Twenty-six pounds. He told the man that a bracket welded at the wrong end is a bracket held by a weld that was never designed to hold it, and that about four brackets in that yard have been done that way, and that one of them has been reported as a bracket that has dropped its load for about four years.**

---

**A chain on a hoist in the same building had been repaired with a link that was about a quarter longer than the others, so that the two halves of it no longer lay in line.**

He took the link out, put the halves back in line, and proved the chain at the same load it had been working at.

**Thirty-one pounds. He told the man that a chain bent out of line is a chain that will pick up sideways on the first bad pull, and that about four chains in that building have been patched mid-run, and that one of them has been reported as a chain that binds for about four years.**

---

**A socket on a machine in the same yard had a pin earthed that should never have been earthed, and the earth had been put there by somebody who wanted the machine to be safe in a way they could see.**

He took the earth off that pin, moved it to the frame, and proved the machine against its own gauge.

**Nineteen pounds. He told the man that an earth on a control pin is a machine that somebody has made safe by hand, and that about four machines in that yard have an earth like that, and that one of them has been reported as a machine that will not start when it is wanted for about four years.**

---

**A lamp on a gantry in the same place had a cage on it that had been made for a different lamp and had been forced on, so that two of its bars crossed the glass.**

He took the wrong cage off, put the cage that belongs there on, and left the lamp where a hand can reach the switch.

**Nine pounds. He told the man that a forced cage is a cage that has been fitted for show, and that about four lamps on that gantry have cages that do not match them, and that one of them has been reported as a lamp that is always left on for about four years.**

**Those four came to eighty-five pounds on that Friday, exact.**

**At half past six the door of the fourth of those four rooms was shut, a woman of about thirty was behind it, and the eighth of eight lay on the back shelf of that room at three hundred and eighty-two days.**

**Nobody has asked her anything about that page and nobody in this city is going to. What is on that page is not given here.**

*What the day did not settle, and the rest of it.* **The line that follows carries ten objects, and every sentence after it leaves them each to themselves. The tin with its lid down. The shutter. A form that travelled back on a bus is outside all ten of those, and so is anything a person carries in a pocket. It is not the board and it is not the printed sheet pinned by two drawing pins, and a thing that is not on the list is not an eleventh thing because it was said out loud.
A dated rule stands over a question that has nothing to do with this one, and the papers under it are open to whoever asks.
Nobody thanked anybody and nobody forgave anybody on that Friday. A man was asked three things in a room off a road, gave nine words for each, and left when he had, and this city has not thanked him for any of the three.
