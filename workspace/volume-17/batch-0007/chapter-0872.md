# Chapter 872 — A Thursday, A Counter And About Four Hours Of It, And A Person At That Counter Working Out What Description Of Four Hundred People This City Is Entitled To Make Out Of Three Places Where Somebody Wrote It Down By Accident, And A Woman Of About Thirty-Eight Who Has Asked The Same Question In Four Rooms And Who Had A Sentence Ready About A Category And Who Said It In About Nine Seconds

**One counter and about four hours of it made the shape of that Thursday, and the question standing at that counter for most of those four hours was what a description is, and who is entitled to make one. Two people at that counter had a sentence ready about it and only one of them said theirs out loud.**

---

Seven names and seven dates went onto that Thursday's sheet, the seventh of the dates written at about ten to five, and at about ten the shutter came down.

**The card standing on end in the two-place rail at that door is one thousand five hundred and ninety-six days, two hundred and twenty-eight weeks to the day. The twelfth of the nineteen ruled lines on the board on two nails is one thousand five hundred and twelve days, two hundred and sixteen weeks to the day. The thirteenth is one thousand four hundred and sixty-three days, two hundred and nine weeks to the day.**

**The fourteenth is one thousand four hundred and twenty-eight days, two hundred and four weeks to the day. The fifteenth is one thousand four hundred and seven days, two hundred and one weeks to the day. The nineteenth of those lines is one thousand two hundred and eighty-eight days, a hundred and eighty-four weeks to the day. The hold on nine crates is one thousand two hundred and twenty-five days, a hundred and seventy-five weeks to the day.**

**And nothing was said in any room in this city that Thursday about what anybody is going to do, and no count was announced anywhere, and the Wednesday of every fourth week that carries one is a day and a half off.**

---

**Four hundred people use a thing every day of their lives and every one of them has done it since before anybody in this city thought to write a question about it.**

The three places where it is written down are a wages book in a fourth district, a day book at a gate in a second district, and a laundry book on a counter in a first district. All three were written by a person doing something else.

**None of those three people in this city has ever been told what they wrote, and nobody in this city has asked any of them, and that has not changed since about nine years ago.**

The question standing at that counter was not whether the thing is safe. It was what a sentence about it has to look like, and where it is allowed to come from, and who may put it in the box.

A woman of about thirty-eight stood at that counter at about half past one with a sheet in front of her and about four of the four hours went into that sheet.

She said in about nine seconds that she had got as far as the first line and that the first line was going to be a description and that she had stopped there.

**Nobody at that counter asked her to finish it and about four people who came to that counter over the rest of that day have said since that they would have asked her and did not.**

---

**And a man of about fifty-two who keeps a register at a counter in a second district came in at about half past two on that Thursday and had his sentence ready before he had sat down.**

He had said in about four seconds, about nine years ago and in a corridor in a first district, that copying those three descriptions out and printing them would make a description this city had made, and that there is nobody over there to say whether it is the one.

Nobody at that counter argued with him then and nobody at that counter argued with him now, and he said in about nine seconds that the whole of the problem is that a sentence about four hundred people turns them into one thing.

**Nobody at that counter said that was not true and nobody at that counter said that it was.**

**And the woman of about thirty-eight said nine words to him at that counter and she said them to him and not to the room, and she had been carrying them for about nine days.**

She said: they are four hundred people and not one description.

He said in about four seconds that that was what he had been saying since about nine years ago and that she had got there from a different direction, and that nobody had said that to him before.

**Nobody at that counter thanked either of them and nobody at that counter has thanked either of them since, and nobody at that counter was forgiven by anybody.**

---

**And then a man of about thirty-eight who works on the second floor of a building in Saltmarket came in at about four and told them about a fourth place.**

He said that a returns sheet in a yard in a second district has the same four words on it in a hand nobody has recognised, and that the yard keeps those sheets because a firm requires them, and that nobody has ever asked that yard for one.

He said in about nine seconds that he has not copied it out and that he has not asked to see it and that he saw it about nine years ago while he was doing something else on a day of his own.

**Nobody at that counter sent anybody to that yard and nobody at that counter has since, and the man of about thirty-eight said afterwards that he had expected about four people to go and had not said so out loud.**

A woman of about thirty-eight said in about nine seconds that there are now four places and that four is a number people begin to arrange things by, and that at three places nobody had arranged anything.

**Nobody at that counter told her she had got there and nobody told her she had not, and about four people at that counter have said since that they thought she had got there and about four have said since that they thought she was frightened of being the one who was wrong.**

---

**And the shape of the ask got one line further on that Thursday and no further, and the line is set down here because it is what about four people in that city have worked out and not one of them has printed.**

The line is that a description of four hundred people which this city makes is a description four hundred people cannot answer, and that a thing which cannot be answered is not a fact and is not a politeness, and that this city has no third thing.

**Nobody at that counter has put that line on anything and nobody in this city has been asked to sign it, and about four people in that city have written it out for themselves and about four have not.**

A man of about thirty-four who has been over there and done it twice was at that counter from about four and was asked nothing about the practice and said one thing about the counting instead.

He said in about nine seconds that four hundred is a number somebody worked out at some point, and that he has never once been asked over there whether the number is correct.

**Nobody at that counter asked him who worked the number out and nobody in this city has asked him, and he said that it is not his to say and that is all.**

---

**A man of twenty-two stood at that counter from about half past four until the shutter came down and was asked nothing, and the one thing he said was about a washer.**

He said that the bolt through the counter foot at the near end has a washer under the head that is not the washer that came with the bolt, and that it is a shade larger and it is holding the head up about a sixteenth of an inch off the plate.

**Nobody at that counter found anything to do with that and nobody came back with a second question, and he has said since that a washer that does not belong is the only thing in that shop that nobody has ever costed.**

Nothing was signed by him in this stretch of days, no sheet was put up to him on that Thursday, and nothing that day went out of that shop with his name at the bottom of it.

---

**What was left of that Thursday went into about two hours of work in a fourth district.**

**A isolator on a board in the same workshop was mounted with its terminals covered in a thermosetting resin that had been applied over the cable entries.**

He took the resin off, re-made the entries, and left the terminals where a hand could get at them.

**Fifty-five pounds. He told the man that resin over a cable entry is an entry that cannot be tested and has not been tested, and that about four boards in that workshop have been sealed that way, and that one of them has been reported as a board nobody can prove for about four years.**

---

**A guard on a lathe in the same yard had been welded back after a repair and and stopped about a quarter of an inch before the trip bar.**

He took the guard off, packed the hinge out, and re-hung it so that the trip bar reaches it.

**Thirty-one pounds. He told the man that a guard welded back and stopped before the bar is a guard that has been repaired and not proved, and that about four guards in that yard have been hung that way, and that one of them has been reported as a guard that does not stop the thing for about four years.**

---

**A kettle in the same building had its element scaled on the underside so that about a quarter of the tube had no water round it at all.**

He took the element out, descaled it, and put it back with the tank filled and proved.

**Eighteen pounds. He told the man that a scaled element is an element heating the water at the edge of it, and that about four kettles in that building have a scale under the tube, and that one of them has been reported as a kettle that takes a long time for about four years.**

---

**A light on a switch in the same place had been put in with the neutral going to earth, so that the switch did nothing to the circuit at all.**

He took it out, put the neutral back on its own terminal, and proved the switch both ways.

**Nine pounds. He told the man that a switch with its neutral on earth is a decoration, and that about four switches in that building were wired that way, and that one of them has been reported as a switch that works sometimes for about four years.**

**Four jobs in a fourth district, four prices, and the four of them came to a hundred and thirteen pounds on that Thursday, exact.**

**At half past six the fourth of those four rooms had its door shut, a woman of about thirty behind it, and the eighth of eight lying on the back shelf of that room at three hundred and eighty-one days.**

**She has not been asked about that page by anybody here and she is not going to be. What is on it is not given on this page.**
