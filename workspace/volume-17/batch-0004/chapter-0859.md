# Chapter 859 — A Monday, A Bench Until Two And A Counter Until About Six, And A Room Off A Service Road Where About Nine People Were Asked One At A Time To Say What Four Hundred People Over A Border Are Without Using A Single Name, And Where Nine People Said Nine Things And None Of The Nine Was The Same As Any Of The Other Eight, And Where A Woman Who Has Been Over There And Come Back Was Not Asked And Said One Thing Afterwards That Nobody Asked Her For

**Nine people in one room were asked one at a time, on that Monday, to say in about nine seconds what four hundred people over a border are, without using a name at all. Nobody in that room had been asked that question before. Nothing in this city opened a room on the Saturday or on the Sunday.**

---

Eleven names went onto that Monday's sheet with eleven dates against them, the last of those dates at about twenty to five, and the shutter came down at about ten.

**Those four units standing off that service road are one thousand five hundred and seventy-five days, two hundred and twenty-five weeks to the day. The sixteenth of the nineteen lines on the board on two nails is one thousand three hundred and sixty-five days, a hundred and ninety-five weeks to the day. The nine hand copies of the front of one page stand at one thousand one hundred and forty-one days, a hundred and sixty-three weeks to the day, and not two of the nine have been laid beside each other.**

---

**There was no room on the Saturday and there was no room on the Sunday, and that is the shape of this city rather than a decision anybody made, and the last room anybody stood in was the one on the Friday before last.**

Nine people came up the stair of the first of those four rooms between about half past six and about eight. About four of them had come about the sheet of nine. The rest came because it is a Monday.

**A man of about thirty-four who owns four vehicles stood at the front and said that there is a name in this city for the stretch of country on the other side of that border, and that he put it into use, and that nobody over there has ever used it, and that tonight nobody in that room is going to use it either.**

He said in about nine seconds that he wants each of them to say what those people are, in about nine seconds, without using a name, and that he will not write any of it down and that he does not want it collected.

**Nobody in that room asked him why not and about four people in that room have said since that they understood exactly why not.**

---

**They went in the order they were sitting in, and here is what eight people said, and no two of the eight said the same thing, and not one of the eight used a name.**

A man of about forty-three in a service group said, in about nine seconds, that they are the people who are on somewhere at two in the morning while this city is asleep, and that they do the same thing four nights and something else on the fifth, and that the fifth is the day the machines get looked at.

A woman of about thirty-one said, in about nine seconds, that they are the people who count themselves, and that the counting is done by whoever is doing it, and that one of them wrote a number against a day of the week in a book of her own about nine years ago and has still not been told what she wrote.

A man of about thirty-eight said, in about nine seconds, that they are the people who do the thing in a room with about nine in it, and that one of the nine has been doing it since before she could lift a box she was carrying, and that this is not something anybody chose.

A woman of about thirty-six said, in about nine seconds, that they are about eleven to about thirty at a time and they come in on one day of the week and they go out on the same day of the week, and that the number is different every week and that nobody anywhere has ever asked them for the number.

A man of about twenty-nine said, in about nine seconds, that they are the people who keep something running in a place where nothing is written down and everything is checked twice, and that if you were standing there you would be asked to check it as well and not asked anything else.

A woman of about thirty-eight said, in about nine seconds, that they are the people who are told in advance by whoever happens to be standing near the machine that day, and that this is a person and not a form, and that a person can be asked whether they would take it and a form cannot.

A man of about thirty-four who owns four vehicles said, in about nine seconds, that they are the people who are not in this city and who have never once been in a room in this city where a number was said out loud in a woman's face.

A man of about fifty-two said, in about nine seconds, that they are the people this city holds three descriptions of, and that all three were written down by three people who were doing something else at the time, and that none of the three has ever been read by anybody who does it.

**And the ninth of the nine, a woman of about thirty-four, was not asked, and had not said she wanted to be, and she did not answer when the room got to her.**

---

**And then she said one thing, about a minute later, and it took about nine seconds, and it was not the thing she had been asked and would not have been if she had been asked.**

She said that none of the eight of those is what a person there would call them, and that she is not going to say what that is, and that she has not said it in this city in about nine years and is not going to start on a Monday.

**Nobody in that room asked her a second thing and nobody in that room has asked her one since, and about four people in that room have said afterwards that they would have asked and did not.**

A man of about thirty-four said in about nine seconds that he is not asking her and that he said so out loud before he started, and that the eight of those are about a way of working and that a way of working is the only thing this city can be told about without asking.

---

**A man of twenty-two was in that room and was not asked anything and said nothing until about half past seven, and what he said was about the nine.**

He said in about nine seconds that seven of the eight could have been written on a form and that the one that could not be written is the one about a room with about nine in it, and that this is the wrong way round for a city that is trying to write something down.

**Nobody in that room agreed with him and nobody in that room disagreed with him, and he said afterwards that he had worked out during the eighth answer that he could not write down any of the eight either, and that he has not found out why.**

A woman of about thirty-one said in about nine seconds that the answers were written in about nine hands on the back of a sheet and that nobody has compared them and that nobody is going to, and that a sheet of nine answers that have not been compared is the only honest thing in this city about those people.

He said that is the first thing anybody has said to him in about nine days that he agreed with, and she said that she was not asking for anything, and neither of them said another word.

---

**And the man of about thirty-four said, at about half past seven, that the run finishes at a town now and that the map is in a drawer, and that the man who keeps that map was not told and has not been told since.**

He said in about nine seconds that he did it on a Tuesday thirteen days ago and that it took about nine minutes, and that the man who put the map up about four years ago out of a tube in a cupboard has looked at that wall every working day since and has not asked anybody why there is nothing on it.

**Nobody in that room said the man who keeps the map should be told, and about four people in that room said privately that he should, and about four said that there is no procedure in this city for telling a man that a thing he put up is not coming back.**

---

**The rest of that Monday, after the counter had shut, was about two hours in a fourth district.**

**A drainage channel in a yard in the same district had about nine feet of grating laid the wrong way up, so that the slots were upside down and the channel had been holding about four inches of water along that run since before the spring.**

He took the nine feet up, turned it, re-bedded it on the original frames, and ran the channel through with a hose to prove it.

**Thirty-eight pounds. He told the man that a grating laid upside down is a channel that is a trough, and that about four lengths of grating in that yard are laid that way, and that one of them has been reported as a yard that ponds for about four years and has had a sweeper put on it twice.**

---

**A door on a weighbridge hut in the same place had been hung so that it opened towards the traffic, with the catch on the outside.**

He took the door off, turned the hinges over on the frame, refitted them, and left the old screw holes plugged.

**Thirty-one pounds. He told the man that a hut door that opens towards the traffic is a door a lorry can catch with a mirror, and that about four doors on that yard are hung that way, and that one of them has been reported as a door that is hit for about four years and has been taken off twice by two different gangs.**

---

**A pipe support in a plant room in the same building had been welded to a ventilation duct instead of the wall, so that every time the fan started the pipe rang.**

He cut the support off the duct, dressed both, re-made it to the wall, and welded a stiffener where it had been flexing.

**Twenty-six pounds. He told the man that a support welded to a duct is a support that rings every time a fan starts, and that about four supports in that plant room are on ductwork, and that one of them has been reported as a noise in that room for about four years and has been blamed on the fan twice.**

---

**A fire door in a workshop in the same place had a length of timber wedged into it at the bottom and had been wedged like that since before the spring.**

He took the timber out, closed the door on its closer, and left a note on the frame about how it had been propped and by whom it had been reported.

**Nineteen pounds. He told the man that a fire door wedged open is a door that is only a door on a sign, and that about four fire doors in that building have been found wedged at some time, and that one of them has been wedged and reported twice in about four years.**

**That Monday came to a hundred and fourteen pounds, exact, which is the four prices on that Monday added up.**

**Behind the shut door of the fourth of those four rooms a woman of about thirty had been sitting since half past six, and the shelf at the back of that room is holding the eighth of eight at three hundred and sixty-four days.**

**No one has asked her about it and nobody is going to ask her. What is on that page is not given here.**
