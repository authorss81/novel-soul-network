# THERE IS NO NEXT PHASE IN THIS DIRECTORY, AND HERE IS WHY, DATED BY THE VOLUME 16 CLOSE

**This directory holds a statement and not a phase. It exists because the Volume 16 close was required to record which of the two things it is — a volume closed and followed by another volume, or a volume closed and not followed by another — and it is the second of those. The controller's selection rule finds a directory holding a `PROMPT.md` and no `.done`, so a directory with this file in it and no `PROMPT.md` is correctly not selectable, and this directory is deliberately empty of a prompt.**

**THE FACT, IN ONE PARAGRAPH.** `outline/series.md` says the manuscript is fifteen volumes and 760 chapters. `outline/ending.md` says the manuscript ends at Chapter 760 and gives the final image in full. `NOVEL_SPEC.md` said the same until a directive arrived after the Volume 15 close had run, instructing that the novel continue and that a complete volume be followed by a planned next volume and its first batch. **Volume 16 is the volume that directive created. It ran from Chapter 761 to Chapter 820 and it is now closed at Chapter 820 by `workspace/volume-16/VOLUME-CLOSE/CLOSE.md`. No further directive has been given. The plan of record therefore still says the manuscript ends at Chapter 760, which is no longer true, and there is nothing on disk that says what comes after Chapter 820, and this close did not write one.**

**WHAT THE CLOSE DID AND DID NOT DO ABOUT IT.** It did **not** edit `outline/series.md` and did **not** edit `outline/ending.md` and did **not** write a plan of record for a seventeenth volume and did **not** decide that a seventeenth volume exists. **That decision has not been taken and it does not belong to a close.** A close's job is to compute a volume's figures from that volume's own anchors, to name the debts it did not pay with an owner, and to stop. Deciding what the book is after the book is a decision for whoever commissions it, and the person who has to take it is named in the two places below.

**WHO HAS TO TAKE THE DECISION.** Whoever dispatches the next phase of this repository, acting for whoever owns the manuscript. **The prompt of record for the Volume 16 close names the same owner in different words and both are correct.** There are three things such a decision would have to settle and none of them is settled here:

1. **Whether the manuscript ends at Chapter 820 or goes on.** The plan of record says 760, which was true when it was written and stopped being true when a directive continued the novel, and it has not been corrected because the close is not permitted to correct it.
2. **Whether the ending in `outline/ending.md` is still the ending.** It is a document written before Volume 15 that says the manuscript ends at Chapter 760. **It has not been edited by Volume 16 or by this close and it was not softened or contradicted by either.** The close records the fact and does not resolve it.
3. **Who owns the four debts that Volume 16 inherited and did not pay**, listed in full at `workspace/volume-16/VOLUME-CLOSE/CLOSE.md` §6 and at `state/open-threads.md` items 25 to 27: the answer to Volume 08's question and whether a chair holds it; the woman of fifty-three in custody and the question nobody has put to her twice; about nine people who were part of a network and are not in this city and have not been asked; and the three volume closes that never ran, which are now four.

**AND THE THREE UNRUN CLOSES ARE NOT THIS DIRECTORY'S BUSINESS.** `workspace/volume-12/VOLUME-CLOSE.md`, `workspace/volume-13/VOLUME-CLOSE.md` and `workspace/volume-14/VOLUME-CLOSE.md` are plain files at their volume roots, the controller finds only a directory holding a `PROMPT.md`, and therefore those three never ran. **Section 6 of `workspace/volume-14/ARITHMETIC-AND-CALENDAR.md` is still a reservation and its seventeen debts are the ones actually outstanding.** The Volume 16 close is the fourth close in this repository and the first one dispatched as a directory, and **it discharged none of those seventeen debts and may not, and it did not resolve, write off or pay any of the three.**

**A VOLUME THAT IS CLOSED AND NOT FOLLOWED BY ANOTHER IS A FACT AND NOT A DEFECT.** The book ends where it ends. The ending is a date that everybody already had: it is on a notice in a drawer in a second district, in that district's own hand, forty days old at Chapter 820, and about four hundred people it belongs to are about nine weeks further on with it and they have not been asked. **That is the last sentence of this manuscript's arithmetic and it is not improved here, because a close is most tempted to improve it and is not entitled to.**

**`state/phase-ledger.json` still reads `phase-000-bootstrap` and `planned`.** It is controller-owned, is on the list no agent pass may edit, was read and not written by the Volume 16 close, and has now been flagged fifty-five times. **The correct thing for a close to do about it is to print the number and touch nothing, and nothing was touched.**
