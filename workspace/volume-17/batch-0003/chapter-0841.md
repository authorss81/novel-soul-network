# Chapter 841 — A Monday, A Bench Until Two And A Counter Until About Six, And A Room Off A Service Road Where About Nine People Came To Hear Whether A Name A Man Copied Off A Map Had Survived A Week Of Being Used, And Two Men Who Drive Vans Quoted It To Each Other In A Shop And A Clerk Asked Which Of Two Names Goes In A Box And Nobody In That Shop Could Settle It

**A Monday was a bench until two and a counter until about six, and the evening went to a room off a service road where about nine people came to hear what a week of using a name had done to it. Two men who drive vans quoted it to each other at the counter. A clerk asked which of two names went in a box, and nobody could settle it.**

---

Eleven names went onto that Monday's sheet and eleven dates with them, the eleventh taken at about twenty to five. The shutter came down at about ten.

**Those four rooms off that service road stand at one thousand five hundred and forty-seven days, two hundred and twenty-one weeks to the day, and the card on end in the rail beside that door is one thousand five hundred and fifty-one, two hundred and twenty-one weeks and four days, and the rail takes two.**

**Line sixteen is one thousand three hundred and thirty-seven days, a hundred and ninety-one weeks to the day. The nine hand copies of the front of one page are one thousand one hundred and thirteen, a hundred and fifty-nine weeks to the day, one corner torn, eight unfinished, and not two compared.**

---

**And the thing that room off that service road was opened for on that Monday was not the name and was not the four hundred people, and about four of the nine had come to find out whether the name had survived a week.**

The name went onto nine clipboards in a depot in a fourth district on the Friday before and onto about nine mouths in a room in a second district the same evening, and it had been in use since and had been said out loud by a man of about thirty-four to about four people who did not want to say it back.

**Nobody in that room had heard anybody over a border use it and nobody in that room asked anybody over a border about it.**

A woman of about twenty-nine said in about four seconds that she had heard it three times in four days and had not known on the first two occasions whether she had heard it or made it up.

A man of about forty-three said that his whole run is written against it now and that a run is written against a place and not against a stretch of country, and that he had a vehicle outside with that name on two sheets of paper clipped to the sun visor.

**Nobody in that room asked him what he intended to do about the two sheets and nobody in that room has asked him since.**

---

**And then the man of about thirty-four who owns four vehicles stood up and asked whether anybody had heard a person who had been over there use it, and about four people in that room said what they knew and none of them knew anything.**

He said that he had asked the man at the bus stop on the Friday morning and the man had said he had never heard it and had been on that road for about nine years.

He said that the man at the bus stop had then asked him a question back, which was whether anybody in this city had ever asked anybody over there what they called it, and that he had not been able to answer that one.

**Nobody in that room asked him what he had said to that and nobody in that room has asked him since, and about two people in that room have said afterwards that they wanted to and did not.**

---

**And about four people in that room had come for something else and stayed for that.**

The man of about thirty-four who works on the second floor of the building in Saltmarket said in about nine seconds that he had been over there and had done the thing himself twice, on a Sunday and on a Wednesday about eleven years ago, and that it worked both times, and that he still does not know whether it is safe.

He said that this has nothing to do with the name and that he had come because about four people in a room he is not in keep saying the name in a way that makes it sound like a shop.

**Nobody in that room said it was a shop and about two people in that room have said since that they thought about it for about nine minutes afterwards.**

A man of about fifty-two said in four seconds that a name on nine clipboards is a fact about nine clipboards and that it has been said that and that he is not going to say it again this week.

**Nobody in that room argued with him and nobody in that room has argued with him since.**

---

**And the rest of that Monday was a counter until about six and about two hours of work in a first district.**

**A rainwater head at the back of a row in the same street had been discharging into the channel the next shop drains into, so that two roofs went down one pipe whenever it rained hard.**

He fitted a new head, cut the hopper back to a proper fall, and hung it on two brackets that go into the joist ends and not into the render.

**Eleven pounds. He told the man that a head shared with the shop next door is one drain doing two roofs, and that four heads on that row are shared that way, and that one of them has a hopper cut so flat that it holds about a pint after every shower and goes out over the front door instead.**

---

**A trade counter in the same building had a kick plate loose at the end where the trolleys come round.**

He took the plate off, found the rail behind it with a detector, drilled through it, and fixed the plate back onto that.

**Seven pounds. He told the man that a kick plate is only as good as what it is fixed to, and that about four counters in that building are plated onto a rail that moves, and that one of them has been taken off and refitted twice in about four years by two different men.**

---

**A pair of butt hinges on a gate leaf at the back had been closed until the leaf did not swing through its own arc without a hand on it.**

He eased the leaves, dropped the pin about a millimetre and came back on it, and left the screw slots alone.

**Four pounds. He told the man that a hinge closed too tight is a hinge that has had its knuckles pinched shut, and that four gates on that yard have been adjusted that way, and that one of them has a leaf that has been rehung twice because it was thought to be out of square.**

---

**A soffit ventilator in the same building had been painted over on both faces and would not turn.**

He stripped the paint back with a strip, freed the linkage, and set the vane so it opens and stays open.

**Nine pounds. He told the man that a ventilator that cannot turn is a hole in a roof that is only a hole, and that about four soffit vents in that building have been painted, and that one of them has a vane on a linkage that somebody has bent so that it jams in the closed position.**

**That Monday came to thirty-one pounds, exact, and that figure is the four of them added together and not any one of them.**

**From half past six there has been a woman of about thirty at her chair in the fourth of those four rooms, the door of that room shut behind her, and the eighth of eight has lain on the back shelf for three hundred and thirty-six days.**

**Nobody has put one question to her about that page and nobody is going to. What is written on it is not given here.**
