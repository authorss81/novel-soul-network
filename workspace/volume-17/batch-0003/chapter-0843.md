# Chapter 843 — A Wednesday, A Bench Until Two And A Counter Until About Six, And A First Floor Above A Line In Saltmarket Where The Room Was Open And No Number Was Said In It Because A Number Is Said At A Sitting And This Was Not One, And About Nine People Came Up And About Four Of Them Came About A Word Nobody Has Written Down, And A Man Of About Fifty-Two Said In About Nine Seconds That There Is No Place In This City Where It Is Written Down On Purpose And That Nobody Ever Decided There Should Not Be One

**No number was said in the room the movement's real finding was said in, because a number is said at a sitting and this Wednesday was not one. About nine people came up to a first floor above a line in Saltmarket and about four of them came about a word nobody in this city has.**

---

Nine names, and the same count of dates against them, went onto that Wednesday's sheet; the ninth date was taken at about twenty to five and the shutter came down at ten.

**Line eighteen is one thousand two hundred and sixty-seven days, a hundred and eighty-one weeks to the day. The man of about fifty-one against the north wall is one thousand one hundred and fifty-five days, a hundred and sixty-five weeks to the day. What is still owed is one thousand two hundred and thirty-nine days, a hundred and seventy-seven weeks to the day.**

**Behind the chair of the woman of about sixty a place has stood empty since a week in the spring, and it was empty on that Wednesday, and this page does not set a figure against it.**

The ninth chair is against that wall with its back to the whole of the room. **It did not move on that Wednesday, and on no page of this stretch of days is the person who moves it named.**

Nobody in that room asked her about the place and nobody in that room has asked her about it in a long time, and this page does not say how long a time that is.

---

**And no number was said in that room on that Wednesday, because a number is said at a sitting and a sitting is a Wednesday of every fourth week and this was not one.**

About nine people came up that stair between about half past six and about eight and about four of them had come about a word rather than about anything else.

A woman of about thirty-four said in about nine seconds that she had come about the word, and that she had asked for it in about nine years and had never once been told it by anybody, and that she has now asked in three rooms in about nine days.

**Nobody in that room gave her the word and nobody in that room has it and nobody in that room looked like somebody who had it.**

She said that the man at a bus stop uses a different word for it and has always used a different word, and that when she asked him what he called it he told her what he called it and she did not understand the answer and did not ask him twice.

---

**And the thing that made the rest of that movement was said at about ten past seven in about nine seconds by a man of about fifty-two, and it was not said to anybody.**

He said that there is no place in this city where it is written down on purpose, and that there never has been, and that this is not because anybody decided there should not be one.

He said that he has looked for about two years and has found three places where somebody wrote it down while doing something else, and that in none of the three did the person writing know they were writing a record of anything.

**Nobody in that room asked him what the three places are and nobody in that room has asked him since, and about four people in that room have said afterwards that they wanted to and did not, because he had said it to the room and not to them.**

A man of about thirty-eight said in about four seconds that a thing nobody has written down on purpose is a thing nobody has ever agreed to, and that this city is full of those and does not print a list.

The man of about fifty-two said that he had found it at about nine in the morning and had gone to a counter and been told about nine hundred pounds and about nine weeks, and that he had come up here to say the other thing, which was that there was nobody to send.

---

**And about four people in that room had wanted somebody to have said that nobody had decided it, and about four had wanted somebody to say that somebody had.**

A woman of about thirty-nine said in about nine seconds that she had wanted somebody to have decided, about nine years ago, that the thing was not going to be written down, and that she has wanted that for about nine years and that she would be ashamed to say so in front of about nine people.

**Nobody in that room said that was a reasonable thing to want and nobody in that room said it was not, and about two people in that room have said since that it was the only thing said that evening they believed.**

A man of about fifty-one against that wall, who has been in that room for about nine years and says so, said in about four seconds that he has heard four things said in that room in nine years and that three of them were about money.

---

**And then a man of about thirty-four who is not in this room and was not in it said nothing, and about four people in that room did not know he had found the three places.**

He works in a first district and about nine people who work with him found two of the three, in the course of about four years of looking at a thing that was not there.

He has said since that he was asked by about four of them whether he wanted to put his name on it and said no in about four seconds, and that he has not been thanked for that and did not expect to be, and that the finding is not his and has not been given to him.

**Nobody in this city has given that finding to anybody, and about four people who know about it have said that if it is anybody's it belongs to the people who looked, and about four have said that means nobody.**

---

**And the rest of that Wednesday was a counter until about six and about two hours of work in a fourth district.**

**A set of points at the end of a run had been made to close on a washer somebody had slipped behind the blade.**

He took the washer out, cleaned the faces, and worked the mechanism through about nine times with a bar before he let it stand.

**Twenty-four pounds. He told the man that a shim behind a blade is a set of points that will close on whatever is put there, and that about four sets on that run have been adjusted with something behind the blade, and that one of them has a wear plate that has been packed about half a millimetre and has taken the packing up with it.**

---

**A length of cable in a tray in the same place had been cut through by a tray that had been dropped back onto it and had its sheath opened about four inches along.**

He cut the length out, ran a new one through on the same route, and dressed the tray so it cannot come down onto a cable again.

**Nineteen pounds. He told the man that a tray put back on top of a cable cuts the cable and hides it, and that about four trays in that place have been put back without anything being checked underneath, and that one of them has a run in it that has been replaced twice in about four years and nobody has ever asked why.**

---

**A lamp on a column at that end of the run had its supply taken up into the bracket instead of through a gland in the base.**

He cut it out, ran a new tail into a gland in the base plate, and left the bracket with nothing in it.

**Twenty-six pounds. He told the man that a supply taken up into a bracket is a bracket that has to be opened every time the lamp is changed, and that about four columns on that run have a cable up inside the bracket, and that one of them has a lamp that cannot be reached without a man standing in a four-foot road.**

---

**A run of handrail at the footbridge had its brackets set only into the mortar joints and not into the brickwork behind them.**

He took the rail off, filled the old holes, drilled each bracket into two courses of brick, and refitted the rail.

**Twenty-six pounds. He told the man that a bracket in a mortar joint is a bracket in about nine millimetres of nothing, and that four runs at that footbridge are fixed into joints, and that one of them has moved about four inches out of square where somebody has stood on it to look over a wall.**

**That Wednesday came to ninety-five pounds, exact, which is the four of them added together.**

**At half past six a woman of about thirty sat down at her chair in the fourth of those four rooms and shut the door, and the eighth of eight stood behind her at three hundred and thirty-eight days.**

**No person in this city has asked her a single question about that page, and the question is not going to be put by anybody here either.**
