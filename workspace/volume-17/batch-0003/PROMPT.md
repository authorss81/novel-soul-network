## 2. The movement, and what it is about

**Four hundred people use a thing every day, none of them is on a form of this body, and none of them can be put on one. The discovery is an omission and not a secret, and it is not a conspiracy and it has no person behind it.**

`outline/volume-17.md` fixes the movement in one sentence: **the practice is described in three places where it was written down by accident and in no place on purpose, and a person in this city is asked to sign something about people who are not in this city and refuses in about four seconds for a reason that is about a signature and not about the border.**

**Three things happen across the ten days, in this order, and they are the movement's spine:**

Chapter 840 closed on a woman of about thirty-four who decided one thing about next week and told nobody. **Movement III opens on the Monday after and a pass must not be written as though she has told anybody.**
