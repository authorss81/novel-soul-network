# Chapter 870 — A Monday, A Counter Until About Six And About Two Hours At It, And Four Days Of Work Booked Over The Fortnight Ahead Of Which A Man Of Twenty-Two Decided To Give One Up, And A Woman Of About Thirty-Four Who Carried A Form Back On A Bus Carrying A Different Sheet In Her Bag That Nobody In This City Has Been Told What Is Written On

**A Monday three days after the last of these days, a counter until about six, about two hours at it, four days of work already booked over the fortnight ahead, and one of them given up. Two women were carrying something that nobody had asked them to carry and nobody has been told what was on either of them. The rest of the day was in a fourth district.**

---

Six names went onto the sheet for that Monday with six dates against them, the last of those dates at about ten to five, and at about ten that shutter was shut.

**Those four converted units off the service road, one of them warm, are one thousand five hundred and eighty-nine days, two hundred and twenty-seven weeks to the day. The sixteenth of those nineteen ruled lines is one thousand three hundred and seventy-nine days, a hundred and ninety-seven weeks to the day. The front of one page written out in nine hands stands at one thousand one hundred and fifty-five days, a hundred and sixty-five weeks to the day, and not one pair of the nine has been laid beside another.**

**And no number was said in any room in this city on that Monday and no count was announced anywhere, because the Wednesday this city counts on is three days off and nobody in this city has said a number in a room for about nine days.**

A woman of about thirty-four who is in the first of those four rooms said in about nine seconds that a figure in a room every fourth Wednesday has stopped feeling like anything, and that the four of them who used to plan round it have gone back to planning round the day.

**Nobody in that room said that was a loss and nobody said it was a relief, and about four people in that room have said since that they would not have noticed either way if they had not been asked.**

---

**The four days of work booked over the fortnight ahead are a meter box, a set of gates, a conveyor and a distribution board, and they are one day each in that fortnight and not one day together.**

A man of twenty-two gave up the third of the four at about half past one on that Monday, and gave it up for about nine seconds of thinking and did not enjoy any of the nine seconds.

**He said in about nine seconds that he had told the man in that yard he would turn up on the Thursday having given nothing away on the way, and that the third day booked falls on the Thursday.**

He said that the reason is that a man in a first district who has four of nine things on his sheet is a man who will say something useful on a morning when nothing has been asked of him for two hours.

**Nobody in that yard asked him what the nine things are and nobody in that yard has asked him since, and the other three days of that fortnight were done exactly as they were booked.**

---

**And two women were carrying things on that Monday that nobody in this city had been told about.**

A woman of about thirty-four who carried a form back on a bus from two days of travel has a different sheet folded in four in the inside pocket of her bag, and on it there are two nine-word sentences in her own hand and nothing else, and she wrote them on the back of a sheet in a room on the Friday before last and said in that room that it was going to stay in her bag.

**Nobody in this city has asked her what is written on it, nobody has asked her about the third word, and she has not said in this city what a person over there would call those people and is not going to start on a Monday.**

A woman of about forty-four who works at the trade four doors along from the book wrote a second sheet that Sunday in her own hand, after his had gone into his pocket, and that one has the road on it and no name on it, and she has been carrying it since, and she has not been thanked for either and has said in advance that she is not going to be.

**Nobody in this city has been told which road is written on that sheet and nobody has asked, and about four people who know about the sheet have said since that a road is not a person and that this is the whole of what she asked for.**

---

**And about four people in this city have said, in about nine seconds each, and separately, that a body of about eight with one person missing has about nine days left of anything it was going to do, and that nobody has told them what it is going to do.**

Nobody in this city has said what that body will do about a trade four doors along from the room where its book stands, and nobody has told the man of about forty-seven which day it is going to be asked on.

**The man who owns four vehicles said in about nine seconds that he will be driving past that road on the Wednesday morning and that if anybody wants to put a sheet in that man's hand they can be on the corner, and that he will not do it and that he has already said so.**

The woman of about thirty-four who carried a form back on a bus was at that counter on that Monday and was not asked anything about the sheet in her bag and was not asked about the third word and did not say in this city what a person over there calls those people.

**Nobody at that counter asked her a second thing about any of it and nobody at that counter has asked her since, and she bought about nine inches of cord and went out at about ten to six.**

Nobody in this city has stood on that corner, and about four people have said since that they would have stood there, and did not, and have given no reason for it to anybody.

---

The three days of work that were not given up were a meter box, a set of gates and a distribution board, and about nine people in that yard had heard about the Thursday by way of about four people who had heard about the fortnight.

**Nobody in that yard asked him what the Thursday was for, and about four people in that yard have said since that they would have asked and did not, and that a man who gives up a day of work for something he will not name is easy to work beside.**

**A man of twenty-two was at that counter from about four, was asked nothing at all, and said nothing until about twenty past four, and what he said then was about a door.**

He said that the counter itself has a foot on it that is not the same as the other three and that about nine years of people leaning on that counter have leaned a little bit to one side without knowing it.

**Nobody standing at that counter had any idea what to do with that, and nobody came back with a second question.**

Nothing was signed by him in this stretch of days, he signed nothing on that Monday, and nothing on that Monday was put up to him.

---

**The counter closed at about six on that Monday and the last two hours of it were worked in a fourth district.**

**A regulator on a compressor in the same place had its cover plate off and had been refitted with four of the six screws, so that it had been breathing round its own edge for about four years.**

He fitted the two missing screws, took the cover off, cleaned the seat, and proved the machine against its own gauge.

**Fifty-five pounds. He told the man that a cover plate with four screws on it is not sealed and never was, and that about four machines in that plant room have been done that way, and that one of them has been reported as a machine that will not hold pressure for about four years.**

---

**A chain on a hoist in the same yard had been repaired with a link that was about a quarter longer than the others and had been put in with a connector that had been used before.**

He took the chain out, took the repaired length off, and put in a new length of the same size.

**Thirty-one pounds. He told the man that a repair in a lifting chain is not a repair until it has been proved, and that about four chains in that yard have been mended, and that one of them has been reported as a chain nobody trusts for about four years.**

---

**A guard on a machine in the same building had been set so far open that the cut-off could not reach it, and it had been set that way by somebody who was being shown how the guard works.**

He set the guard back, proved the cut-off with the gate open, and left it in that position.

**Twenty-six pounds. He told the man that a guard set out of reach is a guard that has been demonstrated rather than fitted, and that about four guards in that building have been set like that, and that one of them has been reported as a machine that runs on with the gate up for about four years.**

---

**A wall plate in the same place had been earthed to a length of water pipe instead of to the building, so that the earth was a pipe that other people run.**

He took it off the pipe, ran it back to the box, and proved it.

**Nineteen pounds. He told the man that an earth on somebody else's pipe is an earth somebody else can turn off, and that about four plates in that building are like that, and that one of them has been reported as a fault nobody could find for about four years.**

**The four of those prices came to a hundred and thirty-one pounds on that Monday, exact.**

**At half past six the fourth of those four rooms was shut and a woman of about thirty was still behind that door, and a shelf at the back of that room was holding the eighth of eight at three hundred and seventy-eight days.**

**Nobody has put a question to her about it and nobody is going to. What is on that page is not given here.**
