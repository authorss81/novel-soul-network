## 2. The movement's spine, and where each of its three things is

**The thing that is told.** Chapter 864. A trade that opens only on a Saturday and on a Sunday is told, at about five past twelve on the only Sunday in this volume, that it is going to be asked about the thing it does. **It is not a confession: nobody is asked a question on that first floor, nobody admits anything to anybody, and nothing was decided.** The page is short, the room is open for about two hours, and the shutter comes down at about two, which is the only shutter clause in sixty chapters of this volume that names an hour other than about ten.
