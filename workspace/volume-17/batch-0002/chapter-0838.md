# Chapter 838 — A Wednesday, A Bench Until Two And A Counter Until About Six, And A First Floor Above A Line In Saltmarket Where The Sixty-First Sitting Was Held And The Count Was Said Once And The Book With A Green Cover Was On Sixty-Seven Lines Before The Room Emptied And On Sixty-Seven Lines After, And The Tin Beside It Was Not Opened, And The Ninth Chair Did Not Move And The Place Behind The Woman Of About Sixty Stood Empty And No Figure For It Is Given On This Page

**A Wednesday was a bench until two and a counter until about six, and it finished in a first floor above a line in Saltmarket, where the sixty-first sitting was held. The count was said once and it was sixty-six, of which sixty-one correspond, and the book with a green cover was on sixty-seven lines when the room opened and on sixty-seven lines when the room emptied, and the tin beside it was not opened.**

---

Nine names went onto that Wednesday's sheet and nine dates with them, the ninth at about twenty to five. The shutter came down at about ten.

**Line eighteen is one thousand two hundred and sixty days, a hundred and eighty weeks to the day. The man of about fifty-one against the north wall is one thousand one hundred and forty-eight days, a hundred and sixty-four weeks to the day. What is still owed is one thousand two hundred and thirty-two, one hundred and seventy-five weeks and four days.**

**Behind the chair of the woman of about sixty a place has stood empty since a week in the spring, and it is empty on that Wednesday, and this page does not set a figure against it.**

The ninth chair is against that wall with its back to the whole of the room. **It did not move on that Wednesday, and on no page of this stretch of days is the person who moves it named.**

Nobody in that room asked her about the place and nobody in that room has asked her about it in about fifty-nine weeks.

---

**And the count was said once, in about nine seconds, in her face, by the woman of about sixty, and it was sixty-six, of which sixty-one correspond, and she said it the way she says the date at the top of a page.**

Nobody in that room said a number back to her and nobody in that room asked her anything else, and no line went into that book that evening.

**The five that are not correspond are not printed in that room and are not printed on this page and are the five that predate the book on a sheet she has never shown anybody.**

A man of about thirty-eight said in about nine seconds that he had had about four words in his mouth since about half past six and had not said them, and that about four people in this city would recognise the four words if he said them.

She said in four seconds that he had said that at the last two of these, and that a person who has said a thing three times has said it, and that this is not a room for the count of mouths.

**Nobody in that room said whether the book opens at some other Wednesday of this kind and nobody in that room has said so since.**

---

**And the whole of what was said about the region over there in that room on that Wednesday was about nine sentences and none of them was about the region.**

A woman of about thirty-four said that she has been asked four times in about nine days whether she knows anything about it, and that she does not, and that she has said so four times, and that she is not going to say so a fifth time in a room.

A man of about forty-four said that he read two sets of four words out loud on the Monday and that one of the eight words means the opposite over there, and that he is not going to say which one in that room, and that he was asked to say it in that room by about four people.

**Nobody in that room asked him again and nobody in that room has asked him since, and about four people in that room had wanted him to and did not say so themselves.**

A man of about fifty-two said in about nine seconds that the fact is not in this city and that the only places it is are about nine people's heads over a border, and that about four of those nine do not know it is theirs.

Nobody in that room said which four.

A woman of about twenty-nine said, in about four seconds, that this is the third sitting in about nine years in which something about that region has been said in the room, and that at the other two it was not said at all, and that she is not sure whether that is an improvement.

**Nobody in that room agreed with her and nobody in that room disagreed with her.**

---

**And about four people stayed the hour, and the whole of what the four of them did with the hour was argue about a form with a box at the bottom of it and about the word in the box.**

The word in the box is *as it has been*, and it was put there by somebody who did not choose it, and about four people in that room said that a word in a box where a date should be is a fact and about four said it is a way of not writing a date.

A woman of about thirty-eight said in about nine seconds that both of those are the same thing said by two people who would rather not be the one who is wrong.

A man of about thirty-four said in four seconds that the form is not the point, and that the point is that nobody in this city has asked anybody over there anything, ever, and that this is a fact about this city and not about the form.

**Nobody in that room said that this city had decided that, and nobody in that room has said it, and there was no decision and nobody has ever looked for one.**

---

**And the man of twenty-two was in that corridor outside that room for about half an hour and he was not asked anything and he is not the person who signs anything in this stretch of days and he was not the person that form was put up to.**

He has said since that he stood in that corridor and worked out that a room can now say a number and then say nothing at all, and that this is an order of business he has not seen in about nine years.

He also said that he had one sentence ready and did not say it, and that the sentence was about the five that are not correspond, and that asking about the five is a way of asking about the sheet she has never shown anybody.

**Nobody in that corridor asked him what the sentence was and he did not say it.**

---

**And the rest of that Wednesday was a counter until about six and about two hours of work in a first district, where a length of handrail had been fixed with brackets set into plaster and not into anything behind it.**

He took the rail off, filled the eight holes, found the wall plate behind with a detector, and fixed the brackets through the plaster into that.

**Twenty-six pounds. He told the man that a bracket in plaster is a bracket in about nine millimetres of board, and that four rails in that building are fixed that way, and that one of them comes off the wall about four inches in a year with a coat on it.**

---

**And a flight of stairs in the same building had about eleven treads with the nosings cut back to fit a carpet.**

He took nine of them up, put a new nosing on each to the same section, and relaid them.

**Twenty-six pounds. He told the man that a nosing cut back for a carpet is a nosing with nothing left to take a heel, and that four flights in that building have been cut, and that one of them has a tread worn about nine millimetres through in the middle.**

---

**And a window in the same building had been painted shut, and it was the second time in about nine years that any window in that building has been painted shut.**

He broke the seal with a plastic wedge and a card, cleaned the rebate out with a wire brush, and repacked the sash with a length of tapered wood shim rather than with paper.

**Eleven pounds. He told the man that a painted window is a window somebody has decided is not a window, and that four windows in that building have been painted, and that nobody in that building has a list of which four.**

---

**And a landing handrail on the same stairs had been painted where a hand went and nowhere else, with a patch about nine inches long in the middle of nowhere.**

He rubbed the rail back where the paint had built on it, took the paint off the underside with a strip, and left the rest of it alone.

**Eleven pounds. He told the man that about four rails on that stair have been painted in patches, and that a rail with a patch in the middle of nowhere is a rail somebody has decided is not a surface in two places.**

---
