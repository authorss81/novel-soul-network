## 2. The movement, and what it is about

**A region is given a name by a person who has no standing to give it a name, and the name is wrong, and nobody in this city can correct it.**

**This is the volume's central act and it is not a reveal.** Nobody in Movement II discovers a conspiracy, nobody finds a hidden order, nobody is named as the person who kept it that way, **because there is no person and there was never a decision.** The discovery is that nobody in this city could have sent anybody to look, and not because anybody forbade it.

**Four things happen across the ten days, in this order, and they are the movement's spine:**

- **The name.** Somebody in this city writes down what the region over the border is called, in a room, in front of about nine people, and the name is a name this city made up, or a name somebody over there uses for one town and not for the region, or a name from a map two generations old. **Pick one of those three and do not soften it.** Nobody in the room can correct it and at least one person in that room says so.
- **The four words.** Somebody works out that the four words over the ruled line on the form mean four different things on the other side and that **one of them means the opposite.** Movement I established the two sets of four words are not the same (Chapter 829) and that nobody said which was right. **Movement II is where the opposite is found, and the finding is not published to the four hundred and is not put in front of the woman of about thirty-four as a correction.**
- **Rafi Pell's cooperative holds a version of it for about nine weeks and says what it would cost.** He is in Movements II and VI. **The cost is named and it is not small and nobody offers to pay it.**
- **The sixty-first sitting, and the book does not open.** Chapter 838.
