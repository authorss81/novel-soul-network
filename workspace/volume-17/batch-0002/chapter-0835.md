# Chapter 835 — A Friday, A Bench Until Two And A Counter Until About Six, About Four Hours Of It At A Counter With Fourteen Callers On It, And A Man Of About Fifty-Two Who Came In And Asked What A Cooperative Held For Nine Weeks Would Cost And Was Given A Figure And A Span Of Somebody's Working Life And Offered None Of It And Was Not Asked To

**A Friday was a bench until two and a counter until about six, and about four hours of it were a counter with fourteen callers on it. A man of about fifty-two came in at half past three and asked what it would cost for a cooperative to hold a version of the four words for nine weeks, and nobody in that shop offered him any of it.**

**And the man of about fifty-two came in at about half past three and did not buy anything and he stood at that counter for about nine minutes.**

He asked what it would cost for a cooperative to hold a version of the four words for about nine weeks.

**He had been told the name of a cooperative and he had it written on the back of a receipt and he said in about nine seconds that he had been told it by a man of about thirty-eight who works in a third district and that the man had said the cooperative was the only thing in this city that had ever held anything on somebody else's behalf.**

Marek said no in about four seconds, and gave a reason that was about the counter and not about the man, which was that the shop is a shop and a cooperative is not a shop.

**The man of about fifty-two said he knew that, said it was not a stupid question, and said he had asked four people this week and been told no three times.**

---

**And the figure and the span were given to him, and they were given in about nine seconds, and neither of them was invented at that counter.**

**The figure is about nine hundred pounds. The span is about nine weeks of one person's working time, and the person is named on a card in a room over a shop and is not named here because he has not been asked.**

A man of about thirty-four who works in that shop said the two figures and said they are not a price and that nobody in that room is selling them.

The man of about fifty-two said, in about nine seconds, that nine hundred pounds is about four months of what about nine men earn between them, and that he has got about eleven pounds in the week he has left in it, and that he did not say that for himself.

**Nobody in that shop asked him about the eleven pounds and nobody in that shop has asked him since.**

---

**And nobody in that shop offered any of it, and Marek was not asked to pay it and did not offer.**

He said, in about four seconds, that he was not going to be asked and that he was not going to offer, and that the reason he said both of those out loud is that he has been in this city long enough to know what happens to a man who says the second one without saying the first.

**A man of about twenty-six in that shop said, in about nine seconds, that the two sentences had been about four seconds apart and that she had heard the first one and had not heard the second one until after.**

He said that he had said the second one on purpose and that this was not a thing he was proud of.

Nobody in that shop said anything about that.

---

**And the man of about fifty-two asked one more question, and it was the question he had come in with, and it was about the four words and not about the money.**

He asked whether the version the cooperative would hold would be the four words as they are written on the form, or the four words as the other side uses them.

He said he has asked four people and that two of them did not know there was a second set and that one of them got the order of two of them wrong and that one of them would not say where she had got hers.

**Nobody in that shop answered him, and the shop does not hold either set, and about two people in that shop have said since that they would have liked to be able to answer and could not.**

A man of about thirty-four said that whichever set it was, it would be a version, and that a version is a thing one set of words says about another set of words and that this is not a small thing to hand to nine weeks of somebody's time.

The man of about fifty-two said he understood that and had not thought of it and he wrote the figure on the back of the receipt.

**He did not pay and was not asked to and nobody has written down that he came in.**

---

**And about nine of the other thirteen callers on that counter were about a shop, and about four of them are worth setting down because they are the ordinary shape of a Friday.**

A woman of about thirty-one brought in a length of chain that fitted the bicycle and had been fitted by somebody who did not know what it was for, and he said so in about nine seconds and charged her about nine pounds and told her that the link in it is a quarter of an inch under what that chain takes and that a person who does not know what a chain is for fits the nearest thing.

A man of about twenty-six brought back a bag he had bought there three weeks earlier with about nine things in it and about four of them were the part he wanted and about four of them were for a different thing entirely, and he was told which four and he said that somebody in a shop in a first district had told him the other four would do and that nobody had told him that in this shop.

**He was told that nobody in this shop had told him that, and he carried the bag away, and nobody in that shop has said anything about the third time.**

A woman of about sixty came in for a lamp she had brought back and was told, in about four seconds, that she had brought that lamp back before and that it was the flex again, and it was the flex, and it cost about four pounds and she said it had cost her eleven pounds the first time and was not asked which shop.

A man of about forty-three asked whether a gate that had been shut about nine weeks ago was still holding, and was told that it was, and paid nothing and was not asked to.
