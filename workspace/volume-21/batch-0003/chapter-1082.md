# Chapter 1082 — He Brought A Lamp To Be Looked At

**A Monday was a bench until about two and a counter until six and then four converted units off that service road, and that Monday afternoon was given over at one counter to a man who wanted something written down and to a light over a door that had been left on all night.**

---

Twelve callers came into that shop on that Monday and twelve names were written against that Monday's book as each of them came in, the last of them let away by about half past nine, and the shutter came down at ten that night.

**Those four converted units off that service road stood at two thousand and two days, two hundred and eighty-six weeks to the day. The sixteenth of those ruled lines, never once written on, stood at one thousand seven hundred and ninety-two days, two hundred and fifty-six weeks to the day. The bag under that long bench is a hundred and seventy-five days old, the printing that has never gone on a wall is a hundred and sixty-one days old, and the four words said at a desk in a first district are a hundred and fifty-two days old.**

---

**He was in that room off that line in Saltmarket until about two, and about four people were in it, and nobody asked him anything, and the woman of about fifty-four said the whole of what she said that day, which was that the light over the door in that room had been burning since she got in.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **That light over the door was on when I came in.**

Marek said: **It is on now.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **It was on at about half past six in the morning and there is nobody in here till nine.**

Marek said: **Then somebody has a key I do not know about.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **I have not said that a key.**

---

**And then at about half past three the man of about fifty-one came in with a lamp under his arm that had been taken out of a kitchen about four years ago and had never worked since, and put it on that counter, and about four people in that shop have said since that he carried it in with him rather than have it sent.**

The man of about fifty-one said: **Will you look at this.**

Marek said: **I will look at it.**

The man of about fifty-one said: **I am not asking you to mend it.**

Marek said: **You are carrying it in under your arm at half past three.**

The man of about fifty-one said: **I have not decided what I am doing with it.**

---

**And then the man said the thing that Marek did not argue with, and about four people in that shop have said since that Marek did not argue with it and has not denied it on any day since.**

The man of about fifty-one said: **You told me on Sunday there is one account of that Wednesday and it is a sentence.**

Marek said: **There is.**

The man of about fifty-one said: **Then you are the only man in this city who can say it is wrong.**

Marek said: **And I have told you I will not say it is wrong and will not say it is so.**

The man of about fifty-one said: **You have told me that twice. I have come back because a third thing happened on that road.**

Marek said: **What happened on that road.**

The man of about fifty-one said: **A woman I have cut hair for twenty years said it to me on a bus and she does not know I have been in that room.**

---

**And then Marek made an offer out loud that was not a trade and about four people in that shop have said since that he made it before the man had finished standing there and that the man did not take it.**

Marek said: **I will mend that lamp and I will not charge you for it.**

The man of about fifty-one said: **No.**

Marek said: **It needs a base plate and about four inches of flex and it is not worth the walk.**

The man of about fifty-one said: **Do not do me a kindness over it.**

Marek said: **It is not a kindness. It is a lamp.**

The man of about fifty-one said: **It is what you have got instead of the thing I asked for, and I am not taking it.**

---

**And then he left the lamp on that counter and went out into the rain with it still under his arm, and about four people in that shop have said since that he did not take it and that he has not been in that shop since.**

The man of about fifty-one said: **I will have it back on Friday.**

Marek said: **I will keep it behind the till.**

The man of about fifty-one said: **Do not write it down that I left it.**

Marek said: **Nobody writes anything down in this shop.**

---

**Nobody thanked anybody in that shop on that Monday and nobody forgave anybody, and about four people at that counter have said since that a man carried a lamp across the city to be told not to be helped, and that the lamp is still behind that till.**

---

**That day's work at that counter and in those four units was finished by about half past five, and at about half past six he went across to four converted units off that service road and worked there until about eight.**

A lamp in that shop had been put in on a holder with the ring missing off one side of it and the ear still on the other.

He put a new ring on and proved the lamp with the shade on and the wall behind it.

**Seventeen pounds**, he said. **One ear and no ring is a lamp that has been on a wall with nothing holding it.**

A socket in that yard had been put in on an outlet with the lid fixed over the entry glands and the conduit arriving from underneath.

He took the lid off, made the entries and proved the socket from the gate in the wet.

**Twenty-six pounds**, he said. **Putting a lid on before the glands are made is putting the lid on for the photograph.**

A cooker in that kitchen had been put in with an oven element that was one turn short of the bottom and had been left to cook the panel above it.

He set the gap and proved the oven coming up and going off twice.

**Thirteen pounds**, he said. **An element a turn short will not tell you. It will cost you the panel above it and never say a word.**

A light in that second unit had been put in on a switch that had been fitted into a metal box with the earth left off the box and onto the plaster.

He put the earth on the box and proved it with an instrument in the wet.

**Twenty-two pounds**, he said. **The earth wants metal. Plaster is not metal and it never has been.**

**Seventy-eight pounds is the sum of those four on that Monday, exact.**
