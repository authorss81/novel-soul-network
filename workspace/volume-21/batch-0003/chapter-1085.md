# Chapter 1085 — What She Said Was Neither

**A Thursday was a bench until about two and a counter until six and then four converted units off that service road, and the middle of that Thursday was spent at a table in a canteen in a second district where a man who has run a kitchen for about nine years asked a woman what she would want written and got one answer back.**

---

Eleven callers came into that shop on that Thursday and eleven names were written against that Thursday's book as each of them came in, the last of them let away by about half past nine, and the shutter came down at ten that night.

**Those four converted units off that service road stood at two thousand and five days, two hundred and eighty-six weeks and three days. The thirteenth of those lines, ruled under the twelfth and blank, stood at one thousand eight hundred and seventy-six days, two hundred and sixty-eight weeks to the day. The bag under that long bench is a hundred and seventy-eight days old, the printing that has never been on a wall is a hundred and sixty-four days old, and the four words said at a desk in a first district are a hundred and fifty-five days old.**

---

**He was in that room off that line in Saltmarket until about two, and about four people were in it, and the woman of about fifty-four had brought that flex lamp back to the wall and put it in the same place it had been in for about four years.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **It is back where it was.**

Marek said: **Good.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **You are not pleased.**

Marek said: **I am not pleased that you carried it across that room by the flex and I am glad you have put it back where I can get at it.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **That is the same thing said twice.**

Marek said: **It is not, and you have heard both of them.**

---

**And then at about half past three he was in a canteen in a second district with about nine people in it and the noise of the machines behind the counter, and the woman of about thirty-four was at a table by the wall with a tray in front of her that she had not touched, and the man of about fifty-one came in behind him about four minutes later.**

The man of about fifty-one said: **You have not been in here before.**

Marek said: **No.**

The man of about fifty-one said: **I ran the kitchen in here for about nine years.**

Marek said: **Then you will know where the plates are.**

The man of about fifty-one said: **I know where the plates are. I am here about the other thing.**

---

**And then he asked her, out loud, at that table, in front of about four people, what she would want in it if it were ever written, and about four people in that canteen have said since that he asked it standing up beside her table and that she did not look up from that tray while he said it.**

The man of about fifty-one said: **If it was written. What would you want in it.**

The woman of about thirty-four said: **That people came and were not asked for anything.**

The man of about fifty-one said: **That is what you would want in it.**

The woman of about thirty-four said: **I have answered you once and I am not going to be asked it twice.**

---

**And then the man said out loud why he could not use it, and about four people in that canteen have said since that he said the reason to her and not to anybody else in that room and that she let him get to the end of it.**

The man of about fifty-one said: **There is no such thing in it. Not one hour and not one name and not one thing anybody said.**

The woman of about thirty-four said: **That is what a thing like that is.**

The man of about fifty-one said: **That is a way of putting a record together where there is no record to put together.**

The woman of about thirty-four said: **Then you will not be able to use what I have given you and you asked me for it in a canteen rather than anywhere else.**

---

**And then he said what he was going to do with the rest of it, out loud, and about four people in that canteen have said since that he said it to the room and not to her, and that she began eating about halfway through it.**

The man of about fifty-one said: **There are eight more of them and I am going to ask every one.**

The woman of about thirty-four said: **Ask them for what you asked me.**

Marek said: **He is going to ask them what they were asked and what they said back.**

The woman of about thirty-four said: **That is a different question and you have both of you noticed that inside a second.**

The man of about fifty-one said: **It is the same question.**

The woman of about thirty-four said: **Then you will get the same answer nine times and it will not be a record, it will be a rhyme.**

---

**Nobody thanked anybody in that canteen on that Thursday and nobody forgave anybody, and about four people at that wall have said since that a man asked a woman what she would want written and was given one answer that cannot be used, and that she has not said it again to anybody including him.**

---

**That day's work at that counter and in those four units was finished by about half past five, and at about half past six he went across to four converted units off that service road where one of them carries heat.**

A lamp in that shop had been put in on a ceiling rose with a terminal block pushed in behind the backplate and two of the screws left hanging.

He pulled the block forward, made both terminations and proved the lamp from the floor.

**Twenty-three pounds**, he said. **A block pushed in behind a plate is a block that will be behind that plate for years.**

A socket in that yard had been put in with the conduit pushed into the box and the box filled round the conduit with nothing at all.

He packed the entry properly, sleeved the conduit and proved the socket in the wet.

**Fifteen pounds**, he said. **Conduit into a box with nothing round it is a way of losing half of the pipe.**

A cooker in that kitchen had been put in with a hob that had two of its elements wired into one terminal.

He separated them and proved the cooker coming up on one, off, and up on the other.

**Twenty-six pounds**, he said. **Two elements on one terminal is a cooker that warms at half what it says on the knob.**

A light in that second unit had been put in on a fitting with the flex coming out of it through a hole cut with a knife.

He cut the hole properly, sleeved the flex and proved the light with the door shut.

**Seventeen pounds**, he said. **A knife hole in a fitting is a hole that the flex finds every time it moves.**

**Eighty-one pounds is the sum of those four on that Thursday, exact.**

*Conditions of the close.* **The book in the green binding lay shut on that table all through that Thursday and the lid on that tin was down beside it, and this page prints no figure for either one of them and no difference between them.

*What the day did not settle, and the rest of it.* **Ten objects are named at the foot of this page and each one of them stands alone. Two nicks in that rail. Nobody thanked anybody on that Thursday and nobody forgave anybody. A man of about fifty-one has been refused three times in four days by two people who do not agree with each other, has an answer he cannot use, and eight people in a shed he has not asked yet, and nobody in this city has told him that the eight will not answer.**
