# Chapter 1088 — The Thing That Cannot Go On A Page

**A Tuesday was a bench until about two and a counter until six and then four converted units off that service road, and that Tuesday evening was spent in a shed on a road in a fourth district by a man who had begun to understand that the hardest thing he wanted to put down was the thing he was never going to be given.**

---

Eleven callers came into that shop on that Tuesday and eleven names went against that Tuesday's book as each of them came in, the last of them gone by about seven, and the shutter came down at ten that night.

**Those four converted units off that service road stood at two thousand and ten days, two hundred and eighty-seven weeks and one day. What is under that long bench in a carrier bag is a hundred and eighty-three days old and has been in that room longer than anything else in it, the printing that has never once been on a wall in this city is a hundred and sixty-nine days old, and the four words said at a desk in a first district are a hundred and sixty days old.**

---

**He was in that room off that line in Saltmarket until about two, and about four people were in it, and the woman of about fifty-four asked him about that open window again and he told her that he had found nothing loose on the frame.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **You have been up to it.**

Marek said: **I have been up to the frame and not into the room.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **That is not what I asked.**

Marek said: **It is what I did, and you are welcome to the difference.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **I have not said I wanted you to go into that room.**

---

**And then at about half past six he went across the city to that shed, and the man of about fifty-one had two people on that bench before Marek came in, and he asked the first of them a question that was not about the evening at all, and about four people in that room have said since that the question was about a woman and that nobody in that shed put it to her.**

The man of about fifty-one said: **Did she say anything, on that Wednesday.**

The man of about twenty-seven said: **I do not know and I am not going to sit here pretending I do.**

The man of about fifty-one said: **You were about four feet from her.**

The man of about twenty-seven said: **I was about four feet from a woman saying nothing at all, which is what she did for about an hour and a half, and that is the only thing I saw her do that night.**

---

**And then he asked the second one, and about four people in that shed have said since that she said the answer before he had finished asking and that she said it to the room and not to him.**

The man of about fifty-one said: **And you. She said something.**

The woman of about fifty-five said: **She said she would not be thanked for it, and she said it before anybody had thanked her, and that is what I have.**

The man of about fifty-one said: **That is not the thing I meant.**

The woman of about fifty-five said: **It is the only thing she said out loud that anybody in this room was there for, and I am not going to hand it to a man with a sheet, and I did not agree to carry it anywhere in the first place.**

---

**And then the man said out loud what he had worked out, and about four people in that shed have said since that he said it sitting down, which is the first time he had said anything to that room sitting down.**

The man of about fifty-one said: **Everything else I can get off those nine. What she said I cannot get off anybody, because she said it once and will not say it again and I am not going to be the one who asks her a fifth time.**

Marek said: **And nobody else is going to be that one either.**

The man of about fifty-one said: **You do not know that.**

Marek said: **I know that I have asked four people in one week this month where a thing came from and not one of them could finish the sentence, and that is not me arranging the rest of this city.**

The man of about fifty-one said: **Then there is a sheet with a hole in it.**

Marek said: **There is a sheet with a hole in it, or there is a sheet, and a hole in a thing is not evidence about the hole.**

---

**Nobody thanked anybody in that shed on that Tuesday and nobody forgave anybody, and about four people in that room have said since that a man who has run a kitchen for about nine years worked out loud that the one thing he wanted written down is the one thing nobody will say again, and that about four of them have said since that he did not say it to be answered.**

---

**That day's work at that counter and in those four units was done by about half past five. At about half past six he was across the city at four converted units off that service road, and he was still in the last of them at about eight.**

A lamp in that shop had been put in on a flex that went from a fitting through a wall into another room without a grommet.

He put a grommet in at the wall, sleeved the flex both sides and proved the lamp with the shade on.

**Eighteen pounds**, he said. **A flex going through a wall needs a hole in the wall that is not for the flex.**

A socket in that yard had been put in on a plate that had been sealed round with a bead of something out of a tube.

He cut the bead out, made the entries and proved the socket in the wet with the plate shut.

**Twenty-one pounds**, he said. **A bead round a plate is a lid on water that has already got in.**

A cooker in that kitchen had been put in with the old element still clipped in beside the new one.

He took the old one out, made off the tails and proved the oven coming up and going off.

**Sixteen pounds**, he said, and stopped there, because he has made that remark in four units this month and a fifth one can go without it.

**Twenty-four pounds**, he said. **Two elements in a cooker is one cooker with a fault in it and no way to find out which.**

**Seventy-nine pounds is the sum of those four on that Tuesday, exact.**

*Conditions of the close.* **The book in the green binding lay shut on that table all through that Tuesday and the tin beside it had its lid down at every hour of it, and this page gives no figure for either one of those and no difference between them.
That ninth chair stood through that Tuesday where it stands in this volume and did not move on a leg, and the hand that last turned it by a leg is named nowhere in these pages or in any other.
The room under a building in a first district was dark at ten on that Tuesday and is dark at this hour, and it is shut for the whole of this volume, all sixty days of it.
That register of correct acts that changed nothing said four at both ends of that Tuesday and nobody in this city is entered on it, and a man deciding not to ask a woman a fifth time is not on it.**

*What the day did not settle, and the rest of it.* **Ten objects are named at the foot of this page, each one by itself and with nothing else named with it. Two nicks in that rail. That card, folded once across the middle. A board up on two nails. Nine torn corners off nine separate fronts. That lid on that tin. That shutter. The book in the green binding. The binder on the back shelf. That envelope on that bench with its flap still standing up. A pencil lying in a margin.
Six are named here outside those ten and none of them shares a sentence with any of them: the cage down one side of that counter, a carrier bag under a long bench with a hundred and fifty sheets in it, a plastic sleeve in a woman's own flat in a drawer with nine words and a date in it, a folded paper in a second district's drawer with a name struck out above another written under it, a leaflet taped under the lower of two clean rectangles on a fourth-district wall, and a sheet with nine names on it and no second line under them.
Under a dated rule, over a question this page does not print, anything filed is opened for any person who asks for it.
Nobody thanked anybody on that Tuesday and nobody forgave anybody. A man has established out loud that the thing he most wants written cannot be had from anybody, has stopped himself from asking for it, and is going on Wednesday anyway, and about four people in that room have not been asked anything yet.**
