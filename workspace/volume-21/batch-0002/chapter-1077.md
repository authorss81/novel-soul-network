# Chapter 1077 — The Card Came Off That Counter

**A Friday was a bench until about two and then a counter until six and then four converted units off that service road, and the middle of that Friday was spent standing at the customer side of a key counter in a first district with a bench down one long side of it and about four people on it.**

---

Ten callers came into that shop on that Friday and ten names were written against that Friday's book as each of them came in, the last of them let away at about half past eight, and the shutter came down at ten.

**Those four converted units off that service road stood at one thousand nine hundred and eighty-seven days, two hundred and eighty-three weeks and six days. What is under that long bench in a carrier bag is a hundred and sixty-five days old and holds a hundred and fifty sheets, the printing that has never been on any wall of this city is a hundred and fifty-one days old, and the four words said at a desk in a first district are a hundred and forty-two days old.**

---

**He was in that room off that line in Saltmarket until about two and about four people were in it, and none of them asked him anything, and then he went across the city and stood at the customer side of a key counter for about an hour and the woman of about thirty-one did not look up more than twice.**

The woman of about thirty-one said: **You again.**

Marek said: **I am stood here.**

The woman of about thirty-one said: **You have not had a key cut.**

Marek said: **No.**

The woman of about thirty-one said: **Then you are not stood here for a key.**

Marek said: **No.**

---

**And then about four people on that bench have said since that the woman of about thirty-one had a drawer open under that till and was looking into it, and that there was a card in her hand, and that Marek did not ask what it was for about nine minutes.**

The woman of about thirty-one said: **I have taken the card off.**

Marek said: **Off the counter.**

The woman of about thirty-one said: **Off the counter and into that drawer, and it has been on that counter since before the shop opened.**

Marek said: **What is on it.**

The woman of about thirty-one said: **The hours, and the two things I will not cut, and about four lines of things I will not do on a Saturday. That is all it has ever said.**

---

**And then she said what it had cost and about four people on that bench have said since that she said it without stopping work and that nobody on that bench said one kind thing to anybody about it.**

The woman of about thirty-one said: **About four of them have not come back.**

Marek said: **Since this morning.**

The woman of about thirty-one said: **Since about Wednesday. Somebody on Wednesday told me about the second thing and I have been not quite myself with people since, and about four of them have read it on me.**

Marek said: **Some of those four were coming for keys.**

The woman of about thirty-one said: **I do not know how many and I am not going to stand here and work that out for you.**

Marek said: **No. I was not going to ask you to.**

---

**And then she gave him half of the second thing and about four people on that bench have said since that she gave it to him rather than to the shop and that she then went back to cutting keys.**

The woman of about thirty-one said: **You will want to know what the second one is.**

Marek said: **I have not asked you for it.**

The woman of about thirty-one said: **I know you have not. She has been telling people that it did not do what she wanted of it. That is the half I have got.**

Marek said: **And the other half.**

The woman of about thirty-one said: **I have not got the other half, and neither have about four of us, and between us we are making one thing out of it anyway.**

Marek said: **Do you have the half.**

The woman of about thirty-one said: **No. Keep it.**

---

**And then Marek said the one thing he had, and the woman of about thirty-one told him to leave it where it was, and about four people on that bench have said since that neither of them raised their voice at any point of it.**

Marek said: **Then this is still in about four mouths and not in a room.**

The woman of about thirty-one said: **Then it is, and I am one of them, and I have been since a Friday in the spring.**

Marek said: **You have asked four times for me to take it off you and I cannot.**

The woman of about thirty-one said: **Nobody can. That is what I have been finding out. Go and do your jobs.**

---

**Nobody thanked anybody in that shop on that Friday and nobody forgave anybody, and about four people heard a woman put a card into a drawer under a till and say out loud that about four people had not come back to it, and about four of them have said since that they did not know whether they thought she should have.**

---

**That day's work at that counter and in those four units was done by about half past five, and at about half past six he went across to four converted units off that service road where one of them was carrying heat and where nothing has been put up on a wall.**

A rose in that shop had been put in with the neutral and the line both landed on the brass and the earth left off altogether.

He put all three on their own terminals and proved the lamp and the tester on it.

**Nineteen pounds**, he said. **And I have said that to four people this month and this is the fourth one.**

A socket in that yard had been put in on an outdoor plate with a knock-out still in the bottom of it.

He knocked it out, sealed it with a proper plug and proved the socket from the gate.

**Sixteen pounds**, he said. **That hole has had about four years of rain going through it.**

A cooker in that kitchen had been put in on a lead that was longer than it needed to be and had been made up with four turns inside the back of the appliance.

He shortened it, re-made the entry and proved the cooker with the oven on.

**Twenty pounds**, he said, **and that is somebody who could not reach the box and would not admit it.**

A light in that second unit had been put in on a fitting with a lamp in it that had been changed for another of a different kind about four years ago and had begun to cook its own holder.

He took the lamp out, put one in that goes in a fitting and proved the light with the shade on.

**Eighteen pounds**, he said. **Somebody has been in that flat for years saying that fitting was wrong and it was the lamp.**

**Seventy-three pounds is the sum of those four on that Friday, exact.**

---

**Half past six on that Friday shut the fourth of those four rooms, and a woman of about thirty was behind that door, and the binder at the back of that shelf was not opened on that Friday or on any other day of this volume, and nothing about what is written in it is set down in this file.**
