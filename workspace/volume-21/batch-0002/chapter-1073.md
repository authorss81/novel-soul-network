# Chapter 1073 — The Bench Outside That Clinic

**A Thursday was a bench until about two and a counter until six and then four converted units off that service road, one of them carrying heat until about nine, and that Thursday was bright and hard and about four of the people who came into that shop all day had been somewhere else first.**

---

Ten callers came into that shop on that Thursday and ten names were written against that Thursday's book as each of them came in, the last of them let away at about half past eight, and the shutter came down at ten that night.

**The four converted units off that service road stood at one thousand nine hundred and eighty-three days, two hundred and eighty-three weeks and two days. A hundred and fifty-seven days of paper are under that long bench in a carrier bag, a hundred and forty-three days of the printing that has never been on a wall is in the same fortnight as it, and a hundred and thirty-four days old is the four words a woman said once at a desk in a first district.**

---

**He was at that bench until about two and nobody asked him anything at all, and about four people were in that room, and one of them was the man of about twenty-seven who had stood at the front of that shed and had been in that room twice since.**

The man of about twenty-seven said: **I have not asked anybody anything in here.**

Marek said: **I know that. You have not.**

The man of about twenty-seven said: **I wanted you to know I had not.**

Marek said: **Then it is known.**

The man of about twenty-seven said: **You keep saying that.**

Marek said: **I keep meaning it.**

---

**And then a woman of about forty-four came into that shop at about four with a shopping bag and stood at that counter for about nine minutes before she said anything, and about four people in that shop have said since that she had been outside a clinic in a fourth district at about eleven that morning and had not stopped.**

The woman of about forty-four said: **You are the one that room belongs to.**

Marek said: **I have been in it and I do not have it.**

The woman of about forty-four said: **That is what everybody says who has been in it.**

Marek said: **It is also what the man who owns it says, and he is about nine years older than me.**

The woman of about forty-four said: **I am not here about him.**

---

**And then she said what had happened outside that clinic, and about four people in that shop have said since that she said it out loud to him and not to the rest of that shop, and that her hands did not stop moving on that bag while she said it.**

The woman of about forty-four said: **I was on a bench outside that clinic at about eleven and there was nowhere dry to sit and a woman of about thirty-nine came out and stood at the end of it.**

Marek said: **And you asked her.**

The woman of about forty-four said: **I had been told she said something about a room. I wanted it in her own words, and I am not ashamed of asking.**

Marek said: **You are not. What did she say to you.**

The woman of about forty-four said: **She said I had been told, and that was where I was starting from. And then she said I would not be having it in anybody else's words.**

---

**And then the woman of about forty-four stopped for about nine seconds and started again, and about four people in that shop have said since that she would not say the rest of it in that building.**

The woman of about forty-four said: **And then she said something else.**

Marek said: **Something else about the room.**

The woman of about forty-four said: **Something else that was not about the room, and I am not going to repeat it in here.**

Marek said: **You do not have to.**

The woman of about forty-four said: **There were about four of us on that bench and I do not think one of us is going to forget it.**

Marek said: **Then you have got about four people who have heard it and not one of them has it whole.**

The woman of about forty-four said: **That is what I have got and I did not order it.**

---

**And then at about half past four Marek asked her the only question he had that was not about the room, and she gave him the answer without being asked twice, and about four people in that shop have said since that he was in no mood for the rest of that afternoon.**

Marek said: **Does she know you have come here.**

The woman of about forty-four said: **She knows by now. She was watching me walk towards this road.**

Marek said: **Did she say anything about that.**

The woman of about forty-four said: **She said that everybody does eventually, and that I should not expect to be the last one.**

Marek said: **Did she say anything else.**

The woman of about forty-four said: **No. And I did not ask her a second time, which I think she noticed, because she looked at me doing it.**

---

**Nobody thanked anybody in that shop on that Thursday and nobody forgave anybody, and about four people heard a third person describe a refusal that had happened on a bench outside a clinic in about nine words, and did not hear what came after it.**

---

**That day's work at that counter and in those four units was finished by about half past five, and at about half past six he went across to four converted units off that service road where the heat is in one of them and where there is still nothing on the walls of any of them.**

A lamp in that shop had been put in on a rose with two cables tied together inside the box and then a third one left hanging out of it.

He cut all three back to their own terminals, binned the knot and proved the lamp from the middle of that floor.

**Sixteen pounds**, he said. **Somebody tied those off to save about four minutes. That knot is the reason two people in this city own one lamp between them.**

A socket in that yard had been put in on an outdoor plate with the lid screwed down on a gasket that had gone hard and split.

He took the plate off, replaced the gasket and proved the socket from the gate in the wet.

**Twenty-two pounds**, he said. **A hard gasket is worse than no gasket, because no gasket at least looks like a decision somebody made.**

A cooker in that kitchen had been put in with the hob and the oven on one cable and a junction box under the worktop that had been filled with cement.

He opened the box, cut the cement out of it and proved the hob with four pans across it.

**Fifteen pounds**, he said. **Somebody filled a box with cement, and I have not worked out yet whether that was to hold it up or to hold it shut.**

A light in that second unit had been put in on a switch with the cable coming out of the top of it and dropping down the wall to within about four inches of the floor.

He took it back up and into the box and proved the light with the door shut and the key turned.

**Twenty-one pounds**, he said, **and that is the one I would not have wanted anybody to find out about instead of me.**

**Seventy-four pounds is the sum of those four on that Thursday, exact.**

---

**Half past six on that Thursday shut the fourth of those four rooms, and a woman of about thirty was behind that door, and the binder at the back of that shelf was not opened on that Thursday or on any other, and this file sets down no count of anything written on what it is holding.**

---

*1076.
Thursday of week 351, at ten. That is the three hundred and eighteenth day of this stretch of days. Ten callers came into that shop on that Thursday and ten names were written against that day's line, the last of them let away by about half past eight.
**A woman of about forty-four said at a counter that she had been asked on a bench outside a clinic in a fourth district at about eleven for one sentence in her own words and had been refused in eight words, and that after the refusal about four people on that bench heard something further that was not about the room, and that she would not repeat it in that shop. The day's four jobs came to seventy-four pounds, exact.**

*Conditions of the close.* **At ten that Thursday the book in the green binding was shut on the table where it had been left the day before and the tin beside it had its lid down, and no number for either of those two things and no comparison of them appears anywhere in this file.
Nothing turned that ninth chair between two o'clock and ten that Thursday, and nobody in that room reached for it. The name of the hand that last turned it is not on any page of this volume and will not be put on one.
That room beneath a building in a first district was unlit at about ten on that Thursday and is unlit at this hour, and it does not open again in this volume.
That register of things done correctly that changed nothing read four at both ends of that Thursday, nobody in this city is entered on it, and a woman not repeating a thing on a bench is not entered on it.**

*What the day did not settle, and the rest of it.* **Ten objects are named here and every one of them stands by itself. Two nicks out of that rail. The card with three questions on it and its one crease and no ruling. That board on two nails with nothing written on any of the nineteen lines. Nine torn corners off nine different fronts. That lid. That shutter and the bar across it. The green-bound book. The binder with the soft foot. An envelope on a bench somewhere with its flap up and nothing behind it. A pencil lying in a margin with the point gone.
Six more stand outside those ten, named one after another and not mixed with any of them: the cage down one long side of that counter, a carrier bag under a long bench with a hundred and fifty sheets in it, a plastic sleeve in a drawer in a woman's own flat with nine words and a date written on it, a folded paper in a second district's drawer with one name struck through and another written under it, a leaflet taped below the lower of two clean rectangles on a fourth-district wall, and a page with nine names on it and nothing under the names.
Behind a dated rule, over a question that is not printed on this page, whatever has been filed may be read by any person who asks for it.
Nobody thanked anybody on that Thursday and nobody forgave anybody. A woman of about thirty-nine was refused the third time, somewhere outside a clinic, in front of about four people, and a different thing went away from that bench than the one that had come to it, and about four people are carrying pieces of it.**
