# Chapter 1078 — Somebody Else Cut Their Keys

**A Monday was a bench until about two and a counter until six and then four converted units off that service road, and that Monday was the one on which about four people who should have been at a counter in a first district were somewhere else, and nobody could put a name to any of them.**

---

Eight callers came into that shop on that Monday and eight names were written against that Monday's line as each of them came in, the last of them let away at about half past eight, and the shutter came down at ten.

**Those four converted units off that service road stood at one thousand nine hundred and eighty-eight days, two hundred and eighty-four weeks to the day. There is a hundred and sixty-eight days of paper in that carrier bag under that long bench, a hundred and fifty-four days of the printing that has never been on a wall is in the same stretch of days, and a hundred and forty-five days is how long a woman at a desk in a first district has been carrying a sentence she did not write.**

---

**He was at that bench until about two and about four people were in that room and none of them asked him anything, and he thought about the thing he wanted and did not ask, and about four people in that room have said since that he was unusually quiet and that the woman of about fifty-four let him be.**

He had a thing he needed that her office could have had in about nine days, and he had not asked her for it since a Thursday in an earlier week, and he had not told her that he was not going to, and on that Monday neither of those had changed and he did not say either of them out loud.

The woman of about fifty-four said: **You have gone quiet in a new way.**

Marek said: **I have.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **Is that about the office.**

Marek said: **It is not about the office.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **Then leave it there.**

---

**And then a woman of about twenty-six came into that shop at about half past three and asked him to cut a key, and about four people in that shop have said since that she had a key cut at a shop on a different road that week and had come here because of it.**

The woman of about twenty-six said: **Can you cut a key.**

Marek said: **I cannot. I am not that shop.**

The woman of about twenty-six said: **The other one was shut on Saturday and there was nothing on the counter to tell me that, so I walked about for about an hour.**

Marek said: **There was a card on that counter that gave the hours.**

The woman of about twenty-six said: **Was there. There was not one on it when I got there and I have been back twice.**

Marek said: **Then somebody has taken it off, and that is not me and I have not heard of it.**

---

**And then the man of about twenty-seven came in at about five and said the thing out loud that about four people in that shop have said since was the only thing anybody said that day Marek did not have a way of answering.**

The man of about twenty-seven said: **I have stopped saying it.**

Marek said: **I did not ask you to stop saying it.**

The man of about twenty-seven said: **You asked about nine people at a counter.**

Marek said: **I asked about nine people and about six of them said no and one of them agreed and then went out and told his own counter, and that was a fortnight ago and I have not asked anybody since.**

The man of about twenty-seven said: **I stopped on a Tuesday.**

Marek said: **Why a Tuesday.**

The man of about twenty-seven said: **Because my sister asked me what it was like in there and I found out I could not tell her without saying it, and I have been the one saying it since about a fortnight.**

---

**And then Marek said the thing he had decided in a launderette and about four people in that shop have said since that he said it to one customer and not to the room.**

Marek said: **Then you are the second person that has stopped for that reason and not because I asked.**

The man of about twenty-seven said: **Who is the first.**

Marek said: **A woman of about thirty-four, and she has not stopped anything at all, and I would not put her with you.**

The man of about twenty-seven said: **Is she one of the four.**

Marek said: **No. And that is the whole of what I have and I am not going to say more than that.**

---

**Nobody thanked anybody in that shop on that Monday and nobody forgave anybody, and about four people heard a man say out loud that a sentence he had been carrying since an earlier week was gone, and he did not say who it was.**

---

**That day's work at that counter and in those four units was finished by about half past five, and at about half past six he went across to four converted units off that service road, one of them carrying heat, and nothing was put on any wall in any of them.**

A lamp in that shop had been put in on a rose with a loop of flex behind the backplate that was doing nothing at all and had been there about four years.

He took the loop out and shortened the cable at the terminal and proved the lamp from the floor.

**Twenty-two pounds**, he said. **And I have taken that same loop out of four of these this month, which is about four people who all thought the lamp was the problem.**

A socket in that yard had been put in on a plate with two of the screws in and the other two missing.

He found two in a drawer in that shop, finished it and proved the socket from the gate.

**Eighteen pounds**, he said. **A plate with two screws in it is a plate that is coming off, and it will be coming off in the wet.**

A cooker in that kitchen had been put in with the oven light wired to the same terminal as the fan and no switch on the light anywhere.

He took the light onto its own terminal, put a switch in for it and proved the oven with the door shut and the light on.

**Twenty-three pounds**, he said, **and that cooker has had a light somebody could not reach since it went in.**

A light in that second unit had been put in on a fitting with the earth sleeved onto the neutral inside the box.

He moved the earth onto its own terminal and proved the fitting with an instrument on the neutral.

**Seventeen pounds**, he said. **An earth and a neutral joined together is two faults for the price of one and neither of them warns anybody.**

**Eighty pounds is the sum of those four on that Monday, exact.**

---

**Half past six on that Monday shut the fourth of those four rooms, and a woman of about thirty was behind that door, and the binder at the back of that shelf was not opened on that Monday or on any other day of this volume, and this file puts no figure on the sheet inside it.**
