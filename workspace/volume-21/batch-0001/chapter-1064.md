# Chapter 1064 — He Did Not Ask Her

**A Thursday was a bench until two and a counter until six and then four converted units off that service road, and the middle of that Thursday was spent crossing a city on a bus and standing on a stair in a building that was not his, and the rain had not stopped.**

---

Seven callers came into that shop on that Thursday and seven names were written against that Thursday's book as each of them came in, the last of them let away at about half past eight, and the shutter came down at ten.

**Those four converted units off that service road stood at one thousand nine hundred and seventy days, two hundred and eighty-one weeks and three days. What is under that long bench in a carrier bag is one hundred and forty-three days old, the printing that has never been on that wall is one hundred and twenty-nine days old, and the four words said at a desk in a first district were one hundred and twenty days old.**

---

**He was at that bench until about two and about four people were in that room and one of them was a man who had come on a rumour in an earlier month and was asking nobody anything, and then he went across the city to a building in a second district and up a stair and did not say that he was going.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **you have not said where you are going.**

Marek said: **I have not.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **that is twice in a fortnight.**

Marek said: **it is.**

The woman of about fifty-four said: **go on.**

---

**And then she was coming down out of an office on a landing in a second district with a folder under her arm, and about four people in that building have said since that she came out of that office at about four o'clock on a Thursday because she had been told he was downstairs, and that nobody had told her.**

Talia said: **you did not tell me where you were going.**

Marek said: **no.**

Talia said: **then how did they know you were downstairs.**

Marek said: **a woman in that building said she had seen me go in.**

Talia said: **and did she come and tell me.**

Marek said: **she did not.**

Talia said: **so how do I know.**

Marek said: **you do not. I am telling you.**

---

**And then they went down about nine steps of that stair together and stopped on the half landing, and about four people in that building have said since that neither of them sat down and that the folder went from under one arm to under the other and back again.**

Talia said: **what is it.**

Marek said: **a woman at a counter in a first district is telling people what happens in that shed, and she was not in the room when the words were said, and about four of them have it now.**

Talia said: **is what she says wrong.**

Marek said: **I have not heard the whole of it and I am not going to stand in a stair and check a stranger's sentence against a room.**

Talia said: **you cannot correct it, then.**

Marek said: **To put it straight I would have to describe that room out loud, and then it would be a description with my name under it, and mine would travel the same as hers and about as fast.**

Talia said: **mine would not travel.**

Marek said: **it would travel by mouth in about four days and nobody would know whose it was, and that is the part I have been turning over all week.**

---

**And then she said the one sentence about it, and about four people in that building have said since that she said it flatly and that it was the only thing she said about that stair all the way down, and that neither of them looked at the other for it.**

Talia said: **You have wanted a correction printed since the spring and you have not asked me for it once this week, and my office would do it in about nine days, and I am not going to be the one who offers it twice.**

Marek said: **you said once in an earlier month.**

Talia said: **I said once in an earlier month and I am not going to say it again, and I want it noticed that I noticed.**

Marek said: **it is noticed.**

Talia said: **I know that. I have worked out what you are doing and I have not worked out whether I am letting you, and neither of us is going to settle that on a stair.**

---

**Nobody thanked anybody in that shop on that Thursday and nobody forgave anybody, and neither of the two people on that stair said one word about the other on the way out of that building, and about four people in it have said since that they went out about four minutes apart.**

---

**The day's work at that counter and in those four units had been finished by about half past five, and at about half past six he went across to four converted units off that service road and the bag was under the long bench where it has been and nothing was put on the wall of any of them.**

A lamp in that shop had been put in on a rose with a foot switch wired into it as well as into the ceiling rose.

He took the foot switch off, put a pull at the door and proved the lamp from the middle of that floor.

"**Eighteen pounds,**" he said. "**Two switches for one lamp is a lamp that somebody will argue about with themselves. Switches in that shop are like that, and one of them has been a landing about four of them have had to stand in the dark working out which switch they were already holding.**"

A socket in that yard had been put in with a faceplate screwed to a batten that had been glued to brick.

He took the batten off, cut two boxes into the wall and proved the socket with the plate off.

"**Nineteen pounds,**" he said. "**A plate on a batten is a plate that comes off in a hard frost and takes a bit of brick with it. Plates in that yard are like that, and one of them has been a gate about four of them have had to put the socket somewhere else until the wall was dry.**"

A cooker in that kitchen had been put in with a flex that went through a hole cut in the plaster and not in the plasterboard behind it.

He took the flex out, cut a proper hole, sleeved it and proved the cooker with the oven door shut.

"**Eighteen pounds,**" he said. "**A flex through plaster is a flex that somebody will one day pull and take half a wall with it. Flexes in that kitchen are like that, and one of them has been a cooker about four of them have had a new kitchen ceiling because of a kettle.**"

A light in that second unit had been put in on a fitting with a lamp holder that was the wrong kind entirely for the shade it had been given.

He took the holder out, put in one that went with the shade, wired it and proved the fitting with the shade on.

"**Nineteen pounds,**" he said. "**A holder that does not go with its shade is somebody who bought what was on the shelf. Holders in that unit are like that, and one of them has been a landing about four of them have had to change a whole shade because the lamp it was hiding was the wrong one.**"

**Seventy-four pounds is the sum of those four on that Thursday, exact.**

---

**Half past six on that Thursday shut the fourth of those four rooms, and a woman of about thirty stood behind that door, and the binder at the back of that shelf was not opened on that Thursday or on any other, and this file contains no count of what is written inside that binder.**
