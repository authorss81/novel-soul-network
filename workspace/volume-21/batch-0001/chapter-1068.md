# Chapter 1068 — The Hands He Cannot Use

**A Tuesday was a bench until two and a counter until six and then four converted units off that service road, one of them carrying heat until about nine, and the second half of that Tuesday was the half that has gone wrong four times in eleven years and is not a mystery to anybody who works with him.**

---

Six callers came into that shop on that Tuesday and six names were written against that Tuesday's book as each of them came in, and the shutter came down at ten.

**Those four converted units off that service road stood at one thousand nine hundred and seventy-five days, two hundred and eighty-two weeks and one day. What is under that long bench in a carrier bag is one hundred and forty-eight days old, the printing that has never been on that wall is one hundred and thirty-four days old, and the four words said at a desk in a first district were one hundred and twenty-five days old.**

---

**He was at that bench until about two and about four people were in that room and one of them said out loud that a woman at a counter in a first district had said something about that room and that he had not heard it from anybody in that building.**

The man of about thirty-four said: **Somebody at a counter told me about that room last week. You have not passed it on to me and I have noticed.**

Marek said: **No.**

The man of about thirty-four said: **You have not even asked me.**

Marek said: **I asked you last Monday and you said no and nobody could have made you say more and I did not take it.**

---

**And then it went wrong at about half past three, at a cooker in a kitchen, and about four people in that shop have said since that he put a screwdriver down on that counter and did not pick it up for about two hours and that one woman asked him about it and he told her.**

A woman of about forty-four said: **Is there anything I can do for you.**

Marek said: **My hands have gone for about two hours and I cannot do fine work.**

The woman of about forty-four said: **You have been doing that for about four years.**

Marek said: **Four times in eleven years.**

The woman of about forty-four said: **Then shut the shop.**

Marek said: **If I shut it I open it Thursday and I lose about four callers a week, and I have not got a Thursday to lose.**

---

**Nobody thanked anybody in that shop on that Tuesday and nobody forgave anybody, and about four people watched a man price two jobs at about a fifth of what they were worth and say out loud twice that he was not charging the whole of it.**

---

**The day's work at that counter and in those four units was not finished until about seven, and at about half past six he went across to four converted units off that service road and worked there for about four minutes on nothing at all and then went home.**

A socket in that kitchen had been put in with the terminals left about three millimetres proud of the face of a box that was set back into the brickwork.

He took the box out, cut a chase and made one that came out level with the plaster, and he proved the socket and priced it.

"**Twenty-two pounds,**" he said. "**A box set back in the brick is somebody who has cut a hole the same size as the box and stopped. Boxes in that kitchen are like that, and one of them has been a cooker about four of them have had to hold a plug in at an angle because the socket was in the wall behind the worktop.**"

A light in that yard had been put in on a fitting with its earth taken off its own metal and left on a screw in the mounting plate.

He put the earth on the metal and left the screw alone, and wired it, and proved it in the wet.

"**Twenty-one pounds,**" he said. "**An earth on a mounting screw is a fitting that passes a test on a dry day and not on a wet one. Fittings in that yard are like that, and one of them has been a gate about four of them have had to bring the light in before the summer to find out whether it still worked.**"

A cooker in that kitchen had been put in with a hob rail and a terminal block fitted behind a cupboard that gets loaded with saucepans.

He loosened the panel, brought the rail out where it can be reached, closed it up and proved the hob from the front.

"**Eight pounds,**" he said. "**I am not charging you the whole of that one. It is done and it will hold, and I have had a bad couple of hours and you should not be paying a full price for it.**"

A lamp in that second unit had been put in on a rose with a lamp inside it that had been the wrong one for about four years and had been getting hot.

He took it out, put in one that suited it, and proved the fitting with his elbow.

"**Five pounds,**" he said. "**I am not charging you the whole of that either. That lamp should have come out about four years ago and it is out now.**"

**Fifty-six pounds is the sum of those four on that Tuesday, exact, and two of those four were priced at about a fifth of what they were worth.**

---

**Half past six on that Tuesday shut the fourth of those four rooms, and a woman of about thirty stood behind that door, and the binder at the back of that shelf was not opened on that Tuesday or on any other, and this file gives the page that binder is holding no figure whatever.**
