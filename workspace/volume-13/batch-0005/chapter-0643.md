# Chapter 643 — A Thursday, A Day And A Night Of Bus Travel From Where He Learned It, A Supply Room On The Far Side Of A Town This Entry Does Not Describe, A Question Put In About Nine Seconds By A Man With Nothing To Give, Four Noes Of About Nine Seconds Each From A Woman With A Hand Truck, One Yes That Is Not A Speech, And A Condition That Nothing Goes Through A Person

**A Thursday was a day and a night of bus travel and a supply room on the far side of a town this entry does not describe. This one is a question asked in nine seconds by a man with nothing in his hands, four refusals of nine seconds each, a yes that is not a speech, and one condition with a person at the bottom of it.**

---

He got off it at half past one in the afternoon and the town came in on the road in pieces over about two hours, the same as it did nine days ago, and he walked the last of it because there is nothing in that town to get a wheelbarrow out of. **It takes a day and a night to get there, the whole round trip is four days, it runs on a Wednesday and on a Saturday, and the next one out of this city is the day after tomorrow.**

Four rooms off a service road in a city he was not standing in are one thousand one hundred and fifty-eight days, a hundred and sixty-five weeks and three days, four of them and one behind, warm. **The card standing on end in the rail in that city is one thousand one hundred and sixty-two days, one hundred and sixty-six weeks to the day, four days past them, and the rail takes two.**

The hardboard's twelfth line, in a shop in that city, is one thousand and seventy-eight days, a hundred and fifty-four weeks to the day, and its thirteenth is one thousand and twenty-nine, a hundred and forty-seven weeks to the day, and there is a reason for that difference and no one has ever looked for it.

The separation is five hundred and thirty-eight days, seventy-six weeks and six days, shut in a form about nine weeks ago and walked every day since and not one day of it shorter.

---

**The supply room had four people in it and three of them were there when he came and the fourth came in four minutes after.**

The woman of about thirty-one with a hand truck. A man of about fifty-one at a desk who has been at that school about nine years. A woman of about nineteen with a box of staples. And he.

She was moving three boxes and she did not stop and she said, **"You're on the Saturday bus this time round and there's two days in it, and if you've come to say you're sorry, don't, because you'd be doing it at a distance and a distance is the only thing you've got.**"

"I'm not here to say I'm sorry."

"**Then what.**"

---

**And he put it in nine seconds, and it was four sentences, and he had not rehearsed any of them, and there is no version of this in the plan of record and none in any file in this repository.**

"The thing that was running has had no name on it since Monday morning. There's a piece of paper in a tool roll in Lattice Ward with a number and a date on it and a woman's name at the bottom, and that piece of paper is about me and it is not about a list, and the man who used to be on that thing is on it. Somebody has to answer for what happens next and it is going to be a person and not a room, and the only other name in this arrangement that anybody in this city has ever taken something away from is yours, and I have no right to put it there and I am putting it there."

She put the truck down. It took her four seconds to put it down, and three of them were the truck.

---

**And she said no, and she said it four times, and each of the four took nine seconds, and the four of them are not one sentence said four ways.**

"**No. I run a clinic with a door that has nothing on it and a supply room on the wrong side of a town, and I am not a person who answers for arrangements between rooms. I have nine people who hand me things because I am here and I have never once been asked to decide anything and I have not missed it.**"

He said nothing.

"**No, and that one was too easy, so here's the other one. You are not the person to ask. There is a piece of paper in your pocket that says in nine words that you are not the person anything comes to, and you took it in nine seconds without arguing, and now you have come a day and a night to put a name on something else, and I have been in a room with you twice and both times a man with a better reason than me said a thing was the arrangement.**"

He said nothing.

"**No. I am owed a month of somebody else's decisions. If I take this then the two of them sit in the same place and in a month somebody will come here and say to me: you had it, you had the plan, and you had the month, and that's the two things, so that's settled. And it isn't settled. It will never be settled and a person who says it is has been told the month was worth having, and it wasn't, and I know that because I have been owed it since the spring and I have not spent a day of it.**"

Still nothing.

"**And no, and this is the one that is actually the reason. I have been a room for four years. There is a clinic in this town that people walk to because there is a light on in it, and a supply room that a school walks to because there is a woman in it, and you want to make me the place a thing goes to, and I have spent four years being a place things go to, and I have watched what it does to the person it happens to, and last month it was done to me, and I am not doing it to somebody else with a name on a piece of paper.**"

---

**And she said yes in nine seconds, and it was not a speech, and she said it to the boxes on the truck and not to him.**

"**Right. I've got a thing to say and it's about four days old and I've not said it to anybody, so it's the last time I'll be saying it. What I want is that nothing goes through a person. Not you, not a van, not a man with a clipboard. Nine rooms, nine pieces of paper, and a person carries them. That's the whole of it. You can have that on Monday or you can have it never.**"

"That's yours to set."

"**It's mine to set because I'm the one it happened to,**" she said. **"That's not authority. That's a bill.**"

---

**Nobody in that room agreed with her, and one of the three other people in it said a thing and two of them did not.**

The man of about fifty-one at the desk said, in the voice of a man confirming the price of milk, **"That's a lot to put on somebody who's already got it."**

"**It's less than you've got,**" she said. "**You've got a room and a job and about nine years and you put a stop on a van for a fortnight because it was Tuesday. I want it written down that you said that and that I heard you, and then I want you to go back to your desk.**"

He went back to his desk. **The woman of about nineteen with a box of staples did not look up at any point, and she had not looked up nine days ago either, and there is a question about that woman which is a question about a supply room and not about anything else in this entry.**

**And what he said, in nine seconds, and it took him the whole of the time to get it out, was that she was owed a month and about fourteen weeks besides and that she was now owed the rest of it as well, because a person who answers for a thing they had a thing done to is doing the same work twice.**

"**I know that,**" said the woman of about thirty-one. "**I worked it out about four days ago. Nobody is going to give it back. I don't want it back. I wanted it said out loud in a room to a person who has to hear it, and you have now heard it, and you are the fourth, and the fourth is the one that does it, because the other three were not going to do anything about it either.**"

Nobody in that room said a word. **He had a pen in the fourth pocket of a tool roll a day and a night behind him and he did not get it out, and that is the second time in about two weeks and the first time had been a condition and this one did not.**

---

**What he wanted, for four minutes in that supply room, was to be told to go away, and he would have gone.**

That is the want and it did not get said. He has wanted a great many things in about four months and most of them were about a room, and this one was about a door, and he had it ready the whole time, and when the fourth no came he was about to use it and he did not, and the reason he did not is that a man who leaves a room the moment he is told to has not been in it.

**And the small thing he gave up on the Thursday was about nine words long and it died in that room. She had said a sentence to a hand truck nine days ago a month of somebody else's decisions, and he had it, and he could have said it back to her, and saying it back would have been the only available way of saying he agreed with her, and he did not say it, and the sentence is not on anything, and it belongs to a town and not to a page.**

---

**Behind the door of that supply room there are about nine pegs and eight of them have a coat on them that is worn through at the elbow.**

The ninth peg is empty. **The coat that was on it nine days ago, new, in a bag, with the ticket still on the sleeve, is not on it, and there is a person in that school this week who was not there last week, and nobody in that building will say who, and Marek asked no such question of anybody in that town and asked none here.**

Four people in this city can each say one true thing about that ninth peg and not one of the four was ever asked.

---

In the city he was not in, that stair was taken at half past six by one other person, thirty-odd, who sat down by the door and was still sitting there at about ten. Not one word reached her out of that building in three and a half hours and none ever will. The page carrying her name at the head of it is the eighth of eight and lies in a ring binder on the back shelf of that room, and it did not come out of it on any day of this fortnight or of the one before.

The rail in that city takes two. The book with the green cover is on sixty-two lines and the tin is on seventy-three with the lid on, and **no sitting fell on this Thursday in either city and this entry does not say what a sitting does when there is none of it.**

Six objects are named on this page and not one of them is set beside another: a hand copy of the front of a page, a card on end in a rail of two, a form with two boxes in it, a job docket with nine places along its back, a plate of iron with four slots cut into it, and a sheet that came back to a room.

---

**There are nine trays out on a table in that supply room and eight of them have a label in a hand that is not hers, and the ninth has a label in her own hand with a date on it that is four days old.**

The eight labels are about sizes and about what goes in them and about four of them are in a hand that has been on this table since before the man of twenty-two existed. The ninth says *ORDERED* and then a date, and the date is four days old, and the date is the day a man of twenty-two got on a bus in this city at about six in the evening with a question in him and a copy of a form in a pocket.

**Nobody in that room mentioned it, and she did not, and there is a form in this city on which a person could have written a date down about nine days ago and did not, and the reason she wrote it on a tray label instead is that a tray label is a thing in her own building.**

He looked at it for four seconds and then looked at the other eight and then at the floor, and the woman of about nineteen with a box of staples went past him twice carrying trays and did not say a word on either pass.

---

*One thousand one hundred and fifty-eight and one thousand and seventy-eight are three days on from a whole number of weeks, and one thousand one hundred and sixty-two is a whole number of weeks exactly, and the card that is on it has not been looked at by anybody in about nine weeks.*
