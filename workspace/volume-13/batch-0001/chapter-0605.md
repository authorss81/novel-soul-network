**"One question,"** she said, at about half past four, and she did not come to him to say it and she did not lower her voice and she did not wait for the room to be quiet. **"The trays — is a return anything once it is in the building but not in a tray?"**

Eleven words and a half.

**"It's a notice,"** he said, in about nine seconds, and it was true, and it was the right answer, and it was not what she wanted, and he knew that while he was saying it and he said it anyway.

**"Right,"** she said. **"Then I need to know which of the four it is in, and the four are a room, and the room is a woman who is not on my schedule, and the schedule is a thing I have to publish."**

**"Then it is not publishable this week."**

**"No."**

**"Say that to the people who want it published."**

**"I am going to say it to about eleven of them between now and the end of the month and I am going to say it eleven times, and about four of them will not believe me and about two of them will ask me who told me, and I am not going to tell them, and they will work out that it was you inside about a minute because you are the only person in this city who has been in that room."**

**"I have not been in that room. I was on a landing."**

**"You were on a landing with her,"** she said, **"and she has told two people about it and one of them is my deputy and my deputy has a diary."**
