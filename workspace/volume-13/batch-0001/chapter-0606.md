# Chapter 606 — A Thursday, A Queue Of Four, A Form With One Line In It And A Number That Is The Ninth Of Its Kind, Nine Seconds In Which The Condition Of A Return Was Said Out Loud In A Face To About Four People, And A Man Of Twenty-Two Who Wrote Down The Number And Not The Sentence

**The Thursday was a bench until two, a counter until about six, and four rooms off that service road from about half past six, and its shape was nine seconds in a queue in which a person told a man of twenty-two what the price of her coming back was, and he wrote the number down and did not write the sentence down.**

---

Eleven dated jobs on the Thursday and eleven people at that counter, the final one at about ten past five. Bench, counter, service road: until two, then from two to six, then from about half past six. **The heat in that flat is on, four names on that one tenancy are carrying it, and a fortnight of a cook's own wages is owed by nobody and is going to be owed by nobody at the end of this month.** Those four rooms are one thousand and eighty-one days, a hundred and fifty-four weeks and three days, four of them and one behind, and they are warm.

The card in that rail is one thousand and eighty-five days, a hundred and fifty-five weeks to the day, four days past the room, and the rail takes two.

**The nineteenth line on the two nails is seven hundred and seventy-seven days, a hundred and eleven weeks to the day, and the hold of the man of about thirty-three in another city is seven hundred and fourteen days, a hundred and two weeks to the day. Neither of them is a day anything happened to the heating in this city, and the two of them are exactly two figures with nothing between them, and this entry has not joined them to anything.**

---

**The woman of about thirty-four who answers that counter told him at about twenty past three with four people in front of him and two behind, and she was not serving anybody while she said it, which she has done twice in about four years and both of the times were about somebody else's business.**

**"There's a form,"** she said. **"One line on it. It's got a number. It's the ninth one of those and there's been eight."**

**"Of what."**

**"Of somebody coming back to a city where they've been living."** She said it as though she were reading a heading. **"It's not a licence and it's not a job. It's a box and a date and a signature and the office keeps them in a drawer, and I saw the drawer, and the drawer's got eight in it and the ninth has her on it."**

**"Right,"** he said.

**"And she said a thing out loud, in a corridor, with me and two others stood there, and I'm telling you because you're going to hear it from about four people by Friday and I'd rather you heard it from me while I'm saying it to your face."**

---

**"Say it."**

**"She said that she is going to be able to take you off a plan, and that when she does it goes on a paper, and that you are not going to be asked whether you agree first."** The woman of about thirty-four said all of that in about nine seconds to about four people, two of whom were strangers at a counter and one of whom was a man of about fifty-one who had come in for a battery and who did not look up.

He did not say anything for about four seconds.

**"What is on the form,"** he said. **"Is there a box for what she says."**

**"There's a box for the date and a box for the number and a line at the bottom, and the line at the bottom is a signature, and the signature is hers."**

**"So the sentence is not on it."**

**"No."**

**"So the sentence is in a corridor with three people in it and I am one of them, and I am the only one of the three who cannot be asked to say what it was."**

**"You're the only one of the three who'd have to be in a room with it,"** she said, **"and that's the difference, and it's the whole of what the form is, and I'd go and read the form before you say anything clever about it."**

---

**He did not ask what the other eight forms were. That is the small thing he gave up on the Thursday, and it is on no form, and the woman of about thirty-four noticed him not asking and said one more thing about it and then stopped.**

**"You didn't ask."**

**"No."**

**"It's four of them going back to two towns and the rest are people who came back to this one,"** she said. **"That's all I know and that's all I've looked at, and I'm not going to look at the rest, and neither should you, because the drawer is hers."**

**"Understood."**

**"You have not understood anything at all,"** she said, and went and served the next one, and nobody thanked her and he did not ask her a question about it, and there is no form in this city on which a person's telling you something at a counter is anything.

**He wrote the number on the back of his own hand with a biro at about half past three while a man of about twenty-four explained what he wanted done to a kettle, and he wrote the number and not the sentence, and he has the number on his hand until about six, and by the end of the month he will not be able to tell anybody what he agreed to, and the number will be on about nine sheets in a drawer in a building that is not his.**

---

**What he wanted, on the Thursday, and did not say, in a face, to about four people: to be the man that condition was written for.** He has spent about twelve volumes being the man who is asked, and the sentence she said in a corridor is the first one anybody has said in about twelve volumes that is about what he is not allowed to do, and it was said to him by a person entitled to say it, and about four people in a shop heard it, and none of them is going to forget it, and he is not going to be able to do a single thing about it.

Four rooms off that service road from about half past six. The man of about fifty-one was against that north wall with the battery he had come in for in his coat. **Nobody said one word to the woman of about thirty who was in the chair by the door, nobody put a question to her, and the page belonging to her — the eighth of eight in a ring binder on a shelf — did not come out of that binder on the Thursday.**

**The count of right acts that altered nothing is still at two, and this Thursday did not move it.** A condition stated out loud in nine seconds in a corridor by a person entitled to state it is not a correct thing that changed nothing, because it changed everything and changed it in about four seconds, and the two things on the register are both a woman of about fifty-two in the first of the four towns doing something she has been doing for about four years without being asked to do anything different.

Nobody on that counter said thank you to anybody, the roller shutter went down at about ten as it does on every working evening, and there is no Sunday inside this day and no Sunday inside this week either.

**What is left to set down is a shape and some figures rather than a scene, and it is set down here one time. The man of twenty-two can do, on this sixth day, exactly what he could do on the first page of the first week, and nothing has been added to him and nothing taken off him, and a number written on the back of a hand is not a power and is not a permission. The card in the rail is one thousand and eighty-five days in a rail that holds two and it did not move. Nineteen lines on two nails and no twentieth. The nine hand copies of the front of a page are six hundred and forty-seven days, ninety-two weeks and three days, eight of the nine unfinished, the first disagreement in the fourth line not found, and no two of the nine compared. The post at the corridor end is six hundred and twenty-nine days, eighty-nine weeks and six days. The separation is four hundred and sixty-one days, sixty-five weeks and six days, and it is not shorter and it is not ended. The drawer under that bench is shut and holds four sheets and was not opened on the Thursday, and it is a different drawer from the one in a building in a second district with about nine names on a pad on its door. The public body's four-word name is in none of this page. There is a plate of iron with four slots under a floor in the first of the four towns and it stayed there on the Thursday and nobody has called it anything.**

*609. *Nine seconds, four people, a corridor, and a box that is not on the form.* **Who said it first, and where: the woman of about twenty-four, in a corridor on that floor at about half past two on the Thursday, to three people. Who said it second, and where: the woman of about thirty-four, at a counter in Lattice Ward at about twenty past three, to about four people, two of them strangers and one of them a man of about fifty-one waiting for a battery. What was said, in about nine seconds: that she is going to be able to take him off a plan, that when she does it goes on a paper, and that he is not going to be asked whether he agrees first. What is on the form: a box for a date, a box for a number, and a signature at the bottom, and the number is the ninth and the drawer beside it holds eight. What is not on the form: the sentence, which is in a corridor with three people in it, of whom he is the only one who cannot be asked to say what it was, because the others can be put in a room with it and he is in it. What was asked: whether there is a box for what she said, and the answer was no. What was not asked: what the other eight forms are, and the woman of about thirty-four, who did look, said that four of them are people going back to two towns and the rest are people who came back to this city, and then said that she was not going to look at the rest and that neither should he, because the drawer is hers. What was written down: the number, on the back of the left hand, in biro, at about half past three, and not the sentence. What was wanted and not said: to be the man that sentence was written for, which is the first thing anybody has said to him in about twelve volumes that is about what he is not going to be allowed to do, and it was said by somebody entitled to say it, in a shop, to four people, and he cannot do one thing about it.**

*What the day did, and what it did not touch.* **A condition was said out loud twice in one afternoon, once in a corridor to three people and once at a counter to about four, and a man of twenty-two heard both and wrote down a number and not the sentence, and did not ask what the other eight forms were.** Nobody thanked anybody. Nobody was taught, shown, assessed or helped. The drawer under the bench is shut with four sheets and was not opened, and the drawer in a first floor in a second district holds nine sheets of which eight are not hers and neither drawer is ever joined to the other on any of these pages. The book holds sixty-one lines and the tin holds seventy-three and neither of them was opened on the Thursday.
