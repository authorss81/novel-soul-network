# Chapter 601 — A Monday, Eleven Jobs Dated, Eleven People Through A Counter In Lattice Ward, And A Sheet Of Paper Off The Back Of A Form That Came Back With Three Words On It In A Hand That Is Not A Postmark, And An Hour Between Three And Four That Was Available And Was Not Taken

**The Monday was a bench until two and a counter until six and four rooms off that service road from about half past six, and its shape was a sheet the size of a docket that came back because the room printed on it has been a cupboard on a landing since the spring, and about eleven people through the counter, and one hour that was available and was not taken.**

---

There were eleven jobs booked for the Monday and each carried a date, and eleven people came over that counter, and the last of them was let in at about ten past five. Bench to two. That counter from two until six. Four rooms off the service road after half past six. **The heating in those rooms is on and set above the winter's setting, and the bill for the difference is being carried by four names on that one tenancy, and a cook laid a fortnight of her own wages into it in the spring and nobody owes that back to her now or at the end of this month.** Those four rooms are one thousand and seventy-one days, one hundred and fifty-three weeks to the day, four of them and one behind, and they are warm.

The card in the rail along the front of that counter is one thousand and seventy-five days, one hundred and fifty-three weeks and four days, four days past the room, and the rail holds two.

**The nineteenth line on the two nails above that bench is seven hundred and sixty-seven days, one hundred and nine weeks and four days, and the hold of a man of about thirty-three in another city is seven hundred and four days, a hundred weeks and four days. Neither of those two figures is a day anything happened to the heating in this flat, and neither of them is a day anything happened to the heating in this city, and this entry attaches no days-off to that heating at any point in it.**

---

**The woman of about thirty-four who answers that counter put it on the counter at about ten past two and did not put it down like a job.**

**"That came in with a bag on Monday morning,"** she said. **"It is the third one this month and the other two are under the window and neither of them is a repair."**

It is a sheet of paper about the size of a docket. There is a man's name on it in ballpoint, and a date, and two boxes ticked and two boxes not ticked, and it has been folded twice and unfolded once and folded again, and on the front of it, in pencil, in a hand that is not his and is not hers and is not anybody who has ever stood in this shop, there are three words.

**NO SUCH ROOM**

He read it twice. The address is a street he could walk to in about eleven minutes. A building. A first floor. **Room nine, and the nine is not the room, it is the number of the room, and there is a difference and it has been the whole of what is going on here before anybody in this shop noticed it.**

**"Who sent it,"** he said.

**"Nobody. It came in a bag."** She had a hand on the till drawer and did not open it. **"The bag comes on a Monday. That is the day it comes."**

---

**He did not walk there that afternoon, and the reason he gave himself took about nine seconds and every part of it was correct.**

A man who goes and looks up an address is a man rooms send their returns to. If he walked up that street at about half past three and stood in the room and asked what the room was for, then in about a month somebody in this city would find out that he knew, and somebody else would ask him which room to put a thing in, and the answer would be *ask him*, and then the returns would come to a bench in Lattice Ward and to a counter in Lattice Ward and to a man of twenty-two who is the person everybody goes to, **which is what everybody has been telling him for about twelve volumes is the shape of him, and it has never once occurred to him that the shape of him is a room.**

He had the hour. The counter was quiet at about three and it was quiet at about half past three and it was quiet until four, and he had a coat on the hook and the street was eleven minutes, and at about ten past four he had a customer.

---

**The small thing he gave up, which is on no form and which nobody noticed: the counter's day sheet has a column headed for goods returned to us, and that column is for a thing that came back to this shop, and this had not come back to this shop. He wrote it instead on a docket, and dated the docket, and put the docket in the sleeve on the wall, and it is the first piece of paper this shop has ever spent on something that is not a repair.**

The three other dockets in that sleeve are about a bench and a cage and a rail. **The fourth is about a room that is not there, and it is the only one of the four that cannot be closed by anybody who comes in and asks what it is for.**

He wanted the address to be wrong in a way that meant somebody had made a mistake he could go and find. It was not wrong. **Every part of it was correct when it was printed, and it is still correct now, and the building is still there, and the first floor is still a first floor, and the room is not there because the room stopped being a room in the spring, and nobody decided that and nobody told the man whose name is on the sheet and nobody has told the woman who carried the bag.**

---

Four rooms off that service road from about half past six. **The man of about fifty-one was against that north wall with the same newspaper folded the same way and he said nothing on the Monday and has still not been handed a written thing by anybody, and nobody has asked him for one.** The woman of about thirty was in the chair nearest the door and was not spoken to and was not asked a question and the page that is hers is the eighth of eight in a ring binder on a shelf and it was not read on the Monday and the binder was not opened.

**A register of correct things that changed nothing, done by people owed nothing, stands at two and is not moved by this Monday.** Both of the two are the same woman of about fifty-two in the first of the four towns: a clipboard on a nail inside a cold-store door, and four torn pieces of one list in a coat pocket at that town's gate. **A sheet of paper written down correctly on a docket is not a third instance, because a docket is not a correct thing done in about nine seconds, it is a piece of paper, and the person who wrote it is owed nothing and is owed a great deal that nobody has offered him.**

Nobody thanked anybody at that counter and the shutter came down at about ten, and no shop in this city opens on a Sunday and no day of this one is a Sunday.

**What follows is a figure and a shape rather than a scene, and it is set down once here. The man of twenty-two can do, on the first page of this one, exactly what he could do on the last page of the last one, and nothing has been added to him and nothing taken off him. The card in the rail is one thousand and seventy-five days in a rail that holds two, and it did not move on the Monday. The board on two nails carries nineteen lines and there has never been a twentieth. The nine hand copies of the front of a page are six hundred and thirty-seven days, ninety-one weeks to the day, eight of the nine unfinished, and the first disagreement in the fourth line is still not found, and no two of the nine were compared on the Monday or on any other day this week. The post at the corridor end is six hundred and nineteen days, eighty-eight weeks and three days. The separation is four hundred and fifty-one days, sixty-four weeks and three days, and it is not shorter and it is not ended, and the two people it counts between have not been in the same room since before the last volume and neither of them has said so to anybody. The drawer under that bench is shut and holds four sheets, one of which has the word NO on it and four lines under it, and it was not opened on the Monday. The nine lines he was asked for five times were not written a sixth time and are on nothing. The four words that name the public body were said once, in a room, in a room in another book, by the person entitled to say them, and are in none of these pages. Beneath a floor in the first of the four towns there is a plate of iron with four slots in it. It did not move on the Monday. It did not come into this city on anybody's van, and nobody has given it a name.**

*604. Monday of week 221, at ten, and this entry is the first day of a volume, laid out as bench, counter, service road, at two, at six and at about half past six, over eleven dated jobs and eleven customers, the last of them at about ten past five, one sheet off the back of a form with a man's name and a date and two boxes ticked, three words in pencil on the front of it, a first floor and a number where a room is not, an hour between three and four that was available and was not taken, one docket spent on a thing that is not a repair, the nineteenth line and a hold in another city, and nobody thanked at any point in it.*

*Conditions and docket.* **The Monday held eleven jobs, a date on each of them, and eleven customers, the last of them at about ten past five.** The day itself: a bench in Lattice Ward until two; that counter from two until six; four rooms off the service road from about half past six. Standing behind that counter, never rearranged: a bench; a cage, a man in it, face to the wall; a roller shutter; a tool roll whose fourth pocket is empty this week; a wall list in a sleeve; a shelf where finished work waits; a rail that holds two; and a day sheet with one column on it that has a heading and no use this month. Callers: eleven, of whom one was not a customer. Refusals: three. Work: eleven, three declines, nothing escalated, nothing handed back. Charge: eleven pounds, exact, nothing out. The heating in that flat: on, above the winter's, four names on one tenancy carrying the difference, a fortnight of a cook's own wages owed by nobody and remaining owed. The rooms: one thousand and seventy-one days, one hundred and fifty-three weeks to the day, four of them and one behind, warm. Rail card: one thousand and seventy-five days, one hundred and fifty-three weeks and four days, four days past the room, rail of two. The board on two nails: twelfth, nine hundred and ninety-one; thirteenth, nine hundred and forty-two; fourteenth, nine hundred and seven; fifteenth, eight hundred and eighty-six; sixteenth, eight hundred and sixty-one; seventeenth, eight hundred and forty-three; **eighteenth, seven hundred and eighty-nine; nineteenth, seven hundred and sixty-seven, one hundred and nine weeks and four days**. Added to that board: nothing at all, and no twentieth line has ever been started. The post at the corridor end: six hundred and nineteen days, eighty-eight weeks and three days. The nine hand copies of the front of a page: six hundred and thirty-seven days, ninety-one weeks to the day, eight of the nine unfinished and the first disagreement in the fourth line still not found, and no two of the nine compared. The hold in another city: seven hundred and four days, a hundred weeks and four days. The man of about fifty-one at the wall: six hundred and seventy-seven days, ninety-six weeks and five days, and nothing has been handed to him in writing. The ask: seven hundred and sixty-one days, one hundred and eight weeks and five days. The separation: four hundred and fifty-one days, sixty-four weeks and three days, not shorter and not ended.

*One sheet, three words, and a bag that comes on a Monday.* **What arrived: a sheet off the back of a form, about the size of a docket, folded twice and unfolded once, with a man's name in ballpoint, a date, two boxes ticked and two boxes not ticked, and three words in pencil on the front reading NO SUCH ROOM. Where it came from: in a bag, on a Monday morning, with about two others that are under a window and are not repairs. What the address named: a street in a second district, a building, a first floor, and a number where a room used to be. How far: about eleven minutes on foot, which is a figure of this entry and not a published distance, and no town in this city or in any of the four towns is described by a distance. What was done with it: written on a docket, dated, and put in a sleeve on a wall, because the day sheet has one column and the column is for goods returned to this shop. What was not done: the hour between three and four was available and was not taken, and the reason took about nine seconds and every part of it was correct, and the reason is printed in the body and is not a rule. The column: the two are the nineteenth line at seven hundred and sixty-seven days, one hundred and nine weeks and four days, and a hold in another city at seven hundred and four days, a hundred weeks and four days, and one of those two figures is an age in days and the other is also an age in days, and there is no day anything in either of them happened to the heating in this city. Who said what, in a face: the woman of about thirty-four who answers that counter, in about nine seconds, twice, both of them about where it came from and neither of them about what it means. Who was thanked: nobody, at the counter, on the bench, or in four rooms off a service road.**

*What the day did, and what it did not touch.* **A sheet of paper came back to a shop because a room went out of use in the spring, and one man wrote it down on a docket and did not walk eleven minutes to look at it, and the whole of what is going on in this whole business is in that not-walking and is not in the walking.** Nobody thanked anybody. Nobody was taught, shown, assessed or helped. The book stands at sixty-one lines and the tin at seventy-three. The register of things done right that altered nothing is closed at two, and the woman of about thirty-four was right about two things on the Monday and owed nothing for either. A sheet off the back of a form is a different object from the card in the rail, and the sleeve on that wall now holds four dockets and three of them are about repairs. The drawer is shut with four sheets in it and was not opened. The four words that name the public body are on no page of the Monday. The number of sheets in the four trays is not given in this entry because nobody has counted them and nobody in this city is entitled to, and the four words that name the public body are on none of the ten days and are not going to be on the other forty.
