## 2. WHAT THIS MOVEMENT IS FOR, FROM THE PLAN OF RECORD

1. **GWEN ASHBY STOPS, AND SHE IS NOT TALKED OUT OF IT, AND HER STOPPING IS AN ORDINARY ACT WITH A DAY ON IT.** What she does on the day she stops is given to her and it is small, and it is not a scene, and nobody in this volume is on her side of it and nobody is against her. **The four trays are emptied and the sheets are not thrown away, and where they go is on the page, and the where is worse for everyone and better for the sheets, and both of those are true on the same day.**
2. **NINE ROOMS ARE WALKED BY A PERSON WITH AN ORDINARY JOB, AND THE NINE OF THEM DO NOT AGREE ABOUT ANYTHING.** A clinic, two schools, a cold store, a water office, a bus depot, a council house, a kitchen and an exchange room. **Nobody in the nine is a villain, and the man who walks them is not a hero, and he walks them because he is the one who has to, and the reason he has to is a sentence in about nine seconds said by a person entitled to say it.**
3. **A RETURN IS FOUND IN A TRAY THAT IS ABOUT FOUR YEARS OLD, IN A HAND NOBODY RECOGNISED, AND THE HAND IS A PERSON'S, AND THE PERSON IS ALIVE, AND SHE HAS BEEN TOLD TWICE THAT IT WAS RECEIVED.** This is the movement's centre and it is not a mystery and it is not a device and it is on the page as a sheet of paper in about nine seconds of a face.
4. **THE FORTY-FIFTH SITTING, AND THE BOOK DOES NOT OPEN, AND THE WHOLE OF WHAT HAPPENED IS THAT A CALLER SAID A THING THAT HAD ALREADY BEEN SAID TO HER FACE BY THE MAN WHO STOOD BEHIND HER.** About eleven people up a stair, nine items at four minutes, one thing that is not an item, the book at sixty-one lines before the hour and sixty-one lines after it, the tin at seventy-three, the count announced at fifty of which forty-five correspond, the ninth chair hard against that wall with its back to the room, unmoved, and its mover not named, and nobody in that room asking her whether she is all right.
5. **THE MOVEMENT CLOSES ON THE FIGURE THAT IS THE VOLUME'S PROBLEM AND IS GIVEN HERE FOR THE FIRST TIME IN A FACE: there are about four hundred and fifty people in this city who can each say a true thing out loud, and about nine of them can be reached by a person standing in a room, and not one of them has anywhere to put an objection.** It is inherited from Volume 12 and it is not resolved here and it gets worse.
