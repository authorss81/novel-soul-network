# Chapter 446 — Four Things In One Afternoon On A Road, And A Man Of Fifty-Eight Who Is Right About The Load

The Wednesday of week one hundred and sixty-three was spent at a bench in Lattice Ward until about two and at a counter until about six and from about half past six in one room off a service road, and the one thing that is true of it is that **between about one and about four on the same afternoon, about four things went wrong on a road on the far side of this city, no name is attached to any of them, about nine doors came off their hinges in four buildings and nobody was hurt and nobody is paid to put them back, four rooms in four towns each refused a cue and did nothing else, a check at a set of scales stopped being a check and about nine lorries stood on a road, and a room that had gone dark on purpose went dark again and about eleven people lost a week, and the man of twenty-two was at that counter the whole of it and was in none of the four rooms and was not on the bus.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last of them about six in the evening. The word was at nine and ten and is told to nobody. **Heating: on, dearer, and four names on a lease cost more than they did, and a fortnight a cook paid for is paid for by nobody and is not resolved.** The room off that service road is six hundred and sixty-seven days old and is warm, and about nine people came in on the Wednesday evening for the temperature and none of them was asked anything.

The card in the coat pocket is six hundred and seventy-one days old, which is four days more than the room is, and it has been in that pocket since the Wednesday it came out of that rail, and it went into no gate on a road on Wednesday and it went into no hut and it went into no other pocket.

---

He was at the bench from about half past seven until about two and at the counter from about two until about six.

A woman of about twenty-eight brought a part in at about ten that needed a fitting and he did it in about half an hour and she did not watch and she did not thank him and she paid and went.

A man of about thirty-four came at about four and asked him a question about nine days of work he had agreed to in a term that ended in the spring, and he said the two of those days are not going to happen and he is not going to say why, and the man of about thirty-four said that was what he had been told and went.

**He had the nine days on a sheet of paper with his own name against all nine of them and a reason against none of them, and the sheet has been in a drawer in a kitchen in the second of the four districts since an earlier week, and nobody has asked for it back and nobody has been shown it.** Seven of the nine are gone. The other two are not going to happen. The sheet does not say so and neither of them said so out loud today.

**He worked the rest of that hour with the lamp on the arm and a radio on the other side of the room that was turned down so far that he could not tell whether it was on.**

**He had agreed to that block of about nine days on a Saturday in that term, and the term has ended, and he said yes to the Saturday, and he is here and the counter is open and about four people came to it in the hour after four and none of them is anybody from a road.**

---

**At about one on that Wednesday, in the first of the four towns on that road, nine doors came off their hinges in four buildings between about one and about four in the afternoon.**

They were ordinary doors. They were the doors people go in and out of. Nine of them, in four buildings, in about three hours, and the hinges came out of the frame and the frames are sound and the doors are not heavy.

**Nobody was hurt. Nobody has been paid to put them back, and nobody has been hired to put them back, and the people whose doors they are cannot get them back on without paying somebody themselves and about four of them have not done it and about four have.**

Nobody knows why. Nobody has said why. **No name is attached to any of it and no name can be, because there is nobody to attach one to: the nine doors went in about three hours in four buildings and nobody in this case saw anything and nobody in this case was asked and nobody in this case can be shown anything.**

A man of about fifty-six on a contractor's books was inside two of those four buildings on Wednesday afternoon, on his own business, and has not said so and has not been asked and nobody has connected him to the nine doors and nobody is going to.

---

**At about two on that Wednesday, a cue arrived at four rooms in four towns, and each of the four did nothing else.**

That is the whole of it. A thing the four rooms had been built to answer arrived, and each of the four sat with it and did not answer, and did not pass it on, and did not write it down, and did not do anything else about it.

**It is not a chain and it is not a technique and it is not the four rooms copying each other, because none of the four knows the other three exist and two of them have never heard of the other two and one of them refused before the thing arrived, four days earlier, without being asked what it was going to be asked to do.**

A refusal that does nothing else is a thing an ordinary person can do with nothing at all. There is no card for it, no key, no authority, no name, no office, no document and nobody's permission. **Nothing about it got easier. Four of the four found it harder than the people they work beside, because the people they work beside were not asked and they were asked.**

---

**At about half past two on that Wednesday, the check at a set of scales in the second of the four towns stopped being a check.**

The man of about twenty-six who has been the second person on every check since the spring was not on the pad that afternoon, and the man of about fifty-eight who keeps the only written record in that town would not sign the one line on the card that says a person confirms it is done at that site, and he was right about the load and he has been right about the load all along, because a load that was wrong before it came to that pad cannot be caught by counting pallets against a ticket after it has arrived.

**About nine lorries stood on that road from about half past two until about six.** Nobody was hurt. Nothing was lost that anybody can name. The loads went in the evening and the drivers did not go home and about four of them have said so out loud, in about nine seconds each, in their own words, to a lorry park and not to a person, and the thing they named was the day, and not one of the four was thanked and not one of the four was repaid and nobody improved on what they said.

The man of about fifty-eight was thanked by nobody. He has been thanked by nobody in about nine years and he is right about the load, and about four hundred competent people in this city would call what a man of twenty-two did on Monday a waste, and he is one of them, and the reason he is one of them is that a thing two of them do is not a rule and a piece of card in a coat pocket is not a way past that, and he has not been told that any of it happened and is not going to be.

---

**At about three on that Wednesday, a room in the third of the four towns that had gone dark on purpose went dark again, and about eleven people who had work on the Wednesday did not have it.**

The room belongs to a man of about fifty-four who works nights at a depot. He is owed nothing, he is not thanked, and he is the man who said the true thing once, to the face of a woman in a room above a line in this city, in an earlier week, and nobody thanked him for that either and he has not said a second thing.

**He was wrong about what refusing costs.** He told the about eleven on the Wednesday afternoon that a room that does nothing costs nothing, and it cost those about eleven a week each, and he was right to do it and he is wrong about what it cost and both of those are true at once and nobody in that room improved on either half.

He named the cost out loud. He said, in about nine seconds, to about nine people in that room, that eleven of them had a week of work on the Wednesday and do not have it, and that it is gone, and that he is not going to be asked to be sorry for it and is not going to say he is.

**Nobody thanked him. Nobody said he was wrong to refuse. Nobody improved on the week. The week is not paid back by anybody and eleven people do not have it back and it is not going to be in anybody’s hand on Thursday.**

---

**Nobody in this city knows a name and this case has looked for one for three days and will look for one for as long as there is a question to look for it with.**

A depot is not an office that examines strangers. A company training room is not an office that examines strangers. A set of scales with a hut by it is not an office that examines strangers. There is no office on either side of that road that would let a stranger be examined by a stranger, and nobody on this side of it has asked to examine anybody, and the four who wanted to look at somebody's wrist looked at each other.

**He was at a counter in Lattice Ward from about two until about six on the Wednesday, four miles from a room off a service road and on the far side of this city from the first of the four towns, and the four rooms were in four towns and he was in none of them and he was not on the bus, and the card in his coat pocket was about four miles from the man of fifty-three who offered it on Monday and on the far side of this city from a gate on a road where it would have worked, and it went to neither.**

---

Two nails in that room carried nineteen lines and the newest of them is a lift mechanic’s, eleven words, signed, about the middle, and it is the only promise on that board and it is three hundred and sixty-three days old, and nothing was added to it and nothing was removed from it and nothing was rubbed off it.

**A card in a coat pocket is a piece of card about the size of a playing card with a place-name on one side and a week-number with nothing after it on the other, in a hand that is not the hand of the man carrying it, and it is not a ticket and it is not a key and it opens nothing and it has no title and no office and nothing on the back of it.** The wall list in its plastic sleeve in the repair shop in Lattice Ward is a fourth object and has never been on two nails. The hardboard is a fifth. The master’s original is in a fourth pocket in a tool roll with a blank fifth line and a drawer that is shut. The page of paper with about eleven lines in it is a sixth, in a fourth building. The sheet of about nine lines in a room in the second of the four districts is not any of the others. The bar across the inside of a door in the third of the four districts and the bar across the inside of a door in the second of the four towns are not the six and not each other and nobody has been told there are two.

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.
