## 4. The one defect this phase found in its own instruments, and it is a matcher defect and not a figure

**THIS PHASE'S FIRST APPARATUS RUN RETURNED 40.412 IN AGGREGATE AND 33.560 FOR MOVEMENT IV, AGAINST A PUBLISHED 39.982 AND 31.652, AND THE DIFFERENCE WAS NOT IN THE FILES. It was in the boundary. The run detected the load book as the first line beginning with an asterisk, and at `workspace/volume-08/batch-0006/chapter-0387.md` the first such line is the `* * *` marker on its seventy-fifth line, which is two hundred and twenty-one words before the entry line on its hundred and tenth, and everything between them was counted as apparatus. Restating the boundary as `^\*\d+\. ` returns 39.982 and 31.652 and every other figure in the table above, and the four other movements were wrong by a smaller amount for the same reason and are right now.**

The generalisation is the finding and it belongs to the next phase: **in a file where the `* * *` marker is legal, "the italic entry line" is not the same thing as "a line beginning with an asterisk", and any instrument that says the second while meaning the first will silently swallow the marker and everything above it.** Volume 09's one marker is at Chapter 437, so the same defect is live for the whole of Movement IV and this repository has been describing the boundary in prose since Volume 05. The house boundary is now stated as the pattern, and a volume-09 batch that writes its own version of the instrument must use the pattern and not the description.

**THE DISAGREEMENT, STATED PLAINLY. `state/current.md` and `state/continuity.md`, both written by the Volume 08 audit, say that the next phase is a writing movement at `workspace/volume-09/batch-0003/`, and they say that `batch-0001` and `batch-0002` are both phases whose work is discharged on arrival. **HOW IT IS SETTLED, AND IT IS SETTLED ON THE EVIDENCE AND NOT ON PREFERENCE. The two state-file sentences were written before this phase was dispatched, from a vantage point at which the next unwritten prompt was the only thing that could come next, and they are correct about the two documents and silent about the cards: the plan and the calendar are on disk, and the audit's claim that the outline phase's work is discharged on arrival is true of those two documents and false of the third thing an outline phase owes.
