# Chapter 429 — A Record Asked For In About Nine Seconds And Not Given, And A Man Of Fifty-Eight Who Is Right About One Thing On The Way Out Of The Door

The Wednesday of week one hundred and fifty-seven was spent at a bench in Lattice Ward until about two, and from about half past two in an office in a third district of this city until about half past four, and the one thing that is true of it is that **a woman of about sixty whose job is to ask questions asked, correctly, for a record of one afternoon in one room in a building that is not in this city, and a man of fifty-eight of the office that retains emergency authority did not give it and did not pretend he had not got it and did not pretend he had, and on the way out of the door he said out loud that his office did not write the sentence nobody can find and cannot say who did, and nobody thanked him for that either, and a man of twenty-two was in that office and did not answer the question she asked him first.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last of them about two in the afternoon, and his day was a bench in Lattice Ward from about half past seven until about two and then a corridor in a third district from about half past two for about two hours. The word was at nine and ten and is told to nobody. **Heating: on, dearer, and four names on a lease cost more than they did, and a fortnight a cook paid for is paid for by nobody and is not resolved.** The room off that service road is six hundred and twenty-five days old and is warm, and about four people came in on the Wednesday evening for the temperature and none of them was asked anything.

The card in the coat pocket is six hundred and twenty-nine days old, which is four days more than the room is, and it has been in that pocket since the Wednesday it came out of that rail, and a card's age does not stop because a card has been picked up.

---

**He is about fifty-eight. He is not a clerk and he holds no post. He has a bad hip and he stands up to argue and sits down to stop arguing. He is not defeated and he is not turned and he does not get a new wing. He is not in this city in any building but this one this week, he is not in a kitchen, and he is not in any of the four towns on the road and has not been on that road in this case at all.**

The office had about nine rooms' worth of paper in it, a counter, a wall binder of printed standards, and a woman of about sixty whose job is to ask questions.

**About four hundred competent people in this city can each invoke a paragraph printed on the back of a page of paper, and not one of them has anywhere to put an objection, and the woman of about sixty has been the person who asks the questions in three of those places and has never once been given a place to put one.**

---

She asked the man of twenty-two first, and it took her about nine seconds, and it was not an ambush and it was not a trap and she did not enjoy it.

"**Is there a document.**"

He did not say no. He did not say yes.

"**I have a piece of card with a place-name on it. It is not a document and it has nothing on the back of it and it opens nothing, and I have never seen the thing you are asking me about, and I am not going to tell you that it is not there.**"

She wrote nothing down. She asked him nothing else, and she did not say that she believed him and she did not say that she did not, and nobody in that office improved on what he said and nobody thanked him for it.

---

Then she asked the other one, and she asked it correctly, which means she said why she wanted it, what she wanted it for and how long she would hold it, and she asked for one thing and not for the office's file.

"**I want a record of one afternoon in one room in a building that is not in this city, in which about nine people were present, and the answer that was going through in that room. I have asked for it in the proper way and I am asking for it again in the proper way.**"

It took him about nine seconds.

"**No.**"

**He did not say he had not got it. He did not say it had been destroyed. He did not say it had never existed. He said no, and then he said the other thing, and the other thing is the reason nobody in this case can prove him wrong about any of it.**

"**There is no copy of that afternoon in this office and there never was and I would know, because I have the file for that year open on my own desk and I have had it open twice this year.**"

Nobody improved on that. Nobody asked him how he knew. Nobody asked him to show the file and he did not offer to show it and nobody asked him to.

Nobody said thank you. Nobody said he was right. Nobody said he was being obstructive and nobody said he was being helpful.

---

**And on the way out of the door, to nobody in particular, he said the other thing, unasked, and it took him about nine seconds, and it is the one sentence in about as long as this case has been going that nobody in this case has ever heard anybody say.**

"**We did not write it. Whatever anybody is looking for, we did not write it and I cannot tell you who did, and I have been asked that twice this year and both times by people who had already decided.**"

Nobody asked him which two. Nobody asked him to write it down. Nobody thanked him for saying it.

**He is right about it, and the reason he is right is in a file on his own desk and not in anything anybody in this case can see, and being right about it is not being forgiven for the nine seconds, and nobody in this case has thanked him for either and about four people in this city have said since that he should have handed it over and about four have said that he could not have, and the two groups have not met and are not going to.**

**He is not a hero. He is not a villain. He is not defeated and he is not turned and he did not get a new wing, and he went back to his desk with a bad hip and did not telephone anybody and has not said anything to anybody since, and nobody has asked him anything else.**

---

He was at a counter in Lattice Ward until about six and then at a shutter until about ten.

**Nothing was signed. Nothing was adopted. Nothing was written down by anybody in that office on that afternoon, and the file on the man's desk is not in this case and was not shown and will not be asked for again by anybody in this case.**

He walked to a street in this city at about ten and stood outside a door in it for about two minutes and then went home, and nobody in this case knows he did that and he did not say.

Nobody thanked anybody.
