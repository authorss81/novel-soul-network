# Chapter 423 — Nine Seconds At The End Of A Bench, And A Second Refusal By The Person Who Owes Nobody An Answer

The Thursday of week one hundred and fifty-five was spent at a bench in a building in Lattice Ward from about half past seven until about five, and the one thing that is true of it is that **Sera Quill, who is forty-five and is the lead repairer at Civic Spine 7 and has been in that building eleven years, was asked, correctly, for the second time anybody has asked her that, to speak for the maintenance crews, and refused in about nine seconds, and she was right about the refusal and wrong about one specific thing she said after it that nobody improved on and nobody contradicted, and the reason she gave was not the reason she had, and she looked at the door in the middle of it.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last of them about five in the afternoon, and the eleven were done in a corridor and a bay in a different building in the same district from the shop he is at on the other four days. The word was at nine and ten and is told to nobody. **Heating: on, dearer, and four names on a lease cost more than they did, and a fortnight a cook paid for is paid for by nobody and is not resolved.** The room off that service road is six hundred and twelve days old and is warm, and about nine people came in on the Thursday evening for the temperature and none of them was asked anything.

The card in the coat pocket is six hundred and sixteen days old, which is four days more than the room is, and it has been in that pocket since the Wednesday of the fortnight before last, and a card's age does not stop because a card has been picked up, and it was over a chair in a rented room in this city for four hours on Wednesday night and it was not out of it.

---

Civic Spine 7 is in Lattice Ward and it has a corridor and eleven men and a bench and a tool roll and a lead repairer's letter in a frame on the wall, and Sera Quill is forty-five and has been in that building eleven years, and she has a braid she keeps in her pocket and puts in when she is at the bench and takes out when she is not.

**She is not a deputy and she is not a category and she holds no post and nobody thanked her on Thursday and nobody has thanked her for the eleven years either, and about four people in that building have worked out on their own that the whole of what she is owed is that nobody asks her twice.**

The man of about twenty-nine who works on lifts was on a ladder in that building on Thursday morning and said nothing to anybody, and he has been asked nothing by anybody in this case and is not going to be.

---

The man who asked her was about thirty-four and had the contract for a building of twenty-two flats off a street in another part of this city, and eleven of the flats had a lift that was not running, and he had a room booked for the following week with about nine people in it.

He had been in the corridor about forty minutes before he asked. He had asked two of the eleven men already and both of them had said they would have to ask her, and he had not been told anything about that by either of them afterwards and both of them had been honest.

"**I need one sentence from you on Thursday, in the room, in front of whoever turns up.**"

"**No.**"

It took her about nine seconds and she did not raise her voice and she did not stop what she was doing first.

"**You did this before.**"

"**Yes.**"

Nobody improved on the no. Nobody said she had been harsh. Nobody said he had asked badly. Nobody thanked her and nobody asked her a second question about it and nobody in that corridor has ever asked her to say it again.

**She was right about the refusal. There are eleven men in that building and not one of them is hers, and a person is not a post, and the crews do not go into a room with a landlord because somebody who repairs lifts has said they will.**

---

Then she said the other thing, unasked, in about nine seconds, and it is the thing she was wrong about.

"**And none of them would be in that room on a Sunday anyway, so do not build your Thursday on me.**"

**Two of them would be. They would be there on their own account and not because she had sent them and not because anybody had asked them to be, and one of them would be there because he is the only one of the eleven who can get into the machine room without a lift being already out, and the other one would be there because she has said to him twice that a fault report from a resident is a fault report and not a complaint. Nobody in that corridor told her she was wrong about that part. Nobody in that corridor contradicted her about anything. About four of the eleven men in that building have said nothing about it and about four have said something else since, and the two groups have not met and she has not been told either.**

Nobody said she was right. Nobody said she was wrong. Nobody asked her which two she meant.

---

**And the reason she gave was not the reason she had.**

She said that a person is not a post and that the crews are not hers to hand out to a room, and that part was the shape of the thing and not the size of it. She said the first half of the sentence and then she looked at the door for about two seconds, and there were four men in the corridor and the door was the only one of the four that was shut, and then she started the sentence again lower down and finished it without stopping.

**Nobody in that corridor has ever asked her what she was looking at. Nobody has ever asked her to finish it the other way. About four people in this city have wondered about the door and about four have not, and neither group has said anything to her about it, and she is not going to be asked, and she is not going to be thanked.**

She is owed nothing and she is not a villain and she is not frightened of anybody in that building and she is not a post. She is the person who repairs the thing and is asked to be the thing.

---

The rest of the Thursday was eleven jobs and about four of them were the same fault in four different places in that building, and nobody in that district had seen him before and nobody asked him where he had learned any of it and nobody was thanked.

At about a quarter past five the man of about thirty-four was in a car park and then he was not, and the room was booked for the following week, and the sentence did not get said in it, and nobody has been told by anybody in that room that it was not going to be said.

**The man of thirty-four is not a villain and is owed nothing and is not in anybody's case, and two of the eleven men he had already asked said they would have to ask her, and neither of them was thanked either and neither of them was asked twice and he did not ask a third time.**

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.
