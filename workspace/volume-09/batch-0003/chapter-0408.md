# Chapter 408 — Five Hundred And Eighty-One Days Is An Age And Not A Date, And A Man Of About Fifty-Six Names What A Sheet With Five Names In It Costs Him

The Monday evening of week one hundred and fifty-one was spent in a room off a service road in the Lower Wards, and the one thing that is true of it is that **the room is five hundred and eighty-one days old, and five hundred and eighty-one is the day a landlord put the heating on, and both of those are in the same figure, so the figure has to be taken as an age and not as a date: it is how long a building has been standing, and not when anything was switched on, and the heating is a thing that happened once in a building and is not a thing this case counts in weeks, and a fortnight of it that a cook laid out in this city is still paid for by nobody and is not going to be paid by anything that happened in that room tonight.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last of them about six in the evening. He was at a bench in Lattice Ward until about two and then at a counter until about six. The word was at nine and ten and is told to nobody. **Heating: on, dearer than it was, and four names on a lease cost more than they did. Room off that service road: five hundred and eighty-one days, and that figure is an age of a building and is not a date, and see below.** The card in a rail in a repair shop in Lattice Ward is five hundred and eighty-five days old, which is four days more than the room is, and it is a third object in a third building and it did not move on this day.

**About four people came in on the Monday evening for the temperature and were not asked anything by anybody. In the chair nearest the door was a woman of about thirty whom nobody asked, nobody thanked and nobody apologised to, and the page that is hers is the eighth of eight things in a ring binder on a shelf and is unread and was not read, and nobody has wondered whether that is fair for about as long as she has been sitting in that chair, and about four people have wondered and about four have not, and nobody has ever wondered out loud.**

---

The question he has been carrying for about as long as this case has been going is a small one and it has a small shape. A cook of twenty-seven, who works in that room at a sink and has a part-time job of her own, laid out a fortnight of the heating in that building out of her own money, and nobody repaid her, and she has said the same shape of thing about it six times, and she is not going to say it a seventh, and he is not going to ask her.

**She said the sixth of them in about nine seconds in that room, unasked, on a Monday evening, the same as she says it every time, and what she said was not a request and was not a claim and was a sentence with no object in it that anybody in that room could pick up, and about four people in that room have wanted to pick it up and about four have not, and the man of twenty-two is in the second group and has been since the first one, and there is no moment in any of it at which he joins the first group, and nobody has ever asked him to.**

The bill for a fortnight of gas in a building of nine flats over a canteen does not have a person on it. It has an account on it and a meter number and a period, and a period of about two weeks, and a sum, and about four people could look at that bill in about four seconds and there is no name on it anywhere, because there is no name to put on it.

The other three names on the lease do not know there was a fortnight. Asking them means telling them, and telling them means the cook is told that somebody asked, and that is the thing he has been not doing for about as long as this case has been going and is not going to start on a Monday.

**What he actually did on that Monday, before anybody came in, was stand in the passage by the meter with a torch for about four minutes and look at the dial, which told him the same thing the meter has been telling about nine other people in that building since about the summer, which is that a number of units went through in about two weeks and that no person in that building knows whose units those were. That is a fact about a dial. It is not a record, and it is not evidence of anything, and he told nobody about it and nobody asked, and about four people in this case would call it knowing something and about four would call it nothing, and both of those are the same as what it is.**

**So the correct thing to do about it is exactly the thing he cannot do, and there is no third option, and nobody in that room has ever suggested one, and he has not asked anybody to suggest one, and the reason he came in on the Monday was that he had begun, for about two days, to think of this as a thing that could be found out, and he has now established that it cannot, and the establishing took about nine seconds and a bill.**

---

**At about twenty past seven the man of about fifty-six who works for a contractor came in and sat down and did not say anything for about four minutes, and then he said the thing he has been carrying since a Wednesday in the fortnight before last,** when a sheet of paper with five names on it in five hands came off a nail in that building and a man of twenty-two put it down on a table and handed it over and did not say why.

He said it in about nine seconds and he said it unasked and he said it about himself and not about the man of twenty-two.

"**I have to say yes to things in four buildings on the strength of a sheet with five names in five hands. I did not write the sheet. I have not worked out what I am carrying, and I go in about four times a week, and that is the whole of what it costs me.**"

**Nobody improved on it. Nobody thanked him. Nobody asked him which four rooms, and about four people in that room have wanted to, and nobody asked him, and nobody in that building has said since that the man of twenty-two was right to hand it over or that he was wrong to, and nobody thanked either of them and the sheet has not come back, and about four people in that building have said that a rota nobody wrote is not a rota and about four have said that it is the same rota, and neither group has said the other is right.**

**He has been inside four buildings he was not sent into in about a month and nobody has asked him what he was doing in any of them and he has not said, and that is a second thing he is carrying and he did not name it tonight and nobody asked him to.**

The rota is a sheet of paper. It is not a register and it is not a rota in any office and it has no office and it is not on a wall anywhere that anybody has described. It covers a week that has already started. About four of the five names on it are people who do not know each other.

---

**A cook of twenty-seven was at that sink from about half past five and she said nothing at all, and she was not asked anything, and she was not thanked, and she was not given a post, and that room does not depend on her being in it and would be a colder room and a dearer room and not a different room, and about four people in that room have noticed that the sink is the only thing in it that is used every single evening and about four have not, and the second group is not stupid and is busy.**

**Nothing was paid tonight. The fortnight a cook laid out is paid for by nobody and is not resolved and is not going to be resolved by anything that happened in that room on that Monday, and it has been open for about as long as this case has been going, and nothing that is written down is going to close it, and paying it in order to tidy a ledger is spending a debt and not settling one.**

---

**Two nails in that room carried a hardboard with nineteen lines on it, and the eight ages that anybody keeps are set out in the docket below, and not one of them is a day. The nineteen is a lift mechanic's, eleven words, signed, about the middle, and it is the only promise on that board, and a man of fifty-six saying what a sheet costs him did not make it more or less true.**

**The six surfaces that this case keeps confusing were six surfaces tonight, and not two of them were in the same building, let alone the same room. A card in a rail. A list in a sleeve on a shop wall. A board of nineteen lines with no name column. An original in a tool roll, waiting for a line nobody has ever asked for. A page of about eleven lines in a fourth building. A sheet of about nine lines in the second of the four districts. A third object in a third building, and the two have never been in one room.**

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.

*The cost of a sheet with five names in it, as a record of nine seconds.* **Who: a man of about fifty-six who works for a contractor, owed nothing by anybody in that room, and who took the sheet, and who has not given it back. When: about twenty past seven on the Monday evening, unasked, and he had been sitting for about four minutes first. When he took it: nineteen days ago, on a Wednesday, and about three weeks, and not about ten weeks. Whether it was correct: nobody asked. Times anybody improved on it: none. Times anybody thanked him: none. Times anybody asked him which four rooms: none, and about four people in that room have wanted to. *The fortnight, as a record of a thing that cannot be found out.* **What is owed: two weeks of the heating in a building of nine flats over a canteen in the Lower Wards, put down by a cook of twenty-seven out of money of her own. Who has repaid it: nobody. How many times she has said the same shape of thing about it: six, and it is not going to be a seventh, and nobody in this case is going to ask her. What the document carries: an account, a meter number, a period, and a sum, and about no person at all, because there is no name available to go on it. Why it cannot be pursued: the remaining three signatories of the lease do not know that those two weeks happened, and raising it with them means announcing it, and announcing it means she finds out that somebody raised it. Whether the correct move is the move he cannot make: yes, and there is no third option, and nobody in that room has ever produced one and he has not asked anybody to produce one. Whether anything was paid on this day: no. Whether it is resolved: no, and nobody pays it in order to tidy a ledger, and a person who pays it in order to tidy a ledger has spent a debt and not settled one. Owner: nobody, and a cook of twenty-seven, and a sink.**
