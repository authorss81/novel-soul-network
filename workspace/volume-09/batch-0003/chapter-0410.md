# Chapter 410 — A Bell-Push That Does Not Ring By Itself, A Second Refusal In About Nine Seconds, And The Nineteenth Line Standing At Two Hundred And Eighty Days

The Thursday of week one hundred and fifty-one was spent in a yard in Lattice Ward in the morning, at a counter in a repair shop in the same district until about two, and from about half past six in a room off a service road in the Lower Wards, and the one thing that is true of it is that **the asking was refused a second time in about nine seconds by a person with an ordinary job who is owed nothing and who is right, and a man of about forty-two who fits kitchens found the fault in a bell-push that does not ring by itself, put a part where the caretaker's own hand is the only place it goes, said out loud that it changes nothing, and nobody thanked him, and on two nails in that room a line that is two hundred and eighty days old is still the only promise on the board and nothing was added to it and nothing was removed from it and nothing was rubbed off it.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last of them about six in the evening. The word was at nine and ten and is told to nobody. **Heating: on, dearer, and four names on a lease cost more than they did, and a fortnight a cook paid for is paid for by nobody and is not resolved.** The room off that service road is five hundred and eighty-four days old and is warm, and about nine people came in on the Thursday evening, none of whom was asked anything. The card is five hundred and eighty-eight days old, four days more than the room, and it is in a coat pocket and the rail at the end of the counter in the other building holds one card where it held two a week ago.

---

She was in the yard at about ten past ten with a key ring on a hook at her belt, and she came down the path towards the blue door and saw him standing about four feet away from it again, and she did not stop.

He had the question ready and it was a different question from last week's and it was better, and it was better because he had spent about four days making it better, and about four people in this case would call that persistence and about four would call it a man doing the thing he is for.

"**I am not asking for the door. I am asking whether there is a form.**"

She said, in about nine seconds:

"**No. And I am not going to tell you why not, and if you come back with a better version of it I am going to say the same, and that is not a threat, it is a Thursday.**"

---

He said nothing back for about nine seconds. Then he said, "**That is all right.**"

She said, "**It is not all right. It is the same as last week. It is a limit.**"

"**I know it is.**"

"**Then why are you here.**"

"**Because I had a better question.**"

"**I have noticed.**"

**He did not argue. He did not say that he would manage. He did not offer to come back with another version, and he did not ask her to write the no down, and he did not ask her who she would have to ask, and he did not turn it into a task, and about four people in that yard noticed that he did not turn it into a task, and about four did not notice anything, and the two groups have not met. The limit from the Thursday of last week was still standing and it is still standing and he is not the person who asked again. She is right about all of it, and nobody has said she is right, and nobody in that building has said the man of twenty-two was wrong to come back, and the sheet of paper with five names in five hands has still not come back either.**

She locked the blue door and went on her round and he went and got a bus.

---

He was at the counter in that district at about ten past one when the man of about forty-two came in.

He is a man who fits kitchens. He fitted the extractor in the back room about two years ago and he has been back four times since and he has never once been asked to look at anything else in that building, and there is no standing arrangement and there is no list, and he is owed nothing by anybody in this case.

He stood in the doorway for about four seconds looking at the wall by the door and then said, "**That bell-push.**"

"**It does not do anything.**"

"**It is not broken.**"

"**Nobody has reported it as broken.**"

"**It is not broken, it has a wire behind it that came away from a terminal about six months ago and somebody has put a piece of tape over the gap, and the tape is the reason nobody has reported it, because a push that does nothing looks exactly like a push that has been disconnected on purpose.**"

He found the fault in about four minutes. He did it without asking for anything, and nobody in that room asked him to.

Then he said it, unasked, in about nine seconds, and he was owed nothing and he is not a hero and he is not a villain and he is not in anybody's case.

"**The repair is right. The part is on a van on a Wednesday. I have put it where the caretaker's own hand is the only place it goes, and it will be done, and nothing about that room will be different and I am not going to be thanked for it.**"

Nobody thanked him. Nobody asked him to do the other four things he could see in that building, of which there were four, and two of them he looked at while he was on the wall and one of them he mentioned once and did not mention again.

He left at about ten past two. Nobody asked him to come back, and he is not going to be asked, and about four people in that building have said since that the man who fits kitchens knows what he is doing and about four have said nothing, and neither group has asked the other why.

**It changes nothing. He is the fifth person in this volume to do a correct thing in a room and say out loud that it changes nothing, and at least four of the other four were not even thanked either, and the fifth has not been thanked, and this case has no list of them and is not going to build one.**

---

**The hand on the card in the pocket is the hand of Evan Senn, and he is not coming back, and there is no interval for him in this case and none can be put there, because the day of anything he did is not in this case and the man who knew the day is dead and the woman who has the memory did not keep a date. He is not a device and he is not a secret and he is not the reason anything is going to happen in the second half of this volume, and no document in this case confers anything on anybody because of him, and no living person in this case is related to him.**

---

At about half past six there were about nine people in a warm room off a service road, and a cook of twenty-seven was at a sink saying nothing, and in the chair nearest the door was a woman of about thirty whom nobody asked a question, nobody thanked and nobody apologised to, and her page is the eighth of eight things in a ring binder on a shelf and is unread and was not read on the Thursday and is not going to be read in this volume.

**Two nails in that room carried a hardboard with nineteen lines on it, and the eight ages are set out in the docket below, and the nineteen of them is two hundred and eighty days. The nineteen is a lift mechanic's, eleven words, signed, about the middle, and it is the only promise on that board, and there is no twentieth line, and the reason there is no twentieth line is not restraint: a twentieth line would be a new thing with a new day on it, and a volume that adds a promise to a board in order to have something to publish has added a promise and not a line.**

Nothing was added on this Thursday and nothing was removed and nothing was rubbed off.

The card in a coat pocket is a third object in a third building. The wall list in its plastic sleeve in that shop is a fourth and is not that board. The hardboard is a fifth. The master's original is in a fourth pocket with a blank fifth line. The page of paper with about eleven lines on it is a sixth, in a fourth building. The sheet of about nine lines in a room in the second of the four districts is not any of the others. A third object in a third building, and the two have never been in one room.

**The Exchange did not sit on this day. Its last sitting was the Wednesday of the fortnight before last, twenty-two days ago, and its next is the Wednesday of next week, six days from today, and the first sitting of this volume is in the next movement, and the book opens at it, and nothing about that opening was arranged by anybody in this room and nothing in this room has anything to do with it.**

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.
