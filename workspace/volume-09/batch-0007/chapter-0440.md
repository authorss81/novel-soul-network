# Chapter 440 — A Thursday After A Sitting, A Word Said Once In A Mouth, And Nothing Repaid

The Thursday of week one hundred and sixty was spent at a bench in Lattice Ward until about two and at a counter until about six and from about half past six in one room off a service road, and the one thing that is true of it is that **a cook of twenty-seven said one thing out loud in about nine seconds to a room and nobody improved on it and nobody thanked her, and the book in a back room stands at fifty-five lines after yesterday and the tin stands at seventy-three, and nobody in this case was repaid a thing they are owed.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last of them about six in the evening. The word was at nine and ten and is told to nobody. **Heating: on, dearer, and four names on a lease cost more than they did, and a fortnight a cook paid for is paid for by nobody and is not resolved.** The room off that service road is six hundred and forty-seven days old and is warm, and about nine people came in on the Thursday evening for the temperature and none of them was asked anything.

The card in the coat pocket is six hundred and fifty-one days old, which is four days more than the room is, and it has been in that pocket since the Wednesday it came out of that rail, and a card’s age does not stop because a card has been picked up, and the rail at the end of the counter in the other building holds one card where it held two before that Wednesday.

---

He was at the bench until about two and at the counter until about six and heard nothing of yesterday except what the shutters told him. At about half past six he walked four miles to the room and sat at the end of the second bench.

**Nobody told him what was said to a face yesterday and nobody asked him whether he knew, and the only person who could have told him is four miles off in a room whose shutters go up at about ten and come down at about eleven, and he was at a bench at about ten. The man of about thirty in overalls, who put a line into a book yesterday, was at work at about half past four on a floor four miles off, and he did not come to that shop and nobody saw him and nobody has asked him what he said.**

The day had a shape he was used to. Eleven on the docket and eleven in date, the last of them at about two. A woman of about thirty-four came in at about half past three and asked whether the man who fitted her door on the Friday before last would be there on a Tuesday, and he said he did not know, and she said she would come back on a Tuesday, and he said nothing about which Tuesday, and she went. He did not write her name on a list for a Tuesday and there is no list.

At about half past four somebody telephoned the shop and asked for a man who does not work there and does not work in this district, and he said the shop did not take messages and the caller did not leave a name and the call was not written down. Nobody in that building knows he took it.

At about half past six he walked four miles to the room and sat at the end of the second bench.

A cook of twenty-seven was at the sink with her sleeves up. In the chair nearest the door was a woman of about thirty whom nobody asked a question, nobody thanked and nobody apologised to, and the page that is hers is the eighth of eight things in a ring binder on a shelf and is unread and was not read on Thursday.

A man of about fifty-one with a tool bag was not in that room and nobody has asked him about anything for about thirty-six weeks and one day, and nobody has renewed the asking and nobody has stopped it. A man of about thirty-three, who holds four buildings and about nine hundred doors with no stamp and no reference and no date, was not in that room on Thursday, and his hold went on for about forty weeks without anything being asked of him and without anything being given to him.

The cook put a pot to soak and turned round with her hands wet and said it to the room and not to anybody in it, and it took her about nine seconds.

"**Eight eat here on a Thursday and the tap runs cold and the Assembly has never asked us what for.**"

Nobody improved on it. Nobody asked her what she meant. Nobody asked her which eight and nobody asked her which Thursday.

**The eight are not a list anywhere.** She has counted them out loud in that kitchen on a Thursday and on a Friday and the count has been eight both times, and two people who are in that kitchen on a Wednesday are not in it on a Thursday, and nobody in that kitchen has said why and she has not asked and is not going to. The tap has been cold at six in the evening for about four months and it was cold before that for longer, and the water is not turned off at any hour of any day, and the thing that happens at six is that the pipe stops and then starts again, and a person can see it start.

**She said the word and she went back to the pot.** She did not say it twice and she did not say it louder, and she did not say it to him, and she has said it to a room that is not a room and she holds no post in it and she is not a voice in it and she will not be asked what for by anybody who can do anything about it.

**She is owed nothing for saying it and she holds no post and she is not the answer to anything, and nobody thanked her and nobody is going to.**

---

Nobody asked him about yesterday. Nobody asked whether the book grew and nobody asked what was said to a face. About four people in that room knew there had been a sitting yesterday and about four did not, and the two groups have not met.

**The four who knew did not know what had happened in it.** There was a sitting four miles off on Wednesday morning and a book went up by one line in it, and of the people in this room who know that much, not one knows what the line says, and none of them was there, and nobody has asked and nobody is going to. The shutters on that landing go up at about ten and come down at about eleven and nobody in this room has ever seen them move.

Two nails in that room carried nineteen lines and the newest of them is a lift mechanic’s, eleven words, signed, about the middle, and it is the only promise on that board and it is three hundred and forty-three days old, and nothing was added to it and nothing was removed from it and nothing was rubbed off it.

**A card in a coat pocket is a piece of card about the size of a playing card with a place-name on one side and a week-number with nothing after it on the other, in a hand that is not the hand of the man carrying it, and it is not a ticket and it is not a key and it opens nothing and it has no title and no office and nothing on the back of it. The wall list in its plastic sleeve in the repair shop in Lattice Ward is a fourth object and has never been on two nails. The hardboard is a fifth. The master’s original is in a fourth pocket in a tool roll with a blank fifth line and a drawer that is shut. The page of paper with about eleven lines in it is a sixth, in a fourth building. The sheet of about nine lines in a room in the second of the four districts is not any of the others.**

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.

*The one thing, as a record of nine seconds said to a room.* **Who said it: a cook of twenty-seven, at a sink with her sleeves up, owed nothing, holds no post. To whom: a room, and not to anybody in it. When: on Thursday evening after a pot was put to soak, which is the fourth Thursday running that this has been said to a room and the second that anyone in this case has been told it was said. How long: about nine seconds. What the sentence is: in the speech above, in her own words, and it is not written down anywhere, and it is not repeated by anybody in this case and it is not going to be. Whether the word in it has been said in this case before: yes, twice, both times in a mouth and by a person with a job, and both times to a room and not to a person. Whether anybody in this case has said it back to her: no. Times anybody improved on it: none. Times anybody asked what she meant: none. Times anybody asked which eight: none. Times anybody asked which Thursday: none. Times anybody asked what for: none, and nobody who could act on the answer is in that kitchen. Whether she was thanked: no. Whether she is the answer to anything: no. Whether the tap ran cold at six that evening: yes, and about four people were in that kitchen and saw it and none of them said anything about it and none of them said anything about her. *What the day did not touch.* **The eight were not counted by anybody else and the number was not written down. The tap was cold at six and nobody turned anything off, and the pipe started again on its own at about a quarter past, and nobody in that kitchen said so to her, and she was not asked about the sentence again. The word is in one mouth and it is not on a surface and not on any sheet that anybody keeps and not in a document and not in a set of minutes, and the minutes of that place are kept by people who do not eat there.
