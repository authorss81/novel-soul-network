# Chapter 413 — Four Seconds For A Question He Answered Wrong, And Four Seconds For A Refusal Nobody Thanked Him For

The Thursday of week one hundred and fifty-two was spent on a bus that took about two hours each way, and on the ground in the first of four towns on that road for about four hours, and the one thing that is true of it is that **a woman of about forty-four who hires out a hall asked the man of twenty-two one question in about four seconds and the question was not the one he came with, and he gave a wrong answer to it in one word, and she corrected him in about nine words in her own words and nobody improved on her correction and nobody thanked her, and a man of about sixty-one who mends shoes was asked to keep the card and said no in about four seconds and was not thanked, and he said out loud unasked that three people from this city have asked him the same thing in nine weeks and he has answered it three times differently and cannot tell anybody which of the three was true.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last of them about six in the evening, and the eleven were eleven jobs in a town he has not worked in before, done in the order the card gave them, and the card is a card in date and carries no address. The word was at nine and ten and is told to nobody. **Heating: on, dearer, and four names on a lease cost more than they did, and a fortnight a cook paid for is paid for by nobody and is not resolved.** The room off that service road is five hundred and ninety-one days old and is warm, and about four people came in on the Thursday evening for the temperature and nobody asked them anything.

The card in the coat pocket is five hundred and ninety-five days old, which is four days more than the room is, and it has been in that pocket since the Wednesday of the week before last, and a card's age does not stop because a card has been picked up, and **it is in a town two hours of bus from a counter in this city and it is not in that counter and nobody in that town has been told whose it is.**

---

The bus went at about half past six in the morning and came round at about four in the afternoon, and it came once and went once, and a shop in that town shut at four.

There were about nine people on it and he knew none of them, which is also not a fact about any of them. A man of about forty-four was on it who lives in this city and had come up the road to mend a boiler in a building with a caretaker, and he had told his employer on Friday that he would be back on the Thursday.

---

The town had a market cross and about four stalls trading and a shoe stall with a board out of the front of it, and a hall with the chairs stacked against the far wall six high, and nobody has hired that hall since a funeral in the spring.

**A building that had been a shop had a kitchen in it, and a woman of about fifty-four feeds about nine people a day out of it, and nobody thanked her and nobody asked her a question and she is not the answer to anything, and she is not in a kitchen in this city and no sentence puts the two in one clause.**

Marek got off at about a quarter past nine with the card in his coat and about nine hours before the bus came round, and he did eleven jobs between then and four, in the order the card gave them, in about nine buildings and yards, and about four of the eleven were the same fault in four different places, and nobody in that town had seen him before and nobody asked him where he had learned any of it and nobody was thanked.

He went to the shoe stall first, because the shoe stall was the reason he had come and he had not told anybody in that town the reason he had come.

---

The man at that stall mends shoes and has done for about forty years. He is about sixty-one. His name is Bert Sowerby, and it is in this sentence and on no surface and in no document and in nobody's mouth.

"**Are you the maintenance.**"

"**No.**"

"**You are the other one.**"

"**I am not anybody's.**"

The man at the stall put down a shoe and looked at him for about four seconds.

"**You have come up the road with a card.**"

Marek did not say yes and did not say no. He took it out of the coat pocket and put it on the board, face up, where the place-name was, and the shop on that high street was a hundred yards behind him and a market cross was thirty feet away and a hall with its chairs stacked against the far wall was on the other side of the road.

The man looked at the front of it, and he did not touch it, and he read it the way a person reads a name off a wrapper.

"**NETHERBECK.**"

"**That is where I am.**"

"**That is where I am, yes.**"

"**I am asking you to keep it.**"

"**No.**"

It took about four seconds and the man said it without any particular weight in it, the way a person says no to a thing that is not about anything.

"**I am not keeping it for you and I am not keeping it for whoever wrote it, and if I put it under the till it goes in a drawer and the next man has it, and if I put it in the window somebody takes it.**"

Nobody improved on it. Nobody thanked him. Marek did not offer a better version and did not say he would come back and ask a second time.

Then the man said the rest of it, unasked, and it took him about nine seconds.

"**You are the third.**"

"**Third.**"

"**Third one out of that city in nine weeks to sit on that board and ask me that. The first of them I told it was none of my business. The second of them I said I would think about it, and I have not thought about it and I am not going to. You are getting a third answer and it is the only one of the three I would give again, and I could not tell you which of the three of them was true, and I am sixty-one and that is a thing to be.**"

Marek did not ask him to remember. He did not ask him which was true, and he did not say he was the second one or the first one, and he did not say that the card was his father's, and the man at the stall was not told that and did not ask and never will.

Nobody said he was right. Nobody said the man of twenty-two was wrong to have asked. Nobody thanked either of them.

---

The hall was on the other side of the road and the woman who hires it out was about forty-four and she was carrying a folding table in by herself through a side door, and he offered to take it and she let him.

Then she asked him the question, and it took her about four seconds, and it was not the question he had come with.

"**Do you work for somebody.**"

"**Yes.**"

She put the folding table down and stood there and did not take the second half of it back and did not go inside.

"**The last three said that too.**"

"**I am sorry.**"

"**I did not ask you to be sorry. I have believed none of them, and that is not because you are the fourth. That is because a person who works for somebody in that city cannot tell me what the somebody wants, and a person who does not work for anybody in that city is going to be at my door in nine weeks as well, and you are all the same shape from where I am standing, and I am not going to be a fourth thing this town keeps.**"

It took her about nine seconds and it was the ordinary shape of a thing a person with an ordinary job says, and it was not said to be impressive and it was not a threat and it was not about him.

Nobody improved on it. Nobody thanked her. Nobody asked her what the other three had come for, and nobody told her, and about four people in that hall have since said the same shape of thing and about four have not, and the two groups have not met.

**She did not offer him the hall. She did not ask him to look at it. She was not asked, and the chairs stayed stacked against the far wall six high, and the hall was not a room anybody put forward that day.**

**And then she said the thing that was the actual sentence of the day, in about nine seconds, and it was to a stranger and she was owed nothing and nobody thanked her and she is not going to be asked to say it again.**

"**Three of you in nine weeks and I have nobody to tell that to. That is the whole of it. I am not asking you to write it down and I am not asking you to take it anywhere, because if you do that I will be the woman in that hall who said the thing to the man from the city, and I have been that twice already this year for somebody else and I am not doing it a third time.**"

Nobody wrote it down. Nobody asked her a second question. Nobody improved on it and nobody thanked her and she went in and shut the side door and the six-high stack of chairs did not move.

---

**The cost, and it is the first of four on this road in four days, and it is not his, and nobody in this case asked him about it, and both of those facts are printed.**

The man of about forty-four who lives in this city came up on that bus to mend a boiler and had told his employer on Friday that he would be back on the Thursday and would not be. He said it to nobody in particular, standing by the window on the way down, and it took him about nine seconds.

"**That is a day's pay off a week and it does not come back this year. I have told them Thursday and I am not going to say why I cannot.**"

Nobody asked him. Nobody said he was wrong to come and nobody said he was right to come, and he is not a villain and is owed nothing, and about four people on that bus noticed and about four did not, and neither group has asked the other why.

He and the man of twenty-two were on the same bus for two hours and neither of them knew the other was going to be there, and the day each of them lost is not the same day, and **the man of twenty-two lost no day at all, and the four jobs in that town on that Thursday were done and the card in date says they were done and the card carries no address.**

---

At about four the market packed up and the shop shut and the bus came round, and a man of about sixty-one was still at the shoe stall with the board out and the card was in a coat pocket and not on the board, and the hall had its chairs stacked against the far wall six high, and about nine people had eaten in a building that had been a shop.

**The card in that pocket is a piece of card about the size of a playing card. It is not a ticket and it is not a key and it opens nothing. It is not a card in a rail in a repair shop in Lattice Ward and that rail holds one card where it held two, and it is not the wall list in its plastic sleeve in that shop, and it is not the hardboard on two nails in a room off a service road four miles from a counter, and it is not the master's original in a fourth pocket with a blank fifth line, and it is not the page of paper with about eleven lines on it, and it is not the sheet of about nine lines in a room in the second of the four districts. It is a seventh thing, in a coat, in a town, and none of the six has ever been in a town.**

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.
