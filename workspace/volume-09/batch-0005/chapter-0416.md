# Chapter 416 — A Limit That Was His Own, And Two Sentences Said In One Street By Two People Who Have Not Met

The Wednesday of week one hundred and fifty-three was spent on a bus each way and on the ground in the third of four towns on that road for about three hours, and the one thing that is true of it is that **a woman of about thirty-six who runs a launderette asked him whether he was the one going to keep coming up the road with a card and he said no in about four seconds, and the no was correct and she was right about it and nobody improved on the no and nobody thanked her, and in the about nine seconds after the no he heard that the no was his own and not a rule that stops him, and he did not turn it into a task, and the thing he would have turned it into was about nine days of work in two districts in this city that a man of about thirty-four has already written his name against, and two of the four sentences of this fortnight were said in that street by two people with ordinary jobs who have not met and are not in one room, and one of them does not live in the town he was describing and is not in it.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last of them about six in the evening, and the eleven were eleven jobs in a town he has not worked in before, done in the order the card gave them, and the card is a card in date and carries no address. The word was at nine and ten and is told to nobody. **Heating: on, dearer, and four names on a lease cost more than they did, and a fortnight a cook paid for is paid for by nobody and is not resolved.** The room off that service road is five hundred and ninety-seven days old and is warm, and about four people came in on the Wednesday evening for the temperature and nobody asked them anything.

The card in the coat pocket is six hundred and one days old, which is four days more than the room is, and it has been in that pocket since the Wednesday of the fortnight before last, and a card's age does not stop because a card has been picked up. **The card in the coat pocket is not a card in a rail in a repair shop in Lattice Ward, and it is not the wall list in its plastic sleeve in that shop, and it is not the hardboard on two nails in a room off a service road, and it is not the master's original in a fourth pocket, and it is not the page of paper with about eleven lines on it, and it is not the sheet of about nine lines in a room in the second of the four districts. The six have never been in one room, and this is the first week in which one of them has crossed a line a reader can draw, and naming them apart is the whole of what the naming does.**

---

The town had a launderette and a shop and a bus shelter and a street, and there was a loading bay at the end of the street where two lorries stand on a Tuesday and one on a Friday, and on that Wednesday there was one.

Marek got off at about eleven with about three hours before the bus came round, and he did the eleven jobs in the order the card gave them, and about four of them were the same fault in four different places, and nobody in that town had seen him before and nobody asked him where he had learned any of it and nobody was thanked.

---

The woman who runs the launderette is about thirty-six. She is not a villain and is owed nothing and is not frightened of him and has a limit and states it.

She had the card in her hand because he had put it on the counter with the place-name up, the way a person puts a thing down when a town has asked them a question and the question is about them.

"**Are you the one who is going to keep coming up the road with a card.**"

"**No.**"

"**No.**"

"**No.**"

"**Right. I have had a yes off somebody with one of those and I did not believe that either, so I am not going to be hard on a no.**"

Then she said the other thing, and it was the third of the four sentences on this road, and it took her about nine seconds, and she was owed nothing and nobody thanked her.

"**There is nothing in this town anybody can put a question to. I have said that twice. The second time I said it I also asked them not to come back, and I am not telling you why I said it twice, and I am not saying why I asked them not to come back either, and you are not going to ask me, and if you do I will say the same thing I have said twice.**"

Nobody improved on it. Nobody thanked her. Nobody asked her for the reason, and nobody is going to, and the launderette stayed open and she did the two machines that were on and she did not stop talking to be helpful.

---

He was not going to ask. That was the thing he noticed about himself in about nine seconds, standing at the counter of a launderette with a piece of card on it.

**She had asked him a question and he had answered it in four seconds and the answer was his and not a rule that stopped him, and there was nothing in that launderette or in that town or in this city that would have stopped him, and in the about nine seconds after the no he worked out what he was about to do, and it was four things and all four of them were the same shape.** He was going to write down the names of the four places. He was going to make a list and keep it. He was going to find out which one of them was nearest to which and how often a thing went up the road. He was going to leave a number with somebody here and have the number ring.

**And the number would have been that number, and there is no name on it in this case, and nobody in any of the four places would have to have heard of any of the others, and it would have taken him about four minutes to arrange, and what it would have bought him was nine days of grade-two work in two districts in this city that a man of about thirty-four in an office in a third district has already written his name against in a corridor, and a fortnight after next is the fortnight after next whatever else it is.**

He did not do it. He did not say he would manage it. He did not say he would come back. He did not ask her to write anything down and she did not offer and there was nothing to offer.

**He did not turn the limit into a task. About four people on that street noticed that he did not and about four did not, and the two groups have not met and are not going to.**

---

Then he said the name, and he said it once, and it was in a mouth, and it was said to a person with an ordinary job who did not ask him why and is not going to be asked to hear it again and is not thanked.

"**That card was my father's. Evan Senn. He is dead and I do not know which part of this is his and I am not going to work it out in a launderette, and that is all I am going to say about it and I am not going to say it again.**"

She did not ask. She did not say she was sorry, because he had not said anything to be sorry about. She said, in about four seconds:

"**Then you are the third one and you have told me the most about yourself of the three and it has not helped me at all, and that is the truth and I would rather say it than be pleasant.**"

Nobody improved on it. Nobody thanked her and nobody thanked him.

---

**And the fourth of the four sentences was said in that street, about two hours later, by a man who does not live in that town and is not in it.**

There was one lorry in the bay and a man of about thirty-eight loading it who had come up the road from somewhere else and was going back down it. He was not asked anything and he was not introduced to anybody and nobody in this case asked him to speak.

He put the last thing on and came back and stood at the tailboard and said it to the same stranger, unasked, in about nine seconds.

"**The road stops at the last farm. The bus went twice a week until it could not go at all. I am one of about four people it costs. And I have now said that twice to somebody from your city and neither of them wrote it down.**"

Nobody thanked him. Nobody asked him to say it a third time. Nobody asked him what the first of the two was or to whom he said it. Nobody asked him what the other of the about four people it costs does, and about four people in that bay have since said that a lorry driver is a lorry driver and about four have said something else, and the two groups have not met.

**He is not in the town he was describing. He is not standing in it. He said a thing about a place he does not live in, to a stranger, on a street in a different town, and the town he was describing is the fourth of the four and there is nothing in it that this case can stand in.**

---

**A woman of about thirty-one was on the bus back down the road. She had come up to see somebody in that town and she was going to be on a bus at six in the morning instead. She said it to the window, unasked, in about nine words.**

"**I have lost today and I have to be up at six for it and I have not seen who I came to see, and that is a day of mine and it does not come back.**"

Nobody asked her. Nobody said she was wrong to come and nobody said she was right to come and she is owed nothing and she was not thanked, and the man of twenty-two was four rows further forward and did not hear it and has not been told it.

---

At about four the bus came round, and the launderette was still open, and the bay was empty, and the card went back into a coat pocket, and there is a place-name on one side of it and a week-number with nothing after it on the other, and it is not a ticket and it is not a key and it opens nothing.

**Four places on a road have each said one sentence to the same stranger about whether this city is going to keep asking. The four sentences are not the same sentence. The four towns have not met. Nobody has told any of the four what any of the other three said, and the two that were said in this street have not been told that the other two were asked anything at all. The man who heard all four is going home, and nobody asked him to carry them, and he is not going to.**

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.
