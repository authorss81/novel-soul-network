# Chapter 418 — A Rail With One Card In It, And Four Sentences He Is Telling Nobody

The Monday of week one hundred and fifty-four was spent on a bench in Lattice Ward until about two and at a counter until about six, and the one thing that is true of it is that **a man of twenty-two stood at the end of a counter and looked at a rail of about forty card slots with one card in it and put nothing into it and took nothing out of it, and nobody in that shop mentioned the card that is not there and nobody asked the man of about fifty-three who stands at that counter about it, and then in a warm room off a service road he worked out the only thing four days on that road have actually produced, which is that four places have each said one sentence to the same stranger and the four sentences are not the same sentence and the four places have not met and nobody told any of them what the others said, and he is telling nobody, and this was the Monday of nine days of work he agreed to in a corridor a fortnight ago and the first of the nine is gone.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last of them about six in the evening. The word was at nine and ten and is told to nobody. **Heating: on, dearer, and four names on a lease cost more than they did, and a fortnight a cook paid for is paid for by nobody and is not resolved.** The room off that service road is six hundred and two days old and is warm, and about four people came in on the Monday evening for the temperature and nobody asked them anything.

The card in the coat pocket is six hundred and six days old, which is four days more than the room is, and it has been in that pocket since the Wednesday of the fortnight before last, and a card's age does not stop because a card has been picked up, and **the rail at the end of the counter in the other building holds one card, and a card in a pocket in this city is not a card in a rail, and a card in a rail is not a card in date, and the card in date carries no address.**

---

He stood at the end of that counter at about one o'clock with his hand flat on the wood beside the rail and not on it, and he looked at the rail for about four minutes, and there was one card in about forty slots.

The man of about fifty-three was at the bench with a lamp on an arm above it and he came over at about a quarter past one and looked at the rail and then at the counter and said nothing about it.

"**You are stood at my rail like it is a queue.**"

"**It is a queue of one.**"

"**It was a queue of two.**"

"**It is a queue of one and you have known that for a fortnight.**"

"**I have.**"

Nobody asked him anything else about it. He went back to the bench. **Nobody in that shop mentioned the card that is not there and nobody asked the man of fifty-three about it, and he has not mentioned it since, and there is no slot the missing one came from and no record that one was ever there, and a rail is not a register and the absence of a thing from a rail is not an entry, and about nine people in that district could not say in a morning which of about forty slots it was in either.**

He put nothing in. He took nothing out. He did eleven jobs.

---

At about half past one a telephone on that bench rang and it was a man of about thirty-four in an office in a third district, and Marek said he would ring back, and he did ring back at about half past three, and what he said on the telephone was that Tuesday was not going to work either.

"**Right.**"

"**I am not going to be there on Tuesday.**"

"**Right.**"

"**I have not been there all week and I am not going to start on a Tuesday.**"

"**That is three days of the nine.**"

"**I know how many it is.**"

Nobody said he had better think about it. Nobody said they would find somebody else. Nobody said that it would be there next week, and nobody wrote any of it down, and the sheet of paper in that office has three of nine days marked against his name and the marks are a pen and a sheet of paper beside a laptop, and there is nowhere on it for a reason.

**He is owed nothing and he is not a villain and he is not going to be asked about the three days and he is not going to be told. Nobody was told. He did not say.**

---

At about half past six there were about nine people in a warm room off a service road, and a cook of twenty-seven was at a sink saying nothing, and in the chair nearest the door was a woman of about thirty whom nobody asked a question, nobody thanked and nobody apologised to, and the page that is hers is the eighth of eight things in a ring binder on a shelf and is unread and was not read on Monday.

Two nails in that room carried nineteen lines and nothing was added to them and nothing was removed from them and nothing was rubbed off them, and the newest of the nineteen is a lift mechanic's, eleven words, signed, about the middle, and it is the only promise on that board and it is two hundred and ninety-eight days old, and it has not been written on this week of the road and it was not written on while he was on the road and it is not being written on now.

**And the room does not depend on him being in it, and did not while he was on the road, and about four people who come for the temperature worked that out for themselves in the four days he was away and about four did not, and the two groups have not met.**

He sat down at about seven and did not say anything for about nine minutes.

Then he worked out, on his own, in order, the whole of what four days on that road had produced, and he did not say it to anybody in that room and he is not going to.

**Four places. One sentence each. In about nine seconds each, to a stranger, by somebody with an ordinary job who was owed nothing and was not thanked. The four sentences are not the same sentence. The four places have not met and about two of the four have not been told that the other two were asked anything at all. Nobody has been told what any of them said. Nothing is on a list. There is no list. There is nowhere in this city to put four sentences, and he is the only person alive who holds more than one of them, and he is not going to be on that road again for about nine weeks, and a man of about thirty-four in an office in a third district has a name against nine days of work in two districts and does not know that four of them are gone.**

Nobody asked him what he had gone up the road for. About four people in that room have an idea and about four do not, and the two groups have not met, and nobody in that room has asked him to write anything down and there is no paper on that counter.

**A card in a coat pocket is a third object in a third building. The wall list in its plastic sleeve in the repair shop is a fourth and is not that board. The hardboard is a fifth. The master's original is in a fourth pocket with a blank fifth line and a drawer that is shut. The page of paper with about eleven lines on it is a sixth, in a fourth building. The sheet of about nine lines in a room in the second of the four districts is not any of the others, and a list with four place-names on it has not been written and does not exist, and a sheet of paper in a corridor in a third district with one name against nine days is not any of the six either and is not a card in date.**

**The Exchange did not sit today. Its last sitting was the Wednesday of the week before last, forty days ago, and its next is the Wednesday of the week after next, sixteen days from today. The four figures are a book at fifty-four lines, a tin at seventy-three, a count announced thirty-two times of which twenty-seven correspond, and nineteen years of it, and none of the four is convertible into another and the difference between the book and the tin is not a number and is not printed as one anywhere.**

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.
