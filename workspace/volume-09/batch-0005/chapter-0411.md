# Chapter 411 — Nine Days Agreed To In A City He Is Going To Leave, And A Rail Holding One Card

The Monday of week one hundred and fifty-two was spent on a bench in Lattice Ward until about two and at a counter until about six, and the one thing that is true of it is that **a man of twenty-two said yes to about nine days of grade-two work in two districts of this city, in a room in a third district, to a man of about thirty-four who writes names in a list, and he gave as his reason that he was going to be here, and the reason he is going to be here is that a bus goes out on a Thursday, and neither fact is written down and neither is on anything, and the man who is going to be paid for the nine days did not ask where he was going and the man of twenty-two did not say, and a rail at the end of a counter held one card where it held two a fortnight ago and is not a register and the absence of the other one is not a record of anything.**

---

Eleven on the docket and eleven in date; eleven attended, the last of them about six in the evening. The word was at nine and ten and is told to nobody. **Heating: on, dearer, and four names on a lease cost more than they did, and a fortnight a cook paid for is paid for by nobody and is not resolved.** The room off that service road is five hundred and eighty-eight days old and is warm, and about nine people came in on the Monday evening for the temperature and none of them was asked anything.

The card in the coat pocket is five hundred and ninety-two days old, which is four days more than the room is, and it went to its rail on the Thursday of the week before that room opened, and **a card's age does not stop because a card has been picked up, so the figure is the same this afternoon as it was on the Wednesday before it, and the object is in a coat pocket and not in a rail and has been since the Wednesday.**

---

He got the call at about ten past eleven, on the shop telephone, which is a telephone that sits on the bench and is used about four times a week by people who work in that building.

The man on the other end of it said his own name and then said that he was in an office in another district and that he had a list in front of him, and that two people had gone off in his week and that he was short, and that he was asking whether the man of twenty-two could take about nine days of grade-two work in the fortnight after next, in two districts, and that it would be on the same rate as the rest of it.

"**When you say after next.**"

"**I mean the fortnight after next. Nine days across it. Two of them are in the north side and seven of them are where you are already, and I want them before the middle of the month, and I do not particularly want why.**"

"**Nine days is most of a week and a bit.**"

"**It is nine days, and it is before the middle of the month, and I did not say I was not going to.**"

---

They met at about half past one in a corridor with a window at the end of it, and there was a table with a laptop on it and a list on a piece of paper beside the laptop and the piece of paper was the one with the names on it, and about four of the names on it were people who did not work in this city.

The man of about thirty-four was not a villain and is owed nothing and has a job that consists of filling in about nine rows of a spreadsheet and being the person who asks. He did not know what the man of twenty-two was and did not ask.

"**I am not going to ask why you want nine days.**"

"**I am going to be here.**"

"**You are going to be here.**"

"**I am going to be here. That is the reason. There is nowhere on this to put a town.**"

He wrote a name against nine days on the list, and the name was the man's own and it is his own surname and not the name on a piece of card, and he wrote it in the ordinary way with a pen off a rack on the table.

Then he said the thing that this case is not going to record anywhere, which is that he had not been asked where he was going and had not offered it, and that the office had a list and no column for a destination.

"**No.**"

"**So if I do not go.**"

"**I will not be here, and you will not be able to tell me that I said I would be, because I have not said it in writing and neither have you.**"

"**That is right. I will still know.**"

**Nobody asked him to reconsider. Nobody sent him anything afterwards. The list has his name against nine days in two districts in the fortnight after next, and about nine weeks from now that name will still be on the list, and nobody in that office will remember a conversation about a bus in a corridor, and neither of them wrote down the word Thursday, and the word Thursday is not in this entry and is on nothing anywhere.**

---

Back at the bench at about two, and the rail was at the end of the counter and the man of about fifty-three was on his round of the shop and did not say anything about it.

**The rail has no order in it. It has about forty slots in it and one card in one of them and there is no index and there is no book behind it, and about nine people in that district could not say in a morning which of the forty it was in either, and there is no slot the missing one came from because a rail does not keep that, and nobody in that building has asked the man at the counter to keep one and nobody ever has.**

He looked at it for about four minutes. He put his hand on the counter beside it and not on it, which is what he did the last time as well, and he did not take anything out of it and he did not put anything into it.

"**You are stood at my rail like it is a queue.**"

"**It is a queue of one. It was a queue of two a fortnight ago, and I did not know that and now I do.**"

"**Nobody had to tell me.**"

---

He did two jobs in the afternoon and one of them was across town and took him until about half past five, and the last of the eleven was done at about five, and the word was at nine and ten all the way through it and it is told to nobody and it has been said out loud in this case to one person, once in all of it, and it is not going to be said to anybody else.

At about a quarter past six the man of fifty-three took the shutter down to about a foot and stood on the step for a moment, and he said the thing that is not asked for and is not a cost anyone had asked him to name.

"**You have been in here every day this fortnight with a hand on that counter and no job on it, and I have not asked you about that, and I am not going to, and I would rather you knew that I noticed than worked out for yourself that I did.**"

Nobody thanked him. Nobody improved on it. Nobody asked him what it had cost to be the end of a rail, because he had said something else and not that, and about four people in that district have since described the counter and about four have described the rail and the two groups have not met and are not going to.

**The hand on the card in that pocket is the hand of Evan Senn, and there is no interval for him in this case and none can be put there, because the day of anything he did is not in this case and the man who knew the day is dead and the woman who has the memory did not keep a date. He is not a device and he is not a secret and he is not the reason anything is going to happen in any of the months this case has left in it, and no document in this case confers anything on anybody because of him, and no living person in this case is related to him, and the card in that pocket is a piece of card about the size of a playing card and it is not a ticket and it is not a key and it opens nothing and there is no name on it and it is not a form.**

---

At about half past six there were about nine people in a warm room off a service road, and a cook of twenty-seven was at a sink saying nothing, and in the chair nearest the door was a woman of about thirty whom nobody asked a question, nobody thanked and nobody apologised to, and the page that is hers is the eighth of eight things in a ring binder on a shelf and is unread and was not read on Monday.

Two nails in that room carried a hardboard with nineteen lines on it and the eight ages are set out in the docket below, and the newest of the nineteen is a lift mechanic's, eleven words, signed, about the middle, and it is the only promise on that board and it is two hundred and eighty-four days old, and nothing was added to it this Monday and nothing was removed from it and nothing was rubbed off it.

The room does not depend on him being in it. It did not depend on him being in it on the four days he was not in it last week, and about four people who come for the temperature have noticed that and about four have not, and the two groups have not met.

**A card in a coat pocket is a third object in a third building. The wall list in its plastic sleeve in that shop is a fourth and is not that board. The hardboard is a fifth. The master's original is in a fourth pocket in a tool roll four feet from a rail, with a blank fifth line and a drawer that has not been opened. The page of paper with about eleven lines on it is a sixth, in a fourth building. The sheet of about nine lines in a room in the second of the four districts is not any of the others. Nine minutes of the day were spent on a list in a corridor with a name on it and that list is not any of the six either, and it is not a card, and it has no address on it, and a place-name is not on it.**

**The Exchange did not sit today. Its last sitting was the Wednesday of the fortnight before last, twenty-six days ago, and its next is the Wednesday of the week after next, two days from today, and at that sitting the book opens. The four figures are a book at fifty-three lines at the close, a tin at seventy-three, a count announced thirty-one times of which twenty-six correspond, and nineteen years of it, and none of the four is convertible into another and the difference between the book and the tin is not a number and is not printed as one anywhere.**

Nobody thanked anybody. The shutter came down at about ten.
