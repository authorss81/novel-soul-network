**917 — Wednesday of week 310, day 2058, entry 920, the two hundred and twenty-second day. Thirty-seven pounds, nine callers.** A man of about thirty-three says two things in about nine seconds: **a room somebody has set up for people can be counted, and a room with people in it cannot.** **Nobody in that room said he was wrong and nobody agreed with him, and both of those went on past the shutter.** About four people in that room have said since that they had been counting the first of those two for about four years and had not noticed. A man of about fifty-seven at that wall is asked nothing about himself. He has said since that he has been in about nine rooms in this city that had been set up for him and never once in one set up for anybody else.

**918 — Thursday of week 310, day 2059, entry 921, the two hundred and twenty-third day. Forty-two pounds, eleven callers. NINE CHAIRS AND NO BODIES.** Nine wooden chairs are carried up out of a cupboard at the bottom of that stair and set out in a row in front of that table before anybody goes up, and nobody in that room has said who sent for them, and nobody had been told which chair was theirs. **About nine people come in and about eight sit down; a man of twenty-two stands at the back by the rail for the whole evening and nobody asks him why.** People shifted along that row. **At the shutter one chair is standing empty and nobody in that room could have said which of the nine it was, and nobody in that room has said.** About four have said since that they counted the chairs before they counted the people. **The space on that form has something in it and nobody in that room saw it put there and nobody has said who.**

**919 — Friday of week 310, day 2060, entry 922, the two hundred and twenty-fourth day. Forty-four pounds, eight callers. THE SENTENCE IS ON THE WALL.** It has been there since before the Monday of that week and nobody in that room wrote it there, and **nobody in this city has said who put it there and nobody has taken it down on any of the ten days of this movement.** About four people have said since that nobody had asked them. One of them has written something on that wall herself, in about four years, on a day when that room was empty, and has not been able to say whether those words are hers. **A man of about thirty-three finds that the words on that wall are not in the order she said them in. Nobody in that room took him up and nobody put him down.**

**920 — Monday of week 311, day 2063, entry 923, the two hundred and twenty-fifth day. Forty-one pounds, ten callers. THE THING IS GONE OUT OF THAT ROOM.** The nine chairs are still in that room in the same gaps and nobody has said which one is not used. **The key a woman of about sixty-two held for about four years is not in that room and nobody in that room said where it went and she has said nothing about it to anybody.** **Nobody in that room thanked her and nobody asked her to stay, and about four people in that room have said since that they would have asked her to stay**, and one of the four has said that asking her to stay would have meant asking her to explain. **Nobody in that room said that the nine rows on that sheet had been dealt with and nobody said that they had not been dealt with either, and this page does not say which of the two is a true one.**

**AND THE FIVE THINGS A LATER PASS MUST CARRY FORWARD FROM THESE TEN.** **The fifth column is empty on all nine rows and was empty when this movement opened.** **The sentence about what a sheet is for is on a wall in that room and nobody has said who wrote it.** **The space for a date on a form was full by the Thursday of week 310 and nobody saw it filled.** **Nine chairs with no names on them, one of them unused and unidentifiable.** **A woman of about sixty-two, not thanked and not asked to stay.**

# LIVE — MOVEMENT V, CHAPTERS 921 TO 930, SUMMARIZED

**These ten summaries were written after the last measurement of the ten files at `workspace/volume-18/batch-0005/` and not before it. The measure of record is `workspace/volume-18/batch-0005/SUMMARY.md`; this file carries no measurements of its own. One sitting was held on one of these ten days and the book did not open at it, and no count was said aloud in any room in this city on any of the other nine.**

**921 — Monday of week 312, day 2070, entry 924, the two hundred and twenty-sixth day of this stretch. Forty pounds, nine callers. THE SPACE ON THE FORM IS EMPTY AGAIN.** It had something written in it on the Friday before and at about seven on that Monday it was empty, and nobody in that room saw the thing taken out of it and nobody in this city has said who emptied it. **The woman of about forty-three said into somebody's face that somebody has been at that space and that she is not going to sit there and work out who, and nobody said anything back.** About four people in that room have said since that they were not looking at it at about seven and had other things to be doing at about seven on a Monday. A man of twenty-two has said since that the part of that Monday he has gone back to is not the empty space and is that nobody in that room asked her a single question. **The nine chairs are in that room in the same gaps and nobody has said which one is not used, and the sentence on the plaster above the rail has not been taken down and nobody has said who put it there.**

**922 — Tuesday of week 312, day 2071, entry 925, the two hundred and twenty-seventh day. Forty-two pounds, eight callers. ONE OF THE NINE IS NOT ONE OF THE USUAL ONES.** Nobody in that room had been told that anybody was coming and there is nothing in that room by which anybody could have been told: there is no list in it, there is no book kept of who comes into it, and the door is on a latch anybody coming up that stair can put up from the landing. **She said one thing into one person's face at about nine o'clock, quietly, and nobody else heard all of it, and nobody in that room wrote it down and nobody has written it down since. Nothing about that Tuesday evening reached the Wednesday**, and about four people in that room noticed that and cannot work out whether anybody decided not to bring it up or whether there was nothing to bring.

**923 — Wednesday of week 312, day 2072, entry 926, the two hundred and twenty-eighth day. Forty-three pounds, ten callers. THE SIXTY-SEVENTH SITTING, AND THE BOOK SHUT.** Ten names and ten dates went onto that Wednesday. **The count was said once at about half past six into the face of that room: seventy-two, of which sixty-seven correspond. Nobody said anything for about nine seconds afterwards. At about half past seven the woman who holds that room put her hand flat on the book in the green binding and did not open it and took her hand off it again, and that book stood at sixty-nine lines when that room opened and stood at sixty-nine lines when the shutter came down.** The tin beside it stood at seventy-three with its lid down and was not opened at any part of the sitting. **Nobody in that room was given any reason and nobody asked for one, and about four people in that room have said since that they had a reason ready and did not use it.** Nobody in that room said anything about the oldest of those nine dates and nobody said anything about it not standing about four years behind. **A man of twenty-two said at about eight o'clock that nobody has ever put anything in that fifth column and that he is not going to be the first one, and nobody in that room took that up.**

**924 — Thursday of week 312, day 2073, entry 927, the two hundred and twenty-ninth day. Thirty-nine pounds, nine callers. THE ORDINARY DAY AFTER A SITTING.** About nine people were in that room and about nine had been in it on the Wednesday, **and about four of the ones who had been there on the Wednesday were not there on the Thursday, and nobody in that room said why any one of those four was not there and nobody asked any one of the four who was there why the others had not come.** One of those four has said since that she does not know whether she is expected and that nobody has said to her that she is and that she has not asked. About four people in that room have said since that they had each assumed for about four years that they knew when the next one was. **Nobody in that room can say when the next one is, and there is nothing in that room that says.**

**925 — Sunday of week 312, day 2076, entry 928, the two hundred and thirtieth day, AND THE ONE SUNDAY OF THIS VOLUME. Forty-two pounds, four callers, and the shutter came down at about two.** A room at the back of a hall off a road on the other side of the city from the line, used on that day of the week and on no other day and for about four years, with tables against two of its walls and nothing on any of them and no list of anybody on any wall of it. **Nobody in that city keeps a list of who comes into that room and nobody has ever sent word to anybody that a person was coming.** One of the nine came in at about half past twelve and nobody in that room had known, and she had nothing with her and she stayed until that room was emptied. **At about a quarter to two she said one thing into somebody's face and what she said was a question, and nobody in that room answered it. Nobody in that room asked her one question about why she had come, and about four people in that room have said since that they would have asked, and one of those four has said that asking would have meant making her answer for the room.** Nothing that was said in that room has been entered anywhere since. A man of twenty-two was not in that room and heard about that Sunday about a week afterwards from somebody who has not said who.

**926 — Monday of week 313, day 2077, entry 929, the two hundred and thirty-first day. Thirty-seven pounds, nine callers. WHAT A SUNDAY DOES TO A ROOM, SAID BY SOMEBODY WHO WAS NOT IN IT.** At about half past seven the man of about thirty-three said into the face of the woman of about forty-three that it takes a room a week to come back. **Nobody answered him and nobody took it up. He was not in that room off the road on that Sunday, he does not know what happened in it, he has said that he would have gone if he had been asked and that nobody asked him, and nobody in that room asked him where it came from.** About four people in that room who were in the other room on that Sunday have said since that nobody has asked them what was said in it. **The sheet with four columns was on its own table at about seven and at about nine with the fifth heading standing over nothing on all nine of its rows, and nobody in that room said one word about it.**

**927 — Tuesday of week 313, day 2078, entry 930, the two hundred and thirty-second day. Forty-three pounds, ten callers. NOBODY HAS ASKED HER WHY SHE CAME, AND A SECOND PERSON CAME.** Nobody in that room has asked the one who came on the Sunday one question about why she came, and about four people in that room have said since that they would have asked; one of those four has said that she would have asked it in the doorway before the other had sat down, and that she has thought about the doorway since. **A woman of about twenty-nine, who is a relative of one of the nine and is not on that sheet, was in that room, and she had been in it once before, on the Tuesday of the week before, and nobody in that room had been told she was coming on either occasion.** She said one thing into the face of the woman of about forty-three at about half past seven and it was about that sheet and nobody has said since what it was. **Nobody in that room has said that the nine rows on that sheet have been dealt with and nobody has said that they have not been dealt with either.**

**928 — Wednesday of week 313, day 2079, entry 931, the two hundred and thirty-third day. Forty-two pounds, eight callers. NOT A SITTING. NINE DATES DOWN A PAGE IN ABOUT NINE SECONDS.** A man of about thirty-three stood up at about eight o'clock and read nine dates out of a page in his own handwriting, copied twice out of a file in a records room in a second district that is a landlord and a filing system, and **the fourth of them was earlier than the third and the eighth of them was earlier than the seventh.** That took about nine seconds and he put the page face down and did not say one word about what it meant. **Nobody in that room said that he was wrong and nobody said that the nine dates run in order, nobody asked him what he thought it meant and he has said since that he does not know, and not one of about nine people in that room said what anybody should now do differently on account of those nine dates.** About four of them have said since that they understood all of it and have not been able to say what it was for. **A man of twenty-two has said since that he had read those nine dates before that Wednesday and had got them out of order himself about a month before and had said nothing to anybody then either, and that he does not know whether that makes two people who have done it or one person who has done it twice.**

**929 — Thursday of week 313, day 2080, entry 932, the two hundred and thirty-fourth day. Forty-four pounds, eleven callers. TWO KINDS OF ROOM, AND THE SAME TWO THINGS SAID AGAIN.** The room above the line has nine chairs standing in a row that anybody can go up that stair and count. **The room off that road that was used on the Sunday has nothing set out in it and no chairs in it at all, and anybody can go in there and count the people in it, and nobody counted the people in that other room on that Sunday and nobody in this city has said since how many were in it.** About four people in that room were in a position to count them and did not and cannot account for that. At about half past seven the man of about thirty-three said the two things again in about nine seconds, and nobody contradicted him and nobody agreed with him, and about four people looked at the row of chairs while he was saying it. **He has said since that he said it the second time because he had worked out in the week before that he had only ever said it the once.** About four of those people have said since that they have all been counting the first of those two for about four years; one of them has said that a column on a page of her own was that column, and a second of them has said that the second of the two can be counted by anybody prepared to be the one who counts, and that nobody in this city is prepared and that this is the part that took four years.

**930 — Friday of week 313, day 2081, entry 933, the two hundred and thirty-fifth day. Forty-two pounds, nine callers. NOTHING WAS DECIDED.** **The ninth chair was against that wall with its back to the whole of that room and it had been against that wall on every one of the ten days of this movement and it did not move at any hour of that Friday, and nobody in that room has said how long it has been there and nobody in this city has said who put it there.** The sheet with four columns was on its own table from about seven and it will be on that table on the next ordinary morning with the fifth heading standing over nothing on all nine of its rows. **Nobody in that room said where a key had gone, nobody asked the person who came on the Sunday one question about why she came, and nobody said who filled a space on a form or who emptied it again.** Nobody in that room said that the nine rows on that sheet had been dealt with and nobody said that they had not. **This page names the place behind that woman's chair once and puts no figure on it, and no other page of this movement names it.**

**AND THE SIX THINGS A LATER PASS MUST CARRY FORWARD FROM THESE TEN.** **The fifth column is empty on all nine rows and was empty when this movement opened.** **One of the nine came to a room on a Sunday, unasked, said a question, and was not answered, and nothing said there has been entered anywhere.** **A woman of about twenty-nine, a relative of one of the nine and not on the sheet, came into that room twice and nobody asked her why either time.** **A man of about thirty-three said what a Sunday does to a room without having been in it, and read nine dates out of order without being asked, and could use neither.** **Nothing was decided, and nobody thanked anybody and nobody forgave anybody, and the shutter came down at about ten on nine of the ten days and at about two on the Sunday.** **The place behind the woman's chair is named on Chapter 930 of this movement and carries no figure anywhere in it.**
