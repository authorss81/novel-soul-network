**1096**, Wednesday, day 2387, entry 1099, counter 341, seventy-two pounds, fourteen callers. **The man who has been asking for nine weeks asks the last person he had not asked, gets an answer, and four people in that room each say out loud which of the answers already given that week they think it was, and all four pick a different one and none picks the one that was given; he says out loud that eight answers to one question is a rhyme and not a record, a woman tells him there were nine of them, and Marek tells him that what he has is not nothing and then stops halfway again. **1098**, Friday, day 2389, entry 1101, counter 343, seventy-nine pounds, twelve callers. **A woman of about forty-five tells Marek that a man at a counter in another district has been talking about that shed and cannot repeat a word of it when he asks her once; the only parts about four people in that shop can get are a kitchen, and a woman, and about nine people not saying anything; and the man of about thirty comes in for a bulb at about half past five and asks her three questions in about four seconds.**
