# Character State

Updated after every batch. Tracks where each character is at the end of the last written chapter, what they want, and what they will refuse to do. Canon detail lives in `state/continuity.md`; this file tracks movement.

## Marek Senn — protagonist

**At the end of Volume 01:** twenty-two, third-year, **not a licence holder**, four documents, and a narrow one. He performed a working non-hinged boundary release on the Tuesday of week 14 in room one of the practical building, in front of a panel, with four seconds in which a first-year's forearm was cut by a steel rail and in which he could not stop the practice by himself. **The original transcript line stands and is not amended: *not completed. Fourth beat absent. Performance dependent on external cue.* The Solo Seal is not granted and was never asked for.** He holds a **provisional Working Share** with seven headings, four named partners who may end it by word, a term, a review, and a fourth condition that he may not hold a practice alone on Institute equipment, on Institute premises, or under Institute insurance, at any time, unlimited by hours. He holds a **one-term conditional scholarship extension** on six conditions, and he minuted the price of the fifth one out loud in a room rather than let anybody else say it.

**Physical state:** bad right shoulder from a jammed gate in Nine Bridges, still bad, never re-injured. Split thumbnail. Pencil behind one ear. Work gloves under his school jacket. **He went grey in a corridor at ten past ten on the Tuesday morning and was told to sit down and would not.** A boy of twenty's terror arrived in him on the Tuesday and did not lift for hours. **He had a band of pain behind both eyes from the first four minutes of a scored window and finished anyway.** He stands on a stone landing for four hours at a time, and in about two weeks' time he will be off nights.

**Learned in Movement V, in his own words:**
- A key is a thing you are given. I would be making one. *(Ch 41, on the grey card, and it is the reason the card is a card and not a rule.)*
- Four people who have never heard each other say the same thing is the plan. A card is not four people. A card is me, four times. *(Ch 41)*
- I do not want to see what any of them does before Tuesday, and if I see it I will know what it is supposed to look like, and then I will be a room with a man at the front of it. *(Ch 42)*
- I broke rule three and I broke it in a hurry to get to somebody else, which is the only way I have ever broken it. *(Ch 43, on a hillside, with eleven men in earpieces.)*
- You have put a list on a wall and I heard about it in four days. *(Ch 50, Nkechi Osei, and he writes none of it down.)*
- Nobody came. *(Ch 50, the ledger, Monday at nine.)*
- Rule 1 does not stop this. Nothing on this page stops a fella saying something in a doorway, and there is not going to be one, and everything else in this book is what is left. *(Ch 50, the inside cover of the maintenance ledger, and the only line on the page that was entirely his.)*

**Wants now, in order:** to be a person whose job it is to look at a stranger's wrist, which is the only thing anybody in this case has ever needed and which does not exist as a job in this city; **and then, newly, to work out who takes the name out for the people who will sit in rooms that cost them one, which is the question Talia Venn asked him in a lay-by and which he cannot answer by having a room.**

**Will refuse:** to use a share he has not been given; to put a name in a building so that somebody can be found later; **to write a fifth line on a card in a boy's handwriting in a boy's own words;** to file a records request that would make ninety households findable; to let a room become a room he was in; to be thanked for a list, a bag of washers, a tap, or a decision somebody else made.

**Cannot yet do:** perform the fourth beat on demand; hold a line for more than a minute and forty-one seconds; stop a practice he is inside of; take a count out of a person; find a public room; ask a stranger for something he cannot explain in one sentence; be in a room without being the word in it; stop being the person a street telephones; **and — the newest thing — he cannot get a word into the air in a shape that will move a room, and the only reason he could see the shape of the fault was the same reason he could not do the third beat.**

**Flaw, named and NOT resolved:** **he is a key-holder by reputation, arrived at without a certificate, with no register on which the fact could be struck off** — and on the Tuesday the thing that made him the fourth thing in the room was the thing that made the rail go across a girl's arm, and he has not been asked about it by anybody and has not been able to write it down. **The back wall is the most expensive seat in the building, and in Chapter 50 he stands at a bench that is against a wall, with his back to it and his face to the door, and notices that it is the first time the back wall and the work have been the same place.**

## Talia Venn — primary romance, consent advocate

**At the end of Volume 01:** twenty-four, resonance nurse on the Saltmarket route. **She has a request with a reference number on it that asks her for every page she has written since the eighth week, and about two thirds of what it asks for is other people's, and she is not going to hand a man in an office two thirds of other people's. She has to answer it in about a fortnight.** The plate from a man's wrist is still in the second drawer of a trolley in her vehicle and it is not going back on anybody. She refused the Office's recommendation a second time, in a room, in her own words, and the chair recorded hers as the strongest reason in the building.

**What she did in the last week, in her own terms, and none of it was a reward:**
- She **shut the fifth room in one word** — *Then it is shut* — and refused to let the fourteen pounds be the reason, because *that is how a rule turns into a price*, and put the real reason out loud: a room that costs your name is a room you can have, and in four years there will be two thousand people in rooms that cost their names, and every one of them will have been told by somebody kind that it was the price of the room. **And then she asked the question nobody had asked: *Who would take it out for them.*** The answer is nobody and she does not know how to fix it and neither does he.
- She **argued them into separate workrooms** as a professional, in ninety seconds, in a car park: the clinic and the shop are four miles apart and are going to stay four miles apart, because a room with a door and both of them in it is a room where the door is the reason people come, and every person who came through it would be finding them.
- She agreed **Thursdays**, and the whole of it is *we had an hour*, and there is no form and nothing in either of their records, and if he makes it into anything else she will not come.
- She **did not kiss him**, and said why in one sentence so he would not spend a fortnight on it: she would be doing it to end a day, and she has watched somebody do that to somebody else this term and watched what it cost, and she is not going to be the second one of us.
- She **argued with him in front of nine people about a first-year's forearm** on the Tuesday and neither of them apologised and neither of them went and stood somewhere else, and she has minuted that she is not giving it back and is not going to be a thing that happens to him.

**Want:** a standard that survives fear, fatigue, a person who cannot explain what a default means, and a district office. **She is not available, has not softened, is not a rescue, is not a deputy, and is not a romantic payoff. The Chapter 7 page stays closed and the Chapter 33 page is not reopened.**

## Tam Arrick — the volume's first cost

**What he did in the last week, and none of it was forgiveness.** He came to a room on a Tuesday and held a line after the practice had gone out of it, on purpose, having been asked and having decided not to be the thing that let go, and a voice came along that line saying *I am here* and it would have worked. He said that the registrar will promise him the other two fingers and not the middle one and that he is not going to be told about it by anybody. He said he held the line and does not know what it was. **On the Saturday of week 14 he told Marek to leave his name in every piece of paper in the case, because a boy in a file is the only reason the department has a boy in it instead of a kit — and on the condition that the fifth line of the copied card is in his handwriting and not Marek's, and Marek did it wrong first, in pencil, with a full stop, and was told to leave the full stop off and that nobody would ever know that either.** He will be telephoned by a department in about six weeks and he will not be able to tell whether they already know about him, and he chose that, and he was told the price out loud in a room the day before he chose it.

**COLUMN TWO IS WRONG. THE HAND IS GOING TO BE TOLD TO DO THIS, AND I AM THE ONE WHO IS GOING TO LET IT, AND I AM NOT TELLING ANYBODY FOR A FORTNIGHT.** Written in biro on the Saturday of week 13. **Asha Reed knows and has carried it for two days and did not say it. ## Hollis Pratt — the limit of institutional authority

## Oren Vey — the temptation, and the volume's best argument
