### Who these people are, in the form a later volume has to quote, and the roll-up of what the three closed volumes did to them

**Marek Senn, 22.** Third-year at the Nacre Institute of Applied Practice in Volume 01, night repairer at Civic Spine 7; by the end of Volume 02 four days a week on grade two for a district service and two Fridays a week on a release callout at grade three. **No Solo Seal and never one — the transcript line stands as written and the panel has no power to grant it.** The transcript line is *not completed. Fourth beat absent. Performance dependent on external cue.* A provisional Working Share with seven headings, no word *scope*, four named partners who may end it by word, one term, a review, and a fourth condition. **Volume 01's fourth condition was that he may not hold a practice alone on Institute equipment, on Institute premises, or under the Institute's insurance, at any time, unlimited by hours. Volume 03 changed it and Volume 04 inherits the Volume 03 wording, and Chapter 167 is the authority for the wording: *There is a condition on my share. I may not demonstrate, teach, assess or assist at any practice in this department, on its premises, under its insurance, at any time, unlimited by hours, and the department may put four names in front of any of them.* It is *this* department and not *that* department, and a Movement III review pass corrected two chapters that had drifted.** The condition stops him doing the thing and does not stop a member of the public asking him about it in a room with a microphone, and there is still no mechanism in this city that can tell the difference. **A rule about who may be asked is a rule about who is findable, and no such rule may be invented.** He takes responsibility before being asked and hides the cost; his need is to accept chosen interdependence without turning it into ownership. He is in a corridor in most of Volume 04 and solves nothing alone.

**The word.** Nine, ten and eleven a minute, uncorrectable, origin asserted in a load book in week twenty and never dramatised, **told to nobody.** He said it out loud once in front of four hundred and six people, was not believed, and has said he will not say it twice. The consequence has changed form three times and its present form is a work failure, not a comfort: he has talked over three people in a fortnight and failed to hear one, and those are the same thing.

**The master.** A corner of card about two inches by three, a photocopy of a photocopy, a fifth line in a hand that got smaller towards the end. **It is in the inside pocket of a jacket on a chair in the repair shop and the decision about it is unmade after a term and a half. The original is in the fourth pocket of the tool roll with the fifth line still blank and nobody has ever asked him to fill it in.** The drawer under the bench holds five things, four of them other people's sentences, and one of them a letter whose own last line says it should not be produced without that office being asked first, **and there is no proceeding.**

**Talia Venn, 24.** Resonance nurse at a mobile public clinic, part-time student for a civic-advocate certificate, primary romance and the volume's most exacting critic. **Chosen proximity and separate workrooms, four miles apart, and the separate workrooms are a professional refusal and not a romance.** Milestone 1 was spent in Chapter 54 of Volume 02 as friction with receipts; **Series milestone 3, trust under exposure, was spent in Chapter 135 under pressure, with no declaration, no scene and no rescue, and neither of them says one word about it afterwards. It may not be spent again.** The bench is not reopened, Chapter 102's Tuesday is not revisited, the Chapter 7 page stays closed. **Milestone 4, *public and private friction*, belongs to Volumes 05 and 06 and nothing in Volume 04 may pre-empt it, and Chapter 160 does not touch the relationship at all.** She refuses a plural and a category, and her request for her own pages has a reference number and is answered in about a fortnight, two thirds of it other people's, and she will not produce it.

**Rafi Pell.** Roommate, courier, the first person who would say yes, and he says yes before he understands the boundary. He counts to two out loud, visibly, in a kitchen, in a market in front of forty people, and in a repair shop, and has said out loud that the habit does not get an exemption for a shop either. **He writes a counterpoint practice of his own with no certificate and two named people who can end it and nobody took a vote about him.** The word *variant* is not forgiven and he is not to be put back in a room.

**Tam Arrick, 20.** Third-year student, night work at a Saltmarket kitchen, the classmate Marek saves in Chapter 3, **the volume's first cost and its most exacting critic.** Two cut tendons; the middle finger has not moved since the week before the term began; three months of lost kitchen wages; a written non-hinged recovery plan in his own hand with a prognosis that does not include getting the kitchen back for a while. **He tells Marek to stop speaking for him, and by Chapter 50 he holds his own boundary release on his own hand, and none of it is ever reset.**

**Sera Quill.** Lead repairer at Civic Spine 7 and Marek's mentor relationship, and **a woman of forty-five and the braid is hers.** She teaches limits while he repeatedly tries to exceed them. She refused twice in the same tone and has never said the name aloud. She took custody of the 4-19 cage, lifted nothing, wrote her warning into the margin of the first page of the ledger on the back of a form gummed in facing outwards, and says out loud that a padlock and a woman's word is not good enough and has written it down anyway. The relay cabinet label reads **KIT 4-19 — DO NOT REISSUE** with no name and no date on it.

**Davan Rusk.** Practicum instructor, Threadline-certified, twenty-two years in the department, **the key-holder for the missing fourth beat by position and by belief, and not a villain and must never become one.** By the end of Volume 01 he is a reluctant witness who has to admit that his own training produced the cue he supplied.

**Deputy Anneke Roos.** The institutional squeeze. **Hollis Pratt**, the limit of institutional authority. **Oren Vey**, the temptation and the volume's best argument, never defeated, never the author, owed nothing further after Volume 02.

**Leo Marr, 35.** Appears in Volume 03. **She has published eleven names of her own people in nine words to prove the group was used, and she is owed a limited emergency notice and nothing else and is not to be asked twice.** She agreed the notice and refused institutional membership in nine words, said once, and put her own name on a piece of paper in her own hand on purpose, and not a category. **She has not cut the wire, is not the author of anything, is not a villain, is not defeated, does not join anything, has still never used the word, and is not a name in the fourth place on a district form.**

**Iven Sore, 58.** Continuity Office. In Volume 04 Movement I he answers three questions with three smaller questions, gives a count — six lines relate to the binder, two of about nine hundred have the hand column filled in, one of those two has an author still in this city — **refuses to say which and why, and does not agree to anything.**

**Asha Reed.** First-year student, organises a low-cost practice study group, and is the volume's most likely institution and must not be softened into a deputy. **Her sentence has been said seven times and is never said again.**

**Sunniva Bellhouse, 58.** Printer, a shop four feet wide in the Quiet Quarter, nineteen years. **Not a liar and not a villain. She is not in Leo Marr's document and has not been asked and would not say.** A boy of nineteen on her till.

**Petar Vance.** Threadline field service engineer, nineteen years, holds the second holder of a box number and a four-letter post town, gave one fact — about nine hundred plates, four buildings, a room list that is not his — and said that if anybody writes it down he has not said it and he will be able to deny it.

**Marta Selby.** The carrier in Chapter 136. Told the cost before it started, did not want it, objected before she agreed, **and stopped it on the sixth of the seven headings because the sixth came out of her as a road and not as a fact. Her round was re-cut on the Monday of week forty and she is not going to do it again for him, for a district, or for a woman in Crown Terrace with a clipboard. She may not be asked again and may not be told she was right in a way that costs her anything.**
