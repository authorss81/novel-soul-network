Split thumbnail on the hand he braces frames with; pencil behind one ear; work gloves under his school jacket. **Wants now, in order:** the Wednesday meeting at the Exchange, where he has no vote and four minutes; and the yard on the Meridian, where a card has been on a wall for thirty-one years and nobody has walked in. Underneath those: to know whether the number on the wall is a thread or a mistake. **Underneath that, unchanged: not to be believed, but to be in a room where a question is allowed to stay open — except that he has now discovered the second thing, which is that being right makes the people near him findable, and that this cost is paid by other people.**

**Learned this batch, in his own words:**

- He turned his head at the fourth place too, on a stairwell, on his own, with no witnesses, and my mouth goes to the word before I do. (Ch 22)

- The only tool in this city for finding the fifty houses is a form, and a form is the least private thing in any building, and a woman in a cardigan told me that on the Monday and I have written it down three times. (Ch 23)
- The demonstration requirement is not a technique I have to invent. It is the exact thing that five people refused in a room, and I have got it out of other people's mouths and not out of mine, because a room with me in it is a room where the man in it is the thing being said. (Ch 29)

**Will refuse:** to use a share he has not been given; to accept a transcript that requires a false cause; to let Tam's recovery plan be written for him; to ask a person twice for the same thing; to improve on a no; to be thanked in a corridor; to put a person's name on a page for the sake of the page; **to take the noticeboard sheet down, having been told the price of it and written the price down.**

**Cannot yet do:** hold a line alone; perform the fourth beat; reproduce his four seconds; do the thing five people did in a market; keep his mouth out of the fourth place; ask a stranger for something he cannot explain in one sentence; be in a room without being the word in it.

**Flaw, in operation, now named by four people and by him:** he is the key-holder. **And in Chapter 29 he worked out that a room with him in it is a room where the man in it is the thing being said, which is a second and worse version of the same flaw, and he is not allowed to fix it and has only decided to stop causing it.**
