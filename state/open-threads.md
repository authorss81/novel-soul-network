# Open Threads

These are the initial author promises. They are not resolved until the corresponding chapters and volumes pay them off.

1. **The missing beat:** Threadline’s first training kit leaves out a consent boundary. The first batch must establish the concrete technique, the affected person, and the cost of the opening rescue.
2. **The Hinge:** Someone is making learners depend on a hidden key-holder. The first clues must point toward Threadline without revealing Iona too early.
3. **Iona and the Crown Clause:** Iona’s responsibility for the Marrow Falls amendment is the central mystery. Seed her presence through records, former colleagues, and the shape of the missing rule.
4. **Marek’s counterpoint scar:** His childhood accident explains why he can detect unclosed return paths. The explanation must be earned across later volumes, not used as a chosen-one reveal.
5. **Marek and Talia:** Talia’s correction of an unauthorized share is the first relationship test. Their romantic arc must remain slow, contested, and separate from public office.
6. **The public institution:** The story must build from a First Knot to a Practice Commons and finally a federated Civic Commons. The ending cannot be a private victory.
7. **Evan Senn:** His missing history and deliberate self-removal remain a middle-volume family mystery; he does not return or leave a magical inheritance.
8. **The unresolved consequence:** A successful hinge rescue may leave someone dependent. The team must show recovery, refusal, and repair rather than a clean reset.
