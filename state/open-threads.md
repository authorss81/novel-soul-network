# Open Threads

These are the initial author promises. They are not resolved until the corresponding chapters and volumes pay them off.

1. **The missing beat:** The first brace has a missing fourth boundary-release beat; deleting that performed check makes the instructor’s substituted cue the learner’s only trusted completion. The first batch must establish the concrete rescue, the affected person, and the cost of the opening rescue.
2. **Solo Seal and scholarship:** Volume 01 must make the exam choice concrete. Marek does not earn the seal; he passes a separate witnessed assessment, receives a provisional Working Share, and receives a one-term conditional scholarship extension.
3. **The Hinge:** Someone is making learners depend on a hidden key-holder. Threadline assembled and sold the opening kit, but its unnamed upstream licensor—not Threadline as originator—must carry the first supply-chain question without revealing Iona too early.
4. **Iona and the Crown Clause:** Iona inserted an unauthorized amendment into a working copy of the Civic Compact’s emergency protocol during the Marrow Falls outage. The original public Compact required a Return Path and did not permit silence as agreement. Seed Iona through records, former colleagues, and the shape of the missing rule.
5. **Marek’s counterpoint scar:** His childhood accident explains why he can detect unclosed return paths. The explanation must be earned across later volumes, not used as a chosen-one reveal.
6. **Marek and Talia:** Talia’s correction of an unauthorized share is the first relationship test. Their romantic arc must remain slow, contested, and separate from public office.
7. **The public institution:** The story must build from a First Knot to a Practice Commons and finally a federated Civic Commons. The ending cannot be a private victory.
8. **Evan Senn:** His missing history and deliberate self-removal remain a middle-volume family mystery; he does not return or leave a magical inheritance.
9. **The unresolved consequence:** A successful hinge rescue may leave someone dependent. The team must show recovery, refusal, and repair rather than a clean reset.
