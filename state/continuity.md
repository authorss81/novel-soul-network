### Volume 01 — *The Missing Beat*, Chapters 1–50, and what later volumes inherit from it

The rescue brace in Threadline's kit revision 4-19 has its fourth boundary-release beat deleted and replaced by the instructor's spoken cue, and it has only ever worked because everyone in the room was taught to wait for that voice. **That definition of a hinge is canon and is still the definition: a hinge is not merely a missing boundary label, it deletes the boundary-release beat that should apply the limit and complete the practice and installs a key-holder's cue as the learner's expected replacement.** The volume's question was not whether Marek could pass an exam but whether a practice can be finished by the person doing it, and the answer was delivered by other people refusing to wait: Tam Arrick telling him to stop speaking for him and writing his own recovery plan, Nkechi Osei telling him a list on a wall is not the thing she asked for, Yvette Kohl refusing the category, a records clerk drawing a ruled box headed UPSTREAM LICENSOR with nothing in it because the co-operative will not invent one.

Load-bearing and still true: **Marek never receives the Solo Seal**, and the seal line stays as written — *not completed. Fourth beat absent. Performance dependent on external cue.* The provisional Working Share is granted in Chapter 49 and never earlier, with seven headings, four named partners who may end it by word, and a fourth condition. **Tam Arrick's forearm injury, his lost kitchen wages and his slow recovery are permanent costs and are never reset. The Institute's manual is still 3-08 and nothing in any volume repairs the paperwork. The Crown Clause is never named in prose. Nell Senn does not appear and is not written about.** Threadline is the contracted assembler and seller and is not the author. The three accounts of one morning — the Institute's, the Choir of Practice's, the Quiet House's — do not match, none of them is wrong, and there is no office in this city whose job it is to reconcile them. **A rule that governs a page cannot govern a mouth** is the volume's last finding and the hand-off, and it is Rule 1 in the maintenance ledger, copied from Bea Nunn's board at the Exchange with the credit on it.

### Volume 02 — *Threadline*, Chapters 51–100, and what later volumes inherit from it

He is offered a wage, a training certificate and an address by the company that sold the kit, and has to work out which of the three he is allowed to want. **The certificate is a door for a twenty-year-old whose middle finger has not moved, and that is the volume's moral centre and it is never said out loud in those terms.** Threadline's advantage is not concealment: a standardized technique with a trained holder in it produces fewer reportable accidents, and a city that has just been frightened by a hinge wants fewer accidents. **Threadline is not defeated in this volume and must not be in any volume** — it is shown to be a conduit, made answerable on a narrow set of facts, and left standing as a legal and political power.

Load-bearing and still true: the local lab and Threadline's relevant supply records are exposed and the kits are recalled, **and the recall is a company doing the right thing for the wrong reason, and Marek is not the reason for it and does not get the credit.** Rafi Pell recovers with a counterpoint routine he wrote himself, which has no certificate and two named people who can end it, and joins a worker co-operative. **Marek keeps the job and loses the argument, and the job is the thing that makes him a key-holder on a company's rota with a supervisor who can confirm his releases, and he takes it anyway.** The fifth room stays shut, and the question of who takes the name out for the people who will sit in rooms that cost them one is answered with **nobody, and a list of the four hundred people who will be told by somebody kind that it was the price of the room.** Sera Quill's maintenance work, the cage she has taken custody of and lifted nothing from, and the printed label **KIT 4-19 — DO NOT REISSUE** with no name and no date on it. The power state at the end of the volume is Stage 2 Counterpoint with a costly relay and a permanent sensitivity, and **the demonstration in Chapter 97 fails and he is the reason.** Milestone 1 was spent in Chapter 54 and Volume 02's milestone 3 in Chapter 99 in nine words.
