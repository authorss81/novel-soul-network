## 2. The load-book entry that was dated to a day the book had already spent

`chapter-0083.md:155` read `*86. Saturday of week 22…*`. The chapter's scenes are the **Wednesday** of week 22 (`chapter-0083.md:9`), the batch's own day map says Wednesday, `state/current.md` says `86 Wed`, and Saturday of week 22 is entry 89 in Chapter 86. The sentence above the entry agreed with the entry — *He wrote the entry on the Saturday morning* — and the entry's own body was written for a Wednesday night: *The Wednesday came and went*, *a prediction, made on a Wednesday night*, *it took me until half past nine tonight*.

**The entry is the Wednesday of week 22 and is written on the Wednesday night, and the frame sentence now says so.** That has one consequence inside the entry, which had been counting from the Tuesday to a Saturday: *I have now had three days of not ringing him*, *three days of not asking anybody*, *it is about three days*, *a man who waited, on purpose, for three days* and *prove the three days were for any reason*. **From the Tuesday to the Wednesday it is one day, and all five are now one day.** No sentence was cut and the entry is still the shortest in the book.
